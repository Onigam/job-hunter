# Negotiation Script — Brokerforce Founding Engineer (Paris)

---

## Market Context

**Role:** Founding Engineer, Hexa/eFounders-backed startup, Paris
**Candidate base:** Geneva, Switzerland
**Key factor:** Paris salaries are lower than Geneva rates, but Hexa portfolio companies compensate with meaningful early equity

### Paris market rates (2025-2026) for this level
- Founding/First Engineer at seed-stage startup: €70k-€90k base
- With AI/ML specialization + founding-team expectation: €85k-€100k
- Comparable profiles at eFounders portfolio co. (Alan, Spendesk era): €80k-€95k base + BSPCE

**Magino's profile premium:** 17 years, co-founder track record, acquisition exit, production AI experience. Top of the range is justified.

**Geneva-to-Paris note:** Magino's cost of living in Geneva is substantially higher than Paris (Geneva is ~30-40% more expensive). If asked to relocate or commute, that's a negotiating lever. If remote, Geneva-based compensation expectations should be discussed explicitly.

---

## Opening Position

**Target base:** €95,000
**Equity target:** 0.3-0.5% BSPCE (founding engineer pool), 4-year vest, 1-year cliff
**Extras worth asking for:** remote flexibility (given Geneva base), equipment budget, conference/training budget

**Anchor line:**
"Based on the scope of the role and what I've seen from comparable founding engineer positions in the Hexa portfolio, I'm thinking around €95k base. I'm more interested in the equity structure and the technical opportunity than squeezing the salary number, so if the base is a constraint, let's talk about how the BSPCE is structured."

---

## If They Come In Low (€70k-€80k)

**Script:**
"I appreciate you being direct. €[X] is below where I'd need to land, and I want to be honest about that rather than waste both of our time. My reference points are founding engineer roles at comparable Hexa-backed companies, and the range there is typically €85k-€95k for someone at this level. I'm genuinely interested in Brokerforce, so I'd ask: is there room to move on the base, or would you make it up on the equity side? If the BSPCE pool is meaningful and the cliff is fair, I'm open to a conversation."

**Minimum threshold:** €82k base + BSPCE or €88k+ base with minimal equity. Don't go below this.

---

## Equity Negotiation

### What to expect from a Hexa seed-stage company
- BSPCE (French equivalent of stock options), likely 0.1-0.5% for a founding engineer
- 4-year vesting, 1-year cliff is standard
- Hexa often retains 15-20% of the cap table, so check post-funding dilution assumptions

### What to ask
1. "What's the current fully-diluted cap table look like, and how much has been set aside for the employee option pool?"
2. "What was the last round's post-money valuation, and when do you expect the next raise?"
3. "What's the accelerated vesting provision in case of acquisition?" (double-trigger acceleration is worth pushing for, especially given Magino's prior acquisition experience)
4. "What's the exercise window post-departure?" (push for 5-10 years, not 90 days)

### BSPCE counter-script if they offer below 0.2%
"For a founding engineer role where I'm expected to co-own the architecture and help shape the engineering culture, 0.1% doesn't reflect the actual risk and contribution scope. Comparable founding team allocations in the eFounders portfolio are typically in the 0.3-0.5% range. I'd ask you to revisit that before we agree on the overall package."

## Geographic / Remote Negotiation

Magino is based in Geneva. The role is posted as Paris-based with occasional remote.

**Conversation opener:**
"I want to address the location early. I'm in Geneva, which works well for Paris given the rail connection. I'm open to being in Paris for key weeks, team offsites, and the critical early-stage moments. But I'd want to structure this as remote-first with agreed Paris cadence rather than 4-days-a-week commute, both for practical reasons and because I think the role doesn't require me to be physically present to be effective. Is that a conversation we can have?"

**If they insist on Paris full-time:** "Full-time Paris relocation would require a cost-of-living adjustment. Geneva is significantly more expensive than Paris, and I'd need to make sure the package reflects that transition, both in the base and potentially a one-time relocation allowance."

## Competing Offer Leverage

If you have another offer or are in late-stage conversations elsewhere:

"I want to be transparent: I'm at an advanced stage with another company, and I expect to have a decision to make in the next [10-14] days. I'm telling you this not to create pressure, but because Brokerforce is genuinely the role I'm most interested in, and I'd rather move toward an offer here than have the timeline make the decision for me."

If no competing offer: don't fabricate one. Instead, use timeline pressure: "I've been in the market for a few months and I'm close to making a decision. I want to make sure Brokerforce is part of that."

## Responses to Common Pushback

**"Our budget is tight at this stage."**
"I understand that's real for an early-stage company. That's exactly why I want to make sure the equity reflects the actual risk I'm taking on. If the base has a ceiling, let's make sure the BSPCE is structured to compensate."

**"We need someone who can start immediately."**
"I can make that work. I'd give appropriate notice to my current employer, but I'm not in a situation where that's months of delay. If there's urgency, we can discuss the transition timeline as part of the offer."

**"Your experience is strong but you're overqualified."**
"I hear that concern, and I'd reframe it: I'm not looking for a management role. I want to be in the code, building real systems, and doing that at a company where what I build matters from day one. That's precisely what founding engineer means to me. Overqualification is a risk if someone gets bored. I don't get bored when the problems are real."
