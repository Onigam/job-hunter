# Negotiation Script: Founding Engineer (Senior/Staff), Sailor Health

## Context to keep in mind

Sailor is a true early-stage startup, the 4th engineer, HQ in New York with remote flexibility, building an AI-native clinical operations platform for senior mental health. They're backed by founders out of Ramp, Nourish, Headway, Charlie Health, and Spring Health, have a live Medicare partnership, and are reportedly doubling headcount or usage roughly every 75 days, which is a fast, thin-runway stage where equity matters as much as cash and process is still being invented. Public aggregator data on this posting points to a base range around $125K to $175K for the senior tier, which is a starting band, not a ceiling, especially for someone bringing founding and platform-scale experience rather than pure IC years. The bigger open question isn't the number, it's geography: I'm in Geneva, the posting lists New York or remote, and a seed-stage US health-tech company may or may not have a structure for a Switzerland-based hire at all. That has to get resolved before comp does.

## Switzerland market anchors (keep in your pocket, don't lead with them)

Geneva isn't the natural anchor for a US-based, dollar-denominated seed-stage role, but it's worth knowing as a floor. A Staff-level fullstack engineer in Geneva with founding and platform-scale experience typically sits in the CHF 160K to 190K range in a mature company; at a seed-stage startup, cash comp is usually well below that, offset by equity and speed. The honest comparison isn't "what would this role pay in Geneva," it's "what does a US seed-stage founding engineer package look like," since that's the market Sailor is actually competing in.

## Opening move: clarify scope and structure before any number

"Before we get to comp, I want to understand two things: is a Switzerland-based hire something you've structured for before, and what does the reporting and overlap expectation look like day to day? I want to make sure this is workable on both sides before we talk numbers."

## Opening anchor, once scope is clear

If they confirm a remote, Europe-based arrangement works: "Given the scope, founding-engineer ownership of the clinical ops layer plus AI feature work, and the fact that I've built a platform from zero through an acquisition before, I'd want to be talking about the upper end of your range, call it $165K to $180K base, with a founding-tier equity grant that reflects being the 4th engineer, not the 40th."

## If they come in low on cash

"I get that a seed-stage budget is real and I'm not trying to force a number that breaks your model. If base has to land lower, I'd want that made up on the equity side, and I'd want a defined comp review at six months tied to specific milestones, not a vague 'we'll revisit it later.'"

## Equity questions

Ask for the exact percentage or share count and the fully diluted share total, not just one or the other, since a percentage without the denominator tells you nothing. Ask about the strike price and the valuation from the last round or SAFE. Confirm the vesting schedule, one-year cliff and four-year vest is standard, and ask directly whether there's any acceleration on acquisition, given that Alaya's own acquisition is the reason I know to ask. At this stage, also ask how much of the option pool is already allocated versus reserved for future hires, since that affects how much your grant could get diluted in the next round.

## Geographic and remote flexibility

If they push back on hiring outside the US at all: "I want to be upfront that I'm not planning to relocate to New York. If that's a hard requirement for this role, better to know now than after we've both spent time on this. If there's flexibility, especially given how much of the early product and clinical workflow work can happen async, I'd like to hear what that looks like in practice."

## Competing offer template

Use only if a real competing process exists. Never invent a number or company name.

"I'm also in later-stage conversations for a comparable founding or staff-level role that has a clearer answer on the Europe question. Sailor's mission is the one I actually want to work on, so if there's room to move on base, equity, or the remote structure, this is where I'd want to know before I have to decide."
