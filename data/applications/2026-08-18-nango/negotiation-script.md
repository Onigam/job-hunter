# Negotiation Script: Staff Backend Engineer, Nango

Context that changes the math here: Nango is YC W23, roughly 14 people, mid-seven-figure ARR, and has raised only around $2M disclosed, so this is still an early-stage cap table, not a company that just closed a large round. Listings for this role have shown base ranges from roughly $120,000 to $220,000 depending on country, which tells you they're running geographically adjusted bands rather than one flat number. The role is fully remote with no office, hiring across 20+ countries including Switzerland, so the Swiss band is the one to anchor on, not the US one. Staff-level IC, not a management hire, comp should reflect scope and ownership, not headcount managed.

## Market anchor

- Staff-level backend engineer, Geneva-based remote, at a well-funded early-stage startup: roughly CHF 150,000-185,000 base, reflecting Swiss cost of living and 17+ years of experience with founder-grade ownership.
- US-anchored bands for this exact role have topped out around $220,000, which at current exchange rates lands close to the top of that Swiss range, so there's real room to anchor high without asking for anything unusual.
- Early-stage equity at a 14-person, YC-backed company should be meaningfully sized for a staff hire, this isn't a later-stage grant with diluted upside.
- Opening anchor: CHF 165,000 base plus an equity grant sized for a staff-level early hire, not a standard senior engineer slice.

## Opening anchor script

"Given the scope of this role and that I'd be joining as a staff-level IC on a fourteen-person engineering team, I'd expect a base around CHF 165,000 if it's priced against where I'm based in Geneva, plus an equity grant that reflects joining this early. I saw your ranges vary quite a bit by country, so can you tell me which band Switzerland actually falls under, and whether that's base only or total comp?"

## If they come in low

"I hear the number. Is that the Swiss-adjusted band, or closer to where a different country in your range would land? I ask because the scope here, owning core backend infrastructure at staff level, is what I'm anchoring the ask to, not just the base geography."

If they hold the line: "I can flex on base if the equity grant is sized to reflect a staff-level early hire rather than a standard senior slice, or if there's a defined comp review after the first six months tied to scope. What I'd rather not do is anchor below CHF 150,000 equivalent, that's under market for this level in my location."

## Equity notes

- Ask for the current valuation basis (last round or 409A-equivalent), the strike price, and the vesting schedule, standard is four years with a one-year cliff, confirm Nango follows that.
- At $2M disclosed raised against a mid-seven-figure ARR, the company is likely capital-efficient and hasn't diluted heavily, ask directly what percentage or share count a staff-level grant represents rather than accepting a dollar figure alone.
- Ask about exercise windows post-departure, early-stage grants sometimes carry short post-termination exercise windows that matter more than the headline grant size.
- Since Nango is open source, ask whether any of the roadmap or ownership is tied to community-facing work that affects how equity vesting or promotion criteria get evaluated.

## Geographic discount pushback

"I noticed the posted ranges span from about $120,000 to $220,000 depending on country. Geneva is one of the more expensive cities globally, on par with or above US tech hubs for cost of living, so I'd want the band here treated as high-cost, not discounted the way some remote-first companies do by default. Happy to share comparable data if that's useful."

## Competing offer, if genuinely in play

"I'm in later-stage conversations with another company at a comparable scope and similar range to what I mentioned. This role is the one I'm most drawn to because of the specific infrastructure problem you're solving, but I wanted to be upfront about timing so we can move at a pace that works for both of us."

## Close

Being early-stage and YC-backed is a reason to ask for more equity, not less base, a fourteen-person team running millions of tasks a month for hundreds of customers has already proven the product works, so the risk premium on the base salary should be lower than a true pre-revenue startup, even if the equity upside is real. Get the base, equity percentage or share count, vesting schedule, and exercise window terms in writing before accepting, early-stage offers move fast and verbal numbers shift between the recruiter call and the written offer more often than they should.
