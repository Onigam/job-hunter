# Negotiation Script: Fonio, Founding Product Engineer (m/w/d)

## Context to anchor on

Fonio is a Vienna-based voice AI company, around 50 people today and reportedly doubling headcount by the end of the year, with roughly €20M raised and a customer base past 9,500 businesses. That's a company that's already past the seed-stage scramble, closer to a well-capitalized Series A profile, which matters for comp: there's real budget, but a founding-level title at 50 people also means the cheapest founding equity is already gone. Engineering is remote within plus or minus three hours CET, and Geneva sits inside that window without any argument needed, so unlike most remote roles I look at, geography isn't the opening fight here. The harder question is currency and market: Fonio pays out of an Austrian entity most likely, and Austrian or German tech comp for a senior engineering hire runs meaningfully below Swiss rates for the same seniority, so the negotiation is really about which market's numbers apply to a Geneva-based hire on a fully remote, EU-adjacent contract.

## Opening anchor

Swiss market comp for a Senior Staff or founding-level engineer in Geneva typically runs CHF 150,000 to CHF 190,000 base, before equity. Central European tech comp for an equivalent founding engineer role, Vienna or Berlin, tends to land closer to €90,000 to €120,000, so there's a real gap to bridge depending on which currency and market the offer gets built around.

"Given the scope here, end-to-end ownership of a feature that thousands of companies rely on, plus taking over Voice AI prioritization mid-term, I'd want to anchor toward the CHF 160,000 to CHF 175,000 range if we're structuring this as a Swiss-based remote contract, or somewhere around €125,000 to €140,000 if it runs through an Austrian or German entity instead. On equity, I'd be looking at 0.3% to 0.8%, which reflects that you're past the earliest seed stage but still early enough that a founding engineer hire should carry a real stake."

## If they come in low on salary

A company that's raised €20M and is doubling headcount isn't cash-constrained in the way a pre-seed team is, so a low opening number is more likely a starting position than a real ceiling.

"I understand there's a comp band you're working within, and I don't need to be at the very top of it. But if the number needs to sit closer to €95,000 or €100,000, I'd want the equity to move up to compensate, closer to 1%, and I'd want a written commitment to revisit base once the feature I own is actually live and being used, rather than waiting for a generic annual cycle."

## Equity negotiation

At 50 people and post-€20M, the option pool is a different conversation than it would be at 10 people.

"Before we settle on a number, I'd like to understand how much of the pool is already allocated across the current team, what the strike price looks like given the last round's valuation, and whether there's a near-term raise on the horizon that would reset that basis. Standard four year vesting with a one year cliff works fine for me. What I'd want to avoid is a stake priced against a valuation that's already stale by the time it vests."

## Geographic and remote angle

This is the one part of the conversation that's genuinely in my favor here.

"I'm based in Geneva, which sits comfortably inside your CET window, so there's no timezone problem to solve and no relocation ask either. What I would want clarity on is the payroll structure: whether I'd be on Austrian or German payroll, going through an EOR, or contracting directly, since that changes both the tax picture and which market's comp numbers are the right reference point."

## Competing offer template

Use only if there's an actual live process elsewhere.

"I'm further along with a couple of other founding-stage teams at a similar comp level right now. I'd rather join the team and product I actually want to build, not just chase the best number, but if there's flexibility once you're ready to make something formal, I'd want to know before I close things out elsewhere."

## Closing move

"None of this needs to be a blocker. If the package reflects the ownership this role actually carries, feature to production, client feedback loop included, and the payroll and currency question has a clean answer, I'm ready to move fast."
