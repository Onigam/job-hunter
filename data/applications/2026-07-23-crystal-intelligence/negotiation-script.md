# Negotiation Script: Crystal Intelligence, VP of Engineering (Remote)

## Context

Posted range: $150,000 to $250,000 USD base, full-time remote, listed against US locations (San Francisco, Austin, Texas). Crystal Intelligence has no Switzerland presence, so this is a USD offer from a US-anchored company, not a Geneva-benchmarked one. The Swiss numbers below are a personal reference point for evaluating the offer, not something to raise with Crystal as if they owe a Geneva rate. The spread between $150k and $250k is wide enough that it's almost certainly calibrated by seniority and track record on platform migrations rather than a fixed band, which is worth confirming early.

## Switzerland market anchor (for personal reference)

VP Engineering and equivalent Staff/Principal-plus roles in Geneva and Zurich at established tech companies typically land CHF 200,000 to CHF 260,000 total comp, with meaningfully lower income tax than most US states and employer pension contributions on top. A $220,000 to $250,000 USD base, converted and adjusted for the absence of state tax, sits close to or above that band before any bonus or equity. That means the top third of Crystal's posted range is competitive with staying local, so the case for anchoring high rests on scope, the migration mandate, and comparable market data, not on need.

## Opening anchor

Don't open in the middle of the posted range. Open near the top and justify it with the scope of the mandate, not personal circumstances.

Script: "I've seen the range posted for this role, $150K to $250K. Given the scope here, owning an end-to-end migration across six live products with SLA exposure for regulators and law enforcement, plus the budget and hiring plan, that reads like the top third of the band to me. I'd like to talk about landing at $230K to $240K base, plus whatever bonus and equity structure applies at this level."

## Counter-offer script (if they come in low, below $190K)

Don't get defensive. Ask what the number is anchored to, then redirect to scope and the migration mandate specifically.

Script: "Thanks for sending that over. Can I ask what level or track record that number assumes? Looking at the responsibilities, an end-to-end legacy-to-AI-native migration across Crystal Expert, Foresight, Monitor, the Risk Check API, Data Intelligence, and Crystal Light with no SLA regressions, that's closer to the top of your posted band. Based on comparable platform-migration VP roles I've seen, I was anchored closer to $220K to $230K base. Is there room to move before we get into equity or bonus?"

If they hold firm on base but have flexibility elsewhere: ask about a sign-on bonus in the $15,000 to $25,000 range to bridge the gap for year one, or a defined six-month comp review tied to migration milestones (first product cut over, SLA metrics held).

## Equity notes

Compensation posted doesn't mention equity explicitly, so ask directly rather than assuming none exists:

- Ask whether there's an equity or options component at all for this level, and if so, whether it's ISOs, RSUs, or phantom equity, since Crystal isn't a public company.
- If options, ask for the strike price and the most recent 409A valuation date, not just a share count, and what percentage of the fully diluted cap table the grant represents.
- Confirm the vesting schedule. Standard is four years with a one-year cliff; push back if theirs runs longer given the seniority of this role.
- Ask about early exercise eligibility and the post-termination exercise window, since a VP-level grant should come with more favorable terms than a standard IC grant.
- Don't let an equity story substitute for a below-market base. Base is what compounds into savings and any pension contribution set up independently as a remote hire.

## Geographic discount pushback

If a recruiter tries to apply a lower band because the hire would sit in Switzerland rather than the US:

Script: "I understand geography sometimes factors into remote comp, but the mandate here, owning the migration end-to-end with budget and hiring authority, doesn't change based on where I'm sitting. I'd want to understand the reasoning before assuming a discount applies to a role at this level."

## Competing offer angle

Use only if there's a genuine parallel process. Vague or invented claims tend to backfire with experienced recruiters at this seniority.

Script, if real: "I'm further along in another process for a comparable scope and base. I'd rather prioritize this one if we can land close to the range we discussed."

Script, if no competing offer exists: don't invent one. Lean on the posted range and the migration mandate instead. "A mandate this specific, six products, a hard SLA constraint, and full budget and hiring ownership, tends to price at the top of a posted band like this one, and that's the number I'd want to land at."

## Close

Once base and any equity are aligned, confirm start date flexibility (targeting September), ask whether the sign-on or bonus structure is documented in the offer letter rather than left verbal, and request that the six-month migration-milestone review be written into the offer, not just discussed.
