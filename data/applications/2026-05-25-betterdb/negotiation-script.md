# Negotiation Script — BetterDB Founding Engineer

---

## Context & Market Positioning

BetterDB appears to be bootstrapped and in early access/beta. No public funding rounds found. This means:
- Cash compensation may be below funded-startup norms
- Equity will be the primary upside lever
- Founding engineer status is real leverage: you're employee #1 or #2

**Geneva market rate anchors (2025-2026):**
- Senior Engineer (5-8 years): CHF 140,000-170,000
- Staff/Principal Engineer (10+ years): CHF 170,000-210,000
- Founding Engineer premium (cofounder-equivalent risk): CHF 190,000-230,000
- AI/platform engineering premium: +10-15% above baseline

**Magino's anchor:** CHF 200,000 base. Justified by:
- 17 years total experience
- Founding engineer track record (Alaya, acquired by $1B+ company)
- Staff-level AI integration expertise
- Geneva cost of living (one of the highest globally)
- Competing opportunity cost

---

## Opening Anchor

If asked about compensation expectations directly:

> "For a founding engineer role, I'm anchoring at 200K CHF base. That's in line with what Staff-level engineers with a founding background command in Geneva, and it reflects the risk profile of joining early. I'm open to structuring part of that as equity if the equity terms are meaningful, but I'd want to see the base stay above 160K even in a mostly-equity scenario."

---

## If They Come In Low (120K-150K CHF)

> "I appreciate the transparency. That range works for an early-stage startup on a tight budget, but it's below what I need to make the risk/reward math work for my situation in Geneva. I'd need to understand the equity package in detail before I could get comfortable at that base. What does the option pool look like, and what's the current cap table situation?"

Follow-up counter:

> "If the equity is meaningful and the vesting terms are clean, I could see making this work at 160K base plus equity. Below that I'd struggle to justify the opportunity cost given what I'd be walking away from."

---

## If They Come In Mid (150K-170K CHF)

> "That's closer. I'd counter at 185K, which still sits below my anchor but reflects a realistic compromise for an early-stage company. I'd also want to make sure the equity package compensates for the gap — what percentage of the company would I be looking at, and where are you on the valuation?"

---

## If They Come In at or Above 180K CHF

> "That works as a starting point. Before I accept, I want to understand the equity structure in detail. The base makes sense; the equity is where the real upside is at this stage, and I want to make sure we're aligned on what that looks like over a four-year horizon."

---

## Equity Negotiation

**What to ask for as founding engineer (bootstrapped, early-stage):**
- 1-2% equity minimum; 2-3% if you're truly the first hire
- 4-year vesting, 1-year cliff (standard)
- Push for double-trigger acceleration on acquisition (most important protection)
- Ask for early exercise rights (ISOs/NSOs in a US-structured entity, or equivalent)

**Questions to ask before accepting any equity number:**

1. "What's the current fully diluted share count, and where does my grant sit in that?"
2. "What's the exercise price, and is it based on a current 409A valuation?"
3. "Is there an acceleration clause on acquisition, and is it single or double trigger?"
4. "What percentage of the company does this represent today, and how does that change if you raise a round?"
5. "What's the option pool size, and is it pre- or post-money?"

**If the equity feels thin:**

> "I want to make this work, but the equity math matters as much as the base at this stage. If we're at X% now with a Y share count, I'd need to see either a larger grant or a clear commitment to refresh grants on future rounds to make the risk profile work for me."

---

## Geographic Discount Pushback

If they try to apply a "remote discount" since BetterDB may not be Geneva-based:

> "I'm based in Geneva, which is one of the most expensive cities in Europe. Remote doesn't change my cost of living. The market rate I'm anchoring to is what engineers at my level command in this market, and it's the same whether I'm commuting or not. I wouldn't expect a remote employee in San Francisco to take a discount because the company isn't local, and the same logic applies here."

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

If you have another offer (or to create urgency):

> "I want to be transparent: I'm in conversations with [Company X] at [range]. I'd prefer to work with BetterDB given the problem space and the stage, but I need the package to be close enough that I'm not taking a significant step back financially. I'm not trying to play you against them; I just want to give you the full picture."

If you don't have a competing offer but want to signal opportunity cost:

> "I'm actively looking, so my timeline is reasonably short. I have a couple of conversations that are progressing, and I'd rather move forward here if we can get the terms to make sense."

---

## Walk-Away Threshold

- Base below CHF 140,000: don't accept unless equity is genuinely exceptional (3%+ with clean terms)
- Base below CHF 120,000: decline regardless of equity, given Geneva cost of living
- Equity below 0.5% fully diluted at current cap table: push hard or walk
- No acceleration clause on acquisition: push hard; this is non-negotiable for a founding hire

---

## Closing Move

Once terms are agreed in principle:

> "I want to close this cleanly. Can we get the offer letter in writing by [date]? I'll review it, ask any clarifying questions, and come back to you within 48 hours. I'm not planning to renegotiate once I've said yes in writing."
