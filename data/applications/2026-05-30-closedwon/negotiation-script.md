# Negotiation Script — VP Engineering @ ClosedWon

---

## Market Context

**Role:** VP Engineering, fully remote (US startup)
**My location:** Geneva, Switzerland

### Switzerland market anchors (VP Eng level)
- Geneva / Zurich VP Engineering, tech companies: CHF 180,000 – 230,000 base
- Senior Staff / Head of Eng at scale-ups: CHF 160,000 – 200,000 base
- Geneva cost of living index: ~30% higher than most European cities; comparable to San Francisco

### US market anchors (remote VP Eng at startup)
- Series A / early-stage US startup: $180,000 – $240,000 base
- Equity typical range for VP Eng at early startup: 0.5% – 1.5% (4-year vest, 1-year cliff)

**Opening anchor target:** CHF 210,000 base (or $200,000+ if they price in USD), plus equity.

---

## Opening: First Offer Conversation

**If they ask your number first:**

> "I've been looking at VP Engineering roles in the $200k range in USD, which aligns with my market data for this level. That said, I care more about the total package, base plus equity plus the role itself, than hitting a specific base number. What's the band you're working with?"

**Goal:** Get their range first if possible. Never anchor below your target.

---

## They Come In Below Anchor

**Scenario:** They offer $150,000 – $170,000 (common for startups trying to geo-adjust)

**Response script:**

> "I appreciate the offer. To be direct with you: I'm Geneva-based, and my cost of living and market benchmarks are anchored to Swiss rates, not Eastern European or lower-cost-of-living baselines. Geneva is one of the more expensive cities in the world. The VP Engineering market here runs between CHF 180,000 and 230,000, which in USD terms puts me in the $190k – $240k range depending on exchange rates. I'm not asking you to pay above your band, but I'd need the base to be closer to $190,000 to make this work practically."

**If they push back on geo-pricing:**

> "I understand the desire to price by location, but I'd note that my output is identical regardless of where I'm sitting. You're getting someone who built and scaled a platform to acquisition in a high-cost market with high-cost talent. If anything, you're getting leverage on the talent pool you'd be competing with in San Francisco. The ask is $190k minimum base, which I think is fair given the level and my track record."

---

## Equity Negotiation

**Target:** 0.75% – 1.25% for VP Eng joining at early stage, 4-year vest, 1-year cliff

**Opening:**

> "For VP Engineering at this stage, I'd be looking at 1% equity on a standard 4-year vest with a 1-year cliff. What's the current cap table looking like, and what's the last 409A valuation?"

**If they come in at 0.25% – 0.5%:**

> "That's below what I'd expect for a VP-level hire at this stage. At a company building toward a significant exit, this role is foundational, not operational. I'd want to be at 0.75% minimum to feel like a real stakeholder. What flexibility do you have on the option pool?"

**Key questions to ask about equity:**
- What is the current 409A valuation and when was the last round?
- What's the total option pool size and how much is unallocated?
- Are these ISOs or NSOs? (ISOs are better for US tax purposes, matters less for CH)
- Is there double-trigger acceleration on change of control?
- What's the exercise window post-departure? (90 days is standard but limiting; push for 2-5 years)

---

## Geographic Discount Pushback

**If they say:** "We typically adjust for location and Geneva is expensive, but we're a US company..."

**Response:**

> "I hear that. Two things: first, my benchmarks are what they are, and I need a package that covers my actual cost of living. Second, Geneva-based engineers at VP level are competing in a market where Zurich fintech and Geneva-area multinationals are paying CHF 200k+. If you want someone in my timezone who has both a founding background and enterprise-scale experience, this is the market you're competing in. I'm not asking for a premium over US rates, I'm asking for rough parity."

---

## Competing Offer Framing (if needed)

Even without a live competing offer, you can use this:

> "I'm in conversations with a couple of other companies, one of them Geneva-based, and the base range I'm hearing is consistently in the CHF 190,000 – 220,000 range. I'd like to close on something with ClosedWon if we can get close to that. What room do you have to move?"

---

## Total Package Checklist

Before accepting, verify:
- [ ] Base salary (CHF or USD equivalent at current rate, locked or floating?)
- [ ] Equity: percentage, vesting schedule, cliff, exercise window
- [ ] Equipment budget (standard for remote: $2,000 – $3,000 one-time)
- [ ] Home office / internet stipend (CHF 100 – 200/month is standard)
- [ ] Health insurance note: Swiss law requires Magino to hold Swiss health insurance regardless; US company health benefit may not apply
- [ ] Vacation: Swiss standard is 4 weeks minimum; US startups often say "unlimited" which can mean less in practice
- [ ] Contract type: employee vs. contractor (CHF/tax implications differ significantly in Switzerland)

---

## Final Accept / Walk-Away Guidance

**Accept if:** Base CHF 190,000+ (or $180,000+), equity 0.75%+, standard vesting, reasonable total package.

**Walk away if:** Base under CHF 170,000 with no meaningful equity upside, or contractor arrangement with no benefits at a rate that doesn't compensate for the missing employment protections.
