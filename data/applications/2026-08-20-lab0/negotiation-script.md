# Negotiation Script — Lab0, Founding Engineer

## Context that shapes this negotiation

The posted range is $40,000-$120,000 salary with 0.10%-1.00% equity, for a Bengaluru-based role. That range is set for the Indian engineering market. I am Geneva-based. Before any number gets discussed, the location and work-arrangement question has to be resolved, because the same title pays very differently depending on where the seat actually is.

There are really three possible conversations, not one:

1. **I relocate to Bengaluru.** Then their posted range is close to locally appropriate, and the negotiation is mostly about landing at the top of it plus equity, relocation support, and visa/immigration costs covered.
2. **I stay in Geneva, remote.** Then the posted range is not usable as an anchor at all. A Swiss-based Staff/Founding-level engineer costs the market roughly CHF 160,000-220,000 base for comparable seniority, and a YC seed-stage startup paying that in cash is unrealistic, so this scenario is really an equity-heavy structure, not a salary negotiation.
3. **Hybrid or short relocation stints.** Rare for a two-person-stage founding role, but worth naming as an option if the company is flexible on where engineering happens.

Assume the recruiter call opens without addressing this. Raise it first, before compensation, so the rest of the conversation isn't wasted anchoring against a number that doesn't apply.

## Opening the location question

"Before we get into comp, I want to flag something so we don't waste each other's time. I'm in Geneva, and the listing is Bengaluru-based. Is relocation something you need for this role, or is there flexibility on where the engineering seat sits? That answer changes what a fair number looks like a lot."

## If relocation is required (scenario 1)

**Opening anchor:**
"If I'm relocating, I'd want to land at the top of your posted range, $120,000, plus the equity at the high end of your band, and I'd need relocation costs and visa sponsorship covered outside the salary number, not carved out of it. For a founding engineer taking on agent architecture, infra, and live customer implementations from day one, that's the fair read of your own range, not a stretch above it."

**If they counter low (say $70,000-$85,000):**
"I hear you, but that's mid-band for a role you're describing as owning the agent loop, the infra, and the customer-facing implementation risk from day one. I'd rather we find the number that reflects that scope than have me negotiate again in six months once the scope is confirmed. Where's the room, base or equity?"

**Relocation and visa ask:**
"I'd want relocation costs, temporary housing for the first month, and full visa sponsorship handled directly, not reimbursed after the fact out of pocket. That's table stakes for asking someone to move continents for a seed-stage bet."

## If remote from Geneva is viable (scenario 2)

**Opening anchor:**
"If this stays remote out of Geneva, the posted range doesn't reflect the market I'm actually in. A Staff-level engineer here runs CHF 160,000-220,000 in cash comp. I don't expect a seed-stage YC company to match that in salary, so I'd want this structured as a lower cash number with equity doing the rest of the work, something closer to $90,000-$100,000 base with equity toward the top of your 1% band, vesting over four years with a one-year cliff."

**If they push back on the cash number:**
"Understood if cash is tight this early. I'm open to going lower on base if the equity moves up to compensate and we're explicit that we revisit both once the first funding milestone lands. What I'm not willing to do is take the Bengaluru-market number while living in one of the more expensive cities in Europe."

**If they say the role truly requires being in-person:**
"Then let's go back to the relocation conversation, because remote isn't actually on the table and I'd rather know that now than discover it three offers in."

## Equity negotiation notes (either scenario)

- Ask directly what the current fully diluted share count is and what round this equity is priced against. A 1% headline number means very different things depending on dilution to date.
- Standard four-year vest, one-year cliff is fine; push back only if they propose anything longer or a cliff beyond a year.
- Ask about acceleration on acquisition. Given the Alaya-to-Benevity path, this is a real question to ask, not a hypothetical: "What happens to my vesting if the company gets acquired in year two?"
- Confirm exercise window after departure. Anything under 90 days post-termination is worth pushing on; some YC companies now offer extended windows, ask rather than assume.

## Competing offer leverage template

Use only if genuinely true at the time:

"I want to be straightforward that I'm also further along with another opportunity that's closer to Swiss market comp for a remote Staff-level role. Lab0 is the more interesting technical problem to me, agent architecture against live enterprise systems is a harder and more honest bet than most agent startups are making, so I'd rather make this work than default to the other offer on comp alone. Is there room to close the gap?"

## Closing line for any scenario

"I want this to work, the problem is the right kind of hard. I just need the comp conversation to start from the market I'm actually being asked to work in, whichever one that ends up being."
