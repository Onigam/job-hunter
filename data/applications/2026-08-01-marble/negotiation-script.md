# Negotiation Script: Marble, Co-Founder CTO, Physical AI for Process Industries

Posted structure: EUR 3,000 monthly allowance during the studio program, EUR 250,000 inception funding at incorporation, 80% founder ownership, seed fundraising support, visa sponsorship geared toward France. This isn't a salary negotiation in the normal sense, it's a founder-terms negotiation: cash runway, equity split, and location all move together, and each one changes what the others are worth.

## Reading the offer

A EUR 3,000 monthly allowance is a pre-seed founder stipend, not a Swiss or French tech salary, and it isn't meant to be one. The real comp is the equity plus the EUR 250,000 inception check plus whatever seed round the studio helps close. Magino should evaluate this like an actual founder round, not like a job offer with a low base, and negotiate the studio terms accordingly rather than pushing hard on the monthly number, which the studio likely can't move much without changing every other deal it runs.

The 80% founder ownership figure needs unpacking before anything else: 80% of what, at what valuation, split between how many co-founders, and vested over what schedule. That number is the actual offer, more than the monthly allowance is.

## Opening questions before any counter

- "Is the 80% founder ownership split between me and other co-founders on this venture, or is it mine alone if I'm the sole technical co-founder at incorporation?"
- "What does the studio's 20% convert into at seed, and does it get diluted alongside founder shares or does it have anti-dilution protection the rest of us don't have?"
- "Is the EUR 250,000 inception funding a loan, a SAFE, straight equity investment, or non-dilutive grant money, and does it come with board seats or veto rights attached?"

Getting clear answers here matters more than any number Magino could counter with blind.

## Vesting and cliff

Standard founder vesting is 4 years with a 1-year cliff, and it should apply symmetrically, if Magino leaves or is pushed out before the venture reaches seed, unvested shares return to the pool, but the same clock and cliff should apply to the studio's own stake, not just the founder's. Ask directly whether the studio's 20% is subject to any performance milestones tied to Magino's continued involvement, since that changes how much control actually sits with the founder day to day.

## If the allowance feels too low for the relocation ask

Counter script: "The EUR 3,000 allowance works as a bridge during the program, but the visa track you've described points toward France, and I'm currently in Geneva. I'd like to explore whether this venture can be based out of Switzerland instead, given the chemicals and pharma cluster around Basel and the Valais sits closer to a meaningful share of the target customers than most French sites would. If Switzerland isn't workable for the studio's structure, I'd want the relocation and visa support to cover a Geneva-to-France move specifically, not a generic package." Anchoring on customer proximity reframes the location as a business decision, not a personal preference.

## Seed fundraising support

Ask what "support" actually means in practice: warm intros only, a lead investor commitment, a bridge extension if the first seed attempt stalls. Push for this in writing: "What happens to my equity and the venture if we don't close a seed round within the studio's usual timeline, does the venture wind down, do I keep my vested shares, and is there a runway extension option before that decision gets made?" A studio's own success rate on follow-on funding, which they'll likely cite, is a good opening to ask for the actual data behind that number for ventures in this specific vertical.

## IP and downside protection

Confirm in writing that all IP built during the program belongs to the venture entity, not the studio parent, and ask what happens to that IP if the venture is discontinued before seed, specifically whether Magino retains any rights to reuse general approaches, not proprietary client data, in a future venture.

## Competing offer angle

Use only if genuinely in play: "I'm also evaluating a Staff or Principal platform role with a funded company that pays Swiss market cash comp, so the calculus here is founder upside against near-term cash. I'd rather understand your actual terms in full than negotiate against a number I can't disclose." This keeps pressure on clarity rather than an artificial deadline.

## Close

End with a specific next step rather than an open-ended "sounds interesting": ask for the cap table structure and SAFE or equity documents in writing, propose a date to discuss the Switzerland-versus-France question directly with whoever owns that decision at the studio, and confirm what technical work sample or pilot conversation, if any, comes next in their process.
