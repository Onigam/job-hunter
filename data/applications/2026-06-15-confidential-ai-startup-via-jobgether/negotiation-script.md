# Negotiation Script — Tech Lead, LLM & Generative AI (Confidential AI Startup via Jobgether)

**Company:** EverAI (identified from Jobgether listing)
**Contract type:** B2B preferred (their stated preference)
**Location:** Full Remote Europe, Switzerland-based
**Role level:** Tech Lead (equivalent to Senior Staff / L6)

---

## Market Anchors — Switzerland, Senior AI Engineer / Tech Lead

### Geneva/Switzerland market rates (B2B contractor, 2026)

| Profile | Daily rate (CHF) | Annual equivalent (240 days) |
|---|---|---|
| Senior AI/ML Engineer, Switzerland | CHF 700–900/day | CHF 168,000–216,000 |
| Staff/Tech Lead AI, Geneva | CHF 850–1,050/day | CHF 204,000–252,000 |
| Principal/VP Engineering, AI startup | CHF 1,000–1,300/day | CHF 240,000–312,000 |

**Your target range as B2B Tech Lead:** CHF 850–1,000/day, monthly invoice.

Equivalent if they ask for a monthly figure: CHF 18,000–22,000/month (at ~21 billable days/month).

**Why B2B adds ~25-30% over employment:**
- You pay your own social contributions (AVS/AI/APG in Switzerland: ~10.5%)
- No employer accident/health insurance
- No paid sick leave guarantee
- No Swiss legal protections on notice periods
- Accounting, contracts, liability: your cost

When anchoring, note: "As a B2B contractor based in Switzerland, the effective cost to me of social contributions and absence of employer benefits means the rate needs to reflect that delta compared to an employment equivalent."

## Opening Anchor Script

When they ask for your rate expectation:

> "Based on comparable Tech Lead roles in AI/ML here in Switzerland and the scope of what you're describing, I'm targeting CHF 950 per day on a B2B basis. That's in line with the Geneva market for Staff+ AI engineering roles and accounts for the contractor overhead on my side. I'm open to discussing how that translates to a monthly arrangement if that's easier to budget."

If they push back with a range:

> "Can you share where you're benchmarking this? I want to make sure we're comparing apples to apples since the B2B structure changes the effective number significantly compared to an employment equivalent."

## Counter-Offer Script (if they come in low)

**Scenario:** They offer CHF 700/day or below, or a monthly rate below CHF 15,000.

> "I appreciate you putting a number on the table. I want to be direct: at CHF [their number], the effective compensation after Swiss contractor overhead lands below what I'd accept for a salaried role at this level. Given that the role requires someone to both lead the team technically and stay hands-on in the codebase at 80M+ tokens per day of scale, I think the senior market comparison holds. I'm flexible on structure but need to land somewhere around CHF 900/day to make this work. Is there room to move there?"

**Scenario:** They say the budget is fixed.

> "If the day rate is capped, are there other levers? Equity, a signing bonus, or a performance review at six months with a rate adjustment would all be things I'd consider as part of the total package."

## Equity Discussion (if they offer options)

EverAI is described as "top 15 AI companies worldwide" with 50M users. Likely Series B+ or profitable, but startup options pool may still exist.

**Reasonable asks for Tech Lead joining at this stage:**

- **Options:** 0.20%–0.50% of fully diluted shares, 4-year vest, 1-year cliff
- **Strike price:** Confirm it's set at current FMV, not inflated
- **Exercise window:** Ask for 5–10 year post-termination window (standard US/Malta startup has 90 days; push back on this)
- **Anti-dilution:** Weighted average broad-based is standard; avoid simple anti-dilution

**Script:**

> "On equity: I'd expect something in the 0.25–0.50% range for a Tech Lead joining at this stage. Can you share the current cap table structure or at least the size of the option pool? I want to understand what that percentage is worth in context, not just in nominal terms."

If they resist sharing cap table:

> "At minimum, can you share the last round valuation and the total option pool size? Without that, it's hard to evaluate what equity actually means in this offer."

## Geographic Pushback Script (Remote from Switzerland)

EverAI is Malta-based and the role is full remote. They may benchmark salaries against Eastern Europe or lower cost-of-living markets.

> "I want to be upfront that I'm based in Geneva, which has one of the higher costs of living in Europe. I understand you benchmark against the European market broadly, but if the role is truly remote and location-agnostic, I'd ask that the rate reflect the output and the profile rather than the cost of living in Malta or Eastern Europe. The skills and the track record are what they are regardless of where I sit."

## Competing Offer Leverage Template

Even if you don't have a live competing offer, you can reference the market:

> "I'm actively in late-stage conversations for two other roles at comparable level, both also in AI infrastructure. I'm not using that as a pressure tactic, but I want to be transparent that the decision will come down to the total package and where I think the work is most interesting. This role is genuinely compelling to me for the technical depth, but I'd need the compensation to be in the right range to make it a clear choice."

---

## B2B Contract: What to Check Before Signing

- **Notice period:** Push for 30 days minimum, 60 days preferred (they may want 2 weeks)
- **IP clauses:** Confirm IP is limited to what you create in scope; not a blanket assignment
- **Non-compete:** Malta law is more permissive than Swiss; clarify which jurisdiction governs
- **Non-solicitation:** Standard for team members, but watch for customer non-solicitation language that's too broad
- **Payment terms:** 30-day net is standard; resist 60-day net
- **Renewal/extension:** Ask for automatic renewal unless either party gives notice
