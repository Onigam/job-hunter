# Negotiation Script: Nexthink SA, Staff Backend Engineer

Context going in: this is a Staff-level IC role on the data platform team, not a management or architect-lead position, so the comp band tracks senior technical scope rather than headcount owned. Nexthink is PE-backed (Permira took a majority stake in 2021) and established, with 1,500+ customers, so expect base plus bonus plus a long-term incentive component rather than founder-style equity. The role is in Lausanne; I'm in Geneva, close enough that relocation and cost-of-living discounts don't apply.

## Market context

- Staff Software/Backend Engineers in the Geneva-Lausanne corridor at companies of Nexthink's scale typically land in the CHF 150,000-190,000 base range. The top of that band goes to candidates carrying real production ownership, including on-call and L3-level responsibility, which this posting explicitly asks for.
- Ten-plus years of Java/SaaS experience plus messaging and streaming systems (Kafka) is a specific, harder-to-source combination than general backend work, which argues for the upper half of that range rather than the middle.
- Bonus at Staff IC level usually runs 10-15% of base. Worth asking for target versus actual historical payout rather than the headline number alone.
- As a PE-backed private company, equity is more likely a long-term incentive plan or phantom equity tied to a future liquidity event than a standard option grant. That needs to be priced and timed explicitly, not assumed to carry a fixed value.

## Opening position

"I don't see a range listed, so I'd like to start there. Given the scope, owning delivery on the data platform plus L3 support and mentoring, I'd be looking at a base around CHF 175,000, with bonus and long-term incentive structured on top of that. I'd rather anchor on a real number and adjust from data than negotiate blind."

## If they come in low

"I'd want to understand how this number was built, since Staff-level backend roles with production ownership and platform scope typically land higher in this market. If there's a banding constraint internally, I'd rather hear that directly so we can figure out where the actual flexibility sits, whether that's base, bonus target, or the long-term incentive piece."

Key move: don't treat a lowball as the market number without asking what it's benchmarked against, and don't let broad "Senior Engineer" banding absorb what is, on the responsibilities listed, a Staff-scoped role.

## Equity and long-term incentive questions to ask before agreeing to anything

- What form the long-term incentive takes: phantom equity, RSU-equivalent units, or a cash-settled plan tied to a future exit, since Nexthink isn't publicly listed and Permira's ownership structure changes what "equity" actually means here.
- Vesting schedule, cliff, and whether the plan pays out on partial or only full liquidity events.
- Whether there's an annual refresh or the initial grant is a one-time number.
- Bonus target versus what the team has actually been paid out over the last two years, not just the headline percentage.
- What happens to unvested long-term incentive in a future acquisition or secondary sale. Worth asking directly since I've been on the acquired side of exactly that scenario once already, at Alaya.

## Geographic framing

"I'm based in Geneva, forty minutes from Lausanne, so there's no relocation cost and no case for a location-based discount. I'd expect the offer to reflect the Geneva-Lausanne market rate for a Staff-level backend role directly."

## Production ownership and scope framing

"The posting is explicit about L3 support alongside feature delivery. That's meaningfully more scope than a pure feature-team role, and I'd want that reflected in leveling and comp, since carrying production ownership on a platform serving 25 million-plus endpoints sits differently on the band than a role that hands off once code ships."

## Counter-offer script if the number lands mid-band

"That's closer, but the L3 and mentoring scope in this posting is Staff-plus work, and I'd want the base to reflect that rather than sit at the midpoint for the title. If base is fixed at this stage, I'd want to make up the difference on bonus target or the long-term incentive grant."

## Competing offer template (use only if real)

"I'm further along in a process for a comparable Staff backend role elsewhere, with total comp tracking toward roughly CHF [X]. I'd rather make this work since the data platform problem here is the more interesting one, but the numbers need to be in the same range for that to be a genuine choice."

Only use this if there's an actual competing process in flight.

## Closing line for any round

"Send me the full package in writing, base, bonus target, and the long-term incentive structure together, and I'll turn around a decision fast. I'd rather negotiate once with complete numbers than go back and forth piece by piece."
