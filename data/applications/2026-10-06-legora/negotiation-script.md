# Negotiation Script: Legora, Staff Software Engineer (Platform Team)

## The shape of this offer

The posting is for **Stockholm HQ**, full time, with no published salary band. Legora is a Swedish, VC-backed legal AI company that reports $200M+ ARR and 2,100+ customers across 80+ countries. It's growing very fast and has raised large rounds recently (check the latest round and valuation before any comp call, the numbers move quickly). The role drew 200+ applicants within hours, so they have options; the case for top-of-band has to rest on founder plus Staff experience, not on scarcity.

Two structural questions come before any number:

1. **Location.** Magino is in Geneva. Either this means relocating to Stockholm, or Legora agrees to a remote or hybrid setup from Switzerland (via an employer of record or a contractor arrangement). Ask this in the first recruiter call. It changes everything below.
2. **Currency and market.** Stockholm pay is well below Geneva pay in nominal terms, but so is the cost of living. At recent rates (verify on the day) SEK 1.0M is roughly CHF 80k to 85k. Any remote-from-Geneva offer paid on a Stockholm scale will look low next to Swiss anchors, so frame the ask around the role's scope and the Swiss market, not around Stockholm averages.

## Market anchors

Legora's competitors for talent (Harvey, other well-funded AI companies, plus big tech in Stockholm and Zurich) are the real reference set, not the average Swedish employer.

## Scripts

### Opening (when they ask for expectations)

"For a Staff platform role at a company growing as fast as Legora, I'm looking at a base around SEK 1.45 million if I relocate to Stockholm, or around CHF 210k if we set this up from Geneva. That reflects the Staff market in Zurich and Geneva, and the fact that I've built a platform from zero through an acquisition, not just worked on one. I'm flexible on the split between base and equity, so I'd like to see the full package before narrowing it down."

### If they come in low

"Thanks, I appreciate you sharing it. The base is below where I'd need to be for this scope. The posting asks the Staff engineer to lead engineers end to end, own reliability and drive process improvements across the platform team, which is close to what I did as a co-founder and then as Senior Staff at Benevity. Is there room to move the base toward [target]? If the band is fixed, I'd want to talk about the equity grant and a signing bonus instead."

### Geographic discount pushback (remote from Geneva)

"I understand you benchmark against Stockholm. The trouble is that a Stockholm salary paid in Geneva isn't the same offer; my housing, insurance and social charges are on a Swiss scale. You'd also be getting the same scope and hours as a Stockholm hire. I'm not asking for Zurich big-tech pay, just a number that's competitive for a Staff engineer in Switzerland. Can we meet around CHF [target]?"

### Competing offer template (use only if real)

"I want to be open with you: I'm also in late stages with [company] for a [role], and their offer is around [number] base plus [equity]. Legora is my first choice because of the platform problems you're dealing with at your scale, so I'd rather make this work. Is there flexibility to close the gap?"

Never invent a competing offer. If there isn't one, say you're "in conversations with other companies at a similar stage", which is true for an active search.

## Equity notes

Legora is a private Swedish company, so equity is most likely employee stock options or warrants. Questions to ask, in this order:

1. **How many options, and what's the total share count?** A percentage of fully diluted shares is the only number you can reason about. "Options worth X at our last valuation" says little.
2. **Instrument and tax treatment.** Swedish qualified employee options (QESO) get favourable tax treatment, but larger companies often no longer qualify, which means warrants or ordinary options with different tax at grant or exercise. If employed in Switzerland via EOR, Swiss taxation of options applies; get advice before signing.
3. **Strike price and preference stack.** After several large rounds there's significant liquidation preference ahead of common. Ask whether options are on common shares and what the strike is relative to the last preferred price.
4. **Vesting and cliff.** Standard is 4 years with a 1-year cliff. Ask for monthly vesting after the cliff.
5. **Exercise window after leaving.** Short windows (90 days) can force a big cash outlay. Ask for an extended window (5 to 10 years) or confirm it.
6. **Acceleration.** Ask about double-trigger acceleration on acquisition. Having lived through Alaya's acquisition by Benevity, this is a fair, informed question rather than an aggressive one.
7. **Refresh grants.** How are refreshers decided for Staff engineers, and how often?
8. **Secondary sales.** Fast-growing AI companies sometimes run tender offers. Ask whether employees have taken part in one.

## Order of operations

1. First call: settle location (relocate vs remote from Geneva) and level (confirm it's truly Staff, not Senior with a Staff title).
2. Don't give a number before you know the location setup. If pushed, give the Scenario A or B anchor that matches.
3. After the offer: ask for it in writing, including equity details, then counter once on base and once on equity or signing bonus. Keep it to one round if possible.
4. Close with a clear yes on a specific package, so they know a match ends the process.
