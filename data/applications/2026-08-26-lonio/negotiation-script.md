# Negotiation Script, Lonio, Founding Engineer (Zurich)

## Context to keep in mind going in

This is a first-engineer role at a spinoff of BLP Digital, not a pre-seed idea-stage startup. That distinction matters for the negotiation: Lonio already has a validated MVP and paying customers in a CHF 3 billion Swiss compliance market, which is a much stronger position than most "founding engineer" postings imply. The posting itself says compensation structures and titles are flexible, which is an opening, not a red flag, but it also means the number won't be anchored to a public band the way a later-stage company's might be. General Swiss market data for senior/staff-level software engineers in Zurich runs roughly CHF 130,000-160,000 base, but that figure describes an employee joining an established team, not someone taking on architecture, hiring, and culture decisions as the second technical hire. Anchor off the founding-engineer scope and the market opportunity size, not off a generic senior engineer number.

Also worth naming honestly: this is a smaller, earlier company than Benevity or even Alaya at exit, and base cash is more likely to be constrained than at a funded Series A. The real negotiation here is base plus equity plus title, treated as one package, not base alone.

## Opening anchor

Base target: CHF 140,000-160,000, plus meaningful equity, framed around founding-engineer scope rather than a generic senior IC hire. Say it like this:

"Given this is the first engineering hire after the founders, with real influence over architecture and hiring going forward, I'd expect this to land closer to a senior or staff band than a mid-level one, somewhere around CHF 150,000 base, with equity reflecting the founding-level risk and contribution."

## If they come in low (say, below CHF 120,000)

Don't reject outright, ask what the number is actually pricing:

"Is that base reflecting the founding-engineer scope in the posting, hiring, architecture, product direction, or is it priced closer to an early individual contributor role? I ask because owning product architecture and engineering culture from inception is usually a senior-level ask regardless of company size."

If they hold the line on cash, since early-stage companies with recent MVP traction often have more room in equity or title than in salary, push the trade there: ask for a larger equity grant, a faster vesting start, or a more senior title (Head of Engineering, founding CTO track) in exchange for accepting a lower cash number now, with a defined comp review at a set milestone (first funding round, first ten enterprise clients, etc).

## Equity notes

Lonio is a spinoff, not a fresh seed round, so ask directly how the cap table is structured relative to BLP Digital, since that affects how much of the company is actually available to allocate and how dilution might play out differently than a standard seed-stage grant.

- Vesting: 4-year vest with a 1-year cliff is standard in Switzerland too; don't accept longer without a clear reason tied to the spinoff structure.
- Ask what percentage of fully diluted equity the grant represents, not just a number of shares or options, since raw share counts are meaningless without knowing the total pool.
- Ask about the strike price and how the company values its shares currently (Swiss equivalent of a 409A), especially given the spinoff structure may complicate a standard valuation.
- Given the B permit sponsorship offer for EU/EFTA candidates, confirm whether equity vesting or exercise terms are affected by visa status or by leaving the country, since immigration status can interact with option exercise windows in ways people miss until it's too late.

## Geographic and structural notes specific to this offer

Zurich is not a discount market relative to Geneva, cost of living runs comparable or higher, so there's no geographic discount argument to push back on here; the negotiation lever is company stage, not location. Also worth clarifying early: the Claude Max subscription mentioned in the posting is a real perk for AI-augmented development work, but confirm whether tooling budget extends beyond that single subscription, since a founding engineer building AI-powered workflows will likely need broader API and infrastructure budget from day one.

## Competing offer leverage (template, use only if genuinely in play)

"I'm in later-stage conversations with another company at a comparable level, and I'd like to compare on full information before deciding. Could we get to a final structure this week?"

Don't use this without an actual competing process behind it. At a two-person founding team, a bluff is easy to see through and costs trust that matters more here than at a larger company.

## Bottom line walk-away

Given the earlier stage and the flexibility on titles and structure, don't walk purely over base cash if the equity and scope are genuinely strong, this kind of role is priced on upside, not base. But if the final offer lands meaningfully below CHF 110,000 base with thin or vague equity terms, and the founders can't clearly explain the cap table or vesting structure when asked directly, that's a signal worth taking seriously before signing anything.
