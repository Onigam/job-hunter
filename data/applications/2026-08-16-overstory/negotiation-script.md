# Negotiation Script: CTO, Overstory

Context: climate-tech, AI + satellite imagery for utilities, ~100 employees across 15 nationalities, Series A-C growth stage implied by the posting's target profile. Role is Switzerland-based but remote-friendly across a wide country list (US, NL, UK, IE, EE, PT, FR, SE, CH, DK, CA), so comp is likely benchmarked against a global or US-anchored band rather than a pure Geneva local scale. That matters for how the opening anchor gets framed.

## Market anchor (Switzerland + global-remote blend)

- Geneva/Zurich CTO base for a Series B-C startup, local market: roughly CHF 220,000-280,000 fixed, before equity.
- US-anchored remote CTO bands for similarly staged climate/deep-tech startups (Series B-C, ~100 employees): roughly USD 230,000-290,000 base, often paired with a meaningful equity grant since cash tends to run tighter pre-Series-D.
- Given the eligible-country spread, Overstory is plausibly using one global band with a cost-of-living adjustment rather than 11 separate local scales. Worth confirming early in the process rather than assuming either extreme.
- Opening anchor: CHF 260,000 base (or USD-equivalent if the band is dollar-denominated), positioned as the middle of the Swiss range and defensible against both the local and US-remote comps above.

## Opening anchor script

"Based on what I've seen for CTO-level roles at climate and deep-tech startups around this stage and team size, both in Switzerland and across comparable remote-first companies, I'd expect a base in the CHF 250,000 to 280,000 range, plus a meaningful equity component given the stage. I'm flexible on structure if the total comp lands in that zone, I just want to make sure we're anchored on the same numbers before we go further."

## If they come in low

"I hear you on the number. Can you walk me through how the band was set, whether it's Geneva-local, US-anchored, or blended across the eligible countries? I ask because the range I'm working from assumes a company at your stage and team size, and I want to make sure we're comparing the same thing before I adjust my expectation."

Follow-up if they hold the line: "I can work with a lower base if the equity or an earlier review date makes up the difference. What I can't do is anchor below CHF 220,000 fixed, that's below what a Staff+ IC role pays in this market, and this is a C-suite scope."

## Equity negotiation (Series A-C stage)

- Ask for the fully diluted percentage, not just the number of shares or options, this stage of company changes cap table math fast between rounds.
- Standard structure to expect: 4-year vesting, 1-year cliff. Push back if the cliff is longer than a year, that's outside market norm for an exec hire.
- Ask directly about anti-dilution protection and whether the company has a history of down rounds or bridge financing, both compress the value of a nominal percentage quickly.
- For a CTO joining at Series B-C (not founding), a reasonable range is 1.0-2.5% fully diluted depending on how far along the raise is and how much of the technical org already exists. Early Series B skews higher in that band, Series C skews lower.
- Ask whether there's a refresh policy tied to performance or the next round, not just the initial grant.

## Geographic discount pushback

"I noticed the role lists a wide set of eligible countries, which tells me the band probably isn't purely Geneva-indexed. Geneva is one of the more expensive cities globally, on par with or above most of the US hubs on that list, so I'd want to make sure the offer reflects that rather than defaulting to a lower blended average. Happy to share cost-of-living data points if that's useful on your end."

## Competing offer, if genuinely in play

"I'm currently in later-stage conversations with another company at a similar stage, and the numbers we've discussed there are in the range I mentioned earlier. I'd rather move forward with Overstory if the scope and comp both make sense, this role's mandate is the one I'm most drawn to, but I wanted to be transparent about where things stand so we can move at a pace that works for both sides."

## Close

Don't over-anchor on cash alone if the equity story and the mandate (technical vision, distributed team, ML-heavy product) are strong, those are the parts of this specific role that are hard to replicate elsewhere. Get the base and equity ranges in writing before the final round, and confirm the review cadence for both, so there's no ambiguity if the round timeline shifts.
