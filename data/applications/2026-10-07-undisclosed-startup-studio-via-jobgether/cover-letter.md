Most fractional CTO postings I've read are one company's interim hire with a nicer title. This one asks for two or three businesses at once, plus technical audits ahead of investment decisions, so the job is mostly judgment: what each founder should build now, what can wait, and what an investor needs to hear before signing. With a team in the Philippines in the picture, I'd guess that judgment also has to travel well across time zones.

Mine mostly came from making those calls myself. I co-founded Alaya in 2016 and built its platform from a blank page on Node.js, React and PostgreSQL, hiring the first engineers and setting up code review and CI/CD before we had rules about much else. It grew to 50+ enterprise clients and was acquired by Benevity in 2021. Since then, as a Senior Staff Engineer, I've worked across teams I didn't manage, which is close to how fractional work feels; about 70% of two teams adopted AI-assisted review within three months, with no mandate. My cloud work is AWS rather than Azure, and I haven't shipped C# in production. Better you hear that from me.
