# Negotiation Script: Senior Architect (Fractional CTO), Undisclosed startup studio via Jobgether

**Format:** fractional CTO for 2-3 Swiss tech businesses, full-time or part-time/hourly, remote within Europe, occasional in-person founder and investor meetings. No compensation disclosed.

The first thing to settle is the engagement model, because the numbers mean different things depending on it. Ask early: "Is this an employment contract with the studio, a contractor agreement, or a mix where the portfolio companies pay part of it?"

---

## 1. Market anchors (Switzerland, 2026, my estimates)

These are working ranges from Swiss freelance and salary benchmarks for senior architect and CTO roles. Check them against current listings (Michael Page / Hays Switzerland salary guides, Glassdoor Geneva/Zurich, freelance platforms) before the call.

| Model | Low | Typical | Strong |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hourly contractor rate, senior architect / fractional CTO | CHF 140 | CHF 170-190 | CHF 220+ |
| Day rate (8h) | CHF 1,100 | CHF 1,350-1,500 | CHF 1,800 |
| Monthly retainer per portfolio company (~2 days/month) | CHF 2,500 | CHF 3,500-4,500 | CHF 6,000 |
| Fixed-fee technical audit / due diligence (5-8 days) | CHF 6,000 | CHF 8,000-10,000 | CHF 14,000 |
| Full-time employee, Geneva/Zurich (base) | CHF 160k | CHF 180-210k | CHF 230k+ |

**Why the contractor rate sits well above salary ÷ hours:** as self-employed in Switzerland I'd carry AHV/AVS contributions myself (roughly 10%), no employer pillar 2 (LPP), no paid holidays or sick leave, and unbilled time between engagements. A rough rule is that a contractor needs about 1.5-1.8x the hourly equivalent of a salary to break even.

## 2. Opening anchor

**If hourly/contractor:**
> "For fractional CTO work in Switzerland I'm at CHF 190 an hour, and I'd price technical audits for investment decisions as a fixed fee, usually around CHF 9,000 depending on scope. That reflects 17 years in architecture, including building a platform from zero through an acquisition by a $1B+ company, and it's in line with what senior independent architects charge here."

**If employment (full-time):**
> "For a role covering two or three companies at CTO level, I'm looking at a base of CHF 210,000, plus a clear arrangement on equity in the portfolio companies I support."

**If employment (part-time):** pro-rate the full-time figure and say it explicitly: "At 60%, that's CHF 126,000." Don't let a part-time salary get negotiated as though it were a full-time one.

**Walk-away floor (private, never say it):** CHF 150/hour, or CHF 175k full-time base. Below that, the time is better spent on full-time Staff/Principal roles.

---

## 3. If they come in low

**Script A (generic low offer):**
> "Thanks, I appreciate the transparency. That's below what I'm seeing for this kind of scope. You're asking for architecture, code review, mentoring, recruiting and investment audits across three companies, which is three jobs' worth of context. I could get to CHF 175 if we agree audits are billed separately as fixed fees. Would that work?"

**Script B (they quote an offshore or "European remote" rate):**
The Philippines office and remote-within-Europe framing may mean the studio benchmarks against cheaper markets.
> "I understand the studio runs a distributed team. The companies I'd support are Swiss, the founders and investors are here, and the role includes being in the room for those meetings. Swiss rates are the right reference, the same way your clients pay Swiss prices for Swiss-facing work."

**Script C (they want hourly only, uncapped):**
> "Hourly is fine for ad hoc work. For the ongoing CTO role with each company, I'd suggest a monthly retainer with a set number of days. It's more predictable for the founders and it stops the relationship turning into a timesheet argument."

**Script D (they push on the C#/.NET or Azure gap to justify a lower rate):**
> "Fair to raise. The role asks for C#/.NET or Node.js, and Node.js is where I'm strongest. For Azure, the architecture work transfers directly from AWS. If a specific portfolio company is all .NET, I'm happy to agree a lighter first month on that one, but I wouldn't discount the rate for the role as a whole."

## 4. Equity (if offered in portfolio companies)

Fractional CTO equity is usually advisor-style. Reasonable asks:

- **Size:** 0.25-1.0% per company, depending on stage and days committed. Pre-seed with real CTO duties sits near the top of that range, Series A advisory near the bottom.
- **Vesting:** monthly over 24 months (advisor standard), not the 4-year employee schedule. Push back on a 4-year vest for a part-time role.
- **Cliff:** 3 months maximum. A 12-month cliff on a fractional engagement mostly protects them, not me.
- **Acceleration:** single-trigger on acquisition for advisor grants, or double-trigger at minimum. Alaya's exit is a good reason to bring this up: "I've been through an acquisition and I know how unvested equity gets treated."
- **Anti-dilution:** advisors rarely get it, and asking for it can look naive. Ask instead for pro-rata information on future rounds and a cap table view at grant.
- **Instrument:** confirm whether it's actual shares, options or phantom shares. Swiss GmbH and AG structures differ, and phantom shares are taxed as income.
- **Cash vs equity:** equity shouldn't replace cash at this level. "Happy to take equity on top of the rate, not instead of it."

Script:
> "On equity, I'd suggest a standard advisor grant in each company I'm actively working with, around 0.5%, vesting monthly over two years with a three-month cliff and acceleration on change of control. The cash rate stays as discussed."

---

## 5. Geographic discount pushback

> "Being in Geneva is part of what you're getting: same time zone as the founders, at an investor meeting in Zurich or Lausanne the same day, and I know how Swiss companies hire and contract. Geneva is also one of the most expensive cities in Europe. A remote-Europe rate doesn't fit a role that needs a Swiss presence."

If they argue "fully remote within Europe" means a pan-European rate:
> "The remote flexibility is useful, but the companies are Swiss and so are the meetings. I'd price it the way they would if they hired a local CTO, which would cost them a lot more than this."

---

## 6. Competing offer template (use only if true)

> "To be transparent, I'm also in conversations about a full-time Staff/Principal role in Switzerland in the CHF 200k+ range. I'm interested in this one because of the variety and the founder-facing work, but the rate needs to make it a realistic alternative. If we can get to CHF 185 an hour with audits billed separately, I'd be happy to move forward this week."

If there's no concrete competing offer, don't invent one. Say instead:
> "I'm talking to a few companies about Staff and Principal roles, so I'm weighing this against full-time options."

---

## 7. Other terms to secure

- **Minimum commitment:** a floor of hours or days per month, so "part-time" doesn't shrink to nothing.
- **Notice period:** 30 days both ways for a contractor agreement.
- **Non-compete:** none, or narrowly scoped to direct competitors of the specific portfolio companies. Fractional work depends on being able to take other clients.
- **IP:** work product goes to the client company; pre-existing tools, templates and prompt libraries stay mine.
- **Expenses:** travel to in-person meetings reimbursed. The annual Philippines trip should be fully paid and billable, or clearly optional.
- **Liability:** cap it at fees paid, especially for investment audits, where an investor might later rely on the report.
- **Payment terms:** 30 days, monthly invoicing, CHF.
