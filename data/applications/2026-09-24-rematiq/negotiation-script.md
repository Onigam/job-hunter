# Negotiation Script: Principal Software Engineer, Platform @ REMATIQ (Berlin)

## Context
- REMATIQ GmbH, Berlin. Founded in 2023 by David Boutellier and Florian Scherer. AI-native product compliance for MedTech (MDR, FDA).
- Funding: €5.4M seed led by Project A Ventures (April 2025), with Amino Collective, HelloWorld and angels including Boris Lokschin. Seventeen months after that round they're probably preparing a Series A or already raising one. Ask about runway.
- Role: Principal IC, backend-heavy TypeScript, hybrid with **4 days a week in the office in Berlin-Mitte**. No salary range in the posting. 186 applicants in three days, so expect a competitive process.
- **The key point:** this is a German role paid in EUR, and you live in Geneva. The usual "Geneva is expensive" argument works *against* you here: they'll pay Berlin rates, and Berlin rates are well below Geneva. Your negotiation is about (a) the Principal level, (b) relocation or commuting costs, (c) VSOP terms, and (d) fewer days on site, not about cost of living.

## Your target

- **Walk-away:** €125k base, VSOP of at least 0.4% on a standard 4-year vest with a 1-year cliff, fair good-leaver terms, and a relocation package. Below that, leaving a Swiss salary doesn't make financial sense.
- **Target:** €140k base, VSOP of 0.6% to 0.8%, €10k to 15k relocation (or a commuting budget if the in-office days drop), and a written salary review when the Series A closes.
- **Opening anchor:** €150k base and 1% VSOP. Justify it with 17 years of experience, having built a platform from zero to an acquisition by a unicorn, and a Principal scope that includes setting the technical roadmap with the founders.

## Script: when they ask for your expectations

"I've looked at where Principal roles sit in Berlin, and I also know I'd be taking a pay cut compared with Geneva. I'm fine with that for the right company. For a Principal role that shapes the roadmap with David and Florian, I'm thinking around €150k base, with meaningful VSOP, somewhere near 1%. I'm flexible on how cash and equity are split. Can you tell me how you've set the band for this role, and how the VSOP pool is structured?"

Then stop talking and let them answer first.

## Counter-offer script: if they come in low

"Thanks, I appreciate the offer. Where it lands on base is closer to a senior engineer band in Berlin, and what we've discussed is a Principal scope: roadmap ownership, architecture calls on a compliance-critical backend, and raising the bar across the team. I can get closer to your number if the equity reflects that. At €[their number], I'd need [X]% VSOP, a relocation budget, and a written review at the Series A. Would that work?"

If they won't move on base: "Understood. Then let's look at the other levers: VSOP percentage, the leaver terms, relocation, and the on-site days. I'd rather find a package that works than keep going back and forth on one number."

## VSOP and equity notes (German startups are different)

Most Berlin startups grant **VSOP (virtual shares)**, not real options. Before you sign, get answers to these questions:

1. **What percentage of fully diluted capital** does the grant represent, and what's the current strike or base value? A number of units means nothing without the total.
2. **Payout triggers:** usually only an exit (sale or IPO). Ask whether there are secondary or buyback windows.
3. **Leaver clauses:** good leaver vs bad leaver. Push for vested units to stay yours as a good leaver, without a steep decline after you leave (some German VSOPs cut vested units over time after departure).
4. **Vesting:** 4 years with a 1-year cliff is standard. Ask for acceleration on change of control (double trigger).
5. **Dilution:** VSOPs usually dilute along with real shares. Ask how the pool gets refreshed in the Series A.
6. **Tax:** VSOP payouts are taxed as income in Germany. If you stay a Swiss tax resident for a while, get advice on how that works across borders.

## Location and relocation (your strongest non-cash lever)

Four days a week in Berlin is a real ask for someone in Geneva. Options, from best to worst for you:

1. **Hybrid from Geneva:** one week a month in Berlin, or two days a week, with travel paid. The Swiss timezone is identical, and your Staff-level remote experience at Benevity backs this up.
2. **Staged relocation:** 3 to 6 months commuting weekly with travel and accommodation covered, then a full move.
3. **Full relocation:** €10k to 15k package, or covered costs, plus temporary housing for the first 1 to 2 months.

Script: "I'd rather sort out location openly now. Four days a week in Berlin means relocating. I'm open to talking about that, but it has a cost, and for a Principal role I'd also like to understand how firm the four days are. Would a model like one week a month on site work for you, at least for the first six months?"

## Using a competing offer (template, only use it if it's true)

"I want to be transparent: I'm in late-stage conversations with a Swiss company for a Staff role, and their offer is higher on cash. REMATIQ is my first choice because of the problem and the stage. If we can get to [target] on base and [X]% VSOP, I'm ready to commit this week."

Never make up a competing offer. If there isn't one, say "I'm in other processes" and leave it there.

## Questions that improve your position

- "What's the runway after the seed, and where are you on the Series A?"
- "Who does this role report to, and how will the technical roadmap be decided between product, ML and founders?"
- "What would a great first six months look like?" Their answer becomes the basis for your review at the Series A.
