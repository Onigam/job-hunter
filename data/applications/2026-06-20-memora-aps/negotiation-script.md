# Negotiation Script — Memora ApS Founding Engineer

---

## Market Context

Memora advertises "$100,000 or more USD" with "senior IC salary (location-adjusted)." The location-adjusted clause is double-edged: it can mean a Geneva premium or a Copenhagen-parity reduction. Geneva is one of the most expensive cities in Europe, with senior engineer compensation benchmarks well above any "location discount" logic.

**Geneva market anchors (2025-2026):**
- Senior Staff Engineer (IC, 15+ years): CHF 160,000-200,000 base
- Founding Engineer at a Series A-stage startup with meaningful equity: CHF 140,000-170,000 base + 0.5-2% options
- $100k USD = ~CHF 88,000 at current rates, which is below the Geneva junior engineer median

The posted floor ($100k USD) is likely the floor for lower cost-of-living markets. Geneva justifies a significant premium.

**Target range:** CHF 155,000-170,000 base (approximately $175,000-195,000 USD), with equity negotiated separately.

---

## Opening Anchor

When they ask for your salary expectation, give a range anchored high and anchor it to Geneva specifically:

> "Based on Geneva market rates for a Staff-level founding engineer role, I'm looking at CHF 155,000-170,000 base. I understand you list a location-adjusted salary, and Geneva sits near the top of European cost-of-living indices. I'm happy to discuss the equity component separately since that's part of what makes a founding-engineer role meaningful at this stage."

If they push back on the range being high:

> "I've looked at comparable founding engineer roles in Swiss tech and at Series A-stage companies with European ops. CHF 155k is the floor for someone with 17 years of experience, two platform builds, and a previous acquisition. I'm not anchored to a specific number, but I'd want to understand how you think about Geneva cost of living before we narrow down."

---

## If They Come In Low (at or near $100k USD)

Do not accept without exploring. Acknowledge, then redirect to the full package:

> "I appreciate you putting a number on the table. At CHF 88,000 equivalent, that's below what I need to make the move work from Geneva. Before we get stuck on base, can you tell me more about the equity structure? If the equity is meaningful, I can look at the total compensation picture differently."

If equity alone doesn't bridge the gap:

> "My floor for a founding engineer commitment in Geneva is CHF 135,000 base. Below that, the math doesn't work regardless of equity. If the base can move to at least CHF 140,000, I'm confident we can find a number that works on both sides."

---

## Equity Negotiation

For a 5-person startup, a founding-engineer grant should be meaningful. Standard range for employee #6-10 at seed/early Series A: 0.25-1.5% fully diluted.

**Questions to ask before committing:**

1. What's the current fully diluted cap table, and what percentage does this grant represent?
2. Is this in options or restricted shares? What's the strike price relative to the last 409A valuation?
3. Standard 4-year vest, 1-year cliff? Any acceleration on change of control?
4. Have you raised external capital? What's the current stage and runway?
5. Are there any anti-dilution provisions for early employees in a down round?

**If the equity is below 0.3% fully diluted:**

> "For an engineering employee at this stage and team size, I'd expect something in the 0.5-0.75% range. If the base is also below CHF 140k, the total package gets difficult to justify against my current comp. Is there flexibility on the equity size?"

**On vesting language:**

> "One thing I care about: double-trigger acceleration if there's an acquisition. I've been through an acquisition (Benevity/Alaya), and I know how those situations play out for equity holders. Is that already in your standard grant?"

---

## Geographic Premium Argument

If they push the "location-adjusted means we pay Copenhagen rates" angle:

> "I understand the logic of location-adjusted comp, but Geneva doesn't work the same way as a remote-friendly lower-cost market. A comparable engineer in Berlin or Warsaw makes sense to comp differently than Geneva. Geneva income taxes, health insurance, and housing costs run ~40% higher than Copenhagen. If the goal is location-adjusted, Geneva should actually be at or above Copenhagen, not below it."

Supporting data points (mention casually, don't lecture):
- Geneva ranked #2-3 most expensive city in Europe consistently (Mercer, ECA)
- Swiss income taxes vary by canton; Geneva canton is mid-to-high
- Mandatory health insurance (Krankenkasse) for a family: CHF 800-1,200/month employee-side

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

If you have other offers in process, use them naturally:

> "I'm talking to a couple of other companies at the moment, one of which has come in at [X]. I'm more interested in Memora because of the scope and stage, but I'd need the comp to be in the same territory to make that call."

If you don't have a competing offer, don't fabricate one. Instead:

> "My current comp at Benevity is [X]. I'm not leaving for a pay cut, so I need to at least match it, and ideally improve on it in either base or equity."

---

## Benefits to Clarify

The listing says "Danish-equivalent benefits." Clarify what that means in practice for a Geneva-based remote employee:

1. Who pays Swiss mandatory health insurance contributions (if any employer contribution)?
2. Pension/retirement contributions: Danish model includes ATP contributions; confirm if anything comparable exists for CH employees
3. Annual leave: confirm 5 weeks minimum (Swiss legal standard is 4, Danish norm is 6)
4. Equipment budget and home office setup
5. Quarterly offsites: travel budget, work visas if needed

---

## Walk-Away Point

If base cannot reach CHF 130,000 and equity is below 0.4% fully diluted, the total package is below market for this profile and role scope. At that level, politely decline and stay in touch:

> "I really like the team and what you're building. The comp doesn't work at this stage, but I'd genuinely be open to revisiting if the situation changes, either on your end or mine."
