# Negotiation Script - Founding Engineer, Applied Labs

Context: seed-stage (raised $5.2M, led by Abstract with Point72 Ventures, Outlander, and Tetra), founded January 2024 by ex-Scale AI / ex-Canal operators, New York-based with remote policy unconfirmed. Founding-engineer scope on agent orchestration, tool-use, planning, memory, and evals. At this stage comp is usually base-plus-equity with real room to move on the equity side, less on cash since seed runway is finite.

## Market anchors

- **US seed-stage founding engineer (NYC, comparable seed $)**: base roughly $170k-$220k, equity 0.75%-2.0% depending on how early and how much they need this hire specifically. Founding engineer #1-3 at a $5M seed sits toward the higher end of that equity range.
- **Switzerland reference (Geneva Staff/Principal equivalent)**: CHF 150k-190k base is the going rate for Staff-level product engineering in Geneva, but that reference matters less here since Applied Labs is pricing in USD against a US market, not a Swiss one. Use it to justify why a "remote discount" doesn't apply, not as the primary anchor.
- **Blended approach**: anchor near the top of their internal band for this seat (likely $190k-$210k base if it exists) plus equity at the higher end of what a $5.2M seed can offer a technical founding hire, since the role description reads as one of the first 3-5 engineering hires.

## Opening anchor

"This scope is a good match for me, owning orchestration, planning and evals end to end is the kind of ownership I built into Alaya from day one, and I think that's worth pricing at the top of what a founding engineer role like this typically carries. Based on comparable seed-stage founding engineer offers I've seen recently, that's around $200k base with equity in the 1.0%-1.5% range, four-year vest with a one-year cliff. Does that fit inside what you've budgeted for this seat?"

## If they come in low

"I hear you on the seed-stage constraints, that's normal at this stage. Can you walk me through what's driving the number, is it the cash budget or how the equity pool is split across the founding team? If cash is the constraint, I'd rather take more of the difference in equity than leave it on the table entirely. If equity is the constraint, let's talk about accelerated vesting on a change of control, or revisiting the grant after the first funding milestone."

Counter-offer template: "I can work with a base around [their number + 10-15%] if the equity moves up to [target%], with a standard 4-year vest, 1-year cliff, and single-trigger acceleration on acquisition, given this is a founding-level seat."

## Equity specifics to nail down

- Confirm the fully diluted share count, not just the percentage, an option grant quoted against a small denominator looks bigger than it is.
- Ask about the strike price and whether there's an early-exercise option (83(b) election matters here given the tax treatment).
- Push for a 1-year cliff, standard 4-year vest, and ask directly whether there's any anti-dilution protection for early employee grants through future rounds, most seed-stage companies won't offer it but it costs nothing to ask.
- Ask what happens to unvested equity on termination without cause, and whether there's a post-termination exercise window longer than the default 90 days, some later-stage-minded startups now offer 5-10 years.

## Geographic / remote pushback

If they try to price the role down because I'm in Geneva rather than New York: "The role's scoped as fully remote in the posting, and Geneva isn't a low-cost location, cost of living here runs above most US metros. I'd expect comp to track the seniority and scope of the role rather than my zip code, especially since the work itself, agent reliability and evals, doesn't change based on where I'm sitting."

## Competing offer as a bargaining chip (use only if genuinely applicable)

"I'm in later-stage conversations with another company at a similar stage, and their offer is in a similar range on cash but light on equity. I'd rather join Applied Labs given the scope and the team's background, so if there's room to close that equity gap, I'm ready to move quickly."

## Close

"I want this to work for both of us. If we can land around [base] and [equity%] with the vesting terms above, I'm ready to sign and start conversations about the first 90 days."
