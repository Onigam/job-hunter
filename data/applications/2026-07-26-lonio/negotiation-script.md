# Negotiation Script: Founding Engineer, Lonio

## Market Context

**Role level:** First engineer post-founders, effectively founding/Staff-level scope despite the "founding engineer" title
**Location:** Zürich, Switzerland (on-site or heavy in-person expected for a team this size)
**Company profile:** Spin-off of BLP Digital, which raised $50M from Goldman Sachs on profitable growth and 450+ corporate customers. Lonio itself already has a working MVP and paying Swiss SME customers, priced at CHF 10-20 per employee per month against a CHF 3B annual outsourcing market. This is not a pre-revenue seed bet; it inherits real backing and a proven playbook from the parent company, which matters for how hard to push on cash comp.

**Zürich market benchmarks, Senior/Staff engineer with founding-engineer scope (2025-2026):**
- P50 Senior Engineer, Zürich tech: CHF 130,000 - 150,000 base
- P75 Staff-level or founding-engineer scope: CHF 155,000 - 180,000 base
- First-engineer hires at well-capitalized spin-offs (parent company profitable, institutional backing): up to CHF 190,000 base, since the risk profile is closer to a well-funded Series A than a true seed
- Your target band: **CHF 160,000 - 185,000 base**

Note the framing gap: "founding engineer" job titles at true zero-revenue seed startups often come with depressed base pay because the risk is real and unpriced. That discount doesn't fully apply here. BLP Digital's Goldman Sachs round and Lonio's existing paying customers are evidence the downside case is narrower than the title suggests, so anchor accordingly.

---

## Your Leverage

1. **Founding-engineer experience that already produced an exit.** Alaya went from a blank page to acquisition by a $1B+ acquirer; this isn't a hypothetical "I could build this," it's a track record of having done it once already.
2. **Platform experience at both ends of the company lifecycle.** Zero-to-one at Alaya, then integrating into a much larger org post-acquisition at Benevity. Lonio needs someone who can build the initial architecture and also anticipate what it looks like once the company scales past the first ten people.
3. **AI-augmented engineering practice, not just AI-curious.** Lonio's philosophy is that AI amplifies talent; driving AI tooling adoption across two teams at Benevity without formal authority is a concrete demonstration of that, not a claim.
4. **Parent company de-risking.** BLP Digital's Goldman Sachs backing and Lonio's existing customer base are legitimate reasons to push above typical first-engineer comp, since the equity is less speculative than a standard seed-stage grant.
5. **Swiss-based, no relocation or permit friction.** Removes a variable Lonio explicitly has to manage for other EU/EFTA candidates needing B permit sponsorship.

## Opening Anchor

When asked "What are your compensation expectations?":

> "Given the founding-engineer scope of this role and the fact that Lonio isn't starting from zero, there's a working MVP, paying customers, and a parent company that just closed a $50 million round, I'm targeting base compensation in the CHF 170,000 to 180,000 range, plus a meaningful equity grant given I'd be the first engineer. I'm flexible on the split between base and equity. What range are you working with?"

**Why this works:**
- Names the "this isn't a typical seed bet" argument directly instead of assuming they'll connect BLP Digital's backing to Lonio's risk profile themselves
- Anchors near the top of the realistic band
- Signals equity is expected, not a bonus, without giving a number yet

I was expecting something closer to CHF 165,000-175,000 base. Is there room to move there, or is equity where you have more flexibility?"

"That's closer to where I need to be. I'd want to land around CHF 175,000 to reflect the founding-engineer scope. If base is capped there, let's talk about the equity grant, since as the first engineer I'd expect that to carry real weight in the total package."

"Understood, then let's focus on equity and the rest of the package. Given I'd be the first engineer after the founders, I want to make sure the equity reflects that, not a standard early-employee grant. Can you walk me through the option pool size and what a first-engineer grant typically looks like here?"

## Equity Notes (First Engineer, Post-Founders)

**What to ask for:**
- Target: 0.75-2.0% fully diluted (FD), meaningfully above a typical employee #5-10 grant, since the posting explicitly frames this as the first engineer hire
- Cliff: standard 1-year; push back on anything longer
- Vesting: 4-year, monthly after cliff
- Acceleration: ask for single-trigger acceleration on change of control given the parent company's M&A history (BLP Digital itself could be an acquirer or acquisition target down the line)

**Key questions to ask:**
- "What's the current fully diluted share count, and is that pre- or post- the option pool refresh?"
- "What strike price are options issued at today, and has there been a recent 409A or Swiss equivalent valuation?"
- "Is Lonio's cap table separate from BLP Digital's, or is this equity in the parent company?" (Important: confirm which entity the equity is actually in before comparing numbers to any BLP Digital benchmarks.)
- "What's the plan for follow-on grants as the team grows past the first few hires?"

**If they give a share count instead of a percentage:**
Calculate: (your grant / total FD shares) x 100 = your percentage. Get this in writing before signing anything.

**Red flags:**
- Equity offered only in a holding entity with no clarity on how it relates to Lonio's actual cap table
- No disclosed share count or valuation basis
- Vesting cliff longer than 12 months
- No acceleration clause given the parent company's active M&A profile

## Non-Cash Package Notes

- The posting mentions a Claude Max subscription as a perk; treat this as a signal they're serious about AI-augmented workflows in practice, not just in messaging, but don't let it substitute for base or equity in the actual negotiation.
- B permit sponsorship is listed for EU/EFTA candidates; not applicable here, but worth confirming there's no unintended pay discount tied to that assumption.
- Zürich cost of living is comparable to or higher than Geneva; there's no geographic discount to push back on here, unlike roles anchored to lower-cost hubs.

## Competing Offer Leverage

If in active conversations elsewhere:

"I want to be transparent: I'm in later-stage conversations with another company right now. Lonio is my preference given the stage and the founding-engineer scope, but I'd need to move on a timeline of about two weeks to keep things fair to everyone."

If no competing offer: don't invent one. Use timeline framing instead: "I'm actively interviewing elsewhere and would like to close this out within the next couple of weeks."

---

## Non-Negotiables to Protect

- Base no lower than CHF 150,000 given the founding-engineer scope and BLP Digital's backing
- Equity grant clearly documented against Lonio's own cap table, not a vague reference to the parent company
- 1-year cliff maximum, with single-trigger acceleration language in writing before signing
