# Negotiation Script: Co-Founding CTO, pre-seed AI smart-home platform (via Clera)

## Context before talking numbers

A few facts shape this whole negotiation:

1. **Clera is an intermediary, not the employer.** Clera is an AI talent-agent startup (Munich roots, $3M pre-seed) that places candidates with startups. The actual company is an unnamed pre-seed smart-home/energy startup. First goal: get the company name, the founders' names and a direct call with them. Don't negotiate equity through a recruiter.
2. **The posting is Munich, mostly on-site, relocation required, no visa sponsorship.** No visa is needed (EU/Swiss free movement), but a move from Geneva to Munich is a real cost and a real decision. The package has to be priced with that in mind.
3. **Posted package: €60k-100k base plus "up to 5%" equity on an earn-in.** For a genuine co-founder seat at pre-seed, 5% is low. It's closer to a first-employee or late-joining CTO grant. The equity conversation matters more than the salary one here.
4. **Pre-seed means the salary may depend on a round that hasn't closed yet.** Ask how much runway exists and whether the salary starts now or on closing.

Questions for the first founder call:
- "How much have you raised, from whom, and how many months of runway does that give at the salaries you're planning?"
- "What does the cap table look like today, and how much is set aside for the pool?"
- "Is the equity real shares in the GmbH/UG, or virtual shares (VSOP)?"
- "What does 'earn-in' mean here: time-based vesting, milestones, or both? Who decides whether a milestone is met?"
- "How strict is on-site in Munich, and how does Mallorca fit in? Is there a pilot there?"
- "Do you have hardware in customer homes today, and who built it?"

## Market reference (estimates, verify before the call)

Rough ranges from general 2025-2026 market knowledge. Check against the Index Ventures OptionPlan data, Carta/Ledgy startup benchmarks, the Swissdevjobs salary report, levels.fyi, and ideally one or two founders in the Munich ecosystem (UnternehmerTUM, Start Munich).

| Reference | Base salary (approx.) | Equity (approx.) |
|---|---|---|
| Co-founding CTO, pre-seed, Munich | €70k-100k | 10-25% if truly a co-founder, founder-style vesting |
| Late-joining CTO (after founders, pre-seed/seed), DACH | €90k-120k | 3-8% |
| Founding engineer, Munich seed | €75k-100k | 0.5-2% |
| CTO/Head of Eng, seed startup, Geneva or Zürich | CHF 150k-190k | 2-8% |
| Senior Staff Engineer, Swiss scale-up (my current reference) | CHF 190k-230k | options, smaller |

The honest comparison: this role pays roughly half of my Swiss cash market in exchange for founder-level equity. That trade only makes sense if the equity really is founder-level.

**My numbers**

- **Opening anchor:** €100k base (top of their band), 10% equity in real shares, 4-year vesting with a 1-year cliff.
- **Target:** €90k-100k base, 7-10% in real shares.
- **Walk-away:** under €85k base combined with 5% or less, or equity only as VSOP with a milestone earn-in the founders judge alone. At that point it's a below-market employee role with co-founder workload.
- **Cash-light alternative:** if they can't pay €90k+ before the round closes, accept a lower base (€70k-75k) only with a written step-up to €100k on closing the seed round, and a larger equity grant (10%+) to compensate.

## Opening anchor script

> "Before we get into numbers, I want to make sure we're describing the same role. If I'm the technical co-founder, owning architecture, the team, the roadmap and the investor story, then I'd expect founder-level terms. For me that means €100k base, which is the top of your range, and around 10% in real shares, vesting over four years with a one-year cliff like everyone else at the table. That's well below what I'd earn in Switzerland, and I'm fine with that, because the equity is where I'd be making my bet."

Say it, then stop.

## If they come in low (for example €70k and 3-5%)

> "I understand cash is tight at pre-seed, and I'm not going to argue much on base. The equity is the part I can't accept as it is. At 5% with an earn-in, I'd be taking on a co-founder's risk and workload on terms closer to an early employee's. Here's what would work: we meet at €90k, the equity goes to at least 8% in real shares on standard four-year vesting, and base steps up to €100k when the seed round closes. If the equity has to stay lower, then the salary has to be much closer to market, and at that point we're really talking about a VP Engineering hire, not a co-founder."

If they don't move, trade in this order:
1. Equity percentage and form (real shares over VSOP), because it's worth the most.
2. Vesting terms (acceleration, good-leaver definition, see below).
3. Written salary step-up on closing the next round.
4. Relocation support and a transition period (see below).
5. Title and board seat or observer rights.

## Equity details to get right

- **Real shares vs VSOP.** In a German GmbH, founders hold real shares (notarised transfer). VSOPs are cash bonuses that only pay out on exit, with no voting rights and usually worse tax treatment. A co-founder should hold real shares. If they insist on VSOP, it isn't really a co-founder offer.
- **Join before the valuation rises.** Buying real shares at nominal value is only possible while the company is still valued low. After a priced round, receiving shares below market value can be taxed as income in Germany, so timing matters. Get a German tax advisor to check this before signing; it can change the numbers a lot.
- **Vesting.** Ask for the same schedule as the existing founders: 4 years, 1-year cliff, monthly after that. Milestone-based "earn-in" is only acceptable if the milestones are written down, measurable and not decided by the other founders alone.
- **Good leaver / bad leaver.** Bad leaver should mean cause (fraud, serious breach), not "we disagreed on strategy." Good leaver keeps vested shares at fair value.
- **Acceleration.** Ask for double-trigger acceleration (change of control plus termination) on at least 50% of unvested shares.
- **Anti-dilution.** Founders don't normally get anti-dilution protection at pre-seed, and asking for it looks naive. Ask for pro-rata information rights and to be part of every future financing discussion instead, plus a board seat or observer seat.
- **Shareholder agreement.** Ask to see the existing shareholders' agreement and any convertible notes or SAFEs before signing. Convertibles still to come can dilute more than the headline cap table suggests.

## Pushing back on location (Geneva to Munich)

The posting says relocation is required. Know what you want before the call; don't improvise here.

> "I'm based in Geneva. Moving is possible, but it's a big decision and I'd rather do it properly. What I'd propose is a transition: three to four months where I'm in Munich two or three days a week and the rest from Geneva, with travel covered, then a full move once we've both confirmed it's working. I'd also want a relocation package to cover the move itself."

Things to ask for:
- A relocation allowance (€5k-10k is reasonable even at pre-seed, or a signing amount paid on arrival).
- Travel costs covered during the transition period.
- Clarity on Mallorca: how often, who pays.

Also check before accepting: German income tax plus social charges are higher than in Geneva, and Munich rents are high. Run a net-income comparison (Geneva vs Munich at €90k-100k) so the real cut is visible before agreeing to anything.

## Referencing other options (template)

Use only if something real is in play. Never invent a number.

> "To be transparent, I'm also in conversations for Staff-level roles in Switzerland, where the cash is roughly double what we're discussing. I'm not asking you to match that, and I wouldn't be having this conversation if the company didn't interest me. But it's the reason the equity has to carry real weight, and why I need the terms to look like a co-founder's."

## Before signing

- Meet every founder at least twice, including once in person, and ideally spend a working day together on a real problem.
- Speak to at least one investor, if there are any.
- Get the shareholders' agreement, the cap table and the vesting terms reviewed by a German startup lawyer and a tax advisor (cross-border: Swiss departure and German arrival).
- Get the salary step-up, relocation terms and title in writing.
