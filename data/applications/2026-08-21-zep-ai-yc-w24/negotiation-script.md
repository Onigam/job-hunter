# Negotiation Script: Zep AI (YC W24), Staff Engineer

Context to keep in mind going in: the posting discloses a base range of $175,000 to $250,000 with 1.00% to 1.50% equity, for San Francisco or Remote (US). It also lists US citizenship or visa sponsorship as a requirement, and I'm based in Geneva, so the location question needs resolving before comp does. This script assumes the process gets far enough to reach that conversation, and treats the disclosed band as the primary anchor rather than trying to argue past it given the location mismatch works against me.

## Market context

- The disclosed $175k-$250k band is Zep's own stated range for this level, likely Staff/L5-equivalent, in a company of roughly YC W24 vintage with named investors including Engineering Capital. Total comp with 1.00%-1.50% equity at this stage can swing widely depending on the last priced round; ask for the 409A valuation before treating the equity number as fixed.
- Geneva-based comp for a comparable Staff Engineer role runs roughly CHF 170,000-220,000 base, but that figure only matters if the role converts to a genuine remote-from-Switzerland arrangement, since the posted band is tied to US employment and possibly sponsorship.
- If visa sponsorship or relocation to SF is the only path forward, use the disclosed range as the anchor and negotiate inside it. If remote-from-Europe is workable, expect a different conversation entirely, closer to an international contractor or EOR arrangement, and ask what that would look like early rather than assuming it maps onto the posted band.

## Opening position

"Thanks for sharing the range. Before we get into a number, I want to make sure the location question is settled, since I'm in Geneva and the posting points toward SF or US remote. If a European arrangement works, I'd want to understand how that maps to the posted band before anchoring anywhere. If US-based employment is a hard requirement, I'd want to be talking near the top of the disclosed range, around $240,000, given the platform and AI-integration experience I'd bring even with a short ramp on Go."

## If they come in low, or the location question resolves unfavorably

"I understand the geography adds friction here, sponsorship, time zones, or both. I'd expect that to show up somewhere in the process, but I'd rather it show up as a real conversation about what arrangement works than a lower number with no explanation. If equity is meant to offset a lower base, I'd want the vesting schedule and strike price in front of me before comparing it to a straight base figure."

Key move: don't let the location gap silently become a comp discount without naming it directly.

## Equity and vesting questions to ask before agreeing to anything

- Last priced round and current 409A valuation.
- Standard 4-year vest with a 1-year cliff, or something non-standard for this stage.
- Refresh policy at year 2, or only on promotion.
- Double-trigger acceleration on acquisition, worth asking given I was on the acquired side once already at Alaya.
- ISOs versus RSUs, and the post-departure exercise window.

## Relocation and remote framing

If SF relocation with sponsorship is required: ask for a relocation package, flights, shipping, temporary housing for four to six weeks, as a separate line item from base, and confirm the sponsorship timeline in writing before treating an offer as final.

If remote-from-Switzerland is genuinely on the table: ask directly whether the disclosed band even applies, or whether international hires run through a different comp model entirely. Better to know that in week one of negotiation than to anchor on a number that was never actually available to a non-US remote hire.

## Competing offer template (use only if real)

"I'm in later-stage conversations with another company for a comparable role, and comp there is tracking toward [$X]. I'd rather make this work here if the numbers can get close, since the problem you're solving is the one I actually want, but they need to be in the same neighborhood."

Only use this if there's an actual competing process in flight.

## Closing line for any round

"I want this to work for both of us. Send me something in writing and I'll turn it around quickly."
