# Negotiation Script: Eatomics (Technical Co-Founder / CTO)

## Context to keep in mind
This isn't a normal salary negotiation. Eatomics is pre-incorporation, single-founder (Luca Hagenmayer), and the posting is explicit that equity is "pending legal incorporation" with no cash number attached. The requirements listed (internship-level web dev, "familiarity" with cloud platforms) are junior-level, while the title is Technical Co-Founder/CTO with full ownership of architecture and hiring. That gap is an opening: Magino isn't negotiating against a budgeted senior-hire salary, he's negotiating a founder-equity split against someone who posted a junior req for a CTO-shaped hole. The conversation should stay equity-and-terms first, cash second, and should surface early whether there's any capital in the company at all.

## Switzerland market anchors
- Senior/Staff Engineer, Switzerland (Geneva/Zurich): CHF 150k-190k base, non-founder roles.
- CTO / VP Engineering at a funded early-stage startup (seed to Series A), Switzerland: CHF 160k-230k base once salaries start, plus 1-8% equity depending on stage and capital contributed.
- Pre-seed/pre-incorporation technical co-founder, no funding yet: cash comp is typically zero to minimal (stipend or deferred), and the real number under negotiation is the equity split, not salary.
- For reference, Magino's Alaya exit (co-founder, built and sold to a $1B+ acquirer) is a stronger comp anchor than any salary survey: it's proof the founder-equity bet has paid off once already.

## Opening anchor
Lead with the equity split and governance terms before cash, since there's no funding yet to negotiate cash against.

> "Before we talk numbers, I want to understand where the company actually is: is there a cap table yet, any capital in, any other technical work done? Assuming this is genuinely ground floor, I'd want to talk about this as a co-founder split, not an early-employee grant. Given I'd be the sole technical co-founder taking on architecture, hiring, and delivery, I'd expect something in the 15-30% range depending on what capital and traction already exist on your side, standard four-year vesting with a one-year cliff, and a real say in product and hiring decisions, not just execution."

## If they come in low (e.g. under 10% equity, or "sweat equity, we'll discuss numbers later")
> "I get that everything's early and nothing's formalized yet, that's normal at this stage. But I'd rather nail down the split and vesting now, on paper, than build for a few months on a handshake. If you're not ready to commit to a real number, I'm open to a short paid trial period, say four to six weeks, scoped to a specific deliverable, with the co-founder conversation happening once we both know it's working. What I won't do is start building the actual platform on a vague 'we'll figure out equity later.'"

## Equity-specific terms to nail down
- **Vesting:** four years, one-year cliff, standard for both founders (ask if Luca's shares vest too; if only the technical co-founder vests, that's a red flag).
- **Cliff protection:** what happens if Eatomics fails to raise within 12-18 months, does unvested equity have any acceleration on acquisition or does it just evaporate.
- **Anti-dilution:** ask how future funding rounds affect the split, and whether there's a pro-rata right to maintain percentage in the first priced round.
- **Founder agreement:** get the split, vesting, and IP assignment in writing (even a simple founder agreement) before writing production code, not after.
- **IP:** confirm all code and architecture decisions are assigned to the company, not left ambiguous, since that becomes a fundraising blocker later if unresolved.

## Cash / stipend
Since the posting implies no salary yet, the ask here is really about runway and expectations, not a number.

> "If there's no cash comp until you raise, I need to know that going in and plan around it. Once you do raise, I'd want a CTO-level base built into the round, in the CHF 160k-200k range for Switzerland, reviewed at the raise, not left informal."

## Geographic angle
Eatomics lists remote or Zurich/St. Gallen, so the usual Geneva cost-of-living pushback doesn't really apply here, remote and Zurich are both fine anchors. If they try to lowball based on "remote is cheaper," the response is that co-founder equity isn't priced by geography, it's priced by contribution and risk carried.

> "Remote works for me, but the equity split shouldn't move based on where I'm sitting. What matters is what I'm building and how much risk I'm taking on before there's funding."

## Competing offer as a bargaining chip (use if applicable)
> "I'm currently Senior Staff at Benevity, on a stable comp package with no pre-funding risk. Walking away from that to build something from zero only makes sense if the equity and terms reflect that I'm the one taking the real bet here. I've done this exact trade once before, at Alaya, and it worked out. I want the terms to look like that this time too."

## Walk-away line
> "I'd rather find out now that the terms don't work than three months into building the platform. If we can't agree on a real split and it in writing, I don't think this is the right fit for either of us."
