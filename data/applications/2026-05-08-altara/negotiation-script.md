# Negotiation Script — Altara Founding Engineer

## Context

- **Listed range:** $140k-$200k + equity
- **Role level:** Founding Engineer (Staff/Principal equivalent, pre-Series A)
- **Location:** On-site San Francisco (critical mismatch: Magino is in Geneva)
- **Stage:** Seed (founded 2025, Greylock-backed, Jeff Dean as advisor)
- **Key variables:** Relocation, equity percentage, vesting terms

---

## Market Reference Points

**SF Bay Area — Staff/Senior Staff Engineer (2025-2026):**
- Base: $200k-$280k at established tech companies
- Base: $170k-$230k at well-funded startups
- Founding Engineer at seed stage typically takes a discount on base in exchange for meaningful equity

**Founding Engineer equity norms (Greylock-backed seed):**
- First 1-3 engineers: 0.5%-2.0% typical range
- Higher end (1%+) if joining pre-product or very early traction
- Standard vesting: 4 years, 1-year cliff
- Acceleration on acquisition: double-trigger preferred

**Altara specifics:**
- $140k-$200k listed — the low end is below market; the high end is at market for SF but below top-of-band
- Physics/scientific AI is a specialized domain; that premium is real and worth naming
- Greylock backing is strong signal that a Series A is expected within 12-18 months (comp refresh opportunity)

---

## Before the Offer: Key Questions to Ask

Before any numbers are on the table, get these answered so you can evaluate the full package:

- "What's the current cap table look like, and where does founding engineer equity land?"
- "What's the valuation at last raise, and how much runway do you have?"
- "Is there an option pool expansion expected at Series A, and how does that affect current holders?"
- "What's the vesting schedule, and is there any acceleration on change of control?"
- "What's the relocation support package?"

---

## Relocation (Primary Issue)

The on-site SF requirement is the biggest practical barrier. Address it directly and early.

**If they ask whether you'd relocate:**

"Yes, relocation is something I'm open to for the right opportunity. I'd want to understand the support package. Relocating from Geneva to San Francisco is a significant logistical and financial undertaking: visa, moving costs, housing transition. What does Altara typically offer for international candidates?"

**What to request:**
- Visa sponsorship (they mentioned case-by-case, so confirm this is available)
- Relocation stipend: $15k-$25k is typical for international moves at funded startups
- Temporary housing allowance: 1-3 months
- Timeline flexibility: 3-4 months lead time is reasonable

**If they push back on relocation support:**

"I understand budget is tight at this stage. That said, relocating internationally without support isn't something I can absorb personally. If the full package isn't possible, could we consider a higher base to account for transition costs, or a one-time signing bonus that covers the gap?"

---

## Compensation Anchor

**Opening position:**

"Given my background (17 years, co-founding and scaling a platform to acquisition, staff-level experience at a post-acquisition company), I'm targeting the $220k-$240k range for base. I know that's above the listed band, and I'm open to discussion if the equity is genuinely meaningful."

**Justification points to reference:**
- Staff/Principal experience, not mid-level
- Founding engineer at a previous company (not the first time in this seat)
- AI/agentic systems experience in production (specific to what Altara is building)
- Relocation from Geneva (real cost, not hypothetical)
- Domain scarcity: physical sciences + AI engineering is a small intersection

**If they hold at $200k base:**

"I can work with $200k if the equity is at the higher end of founding engineer range. What I'd need to see is something in the 1%-1.5% range with standard vesting. The base-equity trade-off only makes sense if the equity has real upside, and with Greylock backing and this team, I think it does."

---

## Equity Negotiation

**If they offer below 0.75%:**

"I appreciate the offer. For a founding engineer joining at this stage — pre-product, pre-Series A, on-site SF — the standard range is 0.75%-1.5%. The base is already below market for SF, so the equity is where the risk-adjusted comp needs to be competitive. Is there room to move this to at least 1%?"

**Key terms to push for:**
- Double-trigger acceleration on acquisition (not single-trigger, which only fires if you leave)
- Early exercise option (83(b) election — critical for tax efficiency at seed)
- Anti-dilution protection or pre-emptive rights if offered (uncommon but worth asking)
- Cliff confirmation: standard 1-year, get it in writing

**If they say the cap table is tight:**

"I understand equity is a constrained resource at this stage. If the percentage can't move, could we look at a shorter vesting schedule (3 years instead of 4) or a refresher grant tied to Series A? I want our incentives to be aligned for the long term."

---

## Counter-Offer Script (if initial offer is low)

**Scenario: They offer $180k base + 0.5% equity**

"Thank you for putting a number on the table. I want to be direct: the base is below what I'd need given my level and the relocation costs, and the equity is at the low end for founding engineer. I'm not walking away from this; I think Altara is doing something real. But I'd need to see either $200k+ base with 0.75% equity, or $190k with 1% equity. That's where I can get to yes. What flexibility do you have?"

**If they can only move one lever:**

"If base is fixed at $180k, then I'd need the equity to reflect the full risk of the move, which for me is 1.25%. Alternatively, if equity is fixed at 0.5%, I'd need $215k base plus a relocation package. I'm trying to find the version of this that works for both sides."

---

## If a Competing Offer Exists (or to create pressure)

"I want to be transparent: I'm in late-stage conversations with another company in the Bay Area. Nothing is signed, and my preference is actually Altara given the scientific angle and the founding team's background. But I need to be able to compare real numbers. If you can get me an offer in the next week, I can give you a decision before I need to respond to the other."

---

## Notes on Timing and Process

- At seed stage, founders often have real flexibility but limited cash
- Push for equity over base if you believe in the upside
- Get everything in writing: the option grant number (shares, not just percentage), the current total shares outstanding, the exercise price, the vesting schedule
- Calculate the implied value of the equity at a 10x exit (post-dilution, assuming Series A and B), not just the percentage today. A 1% stake at $10M post-money becomes roughly 0.5%-0.6% post-Series A dilution
- Visa timeline: H-1B cap-subject filings are annual (lottery in March). Alternatives: O-1A (exceptional ability — this profile may qualify), or if the company has a treaty, E-3 (Australia) or TN (Canada/Mexico). Check with an immigration attorney early.
