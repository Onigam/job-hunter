# Negotiation Script: Kensai (Product/Backend Founding Engineer, 1-2% Equity)

## Context that shapes the negotiation

Pre-seed, third technical hire, founding team of a first-time-doctor-founder (Leonie, Switzerland-based longevity clinic background), an ex-Palantir operator (Karina), and one existing engineer (Boe). No cash comp or funding size was disclosed in the posting, which usually means either genuinely early or deliberately vague until they see who's interested. The 1-2% equity band tells us roughly where they're pricing this: real founding equity, but priced as hire three, not co-founder. Role is remote, UK-oriented hours, which matters for the cash conversation since UK and Swiss cost-of-living benchmarks diverge a lot.

## Switzerland market anchors

- Senior backend engineer with founding/0-to-1 experience, Geneva-based, remote for a funded UK or US startup: cash comp is commonly benchmarked lower than a full Swiss-market role since the employer isn't Swiss, but Geneva living costs are a legitimate anchor to raise, expect a realistic range of CHF 110k-150k cash equivalent if the company has any funding at all.
- At true pre-seed with no disclosed raise, cash is often far below that, sometimes CHF 60k-90k or a deferred/reduced-salary arrangement until a seed round closes. Worth asking directly whether they've closed pre-seed funding or are still raising, since that changes what's realistically on the table.
- Equity for a third technical hire at pre-seed: 1-2% as posted is within the normal band for a strong senior hire (not co-founder), so the equity number itself isn't necessarily the fight, vesting terms and strike price are.
- Geneva cost of living runs meaningfully above London and well above most of the UK, worth naming since the role skews UK hours and may default to UK benchmarks.

## Opening anchor

"Given I'd be building the core data infrastructure the reasoning layer depends on, effectively the plumbing the whole product sits on, I'd want to talk cash in the CHF 120k-140k range if the company's funded, or a lower base with a larger equity cushion if we're still pre-close on funding. On equity, I'd want to understand where in the 1-2% band you're thinking and what the fully diluted cap table looks like before we land on a number." Anchor on getting funding status and cap table clarity first, since the right ask depends entirely on which situation this actually is.

## Counter-offer script if they come in low

If cash comes in well under CHF 100k with no funding context given: "I get that pre-seed budgets are tight, and I'm genuinely open to a lower cash number if the equity and vesting reflect the risk I'm taking on as hire three. But I'd want to understand whether that number is because funding hasn't closed yet or because that's the target range regardless, since those are different conversations. If it's the former, I'm fine structuring something that steps up once a round closes."

If equity lands at the bottom of the 1-2% band with a long cliff or unusual vesting: "1% is on the low side for building the ground-truth data layer a health-regulatory AI product depends on, that's core infrastructure, not a support function. I'd want to see something closer to 1.5-2%, standard four-year vesting with a one-year cliff, and clarity on whether there's an ESOP pool carved out before my grant that would dilute it further."

## Equity negotiation notes

- Confirm strike price and 409A-equivalent valuation basis before agreeing to a percentage, a percentage means nothing without knowing the valuation it's priced against.
- Standard four-year vesting, one-year cliff is the floor to ask for, not a concession from them.
- Push for double-trigger acceleration (termination plus change of control), single-trigger is rare this early but worth explicitly ruling out rather than assuming.
- Ask whether any advisor or early-investor allocations sit ahead of the employee pool in a way that would dilute a 1-2% grant meaningfully at the next round.
- Given the role is architecting core data infrastructure rather than a peripheral feature, it's fair to ask whether the equity band flexes for someone who can ship the MVP pipeline solo versus needing significant ramp time.

## Geographic discount pushback

If they anchor on UK or lower-cost-region cash benchmarks: "I know the team's UK-based and the role runs UK hours, so I'm not expecting full Geneva-market cash. But Geneva costs run meaningfully above London, and I'd want the offer to acknowledge that gap rather than default fully to a UK number, even if we land somewhere between the two rather than at the top of the Swiss range."

## Competing offer template

Use only if genuinely applicable: "I'm also further along with a couple of funded platform engineering roles at senior IC levels, so the equity upside and the ground-floor scope here need to clearly outweigh a more conventional offer with disclosed funding behind it. I'd rather get the terms right now than have this stall later over a number we could have settled up front." Keep it factual, a pre-seed founder weighing this against a funded alternative is a real and legitimate comparison, not a bluff, so there's no need to oversell it.
