# Negotiation Script: Mimica Automation, Head of/VP of Engineering

Context: fully remote role, hiring window UTC-5 to UTC+3, so Mimica is benchmarking against a wide geographic spread, likely anchored somewhere between UK (their London HQ) and US remote norms. Series B, $26.1M raised, ~100-200 employees, engineering scaling from 50 to 100+. This is the first Head of Engineering hire, reporting to the CTO and Co-Founder, so the mandate is broad: technical vision, EM coaching, hiring strategy, and process across eight cross-functional teams. That scope is the strongest point in this negotiation, not just the title.

## Market anchors

Head of Engineering / VP Engineering at a Series B company of this size typically lands in a wide band depending on which geography the company benchmarks against. UK-anchored (London HQ default): roughly GBP 150,000 to 190,000 base plus equity. US-anchored, which is common for VC-backed startups whose investors and board comps are US-based even when the team is distributed: roughly USD 220,000 to 270,000 base plus equity. Geneva-equivalent for this seniority, a VP/Head role owning an org of 50 to 100+ engineers, runs CHF 220,000 to 260,000, which converts to a figure on par with or above the US band at current exchange rates. Since Mimica hires across a wide remote window rather than a fixed office location, there's no natural reason to default to the lowest of these three, and the opening anchor should say so explicitly.

## Opening anchor

"Given this is a mandate to build the Head of Engineering function from scratch across eight cross-functional teams during a 50-to-100 scaling curve, I'd frame this as VP-level scope rather than a first-line Director role. For a fully remote setup I'm looking at USD 230,000 to 250,000 base, plus a meaningful equity grant that reflects the ownership this role is being asked to take on process, hiring, and manager development. I'd rather open with the real number than negotiate up from somewhere lower."

## If they come in low

"I understand the internal band may be calibrated to a different geography or to a Director-level scope. Two things push against that: first, the role as described, owning technical vision and process across eight teams plus coaching the EM layer, sits closer to a VP mandate than a standard Head of Engineering slot reporting into someone else's org design. Second, since the team hires across a UTC-5 to UTC+3 window already, geography isn't really the constraint here, scope is. Is there room to move the base toward 235, or close the gap through equity and an earlier compensation review, say at month nine rather than the standard annual cycle?"

## Geographic discount pushback

If Mimica tries to anchor low because the hire is remote from Geneva rather than London: "I'd push back on that gently. Geneva runs on par with or above London for senior technical talent, and being remote doesn't change the scope of the mandate, building the function for an org that's about to double. If the concern is time zone overlap, Geneva sits well inside the window you're already hiring across, so that's not really a factor either."

## Equity negotiation

Series B is earlier-stage than a late unicorn round, so there's real room to negotiate percentage, not just headline value. Ask for:
- The strike price basis from the most recent round, in writing, before agreeing to a number
- A standard four-year vest with a one-year cliff; resist anything longer on the cliff
- Where in the fully-diluted cap table this grant lands, a Head of Engineering hire at Series B with this scope should expect somewhere in the 0.3% to 0.6% range depending on how early the equity conversation happens
- Refresh grant timing and whether early exercise is available, since that matters more at this stage than the headline percentage alone

## Competing offer template

Use only if there's an actual parallel process running; don't invent one.

"I want to be upfront that I'm also further along in a conversation with another company, and the numbers there are close to what I've opened with here. I'd genuinely rather build this function at Mimica given the product and the stage, so if there's room to close that gap, I'd rather spend the next round finalizing details than comparing offers side by side."

## Close

Don't accept verbally on the call. Ask for the written offer, take 48 hours, and confirm base, equity grant size and strike basis, review cycle timing, and remote work terms are all spelled out before responding.
