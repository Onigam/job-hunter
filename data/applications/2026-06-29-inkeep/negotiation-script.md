# Negotiation Script — Inkeep, Member of Technical Staff (Principal TypeScript Engineer)

## Context

Inkeep is a YC-backed, Khosla-backed AI agent platform. The Senior-level posting listed $180K-$250K base with 0.1%-0.4% equity, on-site in San Francisco or New York. The Principal role will be above that range. No published compensation for Principal was available at time of writing.

Magino is based in Geneva. The on-site requirement is a real variable: if they want in-person, the negotiation involves relocation; if they're open to remote for Principal-level, the framing shifts. Clarify location expectations before discussing numbers.

---

## Market Framing

Principal TypeScript engineers at YC-backed companies in SF or NYC with 10+ years experience and a prior exit typically land $240K-$320K base. The Senior ceiling was $250K; Principal is conventionally 15-25% above Senior. The equity range for Senior was 0.1-0.4%; at Principal level, a credible ask is 0.3-0.6%.

Magino's profile adds specific leverage: co-founding experience with a completed acquisition, hands-on AI agent and multi-agent work, and SDK/API design experience that maps directly to what Inkeep builds. That profile is harder to find than a strong Senior.

If they want in-person and Magino relocates, the relocation cost and timezone disruption to personal infrastructure are legitimate negotiating inputs.

---

## Opening Position (On-Site SF or NYC)

**Base:** $275K  
**Equity:** 0.5%  
**Vesting:** 4 years, 1-year cliff, double-trigger acceleration on change of control  
**Relocation:** $15,000-$20,000 lump sum (SF 1BR: $3,200-$4,500/month; first and last plus deposit is the immediate hit)

Script:
> "I've looked at what Principal-level TypeScript engineers are taking in SF for AI developer tools companies at this stage, and I'm targeting $275K base with 0.5% equity. The Senior range you've listed publicly goes to $250K; Principal with my background and the acquisition experience is above that. If relocation is part of the conversation, I'd want to discuss a package to make the move clean."

**Base:** CHF 250,000-280,000 gross annual (Geneva market for Staff+ with 17 years experience is CHF 200K-240K; Inkeep is a US-funded company and should be compensating above local market given the role level)

If you're open to remote for the right candidate, I'd want to make sure the comp reflects the role level rather than being adjusted down for geography. I'm targeting CHF 260K or the USD equivalent, plus 0.5% equity. Geneva is expensive, and the timezone overlap I've been managing for Benevity since 2021 is workable."

Script:

Can we get to $250K base and 0.4% equity? That's the floor that makes sense for the role level and for making the relocation work."

Don't go below $220K base + 0.3% equity for an on-site role requiring relocation from Geneva.

---

## Equity Deep Dive

Questions to ask before signing:

- What is the current 409A valuation and strike price for new grants?
- What is the total option pool as a percentage of fully diluted shares?
- Are these ISOs or NSOs? (ISOs are more favorable for US taxpayers; clarify how they treat international employees or employees who later relocate.)
- Is there double-trigger acceleration on a change of control? Without it, an acquisition before your vest completes leaves you with unvested shares in a company that no longer exists independently.
- What was the last priced round valuation and the liquidation preference structure?
- What is the standard refresh cadence for tenured employees?

Script for equity discussion:
> "The range you've listed for Senior was 0.1-0.4%. I'm expecting to be at the top end for Principal, which I'd put at 0.5%. Can you confirm what that looks like on a fully diluted basis and what the current 409A strike is? I've been through an acquisition, so I'll want to understand the preference stack before signing."

## Geneva-to-SF Location Negotiation (if on-site required)

Magino has been remote since 2021 and has demonstrated cross-timezone effectiveness with US teams. If they're firm on in-person:

1. Ask for a trial: "I'd want to spend time on-site, especially early. Could we do an extended on-site onboarding period of 4-6 weeks before committing to permanent relocation? That's a lower-risk way for both sides to validate the fit."

2. If relocation is required: request $18K-$20K relocation support, and confirm who covers visa sponsorship legal fees.

3. The timezone argument for remote: Geneva is UTC+2 in summer, UTC+1 in winter. US East Coast overlap is 9am-1pm ET (3pm-7pm Geneva), which covers the core sync window. It's a real constraint but a manageable one for a Principal-level IC who attends fewer synchronous meetings than a manager.

## Competing Offer Leverage

If you have another offer:
> "I have another offer at $[X] base with [Y]% equity. I'd prefer Inkeep for the platform engineering focus and the direct exposure to developer tools at scale, but I need the numbers to be in range. Can we get to $275K and 0.5%?"

If no competing offer, use market data:
> "Based on what I've found for Principal TypeScript engineers at YC-backed AI companies in SF, $260K-$280K and 0.4-0.5% is the current market. I'd rather work backward from there than from a number that doesn't reflect the level."

---

## Floor (Walk-Away Point)

On-site SF/NYC: do not accept below $230K base + 0.3% equity + relocation support.

Remote: do not accept below CHF 220,000 gross (or USD equivalent) + 0.3% equity.

Below those floors, the risk-to-reward for leaving Benevity at Staff level isn't justified unless the equity story changes materially — for example, a credible near-term Series B at a valuation that makes 0.3% worth $3M+ on exit.
