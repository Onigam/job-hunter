# Negotiation Script — Momentic, Founding Engineer (Infrastructure)

---

## Market Context

**Company stage:** Post-Series A ($15M raised, Standard Capital lead, YC alumni). Team is ~7-10 engineers. Infrastructure founding engineer is a critical hire at this stage.

**Compensation reference (USD, SF market):**
- Their Frontend Founding Engineer posted: $150K-$220K base
- Infrastructure/Platform roles typically price 10-15% above frontend at this seniority
- Market range for this role in SF: $180K-$240K base + significant equity
- Total comp target (base + equity at current valuation): $250K-$350K+

**Geneva context:** If they offer remote or hybrid to Geneva, note that CHF is strong vs. USD, and Geneva cost-of-living is comparable to SF. Don't accept a geographic discount on a role at this level.

---

## Opening Anchor

If they ask your compensation expectations first, don't give a number. Use this:

> "Before I give you a number, I'd rather understand the total package you're thinking about, base, equity, and vesting structure. Then we can talk about where I land relative to that."

If they push for a number:

> "Based on my background as a founding engineer who's built and scaled a platform through acquisition, and given the market for infrastructure roles at Series A startups in this range, I'm targeting $220K-$240K base with meaningful equity. But I'm more focused on the equity upside at this stage than squeezing the last $10K out of base."

Why this works: you anchor high, signal you're equity-motivated (which founders like), and avoid anchoring too low.

---

## If They Come In Low (below $180K base)

Don't reject it. Ask a question:

> "I appreciate the offer. The base is lower than where I was expecting given my profile and the market comps I've seen for infrastructure roles at this stage. Is there room to move on base, or is the equity structure designed to make up that difference?"

Then make them justify the equity:

> "Can you walk me through the cap table at a high level, current valuation, your strike price, and what the vesting schedule looks like? I want to make sure I understand what the full package represents."

If they say base is fixed but equity is flexible:

> "If the base is at $175K, I'd need the equity to reflect the delta. What would it look like to get to 0.75% or higher, given I'm joining at a critical scaling stage?"

---

## Equity Negotiation

**What to ask for:**
- Founding engineer joining post-Series A: 0.4%-0.8% is a realistic range to negotiate toward
- Standard vesting: 4 years, 1-year cliff. Acceptable.
- Ask for: acceleration on change of control (single-trigger or double-trigger)
- Ask for: early exercise option (83(b) election opportunity, important for US options)
- Ask for: the right to see the capitalization table before signing

**Script:**

> "On equity, I want to understand a few things. What's the current fully-diluted share count and the most recent 409A valuation? And is there any acceleration clause on a change of control?"

If they resist sharing cap table details:

> "I'm not asking to audit the company. I just want to understand what my grant represents in terms of ownership percentage and what scenarios would make it meaningful. That's a standard ask for a founding-level hire."

---

## Geographic Discount Pushback

If they try to apply a Geneva or "remote" discount to the offer:

> "I understand the instinct, but the work is the same regardless of where I sit. My background, the infrastructure I'd be building, and the impact on your reliability and scaling are priced on what the role delivers, not my zip code. Geneva is also one of the most expensive cities in Europe, so a geographic cut would actually be a real reduction in purchasing power, not a neutral adjustment."

If they insist:

> "I'd consider a base closer to that range if the equity is structured to reflect the difference. Let's see if we can make the total package work."

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

If you have other offers in process (or can reference the broader pipeline):

> "I'm in active conversations with a few other companies at similar stages. I'm genuinely more interested in what Momentic is building, but I owe it to myself to make sure the package reflects that. If we can close the gap on [base or equity], I'd rather stop the other processes and focus here."

If you don't have a competing offer but want to signal optionality:

> "I'm being thoughtful about where I land next, given I'm leaving a Senior Staff role. I want to make sure the first conversation I have doesn't set a floor I'd regret."

---

## Timing

Don't accept on the spot. Standard line:

> "I appreciate the offer and I'm genuinely interested. Give me 48 hours to review the full details, and I'll come back to you with any questions or a decision."

This is normal, expected, and signals you're serious rather than desperate.

---

## Summary: What to Target

| Component | Target | Minimum |
|---|---|---|
| Base salary (USD) | $220K-$240K | $185K |
| Equity | 0.6%-0.8% | 0.35% |
| Vesting | 4yr / 1yr cliff | standard |
| Acceleration | Single-trigger on CoC | Double-trigger acceptable |
| Early exercise | Yes (ask for it) | Nice to have |
