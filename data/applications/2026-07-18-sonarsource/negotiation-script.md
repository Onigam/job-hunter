# Negotiation Script - SonarSource / VP of Engineering, Data & AI

## Market Context

SonarSource is not an early-stage bet. It's a mature, profitable Geneva-founded company serving 7M+ developers and 400K organizations, backed by growth investors (Insight Partners, Advent, Permira), with a valuation reportedly in the billions after its most recent rounds. That changes the negotiation compared to a startup: less "convince me the equity is worth the risk," more "pay me what a company at your scale pays for this scope."

Public salary data specific to VP Engineering at Sonar is thin. What's visible: a VP Domain Leader listing at Sonar Geneva showed CHF 83,500-126,500, which reads low for a true VP-of-org role and is more likely a broad posted range than the real band a strong candidate lands in. Broader Geneva VP compensation (Glassdoor) sits around CHF 155,000-219,000, and Zurich VP Engineering specifically runs CHF 266,000-393,000, roughly 30-50% above Geneva for comparable scope. Sonar competes for engineering talent globally, not just locally, so the realistic band for this role likely sits above the generic Geneva VP median and closer to the lower half of the Zurich tech range, especially given the seniority (15+ years, org-wide ownership of data, ML, and analytics).

**Working anchor: CHF 240,000-290,000 base**, plus bonus (typically 15-25% of base at this level for a scale-up like Sonar) and an equity or RSU component. This is a starting hypothesis to sharpen once a number is on the table, not a number to quote verbatim on a first call.

---

## Opening Anchor

If asked for expectations before an offer:

"For a VP-level role owning the full Data & AI org, including data engineering, ML, and analytics, I'd expect a base in the CHF 250,000 to 280,000 range, with bonus and equity structured on top of that. I'm Geneva-based already, so there's no relocation cost to factor in, but the scope here is a company-wide platform and governance mandate, and I'd price it accordingly."

If pushed for a single number: "Around CHF 265,000 base, with the rest of the package built around bonus and equity."

---

## If the Offer Lands Low

**Offer at or above CHF 250K base:** Fair starting point. Shift the conversation to bonus structure, equity mechanics, and vesting rather than pushing base further.

**Offer in the CHF 200K-230K range:**

"I appreciate the offer. Given this role covers the entire Data & AI org, strategy, platform architecture, governance, and team-building across multiple specializations, I'd expect this closer to CHF 250,000 to 270,000 base. Is there room to move there, or flexibility on bonus and equity to close the gap?"

**Offer below CHF 190K:**

"To be direct, that number reads closer to a senior director scope than a VP one, particularly for a role that's building and owning a data platform and MLOps function from a leadership seat. I'd need to see a base closer to CHF 240,000 for this to reflect the level and responsibility in the posting."

---

## Equity Notes

Sonar is past the stage where equity is a lottery ticket, so this conversation is about mechanics and dilution protection, not conviction-selling.

Questions to ask directly:
- "Is the equity component RSUs, options, or phantom shares, and how does that interact with the company's current stage toward a potential IPO or liquidity event?"
- "What's the vesting schedule, standard four years with a one-year cliff, or something else at this level?"
- "For a VP-level grant, what's the typical range as a percentage of base, or in absolute value, for someone owning a full org?"
- "If there's an IPO or acquisition in the plan, how does that affect vesting acceleration for leadership roles?"

What to watch for: at this stage the equity is real but almost certainly illiquid for years. Treat it as a meaningful long-term component, not a substitute for a strong base, and don't let a rich-sounding equity story pull the base conversation down.

---

## Geographic Discount Pushback

Geneva is not a cheap-labor market, and Sonar is headquartered there, so there's no credible "remote discount" argument to make here, this is a local-market, in-person-adjacent role. If anything comes up framing Geneva as a lower-cost alternative to Zurich:

"Geneva's cost of living is on par with Zurich for housing and daily expenses, and Sonar's own workforce here is the benchmark I'd point to, not a discounted remote rate. I'd expect the band to reflect a Geneva-headquartered VP role, not a satellite-office adjustment."

---

## Competing Offer Leverage Template

Use only if there's an actual competing process in motion; don't bluff a number that doesn't exist.

"I'm in later-stage conversations with another company for a comparable scope, and the numbers there are landing in the CHF 270,000-plus range for base alone. I'd rather move forward here if the scope and team are the right fit, so I wanted to be transparent about where the market's pricing this so we can see if there's alignment."

---

## Closing Notes

- Confirm reporting line (CTO vs. CEO) before finalizing, it materially affects both scope and comp banding at this level.
- Ask who currently owns data governance and MLOps decisions today, since that shapes whether this is a build-from-scratch mandate (worth more) or a consolidation of existing teams (different negotiation entirely).
- Get the bonus structure in writing: target percentage, what it's tied to, and payout history if this is a private company without public targets to reference.
