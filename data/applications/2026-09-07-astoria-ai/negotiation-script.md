# Negotiation Script: Founding Full-Stack Engineer, Astoria AI

Context going in: this is pre-launch, pre-seed, stealth mode. The posted structure is unusual and worth naming up front, equity-only for the first four to six months, then $190,000-280,000 base plus refresh equity once they close a seed round, with a total equity grant of 0.75%-2.0%. That's effectively two negotiations stacked on top of each other: what the equity-only period actually costs me, and where in the post-seed band the eventual base lands. The role is listed as remote US, Eastern or Central time zones preferred, and I'm in Geneva, so location is a live topic too, not a formality.

## Market context

- Founding engineer comp at true pre-launch, pre-seed startups in the US typically runs 0.75%-2.5% equity when cash is deferred or absent entirely, so the posted range is within norms, but the width of that band (0.75% versus 2.0% is nearly triple) means the number they actually offer matters a lot.
- The promised post-seed base of $190,000-280,000 is a healthy senior/staff range for a US remote SaaS role, but it's contingent on a seed round actually closing on a normal timeline. Six-to-twelve-month slippage on seed rounds isn't rare, so the equity-only period is the real risk to price, not the eventual base.
- A Geneva-based Staff-level engineer would command roughly CHF 150,000-190,000 base locally, which is a useful floor to cite for what a guaranteed income looks like, even though this role pays in USD and isn't Swiss-based. It frames how much unpaid runway is reasonable to ask someone to absorb.
- Founding-stage equity is almost always a straight option grant with a four-year vest and a one-year cliff. With no cash during months one through six, the cliff structure matters more than usual, since leaving or being let go before month twelve with nothing vested turns the equity-only period into unpaid work.

## Opening position

"Given the scope here, full ownership across frontend, backend, and infrastructure on a pre-launch product, I'd want the equity grant to sit at the top of your stated range, closer to 2.0% than 0.75%, given how early this is and how much the equity-only period is asking me to absorb. On the post-seed base, I'd anchor at $240,000 to $250,000 given the breadth of the role, and I'd want the seed-to-base transition defined by a date or a milestone, not left open-ended."

## If they come in low on equity

"I want to understand what the low end of that range assumes, whether it's benchmarked against a hire who joins after some of the product risk is already resolved, or against someone coming in this early with nothing built yet. Joining pre-launch means I'm pricing in the risk that the product doesn't find traction at all, and the equity grant is the only thing compensating for that during the unpaid months. If 0.75% is where you need to land, I'd want to talk about a shorter cliff or an accelerated vest on the first tranche to offset it."

## Handling the equity-only period specifically

"Before I agree to anything, I want clarity on what happens if the seed round takes longer than six months to close, or doesn't close at all. Is there a defined point where the arrangement gets revisited, or a minimal stipend that kicks in past a certain point? I'm comfortable betting on the product, but I want that bet to have an exit ramp if the runway situation on your side changes materially, rather than finding out after months of unpaid work that the plan shifted."

## Counter-offer script if the post-seed number lands mid-band

"That's a reasonable starting point. If base is landing in the low $200,000s, I'd want the equity grant pushed toward the top of the range to compensate, along with a written refresh schedule tied to future rounds so the position doesn't quietly erode as the company raises again. I'd also want the vesting clock on the original grant to start from my actual start date, not from whenever the option paperwork clears post-seed."

## Geographic and time zone framing

"I'm in Geneva, which puts me roughly six hours ahead of Eastern time. I'm used to that kind of overlap from working with Benevity's Canada-based teams, so mornings for me covering your afternoon meetings isn't a new pattern, but I want to be upfront that it's not the same as sitting in an Eastern time zone myself. If daily overlap for a small founding team is non-negotiable, I'd rather know that constraint now than after accepting, so we can figure out together whether a few hours of shared time each day is enough or whether it's a dealbreaker."

## Competing offer leverage template

"I'm in later-stage conversations with another early-stage team where the cash component starts sooner. I'd rather build this with a team solving a problem I actually believe in, but for me to choose an equity-only runway over a role that pays from day one, the equity grant and the post-seed base both need to be at the strong end of what's realistic here."

## Equity questions to ask before agreeing to anything

- Current fully diluted share count and what 0.75% versus 2.0% actually represents in dollar terms against a realistic post-seed valuation, not just the headline percentage.
- Strike price and how it's being set pre-seed, given there's no recent 409A to anchor to yet.
- Whether the vesting clock starts at the equity-only start date or only once cash compensation begins post-seed.
- What happens to the grant if the seed round doesn't close within the stated four-to-six-month window.
- Refresh grant mechanics at the next round, so the founding-hire stake doesn't get silently diluted away by Series A.
