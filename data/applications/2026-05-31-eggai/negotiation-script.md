# Negotiation Script — EggAI Technical Team Lead, AI Agents

---

## Market Context (Switzerland, 2026)

**Comparable roles and bands:**
- Engineering Lead / Tech Lead at EU-based AI startup (remote from CH): CHF 140,000-170,000 base
- Staff Engineer / Senior Staff IC at scale-up: CHF 150,000-185,000
- Head of Engineering / Technical Lead with hiring authority: CHF 170,000-210,000
- AI/ML premium on top of base band: +15-25% in current market
- Geneva location adds ~8-12% vs. Zurich, and is significantly above any EUR-denominated offer

**This role:** Technical Team Lead with hiring authority, AI agents specialization, enterprise production focus. Target base: CHF 170,000-195,000. Opening anchor: CHF 195,000.

---

## Opening Anchor

Use when they ask for your number first or make a first offer below your range.

> "Based on the scope here, what I'm targeting is CHF 195,000 base. That's calibrated against Technical Lead and Staff+ bands in Switzerland for roles with this combination of hands-on AI systems work and team leadership, and it factors in the current premium on people who've shipped production multi-agent systems. I'm open to discussing how that breaks down between base and equity if that makes it easier to structure."

---

## If They Come in Low (Below CHF 155,000)

> "I appreciate the offer, and I'm genuinely interested in the role. That said, CHF [X] is a meaningful gap from where I need to be, which is in the CHF 175,000-195,000 range. I want to make this work, so I'd rather be transparent about that now. Is there room to move on the base, or would it make sense to look at how equity could close some of that gap?"

If they push back on the Swiss market rates:

> "I hear that, and I understand that remote roles often get benchmarked against EU averages. The practical reality is that I'm based in Geneva and have obligations priced in CHF. A EUR-denominated offer at a discount to Swiss market rates is effectively a pay cut from my current situation. I'm not trying to squeeze, but I do need the number to work in CHF terms."

---

## Counter-Offer Script (They Offer CHF 155,000-170,000)

> "Thanks for sharing that. It's not far off, but I'd need to get to CHF 185,000 to be comfortable saying yes. Is there room to move there? If not, I'd be interested in whether the equity package has enough upside to offset the gap, and what that looks like concretely."

---

## Equity Negotiation

EggAI is an enterprise AI startup; equity is a real part of the offer. Push for:

**Target ask:** 0.15-0.25% fully diluted for a Technical Team Lead with hiring authority at this stage

**Standard terms to verify:**
- 4-year vesting, 1-year cliff
- Post-termination exercise window (ask for 5 years if possible, 90 days is a red flag)
- Double-trigger acceleration on acquisition

**Script:**
> "On equity: can you walk me through the current cap table context, at least roughly? I want to understand whether this is options or RSUs, what the strike price is versus the last round valuation, and what the exercise window looks like after departure. For a role at this level, I'd expect to be in the 0.15-0.25% range on a fully diluted basis. Does that fit where you are?"

If they're vague on valuation:
> "Even a rough sense helps me evaluate the equity realistically. A 0.2% grant at a $20M valuation is a very different conversation than at a $100M valuation. I'm not asking for confidential financials, just enough to do the math."

---

## Geographic Discount Pushback

EggAI is a European remote role. They may try to benchmark against EU median salaries.

> "I'm aware that some remote-first companies apply a location factor and discount Swiss candidates. I want to be upfront: my cost of living is in CHF, my tax obligations are Swiss, and my alternative opportunities are priced in CHF. I'm willing to work remote for the right role, but I'm not in a position to accept a significant geographic discount from Swiss market rates. If that's the structure you're working with, it's better to know that now."

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

If you have another offer in hand (or want to create urgency):

> "I do have another process that's moving forward in parallel, and I expect to have something concrete from them within [X weeks]. I'd prefer to close with EggAI if we can get to the right number, but I want to be honest that I can't hold the timeline open indefinitely. Is there a way to accelerate the decision on your end?"

If no competing offer, create urgency differently:

> "I'm being selective about my next move, which is why I'm still in conversations rather than having already accepted something. The longer I stay in process, the more I want to make sure the numbers work before we get to the end."

---

## Non-Salary Items to Negotiate

- Hardware budget (MacBook Pro M4 Max, external monitor): CHF 4,000-6,000
- Remote work setup stipend: CHF 1,500-2,000/year
- Conference and learning budget: CHF 2,000-3,000/year
- Clarity on employment structure (Swiss employment contract vs. contractor/B2B)
- Health insurance: confirm whether CH-compliant or if you handle KK separately

---

## Red Lines

- Below CHF 155,000 base with no equity upside: decline
- No post-termination exercise window longer than 90 days on options: push hard or walk
- Contractor-only structure with no benefits offset: requires +20-30% gross to compensate for missing employer contributions
