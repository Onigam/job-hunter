# Negotiation Script, Inworld AI, Staff/Principal ML Engineer, Serving (Switzerland Remote)

## Context to keep in mind going in

This is the Switzerland-remote version of a role Inworld also runs out of Mountain View, where the posted base range is $270,000-$500,000+ plus bonus and equity, with relocation required. The Swiss listing won't carry the same number, and Inworld will likely anchor lower on the theory that Swiss cost of living or their EU comp bands justify it. General Swiss market data for "machine learning engineer" tops out around CHF 130,000-150,000, but that figure is for mid-level roles and doesn't reflect Staff/Principal scarcity, especially for inference-serving specialists, which is a much thinner talent pool than generic ML. Anchor off the US range and off scarcity, not off the generic Swiss ML engineer number, since that number describes a different job.

Also worth naming honestly: this profile is a platform-and-systems generalist, not a CUDA/kernel specialist, which is most of what this posting screens for. That's a real gap and it will affect leverage in this negotiation specifically. If the offer materializes, it's likely because Inworld wants the orchestration/platform layer around serving, not the kernel-optimization core, so anchor accordingly rather than pretending to compete on the specialist axis.

## Opening anchor

Base target: CHF 190,000-220,000, framed as the Swiss-market equivalent of a Staff-level US band adjusted for the narrower scope this hire would likely own (platform and orchestration around serving, not kernel optimization). Say it like this:

"For context, the US posting for this role runs $270K to $500K base. I know the Swiss-remote version won't track that directly, and I also want to be upfront that my strength here is the systems and platform side of serving infrastructure, not GPU kernel work. Given that scope, I'd expect a base in the CHF 190K-220K range, with the rest of the package reflecting how core you see this seat as."

## If they come in low (say, below CHF 170,000)

Don't reject on the spot. Ask what's driving the number:

"Is that number reflecting the full Staff scope, or is it priced closer to a mid-senior IC band? I ask because the responsibilities in the posting, owning production serving reliability across thousands of concurrent connections, read as Staff-level regardless of geography."

If they hold the line, counter with a structure trade instead of a flat ask: push more into equity or a signing bonus rather than base, since early-stage-adjacent companies often have more room there than in cash comp bands.

## Equity notes

Inworld has raised $125M+ from top-tier VCs, which means real equity value but also real dilution risk this far from an exit. Standard asks:

- Vesting: 4-year vest, 1-year cliff is standard; don't accept anything longer without a clear reason.
- Ask for the strike price and the most recent 409A valuation (or Swiss equivalent) in writing before evaluating any number they quote as a percentage.
- Ask whether there's a double-trigger acceleration clause for acquisition, given this profile's own experience being on the acquired side of a deal at Alaya. That experience is a legitimate reason to ask the question, not just caution for its own sake.
- If early exercise is offered, get clarity on the tax treatment under Swiss law before deciding; this isn't a US-style ISO situation.

## Geographic discount pushback

If they try to lower the offer purely because Geneva "costs less" than Mountain View: Geneva is not a discount city. Cost of living in Geneva runs comparable to or above the Bay Area on housing and health insurance specifically. Use this line if needed:

"Geneva isn't a lower cost-of-living market than Mountain View, it's comparable on housing and higher on health insurance and mandatory costs. I'd push back on treating this as a geographic discount hire rather than a market-adjusted one."

## Competing offer leverage (template, use only if genuinely in play)

"I'm currently in later-stage conversations with another company at a comparable level, and I'd like to make a decision on full information. Could we get to a final number this week so I can compare properly?"

Don't invoke this unless there's an actual competing process; an empty bluff is easy to see through at Staff level and costs more in trust than it gains in leverage.

## Bottom line walk-away

Given the scope mismatch on kernel-level serving work, don't anchor ego on getting the top of the range. If the final offer lands meaningfully below CHF 170,000 base with thin equity, that's a signal the company sees this as a mid-level hire dressed up in a Staff title, and it's fine to say so directly and walk.
