# Negotiation Script, SonarSource SA (Staff Software Engineer, Java, Identity Squad)

Context: established, profitable Geneva-based company (reported ~$400M revenue), not a cash-strapped startup. This changes the negotiation shape from equity-heavy cofounder talks to a standard senior comp negotiation, base plus bonus plus whatever equity or profit-share plan they run for staff-level ICs. Hybrid role, Monday/Tuesday/Thursday in the Geneva office, which removes any relocation discount argument entirely since Magino already lives there.

## Market anchors (Geneva, Staff Software Engineer, 8+ years including tech lead scope)

- Staff-level Java engineers at established, profitable Geneva or Zurich tech companies typically land CHF 145k-185k base, with the higher end reserved for candidates bringing specialized domain expertise (identity/access, security-adjacent systems) or cross-team architecture ownership.
- Total comp including bonus (often 10-15% target at this level) commonly reaches CHF 165k-210k.
- Geneva cost of living is among the highest in Europe, on par with Zurich and above most other Swiss cities. There's no room for a below-market anchor just because the office is outside Zurich.
- SonarSource is privately held with a strong valuation history, so ask directly during the process whether staff-level ICs participate in an equity, RSU, or phantom share plan, since the posting doesn't mention one. If there's no equity plan at this level, that gap should be reflected in a higher base or bonus target instead.

## Opening position

Before naming a number, get clarity on where the offered range actually sits and whether the role carries any equity or long-term incentive component, since profitable private companies vary a lot on this.

Opening anchor: "Given the scope here, owning identity architecture decisions across both cloud and self-managed products at a Staff level with mentoring responsibility, I'd expect a base in the CHF 170k-185k range, plus your standard bonus structure. I've led comparable cross-team architecture work at Benevity post-acquisition, so this isn't a stretch ask relative to the responsibility."

## If they come in low on base

Script: "I hear that the range you're working with is lower than that. Given the Staff scope, the identity domain complexity, and that I'm already Geneva-based so there's no relocation cost to offset, I'd want to understand what's driving the gap. If the base has a ceiling, I'd want to talk about bonus target or a signing bonus that closes some of the difference in year one."

## If there's no equity or long-term incentive plan at this level

Script: "If Staff engineers don't participate in an equity or RSU plan here, I'd want that reflected somewhere else, either a higher base or a larger bonus target, since most comparable Staff roles I'm looking at do include some long-term component. I'm not asking for cofounder-level equity, just parity with what a role at this scope usually carries."

## Geographic discount pushback

If there's any suggestion that Geneva pay should track a lower Swiss city or a remote-from-elsewhere rate: "Geneva is one of the more expensive cities in Switzerland, comparable to Zurich, and I'm already based here full time. There's no relocation saving to pass through, so the offer should reflect standard Geneva market rate, not a discounted one."

## Competing offer as counterweight (use only if real)

"I'm also in later-stage conversations for a Staff/Principal platform role in the CHF 175k-195k base range. I'm more drawn to the identity and access problem space here since it's closer to the architecture work I want to keep doing, but the base needs to be in a comparable range for that preference to win out."

## Notice period and start date

Standard Swiss notice periods run one to three months depending on tenure and contract; confirm this early so it doesn't become a late surprise in the offer stage. Flag it in the opening call rather than waiting for the written offer.

## Close

Ask for the offer in writing with base, bonus target, any equity or incentive plan terms, and vacation/benefits spelled out before verbally accepting anything. If the equity question comes back vague, push for a specific answer rather than accepting "we'll discuss that later," since vague answers at offer stage tend to stay vague after start date.
