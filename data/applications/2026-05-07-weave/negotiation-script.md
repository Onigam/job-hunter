# Negotiation Script — Weave / Founding AI Engineer

## Context

- **Listed range:** $150K–$200K USD base, 0.10%–0.25% equity
- **Location:** San Francisco, CA (on-site expected)
- **Requirement flag:** US citizen or visa holder required
- **Stage:** YC W25 seed, $4.2M raised

---

## Critical Prerequisite: Location and Work Authorization

The listing explicitly requires US citizenship or existing visa. Before investing time in the process, surface this early:

> "One quick logistics check before we go further: the listing mentions US citizen or visa holder. I'm based in Geneva, Switzerland — do you have a path for international candidates, whether that's visa sponsorship or a remote arrangement for the right person?"

If they say no visa sponsorship and no remote: this role is not actionable without a significant life relocation. If they're open to sponsorship (E-3, O-1, or L-1 are typical paths for senior engineers) or to a remote trial arrangement, continue.

---

## Salary Anchor

The listed range ($150K–$200K) is at the low end for a "Founding AI Engineer" designation at a seed-stage YC company in 2026. The founding label implies first or second technical hire, significant equity-like responsibility, and long hours defining what the company becomes. That's not the same as "senior engineer hire #8."

**Market context:**
- SF senior/staff AI engineer market: $200K–$280K base (levels.fyi, comparable YC seed companies)
- "Founding engineer" premium: typically 10–20% above senior IC rates
- Geneva engineering market (if remote): CHF 150K–200K base for Staff+ level, which converts to roughly $170K–$225K USD at current rates — and Geneva cost of living is comparable to SF

**Opening anchor:**

> "I've been looking at comp for founding engineer roles at seed-stage AI companies and the market I'm seeing is in the $210K–$230K range for someone at this seniority level. I'm flexible on the structure — if base needs to stay lower, I'd want to reflect that in equity — but that's the territory I'd need to be in to leave a stable senior role."

---

## Equity Counter

0.10%–0.25% is low for a founding designation. For the actual first or second engineer at a $4.2M seed company, the market standard is closer to 0.5%–1.5%, depending on when you join and what cliff/vesting structure applies.

**Counter script:**

> "The equity range listed, 0.10% to 0.25%, reads more like an early employee hire than a founding engineer. If I'm coming in to shape how the company builds from here, I'd expect to be in the 0.5%–1% range. I understand you're managing dilution at seed stage, but if you want me making founding-level decisions, the equity should reflect that."

**Equity questions to ask:**
- What is the current fully diluted share count?
- What was the price at the seed round? (Establishes current paper value)
- Is there a 1-year cliff with 4-year vesting, or something else?
- What anti-dilution protections exist for early employees if a down round happens?
- Are options ISO or NSO? (ISOs are better for US taxpayers; for a Swiss-based employee, the tax treatment is different and worth clarifying with a tax advisor)

---

## If They Come in Below Range

If the initial offer is at the lower end ($150K–$160K):

> "I appreciate the offer. I want to make this work, but $155K is below what I need to seriously consider leaving where I am. The market for a founding engineer at this stage is meaningfully higher, and I'd be bringing a track record that includes building and exiting a platform at acquisition — that's not a junior hire. Can you get to $190K? If base has a ceiling, I'd be open to a conversation about how to close the gap on equity instead."

---

## Remote Arrangement (if applicable)

If they're open to Geneva-based remote:

> "I'm fully comfortable working US hours or a significant overlap — I've done transatlantic collaboration for years. For a remote arrangement, I'd want the comp to reflect that I'm not getting the SF in-person premium but that Geneva's cost structure is comparable. I wouldn't expect a European discount for a role that's labeled founding and expects founding-level output."

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

If you have other offers in process (or want to create urgency):

> "I'm in conversations with two other companies at a similar stage. I want to be transparent about that because I'd rather take more time with the right fit than rush a decision. But I'm also not going to sit on this indefinitely — if we can align on comp this week, I'm ready to move."

---

## Walk-Away Conditions

- Base below $180K USD with no meaningful equity adjustment
- Equity below 0.35% with no path to higher allocation
- No visa sponsorship and no remote arrangement (given Geneva base)
- Vesting cliff longer than 1 year
