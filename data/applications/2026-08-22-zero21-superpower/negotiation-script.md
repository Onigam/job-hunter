# Negotiation Script: Zero21 (Superpower), Founding Engineer Full Stack

## Context that shapes this negotiation

Superpower raised a $30M Series A in April 2025 led by Forerunner Ventures, with Day One Ventures, Susa Ventures, and Long Journey Ventures also in, and press has pegged the valuation north of $300M. That's a real signal: this isn't a pre-seed founding-engineer offer where equity is a lottery ticket, it's a Series A company that already has a priced round to reference. The role is listed for San Francisco or remote, and I'm in Geneva, so the location question needs an answer before the number does, the same way it did with the Zep AI process. No official range is published for this specific role; general Superpower SF postings on aggregators show roughly $147k-$197k, but that band looks like it covers non-founding engineering roles, not the first engineering hire shaping architecture from a data schema and UI sketches.

## Market anchor

For a founding engineer at a Series A health-tech company already valued above $300M, US comp for someone with 17 years of experience and a prior founding-to-acquisition outcome should land above the generic band aggregators show, in the $190,000-$230,000 base range if the arrangement is US-based employment, plus an equity grant meaningfully larger than a later hire would get, since founding engineers typically negotiate 0.5%-1.5% depending on how early they land relative to the round. If the arrangement ends up being remote-from-Switzerland instead, the honest anchor is Swiss market rate for a senior/staff-level engineer taking on founding scope, roughly CHF 170,000-210,000 base, and the equity conversation should stay the same regardless of where the base lands, since equity is priced off the company, not the employee's location.

## Opening the comp conversation

"Before I put a number out, I want to understand two things: whether this is meant to be US-based employment or if remote-from-Switzerland is genuinely on the table, and what equity band you have in mind for the first full stack hire specifically, since that's usually the bigger lever at this stage than base."

If they ask me to anchor first:

"For a founding engineer role at a Series A company already valued above $300M, I'd expect a base around $210,000 if this runs as US employment, or the Swiss equivalent around CHF 190,000 if it's a genuine remote-from-Switzerland arrangement, plus an equity grant sized for one of the first engineering hires, not a later addition to the team."

## If they come in low

"I hear the number, but I want to separate two things: the base, and what's actually driving it. If the equity is meant to make up the difference, I'd want the percentage or share count in writing, plus the current valuation it's priced against, before comparing it to a straight base figure. If there's room to move on base instead, I'd rather have that conversation directly than treat a lower number as final."

## Equity negotiation notes

- Ask for the fully diluted share count and the post-money valuation from the April 2025 Series A before treating any headline percentage as meaningful.
- Founding engineer grants at this stage typically sit meaningfully above a standard early hire's; push for a number that reflects being one of the first engineers, not employee twenty.
- Standard four-year vest with a one-year cliff is reasonable; anything longer is worth pushing back on.
- Ask directly about double-trigger acceleration on acquisition. Having been on the acquired side once at Alaya, this isn't a hypothetical question for me, it's informed by watching what happens to unvested equity when a deal actually closes.
- Confirm the post-termination exercise window in writing before agreeing to anything verbally.

## Geographic and remote framing

"I want to name the location question directly rather than let it quietly turn into a discount. If US-based employment is a hard requirement, I understand that changes the conversation around sponsorship and where I'd need to be. If remote-from-Switzerland works, I'd want to know whether that runs through a different comp model, an EOR or contractor arrangement, rather than assuming it maps onto a US-only band."

## Competing offer leverage template

Use only if a real process is in flight:

"I'm further along in another conversation for a comparable founding or staff-level role, and the comp there is tracking toward a range I'd want to stay close to. I'd rather build the thing Superpower is building since the health data problem is the more interesting one, but I need the numbers to be in the same neighborhood to make that the easy choice."

## Closing line

"I want this to work. Send me something in writing on both base and equity, and I'll turn it around quickly either way."
