## Negotiation Script: Principal Engineer (Head of Engineering), Growing Fintech, via LuRecruiter

Context that shapes this one: the posting is explicit that this isn't a standard Staff or Principal IC role and isn't a standard Head of Engineering people-management role either, it's the technical owner of an entire system, core platform plus B2C plus B2B, with direct responsibility for both squads' execution but not their headcount. That hybrid scope is usually underpriced if the company defaults to a generic "senior engineer" band, so the first job is making sure the comp conversation is anchored to system-ownership scope, not to a job title. The listing is via a recruiter (LuRecruiter) and the company is undisclosed at this stage, so salary, funding stage, and equity structure all need to be surfaced early rather than assumed. It's remote, which removes the usual Geneva cost-of-living argument but doesn't remove the market-rate one.

## Market anchor

- Swiss-based Staff/Principal Engineer with architecture ownership across multiple product lines, individual-contributor track: base typically CHF 170,000-210,000.
- Hybrid Principal Engineer / Head of Engineering scope, owning technical execution across two squads without direct reports, at a growth-stage fintech: base typically CHF 190,000-230,000, often with a bonus or equity component given the seniority and regulated-domain risk.
- Fintech and blockchain-adjacent companies tend to pay a premium over generic SaaS for this kind of role, given the compliance surface and the cost of a bad architecture call on a ledger or custody system, so the upper half of that band is the reasonable opening position.
- Given 17-plus years of experience, a founding-engineer track record with an exit to a unicorn, and a pattern of being the informal cross-team architecture owner without a title to match, the target is CHF 210,000-230,000 base, plus meaningful equity if the company is early enough that equity is part of how they compensate for this kind of ownership role.

## Opening anchor script

"Given the scope described here, technical ownership of the core platform plus coherence across the B2C and B2B squads, I'd expect a base in the neighborhood of CHF 220,000, plus an equity grant that reflects the fact that this is a long-horizon architectural ownership seat, not a project-length engagement. Before we go further, can you walk me through where the company is in its funding, what the equity pool looks like, and how this role's comp has typically been benchmarked, IC-track or leadership-track?"

## If they come in low

"Help me understand the anchor for that number, is it benchmarked against a Staff Engineer IC role, or against the actual scope in the posting, which includes technical ownership of two squads and the coherence of the whole platform? That combination usually prices closer to a VP Engineering or Head of Platform band than a senior IC one, and I'd want the offer to reflect the accountability that comes with owning architecture decisions the company will be living with for years."

If they hold the line on base: "I can flex on base if the equity or bonus structure reflects the ownership and regulatory risk this role carries, ledger, custody, or payment-adjacent systems don't forgive bad calls the way a typical SaaS product does. What I wouldn't want to do is settle meaningfully below CHF 195,000 base without that being offset somewhere else in the package."

## Equity negotiation, if startup-stage

- Ask directly for the current valuation, the size of the option pool, and whether prior rounds included any secondary sale that gives a sense of real liquidity, not just a headline valuation.
- Get strike price and 409A or Swiss-equivalent valuation basis in writing before comparing any equity number to a competing offer.
- Standard four-year vesting with a one-year cliff is the norm, push back if they propose anything longer or with unusual acceleration conditions.
- Ask specifically about acceleration on acquisition, given the role includes owning architecture for a system that spans B2C and B2B, a future acquirer's due diligence will likely focus on exactly the areas this role owns, so that risk deserves protection in the equity terms.
- If the company is blockchain-adjacent, confirm whether any part of comp is offered in token form rather than equity, and if so ask for vesting, lockup, and valuation-basis terms with the same rigor as equity, token comp carries different risk and liquidity assumptions that need to be priced in separately.

## Geographic and remote-work notes

"I'm Geneva-based and the role is listed as remote, so I don't expect a geographic discount, but I want to confirm early whether comp is benchmarked to a specific market or set globally, since that changes how I think about the number. If there's an assumption that remote hires get priced to a lower-cost region, I'd want to understand that before we're deep into specifics."

## Competing offer template

"I'm in later-stage conversations for a comparable Staff-plus or Head of Engineering scope role, in a similar comp range to what I've outlined here. What stands out about this one is the actual system-ownership scope across B2C and B2B rather than a narrower IC mandate, but I want to be transparent about timing so we can move at a pace that works for both of us."

## Close

This role is priced by its scope, not its title, technical ownership of a core platform plus two product squads is a Head-of-Engineering-level accountability even without direct reports. Get the base, the equity or token terms and their vesting basis, and how comp is benchmarked for a remote hire confirmed in writing before signing. The funding-stage and cap-table question matters most here since it determines whether equity is a real number or a rounding error, and it's worth surfacing before the rest of the numbers get anchored.
