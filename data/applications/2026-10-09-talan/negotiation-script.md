# Negotiation Script: Talan, Agentic SDLC Transformation Lead (Meyrin, Geneva)

## Context before any number

Talan is an international consulting and technology group (7,000+ people, 21 countries, 20+ years in Switzerland). The LinkedIn posting lists the role as executive level and shows no salary. Two things to settle early:

1. **Location.** The LinkedIn page title showed "Des Moines, IA" while the body says Meyrin. Confirm Meyrin/Geneva and the on-site expectation in the first call.
2. **Role shape.** Is this an internal transformation role, a client-facing practice lead, or both? If there's a billable or utilisation target, or a sales/pre-sales component, that changes the comp structure (bonus weighting) and the number you should ask for.

This is a consulting firm, not a startup, so the lever is base + bonus + title, not equity.

## Switzerland market data (Geneva, 2026, indicative)

| Benchmark | Base (CHF) | Variable |
|---|---|---|
| Senior Staff / Principal Engineer, Geneva product company | 170k-210k | 0-15% + equity |
| Consulting Senior Manager, tech/AI practice, Geneva | 150k-185k | 10-20% |
| Consulting Director / Practice Lead, Geneva | 185k-240k | 15-30% |
| Head of Engineering / VP Eng, Swiss scale-up | 190k-250k | 10-20% + equity |

Geneva cost of living is among the highest in Europe, and the posting asks for 10+ years plus SVP/VP/CTO-level background. Your 17 years and a founding-CTO exit put you at the Director / Practice Lead line, not Senior Manager.

**Targets**
- Opening anchor: **CHF 225k base + 20% target bonus** (~CHF 270k total target)
- Realistic landing: **CHF 200k-215k base + 15-20% bonus**
- Walk-away: **CHF 180k base** with a clear bonus plan, or lower base only if total target cash stays above ~CHF 215k

## Opening anchor script

> "Based on what I've seen for transformation leads and practice directors in Geneva, and given this role combines a CTO-level mandate with hands-on agent architecture, I'm looking at around CHF 225k base with a target bonus near 20%. I'm flexible on the split between fixed and variable if the bonus criteria are clear."

If they ask for your current salary first:

> "I'd rather focus on what this role is worth. The scope here is a framework that Talan rolls out across teams and clients, so I'm benchmarking against director-level roles in Geneva."

## If they come in low

> "Thanks, I appreciate it. The number is below what I've seen for comparable scope in Geneva. The posting asks for someone who has run engineering at VP or CTO level and can still build the PoCs, and that profile is scarce right now, especially with real multi-agent experience. Could we get the base to CHF 210k? If the base is fixed by grade, I'd want to look at the grade itself, or a higher target bonus with criteria tied to adoption metrics."

If they cite grade bands:

> "Understood. Which grade is this mapped to, and what would it take to place it one level up? The job description reads like a practice lead role, and I'd want the title and grade to match that, both for internal credibility and for client-facing work."

## Geographic discount pushback

> "The role is based in Meyrin, and I'll be on site there. Geneva rates are the right reference, not a group-wide European band. Housing, health insurance and childcare here are priced accordingly."

## Variable and benefits to negotiate (in place of equity)

- **Bonus:** target %, the split between individual and company criteria, payout history over the last 2-3 years, and whether it's guaranteed in year one (ask for a pro-rated guarantee if you start mid-year).
- **13th month:** check whether the base quoted includes it.
- **Pension (LPP):** employer contribution above the legal minimum is worth several thousand CHF a year; ask for the plan details.
- **Sign-on bonus:** reasonable ask if you're leaving unvested equity or a bonus behind (CHF 15k-30k).
- **Equity / co-investment:** Talan is privately held. Ask whether a management incentive or co-investment plan exists for senior hires; don't assume one.
- **Title:** push for "Director" or "Head of" if the role is client-facing. It affects bonus band and how clients receive you.
- **Learning and tooling budget:** model API credits for experimentation, conference budget.
- **Remote flexibility:** 1-2 days remote per week.
- **Travel:** if client travel across Talan's offices is expected, agree on a ceiling.

## Competing offer template

> "I want to be transparent: I'm in late-stage conversations with another company for a staff-level AI platform role in Switzerland, with a package around CHF [X] base plus equity. Talan's mandate is the more interesting one for me, so I'd like to make this work. Is there room to close the gap on base or bonus?"

Only use this with a real process in play. If there isn't one, keep to the market-data argument.

## Watch-outs specific to consulting

- Profile lists "pure consulting" under companies to avoid. Before negotiating, check how much of the role is internal framework building vs. staffed on client missions. If it's mostly billable delivery, raise the ask or reconsider.
- Clarify the non-compete and client non-solicitation clauses; Swiss non-competes must be limited in scope and duration, so ask for them in writing.
- Get the bonus criteria in writing before signing, not after the first review.
