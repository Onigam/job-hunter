# Negotiation Script — n8n Sr AI Engineer

## Company Context

n8n: $2.5B valuation, $240M raised (Series B+), ~160 employees, Berlin HQ.
Growing fast. Remote-first across Europe. Equity offered to all hires.
Salary bands not disclosed publicly.

---

## Market Data

**EU / Berlin-based Senior AI Engineer:**
- Market range: €90,000–130,000 base
- Senior Staff / Principal equivalent: €110,000–140,000 base
- Equity on top at pre-IPO unicorn stage can be meaningful

**Geneva, Switzerland equivalent (same role, Senior Staff):**
- CHF 160,000–200,000 all-in (base + bonus)
- Cost of living premium vs Berlin: ~35-40%
- Geneva tech market benchmark: Staff Engineer at a scale-up = CHF 170,000+ base

**Target anchor: CHF 175,000–185,000 base + equity**
This is defensible based on Geneva cost structure, 17+ years, and founding engineer track record.

---

## Opening Anchor

If asked for a number first, give a range:

> "I'm targeting CHF 175,000 to 185,000 as a base, given the Geneva market and the seniority of the role. I'm also looking at the equity picture as part of the total package, so I'd want to understand the details there as well."

If they push back on currency (they may pay in EUR):

> "I'm based in Geneva, so CHF is the natural reference for me. In EUR that's roughly €185,000 to €200,000 at current rates, which reflects the cost structure here rather than a Berlin or Amsterdam market."

---

## If They Come In Low (below €100K / CHF 120K)

Don't reject on the call. Ask questions first.

> "Thanks for sharing that. I want to make sure I understand the full structure before responding. Can you walk me through the equity component and how you're thinking about total comp? And is the base band fixed or is there flexibility?"

Then:

> "I appreciate the transparency. The offer as it stands is a bit below where I'd need to be given the Geneva market for this level, which typically sits around CHF 160,000 to 180,000. Is there room to move the base, or would the equity be structured in a way that closes that gap?"

---

## If They Push the "Remote Discount" Argument

Some companies calibrate salary to employee location, others to role market rate. Push back if they apply a Berlin discount to a Geneva-based hire:

> "I'd want to understand the logic there. The work I'm doing is identical regardless of where I'm logging in from, and Geneva's cost structure is meaningfully higher than most European cities. If the band is anchored to a Berlin market, I'd want to have an honest conversation about whether the total package reflects the actual market for this level."

---

## Equity Negotiation

n8n is pre-IPO with a $2.5B valuation. Equity here could be meaningful if the company continues to grow.

Key questions to ask:

1. What is the strike price? (Should be at or near FMV at grant date)
2. What is the vesting schedule? (Standard: 4 years, 1-year cliff)
3. What percentage of the company, or what is the face value at current valuation?
4. Is there a 409A or equivalent recent valuation for the strike price basis?
5. What happens to unvested equity on acquisition? (Acceleration clause?)
6. Post-termination exercise window? (Standard is 90 days, better companies offer 2–5 years)

Script for equity conversation:

> "I'd want to understand the equity piece in more detail. Specifically: what's the current strike price, what's the vesting schedule, and how does the grant size compare to other senior engineers at this level? I'm not asking for a number that benefits me unfairly, I just want to understand how it fits into the total comp picture."

On post-termination exercise window:

> "One thing I always check is the exercise window after leaving. Ninety days is the legal minimum but it puts employees in a tough spot if they can't afford to exercise and pay the tax bill immediately. Does n8n offer a longer window?"

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

If you have another offer (or want to signal optionality):

> "I want to be upfront: I'm at a stage in this process where I have another conversation progressing. I'm genuinely more interested in n8n, but I'd want to make sure we can get to a package that makes the decision straightforward for me."

If the other offer is hypothetical but you want to create urgency:

> "My timeline is fairly short. I'd like to make a decision by end of next week if possible. Is there anything on the offer side that you'd need more time to clarify?"

---

## Benefits to Confirm

- 30 days vacation confirmed
- €1,000 annual learning budget (useful but not a negotiation point)
- Unlimited AI tool budget (real perk for this role specifically)
- Health insurance structure (country-specific at n8n, ask what the Switzerland setup looks like)
- Pension / retirement contribution (in Switzerland: Pilier 2 equivalent or employer pension?)

---

## Bottom Line

Walk if: base below CHF 145,000 with no meaningful equity.
Accept if: CHF 165,000+ base with a reasonable equity grant (0.05%+ at current valuation) and standard 4yr/1yr vesting.
Strong accept: CHF 175,000+ base, equity with longer exercise window, standard benefits.
