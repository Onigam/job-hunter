# Negotiation Script — AlpineAI Senior Software Engineer

## Market Context

**Role:** Senior Software Engineer, Full-Stack / Backend
**Location:** Zurich or Davos (hybrid), based Geneva
**Company stage:** Swiss AI startup, healthcare/public sector focus

**Switzerland market rates (2025-2026) for Senior/Staff Backend Engineers:**
- Zurich market, Senior (5-10 yrs): CHF 130,000 to 155,000 base
- Zurich market, Staff/Principal (10+ yrs): CHF 155,000 to 185,000 base
- AI-focused roles command a 10-15% premium over traditional engineering
- AlpineAI is startup-stage: expect lower cash base offset by meaningful equity

**Magino's positioning:**
- 17 years experience, including founding engineer and platform architect credentials
- Swiss-market incumbent (Geneva-based, knows the local market)
- LLM-in-production experience is genuinely scarce
- Co-founded an acquired company: proof of judgment, not just execution

**Reasonable target range:** CHF 145,000 to 165,000 base + equity

---

## Opening Anchor

When they ask for expectations, go in at CHF 160,000 base.

Script: "Based on the scope of the role and the Zurich market rates for engineers with a similar background, I'm targeting CHF 160,000 as a base. I'm also expecting a meaningful equity component given the stage. Happy to discuss both."

Justification to give if pressed: "I've been benchmarking against current Zurich offers at Staff level, and given the LLM production experience and founding background, that range is where the market puts profiles like mine right now."

---

## If They Come In Low (CHF 120,000 to 130,000)

Script: "I appreciate the offer. The range is lower than I'd anticipated given the seniority of the role and what I know about the Zurich market. I'm genuinely interested in AlpineAI, so I'd want to understand if there's flexibility before I say no. Is the base negotiable, or should we look at equity to bridge the gap?"

If they're firm on cash: "If the base ceiling is CHF 130,000, what does the equity package look like? I'd want to understand vesting schedule, cliff, current valuation, and the percentage before I can evaluate the total comp picture."

---

## Equity Negotiation

AlpineAI is early-stage. They mention employee stock ownership (ESOP). Push for real equity, not a token grant.

**Reasonable ask for this seniority level:**
- 0.15% to 0.35% fully diluted
- Standard 4-year vest with 1-year cliff
- Acceleration clause on acquisition (this matters given Magino's own acquisition experience)

Script: "I've been through an acquisition on the founder side, so I know what equity terms actually matter. The number that matters to me is the fully diluted percentage. What does the current ESOP pool look like, and what percentage would this grant represent? I'd also want to discuss whether there's any acceleration provision on a change of control."

If they can't share the cap table: "At minimum I need the number of shares, total shares outstanding, and the most recent 409A or equivalent valuation to evaluate the offer properly."

---

## Geneva-Zurich Geographic Premium

Magino is in Geneva. Zurich hybrid means either relocation costs or regular travel.

Script: "One thing worth factoring in: I'm Geneva-based, so the hybrid in Zurich or Davos adds real travel costs and time. That's not a dealbreaker, but I'd want the comp package to reflect that reality. Is there a travel allowance, or is the expectation that the base covers it?"

If they push back: "The Geneva-Zurich intercity commute runs about CHF 400-600 per month at half-fare, plus the time cost. I'd want to make sure we're calibrating the base against a Zurich-based equivalent, not discounting it for remote."

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

If you have or are expecting another offer:

Script: "I want to be transparent with you: I have another process moving at similar seniority and compensation level. I'm genuinely more interested in AlpineAI given the regulated AI focus, but I need to know whether your offer will be in the same range before I can make that call. Is CHF 155,000 to 165,000 something you can work with?"

If you don't have a competing offer, don't fabricate one. Instead, reference market data:

Script: "The current market for engineers with this background is CHF 150,000 to 165,000 in Zurich. I'm not going to invent a competing offer, but I want to be honest that I'm benchmarking against that range, and anything below CHF 145,000 base would require me to reconsider."

---

## What to Prioritize if Forced to Trade

1. Base salary floor: don't go below CHF 140,000 given experience level
2. Equity percentage (fully diluted): more important than the headline share count
3. Acceleration on acquisition: given Magino's background, this is personally meaningful
4. Travel/remote flexibility: Geneva-based hybrid is a real cost to negotiate
5. Professional development budget: secondary but worth asking

---

## One-Liner Close

"I'm ready to move forward. The number I need to see is CHF 155,000 base with a meaningful equity grant. If we're in that territory, I don't need to shop this further."
