# Negotiation Script: Solskin by Zurich Soft Robotics, Lead Software Engineer (Zurich)

## The shape of this offer

Zurich Soft Robotics (ZSR) is an ETH Zurich spin-off, a GmbH, that grew out of the HiLo adaptive facade at Empa's NEST. Public aggregators put total funding somewhere between roughly CHF 1.5M and 2.5M (seed round in early 2023, Innosuisse grant, Swiss Climate Foundation, Venture Kick) and headcount around 17 at the end of 2024. Those figures come from third-party sites, so confirm them in the first call.

What this means for the negotiation:

1. **Cash is the tight part.** A seed-stage hardware company with a grant-heavy cap table won't pay big-tech salaries. Expect a base below Swiss scale-up Staff levels and plan to make up the difference in equity, title and scope.
2. **Equity is the real lever, and it's probably virtual.** In a GmbH, real ownership means quotas transferred in front of a notary, which is cumbersome, so many Swiss GmbH startups use phantom or virtual shares (VSOP) instead. Ask exactly what they offer before talking percentages.
3. **The title matters.** LinkedIn tags the role as "Director" level, it reports to the CEO and CTO, and it owns the entire software stack. That's a Head of Software role in practice. Get the title and the reporting line in writing.
4. **The commute is a real cost.** At least three days a week in Zurich, sometimes on customer sites. From Geneva that's about 2h45 each way by train. This belongs in the package, not in the small print.

## Questions to ask before any number is discussed

1. What's the equity instrument (virtual shares, real options, quotas), how big is the pool, and what percentage of the fully diluted company would this grant represent?
2. When is the next funding round planned, and how much runway is there today? A Series A in the next 6 to 12 months changes both the risk and the value of the equity.
3. How many people would this role lead now, and in 12 months?
4. Is the "three days on-site" flexible during a first phase, or for someone commuting from Geneva?

| Early hire in a hardware startup, first 20 people, senior lead | 1% to 2% | | Upper end if it comes with a Head of title |

Takeaway: a base around CHF 160k to 175k with a meaningful equity grant is a fair landing zone for a seed-stage company. Below CHF 150k only makes sense if the equity is real, substantial and the next round is close.

Swiss specifics worth raising:

- **Equity taxation.** Swiss tax treatment depends on the instrument. Phantom/virtual share payouts are generally taxed as income when paid. Real options are usually taxed at exercise, sometimes at grant. Ask for the plan document and run it past a Swiss tax adviser before signing, especially since Geneva rates apply if you stay resident there.
- **Pension (BVG/LPP).** Ask whether the employer contribution goes above the legal minimum. Startups often stick to the minimum, which is worth knowing when comparing offers.
- **Notice period.** Three months is common in Switzerland. They want someone "as soon as possible", so be clear on the earliest real start date.

## Equity terms to negotiate

- **Vesting.** Standard is four years with a one-year cliff. Ask for monthly vesting after the cliff.
- **Acceleration.** Ask for double-trigger acceleration (change of control plus termination). Having gone through the Alaya acquisition, you know exactly why this matters, and it's a credible ask from someone who's been on the founder side.
- **Good leaver / bad leaver.** Make sure vested equity is kept if you leave as a good leaver, and that the definition of bad leaver is narrow.
- **Dilution.** Anti-dilution protection is unusual for employees, so don't spend capital on it. Ask instead for a refresh grant review after the next funding round.
- **Exercise window or payout conditions.** For real options, ask for a long post-departure exercise window. For virtual shares, check whether they only pay out on an exit or also on a secondary or buyback.

## Scripts

### When they ask for salary expectations early

> "I'd rather understand the whole package before naming a number, especially the equity. I'm looking at this as a Head of Software role, owning the stack end to end, so I'd expect the combination of base and equity to reflect that. Can you walk me through how you've structured equity for the team so far?"

If pushed for a figure:

> "For this scope in Zurich I'd be looking at around CHF 180k to 185k base, with flexibility if the equity is meaningful. I know that's high for a seed-stage company, which is why I'm open to shifting some of it into equity."

### If the base comes in low (say CHF 130k to 145k)

> "Thanks, I can see you're being careful with runway, and I respect that. That base is quite a bit below what this scope pays in Zurich, so to make it work the equity side needs to carry more weight. Could we get the grant to 1.5% with double-trigger acceleration, and add a base review tied to the next funding round?"

### Pushing for the title

> "The posting describes ownership of the whole software stack and working directly with the CEO and CTO on direction. I'd like the title to say that, Head of Software, so it's clear to the team, to future hires and to investors who owns what."

### The commute (the Geneva factor)

There's no geographic discount to fight off here, since Zurich pays at least as much as Geneva. The real issue is the cost and time of commuting.

> "I'm based in Geneva, so three days a week in Zurich is about 15 hours of travel a week. I'm willing to do it because the role is worth it, but I'd like the company to cover a GA travelcard, and for the first six months to agree on two fixed office days plus customer site visits as needed. If we get to a point where relocation makes sense, I'd like to talk about relocation support then."

A 2nd-class GA costs roughly CHF 4k per year (check the current SBB price); 1st class is significantly more and is where you'd actually work during the trip. Asking for it is reasonable.

### Competing offer template

> "I want to be transparent: I'm in late-stage conversations for Staff/Principal roles in Zurich with significantly higher cash. Solskin is the one where I'd have real ownership and where the code runs on buildings, which matters to me. I'm not asking you to match big-tech cash, but I'd need the equity and terms to close enough of the gap that it's a reasonable bet."

Only use this if it's true at the time. There are live applications at Google and Microsoft Zurich, so it may well be.

### Closing

> "If we can land on CHF 170k, 1.25% with double-trigger acceleration, the Head of Software title and the travelcard, I'm ready to sign this week."

## Watch-outs

- The posting lists hard requirements outside the profile (STM32 firmware, PyTorch control on physical systems). If they hire anyway, it'll be for platform, leadership and startup experience. Make sure the offer still reflects the lead scope, rather than them pricing in the gaps.
- Ask for the shareholder or VSOP plan document before signing. A promise of "1%" without the plan rules isn't worth much.
- Runway. If the company has less than 12 months of cash and no round in sight, weight the base more heavily and the equity less.
