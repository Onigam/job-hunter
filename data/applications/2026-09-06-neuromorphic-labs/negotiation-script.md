# Negotiation Script: Neuromorphic Labs, Founding Forward Deployed AI Engineer

## Context to keep in mind
- Seed-stage company ($5.1M raised, led by Flying Fish, with Toyota Ventures and Amplify Partners participating). Cash will be tight, equity is the real lever.
- Role is US-based in the posting (Seattle primary, Bellevue/SF secondary, "United States Remote" listed as an option). If they're open to hiring a Geneva-based remote founding engineer, that's a nonstandard ask on their end too, so there's room to negotiate structure, not just numbers.
- Founding-team title plus "meaningful equity upside" language in the posting signals they expect this hire to negotiate like an early employee, not a line hire. Use that.
- No published salary band. Anchor first, don't wait for their number.

## Market anchors (Switzerland-based senior IC, adjusted for seed-stage US remote reality)
- A Senior Staff / founding engineer with 15+ years and an exited startup on their record commands roughly CHF 190,000 to CHF 230,000 base if hired directly onto a Swiss payroll at a well-funded company. Seed-stage startups rarely hit that in cash.
- For a seed-stage US company hiring a European remote founding engineer, realistic cash lands lower than the Swiss benchmark, typically USD 150,000 to 180,000 base, with the gap closed by equity and by not paying Bay Area rent, healthcare, or relocation.
- Geneva cost of living is at least on par with San Francisco once you factor health insurance and taxes on equity gains, which is the actual point to make if they anchor off a "remote discount" assumption.
- Founding engineer equity at seed stage for a non-founder hire typically runs 0.5% to 1.5%, four-year vest, one-year cliff, occasionally with acceleration on a change of control given how early this hire is.

## Opening anchor (say this first, before they name a number)
"Before we get into numbers, I want to be upfront about what I need this to look like. Given the founding scope of this role and what I'd be walking away from, I'm looking at a base around USD 175,000, plus an equity grant in the 1% to 1.5% range with standard four-year vesting. I know seed-stage cash is constrained, so I'm flexible on the split between the two, but the total package needs to reflect that this is a founding hire, not employee number fifty."

## If they come in low on cash
"I get that seed-stage cash is tight, that's not a surprise to me. If the base has to land closer to [their number], I'd want to make that up on the equity side, either a larger grant or a shorter cliff, since equity is the part that actually compensates for the risk I'm taking on a company this early. What room do you have there?"

## If they push the "remote from Switzerland" discount
"I'd push back gently on the idea that Geneva is a discount location. Health insurance here is mandatory and expensive, tax treatment on any equity event is different from the US, and the cost of living tracks pretty closely with the Bay Area once you account for that. I'm not asking for a US Bay Area premium, but I don't think a geographic markdown makes sense either, given I'd also be covering my own setup instead of you paying SF rent or relocation."

## Equity specifics to raise directly
- Ask for the fully diluted percentage, not just share count, since seed-stage cap tables move fast through future rounds.
- Ask whether there's a plan for a follow-on seed extension or Series A in the next 12 to 18 months, since that tells you how much dilution to expect before the grant means anything.
- Push for a one-year cliff, standard, but confirm it in writing rather than assuming.
- Ask about early exercise and an 83(b) election window if it's a US entity issuing options, this matters a lot for a non-US resident and is worth a conversation with a tax advisor before signing anything.

## Competing offer as a bargaining chip (use only if genuinely true, keep it light otherwise)
"I'm currently in later-stage conversations with another company at a similar stage, and comp there is roughly in the range I mentioned. I'd rather join here if the scope and the team are right, but I want to be transparent that I'm comparing on the numbers as well as the mission."

## Closing the loop
"This all sounds like something I want to make work. Can we get the equity terms and the vesting schedule in writing alongside the offer letter, rather than as a follow-up conversation after I've signed? I'd like to review the full picture at once before I give you an answer."
