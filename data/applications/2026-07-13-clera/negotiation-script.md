# Negotiation Script, Clera, Founding Engineer (Potential CTO)

## Context and the thing to resolve before comp

This posting is on-site only, New York, San Francisco or Montreal, and states no visa sponsorship. That's a bigger open question than the salary number: it needs a straight answer about work authorization eligibility in the US or Canada before spending real time on comp, equity or even a first onsite. If sponsorship genuinely isn't available and there's no existing path to work there, no comp package fixes that, and it's worth surfacing early rather than discovering it after several interview rounds.

## Switzerland market anchor (opportunity cost baseline)

- Senior Staff Engineer, Geneva/Zurich, founder track record and an acquisition exit (Alaya/Benevity-level): CHF 170,000-210,000 base at a well-funded company, plus bonus and equity.
- This is the number being walked away from to take an early, unpriced founding-engineer role in a different country. Worth naming once, plainly, not as a threat, just as the baseline the rest of the numbers get compared against.

## US / Montreal market for this specific role

- Founding Engineer with a real CTO path, pre-seed to seed healthtech, NYC or SF: base $150,000-190,000 is the realistic band once a round is closed and there's a specific number to react to rather than "well-funded."
- Montreal typically runs 15-25% below NYC/SF on cash for the same seniority, offset partly by lower cost of living and, depending on citizenship, an easier immigration path than the US.
- Equity for a second technical hire explicitly on a CTO track, described as "owning full technical direction" and acting as "a true technical co-founder," should land meaningfully above a normal early-employee grant, somewhere in the 2%-5% range depending on how much of the product already exists and how far the round has progressed. Below that range is worth questioning given the title and scope on offer.

## Opening anchor (cash)

> "Before we get into numbers, I want to flag directly that I'd need to sort out work authorization for the US or Canada, so let's confirm that's realistic before either of us invests more time. Assuming it is, I'd want to open around $175,000 base, reflecting the Staff-level comp I'd be stepping away from in Geneva, with equity that matches the CTO-track framing in the posting rather than a standard early-hire grant."

## If they come back low on cash, citing pre-seed budget

> "I get that runway is the constraint at this stage, and I'm genuinely open to trading cash for equity if the equity is real. What I'd want instead of a lower number with no path up is a specific trigger, tied to the next round or a defined date, where base moves toward market. I'd rather have that written down than left as a verbal 'we'll revisit it.'"

## Equity negotiation (founding engineer, CTO trajectory)

- Ask for the current cap table before evaluating any percentage in isolation: how much is allocated to the existing technical/product founder, what's reserved for the option pool, and what any prior "first engineer" hires already hold.
- Push for standard 4-year vesting with a 1-year cliff, and ask about early exercise given this is a small enough company that the tax benefit is worth having.
- Get clarity in writing on what "potential CTO" actually means: a defined review point (round close, headcount trigger, time-based) where the title and equity refresh, not an open-ended maybe.
- Ask for single-trigger acceleration on an early acquisition and double-trigger acceleration (change of control plus termination) once there's a larger team, since a second technical hire is more exposed to being sidelined post-acquisition than the original founder.
- Confirm IP assignment explicitly covers any prototype or take-home work done before a formal offer.

## Counter-offer script (verbal or written)

> "Thanks for putting this together, I can see there's real thinking behind the structure already. Given the Staff-level comp I'd be stepping away from and the scope here, full technical ownership with a real CTO path, I'd like to counter at $175,000 base with equity in the 3%-4% range on standard 4-year vesting with a 1-year cliff, plus a written trigger for when the CTO title and any additional equity actually convert. I'm flexible on how the pieces combine, more equity for less cash is fine, as long as the total reflects a co-founder-level bet and not an early-employee package with a bigger job description."

## Relocation and work authorization angle

> "I'm Geneva-based, and I want to be upfront that this only works if my work authorization for the US or Canada is actually sorted, so let's confirm that early rather than late. Assuming it clears, I'd relocate for a role like this, and I'd want a relocation allowance covering the actual move plus a buffer for the visa or immigration process itself, written down rather than assumed."

## Competing offer as leverage (use only if real, or omit entirely)

> "I'm also in conversations for a Staff-level role in Switzerland in the CHF 170,000-190,000 range with no equity uncertainty and no relocation involved. I'd rather build this if the ownership and the equity story genuinely hold up, the problem is more interesting to me than a straight comp comparison, but I want to be transparent about what I'm weighing this against."

## What to hold firm on regardless of the base number

- A written, dated definition of what triggers the "potential" in "potential CTO" actually converting.
- Equity in the founding range (2%-5%), not a standard early-employee grant dressed up with a bigger title.
- Confirmed work authorization and a relocation plan in writing before accepting, not assumed as a detail to figure out later.
- Double-trigger acceleration once the team grows past the first few hires.
