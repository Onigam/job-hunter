# Negotiation Script: Albert (Principal / Distinguished Engineer, path to CTO/co-founder)

## Context

Albert posted the range publicly: up to roughly $750k total, combining base, discretionary bonus, and equity, for a US-based Principal/Distinguished Engineer track with a stated path to CTO or co-founder. That's a US comp band, not a Swiss one, and the negotiation has two separate axes: the headline number itself, and whether being based in Geneva gets used as an excuse to discount it.

## Market anchors

- Geneva cost of living is at or above most major US tech hubs, so there's no legitimate "geographic discount" argument to concede to, unlike negotiating with a company anchored to a lower-cost US region.
- Swiss market data for VP Engineering / CTO-track roles at Zurich and Geneva fintechs (comparably scaled) typically lands CHF 220k-280k base before equity, but that reference point matters less here since Albert's own posted range is US-anchored and already higher.
- For a Distinguished Engineer role with founder-track upside at a profitable, unicorn-valued company, the relevant comparison isn't "senior engineer in Switzerland," it's "founding-level technical hire at a company already past product-market fit." That reframes the entire conversation away from a geography discount and toward ownership and equity.
- Anchor point: aim for the top quartile of the posted range on cash (base + bonus), and push the equity conversation separately since "meaningful equity in a profitable, growing unicorn" is vague enough to need a number attached before agreeing to anything.

## Opening anchor

"I saw the range you posted, up to about $750k all-in, and given the founder-track framing of this role I'd want to be talking near the top of that band on cash, with the equity structured so it actually reflects co-founder-level ownership rather than a standard senior-IC grant. Before I get too specific, can you walk me through how the base/bonus/equity split typically breaks down at that top end, and what the vesting terms look like for someone coming in at this level?"

Wait for their number before naming your own if possible. If they push for a number first: "Given the scope described, I think total comp in the $650k-750k range makes sense, and I'd want most of the flexibility to sit in the equity component since that's where a profitable company like Albert can afford to be generous without it costing you cash."

## Counter-offer script if they come in low

"I appreciate the offer, and I want to be direct: the base and bonus land below where I'd expect for a role described as founder-track at a company with your scale and profitability. My working assumption going in was the top of the range you'd posted publicly, closer to $700k-750k all-in. Can you tell me what's driving the number where it landed, so I understand whether there's room on cash, or whether the gap is meant to be closed on the equity side instead?"

If they cite geography: "I'd push back gently on that. Geneva isn't a lower cost-of-living market than the US hubs you're likely benchmarking against, so I don't think location is the right lever here. If the constraint is internal banding, I'm open to structuring more of this as equity rather than cash, but I'd want that equity priced and vested on terms that reflect the founder-track nature of the role, not a standard new-hire grant."

## Equity negotiation

- Ask directly: current fully diluted share count, most recent 409A valuation, and whether Albert has any anti-dilution protections that would affect a new equity grant (unicorn-stage companies sometimes have preferred stock stacks that meaningfully affect what common or option-holder equity is actually worth).
- Standard 4-year vest with a 1-year cliff is the baseline to expect. For a role explicitly pitched as path-to-co-founder, ask whether there's a mechanism for equity refresh or re-grant tied to milestones (shipping the first net-new product, hitting a growth target) rather than only tenure-based vesting.
- Get clarity on what "co-founder" actually means in equity terms if that path materializes: is there a separate, larger grant at that point, or is the current grant meant to already reflect that possibility. Don't let "path to co-founder" stay a verbal promise with no equity mechanism behind it.
- Ask for the strike price and confirm whether early exercise is allowed, since that affects the tax treatment meaningfully if the equity does appreciate.

## Geographic discount pushback

If any version of "we'd need to adjust for your location" comes up: "I've done the math on this before. Geneva rent, healthcare, and general cost of living put it in the same range as SF or NYC, sometimes higher depending on the category. I'm not asking for a US-hub premium, I'm asking that location not be used as a reason to go below what the role itself is worth given the scope and the path you've described."

## Competing offer leverage template

Use only if there's an actual competing process in motion, even an early-stage one: "I want to be transparent that I'm also in later-stage conversations with another company at a comparable total comp level, so timeline and the actual number both matter to me right now. I'd rather make a decision based on where Albert lands than let this drag, so if there's flexibility on your end, sooner is better for both of us."

If no competing offer exists, don't fabricate one. Instead lean on the specificity of the role itself: "Given the founder-track scope here, I want to make sure the offer reflects that this isn't a standard senior-IC hire, and I'd rather resolve that clearly now than revisit it in six months."
