# Negotiation Script — Amigo AI / Applied AI Engineer (Europe)

## Company Context

Amigo is Series A (Madrona + General Catalyst). That's a credible raise from serious investors, but it's still a startup: likely 50-150 people, burn-conscious, probably not yet at unicorn compensation levels. The role is listed as "2+ years experience" which is a mid-level bar, but the actual work (context graphs, clinical agent evaluation, adversarial testing for patient-facing systems) requires senior engineering judgment to do safely. This discrepancy is negotiating room.

They're Amsterdam-based but this is a remote Europe role. That matters: they're not paying Amsterdam cost-of-living, but they're also not paying San Francisco rates. Geneva is one of the most expensive cities in Europe. That's a legitimate lever.

---

## Market Rate Research

**Remote Europe, Applied AI Engineer (Series A healthcare AI), 2025-2026:**

- Mid-level (3-6 years): €85k-€120k
- Senior (7-12 years): €120k-€155k
- Staff / principal (12+ years, founding experience): €155k-€200k
- Healthcare / regulated domain premium: +10-15%

**Magino's positioning:** Staff level. 17+ years, co-founded a platform acquired by a $1B+ company, led AI workflow adoption across production engineering teams. The fact that the job listing says "2+ years" doesn't change what level of work he'd actually be doing.

**Target range:** €160,000-€180,000 base
**Opening anchor:** €175,000
**Walk-away floor:** €140,000 (below this, the role doesn't justify the Geneva cost basis)

---

## Opening Anchor Script

When they ask for salary expectations, don't give a range first. Anchor high with a single number:

> "Based on my background and the market for this type of work in Europe, I'm looking at €175,000. That reflects my seniority, the regulated domain you're operating in, and the Geneva cost basis. I'm open to a conversation about how equity factors into the total picture."

If they push back with "that's above our band":

> "I'd be curious where your band sits. Given the scope of what you're describing, especially the reliability requirements for patient-facing systems, I think it's worth talking through the full structure."

---

## Counter-Offer Scripts

**Scenario A: They come in at €90k-€110k (treating this as a mid-level role)**

> "I appreciate the offer. I want to be direct: that range works for someone with two to three years of experience. Given what I'm bringing, specifically the production AI systems background and founding-engineer context, I'd need to be at €160k to make this work. I'm also happy to talk about how equity closes the gap."

**Scenario B: They come in at €120k-€135k**

> "That's closer. I was thinking €160k based on the scope and my background. Is there flexibility there? Even moving to €145k would make this much easier to say yes to."

**Scenario C: They come in at €140k-€150k**

> "I think we can get there. My number was €175k, which I'd bring down to €160k given the equity component and the nature of the work. If you can get to €155k with a reasonable equity package, I'm in."

---

## Equity Negotiation

At Series A with a company at this stage and investor profile, meaningful equity is reasonable to expect.

**What to ask for:**
- 0.15%-0.30% depending on how they define the role (if they hire Magino as effectively a senior/staff IC, 0.15-0.20% is appropriate)
- 4-year vesting, 1-year cliff (standard, don't accept anything shorter on the cliff)
- Ask about the current share price and last valuation (Madrona + GC, so probably $50M-$150M post-Series A)

**Script:**
> "On equity: what does the package look like? I want to understand the vesting schedule and where you are in terms of the last 409A. For a role at this level I'd be expecting something in the 0.15-0.25% range, but I want to understand the full picture before reacting to a number."

**If they push back on equity percentage:**
> "I understand the band has constraints. Can we look at an accelerated vesting cliff or a higher initial grant offset by a slightly lower strike? I'm flexible on structure."

**Anti-dilution / pro-rata rights:** These are usually not available below VP level. Don't ask for them unless the role becomes a VP Engineering title. But do ask about information rights and whether you'd have any participation in future rounds as an early employee.

---

## Geographic Premium Script

If they push back on the Geneva basis:

> "I get that you're pricing this as a remote role, not a Geneva role. But my cost structure is Geneva: 2,500+ CHF per month in rent, high cantonal taxes, Swiss healthcare. Remote work doesn't change my fixed costs. The €175k ask isn't arbitrary; it's what makes this work practically. I'm not asking for a San Francisco number, I'm asking for a number that makes sense for where I actually live."

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

If you have or are pursuing other offers, use them directly. If not, frame the market honestly:

> "I'm in conversations with a couple of other companies at roughly this stage. I haven't accepted anything, and I'd prefer to go with you if the numbers work, but I want to be transparent that I'm not holding the line for no reason."

If pressed for specifics: don't fabricate a number. Instead:

> "I'd rather not put a specific number from another company on the table, but I can say the market is validating the range I gave you."

---

## Non-Salary Items to Negotiate

- **Learning budget**: Job listing mentions it; make sure it's confirmed in writing (CHF 2,000-3,000/year is reasonable for a senior engineer).
- **Home office setup**: Ask for a one-time equipment allowance (€1,000-1,500) if not offered.
- **Contract type**: Clarify whether this is employment or contractor. In Switzerland, a contractor arrangement means you self-fund benefits and social contributions; add 25-30% to your rate expectations if so.
- **Trial period**: Standard Swiss employment has a trial period. Make sure there's a mutual exit clause if the role turns out to be scoped differently than discussed.

---

## When to Walk Away

Walk if:
- Total comp (cash + fair value of equity) lands below €140k equivalent
- No equity, or equity with a cliff longer than 12 months
- Role is contractor-only with no path to employment in a reasonable timeframe
- Scope is actually more junior than the JD implied once you get into specifics
