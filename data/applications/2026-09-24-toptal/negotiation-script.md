# Negotiation Script: Chief Technology Officer @ Toptal (Remote, Europe)

## Context
- Toptal is a talent network founded in 2010 by Taso Du Val (CEO) and Breanden Beneschott. It's fully remote, has no headquarters office, and makes $200M+ in annual revenue according to the posting. It's private and has raised very little outside capital, so it's closer to a profitable, founder-controlled company than a VC-backed startup racing to an exit.
- Role: CTO reporting directly to Taso. Open to Canada, South America and Europe (remote). The LinkedIn listing is tagged Zürich. No salary range is published.
- **The key point:** a remote company that hires across Canada, South America and Europe will likely try to pay a location-adjusted rate. You're in Geneva, one of the most expensive places in their hiring area, so hold on to a Swiss benchmark. Also check early whether the role is a **salaried employment contract (via a Swiss entity or an EOR) or a contractor agreement**. Toptal has historically hired many team members as contractors. In Switzerland a contractor has to pay the full AHV/AVS social charges, pension and accident insurance, and gets no employer contributions, so that's worth roughly 15 to 20% of gross. A contractor rate has to be marked up accordingly.
- Be realistic about your position: you're a strong fit on the hands-on AI and platform side, and weaker on "15+ years of engineering leadership, including CTO/VP." Anchor high, but justify the number with scope and AI depth, not headcount history.

## Your target

- **Walk-away:** CHF 280k base as a Swiss employee (or CHF 330k+ equivalent as a contractor), a performance bonus of at least 15%, and some form of long-term incentive. Below that, the role pays less than a Staff/Principal IC job in Zurich for a lot more responsibility.
- **Target:** CHF 320k base, 20 to 25% target bonus tied to measurable AI outcomes you help define, and equity or a phantom/profit-share plan at CTO level.
- **Opening anchor:** CHF 360k base plus 30% target bonus plus equity. Justify it with a CTO scope at a $200M+ company, direct reporting to the CEO, the rare combination of hands-on agentic AI and platform architecture, and Geneva cost of living.

## Script: when they ask for your expectations

"I've looked at what CTO roles pay at companies with revenue like Toptal's, especially in Switzerland, since that's where I'm based and where I'd be employed. For a CTO scope reporting to Taso, owning AI strategy and the full platform, I'm thinking around CHF 360k base with a meaningful bonus tied to AI outcomes, plus a long-term piece. I'm flexible on the mix. Can you tell me how you're structuring the package, and whether this is an employment contract or a contractor agreement?"

Then stop and let them answer.

## Counter-offer script: if they come in low

"Thanks, I appreciate it. The base you've offered is closer to a VP Engineering band on a European remote scale, and what we've talked about is the full CTO scope: AI strategy, architecture, headcount and reliability, reporting to the CEO. I can move toward your number if more of the package is variable. At [their number], I'd want the target bonus at 30%, tied to metrics we agree on together, like agent coverage on matching and vetting, eval pass rates and cost per placement, plus a clear long-term incentive. Can we look at it that way?"

If they won't move on base: "Understood. Then let's work on the rest: bonus structure, long-term incentive, a guaranteed review at 12 months, and severance terms. At this level those matter as much as base."

## Pushing back on the geographic discount

"I get that you hire across a lot of countries and have to benchmark by location. But the role isn't a location. It's one seat, reporting to the CEO, and I'd be doing the same job from Geneva that anyone would do from Toronto or Lisbon. If the package is adjusted to where I live, it should reflect Swiss costs, not a European average. Geneva is among the most expensive cities in the world, and Swiss social charges and health insurance are real costs I carry."

## Equity and long-term incentives (a private, profitable company is different)

Toptal isn't a typical startup. It's profitable, founder-controlled, and there's no obvious IPO date. So equity only has value if there's a way to turn it into cash. Ask:

1. **What instrument?** Stock options, RSUs, phantom shares or profit-share. For a company that may never IPO, a phantom or profit-linked plan with regular payouts can be worth more than options.
2. **Percentage of fully diluted capital**, and the latest valuation or 409A-style price. A number of units means nothing without the total.
3. **Liquidity:** Have there been secondary sales or buybacks for employees? How often?
4. **Vesting:** 4 years with a 1-year cliff is standard. For a CTO, ask for **double-trigger acceleration** on change of control, plus partial acceleration if you're let go without cause.
5. **Leaver terms:** vested equity stays yours as a good leaver, with a long enough exercise window (ask for 5 to 10 years, not 90 days).
6. **Swiss tax:** options are usually taxed at exercise as income in Switzerland, while real shares held privately may produce a tax-free capital gain. Get advice before signing if the instrument is unclear.

## Competing-offer template (even if hypothetical)

"To be transparent, I'm also in conversations for a Principal platform role and a founding CTO role with Swiss companies, and one of them is moving to an offer. Toptal is my first choice because of the agent work on matching and vetting. That's the problem I most want to work on. I'd like to make this work. Is there room to close the gap on [base / bonus / equity] so I can decide without it being about money?"

Only use this if it's true or close to it. Don't invent numbers from another company.

## Other points to raise

- **Contract:** Swiss employment through a local entity or an EOR (Deel, Remote, etc.) is strongly preferred. If they insist on contractor status, add 15 to 20% and ask for paid time off to be written into the contract.
- **Notice and severance:** a CTO serving at the CEO's discretion should ask for 3 to 6 months of notice or severance.
- **Travel:** a budget for regular in-person time with Taso and the leadership team; a remote CTO still needs time in the same room.
- **AI budget:** agree up front on a model and inference budget for the first 12 months, plus the authority to spend it. The posting mentions model economics, so show you've already thought about it.
- **Start date:** respect your notice period at Benevity; ask for a 4 to 6 week gap if possible.
