# Negotiation Script: Principal ML Platform Engineer, Synthesia

Context that shapes the math: Synthesia raised $200M in a Series E at a $4B valuation, with Nvidia's NVentures among the backers, so cash is not the constraint it would be at an earlier-stage company. The role is based out of London with hybrid or remote options across the UK, Germany, Switzerland, and Ireland, and there's a Zurich office in the mix. That combination, well-funded, GPU-heavy infra, London base salary bands, is worth anchoring carefully, since London numbers and Swiss numbers are not the same conversation.

## Market anchor

- Principal-level platform/infra engineers in London at a company of this size and funding stage typically land in the GBP 130,000-170,000 base range, often with a meaningful equity or RSU component post-Series E.
- Swiss-based Principal or Staff platform engineers, Zurich or Geneva, command CHF 160,000-200,000 base at comparable seniority, reflecting both cost of living and the tighter local talent pool for GPU and ML-infra specialists.
- If the offer is structured against London bands while I work remotely from Geneva, that's a mismatch worth naming early rather than after an offer lands low.
- Opening anchor: CHF 175,000 base if employed or paid through a Swiss entity, or the GBP equivalent adjusted upward for Swiss cost of living if paid via the UK entity, plus equity or RSUs reflecting Principal scope on a team whose output sits directly on the GPU cost line.

## Opening anchor script

"Given the scope, owning platform reliability and cost for the systems researchers train and serve models on, I'd expect a base in the range of CHF 175,000, or the equivalent adjusted for Swiss cost of living if this runs through your UK entity, along with equity or RSUs that reflect Principal-level ownership post-Series E. I know compensation structures vary by entity and location, so I'm flexible on the split. Where does this role currently sit within your bands, and is there flexibility on which entity I'd be employed through given I'm Geneva-based?"

## If they come in low

"Is that number benchmarked against London, or against Zurich and Geneva specifically? Those aren't interchangeable markets, and a platform role sitting this close to your GPU spend and model-serving reliability tends to get priced at a premium wherever the person sits, not a discount for being outside London."

If they hold the line on cash: "I can work with a base closer to your standard band if the equity or RSU grant moves up to reflect Principal scope, and if there's a clear path to review that number again at the next vesting or refresh cycle. What I'd want to avoid is landing at the bottom of both the cash and equity ranges at once for a role this central to model-serving cost and reliability."

## Equity / RSU notes (post-Series E specifics)

- Ask whether the equity component is options or RSUs, since post-Series E companies at this valuation sometimes shift toward RSUs, which changes the tax and liquidity conversation meaningfully in Switzerland.
- Confirm standard 4-year vesting with a 1-year cliff, and ask about refresh grant policy tied to performance or the next funding event rather than assuming it's a one-time grant.
- Given the $4B valuation and Nvidia's involvement as a strategic investor, ask directly about the state of the cap table and whether a near-term exit or next round is realistically on the horizon, since that shapes how to weigh equity against cash.
- Ask about the 409A or Swiss-equivalent valuation used for any option grant, and whether early exercise is available.

## Geographic and entity framing

"I'm Geneva-based and can work UK hours comfortably, either fully remote or with time in Zurich given you have an office there. I'd want to understand which entity I'd actually be employed through, since that affects both the compensation benchmark and my own tax and social security situation, and I don't think a London-market discount makes sense for a role this tied to infrastructure cost and reliability regardless of where I'm sitting."

## Competing offer, if genuinely in play

"I'm in parallel conversations for platform and infrastructure leadership roles at comparable scope. What stands out here is the GPU-scheduling and agent-operable tooling problem specifically, but I want to be upfront about timeline so we can move at a pace that works for both sides."

## Close

A well-funded, post-Series E company with Nvidia as a strategic backer has room to price a Principal platform role properly, the constraint is almost never cash at this stage, it's which market and entity the offer gets benchmarked against. Anchor on Swiss market reality given where I actually live and work, get clarity on which entity employs me, and get base, equity or RSU structure, vesting start date, and refresh policy in writing before accepting. At this funding stage, the equity structure and refresh cadence are worth as much scrutiny as the first year's base salary.
