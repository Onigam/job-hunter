# Negotiation Script — Omnilex AI Engineer, Legal Search

## Market Context

**Posted range:** CHF 7,000–11,000/month + ESOP
**Annual at top of range (13-month Swiss convention):** CHF 143,000/year
**Annual without 13th month:** CHF 132,000/year

Omnilex is a seed-stage company (~14 people, $4.5M raised, ETH Zurich-rooted). Cash comp will be constrained relative to a Series B+, but equity has real upside potential if the product traction continues. The Swiss Federal Chancellery as a customer is a meaningful signal.

**Zurich senior staff market range (comparable roles, 2026):**
- Strong individual contributor at Staff level: CHF 140,000–180,000/year total cash
- At a startup with meaningful equity: lower cash is acceptable, but CHF 11,000/month (143K/year) is the floor to negotiate from, not the ceiling

**Your target:** CHF 11,000/month cash + equity package worth discussing separately.

---

## Opening Anchor

If they ask for your number first, use this framing:

> "Based on my profile and the Zurich market for senior engineers with production AI experience, I'm looking at CHF 11,000/month as a starting point, with equity on top. I'm open to discussing the full picture, because at a company like Omnilex the ESOP can be as meaningful as the base. What does the equity structure look like?"

This anchors at the top of their range, signals flexibility on structure, and immediately pivots to equity before they respond to the cash number.

---

## If They Come In Below 10,000/month

Don't accept the first number. Use this:

> "I appreciate the offer. The role and the problem genuinely interest me, which is why I want to make sure we find something workable. CHF [X] is below what I'd need to move from my current position. I'd be comfortable at 10,500, and I'd want to offset the delta with a higher ESOP allocation if the cash flexibility isn't there. What's possible on the equity side?"

This keeps the conversation going, doesn't reject outright, and introduces equity as a lever.

---

## Equity Negotiation

Omnilex is at seed stage with ~14 people. Standard ESOP ask for an early senior hire:

**Target:** 0.3–0.7% of the company (fully diluted), 4-year vesting, 1-year cliff.

**Questions to ask before signing anything:**
- What percentage of the company is the total ESOP pool?
- What's the current fully-diluted cap table structure?
- What's the last valuation or the most recent round price per share?
- Is there a 409A equivalent (or Swiss fair value assessment)?
- What happens to vested options on acquisition (acceleration clauses)?
- Are the options ISOs or NSOs, or Swiss equivalent? What's the tax treatment?

**Script for equity conversation:**

> "I want to understand the equity properly before we finalize. What percentage of the fully diluted cap table does this ESOP grant represent? And what was the share price at the last round? I want to be able to put a realistic floor on the value rather than just treating it as speculative upside."

If they're vague or say "standard grant":

> "I understand each company has its own structure. I'm just trying to avoid surprises. Even a rough sense of where the grant sits relative to the total pool would help me make the right decision."

## Geographic Note (Geneva vs. Zurich)

Zurich and Geneva are comparable in cost of living, but there's a commute cost to factor in if Magino is working hybrid from Geneva.

If they push back on the cash ask:

> "I'm also accounting for regular travel to Zurich from Geneva. If I'm commuting two days a week, that's a real cost on top of the base. That's part of why I want the cash number to reflect the full picture."

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

If you have other processes running (or want to create urgency without fabricating):

> "I'm at a late stage with a couple of other companies, and I'd like to make a decision by end of [month]. If we can agree on terms by [date], I can close the other processes. The Omnilex role is the one I'm most interested in for the domain, but I want to make sure the comp works before I step back from the others."

---

## Closing Move

If the offer is close but not quite there:

> "I'm very close to saying yes. The one thing I'd need is [specific ask: 500 CHF/month more, or 0.1% more equity, or an earlier cliff review]. If that's possible, I'll sign today."

Never negotiate more than two items at once. Pick the one that matters most and close on it.

---

## Walk-Away Threshold

Given Magino's profile (17+ years, founding engineer, acquisition exit):

- Hard floor: CHF 9,500/month + meaningful equity
- Acceptable if equity is genuinely meaningful: CHF 9,000/month + 0.5%+ ESOP
- Below CHF 9,000/month: not worth the commute to Zurich without exceptional equity terms
