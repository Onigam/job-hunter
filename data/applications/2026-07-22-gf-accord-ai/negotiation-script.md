# Negotiation Script, GF-ACCORD AI, CTO and Head of IT (AI)

## What's on the table

Posted range: $110k-170k, no equity. Stealth, pre-funding startup, 1-10 employees, Geneva-based, founder is sole named executive (Georgios Fradelos). Visa sponsorship offered, start expected in 3-4 months.

Two things stand out before any negotiation starts. First, $110k-170k converts to roughly CHF 97k-150k, which is below Geneva market rate for a CTO or Head of IT title even at a tiny startup, let alone one asking for 10+ years of AI/NLP experience and a PhD-preferred bar. Second, "no equity" at a company that is explicitly in the fundraising phase is unusual for a C-level hire; founders normally use equity precisely because they can't yet pay market cash. That combination (senior title, high experience bar, sub-market cash, zero equity) is the thing to push on, not just the number.

## Switzerland market anchors

- Geneva/Zurich CTO or VP Engineering, early-stage startup (pre-Series A, under 10 people): CHF 150k-220k base, typically plus equity in the 1-4% range for a first non-founder technical hire.
- Geneva Senior Staff / Principal Engineer (individual contributor, no C-level title): CHF 140k-190k base, comparable to the top of GF-ACCORD's posted range without any of the CTO scope or title.
- Geneva cost of living is consistently ranked among the highest in Europe; recruiters and comp benchmarks (Glassdoor CH, Levels.fyi Europe data, local recruiter reports) generally treat Geneva and Zurich as the top tier within Switzerland, not a discount tier.
- Read: the posted range prices this role closer to a senior IC than a CTO. That gap is the opening.

## Opening anchor

"Thanks for sharing the range. Based on what Geneva pays for a CTO-level hire with this scope, plus the experience bar you're asking for, I'd expect something closer to CHF 190k-210k base. I know you're pre-funding, so I'm open to structuring part of that as equity or a deferred component tied to the raise, but the all-cash number as posted is below what this role would need to clear the market here."

## If they come in low

"I hear that cash is tight before the round closes, that's normal at this stage. A few ways we could bridge it: a lower base now with a defined step-up once you close funding, a signing bonus to offset the gap in year one, or meaningful equity to compensate for the below-market cash. What I'd want to avoid is a structure where I'm below market on both cash and equity at once, since that's really two discounts stacked on top of each other."

## Equity, despite "no equity" in the posting

- Don't accept "no equity" as final for a CTO/Head of IT title at a company actively fundraising. Ask directly why the role is cash-only: is it a legal/cap-table constraint pre-round, or a deliberate choice?
- If it's pre-round cap table cleanliness, propose an option grant or founder-agreement side letter that vests once the round closes, rather than nothing.
- Target for a first non-founder C-level hire at this stage: 1-3% on a 4-year vest, 1-year cliff, with standard acceleration language if the company is acquired or the founder brings in a new CEO above the CTO.
- Ask about anti-dilution protection for early hires in future rounds, and get the option pool size and current cap table structure in writing before agreeing to a percentage, a small pool means a given percentage is worth far less after the first real raise.

## Geographic discount pushback

If the range gets justified as "we can hire remotely from cheaper markets, so this reflects blended pricing": "I understand you're hiring across multiple cities, but I'm Geneva-based and the role is titled as based here, with in-person or near-in-person expectations for a CTO. Pricing a Geneva CTO seat at a blended international rate doesn't reflect what it actually costs to retain someone locally, and it's the kind of gap that tends to resurface at the next negotiation."

## Competing offer template

Use only if genuinely in play, even informally: "I want to be straightforward that I'm in later-stage conversations with another company for a comparable platform leadership role, at a materially higher total package. I'd rather build something at the ground floor here if the structure makes sense, but I need the offer to be close enough that it's a real choice rather than a clear step down."

## Close

Confirm role scope (Head of IT and CTO combined, or is CTO the eventual title once the round closes and the team grows), get the equity conversation in writing even if the number isn't finalized yet, and get the start-date flexibility (3-4 months out) confirmed in case the funding timeline shifts.
