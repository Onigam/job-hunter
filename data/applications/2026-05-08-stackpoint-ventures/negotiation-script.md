# Negotiation Script — Stackpoint Ventures Principal Engineer

## Context
Stackpoint is a US-based venture studio (Boston, remote-distributed) building AI-native companies in legacy verticals. The role is likely compensated in USD with equity in the studio or its portfolio companies. Geneva cost of living applies as a negotiation lever.

---

## Market Anchors (2026)

**Principal / Staff+ Engineer — Remote US (in USD):**
- P50: $200k-$220k base
- P75: $225k-$250k base
- P90: $250k-$280k base

**Principal / Staff+ Engineer — Switzerland / Geneva (in CHF):**
- Senior Staff at Swiss tech company: CHF 190k-240k base
- Comparable Geneva market rate: CHF 210k-250k
- Purchasing power adjustment vs. US remote: Geneva is ~30-40% more expensive than median US tech hub

**Venture Studio Specifics:**
- Studios often pay 10-15% below top FAANG/unicorn market base
- They compensate with portfolio equity (can be highly valuable or worthless)
- Ask how equity is structured: studio-level vs. per-company, vesting on what trigger

---

## Opening Anchor

When asked about salary expectations, state a number above your target and justify it:

> "Given my background — founding engineer with a $1B+ exit, 17 years in distributed systems and platform engineering, and current AI tooling expertise — and the cost structure in Geneva, I'd be looking at a base in the range of $220k-$240k USD. I'm open to discussion if the equity and overall package makes sense."

**Do not go lower than $190k base without meaningful equity offset.**

---

## If They Come In Low (below $190k)

> "That's below what I'd need to make the move work from Geneva, where the cost of living is genuinely high. I'm not looking for FAANG comp, but $X [their number] would represent a significant step back from what I can access locally. Is there flexibility on the base, or can we make up the gap on the equity side?"

Then ask specifically:
- What does the equity look like at the studio level?
- Is there per-company equity as well?
- What's the vesting schedule and cliff?

---

## Equity Negotiation

For a venture studio, equity is more complex than a standard startup offer. Get answers to:

1. **What are you offering?** Studio equity (shares in Stackpoint itself) or per-company options (in each portfolio company)?
2. **Standard 4-year vest / 1-year cliff?** If non-standard, push back.
3. **Valuation basis?** Studio equity at what most recent 409A or preferred share price?
4. **Dilution protection?** Pro-rata rights on future rounds?

Script for equity discussion:

> "I want to understand the equity structure before I compare this to other offers. When you say X% or X shares, what's the underlying vehicle? Is this studio equity, portfolio company equity, or a combination? And what triggers vesting — time, milestones, company events?"

If they offer per-company equity:
> "Per-company equity in a studio context is interesting but harder to value. The portfolio bet is real but it's spread thin. For that to offset a lower base, I'd want to understand the portfolio size, typical ownership stake, and what liquidity events look like in practice."

---

## Geographic Discount Pushback

If they try to apply a Geneva-to-US location discount (paying less because you're not in a US tech hub):

> "I'd push back on applying a geographic discount here. Geneva is consistently one of the most expensive cities in the world to live in, with cost of living comparable to San Francisco or New York. A location adjustment that goes in the wrong direction doesn't match the data."

Supporting numbers if needed:
- Geneva ranked top-3 most expensive cities globally (Mercer, EIU)
- CHF to USD purchasing power is roughly 1:1 or better for tech comp purposes
- Swiss income tax + health insurance adds real costs not present in most US remote arrangements

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

Even without a competing offer in hand, you can use market data:

> "I'm actively in conversations with a few other opportunities at the staff and principal level — some in the Geneva market, some remote. What I'm looking at there is in the $210k-$230k range for base. I'd want to get to something comparable here before making a decision, because this role is genuinely interesting to me."

If you have a real competing offer: use it directly. Name the range, not the company, and give Stackpoint a 48-hour window to match or explain why they can't.

---

## Non-Salary Terms Worth Negotiating

- **Remote setup stipend:** CHF 2k-3k one-time, or CHF 100-200/month
- **Conference/learning budget:** $5k-$10k/year is standard at this level
- **Equity acceleration on acquisition:** if the studio or a portfolio company gets acquired, full acceleration of unvested grants
- **Clarity on which companies you'll work with:** ask for right-of-first-refusal to stay on if a portfolio company spins out and hires its own team

---

## Bottom Line

- Walk-away floor: $185k USD base (or CHF equivalent), with meaningful equity
- Target: $220k-$240k base + studio equity
- Stretch: $240k+ with portfolio company equity upside and remote setup terms
- Never accept a below-market number based on "this is a studio, it's different" — the work is full Principal Engineer work, the compensation should reflect it
