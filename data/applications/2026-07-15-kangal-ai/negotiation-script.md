# Negotiation Script: Kangal AI, AI Founding Engineer

## Context

Posted range: £71k to £130k base, plus 0.0% to 1.0% equity. London, onsite or remote. Team size 1-10 people, national security / government AI focus, early stage. The bottom of that band reads like a generic "AI engineer" hire, not a founding engineer with a Senior Staff background and an acquisition on the resume. The gap between £71k and £130k is also wide enough that it tells you the company hasn't nailed down what they're paying for yet, which is exactly the moment to anchor high with a clear story.

## Market anchors

**UK / London:** Founding or early engineers at seed to Series A AI startups in London typically land £110k-£150k base for someone at Staff level with a track record, sometimes lower cash if equity is heavier. National security and defense-adjacent AI shops tend to pay slightly below pure commercial AI labs in base but compensate with mission and, sometimes, contract-backed stability.

**Switzerland comparison:** Magino is based in Geneva, where a Senior Staff engineer with founder experience commands CHF 160k-200k base in a comparable local market, and Swiss cost of living runs meaningfully higher than London's. If the role is remote or hybrid with Geneva as home base, that's the right anchor to bring up, not the London floor. If it's onsite in London, use the UK range but push toward the top given the founding-engineer scope and the acquisition track record.

**Framing for both:** don't lead with "I need more because Geneva is expensive." Lead with the scope of the role, founding engineer means architecture, hiring, and technical direction, not just shipping tickets, and the comp should reflect that scope regardless of which city anchors it.

## Opening anchor

"I'm glad the range has room to move, because the top of it is closer to where I'd expect to land. Founding engineer roles at this stage usually come with architecture ownership and early hiring, which is the same scope I had at Alaya before the Benevity acquisition. I'd want to start the conversation around £135k to £145k base, with equity scaled to match the ownership expected of the role, and I'm open to structuring it differently if there's a stronger equity story you'd rather lean into."

## If they come in low

"I hear that £71k to £90k is where you're starting most candidates. Given the founding scope here, architecture decisions, technical hiring, and being the person who owns correctness on a product where mistakes have real consequences, I think this sits closer to a Staff-level hire than a mid-level one. Could we look at £125k as a starting point and revisit after the first six months once the scope is confirmed in practice?"

## Equity negotiation

- Ask for the fully diluted percentage, not just the raw number, before agreeing to anything. 0.5% pre-seed and 0.5% post-Series-A are very different offers.
- Standard structure to ask for: four-year vesting, one-year cliff. Push back on anything longer than a one-year cliff for a founding hire.
- Ask directly whether there's a plan for anti-dilution protection through future rounds, and whether early employees get pro-rata rights.
- For a defense/national-security-adjacent company, ask about exit paths explicitly, acquisition by a larger defense contractor or prime is a realistic outcome and changes how equity should be valued versus a typical consumer SaaS exit.
- If cash is capped near the bottom of the range, ask for the equity percentage to move up correspondingly rather than accepting both figures at the low end.

## Geographic and remote pushback

If they try to anchor purely to London junior-market rates: "I understand the London range, but if this is remote-friendly, I'd ask that we benchmark against where I'm actually based. Geneva rates for this level run higher than London's, and if the expectation is I'm delivering the same architecture and hiring work regardless of time zone, the location discount doesn't really apply here."

## Competing offer as a bargaining chip (use only if true at the time)

"I've got another conversation in progress for a similar founding-level scope that's landed closer to the top of your posted range before equity. I'd rather build here if the scope and comp line up, but I wanted to be upfront about where my other conversations are so we're negotiating with the same information."

## Close

Don't accept on the spot. Ask for 48 hours to review the full written offer, including the equity documentation and vesting schedule, before responding. If the final number is below the anchor but the mission and scope are right, it's fine to accept with a written commitment to revisit comp after a defined milestone, first funding round closed, or six months in, whichever comes first.
