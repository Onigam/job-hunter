# Negotiation Script — fonio.ai Founding Product Engineer

---

## Context

- **Posted range:** €70K-€100K + equity
- **Company stage:** ~€20M raised, ~11,000 customers, 50 people, described as "top 1% in growth"
- **Role:** Founding Product Engineer, remote within ±3h CET
- **Your profile:** 17 years experience, prior founding engineer (Alaya, acquired by unicorn), Senior Staff at Benevity
- **Your location:** Geneva, Switzerland (high COL; Swiss senior engineer market is CHF 150K-180K)

---

## Market Rate Reality Check

The published range (€70K-€100K) reflects Austrian/EU remote market positioning. For context:

- Senior Staff Engineer in Geneva: CHF 150K-180K base
- €100K at current EUR/CHF rates: ~CHF 105K
- The gap is real, but this is a remote role and fonio sets compensation on EU market rates

**The play:** Don't frame this as "I deserve Swiss rates." Frame it as "I'm at the top of your range by experience, and here's why that matters for this specific role."

---

## Opening Anchor

If they ask for your number first:

> "Based on the role scope and what I've seen of the team, I'm thinking around €115K base. I'm flexible depending on how equity is structured — if the equity is meaningful, I'd trade some base for more upside. What's the range you're working with?"

If they reveal their range first (€70K-€100K):

> "I'm probably at the top end of that range, given the founding-engineer scope and the 17 years I'd bring on day one. Would you have flexibility above €100K, or is that a hard ceiling? If there's not much room on base, let's talk about the equity piece."

---

## Counter-Offer Scripts

**Scenario A: They offer €80K-€85K**

> "I appreciate the offer. The role is compelling, and I want to make this work. €85K is below where I'd expect to land given the seniority and the fact that I'd be functioning as a founding engineer, not just a senior IC. Can we get to €95K-€100K? That feels more aligned with the scope."

**Scenario B: They offer €95K-€100K (top of range)**

> "€100K works as a base. The thing I'd want to think through with you is the equity side — at this stage of the company, that's where a lot of the upside lives. What's the current structure for an engineer joining at this level?"

**Scenario C: They say range is firm**

> "Understood. If the base is fixed at €100K, I'd want to make sure the equity grant reflects the founding-engineer scope. Is there room to adjust the grant size or the vesting schedule?"

---

## Equity Negotiation

**What to ask for:**
- Grant size: 0.2%-0.5% is reasonable for a founding product engineer at this stage (post-seed, scaling fast)
- Vesting: 4 years with 1-year cliff is standard; push for monthly vesting after cliff if possible
- Strike price: ask for the current 409A/fair market valuation to understand what "equity" actually means in EUR
- Anti-dilution: ask if there's any pro-rata protection for future rounds (rare, but worth asking at this level)
- Acceleration: double-trigger acceleration on acquisition (protects you if the company is acquired early)

**Script to open the equity conversation:**

> "Can you walk me through the equity structure? Specifically the grant size, strike price, current valuation, and vesting schedule. I want to understand what the potential upside looks like before I can properly evaluate the total package."

**If equity is small (under 0.1%):**

> "I'd like to think through the equity a bit more. If the grant is under 0.1%, the financial upside is modest relative to the base trade-off I'd be making coming from a larger company. Is there room to increase the grant, or would the vesting structure be flexible?"

---

## Geographic Premium Pushback

If they justify the range by saying it's a remote role:

> "I understand the range reflects a remote market rate. I'm not asking for Geneva-adjusted compensation. I am asking for a rate that reflects 17 years of experience, a prior founding build that ended in an acquisition, and the fact that in this role I'd be operating at the top of the scope. Those things don't change based on where I sit."

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

Even without a live offer, you can create urgency:

> "I'm in conversations with a couple of other companies at the moment. Nothing I'm committed to, but I want to be transparent that I'm moving on a roughly two-week timeline. If fonio is the right fit, I'd rather close quickly than let this drag."

If you have an actual competing offer:

> "I have an offer from [Company] at €[X]. fonio is genuinely my preference because of [specific reason: voice AI direction / founding scope / team]. If you can get to €[Y], I can close this week."

---

## Red Lines

- Don't accept below €90K base given the founding engineer scope and experience level
- If base is stuck at €80K or below, require equity above 0.3% to compensate
- Require written equity terms before signing — verbal promises on equity are meaningless
- No cliff longer than 12 months; push for monthly vesting after cliff

---

## Closing Script

> "I want to be straightforward: fonio is a genuine fit for me. The voice AI direction, the product ownership model, the team size. I'm not playing games with the number. I'd like to land at €100K+ with a meaningful equity grant, and if that's achievable, I can commit quickly. What does the path to offer look like from here?"
