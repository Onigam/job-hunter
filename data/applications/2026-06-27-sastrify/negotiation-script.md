# Negotiation Script — Tech Lead @ Sastrify

---

## Market Context

Sastrify is a DACH-focused SaaS startup (remote EMEA). The role is Tech Lead, non-managerial, high IC. No salary published.

**Switzerland market benchmarks for this seniority level (2025-2026):**
- Senior/Staff Engineer, Geneva/Zurich: CHF 160k-210k total comp
- Tech Lead / IC6 equivalent (remote, DACH startup): €110k-€145k EUR base
- For Geneva-based candidates at Staff+ level: CHF 170k-200k is a defensible ask

**Key asymmetry:** Sastrify likely budgets in EUR. Magino is based in Geneva. Switzerland has among the highest costs of living in Europe, which is a legitimate negotiation lever even for remote roles.

---

## Opening Anchor

When asked about salary expectations, aim to anchor in CHF to frame the conversation correctly:

> "Based on my experience level and the Geneva market, I'm targeting CHF 190,000-200,000 total compensation. I know Sastrify likely operates in EUR internally, so I'm happy to work through the conversion together, but I want to make sure we're accounting for Switzerland's cost structure from the start."

If they insist on EUR:
> "In EUR terms, the equivalent range would be around €180,000-€190,000. That's higher than a Berlin or Munich anchor, and it reflects that I'm based in one of the most expensive cities in Europe."

If they want a single number:
> "I'd say €185,000 is a good starting point."

---

## If They Come In Low (under CHF 150k / €140k)

**Script:**

> "I appreciate the offer. The number is a bit below what I was expecting, and I want to be transparent about that rather than pretend it's not an issue. My benchmarking puts the Geneva market for this level at CHF 170k-200k. Is there room to move closer to CHF 170k on base, and if there are constraints there, could we talk about what else is in the package?"

**What to negotiate beyond base:**
- Signing bonus (a one-time bridge is easier for the company to say yes to)
- Extra vacation days (30 is their standard; ask for 32-35 if salary is stuck)
- Learning budget (they offer €1,000 annually; push to €2,000-3,000 for a lead)
- Equipment budget upgrade
- Flexibility on working hours and async default

---

## Equity Negotiation (if applicable)

Sastrify appears to be a funded startup. If equity is on the table:

**Questions to ask first:**
- What is the current valuation, and when was the last funding round?
- What percentage of the company does this options grant represent?
- What is the strike price, and how was it set?
- What is the vesting schedule? Is there a cliff?

**Reasonable expectations for a Tech Lead / Staff-level hire at a Series A-B startup:**
- 0.1%-0.3% of the company in options is a reasonable range
- 4-year vesting with a 1-year cliff is standard; push back on anything longer than 4 years
- Ask whether there is acceleration on acquisition (single or double trigger)

**If the equity grant feels small:**
> "The options package is below what I'd expect for a lead-level IC at this stage. If the base has a ceiling, I'd want to see the equity reflect the seniority of the role. Could you walk me through how you determined the grant size relative to the cap table?"

---

## Geographic Discount Pushback

Startups sometimes apply a geographic discount to remote workers in expensive locations, reducing Geneva-level pay to a "European average." Push back on this directly:

> "I understand some companies use geographic pay bands for remote roles. My concern with that approach here is that I'm in Geneva, which is consistently one of the top two most expensive cities in Europe. A geographic band calibrated to Berlin or Amsterdam doesn't map to my cost of living. I'd ask that you benchmark against Zurich or Geneva specifically, not a European average."

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

If you have or receive another offer:

> "I want to be transparent: I have another process that's moving to offer stage for a role at [company type, e.g., Series B SaaS]. Their package is in the CHF 185k-190k range. Sastrify is my preference because of the technical depth and the LLM/agent work, but I want to make sure we can get to a number that doesn't require me to take a meaningful step back."

Even without a competing offer, this framing works as a soft anchor:

> "I'm in conversations with a couple of other companies at a similar stage, so I want to move efficiently. If the package can get to CHF 175k+, I'm ready to wrap this up quickly."

---

## Red Flags to Watch For

- No equity at a startup of this stage: a meaningful signal about how they value senior IC hires
- Salary in EUR with no Swiss adjustment: worth flagging explicitly, not just accepting
- Probation period longer than 3 months: standard in Switzerland is 3 months
- No clarity on how compensation scales over time: ask what the typical trajectory looks like for a Tech Lead at Sastrify in years 2 and 3
