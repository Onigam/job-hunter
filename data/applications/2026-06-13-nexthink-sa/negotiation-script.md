# Negotiation Script — Nexthink Staff Software Engineer

## Market Context

Nexthink is publicly traded (NASDAQ: NXTK), headquartered in Lausanne with ~1,000 employees. Staff/Senior Staff Software Engineering roles at public B2B SaaS companies in the Lake Geneva area typically land in the following ranges (2025-2026 market):

| Level | Base (CHF) | Total Comp (CHF, incl. RSU + bonus) |
|-------|-----------|--------------------------------------|
| Senior Engineer | 140,000–165,000 | 160,000–200,000 |
| Staff Engineer | 165,000–200,000 | 200,000–260,000 |
| Principal/Senior Staff | 195,000–230,000 | 250,000–320,000 |

Magino fits the Staff-to-Principal range: 17 years of experience, co-founding track record, acquisition exit, current AI/platform focus. Lausanne is ~10-15% below Zurich rates, but Nexthink's public-company comp structure and enterprise client base support the upper half of the Staff range.

---

## Opening Anchor

**Use this if they ask for a number first — which they often do.**

> "Based on what I know of the Lausanne market for Staff-level roles in B2B SaaS, and given my background combining founding-engineer experience with Staff-level work at Benevity post-acquisition, I'd be looking at a base in the range of CHF 200,000–215,000, with total comp above 250,000 once equity and bonus are factored in. Does that land within the range you have budgeted for this role?"

**Why this works:** It anchors high but reasonably, gives them a range rather than a hard number, and immediately invites reciprocal transparency about their band.

---

## If Their Offer Comes In Below Target (Below CHF 180,000 Base)

> "I appreciate the offer. The base is a bit lower than I'd expected for a Staff role at this level, based on what comparable positions are paying in the Lake Geneva market right now. I'm genuinely interested in the role and want to find a structure that works for both sides. Can we look at closing that gap, either in base or in equity?"

**Follow-up if they push back on market data:**

> "Happy to be transparent: I'm seeing Staff SE roles in Lausanne and Geneva come in at CHF 185,000–205,000 base for engineers at 15+ years with platform and AI experience. I don't need to match the top of that range, but I'd need us to be in the right neighborhood to make it an easy yes."

---

## Equity (RSU / Stock Options)

Nexthink is publicly traded, so compensation likely includes RSUs rather than options.

**What to ask:**

- Total RSU grant value at current stock price
- Vesting schedule: standard is 4 years with 1-year cliff, quarterly after
- Whether there are annual refresh grants for Staff+ engineers
- Whether signing bonus offsets the first-year cliff

**Script:**

> "On the equity side, can you walk me through the RSU grant structure? I want to understand the total value at current price, the vesting schedule, and whether there are annual refreshes for Staff-level engineers. That context changes how I think about the total comp picture."

**If the RSU grant feels low (below CHF 40,000/year in value):**

> "The base is solid, but the equity tranche feels light for a Staff role, especially given the AI-native work you're asking this person to drive. If there's room to move on the RSU grant or front-load it with a signing bonus, that would make the total comp picture work better for me."

---

## Geographic Discount Pushback

Nexthink may try to position this at a discount vs. Zurich because of the Lausanne location. Counter:

> "Lausanne is still one of the most expensive cities in Switzerland; the cost-of-living delta with Zurich is meaningful but not dramatic. I'm not expecting Zurich rates, but I'd push back on any framing that treats Lausanne as a significantly discounted market, especially for senior technical talent."

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

If you have another offer in hand (or expect one soon):

> "To be transparent with you, I'm in late stages with one or two other companies. The Nexthink role is genuinely my first choice because of the technical depth of the problem space. But I want to be honest that if the total comp gap is significant, it becomes a harder decision. Is there flexibility to move on the base or the equity to get us closer?"

If the offer is hypothetical or early-stage:

> "I'm actively interviewing at a few other companies. I don't have a competing offer in hand yet, but I expect to in the next two to three weeks. If there's a way to move quickly and close the gap on compensation, I'd strongly prefer to end my search here."

---

## Non-Salary Levers (Use If Base Is Stuck)

If they can't move on base, push on:

- **Signing bonus**: One-time payment to offset the RSU cliff year. Ask for CHF 20,000–30,000.
- **Title**: Make sure "Staff" is explicit in the contract, not just informal. Matters for future comp benchmarking.
- **Remote flexibility**: Lausanne is a commute from Geneva. Negotiate explicit remote days (3/week minimum) and travel reimbursement.
- **Accelerated RSU vesting**: If performance is above expectations, some companies offer early cliff vesting or larger annual refreshes.
- **Professional development budget**: CHF 5,000–10,000/year for conferences, courses, tooling.

---

## What to Avoid

- Don't give your current salary as justification for a number. Anchor to market data, not history.
- Don't accept on the spot. Ask for 48–72 hours to review.
- Don't negotiate via email alone. Get on a call so you can read the response and adjust in real time.
- Don't reveal your floor. "I could make it work at CHF 175k" removes all leverage.
