# Negotiation Script, Hostaway Principal Engineer, Core Platform EMEA

## Context
The public listing (via RemoteOK) shows an estimated $55,000-$90,000 annually, which does not match a Principal-level, 10+ years, leadership-track role at a company that just raised $365 million while staying profitable. That number is almost certainly an auto-generated site estimate rather than the real band Hostaway would offer, so it should not anchor the conversation at all. Treat it as noise and open from Swiss market data instead. The honest framing going in: "I'm Geneva-based, this is a Principal-level scope with team leadership attached, and I'm pricing it against that, not against a generic EMEA-remote listing."

## Switzerland market anchors
- Principal Engineer, Geneva/Zurich, 10+ years with 2+ in a leadership capacity: CHF 180,000-220,000 base at a well-funded scale-up, before bonus and equity.
- Track record that includes a founder exit (Alaya to Benevity) plus post-acquisition architecture leadership typically supports the top third of that band, closer to CHF 200,000-220,000.
- Remote-from-Switzerland roles for foreign-HQ companies usually land 10-15% under a Zurich-office equivalent, but rarely below CHF 165,000 base at true Principal scope.
- Given the company is profitable and recently raised at scale, there is real budget for this band; a lowball offer here reflects negotiating posture more than actual constraint.

## Opening anchor
Open in CHF, not USD or EUR, and make the leadership scope explicit since Principal titles get diluted easily.

> "I'm Geneva-based, so I'm anchoring to Swiss market rates for a Principal-level IC with team leadership responsibilities, not to a general EMEA-remote band. Given the scope in the JD, driving the monolith-to-microservices roadmap, owning architecture decisions at real transaction volume, and mentoring across teams, I'd want to be around CHF 200,000 base, plus equity and benefits in line with what you'd offer a Principal hire. I know public estimates for this posting have been all over the place, so I wanted to state clearly where I'm starting from."

## If they come back low (at or below CHF 150,000 equivalent)
> "I appreciate you sharing a number, and I want to be straightforward rather than just push back on principle. The gap between that and Swiss Principal-level market rate is significant, and it's not a tactic on my end, it's what the local cost structure and the seniority of this scope actually require. Is there room to move the base toward CHF 180,000, or to close the gap through equity, a sign-on, or an accelerated first review?"

## Geographic discount pushback
Hostaway already operates across 40+ countries, so it has some internal banding logic to point to rather than fight.

> "A remote org this distributed needs some kind of geographic banding, that part makes sense to me. But Geneva sits at the expensive end of Europe, not the EU-remote average, and I'd rather have that reflected in the base now than revisit it in six months once I'm already ramped into the role."

## Equity negotiation (profitable, well-funded, not early-stage)
Hostaway just raised $365 million while profitable, which changes the equity conversation compared to an early-stage startup: the value is more real, but the percentage will be smaller.

- Ask for the strike price and the valuation basis from the most recent round before evaluating any equity number in isolation.
- Push for a standard 4-year vest with a 1-year cliff; a longer cliff at Principal level is worth resisting.
- Ask about early exercise and post-termination exercise windows; the standard 90 days is thin at this level, 1-2 years is a reasonable ask.
- Ask for the grant as a percentage of fully diluted shares outstanding, not just a headline option count, since a large-sounding number means little without that context.

## Counter-offer script
> "Thanks for putting an offer together, I can see there's real thought behind the scope. Based on Swiss market rates for a Principal-level architecture and leadership role, and the size of the platform I'd be responsible for, I'd like to counter at CHF 195,000 base, with the equity grant adjusted upward if the base has a hard ceiling. I'm open to how the total package is structured, base, equity, or a sign-on, as long as the total reflects the seniority and ownership this role carries."

## Competing offer as a bargaining chip (use only if real, or omit)
> "I'm in later-stage conversations with another company at a comparable level, where the base is tracking around CHF 205,000. I'd rather move forward here if the numbers can get close, the platform problem at Hostaway is the more interesting one, but I want to be transparent about where things stand."

## What to hold firm on regardless of base outcome
- Title stays Principal Engineer, not a Staff-equivalent role with a Principal-sounding job description.
- Written confirmation that architecture ownership over the microservices roadmap is a real mandate, not just influence, ideally reflected in the first 90-day plan.
- Fully remote confirmed in writing, with no assumption of relocation or return-to-office tied to a future milestone.
- Clarity on reporting line and whether the mentoring scope in the JD comes with any people-management expectation later, since that changes both comp and role framing.
