# Negotiation Script: Google, Senior Forward Deployed Engineer, GenAI, Google Cloud (Zürich)

## The shape of this offer

The posting is for Zürich, full time, with no salary listed. The minimum bar is five years of production AI work, and "Senior" at Google usually maps to **L5**, sometimes **L6** for the stronger end of the band. This is a step below the Senior Staff FDE role applied to on 2026-10-07 (L7). Google pays base, an annual bonus with a target percentage, and RSUs (GOOG) with yearly refreshers. There's no startup equity, so cliffs, VSOPs and anti-dilution don't apply. The levers are the level, the initial RSU grant and the sign-on bonus.

Three things to settle early:

1. **Push for L6 from the first recruiter call.** Magino has 17 years of experience, founded and scaled a platform to acquisition and held a Senior Staff title for five years. Being levelled L5 on a "Senior" req is the default outcome unless someone argues otherwise before the interviews are scheduled. Ask directly: "Is this req open to L6?" Levelling is mostly decided by the interview packet, so it's cheaper to win here than at offer stage.
2. **Office days and travel.** Magino lives in Geneva; Zürich is about 2h45 by train. Ask how many on-site days are expected and how much time goes to customer sites, since FDE roles can be travel-heavy.
3. **Fit check.** The profile says to avoid large legacy enterprises. Google Cloud's FDE group is new and small, but it's still Google. Decide before a long loop whether that trade is worth it.

The takeaway: even at L5, Google Zürich is at or above Geneva's top local rates. The gap between L5 and L6 (roughly CHF 80k to 100k a year) is bigger than anything negotiable inside a band, which is why levelling matters most. Base bands are tight; the room is in RSUs and sign-on.

Swiss specifics: ask about pension (BVG/LPP) contributions above the legal minimum. RSUs are taxed as income at vest at the rate of the canton of residence, so Geneva rates apply if Magino doesn't move.

## Vesting and refreshers

Recent Google grants have vested monthly or quarterly without a one-year cliff, and the schedule has changed over time (front-loaded and flat versions have both existed). **Confirm the current schedule.** If year one is light, size the sign-on to fill it. Ask what a typical refresher looks like at the offered level, because refreshers carry total comp in years three and four.

## Scripts

### When the recruiter asks for expectations

> "I'd like to agree on level before numbers. With 17 years of experience, five as Senior Staff and a platform I built and took through an acquisition, I'm expecting to interview at L6. Can you confirm whether this req supports L6 and share the Zürich band?"

If they insist on a number:

> "For L6 in Zürich, I'm thinking around CHF 400k total compensation a year. I'm flexible on how that splits between base, stock and sign-on."

### If they come in low (e.g. L6 at CHF 210k base, CHF 380k RSUs, no sign-on)

> "Thanks, I want to make this work. The base is close. The equity is the gap: what I see for L6 in Zürich is closer to CHF 500k over four years. If you can move the grant there and add a sign-on for the first year, I'm ready to say yes."

### If they offer L5

> "I appreciate it, and my concern is level rather than money. I've worked at Senior Staff for five years and owned a company's whole platform before that. What would the committee need to see to reconsider L6? If L5 is final, I'd want the top of the band and a defined promotion review at twelve months, in writing."

### Geneva versus Zürich

There's no geographic discount to resist; Zürich bands are among Europe's highest and a Geneva address doesn't lower them. Use the commute instead:

> "I'm based in Geneva, so office days have a real cost. I'm not asking for a different band, but I'd like travel support or a relocation allowance included."

### Competing offer template

Only use this with a real process behind it. Google recruiters verify, and a bluff can hurt in a committee-based process.

> "To be transparent, I'm at offer stage with [company] for a [Staff/Principal] role at about CHF [X] total. Google is my first choice because of the FDE mandate and the feedback loop into Vertex and ADK. If the equity can get closer to CHF [Y], I can sign this week."

Credible comparisons: other big tech Zürich offices, AI labs with Zürich presence, and funded AI startups hiring founding or principal engineers in Switzerland.

## Before signing

Get in writing: confirmed level, base, bonus target, RSU grant in CHF and in shares, vesting schedule, sign-on and clawback terms, expected office days and the share of time on customer sites.
