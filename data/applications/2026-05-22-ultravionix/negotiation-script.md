# Negotiation Script — Ultravionix / Late Co-Founder & CTO

## Context

This is a co-founder role at a very early-stage Geneva deeptech startup (founded 2025, 2-10 employees). The market dynamics are unusual: it's not a standard salary negotiation, it's a founding equity split plus salary conversation. These should be treated as separate but linked negotiations.

---

## Switzerland Market Anchors

**CTO market rate (Geneva, senior level, 17+ years):**
- Established company (50+ employees): CHF 200k-260k base
- Growth-stage startup (Series A/B): CHF 170k-220k base
- Early-stage startup (seed/pre-seed, with meaningful equity): CHF 120k-160k base

**Late co-founder dynamics:**
- "Late co-founder" entering at or after seed typically receives 5-15% equity, depending on how much has already been allocated and how early the ask comes
- Vesting: 4-year cliff vest with 1-year cliff is standard (ESOP framework used by most Swiss startups)
- At 2-10 employees founded in 2025, the company is likely pre-seed or seed, which means equity is the most valuable part of the package

**Reference:** Geneva living costs are 30-40% higher than Zurich depending on neighborhood. A CHF 140k salary in Geneva has meaningfully less purchasing power than the same figure in Lausanne or Bern.

---

## Opening Anchor

When asked about salary expectations, lead with the co-founder frame before the number:

> "Before we talk about base salary, I want to make sure we're aligned on the equity side first, since that's the majority of the value in a co-founding role. Once we've agreed on the equity structure, salary becomes easier to calibrate. That said, to be concrete: for a CTO co-founder role in Geneva, I'm thinking in the CHF 140k-160k range for base, reflecting the early-stage context and meaningful equity."

**Why this framing:** anchors on equity first (where Magino's interest is highest), then sets a credible salary range without leaving money on the table. CHF 140k is defensible as a "startup discount" from market rate; CHF 160k is the high anchor.

---

## Equity Negotiation

**Target:** 8-12% for a technical co-founder joining at seed or pre-seed stage

**Opening ask if equity hasn't been discussed:**
> "In a late co-founder structure, I'd typically expect to discuss equity in the 8-12% range, depending on what's already been allocated and what the cap table looks like. Can you walk me through the current ownership structure and what you're envisioning for the technical co-founder?"

**If they come back with less than 5%:**
> "I want to make this work, but I'll be honest: below 5% starts to feel more like an early employee option grant than a co-founding stake. Given the timeline, the risk, and the scope of what I'd be building, I'd need to understand the reasoning to get comfortable there. Is there flexibility on the vesting timeline or cliff structure to compensate?"

**Non-dilution protections to discuss:**
- Pro-rata rights in future rounds (right to participate to maintain your percentage)
- Anti-dilution provisions (weighted average, not full ratchet)
- Good leaver vs bad leaver definitions (standard Swiss startup terms)
- Acceleration on change of control (single or double trigger)

---

## Counter-Offer Script (If Initial Offer Is Low)

**Scenario:** They open with CHF 110k base and 4% equity.

> "I appreciate you putting a number on the table. I want to make this work. On the salary side, CHF 110k is meaningfully below what I'd need given Geneva costs and my profile, so I'd want to get to at least CHF 130k as a minimum. On equity, 4% is more in the early employee range than the co-founder range. I'm not trying to make this complicated, but I'd need to land at 7-8% minimum to feel like we're structuring this as a true founding role. Can we work back from those anchors?"

**If they can't move on both at once:**
> "If the salary is fixed at X for now, I'm open to that, but it needs to be accompanied by a meaningful equity position. The two are linked in my thinking. A below-market salary with below-co-founder equity doesn't work for me; one of the two has to be right."

---

## Geographic Context (Push Back on Location Discount)

If they try to apply a discount because Geneva is "expensive" for the company:

> "Geneva being expensive is actually an argument for a higher salary, not lower. My rent, transport, and cost of living all scale with Geneva, not with what's convenient for a startup budget. I'm aware this is early stage, which is why I'm already discounting significantly from market. I don't think it's reasonable to discount twice."

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

If you have other conversations going (or want to create urgency):

> "I'm in conversations with a couple of other teams right now, one of which is further along in the process. I want to be transparent because I'd rather be at Ultravionix if we can get the terms aligned quickly, but I need to be able to move on a decision within two weeks."

**Note:** only use this if there genuinely are other conversations, or if you're comfortable holding the position under follow-up.

---

## Final Ask Checklist Before Signing

- [ ] Shareholder agreement reviewed by an independent Swiss lawyer (not just the company's lawyer)
- [ ] Vesting schedule and cliff explicitly documented
- [ ] Leaver provisions (good/bad) clearly defined
- [ ] Drag-along rights limits understood
- [ ] IP assignment clause reviewed (standard but scope it)
- [ ] Non-compete scope: Swiss law caps these, should be narrow in time and geography
- [ ] Salary review trigger: when does the first raise discussion happen? (Tie to a funding event)
