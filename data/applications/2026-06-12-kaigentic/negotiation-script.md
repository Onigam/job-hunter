# Negotiation Script — kAIgentic, Principal Engineer AI Research

---

## Context

kAIgentic is a seed-stage startup ($10M from SMBC Group, 2024). Singapore HQ, operations across India, Europe, US, and Japan. Enterprise AI, deployed at one of the world's largest financial groups. The company has real revenue validation but is pre-Series A, which affects both base salary ceiling and equity opportunity.

Role: Principal Engineer, AI Research. Senior IC with potential to lead a small embedded research team. High ownership, close proximity to CTO.

---

## Market Rate Anchors (Switzerland, 2025-2026)

| Level | Geneva Base Range | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Senior AI/ML Engineer | CHF 155,000 - 185,000 | 5-8 years relevant experience |
| Staff AI Engineer | CHF 175,000 - 210,000 | 8-12 years, some leadership |
| Principal AI/Research Engineer | CHF 195,000 - 240,000 | 12+ years, strategic scope |
| Head of AI / Research Lead | CHF 210,000 - 260,000 | Full team + direction ownership |

Geneva adds a cost-of-living premium of roughly 15-20% over Zurich. SMBC-backed startups typically offer lower base than Big Tech but compensate with meaningful equity.

**Target range for this role: CHF 195,000 - 215,000 base + equity.**

---

## Opening Anchor

When they ask about salary expectations, go high and justify it:

> "Based on my research into the Switzerland market for principal-level AI engineering roles, and given the scope of what you've described, I'm anchoring around CHF 210,000 base. That's consistent with what this level commands in Geneva, and given the startup stage I'd want to pair that with meaningful equity. Happy to talk through both."

If they push back on base due to startup constraints:

> "I understand. I'm not trying to apply a FAANG benchmark to an early-stage company. But Geneva cost of living is real, and I'd want to make sure the total package reflects the seniority of the role. If the base needs to flex, I'd expect equity to compensate accordingly."

---

## Counter-Offer Script

**If they come in at CHF 160,000 - 175,000 (below market):**

> "I appreciate the offer, and I want to be straightforward: that's roughly 15-20% below where I expected to land based on market data for this level. I'm not walking away from the conversation, but I'd need to close that gap somehow. Can we look at either moving the base toward CHF 190,000, or substantially increasing the equity component to account for the difference?"

**If they come in at CHF 175,000 - 190,000 (low but workable):**

> "That's closer to where I'd expect for a senior engineer role, but this is Principal scope with research ownership and potential team lead responsibilities. I'd want to get to CHF 200,000 on base, or if the base is fixed, to revisit the equity grant to reflect the seniority."

---

## Equity Negotiation

For a $10M seed startup, realistic equity benchmarks:

| Seniority | Typical Grant Range (seed-stage) |
|---|---|
| Senior Engineer | 0.05% - 0.15% |
| Staff / Principal | 0.10% - 0.35% |
| Research Lead / Head of AI | 0.20% - 0.60% |

**Ask for:** 0.25% - 0.35% on a 4-year vest with a 1-year cliff.

Standard vesting script:

> "On equity, what I'd want to understand is the grant size as a percentage of fully diluted cap, the current valuation if you can share it, the vesting schedule, and whether there's acceleration on acquisition. The cliff and vesting structure matter as much as the headline number for a role like this."

**If they quote RSUs or options:**

> "Are these ISOs or NSOs? And what's the current strike price relative to the 409A? I want to understand the actual economic value, not just the number of shares."

**Anti-dilution / pro-rata rights:** These are rarely offered to employees at the engineering level, but worth asking at Principal level in a well-funded startup:

> "As a lead research engineer at this stage, would there be any pro-rata rights on future rounds, or is this purely a standard employee equity grant?"

---

## Geneva Discount Pushback

If they argue for a below-market offer because the role is remote:

> "I'm fully remote-capable and I'm happy to work asynchronously with your Singapore and India teams. But the cost of living in Geneva is among the highest globally, and the market rate here is what it is regardless of where the company is headquartered. Remote work doesn't change my rent."

---

## Competing Offer Template

If you have or can credibly reference other conversations:

> "I want to be transparent: I'm in late-stage conversations with another company for a similar principal-level role. Their range starts at CHF 200,000. I have a strong preference for what kAIgentic is building, but I also have to make a decision that makes sense financially. If you can match or get close to that range, I'd close this quickly."

---

## Non-Salary Items to Negotiate

- **Home office budget:** CHF 2,000 - 4,000 one-time setup + CHF 100-150/month
- **Learning budget:** CHF 3,000 - 5,000/year for conferences and courses
- **Start date:** 4-week notice is standard in Switzerland; negotiate if they need faster
- **Trial period:** Swiss law allows 3-month probation; standard, don't fight it
- **Work permit:** If they're not familiar with Swiss hiring, confirm they'll support B or C permit sponsorship if needed (not an issue for Magino as EU national, but confirm)

---

## Walk-Away Line

Below CHF 175,000 base with less than 0.15% equity is not competitive for this level and scope. Be prepared to decline and say so clearly:

> "I've given this a lot of thought. Below CHF 175,000 with the equity on the table doesn't work for me at this level. I genuinely want to see what you're building succeed, but I can't take a 25% market discount on this role. If the budget situation changes, I'd welcome a conversation."
