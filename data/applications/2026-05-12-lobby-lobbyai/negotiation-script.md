# Negotiation Script — Lobby / Founding Engineer

---

## Market Context

**Listed range:** €43,200–€72,000 (from third-party listing). This is almost certainly a German market rate applied to a Swiss role, or a pre-seed placeholder. It does not reflect Zurich cost-of-living.

**Zurich market reality for this level:**
- Senior Full-Stack Engineer (5-8 yrs): CHF 110,000–140,000 base
- Staff/Founding Engineer with 10+ yrs + proven founding track record: CHF 130,000–160,000+
- Early-stage startup founding engineer discount (equity offset): CHF 100,000–130,000 base is reasonable if equity is real

**Lobby's funding stage:** Just closed pre-seed (February 2025 founding). Equity is high-risk but high-upside. They can't pay top-of-market cash. That's the trade.

**Geneva vs Zurich:** Magino is Geneva-based. Zurich is the HQ. Factor in commuting cost or relocation expectations. If it's hybrid/remote-friendly, this is a non-issue. If it requires full-time Zurich presence, push for remote flexibility or a location allowance.

---

## Opening Anchor

When they ask for compensation expectations, answer first with a range, not a number:

> "For a founding engineer role in Zurich, I'd expect a base in the CHF 120,000–135,000 range, with meaningful early equity on top. I'm open to weighting the equity higher if the cash range is constrained given your stage, but I'd want to understand the cap table and vesting terms before landing on a number."

Why this works:
- Anchors high but not unrealistically
- Signals you understand early-stage trade-offs (cash vs equity)
- Keeps the conversation open rather than shutting it down
- Invites them to reveal their constraints, which gives you negotiation room

---

## If They Come In Low (CHF 80,000–95,000 range)

Don't reject immediately. Use this script:

> "I appreciate the offer. The role is genuinely interesting and I've thought hard about it. The base is lower than where I'd need to be given my background and the Zurich market rate for this level. Can we talk about what equity looks like? If the founding engineer package includes, say, 1–1.5% vested over four years with a one-year cliff, I'd be willing to move down on base to somewhere around CHF 100,000–105,000 and treat the equity as the real upside."

If they say equity is below 0.5% for a founding engineer:

> "I'd push back on that gently. You're asking for founding-engineer scope, which means I'd be making architecture decisions that shape the next three to five years of the product. The equity should reflect that. A range of 0.75–1.5% for the first external engineer is standard at this stage. If the cap table doesn't have room, I'd want to understand why before adjusting my expectations downward."

---

## Equity Negotiation

**What to ask for:**
- 0.75–1.5% for a founding engineer joining post-pre-seed
- 4-year vesting with 1-year cliff (standard)
- Double-trigger acceleration on acquisition (protects you if Lobby gets acquired early, which is a realistic outcome given the founders' background)
- Anti-dilution note: ask whether the equity is on a fully diluted basis and whether there's a refresh mechanism after Series A

**Questions to ask before signing:**
- "What's the current fully diluted cap table, and where does the employee option pool sit?"
- "Is there a refresh mechanism after the next funding round?"
- "What's the strike price on the options?"
- "Do you have double-trigger acceleration written into the ESOP?"

**Red flags:**
- Options that vest only on IPO (no cliff, no time-based vesting)
- Strike price set above current 409A valuation
- No anti-dilution protection and no refresh discussed
- Equity pool that's already heavily allocated to advisors and angels before the first engineer

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

If you have another offer (or are expecting one), use it:

> "I want to be transparent — I'm at the final stage with one other company. Their offer is [X]. I'd rather join Lobby given the problem space and the stage, but I need the packages to be in the same conversation. Can we get to [Y]?"

If you don't have a competing offer yet but want to create urgency:

> "I have a few active processes that are moving toward offers in the next two to three weeks. I'd prefer to make a decision before then rather than after, so if we can align on terms this week, that would be ideal."

---

## Location / Remote Negotiation

If they require full-time Zurich presence:

> "I'm based in Geneva. I'm open to spending significant time in Zurich, especially early on, but I'd want to agree upfront on a cadence. Two to three days per week in Zurich feels workable; five days a week would change the economics of the role, and I'd factor in a commuting allowance or housing support as part of the package."

---

## Walk-Away Point

Don't accept below:
- **CHF 95,000 base + 0.75% equity (4yr/1yr cliff)** as the absolute floor for this role at this stage
- If they can't reach CHF 95,000 base AND won't move above 0.5% equity, the risk/reward math doesn't work at Zurich cost-of-living

The role is genuinely interesting and the team seems strong, but the numbers have to reflect the seniority and the founding-engineer expectation being placed on the hire.
