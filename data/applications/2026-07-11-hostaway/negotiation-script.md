# Negotiation Script, Hostaway Staff Engineer (React/Node.js, Trust Accounting)

## Context
Posting lists €100,000-150,000 for the EMEA-remote band, which reads as a broad range covering multiple countries and cost-of-living tiers. Being Geneva-based is the strongest card to play here, since Swiss engineering comp for a Staff-level IC sits well above that band. The honest framing is "I'm not asking you to match Zurich rates for everyone, I'm telling you what it costs to hire someone who already lives here."

## Switzerland market anchors
- Staff/Principal Software Engineer, Geneva/Zurich: CHF 150,000-190,000 base, plus bonus and equity at scale-up/late-stage companies.
- Senior Staff (Alaya/Benevity-level track record, 17+ years, founder background): CHF 170,000-210,000 base is realistic at a well-funded scale-up.
- Remote-from-Switzerland roles at foreign-HQ companies typically land 10-20% below a Zurich-office equivalent, but rarely below CHF 140,000 base for a true Staff level.
- CHF and EUR are close to parity as of mid-2026; treat the posted €150,000 ceiling as roughly CHF 145,000, near the low end of a fair Swiss-remote Staff offer, not the middle.

## Opening anchor
Don't open in EUR. Open in CHF and let them do the conversion.

> "I'm Geneva-based, so I'm anchoring to Swiss market rates for a Staff-level IC rather than a general EMEA-remote band. For this scope, greenfield ownership of a financial subsystem plus the mentoring and cross-functional expectations in the JD, I'd want to be around CHF 175,000 base, plus the equity and benefits structure you'd normally offer at this level. If the €100k-150k range was built around a broader EMEA pool, I get that, but I wanted to flag where I'm coming in from the start rather than have it be a surprise later."

## If they come back low (near or below €130k / ~CHF 125k)
> "I hear you on the range you've published, and I imagine it has to work across a lot of different cost-of-living contexts. From where I sit, the gap between that number and Swiss market rate isn't small, and it's not really a negotiating tactic, it's just what the local cost structure looks like. Is there room to move the base closer to CHF 160,000, or to make up the difference through equity or a sign-on that reflects the ramp-up on a subsystem this size?"

## Geographic discount pushback
Hostaway is remote-first with people in 40+ countries, which usually means they already have some geographic banding logic internally. Use that instead of fighting it:

> "I get that a global remote org needs some kind of banding, otherwise comp conversations never end. But Geneva specifically is one of the more expensive markets in Europe, not just 'EU remote,' and I'd rather have that reflected honestly in the number now than renegotiate it in six months once the ramp-up period is over."

## Equity negotiation (Hostaway is a funded, profitable unicorn, not early-stage)
Because Hostaway is already profitable and at unicorn valuation, equity grants will likely be smaller in percentage terms than an early-stage startup, but the underlying value is more real since there's a price to anchor to.

- Ask for the strike price and most recent 409A or equivalent valuation basis before evaluating any number in isolation.
- Push for a standard 4-year vest with a 1-year cliff; anything longer than a 1-year cliff for a Staff hire is worth pushing back on.
- Ask whether early exercise is allowed, and whether there's any provision for extended post-termination exercise windows (standard 90 days is thin at this level; 1-2 years is reasonable to ask for).
- At unicorn-stage private companies, don't over-index on the headline share count. Ask for the percentage of fully diluted shares outstanding, not just a raw number of options.

## Counter-offer script (verbal or written)
> "Thanks for putting together an offer, I can tell there's real thought behind the scope of the role. Based on Swiss market rates for Staff-level engineering and the greenfield ownership this role carries, I'd like to counter at CHF 170,000 base, with the equity grant increased proportionally if the base can't move that far. I'm flexible on how the total package gets structured, base versus equity versus a sign-on, as long as the total reflects what this level of ownership is worth here."

## Competing offer as a bargaining chip (use only if real, or omit entirely)
> "I'm currently in later-stage conversations with another company at a similar level, and comp there is tracking closer to CHF 180,000 base. I'd rather move forward with Hostaway if the numbers can get close, the trust accounting problem is the more interesting one to me, but I wanted to be transparent about where things stand."

## What to hold firm on regardless of base outcome
- Fully remote confirmed in writing, no return-to-office clause tied to a future funding event.
- Clear title: Staff Engineer, not Senior Engineer with a Staff-sounding scope.
- Written confirmation of the "significant architectural influence from day one" language in the posting, ideally as an actual ownership area in the offer letter or first 90-day plan, not just a talking point from the recruiter call.
