# Negotiation Script: Senior Platform Architect @ Axelera AI

Context: Axelera AI has raised $370M+ across equity, grants and venture debt, is well past seed stage, and offers a Zurich office plus full remote-from-Europe. That combination matters for the numbers below: this isn't an early-stage bet where a low cash offer needs to be forgiven for equity upside, it's a well-capitalized scale-up that can pay Zurich-market cash.

## Market anchors (Switzerland, Senior/Staff Platform Architect level)

- Zurich base salary for Senior/Staff-level architecture roles at funded tech companies: roughly CHF 155,000 to CHF 210,000 base, depending on scope and whether the role carries system-wide ownership.
- Total cash (base plus bonus) at well-funded scale-ups in this band typically lands CHF 170,000 to CHF 230,000.
- Hardware/AI infrastructure companies in Switzerland (comparable to chip and systems roles at ams OSRAM, Nvidia's Zurich site, or similarly funded deep-tech startups) tend to sit at or above the general software-architect range, since the talent pool for accelerator-adjacent platform work is thinner.
- Geneva cost of living runs close to Zurich's, so there's no defensible discount for being based in Geneva rather than Zurich itself.

## Opening anchor

Aim for the top third of the range rather than the midpoint, and justify it with scope rather than just tenure:

"Based on Zurich market data for architecture roles with system-wide ownership at companies at your funding stage, I'm targeting a base in the CHF 190,000 to CHF 205,000 range, plus the equity and bonus structure you'd offer at this level. Happy to talk through how that maps to the scope of this role once we're further along."

Don't name a number before they do if it can be avoided; if pushed early, give the range above rather than a single figure.

## If the offer comes in low

Don't react immediately. Ask what the number is built on before countering:

"Thanks for sending this over. Before I respond with specifics, can you walk me through how the base was set, is that pegged to a Zurich band or a broader European one? I want to make sure we're comparing against the right market."

Then counter with data, not just a bigger number:

"Based on what I'm seeing for Zurich-based Staff-level architecture roles at companies with your funding profile, this comes in below market, closer to the CHF 150,000 to 160,000 band than the 190,000-plus I'd expect for a role that owns platform-wide topology and integration decisions. I'd like to see the base closer to CHF 195,000, with the difference made up in base rather than pushed entirely into equity."

If they hold firm on base, negotiate a signing bonus or an accelerated first equity refresh instead of walking away from the conversation.

## Equity negotiation

Axelera is privately held with a real, if illiquid, valuation from the last round. Treat equity as real comp, but discount it appropriately:

- Ask directly for the strike price, the 409A or Swiss equivalent valuation, and the total fully diluted share count, not just a number of options or a percentage.
- Standard vesting is four years with a one-year cliff; push back on anything longer, and ask what happens to unvested equity on an acquisition, since Axelera itself could be one.
- Ask whether there's a double-trigger acceleration clause (change of control plus termination), especially relevant for a company at this funding size that's a plausible acquisition target for larger AI infrastructure players.
- Don't let a large-sounding option grant substitute for a below-market base. Equity should be the upside on top of a fair cash number, not a replacement for one.

## Geographic discount pushback

If they propose a lower band because the candidate is Geneva-based rather than in a company office:

"Geneva's cost of living is in the same range as Zurich's, and the role is listed as workable fully remote from Europe, so I'd expect the comp band to reflect the role and level rather than which Swiss canton I'm sitting in. If the offer is meant to reflect a broader European remote band rather than the Swiss one, I'd want that made explicit upfront."

## Competing offer template

Use only if there's a genuine competing process in flight; don't fabricate one.

"I'm currently in later-stage conversations with another company for a comparable Staff-level platform role, and I like what Axelera is building enough that I'd rather make this work than default to the other option. If we can close the gap on base to around CHF 195,000, I'm ready to move forward without dragging this out."

## Bottom line to hold

Walk-away floor: CHF 175,000 base plus a real equity grant with standard four-year vesting and a one-year cliff. Below that, the cash gap is large enough that even strong equity upside doesn't close it, given Geneva/Zurich cost of living.
