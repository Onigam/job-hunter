# Negotiation Script: Pango (YC S26), Founding Full-Stack Engineer

## Market Context

**Posted comp:** "Competitive salary" + "significant equity," no numbers given. First engineering hire, YC S26, Stockholm-based, two-person founder team as far as public info shows.

**Stockholm/Sweden market for a senior founding engineer (2026):**
- Regular senior full-stack roles in Stockholm run roughly SEK 700,000-950,000/year base (~CHF 65,000-90,000)
- YC-backed startups paying founding-engineer comp tend to price closer to international bands than local Swedish norms, since they're competing for candidates who could take a US or remote offer. Expect them to frame in EUR or USD even if payroll runs in SEK
- A realistic YC S26 founding-engineer band, first hire, post-PMF but pre-Series A: EUR 90,000-130,000 base (roughly CHF 85,000-125,000) plus 1%-2.5% equity

**Geneva context:** Magino's current market rate as Senior Staff Engineer in Geneva is CHF 180,000-220,000. Stockholm base offers will likely land well under that in raw numbers. The right frame isn't "match Geneva," it's "the founding-engineer equity and ownership make up the difference," but that only holds if the equity grant is real and the base doesn't fall so far that it signals they're underpricing the role.

**Why anchor high anyway:** Pango hit product-market fit two months post-launch and is already serving multi-million-dollar e-commerce brands. That's not a pre-revenue seed pitch, it's a company with real traction negotiating its first hire, which means there's more room than the posting's vague language suggests.

---

## Opening Anchor

When asked about expectations:

> "Given where I'm coming from, Senior Staff at Benevity with a founder background at Alaya, I'd be looking at something in the EUR 110,000-125,000 base range, plus equity in the 1.5%-2.5% band given I'd be the first engineering hire. I know first-hire packages vary a lot at this stage, so I'm curious where your range actually sits before we get more specific."

Why this works:
- Anchors near the top of a realistic band without naming a number they haven't offered yet
- Frames equity ask around the "first hire" reality, which is the strongest lever here
- Invites them to reveal their number, useful since the posting gave none

---

## If They Come In Low on Base

If they offer something in the EUR 60,000-80,000 range (or SEK equivalent that converts there):

> "I hear you on the constraints at this stage, product-market fit or not, seed-stage cash is tight. That said, this is a meaningful gap from what I'd need to make relocating from Geneva work. Could we get to EUR 95,000-100,000? If the cash isn't there, I'd want the equity to move to compensate."

If they hold firm on base:

> "Understood. If EUR 95,000 isn't reachable right now, let's talk about the equity moving to 2%-2.5% instead, and maybe a compensation review built in at 6 or 12 months once we've both seen how the role plays out."

---

## Equity Negotiation

**Standard terms to confirm:**
- 4-year vesting, 1-year cliff: standard, no pushback needed
- Double-trigger acceleration on acquisition or founder departure: worth asking for explicitly, since Pango is early enough that an acqui-hire scenario isn't far-fetched
- Vesting start date tied to actual start, not offer date

**Opening ask:**

> "As the first engineering hire, joining before you've likely closed a priced round, I'd want to be at 2%-2.5%. That's the range where the ownership stake actually reflects being early rather than being employee number one in name only."

**If they offer 0.5%-1%:**

> "That's low for a first-hire grant at this stage, especially with the traction you already have. I'd want to get to at least 1.5% to feel like the equity matches the risk I'm taking on by joining pre-Series A."

---

## Geographic and Relocation Considerations

Pango's posting mentions flexibility "for life circumstances" around the Stockholm requirement, which is worth testing directly rather than assuming it means remote-friendly.

> "Before we get into specifics, I want to understand how firm the Stockholm requirement is. I'm in Geneva now, and I'm open to relocating for the right role, but I'd want to know upfront whether a hybrid arrangement in the first few months is realistic while I sort out the move."

If relocation is required:

> "I'm willing to relocate. I'd ask for a one-time relocation contribution, in the EUR 8,000-12,000 range, to cover flights, temporary housing, and the practical cost of moving a life across borders. That's fairly standard for this kind of ask and it's not something I'd expect to revisit later."

---

## Competing Offer Leverage (if applicable)

> "I want to be upfront that I'm in later-stage conversations with another company right now, and the comp discussion there is further along. I'd rather build something from scratch with a founding team than join something later-stage, but I do need to make a decision in the next couple of weeks. If we can move quickly on terms, that helps me prioritize this."

---

## Final Close

> "The traction you already have changes the calculus here, this isn't a bet on an idea, it's a bet on a team that's already found something working. I want to be the person who builds the engineering foundation under that. Let's get the terms settled so I can start."
