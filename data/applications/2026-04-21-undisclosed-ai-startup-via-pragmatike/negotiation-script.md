# Negotiation Script

## Market frame

This is not a standard engineering manager role. It is a founding-level CTO or Founding Engineer seat with direct ownership of architecture, product execution, AI features, infrastructure, and early team building. In Switzerland, broad CTO salary datasets are noisy, but they still give a useful floor.

- Glassdoor Switzerland, April 2026, shows a typical CTO range of CHF 128k to CHF 230k, with Geneva submissions in the CHF 232k to CHF 268k range and national upside reported toward CHF 296k.
- SpotSalary Geneva, updated March 28, 2026, places CTO compensation at CHF 200k to CHF 250k for 7 to 15 years of experience and CHF 250k to CHF 350k for 15+ years.
- SwissDevJobs 2024 shows Geneva among the higher-paying Swiss tech cities overall, which matters if anyone tries to normalize this role against lower-cost remote markets.

For this role, the right benchmark is not "senior engineer remote EMEA". It is "hands-on founding technical leader with startup execution risk".

## Cash and equity anchor

- Opening cash anchor: CHF 225k base, plus meaningful equity.
- Target working range: CHF 205k to CHF 230k base if the role is truly CTO-level in scope.
- Lower-cash alternative worth considering: CHF 180k to CHF 200k only if the equity is materially stronger and the scope really includes technical leadership, hiring, and product direction.
- Walk-away concern zone: below CHF 180k cash with weak equity, especially if they still expect CTO-level accountability.

## Equity frame

- If they position this as a real founding CTO role, push for roughly 0.75% to 1.5% on a fully diluted basis.
- If they position it closer to founding engineer or first technical hire, a more realistic range is roughly 0.25% to 0.75%.
- Ask them to state the equity as a percentage of the fully diluted cap table, not just a number of options.
- Ask for the current strike price, vesting schedule, and the latest cap table context.
- Standard vesting is 4 years with a 1-year cliff. That is normal. Push for double-trigger acceleration on change of control.
- True anti-dilution protection is uncommon for employee grants in Switzerland. If they will not move on that, ask for a written top-up review at the next financing or a milestone-based refresh grant.
- Ask for an extended post-termination exercise window. Ninety days is weak if the grant is meaningful.

## Opening line

"Given the scope, this sits in founding CTO territory rather than a senior IC band. For a Geneva-based candidate with my background building a company from zero to acquisition and staying hands-on across full stack, platform, and AI work, I would be looking for something around CHF 225k base with equity that reflects real founding-level ownership."

## If they come in low on salary

"I understand early-stage cash constraints, but the role you described carries CTO-level accountability across architecture, product delivery, infrastructure, and early team building. If cash needs to be lower, I would need to see that reflected clearly in the equity package and in the level of authority attached to the role."

## If they try to frame it as 'remote EMEA' pricing

"I would not price this as a generic remote engineering role. You are asking for someone to carry technical strategy and execution risk at the same time, and I am based in Geneva, where the local market for senior technical leadership is materially higher than standard remote IC bands. If the scope stays this broad, the package should reflect that."

## If they say the title is CTO but the package looks like senior engineer

"If the compensation is set at senior engineer level, then we should probably tighten the scope and title accordingly. If the expectation is that I own the technical direction, the product architecture, the AI roadmap, and the early engineering system, then the package needs to match that responsibility."

## Counter-offer template

"I am still interested, but I would need the package adjusted to make sense for the scope. If you can move the base to CHF 210k and strengthen the equity to [X%], I think we are in the range where it is worth moving forward quickly."

## Competing-offer template

"I am speaking with other companies for senior founding and Staff+ roles, so I need to compare this against real alternatives on both cash and upside. This role is compelling because of the ownership and product scope. If we can get the package into the right range, I would rather spend my energy on one serious process than drag this out."

## Practical fallback positions

- Best outcome: CHF 220k to CHF 230k base plus strong equity.
- Balanced outcome: CHF 205k to CHF 215k base plus credible equity and clear scope.
- Cash-light startup outcome: CHF 185k to CHF 195k base plus materially better equity, explicit influence on roadmap, and written alignment on title and mandate.
