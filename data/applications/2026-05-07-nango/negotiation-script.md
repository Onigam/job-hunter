# Negotiation Script — Nango, Staff Backend Engineer

---

## Market Context

**Nango profile:** YC W23 startup, open-source, fast-growing, repeat founders, fully remote. No public funding round announced beyond YC; likely pre-Series A or early Series A. Team described as "veteran dev-tool team" from Netlify, Uber, Algolia.

**Switzerland Staff Backend Engineer market rates (2026):**
- Median for Staff BE at established Swiss tech company: CHF 165,000-195,000 base
- At remote-first YC startups paying Swiss residents: CHF 145,000-180,000 base + equity
- VP/Lead-adjacent Staff roles: CHF 190,000-230,000 base
- Magino's positioning: 17 years, founding engineer track record, direct stack match (TypeScript, Node.js, PostgreSQL), Staff level with AI tooling leadership. Top of band is defensible.

**Equity expectations for a YC-backed early-stage company (Staff hire):**
- Realistic range: 0.10%-0.40% depending on stage, competition for the hire, and how early you're joining
- 4-year vesting with 1-year cliff is standard
- If post-Series A: expect dilution to ~0.10%-0.20% on paper grant before the next round

---

## Opening Anchor

If asked for a target salary range before an offer is made:

> "Based on my experience level, the Switzerland market for Staff backend roles, and the scope of what you're hiring for, I'm targeting CHF 190,000-210,000 in base, plus meaningful equity. I'm more interested in getting the total comp structure right than anchoring hard on any single number, so I'd rather understand what you're thinking before I narrow it down."

Rationale: anchoring at CHF 190k-210k positions you above likely first offer without being unrealistic for the stage. It signals you're open to a conversation on structure (base vs equity) without giving away flexibility immediately.

---

## Receiving the Offer

**If they offer CHF 160,000 or above:**
Don't accept immediately. Thank them, ask for 48 hours to review.

> "Thank you, I appreciate the offer. I'd like a day or two to look at the full package carefully before responding. Can you send it in writing so I have all the details in front of me?"

**If they offer CHF 140,000-159,000:**
This is below market for the Switzerland context. Counter directly:

> "I appreciate you putting together an offer. Looking at the base, I was expecting something closer to CHF 185,000-190,000 for a Staff role at this level, particularly given the Geneva cost of living. Is there room to move on the base, or is there flexibility on the equity side that could bridge that gap?"

**If they say the budget is capped:**

> "I understand there are constraints. Can you help me understand the total comp structure? If the base is fixed, I'd want to look carefully at the equity, any performance bonuses, and how the refresh schedule works. That context helps me evaluate the full picture."

---

## Equity Negotiation

Questions to ask before accepting any equity number:

1. "What's the current share count and what does that make my grant as a percentage?"
2. "What was the valuation at the last funding round, and what's the current 409A?"
3. "What's the vesting schedule, and is there a cliff?"
4. "Do you have a refresh program for existing employees after the first grant?"
5. "What's the pro-rata policy for future rounds?"

**If equity grant feels low:**

> "The equity grant you've mentioned comes out to roughly [X]% based on what you've shared. For a Staff engineer joining at this stage with this level of ownership over core systems, I'd want to be closer to [Y]%. Can you come up on the initial grant, or build in an accelerated refresh in year two?"

---

## Geographic Discount Pushback

Nango is a remote company. Some early-stage YC startups use US salary bands or normalize globally below Swiss market rates. If that comes up:

> "I'm based in Geneva, which has one of the highest costs of living in Europe. I understand some remote companies apply a location adjustment, but Geneva is actually more expensive than most US cities on a purchasing power basis. I'm not asking for a San Francisco premium, just a rate that reflects what a senior engineer in this market would earn."

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

If you have another offer in hand or expect one soon:

> "I want to be transparent: I'm in late stages with [Company X / another company]. Their offer is at [X range / strong equity], and I genuinely prefer what Nango is building. But I need the numbers to be close enough that it's not a difficult decision. What can you do to make this straightforward for me?"

If you don't have another offer but want to apply time pressure:

> "I have a few conversations that are moving quickly, so I'd ideally like to get to a decision within the next two weeks. Is that timeline feasible for Nango?"

---

## Walk-Away Conditions

Know these before the call:

- Walk-away base: below CHF 155,000 with no equity upside that compensates
- Walk-away equity: below 0.10% at pre-Series A with no refresh program
- Non-starters: 100% location-adjusted pay that ignores Geneva market rates; no equity for a Staff hire

---

## After Accepting

Send this immediately:

> "I'm looking forward to joining. Can you confirm the start date, onboarding process, and who I should be in touch with before day one? I'd also like to set up a call in the first week to understand your current architecture and the biggest systems challenges you're working through."

This signals ownership from day one without waiting to be told what to do.
