# Negotiation Script — Ahead Health / Founding AI Engineer

---

## Market Context (Switzerland, 2026)

**Published range:** CHF 145K–155K base + "significant equity"

**Market benchmarks for Staff+ AI Engineer in Zurich/Geneva:**
- Series A/B startups: CHF 150K–175K base
- Scale-ups (post-Series B): CHF 170K–200K base
- Tech giants (Google, Apple Zurich): CHF 200K–250K+ total comp
- Relevant data point: Ahead Health's own team lists Google, DeepMind, Hugging Face as backgrounds, which anchors the talent bar — and the comp expectation — higher than a typical seed stage startup

**Assessment:** The published range is below market for 17 years of experience and founding engineer seniority, but appropriate for early-stage with meaningful equity upside. The correct strategy is to take the base to the top of range or slightly above, then negotiate hard on equity.

---

## Opening Anchor

When they make an offer, do not accept on the call. Say:

> "Thanks for the offer — I appreciate the clarity on the range. I want to think through the full picture. Can I come back to you by [day]?"

Then send by email (or on a follow-up call):

> "I've been thinking about the offer and I want to be direct with you. My target for base is CHF 165K, which reflects my level and the Geneva/Zurich cost of living for someone with founding engineer experience. I understand you're early stage and equity matters here — I'm not asking you to match a FAANG package. But I want to start from a base that makes sense for the contribution I'd be making from day one."

**Why 165K:** It's above the top of their stated range but not absurd. It gives them room to land at 155K–160K, which is a win for both sides. If you start at 155K you have nowhere to go.

---

## Counter-Offer Script (If They Push Back on Base)

If they hold at 155K:

> "I hear you on the budget constraints at this stage, and I take the equity seriously. If 155K is the ceiling on base, I'd want to revisit the equity to reflect that gap. Can we talk about the current cap table, the grant size, and the vesting terms?"

If they offer 160K:

> "That works. I'd still want to discuss the equity in detail before we close — specifically the percentage, the cliff, and whether there's any acceleration provision. Those terms matter as much as the number."

---

## Equity Negotiation

**Founding engineer equity benchmarks (seed to Series A):**
- First 3–5 engineers: 0.3%–1.5% depending on stage and valuation
- If the company has raised a seed round, expect dilution to be factored in
- Ask for the percentage, not just the number of options

**Questions to ask:**
1. "What's the current fully diluted cap table?"
2. "What percentage does this grant represent?"
3. "What's the strike price and the last 409A (or Swiss equivalent valuation)?"
4. "Is there any acceleration on change of control?"
5. "What's the vesting schedule — standard four years with one-year cliff, or something different?"

**Script to push for more equity:**

> "The base is below my market rate, which means I'm essentially front-loading some of my comp into equity. I want to make sure that trade-off is actually reflected in the grant size, not just described as significant. What's the percentage, and how was it set?"

---

## Geographic Discount Pushback

If they try to discount the offer because you're in Geneva (not Zurich):

> "Geneva and Zurich are comparable cost-of-living markets — both are consistently in the top five most expensive cities globally. The Geneva discount argument doesn't hold if we're comparing against Zurich-based market rates. I'd rather not anchor to a geographic discount that isn't really there."

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

If you have another offer in hand (or want to reference the market):

> "I want to be transparent: I have [another conversation / a competing offer] at around CHF [X]. I'm not using it as a threat — I'm sharing it because I'd rather talk numbers directly than play games. Ahead Health is the role I'd choose if we can land on terms that reflect the level of contribution you're asking for."

If you don't have a competing offer, don't fabricate one. Instead:

> "To be honest with you, this is the role I'm most interested in. That's exactly why I want to make sure we get the comp right — I'd rather have that conversation now than feel undervalued six months in."

---

## Walk-Away Anchors

- **Base floor:** CHF 150K (below this, the equity needs to be exceptional)
- **Equity floor:** 0.5% fully diluted for a founding engineer role at seed/early Series A
- **If both are below floor:** Ask for a six-month salary review tied to a specific milestone
- **Benefits to clarify:** Remote policy for Geneva-based work, health insurance coverage, equipment budget

---

## Timing Notes

- Don't rush a counter. Taking 24–48 hours to respond signals you're deliberate, not desperate.
- Do the counter in writing (email) so there's a record and you can choose your words carefully.
- Close the equity conversation before signing anything. Verbal descriptions of "significant equity" are not legally binding.
