# Negotiation Script — Grafana Labs, Staff Software Engineer (Databases, Managed Services)

## Market Context

**Posted range (EMEA):** £103,958 - £124,750 per year (UK / Ireland / European timezones)  
**Grafana Labs stage:** Late-stage private company, Series D ($240M raised, ~$6B valuation as of 2022). RSUs available. Not yet public, IPO trajectory likely.  
**Location factor:** The posted range is anchored to UK / Dublin cost of living. Geneva is materially more expensive than both. Cost-of-living premium over London: ~30-35%.

**Geneva market anchors for Staff Software Engineer:**
- Range at comparable companies (Stripe, Grafana, Sentry, PlanetScale remote): CHF 170,000 - 210,000 base
- Total comp target (base + RSU annual equivalent): CHF 220,000 - 260,000
- Floor: CHF 160,000 base; below this, Geneva expenses make it untenable

**CHF conversion of posted GBP range:**
- Bottom: £103,958 × 1.17 = ~CHF 122,000
- Top: £124,750 × 1.17 = ~CHF 146,000
- Geneva premium applied (×1.32): ~CHF 161,000 - 193,000
- This confirms the ask below is reasonable, not inflated.

---

## Opening Anchor

Use this when they ask for your expectations, or when the first offer arrives.

> "I've been looking at Staff-level compensation in Geneva for remote roles at companies of Grafana's stage, and the range I've landed on is CHF 185,000 - 195,000 for base. I know the posted range is referenced to UK cost of living, and Geneva sits meaningfully above that. I'd want to make sure the base reflects the geography before we get into the equity discussion."

**Why 185-195k:**
- Sits above the Geneva-adjusted top of their EMEA range
- Gives room to settle at 175-180k without feeling like a concession
- Signals you've done the math, not that you're inflating blindly

---

## If They Come In Below 160k CHF (or equivalent GBP)

> "I appreciate the offer. To be direct, at that level it's hard to make the numbers work in Geneva. I'm not looking to maximize base at the expense of equity or scope, but CHF 160,000 is roughly the floor where the role makes sense for me given living costs here. If the base is constrained, I'd want to understand what the RSU package looks like and whether there's flexibility on the refresh schedule, because that's where I can close the gap."

**Follow-up if they hold firm on base:**
> "If CHF [their number] is the ceiling on base, let's talk about the equity. For a Staff role at Grafana's stage, I'd expect an initial grant in the range of $150,000 - $200,000 USD equivalent over four years, with a one-year cliff. Is that in range for this level? And is there an annual refresh mechanism?"

---

## RSU / Equity Script

Grafana Labs is pre-IPO with RSUs. Key questions to ask:

1. "What's the current 409A valuation or the last preferred share price? I want to understand the strike price context."
2. "Is the vesting schedule 4-year / 1-year cliff standard, or is there anything non-standard I should know?"
3. "Is there an annual refresh grant for Staff level? If so, what's the typical refresh as a percentage of the initial grant?"
4. "What's the liquidity policy for employees before IPO? Are there secondary sale windows?"

**Equity counter-offer language:**
> "The base is at CHF [X], which is workable. To bring total comp to where I need it to be, I'd want an initial RSU grant of at least $180,000 USD equivalent over four years, plus an annual refresh that keeps pace with the vesting. That's consistent with what companies at Grafana's stage typically offer at this level."

---

## Geographic Discount Pushback

If they try to apply a location-based discount below the UK rate:

> "I'd push back on that framing. The work is identical regardless of whether I'm sitting in Geneva or London, and I'm delivering into the same EMEA product surface. Geneva has a higher cost of living than London, not a lower one. A geographic discount applied to Switzerland would go in the wrong direction. I'd expect the base to be at least UK-equivalent and ideally Geneva-adjusted upward."

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

If you have another offer in consideration (or want to create urgency):

> "I want to be transparent: I'm in late-stage conversations with another company for a similar Staff role. The timeline is tight on my end. I don't want to use it as a pressure tactic, but it does mean I need to make a decision in the next week or so. If Grafana is the right fit, I'd rather close this quickly than stretch it out."

If they ask for specifics:
> "I'd rather not share the name while we're still in discussions, but the comp range is comparable and the role has similar infrastructure scope. What I can say is that Grafana's product and the opportunity to build this team from the ground up is what I'm most interested in."

---

## Closing Framework

**Ideal outcome:** CHF 185,000 base + RSU grant of $180,000+ over 4 years (annual equivalent: ~$45,000 USD / ~CHF 41,000) = total comp ~CHF 226,000

**Acceptable outcome:** CHF 175,000 base + RSU grant of $200,000 over 4 years = total comp ~CHF 221,000

**Walk-away line:** Total comp below CHF 200,000 (base + annual RSU equivalent) makes this non-competitive against Geneva alternatives.

---

## Notes

- Grafana is remote-first with strong async culture. Don't accept a remote penalty.
- They offer 30 days annual leave as standard. Hold the line on this; it's already in policy.
- Confirm whether Switzerland would require them to set up a local entity or whether they pay through an EOR (employer of record). This affects payroll, social contributions, and benefits. Ask: "How do you currently handle employment for Switzerland-based team members?"
- If EOR: expect slightly lower take-home due to EOR fee structures. Factor this in.
