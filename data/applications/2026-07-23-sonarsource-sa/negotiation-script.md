# Negotiation Script: Senior Software Engineer (Java) - Remediation Agent, SonarSource
**SonarSource SA · Geneva, Switzerland · July 2026**

---

## Market Context

**Sonar company profile:**
- Global HQ in Geneva, offices in Austin, Bochum, Singapore and elsewhere; profitable, $400M+ revenue, not a burn-rate startup
- Serves roughly 75% of Fortune 100 companies; SonarQube, SonarQube Foundation Agent and agentic governance tools are core products, not side bets
- The Remediation Agent work sits close to the company's actual growth bet (AI code review and verification), which usually means better internal visibility and funding than a peripheral team

**Senior Software Engineer comp range in Geneva (2025-2026), backend/infra with AI orchestration experience:**
- Entry senior (5-7 years): CHF 125,000 - 140,000 base
- Market (8-10 years, solid backend + some LLM/agent exposure): CHF 140,000 - 160,000 base
- Strong (10+ years, prior staff-level or founding-engineer scope, like this profile): CHF 160,000 - 180,000 base
- Your target band: **CHF 165,000 - 180,000 base**

Note the title mismatch: the role is scoped as "Senior Software Engineer," but the profile behind this application is Senior Staff-level with founding-engineer and post-acquisition architecture experience. That's a legitimate lever, not a reason to undersell into the standard senior band.

**Likely benefits (typical for Sonar Geneva roles, confirm in offer):**
- Swiss BVG/LPP pension contribution, employer-weighted
- Discretionary annual bonus, commonly 8-12% at IC senior level
- Standard Geneva PTO (mid-20s days) plus seniority additions
- Relocation/on-site support mentioned in the posting; irrelevant here since already Geneva-based

**Total package target:** CHF 175,000-185,000 base plus 8-12% bonus and standard benefits.

---

## Your Leverage

1. **Overqualified for the title, not for the work.** Founding-engineer and Senior Staff experience against a "Senior Software Engineer" req means the ceiling of the band is the realistic floor for this conversation, not the stretch.
2. **Already fluent in the actual problem.** Sandboxed execution, event-driven pipelines, and treating AI output as untrusted input aren't abstract to this profile, they're what the last several years of platform work were built around.
3. **Java plus AI orchestration in one person is rare.** Most candidates are strong in one and weak in the other; this profile has production Java experience (Benevity) and hands-on LLM/agentic workflow experience (Alaya and beyond).
4. **Geneva-based, no relocation, no visa question.** Removes an entire category of friction the posting explicitly flags as a constraint (no visa sponsorship).
5. **Acquisition track record is externally verifiable.** A platform that survived M&A due diligence by a $1B+ acquirer is a data point Sonar can check, not just a claim.

---

## Opening Anchor

When asked "What are your compensation expectations?":

> "Looking at the Geneva market for this level of backend and AI-systems work, and given the founding-engineer and staff-level scope I'd be bringing into a role titled senior, I'm targeting base compensation around CHF 175,000 to 180,000. I'm flexible on how that's structured across base, bonus and benefits. What's the band for this req?"

**Why this works:**
- Names the title-versus-scope gap directly instead of hoping they notice
- Anchors near the top of the realistic range
- Closes with a question, keeping the next move on their side

---

## If They Come Back Low (CHF 140,000-150,000 range)

> "I hear you, and I want to understand what's driving that number, is it the internal band for this title, or something specific about how the role's scoped? Because the work described, moving prototypes into production services and building the safety layer around untrusted AI output, is closer to what I've been doing at staff level than to a standard senior scope. Is there room to either move on base or revisit the title against the actual responsibilities?"

**Then hold ground with a concrete comparison:**

> "To be direct, the Java plus LLM-orchestration combination isn't common, and I'd rather find the right number now than renegotiate the title in six months once the scope becomes obvious."

---

## If They Push Back on Fit for a "Senior" (Not Staff) Title

> "That's fair to raise. I'm not attached to the title, I care about the scope and the comp matching it. If the role stays titled senior, I'd want the compensation to reflect the actual work: production-grade services, mentoring through design review, and the judgment calls that come with 17 years of doing this, not just the label on the req."

---

## Equity / Bonus Negotiation

Sonar is a profitable, established company, not an early-stage startup, so equity (if offered at all) is likely RSUs or a modest options grant rather than founder-style equity with cliffs to negotiate. Focus energy on cash and bonus structure instead.

1. **Signing bonus to bridge a base gap:**
   > "If base is capped by the internal band, would a one-time signing bonus in the CHF 15,000-20,000 range be possible to close the gap for year one?"

2. **Bonus formula, not pure discretion:**
   > "Is the annual bonus tied to specific delivery or reliability metrics for the remediation engine, or is it fully discretionary? I'd rather have clarity on what hitting target looks like."

3. **If RSUs are offered:** ask for the vesting schedule and cliff length up front, and whether refresh grants are standard practice at this level, since that tells you whether the number quoted is a one-time sweetener or part of ongoing comp.

---

## Geographic Discount Pushback

If Sonar frames the offer against a lower non-Geneva benchmark:

> "Geneva's cost of living is part of why I've anchored where I have. This isn't a remote role with flexible geography, it's an on-site Geneva position, so the comp should reflect the Geneva market specifically, not a blended average across offices."

---

## Competing Offer Leverage Template

Use only if an actual competing process exists; don't manufacture one.

> "I'm in later-stage conversations with another company for a role at a similar level. I'd rather move forward with Sonar if the numbers line up, the remediation work is more interesting to me technically, but I do need to make a decision on a reasonable timeline given where that process stands."

---

## Closing Move

Once verbal agreement is reached on base:

> "Sounds good. Can you send the written offer so I can look at the full package, base, bonus structure, pension, and start date, and get back to you within a few days?"

Always get numbers in writing before verbally accepting.
