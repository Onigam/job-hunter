## Negotiation Script: VP Engineering, Finary

Context that changes the math here: Finary raised a €25M Series B backed by PayPal Ventures and Y Combinator, on top of 600,000 users on a live consumer product. That's a real revenue and usage base behind the round, not a pre-launch bet, so this reads as a well-funded scale-up comp conversation, not an early seed gamble. The role reports directly to the CEO and owns a 40-person org, which is VP-of-Engineering scope at a company big enough to have that title mean something. The listing frames it as hybrid, Paris-based or remote with six in-person meetings a year, which matters because I'd stay Geneva-based rather than relocate, and that's the first thing to get aligned on before talking numbers.

## Market anchor

- French Series B fintech, VP Engineering, 40-person org, CEO-direct: base typically lands EUR 140,000-180,000, plus a meaningful equity grant given the stage.
- Geneva-based remote-hybrid VP-level hire, benchmarked against Swiss cost of living rather than the Paris local market: closer to CHF 190,000-230,000 base equivalent. Geneva runs noticeably above Paris on cost of living, and six mandatory trips a year plus ongoing async leadership work justify pricing against the home base, not the office location.
- Given 17+ years of experience, a founding-engineer track record with an exit to a unicorn, and direct relevance (having actually built and scaled a platform from zero, twice over if you count the post-acquisition integration), the target is the upper end of a blended range: EUR 165,000-175,000 or CHF equivalent, plus equity sized for VP-level ownership of a 40-person org.

## Opening anchor script

"Given the scope here, owning both the technical roadmap and a 40-person team reporting to the CEO, and coming from a founding-engineer background with an exit through acquisition, I'd expect a base in the range of CHF 210,000 equivalent, benchmarked against where I'm actually based, plus an equity grant sized for VP-level ownership at this stage. Can you walk me through how comp is structured for a hybrid hire who isn't relocating to Paris, so we're anchored on the same numbers?"

## If they come in low

"I hear the number, and I want to understand where it's coming from. Is that benchmarked against the Paris market specifically, or a blended range that assumes relocation? I'm staying in Geneva, which changes the cost-of-living math, and I want the offer to reflect that rather than default to the local band."

If they hold the line on base: "I can move on base if the equity grant reflects the Series B stage and the scope of the role, ownership over the roadmap and a 40-person team is a lot more than a typical senior IC package. What I wouldn't want to do is settle below CHF 190,000 equivalent, that undervalues both the track record and the six-figure hiring and mentorship responsibility that comes with the org size."

## Equity notes

- Ask what the equity grant is priced against: the post-Series B valuation, or an earlier 409A-equivalent French BSPCE valuation that might sit meaningfully below the round.
- Confirm standard 4-year vesting with a 1-year cliff; ask specifically whether there's any acceleration on a change of control, given Finary already has PayPal Ventures on the cap table and could be an acquisition target itself down the line.
- BSPCE (the French equivalent of stock options) has specific tax treatment that differs from Swiss and US equity; get written detail on exercise windows and what happens if I'm Geneva-based rather than a French tax resident.
- Ask whether refresh grants are tied to funding milestones or performance cycles, since a Series B company is likely to raise again within 18-24 months and early hires often get diluted without a refresh conversation built in.

## Geographic and remote pushback

"The role is described as hybrid with six in-person meetings a year, which tells me the day-to-day expectation is remote-first. I'd want comp benchmarked against Geneva, where I'm actually based and where the cost of living runs above Paris, rather than a flat Paris-market rate with a remote discount applied on top. Happy to share the comparison if it helps make the case internally."

## Competing offer, if genuinely in play

"I'm further along in conversations with another company at a comparable VP or Staff+ level, in a similar comp range to what I've mentioned. Finary is the one I'm most drawn to, the personal finance mission and the stage both fit what I'm looking for, but I want to be upfront about where things stand so we can move at a pace that works for both of us."

## Close

A Series B company with PayPal Ventures and Y Combinator on the cap table, 600,000 users, and a CEO-direct VP role isn't a place to accept a first number without pushback, there's real funding behind it and real scope in the ask. Get the base, the equity basis and what it's priced against, the vesting and acceleration terms, and how the hybrid arrangement actually works day to day, all confirmed in writing before signing. The location-adjustment conversation is the one most likely to move if raised directly rather than assumed away.
