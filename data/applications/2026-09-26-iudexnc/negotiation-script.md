# Negotiation Script: iudexnc, Co-Founder & CTO (Zurich)

## What's actually being negotiated

This isn't a salary negotiation in the usual sense. The posting says it's a cofounder role, "not employment", with "up to equal equity split" vesting over 4 years with a 1-year cliff. iudexnc AG is pre-seed, with a paid pilot at a Swiss insurer, a prototype in production testing (Dextra is listed as live on their site) and DACH insurer conversations underway. Ari (technical CEO) and Simon (commercial) are the existing founders.

So the real variables are, in order of importance:
1. Equity percentage and the terms attached to it.
2. When cash compensation starts, and at what level.
3. Role boundaries with a CEO who is technical himself.
4. Practicalities: Geneva to Zurich, on-site expectations, relocation.

Numbers below are estimates from general market knowledge for 2025-2026. Check them against the Swiss ICT salary survey, levels.fyi (Zurich), and a couple of recent Swiss pre-seed term sheets before any serious conversation. Get a Swiss startup lawyer and tax advisor to review the shareholders' agreement before signing anything.

## Market reference (Switzerland, approximate)

| Reference | Cash (CHF, base) |
|---|---|
| Senior Staff / Principal Engineer, Zurich | 200k-260k |
| CTO / VP Eng, funded Swiss scale-up | 220k-300k |
| Cofounder salary, pre-seed (before a priced round) | 0-90k |
| Cofounder salary after a seed round (CHF 2-5M) | 110k-160k |
| Fractional CTO, Switzerland | 1,100-1,500 per day |

My opportunity cost is roughly CHF 220k+ a year in cash at Staff level. Over the first 12-18 months before a seed round, that's a gap of CHF 150k-300k that I'm effectively investing as sweat equity. Use that figure calmly, not as a threat; it frames why the equity has to be real.

## My numbers

- **Equity anchor:** equal split with the two existing founders, so roughly 33% on a fully diluted founder basis before the ESOP.
- **Target:** 25-33%.
- **Walk-away:** below ~20%, or if I'm vesting and the other founders aren't, or if there's no written path to a market-ish salary after the first round.
- **Cash:** CHF 0-60k until the seed closes if the runway allows, then CHF 140k-160k after a seed round, stepping toward market as the company grows.

## Questions to ask before naming anything

> "Before we talk about splits, can you walk me through the cap table today? Who holds what, is there any existing investor, SAFE or convertible, and have Ari and Simon's shares been put on the same vesting schedule?"

> "What's the runway right now, and how much of it comes from the pilot revenue versus founder money?"

> "When you say up to an equal split, what decides whether it's equal or less?"

Their answers tell you whether "equal" is a real offer or a ceiling they don't expect to reach.

## Opening anchor script

> "I'm treating this as a founder decision, not a job offer, so I'm comfortable with little or no salary until the seed round, as long as the equity reflects that. Given that I'd be building the whole technical side, from ingestion and retrieval to evaluation and the tenant isolation insurers will audit, I think an equal split is fair: roughly a third each before the option pool. I'd also want all three of us on the same vesting terms, so nobody's carrying risk the others aren't."

Then stop talking.

## If they come in lower (e.g. 10-15%)

> "I get why you'd start there. You've already got the pilot and the relationships, and that's real value. The thing is, at 10-15% I'm really an early employee with a CTO title, and I'd price that differently. I'd be giving up ~CHF 200k a year in cash for at least a year and a half. If we can get to around 25%, with the same vesting as you two and a clear salary step after the seed, I'm in. Otherwise I'd need a salary much closer to market from day one, and that eats your runway."

If they genuinely can't move on percentage, trade in this order:
1. Cash from day one (CHF 90k-120k) in exchange for the lower stake.
2. Additional equity tied to milestones, for example a top-up once the seed closes or when the first large DACH insurer signs.
3. A board seat or formal veto on technical hires and architecture.

## Equity terms to get in writing

- **Vesting for everyone.** 4 years with a 1-year cliff is fine, but it should apply to all founders, with credit for time already served by Ari and Simon handled explicitly.
- **Acceleration.** Ask for double-trigger acceleration (change of control plus termination or role change), full or at least 50% of unvested shares. Single-trigger is rarely granted; don't die on that hill.
- **Good leaver / bad leaver.** Get clear definitions. As a good leaver (let go without cause, illness), vested shares should be bought back at fair market value, not nominal.
- **Share type and timing.** In a Swiss AG, founders usually hold ordinary shares directly. Subscribe as early as possible, at nominal value, before any priced round. Buying in later at a discount can create income tax on the difference, while gains on privately held shares are generally tax-free. Confirm the details with a tax advisor, this depends on your canton and situation.
- **Anti-dilution.** Founders don't normally get anti-dilution protection, investors do. Instead ask for pro-rata rights in future rounds and agree the ESOP size (typically 10-15%) before the split, so it doesn't come only out of your stake.
- **IP assignment.** Fine to assign everything related to iudexnc. Carve out anything pre-existing (personal open-source work on GitHub) explicitly.
- **Decision rights.** Agree in writing who owns hiring, architecture and vendor choices on the technical side, since the CEO is also technical.

## Geography (Geneva to Zurich)

On-site collaboration is required and they're in Zurich. Zurich is not cheaper than Geneva, so there's no geographic discount to push back on; if anything the reverse.

> "I'm based in Geneva. I'm happy to be in Zurich several days a week from the start. Before a seed round, I'd like the company to cover travel, for example a GA travelcard, since I'd be doing this without a salary. After the raise, let's revisit relocation."

## Competing offer template (use only if true)

> "I want to be straightforward with you. I'm also in late-stage conversations for a CTO role at a funded company, with a market salary. I'd still rather do this, because the problem is better and it's a founder role. But I need the equity and vesting terms to make that trade-off make sense, so I'd like to close on terms within the next two weeks."

Never invent a competing offer. If there isn't one, say you're comparing against staying at Staff level, which is true and just as strong.

## Red flags that should end the conversation

- Other founders not on vesting, or refusing to discuss the cap table.
- "Equal split" turns into equity only via options granted later at the board's discretion.
- No agreed point at which the CTO gets paid.
- Pressure to sign before a lawyer reviews the shareholders' agreement.
