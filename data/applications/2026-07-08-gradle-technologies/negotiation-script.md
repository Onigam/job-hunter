# Negotiation Script: Gradle Technologies, Founding Backend Engineer (Lucerne)

## Context worth naming up front

The listing asks for 4 years of experience and offers 2.0-3.0% equity, framed as a "Founding" role. Magino has 17+ years and Staff/Senior Staff seniority, so there's a real gap between the posted level and the actual candidate showing up. That gap is an advantage, not a problem to smooth over: either the comp band flexes upward to match a more senior hire, or the equity number needs to reflect that a founding-level grant is going to someone who can operate closer to a technical co-founder than an early-career engineer. Also worth clarifying early: Gradle Technologies already has an established core product (Gradle Build Tool, 15+ years old, 80M+ monthly downloads) and a commercial product (Develocity). A "founding" equity grant at a company with that much history usually means either a new subsidiary, a new product line with its own cap table, or looser use of the word "founding" than the equity percentage implies. Get this in writing before treating the equity number as comparable to a true pre-seed grant.

## Market anchors (Switzerland, senior backend/platform, 2026)

- Zurich senior/staff backend engineer base runs roughly CHF 150,000-190,000; Lucerne typically sits 10-15% under Zurich for comparable roles, which puts a realistic base band around CHF 130,000-165,000 for a mid-senior hire.
- That band assumes the "4 years experience" framing is taken at face value. If the conversation moves toward Magino's actual seniority, Staff-level Swiss comp (Zurich CHF 260,000-300,000, Lucerne discounted maybe 10-15% off that) is the more honest anchor, and the equity should be discussed as founding-level rather than early-employee-level.
- Developer tools / infra SaaS companies with a profitable core product (which Gradle Technologies has, unlike a pre-revenue startup) can usually support a higher cash component than a true zero-revenue founding role, since there's real ARR to point to. Use that to push for more cash, less reliance on equity as the main lever.
- Working anchor range to open around: CHF 150,000-175,000 base if the role stays scoped at the posted level, or CHF 200,000+ if it's negotiated up to reflect actual seniority, plus the equity grant clarified per the note above.

## Opening anchor

"Before I put a number out, I want to understand two things: what this equity grant is actually a percentage of, since the company already has an established product and revenue, and whether the level you're hiring for tracks the 4 years in the listing or is flexible given what I'd bring at Staff level. That changes where I'd anchor."

If pushed for a number regardless:

"Based on senior backend comp in Lucerne and Zurich, plus the founding equity component, I'd want to start the conversation around CHF 165,000 to 180,000 base, with the equity and any bonus structured on top of that."

## Counter-offer script if they come in low

If the offer lands under roughly CHF 150,000 base:

"Thanks for sending this over. I want to be straightforward: this reads more like a standard senior IC offer than a founding one, and given the equity ask and the scope of ownership implied by 'founding,' I was expecting the base to sit higher, closer to CHF 165,000 or above. Is there room to move there, or to make up the difference through the equity terms?"

If they cite a fixed band:

"If base is genuinely capped by the band for this title, I'd want that reflected somewhere else, either a larger equity grant given the founding framing, a sign-on to bridge the gap, or a defined comp review at 6 months once I've shown what a Staff-level hire actually does with this scope."

## Equity negotiation

- Confirm what entity the 2.0-3.0% is a percentage of. If it's a new subsidiary or product line, ask for the most recent valuation or funding basis used to set that number, and whether there's a 409A-equivalent or Swiss equivalent valuation on file.
- Standard vesting is four years with a one year cliff. If it's longer, push back, especially for a founding-level grant where the whole point is early, meaningful ownership.
- Ask directly whether this is options, RSUs, or phantom equity, since Swiss tax treatment differs meaningfully across the three and that affects what the grant is actually worth net.
- Ask about anti-dilution protection and whether there's a plan for follow-on funding rounds that would dilute an early grant before it's had time to vest.

## Geographic and structure pushback

If Lucerne comp gets anchored down using a lower cost-of-living argument relative to Zurich or Geneva:

"Lucerne is cheaper than Zurich, sure, but it's not a discount market by much, and the role's scope doesn't shrink because the city does. I'd rather anchor on the seniority and ownership this role carries than on a city-to-city cost delta."

If remote work is on the table instead of relocating:

"If this works as a remote or hybrid role from Geneva, that's worth being explicit about upfront, since it changes both my calculus and possibly the comp conversation depending on how the company treats remote versus onsite Lucerne comp."

## Competing offer template

Use only if a real competing process exists; a bluffed number collapses the moment someone asks a follow-up question.

"I'm further along in another process at a comparable level, with a base offer in the CHF 190,000 range. I'd rather move forward here if the offer lands in the same neighborhood, the founding scope and the chance to build something new inside an already-proven company is the more interesting problem to me, but the numbers need to be close enough that I'm not leaving something real on the table."
