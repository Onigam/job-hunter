# Negotiation Script — Backend Engineer, Langfuse (ClickHouse)

---

## Market Context

**Role type:** Backend Engineer, individual contributor, working directly with the three founders on ingestion pipeline and platform scaling. Posting language ("core member of the team from day 1") and the founder-proximity framing suggest they're open to senior-level candidates even though the public range reads broad.

**Company context:** Langfuse joined ClickHouse in January 2026. Comp is likely structured through ClickHouse's framework rather than a standalone Langfuse package. ClickHouse is private, so equity is options or RSUs pending a liquidity event.

**Published range:** The public listing shows a wide USD 70,000-130,000 band, which almost certainly spans junior through senior. At 17+ years of experience with a founder-level track record (Alaya, built to acquisition), the anchor should sit at or above the top of that band, not in the middle of it.

**Geographic complexity:** The role wants 4-5 days a week in Berlin. Magino is based in Geneva. This is either a relocation conversation or a remote-from-Europe carve-out; both change the anchor and need to be resolved before base salary is discussed in detail.

**Switzerland benchmark (for comparison, since Geneva is the current base):**
- Senior Backend Engineer, Geneva, well-funded product company: CHF 130,000-155,000 base
- Staff-level IC with platform/infra scope: CHF 150,000-180,000 base
- Swiss employer-side social contributions add roughly 12-13% on top

**Berlin/Germany benchmark (senior backend, funded startup):**
- EUR 85,000-110,000 base is typical; EUR 110,000-130,000 for staff-level scope at a company with real revenue and a recent acquisition behind it
- Berlin cost of living is lower than Geneva or SF, which is a legitimate point in their favor and worth acknowledging rather than fighting

---

## Opening Anchor

**If relocating to Berlin:**

> "Given the scope, owning parts of the ingestion pipeline end to end, working directly with the founders, I'd expect a base in the EUR 105,000-125,000 range plus a meaningful equity component. That reflects senior-level backend work at a company post-acquisition, not an early-career hire. I'd want to understand the equity structure since ClickHouse is private."

**If working remote from Geneva with periodic Berlin travel:**

> "If this stays remote-first with the one-week-a-month Berlin cadence, the Geneva market is the more honest anchor for my situation. I'd be looking at something closer to CHF 150,000 base, with travel costs covered separately. I'm flexible on how we structure that, salary versus a travel stipend, but the total needs to reflect that I'm not lowering my cost base by working from Berlin."

---

## If They Come In Low

**Scenario: They offer near the bottom of the published range (EUR 75,000-85,000 or equivalent).**

> "I want to understand the gap before I push back on a number. The role description talks about owning the ingestion path end to end and working directly with Max, Marc, and Clemens, that's a senior scope. Is the offer reflecting a more junior banding, or is there room to move once we're aligned on level?"

**If they say the band is fixed:**

> "If the base is capped, I'd want the equity grant sized to close that gap, and I'd want to see the numbers behind it, not just a percentage. Can you share the current option pool size, the most recent valuation basis, and the vesting schedule?"

---

## Equity Questions to Ask

Since ClickHouse is private:

1. What is the current 409A or equivalent fair market value per share?
2. What is the strike price on the options offered?
3. What percentage of the company do the offered options represent, and what's the total pool size?
4. Vesting schedule, standard is 4 years with a 1-year cliff, monthly after.
5. What happens to vested options if there's another acquisition or a liquidity event before a public listing?
6. What's the post-departure exercise window? 90 days is common but punishing; push for longer if it's short.

---

## Geographic Premium Pushback

If they anchor to a Berlin cost-of-living discount while expecting Geneva-level output and availability:

> "I get that Berlin is a cheaper market, and I'm not going to pretend Geneva costs the same. But if I'm relocating and working 4-5 days a week in your office, the comp should reflect the seniority of the work, not just the city. I'd rather find a number that's fair on both sides than have this resurface in a year."

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

> "I'm in a couple of other conversations at a similar stage, so I do need to move on a reasonable timeline. I'd rather land here if the scope and the package both make sense, this is closer to the kind of infra ownership I want than most of what I'm looking at."

**If there's a real comparable offer:**

> "I have another offer that's stronger on base. I'd still rather be here, the ingestion pipeline problem and the team are more interesting to me, but I need something in the same neighborhood to make that trade-off make sense."

---

## Benefits and Non-Cash Items Worth Negotiating

- Relocation support if moving to Berlin (shipping, temporary housing, visa/admin help)
- Travel budget and cadence if staying remote-first with periodic Berlin trips
- Home office stipend (EUR 1,500-3,000 one-time)
- Professional development / conference budget
- Signing bonus if the base band genuinely can't move
- Start date flexibility, Swiss notice periods typically run 1-3 months
