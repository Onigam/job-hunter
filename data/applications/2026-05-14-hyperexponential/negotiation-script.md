# Negotiation Script — hyperexponential, Staff Engineer AI Products

---

## Context and Complexity

This role is London-based (hybrid, 2 days/week). Magino is in Geneva. This creates two distinct negotiation scenarios:

**Scenario A: Remote-first arrangement negotiated in** (Magino stays Geneva-based, travels to London occasionally)
**Scenario B: Full London comp benchmark** (if a relocation or partial relocation is involved)

Address the location question early, before comp discussions. Ambiguity about location will derail comp anchoring.

Geneva Staff Engineer with AI/ML focus (CHF):

Geneva is consistently 20-30% more expensive than London by purchasing power. CHF is structurally stronger than GBP. Any London offer needs to be adjusted if Magino is remaining Geneva-based.

## Opening Position

### If they ask salary expectations first (deflect until you have an offer):

"I'd rather understand the full shape of the role first and hear what range you've benchmarked for this level. Staff Engineer compensation varies a lot depending on equity structure and benefits. What range are you working with?"

### If pressed for a number (give a range, anchor high):

"For a Staff Engineer role at this level, with the AI/ML focus and the technical scope you've described, I'd expect something in the £155K-£175K base range in London. If we're talking about a remote arrangement from Geneva, I'd want to adjust for Swiss cost of living, which means roughly CHF 200K-230K equivalent."

---

## If They Come In Low (Under £130K base)

**Script:**

"I appreciate the offer. I want to be direct: the base is lower than I'd expect for a Staff Engineer role with this scope, and it's lower than my current market rate. Can you walk me through how the total compensation package is structured? Specifically, I'd like to understand the equity refresh schedule, the options strike price relative to the last valuation round, and whether there's flexibility on base."

**Leverage points:**
- 17 years of experience including founding engineering and AI systems work in production
- The Alaya acquisition validates technical decisions at scale (not just senior IC work)
- Benevity AI tooling adoption story demonstrates the "technical multiplier" skill that Staff roles specifically need
- Geneva cost of living is factually higher than London; this isn't negotiating in bad faith

**Fallback anchor:** "If the base is a constraint, is there flexibility on equity grant size or L&D budget? I'm interested in making this work, but I need the total number to be in the right range."

## Equity Negotiation

hyperexponential is a16z-backed with $91M raised. The options are likely EMI (UK Enterprise Management Incentive) scheme, standard 4-year vest with 1-year cliff.

**Questions to ask:**
1. What is the current strike price, and when was the last 409A/valuation?
2. What is the total options pool percentage for this role?
3. Is there an acceleration clause on change of control (single-trigger or double-trigger)?
4. Are options EMI-qualified? (affects UK tax treatment significantly)

**What to push for at Staff level:**
- 0.1%-0.3% fully diluted at grant (verify against options pool)
- If they offer below 0.1% FD, push back: "For a Staff Engineer who's expected to shape technical direction and act as a multiplier, I'd expect the equity to reflect that. What's the typical range for this level at hx?"
- Ask about refresh grants: "Is there an annual refresh program, and how is that typically sized?"

**Cliff negotiation (if you've been elsewhere recently):**
"Given my experience level and that I'm bringing direct domain-adjacent expertise, would hx consider a shorter cliff, say 6 months, or an accelerated initial vest? I understand this is outside the standard template, but it's worth asking."

## Geographic Discount Pushback

If they try to apply a "location discount" because Magino is Geneva-based and remote:

"I'd push back on that framing. Geneva is one of the most expensive cities in the world, CHF is stronger than GBP, and the work output is location-independent. A location discount would mean I'm taking a pay cut relative to a London-based colleague doing the same role. I don't think that's a trade I'd make. Is there flexibility to benchmark this to role and impact rather than geography?"

---

## Competing Offer Template

If you have another offer in hand (or are in late stages elsewhere):

"I want to be transparent: I'm in late stages with another company, and I expect to have an offer in hand by [date]. I'm genuinely interested in hx and I'd prefer to make this decision in your favor if the comp is comparable. Can we move quickly on the offer conversation? Specifically, I need to know the base and equity package to make a call."

If no competing offer but you want to create urgency:

"I'm actively interviewing and I have a few conversations at similar companies moving quickly. I'm not trying to create artificial pressure, but I'd rather give you an honest heads-up than disappear unexpectedly."

## Relocation Consideration (Scenario B)

If hyperexponential wants Magino in London more regularly or full-time:

**Minimum ask:**
- Relocation allowance: £10K-£15K (one-time)
- Temporary housing support for first 3 months
- Flight reimbursement for family visits (or remote working weeks in Geneva)
- Hybrid arrangement: 2 days London, rest remote from Geneva

"I'm open to discussing a more regular London presence, but I'd need the company to support the transition practically. What's the policy on relocation support for non-UK candidates?"

## Closing Notes

- hyperexponential's culture explicitly values ownership and direct communication. Be direct in negotiations. They will respect it more than hedging.
- The "zero churn, 50+ insurers" positioning means they're not in survival mode. They have room to pay well.
- The a16z backing and IPO optionality is real upside. Don't trade base for speculative equity if the base is already too low.
- If total comp lands at £155K+ (or CHF equivalent) with reasonable equity, this is a strong offer at this stage of the company.
