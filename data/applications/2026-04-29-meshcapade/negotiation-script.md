# Negotiation Script — Meshcapade Principal Engineer

## Context

Meshcapade is a German GmbH (Tübingen) that was acquired by Epic Games in November 2025. The Principal Engineer role is remote-Europe. Candidate is based in Geneva, Switzerland. This context matters: Epic Games is a large US company with proper compensation bands, but the local entity is still a German GmbH, so there may be friction between what Epic can pay and what the legal entity structure allows.

---

## Market Anchors

**Geneva/Switzerland — Principal Engineer level (Staff+)**
- Base salary: CHF 180,000 to CHF 230,000
- Total comp (with bonus): CHF 200,000 to CHF 260,000
- Reference: Senior Staff engineers at Swiss tech companies (EPFL spin-offs, Lausanne/Geneva scale-ups) fall in this range. Geneva is the most expensive city in Switzerland, consistently ranking in the top 3 globally.

**Germany remote — Principal Engineer**
- Base salary: €100,000 to €150,000 depending on company maturity
- Total comp: €110,000 to €170,000
- Pre-Epic Meshcapade likely paid toward the lower end of this. Post-acquisition, expect upward pressure.

**Epic Games — internal comp reference**
- Epic typically pays US-competitive base for senior IC roles
- For a European remote Principal: could be €130,000 to €180,000 total comp, depending on how they harmonized post-acquisition
- Epic does not currently have a public stock program (private company), so no public equity, but they may offer profit-sharing or internal units

---

## Opening Position

If asked for expectations early (before an offer), deflect once:

> "I'd rather hear what the role's band looks like first. I want to make sure we're aligned on scope before anchoring a number."

If pressed for a range, anchor at the top of Geneva market:

> "Given my level and Geneva as base location, I'm thinking CHF 200,000 to CHF 220,000 all-in. I'm open to discussing how that breaks down between base and variable."

If they push back immediately saying they're a German entity paying German rates:

> "I understand the entity structure. That said, I'm based in Geneva and my cost of living is tied to Switzerland, not Germany. What flexibility does the Epic umbrella give you on this? I want to find a structure that works for both sides."

---

## Counter-Offer Script (If They Come In Low)

**Scenario: They offer €110,000 to €120,000 base (pre-Epic-level Meshcapade range)**

> "Thanks for the offer. I appreciate you putting a number on the table. I want to be straightforward: at €[X], there's a meaningful gap from what I need to make this work given where I'm based and the scope of the role. I think the Principal level here carries real ownership, not just technical contribution, and that's reflected in the range I shared earlier. Is there room to get to €140,000 to €150,000 base, and if not in base, what can we do on the variable or benefits side?"

**If they can't move base:** ask about:
- Remote work stipend (coworking space in Geneva)
- Hardware budget (they mention this in their perks)
- Conference/learning budget
- Signing bonus (one-time, easier for them to approve)
- Performance review timeline (6 months instead of 12 to reassess)

---

## Equity Notes

Meshcapade is now a wholly owned subsidiary of Epic Games. Epic is private (not publicly traded), so there's no liquid stock option program like you'd get at a public company or well-funded startup.

**Ask:**
> "What does equity or long-term incentive look like now that you're part of Epic? Is there a profit-sharing arrangement or any form of Epic equity that's part of the comp package?"

If the answer is no equity: factor that into the base ask. A role with no equity at this level should be compensating in base. The €140,000 to €160,000 floor is appropriate.

If they offer any form of Epic units or profit-sharing, that changes the calculus. Get the vesting schedule and liquidity terms in writing before counting it.

---

## Geographic Discount Pushback

Companies sometimes try to apply a "location discount" for remote candidates outside their headquarters country. Don't accept a German-rate salary for a Geneva-based life.

> "I'm aware some companies apply a cost-of-living adjustment based on HQ location. For reference, Geneva has one of the highest costs of living in Europe, consistently higher than Munich, Berlin, or most German cities. If the adjustment goes the wrong direction, that's a real problem for me. I need compensation that reflects where I actually live."

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

If you have another offer (or are at final stages elsewhere), use it:

> "I want to be transparent: I have another process that's moving fast and at a higher range than what you've put on the table. I'd prefer Meshcapade given the technical challenge, but I need the numbers to be in the same territory. Can we get to [target] by [date]?"

If you don't have a competing offer but want to signal urgency:

> "I have a few other conversations in late stages. I'm not trying to pressure you, but I do need to make a decision in the next week or two. If Meshcapade is the right fit, I'd rather close this cleanly and quickly."

---

## Minimum Acceptable Terms

- Base: CHF 185,000 (Geneva-based) or €130,000 (if paid on German entity and remote-adjusted)
- Variable: not required but welcome
- Benefits: hardware budget, coworking allowance confirmed, full remote
- Walk away if: base is below CHF 160,000 / €115,000 with no path to a 6-month review
