# Negotiation Script: Believe, Staff Engineer Agentic AI (Paris)

## Context that shapes everything

The role sits in Paris, in Core Engineering, and would be paid in euros on a French employment contract. That's the main thing to keep in mind. Swiss market rates are a useful anchor for what you're worth, but Believe will benchmark against Paris, and Paris Staff pay runs well below Geneva. The negotiation is less about matching a Swiss salary and more about three questions:

1. Can you do this from Geneva at all, and how often do you need to be in Paris?
2. What's the total package once bonus, any long-term incentive and travel costs are counted?
3. How does the net figure compare with staying in the Swiss market?

The posting mentions a remote work policy but no salary. Believe has about 2,000 employees across 50+ countries. It's no longer a startup, so there are no BSPCE-style options; BSPCE are reserved for companies under 15 years old, and Believe was founded in 2005. It was also taken private in 2024 by a consortium around the founder with EQT and TCV (verify the current ownership before the call). That matters, because any equity would be a management incentive plan tied to a future exit, not liquid stock.

## Market reference points (estimates, verify before the call)

These are working estimates from public compensation data. Check them on Levels.fyi, Glassdoor and Welcome to the Jungle (filters: Paris, Staff or Principal Engineer, AI/ML), and on Levels.fyi for Geneva and Zurich for the Swiss side.

| Reference | Base | Variable | Total per year |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paris, Staff Engineer at a scale-up or media/tech company | ~€115k to 145k | ~10 to 15% | ~€125k to 165k |
| Paris, Staff AI/ML Engineer at a well-funded AI company | ~€140k to 180k | varies, often equity | ~€150k to 220k |
| Paris, Staff at Big Tech | ~€150k to 190k | bonus plus RSUs | ~€220k to 320k |
| Geneva, Senior Staff at a scale-up | ~CHF 190k to 240k | varies | ~CHF 200k to 260k |
| Zurich, Staff+ at Big Tech | ~CHF 210k to 260k | bonus plus RSUs | ~CHF 330k to 460k |

Takeaway: a good Believe offer probably lands between €130k and €160k total. Gross, that's already below a Geneva Senior Staff role, and the net gap is wider, since French employee social charges take around 22 to 25% before income tax, while Swiss deductions are closer to 12 to 15%. Run the net comparison on a real simulator (for example the French URSSAF simulator and a Geneva tax calculator) before you name any number.

## Your numbers

**Walk-away floor:** decide this before the first call and write it down. Because of the net-pay gap and travel costs, work out the lowest French gross that leaves you no worse off than a comparable Geneva offer. For many people that point lands above €150k.

**Target:** base around €150k to 160k, 15% variable, a written remote arrangement, and travel to Paris paid as a business expense rather than out of salary.

**Opening anchor:** €165k base. You can justify it with 17+ years, a cofounder exit, five years at Senior Staff, and the fact that this is a framework role with org-wide scope, not a squad-level IC role.

## Scripts

### When the recruiter asks for your expectations early

"I'd like to understand the location setup first, because it changes the numbers a lot. I live in Geneva and I'm currently benchmarked against the Swiss market, where Senior Staff roles pay around CHF 200k to 240k. I know Paris is different. For a Staff role with org-wide scope on the agent framework, I'd be looking at a base around €165k, plus variable. What's the band for this position?"

If they push for a single number: "€165k base is where I'd start. I'm flexible on the mix if there's a meaningful variable or long-term incentive."

### Working from Geneva

This has to be settled before salary, because it can make the offer impossible. A French employer with someone working mostly from Switzerland creates social security and tax obligations in Switzerland. Many French companies won't take that on for one person. Ask directly:

"How would the contract work for someone based in Geneva? I'm happy to be in Paris regularly, say two or three days every other week, but I'm not planning to relocate right away. Do you already employ people in Switzerland, or would this go through an employer-of-record?"

Possible outcomes, roughly in order of preference:
- A Swiss contract through an employer-of-record, paid in CHF (usually better for you, and more expensive for them).
- A French contract where most working days are physically in France. That's tough from Geneva unless you go to Paris every week.
- Relocation to Paris. Only consider it if the number clearly justifies it.

### If the offer comes in low

"Thanks. I want to make this work, and the role is a good fit. The base is below what I expected for the scope, though. The posting describes an org-wide framework with standards for every squad, which is closer to Principal than to a single-team Staff role. If we can get the base to €155k and cover travel to Paris as a business expense, I'm ready to move forward."

Then stop talking and let them respond.

### If they can't move the base

"I understand bands are fixed. In that case, could we look at a sign-on bonus to close part of the gap in year one, a higher variable target, and a written agreement on remote days and travel costs? I'd also want a compensation review at 12 months, tied to the year-one goals in the posting: squads shipping on the framework, eval suites in place, and published standards."

### French contract details worth negotiating

- **Trial period (période d'essai):** for cadres it's usually 4 months, renewable once. Ask for no renewal in writing.
- **Forfait jours and RTT:** confirm the number of RTT days. It's often 8 to 12 a year and worth real money in time.
- **Notice period:** usually 3 months for cadres. Ask whether they'd buy out a notice period at your current employer, if that applies.
- **Title and level:** make sure "Staff" maps to a documented level with a visible path to Principal.

### Competing offer template (use only if real)

"I want to be upfront: I'm in late-stage conversations with a company in Switzerland at around CHF [X]. Believe is interesting to me because of the scale of the problem and the agent framework work, but the net difference is significant once French social charges and travel are counted. Is there room to close part of the gap?"

Don't invent a competing offer. If there's nothing concrete, just say you're benchmarked against the Swiss market, which is true.

## Equity notes

Believe is PE-backed and private, so any equity would come through a management incentive plan (free shares, or shares bought through a holding) with value only at a future exit or recapitalisation. Ask:
- Is the Staff level eligible for the management plan at all, or does it start at Director?
- What's the instrument (actions gratuites, BSA, direct investment), and do you have to put in your own money?
- What's the vesting and what happens on departure (good leaver versus bad leaver terms)?
- What's the expected exit horizon?

Treat any number they give as zero when comparing offers. If it pays out, it's a bonus. Push on base and variable, since that's the money you'll actually see.

## Checklist before the call

- Net pay comparison done (France gross versus Geneva gross), with travel costs included.
- Remote and contract setup question asked and answered before any salary figure.
- Floor written down.
- Current Believe ownership and any recent restructuring checked.
