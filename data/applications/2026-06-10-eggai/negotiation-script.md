# Negotiation Script — EggAI, Senior AI Software Engineer

---

## Market Context

EggAI is Munich-based but this is a remote role explicitly open to Switzerland. This creates a geographic tension: Munich senior AI engineer salaries run roughly €85,000–120,000, while Geneva/Switzerland market rates for the same seniority are CHF 140,000–180,000. They may try to apply Munich rates. Don't accept that framing.

**Switzerland market anchors (2025–2026, Senior AI/Platform Engineer):**
- Median senior AI engineer in Geneva: CHF 145,000–160,000 base
- Staff+ level with 15+ years and founding background: CHF 160,000–185,000
- Top-of-band for AI specialization + remote-friendly company: CHF 175,000+

**Your target range:** CHF 165,000–175,000 base  
**Floor:** CHF 145,000 (below this, the Geneva cost-of-living math breaks)  
**Opening anchor:** CHF 175,000

---

## Opening the Compensation Conversation

Wait until they bring it up or until you have an offer in hand. Don't volunteer a number first.

If they ask early ("What are your salary expectations?"):

> "I'm still learning about the full scope of the role, so I'd rather not anchor prematurely. What I can say is that I'm benchmarking against Switzerland market rates for Senior Staff-level AI engineering, which puts me in the CHF 160,000–175,000 range for base. Does that fit the band you're working with?"

If they push for a single number:

> "Based on my research and current conversations, CHF 170,000 is where I'd want to land for a role at this level."

---

## If They Come In Low (below CHF 145,000 or equivalent EUR)

**Script 1: Geographic framing**

> "I appreciate the offer. The role is exciting, but I need to flag a gap. I'm based in Geneva and benchmarking against the Swiss market, not Munich rates. Cost of living here runs meaningfully higher, and CHF 145,000 is the floor that makes sense for my situation. Can we look at whether there's room to move?"

**Script 2: Seniority framing**

> "The number is lower than I expected for a Senior-level role. I'm bringing 17 years of engineering experience, including building and shipping production AI systems and founding a platform that was acquired. I'd expect that background to land in the CHF 155,000–170,000 range. Is there flexibility?"

**Script 3: Hold the line**

> "I want to be straightforward: I'm not going to be able to make it work below CHF 145,000 given where I'm based. If the band doesn't reach there, it's better we know now."

---

## Equity Discussion (Startup-Specific)

EggAI is an early-stage company (Munich, series unknown). Equity is a real part of the conversation.

**If they offer options or phantom shares:**

> "Can you walk me through the vesting schedule, the cliff, and the current valuation you're using for the strike price? And what's the last external round, if there's been one?"

**What you want to see:**
- 4-year vest, 1-year cliff (standard)
- Strike price at or below current 409A/board valuation
- Anti-dilution protections (pro-rata at minimum)
- Information rights if the grant is meaningful (0.1%+)

**If the equity feels thin:**

> "Given the stage, equity is part of how I'd think about this. If the base is below CHF 155,000, I'd want to see the equity package compensate meaningfully for that. What does the options pool look like as a percentage?"

**If equity is significant (0.25%+), you can negotiate base down slightly:**

> "If the equity reflects genuine upside at the current stage, I'm flexible on base. Let's talk about what realistic exit scenarios look like for the options."

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

If you have another offer (or are close to one):

> "I want to be transparent: I'm at late stages with another company in a similar space. Their offer is in the CHF 155,000–165,000 range. I'd prefer to join EggAI based on what you're building, but I can't leave that much on the table without a reason. Is there room to get closer to CHF 165,000?"

If the other offer is hypothetical or softer:

> "I've had other conversations at the CHF 155,000–165,000 level. I'm not trying to use that as a hammer, but it does represent my sense of market rate for this work."

---

## Other Levers (if base is stuck)

- **Signing bonus:** ask for CHF 10,000–20,000 to bridge any gap
- **Annual review clause:** "Can we agree on a 6-month review rather than annual, given it's a new role?"
- **Equipment/home office allowance:** CHF 1,500–3,000 upfront is standard for remote roles
- **PTO:** if Swiss contract, statutory minimums are 4 weeks; push for 5
- **Conference/training budget:** CHF 2,000–3,000/year is reasonable for an AI-focused role

---

## What Not to Do

- Don't accept the first number without at least one counter.
- Don't apologize for your number ("I know it's high, but...").
- Don't let them anchor you on their Munich salary band without pushing back on the geography.
- Don't accept verbal offers: get it in writing before giving notice anywhere.
