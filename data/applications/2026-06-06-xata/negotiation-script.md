# Negotiation Script — Xata Backend Engineer

---

## Market Context

**Company:** Xata — $35M funded, remote-first, years of operational runway. Series A/B stage. Equity is meaningful but not pre-product.

**Role level:** Senior/Staff Backend Engineer (the JD reads "Senior/Staff" — position yourself at Staff, given 17 years and co-founder track record).

**Market anchors (2025-2026):**
- Senior Backend Engineer, remote EU: EUR 110,000-140,000 base
- Staff Backend Engineer, remote EU: EUR 130,000-170,000 base
- Geneva local market equivalent: CHF 145,000-185,000 for Staff level
- AI infrastructure / Postgres tooling niche commands a slight premium vs. generic backend roles

**Xata specifics:** No public salary band disclosed. Remote-first means likely paying EUR rather than CHF, so expect EUR 120,000-155,000 as the realistic offer range. Equity likely 0.05%-0.20% at this stage for a Staff hire.

---

## Opening Anchor

If asked for your number first, give a range, not a point:

> "For a Staff-level role with your tech profile and funding stage, I'd be targeting EUR 145,000-160,000 base, plus equity. That's consistent with what I see in the AI infrastructure market for this level."

If they push back that it's a Senior role, not Staff:

> "Given the scope in the JD, owning projects end-to-end, contributing to architecture decisions, open-source responsibilities, it reads like Staff scope to me. Happy to calibrate if there's a leveling conversation to have."

---

## If They Come In Low (EUR 110,000-125,000)

**Script:**

> "I appreciate the offer. The range is a bit below where I'd expected to land for Staff-level infrastructure work. I've been looking at the market data for senior backend roles in AI tooling, and the EUR 130,000-145,000 band is where similar profiles are being placed right now. Is there room to move on base, or is this a case where the equity structure makes up the difference?"

**Bridge to equity:** If they say base is fixed, ask for the cap table specifics before accepting the equity as compensation.

---

## Equity Negotiation

Ask these questions before accepting any equity component:

1. What is the current fully diluted share count?
2. What was the last round valuation (or the implied 409A)?
3. What is the strike price on the options?
4. What is the vesting schedule? (Standard: 4-year / 1-year cliff)
5. Are there acceleration provisions on acquisition?
6. What happens to unvested options if I leave vs. if the company is acquired?

**Target:** 0.10%-0.20% for a Staff hire at Series A/B. If the offer is below 0.08%, push back:

> "The equity is the main variable here given the stage. 0.05% on a company at this funding level doesn't reflect the risk/upside tradeoff I'd expect. Can you get to 0.12% or higher? That's where I'd feel the overall package is competitive."

---

## Geographic Pushback (Geneva Premium)

If they discount for Geneva vs. other EU locations:

> "Geneva is consistently in the top three most expensive cities in Europe, ahead of Zurich and Amsterdam by several points. My rent and cost of living are indexed to the Swiss market, not the EU average. The EUR 140,000 target already reflects a discount from Swiss domestic market rates, which would be CHF 155,000-175,000 for this level."

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

If you have another offer (or can reasonably create urgency):

> "I'm at late-stage conversations with one other company in the distributed systems / infrastructure space. I'd prefer Xata given the PostgreSQL focus, but I need the packages to be comparable. If you can get to EUR 145,000 base and 0.12% equity, I can move quickly."

If you don't have a competing offer but have had first-round interviews elsewhere:

> "I have a few conversations in progress, but I'm prioritizing Xata for the role itself. I'd rather move fast here than let those play out, if the numbers can meet in the middle."

---

## Benefits to Negotiate Beyond Cash

- Remote co-working budget: ask to confirm the amount (standard at Xata per JD)
- Learning allowance: confirm amount and scope (conferences, courses, books)
- Hardware budget: not listed in JD, worth asking for CHF 3,000-5,000 setup budget
- Company offsites: two per year mentioned, confirm locations/format

---

## Walk-Away Point

Below EUR 120,000 base with standard equity (less than 0.08%) is not competitive for Staff-level infrastructure work with this profile. Don't accept a Senior title with Staff responsibilities unless the comp reflects the actual scope.
