# Negotiation Script — Founding Full Stack AI Engineer (Undisclosed AI Data Startup via Pragmatike)

## Role Context
- Title: Founding Full Stack AI Engineer
- Company: Early-stage AI data startup (stealth / undisclosed — likely pre-Series A or seed)
- Stack: Full-stack + AI/ML integration (Node.js, React, Python, LLM pipelines likely)
- Location: Remote or hybrid EMEA — confirm early
- Via: Pragmatike (recruitment intermediary)

---

## Market Anchors — Switzerland / EMEA Founding Engineer Level

**Senior Full-Stack Engineer (IC), Switzerland:**
- Geneva market: CHF 140,000–175,000 base
- Zurich market: CHF 145,000–180,000 base
- Remote (EMEA, non-CH company): EUR/CHF 100,000–140,000 base

**Founding Engineer / First Technical Hire, early-stage startup (Europe):**
- Pre-seed: EUR 80,000–110,000 base + 1.5–3% equity (4-year vest, 1-year cliff)
- Seed: EUR 100,000–140,000 base + 0.5–1.5% equity
- Series A: EUR 130,000–165,000 base + 0.2–0.7% equity

**AI Engineer premium:**
Demand for engineers with real production LLM/embedding/multi-agent experience is high enough to add 10–15% to standard full-stack rates in 2025-2026. Use this.

**Magino's current comp baseline:**
- Senior Staff Engineer at Benevity (Geneva): CHF 140,000–160,000 total cash
- Any offer below CHF 130,000 in cash needs substantial equity to justify the move

---

## Opening Anchor

When asked about compensation expectations, use this framing:

> "Based on my current role and the Geneva market for senior full-stack engineers with production AI experience, I'm targeting a base in the CHF 140,000 to 155,000 range. For a founding role where equity is a real part of the picture, I'm open to discussing flexibility on the base if the equity stake reflects the founding-level risk and contribution. For this stage I'd expect to be somewhere in the 0.75% to 1.5% range. What does your comp structure look like?"

Key moves:
- Name your number first to anchor the conversation
- Frame equity flexibility as conditional on the stake being meaningful
- Immediately ask them to reveal their structure

---

## If They Come In Below Expectations

**Scenario A: Low base, low equity (e.g., EUR 90k base, 0.3% equity)**

> "I appreciate you sharing that. The base is below where I'd need to land given my current comp and Geneva costs. The equity is also lower than what I'd expect for a founding engineer coming in at this stage. Can you walk me through the cap table and the current option pool? If there's flexibility to get to 1% or above, and the vesting terms are clean, I think we can work with a more flexible base. But I'd need to see the structure before I can say yes to anything."

Do not accept without seeing: cap table, total shares outstanding, last valuation or price per share, option pool size and remaining allocation.

**Scenario B: Acceptable base, minimal equity (e.g., CHF 130k base, 0.2% equity)**

> "The base is workable. Where I'd push back is on the equity. If I'm the first or one of the first engineers and I'm shaping the foundation this company will build on for the next several years, 0.2% doesn't reflect that. I'd want to be at 0.75% to 1% at minimum to feel like the risk and contribution are properly accounted for. Is there room in the option pool to get there?"

---

## Equity Negotiation Specifics

**Minimum acceptable for this role type:**
- Pre-seed: 1.5–3%
- Seed: 0.75–1.5%
- Series A or later: 0.3–0.75%

**Questions to ask before signing:**
- What is the current fully diluted share count?
- What was the last round's price per share or valuation?
- What percentage of the option pool remains after this grant?
- Will there be a refresh mechanism after milestones or additional funding rounds?
- What happens to unvested options if the company is acquired? (look for acceleration clause)
- Is there a double-trigger or single-trigger acceleration on change of control?
- What's the exercise window if you leave? (standard is 90 days, push for 5-10 years)

**On vesting terms:**
- Standard: 4-year vest, 1-year cliff. Accept nothing shorter on the cliff.
- Push for: monthly vesting after the cliff (not annual tranches).
- Nice to have: acceleration on termination without cause (6–12 months accelerated vesting).

---

## Geographic Discount Pushback

If they cite "remote market rates" to justify a lower number than Swiss-equivalent:

> "I understand the remote rate logic, but my expenses, taxes, and cost of living are anchored in Geneva. The roles I'm comparing this to are all benchmarked here. I'm not asking for a premium above what the role is worth, I'm just making sure we're starting from the right baseline."

---

## Counter-Offer Leverage Template

If you have or can create a competing offer:

> "I want to be straight with you: I have another process at a similar stage, and the numbers there are in the CHF 145k–155k range with a larger equity stake. I'm more interested in this role because of the AI data problem and the founding opportunity, but I need the comp to be in a comparable range to make this a clear decision. What's the most you can move on?"

Note: Only use this if you genuinely have another offer in progress. If not, use market rate framing instead: "the range I'm seeing in comparable roles in Geneva."

---

## Pragmatike-Specific Notes

Pragmatike is the intermediary, not the employer. Their margin comes from a placement fee, so they're motivated to close. Use this.

- Ask directly: "Can you share the salary range the client has budgeted for this role?" Recruiters often know and will share if asked plainly.
- If they push you to name a number first: "I'd rather not constrain the conversation before I understand the full package. What range did the client give you?"
- Don't negotiate hard with Pragmatike directly. Get the information from them, then save the real negotiation for the founding team.

---

## Walk-Away Conditions

Do not accept an offer that includes:
- Base below CHF 115,000 (or EUR equivalent) without equity above 1.5% at seed stage
- Equity below 0.5% at any stage for a founding engineer role
- Vesting cliff longer than 12 months
- No cap table or valuation information provided before signing
- No acceleration clause on change of control (for a founding-level role, this is standard)
- Exercise window shorter than 2 years post-departure
