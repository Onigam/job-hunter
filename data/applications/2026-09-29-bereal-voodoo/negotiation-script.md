# Negotiation Script: CTO, BeReal (Voodoo)

Posted location: Paris, reporting to BeReal's CEO. Executive level, full-time.
Posted comp: "competitive salary based on experience", no figure. Benefits listed: Swile vouchers, Gymlib, 100% employer-paid healthcare.
Context: BeReal has 40M+ monthly users and a team of 100+. The posting cites ~$50M revenue this year. Voodoo (~800 people, privately held) acquired BeReal in 2024. Candidate is based in Geneva.

All figures below are working estimates for 2026. Re-check against recruiter feedback, Levels.fyi, Glassdoor, Figures/Ravio French tech benchmarks and anyone you know at Voodoo before any call.

---

## 1. Read the situation first

**This is a Paris role, not a Swiss one.** The usual "Geneva is expensive" argument cuts the wrong way here: Voodoo will pay against Paris bands, and a Paris CTO base is lower than a Geneva one in nominal terms. The real question is how you'd live. Three options, each with a different pay conversation:

1. **Relocate to Paris.** French contract, French tax and social charges. Negotiate against Paris CTO bands plus relocation support.
2. **Commute Geneva to Paris** (TGV is about 3h10). French contract, several days a week in the office. Ask for a travel and housing allowance, and check where you'd be tax resident. Get cross-border tax advice before signing, as the France-Switzerland rules for someone living in Geneva and working in Paris aren't trivial.
3. **Swiss-based with regular Paris presence.** Unlikely for a CTO of a Paris-centred org, but worth asking once. If they say yes, the Swiss anchors below apply.

Ask on the first call which of these they'd consider. Don't give a number until you know.

**Level.** This is a real CTO job: long-term technical direction across mobile, backend, data and infra for a 40M+ user app, reporting to the CEO. Anchor to that scope, not to a head-of-engineering or staff band.

**Equity is the big unknown.** Voodoo is private. Ask whether there's a Voodoo equity plan (BSPCE, free shares/AGA, or phantom shares), whether BeReal has its own incentive plan tied to profitability or revenue, and what vesting and liquidity look like.

## 2. Market anchors (base salary, 2026, estimates)

| Market | Head of Eng / VP Eng | CTO, scale-up (100+ eng org, 10M+ users) | CTO, top-tier consumer scale |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paris, EUR gross | 150k to 190k | 180k to 240k | 230k to 300k |
| Geneva / Zürich, CHF | 230k to 280k | 260k to 330k | 300k to 380k |

On top of base, a Paris CTO package at this level typically includes a variable bonus of 15% to 30% and some form of equity. Geneva figures are here for the option 3 case and as a reference for what you'd be giving up in nominal terms.

**Targets (Paris contract)**

- Walk-away: EUR 200k base, plus a bonus and a written equity grant.
- Target: EUR 230k to 250k base, 20% to 25% target bonus, meaningful equity.
- Opening anchor: EUR 270k base.
- If commuting from Geneva: add a travel and housing allowance, roughly EUR 2k to 3k per month, or equivalent in base.

**Targets (Swiss-based, only if they open that door)**

- Walk-away CHF 270k, target CHF 290k to 310k, open at CHF 330k.

## 3. Opening the conversation

If the recruiter asks for expectations early:

> "Before a number, can I understand the setup? I'm in Geneva today, and whether this is a full move to Paris or a regular commute changes how I'd think about the package. For a CTO role with this scope, 40M+ users, a native app migration and the Go transition, reporting to the CEO, I'm looking at a base around EUR 270k, plus a bonus and equity. I'd also like to understand how equity works for BeReal leadership inside Voodoo."

If they push for one number:

> "Around EUR 270k base for a Paris contract. I'll adjust once I see the bonus structure and the equity."

## 4. If they come in low

**Scenario A: a head-of-engineering number with a CTO title (for example EUR 180k).**

> "Thanks for sharing it. The scope here is full technical ownership across mobile, backend, data and infra for one of the larger consumer apps in Europe, reporting to the CEO. EUR 180k is closer to a VP or head-of-engineering band in Paris. To make this work I'd need to be at EUR 230k base, and I'd want the bonus and equity in writing. Is there room to get there?"

**Scenario B: decent base, weak variable and no equity.**

> "The base is close. What's missing for me is upside. BeReal's path to profitability is the thing I'd be accountable for technically, so I'd like part of my package tied to it, either a Voodoo grant or a BeReal-level incentive tied to revenue or profitability milestones. Can we look at that together?"

**If base is truly capped:**

> "If base can't move, could we look at a sign-on bonus, a higher bonus target, a larger equity grant, and a formal comp review at twelve months against agreed goals?"

**If commuting and they won't fund it:**

> "I'm happy to be in Paris most of the week. The travel and housing cost is real though, roughly EUR 30k a year. Either an allowance or the equivalent in base would close that gap."

## 5. Equity and upside

Questions to ask before accepting:

- What's the instrument: BSPCE, AGA (free shares), stock options, or phantom shares? What entity, Voodoo or BeReal?
- How many shares, at what strike or valuation, and what's the fully diluted share count? A grant without a denominator means nothing.
- Vesting: standard is 4 years with a 1-year cliff. For a CTO hire, ask for monthly vesting after the cliff.
- Acceleration: ask for double-trigger acceleration (change of control plus termination). BeReal has already changed owners once; it's a reasonable ask.
- Liquidity: Voodoo is private. Are there buybacks or secondary windows? What happens to vested equity if you leave?
- Anti-dilution: rarely offered on employee equity, but ask how future rounds or intra-group restructuring would affect the grant.
- Tax: BSPCE and AGA have specific French tax regimes, and your residency (Paris or Geneva) changes the picture. Get advice before valuing the grant.

Treat private-company equity as upside, not salary. Don't give up more than about 10% of base for it unless the grant is clearly large and the liquidity path is clear.

## 6. Competing offer template

Only use if there's a real alternative, even an early-stage one. Never invent an offer.

> "I want to be transparent: I'm also in late-stage conversations for a senior engineering leadership role in Switzerland, with a package around CHF [X]. BeReal is where I'd rather be, because the technical problem is more interesting. If we can get to EUR [target] base with the equity we discussed, I'm ready to commit and stop the other process."

## 7. What else to negotiate

- Title and reporting line in writing: CTO of BeReal, reporting to the BeReal CEO, and clarity on the relationship with Voodoo's central tech and data teams.
- Budget and hiring authority for the engineering org.
- Relocation package or commuting allowance.
- Start date and notice period flexibility.
- Remote days per week, especially if commuting from Geneva.
- Severance terms, given the executive level and the 2024 post-acquisition layoffs at BeReal.

## 8. Walk-away signals

- No equity or incentive at all for a CTO who owns the profitability roadmap technically.
- Real decision rights sitting with Voodoo central tech, leaving the BeReal CTO as a delivery manager.
- A package below EUR 200k base with no path up.
