# Negotiation Script: Nexthink, Staff Software Engineer (AI-native Automation)

Context going in: no salary range is published on the listing, so the number has to be pulled out early rather than assumed. Nexthink is an established, PE-backed Swiss company (Permira), not an early-stage startup, so the comp structure is more likely base plus bonus plus a modest RSU or option grant than a founder-style equity stake. The role is based in Lausanne with a hybrid policy, and I'm already in Geneva, so there's no relocation ask and no cost-of-living discount that should apply.

## Market context

- Staff-level backend engineers in the Geneva-Lausanne corridor typically land in the CHF 160,000-210,000 base range, with the top of that band reserved for candidates bringing distributed-systems depth and a track record at the Staff/Principal bar, which this role explicitly asks for (10+ years, 3+ at Staff/Principal).
- Nexthink's own scale, 1,300+ customers and 1,000+ employees across 9 offices, plus the fact that this is a strategic AI initiative rather than a maintenance role, both push toward the upper half of that band rather than the middle.
- Bonus structures at this size of company usually run 10-15% of base for senior IC roles. Worth confirming target versus historical payout, since a 15% target that pays out at 60% most years isn't actually a 15% bonus.
- RSU or option grants at a PE-backed private company are real but usually modest for an IC hire and tied to a liquidity event timeline that's worth asking about directly rather than assuming.

## Opening position

"I don't see a range listed, so I'd want to start there. Given the Staff-level bar in the posting and the scope, rebuilding Flow's execution model as an AI-native platform, I'd be looking at a base around CHF 195,000, plus whatever the standard bonus and equity structure looks like for a role at this level. I'd rather anchor on a number and adjust from real data than guess blind."

## If they come in low

"I want to understand how this number was built, since Staff-level backend roles with this scope typically run higher in this market. If there's a banding constraint on your end, I'd rather hear that directly so we can figure out where the flexibility actually is, whether that's base, bonus target, or the equity component."

Key move: don't accept a lowball as the market number without asking what it's benchmarked against.

## Equity and bonus questions to ask before agreeing to anything

- What form the equity takes: RSUs, options, or phantom equity, since Nexthink isn't publicly listed.
- Vesting schedule and whether there's a standard cliff.
- Whether there's a refresh policy after year one, or if this is a one-time grant.
- Bonus target versus actual historical payout for the team, not just the headline percentage.
- Whether double-trigger acceleration exists in case of a future acquisition or IPO, worth asking given I've already been on the acquired side of that exact scenario once at Alaya.

## Geographic and hybrid framing

"I'm based in Geneva, which is close enough to Lausanne that the hybrid schedule isn't a friction point, so there's no relocation cost and no argument for a location-based discount either. I'd expect the offer to reflect the Geneva-Lausanne market rate directly."

## Competing offer template (use only if real)

"I'm further along in a process for a comparable Staff-level role elsewhere, with total comp tracking toward roughly CHF [X]. I'd rather make this work since the Flow rebuild is the more interesting problem, but the numbers would need to be in the same range for that to be a real choice."

Only use this if there's an actual competing process in flight.

## Closing line for any round

"Send me the full package in writing, base, bonus target, and equity together, and I'll turn around a decision fast. I'd rather negotiate once with complete numbers than go back and forth on pieces."
