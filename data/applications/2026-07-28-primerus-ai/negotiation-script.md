# Negotiation Script: Primerus AI (Technical Co-Founder / CTO)

## Context that shapes the negotiation

The posting discloses no salary and no equity split. This is a bootstrapped company, €120k ARR, 10+ paying customers, 8 months old, one founder with domain expertise and go-to-market traction but no disclosed technical co-founder yet. That combination means two things going in: cash is almost certainly limited (this isn't funded), and equity is the real lever. A CTO joining post-traction but pre-institutional-funding is not the same negotiation as joining pre-revenue, the founder already has proof the business works, so the co-founder isn't pricing in pure risk, they're pricing in what building the entire technical org from here is worth.

## Switzerland market anchors

- Senior Staff / Principal Engineer base in Geneva or Zurich at a funded startup: roughly CHF 150k-190k cash, before equity.
- CTO cash comp at a Swiss seed-stage startup, when any cash is paid at all: commonly CHF 90k-140k, often below market on purpose because equity is the real compensation.
- Bootstrapped companies at this ARR level frequently can't clear CHF 100k cash for a co-founder in year one. The realistic range to anchor around is CHF 60k-110k cash plus a real co-founder equity stake, not an "employee number one" grant.
- Geneva cost of living is roughly 20-30% above Zurich and well above Berlin or most of the DACH region. That gap is worth naming explicitly since the role is remote-first and the founder may be anchoring on non-Swiss cost of living.

## Opening anchor

Frame it as a co-founder split, not a hire negotiation. "Given this is a technical co-founder role building the entire architecture from here, and you're already at €120k ARR with paying customers, I'd want to talk about this as a founder equity split rather than a standard early-employee grant, somewhere in the 15-25% range depending on how compensation and existing IP are structured, plus a cash component in the CHF 80k-120k range that reflects Geneva living costs while the company is still bootstrapped." Anchor high on equity specifically, since cash is likely constrained and equity is where the real value gets negotiated.

## Counter-offer script if they come in low

If the initial offer undershoots on equity ("we were thinking 5-8%"): "I get that you've built this alone for 8 months and that's real value already created, so I'm not expecting a 50/50 split. But 5-8% reads more like an early technical hire than a co-founder who's about to own the entire architecture and product build-out going forward. Can we look at something closer to 15% as a starting point, with vesting tied to milestones we agree on together, so the split reflects what I'm actually taking on rather than what's left over after the founder round."

If cash is offered at zero: "I can work with low or deferred cash for a period if the equity and vesting terms are right, but I'd want a floor, say CHF 3-4k a month starting after the first 3-6 months, tied to a revenue or runway milestone, so this isn't open-ended."

## Equity negotiation notes

- Ask for a standard 4-year vesting schedule with a 1-year cliff, this is the market norm and a lack of a cliff on either side is a red flag about how the founder thinks about commitment.
- Push for double-trigger acceleration (change of control plus termination) rather than single-trigger, standard founder protection.
- Get clarity on the cap table before agreeing to a number: how much of the company does the current founder hold, is there any outside investment already, and is there an ESOP pool carved out that would dilute the co-founder grant specifically.
- Ask directly whether the current founder has taken any investment or convertible notes. A co-founder split negotiated against an unknown existing dilution stack is not a real number.
- Anti-dilution: for a co-founder grant this early, standard treatment (pro-rata participation rights in future rounds, or at minimum no unilateral dilution without board approval) should be table stakes, not a concession.

## Geographic discount pushback

If the founder anchors on a lower non-Swiss market rate: "I know remote-first teams often benchmark against Berlin or similar, but Geneva runs meaningfully higher on cost of living, and I'd want the cash component reflecting that even if the equity split is the bigger piece of this. I'm not asking for full Swiss market cash, since this is bootstrapped, just for the discount not to be as large as if I were remote from a lower cost-of-living market."

## Competing offer template

Use only if genuinely applicable at the time: "I'm also in later-stage conversations with a couple of funded platform engineering roles at Staff-plus cash levels, so the equity and mission fit here need to make sense against that. I'd rather find terms that work for both of us than let this stall on a number." Keep this factual and low-key, what matters here is having other options, not a bluff, and overplaying it against a bootstrapped founder can read as tone-deaf given they've built real traction with limited resources.
