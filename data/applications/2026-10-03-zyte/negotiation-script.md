# Negotiation Script: Zyte, Staff Engineer (Core & MLOps), Remote

## Context that shapes the numbers

Zyte (formerly Scrapinghub, the company behind Scrapy) is a fully remote web data company with 260+ people across 33 countries. It's private and established, not an early-stage startup. The posting is listed in **Lisbon, remote**, with a published base of **EUR 100k to 115k**.

That published band is the single biggest factor here. It's pegged to Portugal, and you live in Geneva. Three things follow:

- **Expect them to hold the band.** Companies that publish ranges usually have little room above the top. Your realistic target is the top of the band plus a better structure, not a Geneva salary.
- **Employment structure matters as much as the number.** Zyte almost certainly hires Swiss residents through an Employer of Record (EOR) or as contractors. Each option gives very different net pay.
- **Your level is a fit for Staff.** 17 years, a founder exit and five years at Senior Staff. The gaps they'll press on are gRPC, Kafka, Terraform and Vert.x. Acknowledge them, don't let them push you toward the bottom of the band.

## Market anchors (estimates, verify before the call)

Rough figures from public surveys and recruiter data. Re-check on levels.fyi, Glassdoor (Zyte), swissdevjobs.ch, and the Michael Page and Hays Switzerland salary guides.

| Benchmark | Base salary (approx.) |
|---|---|
| Staff Engineer, Geneva, product company | CHF 165k to 210k |
| Staff / Principal Platform Engineer, Zürich | CHF 180k to 230k |
| Staff Engineer, fully remote EU company, "global" band | EUR 120k to 150k |
| Staff Engineer, Lisbon / Portugal-pegged | EUR 85k to 115k |
| This posting | EUR 100k to 115k |

EUR and CHF are close to parity (roughly 0.93 to 0.95 CHF per EUR lately, check on the day). EUR 115k is about CHF 108k, well under the Geneva floor for this level.

## Settle the structure first

| Option | How it works | What it means for you |
|---|---|---|
| A. EOR with Swiss payroll (Deel, Remote, Oyster) | Zyte pays the EOR, which employs you in Geneva with AVS, LPP and accident insurance | Cleanest. Swiss social security handled, but Zyte pays employer costs on top of the base, so they'll resist going higher |
| B. Contractor (you invoice as a Swiss sole trader) | You bill a day rate or monthly fee | You pay all social charges yourself (AVS/AI/APG around 10% as self-employed, plus your own pension and insurance). No paid holidays. Only worth it with a clear premium |
| C. Employment through a Portuguese or Irish entity | Foreign payroll while living in Geneva | Avoid. Working from Switzerland usually means Swiss social security applies, so it gets messy fast |

Line to use:

"I'm based in Geneva and would work from here full time. For Swiss social security to be compliant, an EOR contract is usually the simplest route. Is that how Zyte hires in Switzerland today, or do you work with contractors here?"

## Opening anchor

If they ask for your expectations before stating a number (they shouldn't, the band is public):

"Given the scope, Staff on the core platform with on-call and cross-team mentoring, I'm looking at the top of your range, EUR 115k base. I'd also want to talk about a location adjustment, since Geneva's cost of living is well above Lisbon's."

Aim for: **EUR 115k base** on EOR, plus a location adjustment or sign-on. If contracting: **EUR 650 to 750 per day** (EUR 115k over roughly 220 working days is about EUR 520, plus a premium for unpaid holidays and social charges you'd cover yourself).

Walk-away floor to decide before the call. A reasonable one: EUR 108k base on EOR, or EUR 600/day as a contractor. Below that, the Geneva cost of living makes the role a step back financially, however interesting the work is.

## If they come in low (e.g. EUR 100k to 105k)

"Thanks, I appreciate the transparency on the band. I'm keen on the role, especially the schema registry and control-plane work. What I'm weighing is that the range is calibrated for Lisbon and I'm in Geneva, where comparable Staff roles start around CHF 165k. I'm not asking you to match that. But for 17 years of platform work and a founder background, the top of your band, EUR 115k, feels right. Can we get there?"

If they say the base is capped:

"Understood. Is there room elsewhere? A sign-on bonus, a guaranteed salary review at six months tied to agreed goals, or a larger equity grant would close the gap for me."

## Geographic discount pushback

If they argue the band is global and location-neutral:

"Fair enough, and I like that you publish it. I'd only point out that a location-neutral band set from Lisbon lands very differently in Geneva. If the band is truly global, I'd expect someone at my level to sit at the top of it, not the middle."

## Equity

Zyte is private, so equity is illiquid either way. Ask before you value it:

- Does the Staff level come with stock options, and how many? What's the total outstanding share count, so you can work out the percentage?
- Vesting schedule and cliff (standard is 4 years with a 1-year cliff).
- Strike price and the latest valuation used to set it.
- Exercise window after leaving. 90 days is standard and harsh; ask for 5 to 10 years.
- Acceleration on a change of control (single or double trigger).
- Is there any history of secondaries or buybacks? If not, treat the options as a lottery ticket and negotiate on cash.
- Swiss tax: options are usually taxed as income at exercise (or at grant in some cases). Get a Geneva tax adviser to check the plan document before you count it.

Script:

"Could you share the equity details for this level, the number of options, the strike price and the total share count? I want to understand what percentage it represents and how exercise works if I ever leave."

## Other levers worth asking about

- Home office and equipment budget, coworking allowance in Geneva.
- Learning budget and conference travel (KubeCon, Kafka Summit would be directly useful given the gRPC/Kafka gaps).
- On-call compensation: is there a per-shift stipend? How often does rotation come round?
- Annual company meetup travel paid by Zyte.
- Paid holidays: Swiss legal minimum is 4 weeks; ask for 5 through the EOR.

## Competing offer template (use only if real or credibly in progress)

"I want to be upfront: I'm in late-stage conversations with another company for a Staff platform role, and their offer is around CHF [X]. Zyte is my first choice because of the core platform work and the remote setup. If we can get to EUR 115k plus [sign-on / equity / location adjustment], I'm ready to commit this week."

Never invent a competing offer. If you don't have one, say instead: "I'm in other processes, and I'd like to make a decision within the next two weeks."

## Order of the conversation

1. Confirm the structure (EOR vs contractor) before any number.
2. Anchor at EUR 115k, the top of the published band.
3. Raise Geneva cost of living as a reason for a location adjustment or sign-on.
4. Ask for equity details, then decide how much weight to give them.
5. Collect the softer levers (on-call pay, holidays, learning budget).
6. Get everything in writing before you resign or turn down other processes.
