# Negotiation Script: Lobby, Founding Senior Engineer (Full Stack / Product)

Posted range: CHF 125,000-145,000, Zurich on-site required, founding-level equity package included. Role asks for a minimum of 8 years; Magino has 17+, plus an actual founding-engineer-to-acquisition outcome, which is the main point of strength for the whole conversation.

## Market anchors (Zurich / Switzerland, senior full-stack / founding engineer)

- Senior full-stack engineers at funded Zurich startups typically land CHF 130,000-160,000 base; founding or first-few-engineer hires can sit above that band when equity is thin or the company is pre-seed/seed.
- The posted 125-145k looks calibrated for the 8-year floor of the requirement, not for someone nearly a decade past it with a proven exit.
- Zurich cost of living runs on par with or slightly above Geneva, so there's no geographic discount argument working against Magino here, and none to concede either.
- Total comp should be evaluated as base plus equity together, since a pre-seed or seed-stage founding role often quotes a modest base specifically to preserve the equity pool.

## Opening anchor

Lead with total comp, not base alone: "Given 17 years of experience and a founding-engineer background that includes building a platform through to acquisition, I'm looking at a base closer to CHF 150,000, with a founding-level equity grant on top, rather than the posted 125-145k, which reads as set for the 8-year minimum rather than someone well past it." Follow immediately with the proof point, Alaya to Benevity, so the number lands as a claim backed by results rather than a ritual opening move.

## If they come in at or near the posted range (125-145k)

Counter script: "I hear that the range was set with the 8-year minimum in mind. My background clears that by close to a decade, and I've already run the outcome, building something from zero to an acquisition, that a founding-stage company is betting on repeating. I'd like to explore CHF 145,000 base with a stronger equity grant, or CHF 135,000 base with equity closer to the top of your founding-engineer band, whichever gives you more room on your side." Offering two paths keeps the conversation moving instead of stalling on a single rejected number.

## Equity negotiation

- Ask directly what stage the company is at and what the fully diluted pool looks like before agreeing to a percentage; a headline number means little without the denominator.
- For a founding-stage hire outside the actual founder group, 0.5%-1.5% is a realistic range depending on how early and how large the round is; push toward the top of whatever band they name given the seniority and track record.
- Standard vesting is 4 years with a 1-year cliff; confirm this explicitly and ask about acceleration on acquisition, single or double trigger, since Magino has direct experience being on the other side of one and knows exactly how much that clause matters.
- Ask whether the option pool refreshes before or after new funding rounds, since refreshing before a round dilutes existing holders less.
- Get the strike price and the current valuation basis in writing before agreeing to a percentage, not just the number itself.

## On-site terms

The role requires full-time Zurich presence and Magino is Geneva-based, so this is worth raising even though it isn't a comp issue: "I'm ready to be on-site in Zurich full time, that's not a blocker. I'd like to talk through relocation support and whether a short transition period, splitting time between Geneva and Zurich for the first month while housing gets sorted, is workable." This is a one-time transition ask, not a recurring cost, since Zurich isn't cheaper than Geneva to begin with.

## Competing offer template

Use only if genuinely in play, kept low-key rather than an ultimatum: "I'm in later-stage conversations with another team on a similar founding-engineer role, so I'd like to move relatively quickly here if the fit is right on both sides. I'd rather make a decision based on this conversation than have you compete against a number I can't fully disclose yet."

## Close

End negotiation conversations with a concrete next step, not an open-ended "let me think about it": propose a specific date to reconvene, or ask what Lobby needs from Magino's side, references, a production system walkthrough, a code sample, to get to a final number.
