# Negotiation Script, LightWork AI Lead Backend Engineer

## Context
The posting doesn't disclose a number, just "+Equity," and the role is based in London while I'm in Geneva. That means two things have to get resolved before a real number gets discussed: whether this is on-site London, hybrid, or open to Swiss-remote, and which market's comp norms actually apply. Being the first Lead Backend Engineer hire, reporting directly to a Head of Engineering who's scaled a team to 140+, is a strong anchor on scope and should be reflected in both base and equity, since this isn't a mid-level IC slot.

## Market anchors

**If London-based (relocation or hybrid):**
- Lead/Staff Backend Engineer, London, seed to Series A AI startup: GBP 90,000-130,000 base, first-hire premium and mentoring scope pushing toward the top of that band.
- First engineering-leadership hire (no direct reports yet, but standard-setting mandate) at a founder-backed London AI startup: GBP 110,000-140,000 base is a realistic ask given 17+ years and a founder track record.
- Early-stage London AI companies with Revolut/EF-adjacent founders tend to pay competitively on base but lean harder on equity than later-stage companies; expect the base offer to land conservative and the equity pitch to do a lot of the persuading.

**If remote from Switzerland:**
- Remote-from-Geneva roles at UK-HQ seed/Series A companies typically land 15-25% below a London-office equivalent for cash comp, since UK cost-of-living arguments get made regardless of where the engineer actually lives.
- A defensible remote-Swiss ask for this scope is CHF 140,000-160,000 base, which is below full Zurich/Geneva Staff-level rates but well above a generic "EMEA remote" band.
- GBP and CHF are close enough to parity as of mid-2026 that converting a London GBP offer to CHF and comparing directly is a fair, low-friction way to frame the gap.

## Opening move: clarify location and scope before anchoring on a number
> "Before we get into comp, I want to understand the location expectation, is this London on-site, hybrid, or open to remote from Geneva with regular time on-site? That changes how I think about the number, and I'd rather anchor correctly the first time than renegotiate later."

## Opening anchor (London-based scenario)
> "Given this is the first Lead Backend Engineer hire and carries the mandate to set engineering standards and mentor the team as it grows, I'd anchor to GBP 120,000 base plus a meaningful early equity grant, with the split adjustable if there's more room on one side than the other. I've done this exact shape of work before, building a platform from zero through to acquisition, so I'd expect comp to reflect founding-level ownership, not a standard senior IC hire."

## Opening anchor (remote-from-Geneva scenario)
> "I'm Geneva-based and open to regular time in London, but I'd want comp to reflect that I'm not a general remote hire, Switzerland is one of the more expensive markets in Europe. For this scope I'd anchor around CHF 150,000 base plus equity, and I'm happy to talk through how a hybrid travel cadence might factor into that."

## If they come back low
> "I get that early-stage comp has to balance runway against making competitive offers, and I don't take a first number personally. That said, the gap between what you've proposed and what this scope typically commands, first backend leadership hire, mentoring mandate, LLM integration work, is real enough that I'd want to close some of it before saying yes. Is there room to move the base, or to weight more of the difference into equity given the stage you're at?"

## Equity negotiation (early-stage, no valuation disclosed)
This is the part that needs the most scrutiny, since "meaningful early equity" is a phrase, not a number.

- Ask directly for the percentage of fully diluted shares, not just an option count, and for the last priced round's valuation so the percentage means something.
- Push for a standard 4-year vest with a 1-year cliff; don't accept anything longer than a 1-year cliff for a leadership-track hire this early.
- Ask about the strike price and whether early exercise is permitted, since that matters a lot more at seed/Series A than it does later.
- Ask about the post-termination exercise window; the standard 90 days is thin for equity meant to be a real part of comp, and extended windows (1-2 years) are a reasonable ask for a founding-adjacent role.
- Ask what the next funding round is expected to do to dilution, and whether there's any anti-dilution provision for early hires.

## Counter-offer script
> "Thanks for putting this together, I can see the thinking behind the structure. Based on the scope, first Lead Backend Engineer hire with a standards-setting mandate, and market rates for that level, I'd like to counter at [GBP 120,000 / CHF 150,000] base with the equity grant reflecting founding-team-adjacent ownership rather than a standard early hire. I'm genuinely flexible on how base, equity, and any relocation or travel support get split, as long as the total package reflects the level of ownership this role carries."

## Geographic and stage pushback, if needed
> "I understand comp at this stage has real constraints tied to runway, and I'm not asking you to pay like a later-stage company across the board. But the location and stage arguments can't both cut against me at once, if the ask is Swiss-market seniority with early-stage equity risk, the cash comp needs to at least partially compensate for that risk."

## What to hold firm on regardless of base outcome
- Written clarity on location expectations (on-site frequency, or confirmed remote-with-travel) before signing, not left as a verbal understanding.
- Title and scope matching "Lead," including whether or when direct reports are expected, documented rather than implied.
- Equity terms in writing: percentage of fully diluted shares, vesting schedule, cliff, and exercise window, not just a headline option count.
- Clear first-90-days ownership area (engineering standards, code review process, observability) reflected in the offer or onboarding plan, not just discussed on a call.
