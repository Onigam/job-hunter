# Negotiation Script: Ashby, Engineering Manager (EU/CH-eligible)

## What we know about the band
Ashby's public bands for this role: Americas $200K–$275K USD, EU band €76K–€185K EUR (covers FR, DE, IT, NL, PL, PT, ES, RO, SE, CH, HR, DK). The EU band is wide and clearly built to flex across very different cost-of-living markets, which is the opening we work with. Ashby is post-Series D ($50M, July 2025), backed by Elad Gil and Lachy Groom, so this isn't an early-stage cash-constrained startup, there's room to move.

## Switzerland market anchor
Engineering Manager / Staff-level technical leadership in Geneva or Zurich, at a well-funded but not local Swiss employer, typically lands CHF 160,000–210,000 base, sometimes with a bonus on top at larger companies. Remote-from-Switzerland roles at US/EU-headquartered startups usually settle a bit under pure Swiss-market rate but well above the low end of a pan-European band. For a company at Ashby's stage, CHF 175,000–195,000 base equivalent is a reasonable target range, translating to roughly €185,000–€205,000, at or slightly above the top of their posted EU band.

## Opening anchor
"Thanks for sharing the range. Given the Swiss cost of living and what Engineering Manager roles pay locally in Geneva and Zurich, I'm targeting something closer to CHF 190,000 base, with the rest of the package, equity and benefits, structured around that. I know that's toward the top of what you've posted for the EU band, and I think it's justified given where I'd be operating from."

## If they come in low
"I appreciate the offer. The number you've shared is below what I'd need to make this work, mainly because of where I'm based, Geneva is one of the more expensive markets in Europe and the local comp data reflects that. Is there flexibility to move the base closer to CHF 180,000, or is there room on the equity side to close that gap instead?"

Fallback move if base truly can't shift: ask for a shorter refresh cycle (12 months instead of 18) or a signing bonus that bridges the first year.

## Geographic discount pushback
If they anchor to a lower-cost EU country's rate: "I understand the band has to cover a lot of ground, from Portugal to Switzerland, but Geneva rent and taxes aren't close to the low end of that range. I'd rather we set comp based on where I actually live and work than the midpoint of the band."

## Equity notes
Post-Series D means the equity is less speculative than an early-stage grant but also less numerically explosive, so don't let equity carry the whole conversation, treat it as a real but secondary lever. Ask directly: current strike price, last 409A or equivalent valuation, standard 4-year vest with a 1-year cliff or something else, and whether there's a refresh policy tied to performance reviews rather than only at renewal. For a Swiss resident, clarify early how equity is taxed locally versus at grant or vest in the US, since that materially changes what the number is worth in hand.

## Competing offer as a counterweight (use only if real)
"I'm currently in later-stage conversations with another company for a similar scope of role, and the comp there is in the CHF 195,000–210,000 range. I'd genuinely rather join Ashby if we can get close to that, the team and the stage feel like a better fit, but I do need the numbers to be in the same neighborhood."

## Close
Whatever the final number, confirm in writing: base, bonus structure if any, equity grant size and vest schedule, start date, and remote work terms specific to being based in Switzerland rather than the US or a lower-cost EU country covered by the same band.
