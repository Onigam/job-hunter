# Negotiation Script — Parloa (Staff/Principal Software Engineer)

---

## Market Context

**Role level:** Staff / Principal Software Engineer (12-15+ years expected)

**Company profile:** Well-funded enterprise AI, backed by General Catalyst, EQT Ventures, and Altimeter Capital. Likely Series B or C. Revenue-generating with marquee clients (Allianz, Booking.com, SAP). Not pre-revenue — this matters for equity expectations.

**Location:** Berlin/Munich HQ, remote within Europe. Magino is in Geneva.

**Currency note:** Parloa is a German company so they will likely quote in EUR. Geneva cost of living is materially higher than Berlin or Munich. Push for CHF compensation or a Geneva cost-of-living adjustment if working fully remote from Switzerland.

---

## Market Rate Benchmarks

| Reference | Range |
|-----------|-------|
| Staff Engineer, Berlin/Munich (2025) | €130,000 – €160,000 base |
| Staff Engineer, remote (EU) with Geneva COL premium | CHF 175,000 – CHF 210,000 base |
| Principal Engineer, well-funded scale-up, remote | CHF 195,000 – CHF 230,000 base |
| Contractor/freelance equivalent (daily rate) | CHF 1,100 – CHF 1,400/day |

**Target anchor:** CHF 200,000 base (justifiable at the senior end of the Staff/Principal band with 17 years, founding-engineer track record, and Geneva market).

---

## Opening Anchor Script

When they ask about salary expectations, use this framing:

> "I'm looking at CHF 195,000 to 210,000 base for a Staff/Principal role. That's based on the seniority level you've described, my background, and Geneva market rates — the cost of living here is roughly 30-40% above Berlin. I'd want to understand the total package including equity before making a comparison, but that's my baseline."

**Key points:**
- State a range, not a single number. The floor of your range is what they'll anchor to.
- Mention Geneva cost of living explicitly — it's a legitimate and factual justification.
- Signal you're thinking about total comp, not just base. This opens the equity conversation.

---

## If They Come In Low (under CHF 160,000)

> "I appreciate you sharing that. It's a bit below where I'm targeting for this level. The role description is clearly Staff/Principal scope — 12-15+ years, cross-team architecture, technical strategy. Is there flexibility on the base, or is this the approved band? I ask because I want to find a number that works before we go further."

Then go quiet. Let them respond.

If they push back on the CHF framing:
> "I'm based in Geneva and would be working from here full-time. The cost of living difference between Geneva and Berlin is real and roughly documented — housing alone is 50-60% higher. I'd need the compensation to reflect that, otherwise I'd have to look at local Geneva opportunities as my baseline."

---

## If They Ask for a Single Number

Don't give one. Redirect:
> "I'd rather not anchor too early before I understand the full package — base, equity, and benefits together paint a different picture than base alone. What's the band for this role at Parloa?"

Getting their band first is almost always advantageous.

---

## Equity Negotiation

Parloa is VC-backed at growth stage. Equity is meaningful and worth negotiating explicitly.

**Questions to ask:**
- What is the current valuation (post-money on last round)?
- What percentage of fully-diluted shares does the package represent?
- What is the vesting schedule — standard is 4 years with a 1-year cliff?
- Is there acceleration on acquisition (single trigger or double trigger)?
- What is the strike price versus the 409A or last-round share price?

**Script if they offer options without detail:**
> "I'd want to understand the mechanics before I put a value on it. Can you share the strike price relative to the last round valuation, and the fully-diluted share count? That lets me model the upside in a realistic scenario rather than just treating it as a lottery ticket."

**If the equity package seems thin:**
> "Given the stage and the seniority of the role, I'd expect the equity to be meaningful as part of the total comp story. If the base is at the lower end of my range, I'd need the equity component to be more substantial to make the total package work."

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

If you have or receive another offer, use it directly:
> "I want to be transparent — I'm in late stages with another company at CHF [X] base. I'd prefer to work with Parloa given [specific reason], but I need the numbers to be in the same territory. Is there room to move?"

If no competing offer exists, don't fabricate one. Instead use:
> "I have several conversations in progress at the Staff level, and the range I'm seeing from the market is CHF 195,000 to 215,000 for this scope of role. I'd like to land at Parloa if we can get there."

---

## Benefits and Extras to Negotiate

Beyond base and equity:
- **Home office setup:** CHF 2,000-3,000 one-time, or monthly stipend of CHF 150-200
- **Learning budget:** CHF 3,000-5,000/year is standard at this level
- **Vacation:** 25 days minimum; 30 is reasonable to ask for at Staff level
- **Start date:** Negotiate 4-6 weeks minimum, or use it as a chip to trade for higher base

---

## Final Offer — Accept/Reject Threshold

**Walk away if:**
- Base below CHF 155,000 with no upward movement
- Equity is non-existent or purely token for a growth-stage company
- No Geneva cost-of-living acknowledgment at all

**Accept range:**
- CHF 175,000+ base with reasonable equity (0.05% or better at current valuation) is a fair outcome
- CHF 185,000+ base is a good outcome
- CHF 195,000+ base is the target
