# Negotiation Script: SoSafe, Staff Engineer (IT Automation & AI-native Transformation)

Context going in: the listing is posted out of London with an EU remote option, and no salary range is disclosed on the Ashby posting itself. SoSafe is a Cologne-based cybersecurity awareness scale-up, one Series B in January 2022 ($73M, led by Highland Europe and Acton Capital), no announced round since. That funding stage matters for two reasons: cash discipline is real (the posting itself asks for "financial efficiency," not just speed), and equity grants at this stage are smaller percentages than an early seed company, but on a base that's already de-risked past the pre-revenue stage. Other London-based roles at SoSafe give a rough band: Engineering Teamlead at £69k-£117k, Senior Frontend Engineer at £50k-£85k. A staff-level, org-wide automation mandate should sit meaningfully above the teamlead ceiling.

## Market context

- No public range for this specific role, so the London teamlead band (£69k-£117k) is the best available internal anchor, staff-level should clear the top of that band, not sit inside it.
- Geneva-based comp for a comparable Staff/Principal platform role runs roughly CHF 170,000-220,000 base. Useful as a personal sanity check on any EU-remote offer, even though the hire won't be Switzerland-based, since it sets a floor for what "staff-level, cross-org mandate" should mean in absolute terms.
- Post-Series-B scale-ups with no recent round typically offer equity in the 0.02%-0.08% range for a senior/staff IC hire, RSUs or options depending on jurisdiction, standard 4-year vest with a 1-year cliff. Anything below that range for a role explicitly scoped as "build the foundation for an AI-native future" is worth pushing back on.

## Opening position

"Thanks for sharing the details on scope. Since there's no published range for this role specifically, I'd want to start the conversation with what a staff-level, cross-org automation mandate is budgeted at, rather than working backward from a generic band. Given the ownership here spans architecture, tooling, and org-wide adoption, I'd expect that to be priced meaningfully above a team lead role, not adjacent to it."

## If they come in low

"I hear the constraint on financial efficiency, and it's actually part of why I think this role is worth pricing correctly: the org-wide adoption work only pays off if it's owned by someone senior enough to make the call stick without a rewrite in twelve months. I'd rather we land on a number that reflects that scope now than revisit it after the first *-as-code migration ships."

## Geographic and remote-discount pushback

"I want to understand how the EU remote band is set before we go further. I'm not asking for a London cost-of-living adjustment, Geneva isn't cheap either, but I'd want any adjustment to be based on an actual location-banding policy rather than a flat remote discount applied by default."

## Equity questions to ask before agreeing to anything

- What percentage or share count is on the table for this specific hire, not a generic new-hire range.
- Valuation used for the grant, and whether it's tied to the 2022 Series B price or a more recent internal mark.
- Standard 4-year vest with a 1-year cliff, or a different schedule given the seniority of the hire.
- RSUs versus options, and if options, strike price and the post-departure exercise window.
- Refresh policy after year one, since a lot of the value in this role is in what gets built in year two and three, not the onboarding period.

## Competing offer template (use only if real)

"I'm further along in a process for a comparable staff-level platform role, and the total comp there is tracking toward [$X] with [Y]% equity. I'd rather make this work, the automation and AI-native scope here is the more interesting problem, but the numbers would need to be in the same range."

## Closing line for any round

"I want this to land somewhere that works for both of us. Send me base and equity together in writing and I'll turn it around quickly."
