# Negotiation Script — VP Engineering Leader (Undisclosed via Jobgether)

---

## Market Anchors — Switzerland / Remote VP Engineering

### Switzerland VP Engineering / Head of Engineering (Geneva/Zurich market, 2025-2026)
- Total compensation benchmark: CHF 200,000 to CHF 280,000+
- Base salary typical range: CHF 170,000 to CHF 230,000
- Bonus: 10-20% of base for VP-level
- Equity: varies widely — startup warrants vs. RSUs at scale-up
- Note: Geneva cost of living is materially higher than Zurich on housing; use this to push toward the top of ranges

### Context factors that strengthen Magino's position
- Founding-engineer background with a proven acquisition outcome (Benevity/Alaya) — this is rare
- 17 years of experience across embedded, SaaS, platform engineering
- Active AI strategy practitioner, not a buzzword adopter
- Already operating at VP Engineering scope (without the title) at both Alaya and Benevity
- Geneva-based: no relocation cost, no timezone friction for European roles

---

## Opening Anchor

**Target number to state first**: CHF 220,000 base + bonus structure + equity

**How to say it**:

"Based on the scope — a 50-person org, P&L ownership, and AI strategy mandate — I'm looking at a base in the 220K CHF range, plus a variable component tied to eng KPIs, and equity that reflects this being a senior leadership role rather than a senior IC. Does that land in the right zone for you, or is there a structure I should understand first?"

Why 220K: it anchors high enough to leave room to land at 190-200K if needed, without anchoring so high the conversation ends. It also signals that Magino has done market research, not just picked a round number.

---

## If They Come In Low (under CHF 170K base)

**Script**:

"I appreciate the transparency. That's a bit below what I'd expect for the scope of this role — P&L ownership and leading 50 engineers puts this firmly in VP Engineering territory, not Head of Engineering. Based on what I've seen in the Swiss and remote market for this level, I'd need us to be closer to 200K base to make it work. Is there flexibility there, or is the budget genuinely fixed?"

**If they say the budget is fixed**: ask about equity, variable comp, and review timelines.

"If the base is fixed, then let's talk about the full picture — what does the equity look like, and is there a performance review at 6 or 12 months where base can be revisited?"

---

## Equity Negotiation (if startup or early-stage)

### Key questions to ask
- What is the total shares outstanding? (to calculate real dilution)
- What was the last valuation / last round?
- What is the vesting schedule? (standard is 4 years, 1-year cliff)
- Is there acceleration on acquisition? (double trigger is acceptable; single trigger is better)
- What preference stack sits above common shares?

### Anchor for equity (if pre-Series B)
"For a VP Engineering role at this stage, I'd expect something in the 0.3% to 0.8% range depending on where you are in your fundraising journey. Where does that land relative to your current option pool structure?"

### If they offer less
"I understand that option pools are finite. If we're below that range, can we talk about a milestone-based top-up — so if we hit the targets we'd set together in the first year, there's a refresh that brings total equity into that territory?"

## Geographic Discount Pushback

Some remote companies try to apply a location-based discount even for Switzerland-based candidates ("we pay to local market"). Pushback:

"I want to flag that I'm in Geneva, which is one of the highest cost-of-living locations in Europe. If you're benchmarking to 'local Swiss market', that actually supports the higher end of your range rather than a discount. And the work I'd be doing is identical regardless of my timezone."

---

## Competing Offer Template

If Magino receives another offer or wants to create urgency:

"I want to be transparent: I'm at the offer stage with another company in a similar role. I'd prefer to go with you because [specific reason: scope, mission, team], but there's a 10-day window before I need to respond to them. Is there a way to accelerate the process on your end so we're not both waiting?"

If asked for the number: "They're at CHF 195K base with equity. I'm not trying to auction myself — I'm trying to be honest about where the market is."

---

## Benefits to Negotiate if Base is Stuck

- Professional development budget (CHF 3,000 to 5,000 per year is reasonable at VP level)
- Home office setup stipend (CHF 2,000 to 3,000 one-time)
- Accelerated first performance review (6 months instead of 12) with explicit base increase tied to it
- Co-working stipend if working from Geneva (CHF 200-400/month)
- Additional PTO days (Switzerland standard is 25, push for 30 at this level)

---

## What Not to Do

- Don't give a number first if they haven't disclosed a range — ask: "Can you share the budgeted range for this role so I can tell you if we're in the same ballpark?"
- Don't accept on the spot. Even if the offer is good: "This is very close to what I was hoping for. Can I have 48 hours to review the full package details in writing?"
- Don't negotiate via email for the big number. Do it on a call where you can read the room.
