# Negotiation Script — Pharosyn / Founding AI Engineer

---

## Market Context

**Posted range:** £100k–£150k + equity (London)

**Reality check for this profile:**
The posted range is calibrated for a 0–4 year engineer. Magino brings 17 years including a founding role that led to a unicorn acquisition. The "founding engineer" framing and equity upside are the interesting parts here, not the base comp. Still, anchoring at the top of the posted range is the floor, not the ceiling.

**London market comparables for a senior founding AI engineer (2025–2026):**
- Founding engineer at EF/Seedcamp/A-round stage: £130k–£175k base + meaningful equity
- Senior/Staff AI engineer (non-founding): £120k–£160k
- The 0–4 year requirement in the posting is a recruiting artifact, not a hard ceiling for a strong candidate

**Geneva vs London:** If the role allows remote or hybrid from Geneva, the CHF equivalent of £150k is roughly CHF 165k–170k depending on exchange rates, which is reasonable for senior Geneva tech but not exceptional. Factor this in when framing relocation vs. remote scenarios.

## Opening Anchor

If they ask for a number first, give a range anchored above the posted range:

> "Given my background, including a founding role that went to acquisition, I'd be looking at something in the £150k–£175k range for base, depending on the equity structure. I'm flexible if the equity is genuinely compelling."

If they quote you the posted range (£100k–£150k) first:

> "I saw the posting range. Given what I bring, particularly the founding-to-acquisition experience and the LLM infrastructure work, I'd want to be at the top of that range at minimum. The equity conversation matters a lot here, so I'd rather understand the full picture before anchoring on base."

---

## Counter-Offer Scripts

**If they offer £120k:**
> "I appreciate the offer. Given what we've discussed about the founding engineer scope and what I'd be contributing from day one, I was expecting to land closer to £150k. Is there flexibility to get there, either on base or with an adjustment to the equity package?"

**If they offer £140k:**
> "That's closer. If we can get to £150k base with a meaningful equity stake (I'll want to understand the vesting terms and current cap table), I'm ready to move quickly."

**If they say the budget is fixed:**
> "Understood. If the base is capped, what does the equity package look like, specifically the percentage, vesting schedule, and cliff? A founding engineer stake at this stage should reflect the risk and the leverage I'm bringing, and that changes the math significantly."

---

## Equity Negotiation (EF-Backed Early Stage)

**What to expect:** EF-backed companies at 2–10 employees typically offer founding-level equity. For a founding engineer (not co-founder), the realistic range is 0.5%–2% on a 4-year vest with a 1-year cliff.

**Questions to ask:**
- "What's the current fully diluted cap table look like? What percentage would this grant represent post-Series A?"
- "Is the equity in options or RSUs, and what's the strike price relative to the last 409A valuation?"
- "Is there an acceleration clause in case of acquisition? Single-trigger or double-trigger?"
- "What anti-dilution provisions are in place for early employee grants?"

**Your anchor for equity:**
> "For a founding engineer role, I'd expect at least 1% on a standard 4-year vest with a 1-year cliff. If the company has already done a meaningful seed round, I'd want to understand what that 1% looks like on the diluted table, and whether there's pro-rata in the next round."

**If they push back on the equity ask:**
> "The equity is the main reason a founding engineer role makes sense for me at this stage. If it's below 0.5%, it starts to look like a senior IC role with an unusual title rather than a true founding stake. I'd want to calibrate expectations before we go further."

If they're open to remote:

> "Working remotely from Geneva, I'd be on your timezone with full overlap. That's a non-issue technically. Happy to come to London quarterly or for key milestones."

## Competing Offer Leverage (if applicable)

If you have another offer in hand:

> "I want to be transparent with you: I have another offer at [X range]. I'm more interested in what Pharosyn is building, but I need the numbers to be in the same territory to make the decision straightforward."

If you don't have a competing offer but have active conversations:

> "I have a few conversations at the Staff/Founding Engineer level that are progressing. Nothing signed yet, but the ranges I'm hearing are in the £150k+ territory. I'd want to move quickly with Pharosyn if we can align on comp."

## Summary: Walk-Away Framework

- **Floor on base:** £140k. Below this, the opportunity cost of a founding role vs. a Staff role at a later-stage company doesn't hold.
- **Floor on equity:** 0.75% fully diluted post-seed. Below 0.5%, reframe as a senior hire, not a founding hire, and price accordingly.
- **Remote:** Full remote from Geneva is the preference. London hybrid (2 days/week or monthly) is acceptable with a travel budget.
- **Ideal:** £150k base + 1%+ equity on standard 4-year/1-year vest. That's the number that makes this an easy yes.
