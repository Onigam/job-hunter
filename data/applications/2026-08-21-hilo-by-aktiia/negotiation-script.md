# Negotiation Script — Hilo by Aktiia, VP of Software Engineering

## Context that shapes this negotiation

This is a C-level scope role, reporting directly to the CEO, owning software, data, and infrastructure across mobile, e-commerce, and CRM, and leading a 15-person engineering team based in Belgrade plus external partners. The company has raised over $96M, which puts it past seed and into a stage where a real VP comp band should already exist internally, even if the posting doesn't list one. No salary range was published, so the opening move is finding out what's actually budgeted before anchoring against a market number that may not match their internal band at all.

Location matters less here than it did in prior negotiations. I'm Geneva-based and the role is CET/GMT with travel to Neuchâtel and Belgrade once or twice a month, not a relocation ask. That means Swiss and broader Western European VP Engineering comp is the right anchor, not a discount for remote work.

## Market anchor

For a VP of Engineering at a venture-backed, Swiss-headquartered medtech with meaningful scale (multi-market, regulated device, $96M raised), Swiss and Western European market data points to a base in the range of CHF 190,000-240,000, plus a bonus target of 15-25%, plus a meaningful equity grant. Given the regulated medical device layer and the C-level reporting line, I'd anchor toward the middle to upper part of that band rather than the floor. Belgrade-based engineering leadership roles at this seniority pay less, but the role is explicitly CET/GMT-based with HQ ties to Neuchâtel, so the Swiss anchor is the right one to open with.

## Opening the comp conversation

"Before we get into specifics, can you walk me through the budgeted range for this role? I want to make sure we're both working from the same number before I put an anchor out there."

If they ask me to anchor first:

"For a VP-level role reporting to the CEO with this scope, regulated device plus e-commerce plus CRM, across a fifteen-person team, I'd expect a base in the CHF 200,000-230,000 range, a bonus target around 20%, and equity that reflects a company at your stage. Where does that land relative to what you've budgeted?"

## If they come in low (below CHF 180,000 base)

"I hear the number. Given the scope, owning software, data, and infrastructure across four product surfaces with a fifteen-person team and external partners, that reads closer to a director-level band than a C-level one reporting to the CEO. What's driving the gap, is it the equity making up the difference, or is there room to move on base?"

## Equity negotiation notes

- Ask for the current fully diluted share count and the valuation this grant is priced against; a headline equity number means little without that context.
- At $96M raised, this is very likely a Series B or later company. Push for a grant sized appropriately for a C-level hire, not an early engineer, and get the percentage or share count in writing before verbally agreeing to anything.
- Standard four-year vest with a one-year cliff is fine. Push back on anything longer.
- Ask directly about acceleration on acquisition or change of control. Given the Alaya-to-Benevity outcome, this is a real, informed question, not a hypothetical one: "What happens to my vesting if Hilo gets acquired in year two or three?"
- Confirm the post-termination exercise window. Anything under 90 days is worth negotiating; some later-stage companies now offer extended windows on request.

## Bonus and success-metric alignment

Since the posting names concrete year-one targets, uptime above 99.9%, a unified data backbone, and AI-driven cost reduction, tie any bonus structure to those same metrics rather than vague "leadership" criteria.

"If bonus is tied to performance, I'd want it explicitly linked to the metrics you've already defined, uptime, the data backbone milestone, and measurable AI or automation impact, so there's no ambiguity at review time about what counts as hitting the number."

## Geographic and travel considerations

"I want to confirm the travel cadence in writing, roughly one to two trips a month to Neuchâtel and Belgrade, and understand whether travel costs and time are handled separately from the base comp discussion. I'm not asking for a location discount since this is a CET-based role, but I do want the travel expectations clear before we finalize anything."

## Competing offer leverage template

Use only if genuinely true at the time:

"I want to be straightforward that I'm also further along with another Staff or VP-level conversation in the Swiss market. The reason Hilo is the more interesting problem to me is the regulated device layer combined with consumer-facing infrastructure, that's a harder and rarer combination than most of what I'm seeing elsewhere. I'd rather make this work than default to the other conversation on comp alone. Is there room to close the gap?"

## Closing line

"I want this role to work. The problem is genuinely interesting and the CET timezone makes the logistics easy. I just need the comp to reflect a C-level hire owning four product surfaces and a fifteen-person org, not an early operating role, and I'm confident we can get there."
