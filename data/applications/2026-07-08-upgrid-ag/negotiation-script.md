# Negotiation Script: UpGrid AG, CTO / Lead Engineer / Co-Founder

---

## Market Context

**Role type:** Late-joining co-founder CTO at a Tenity-backed, seed-stage energy fintech startup based in Baar (Zug canton). The posting itself is explicit that this "isn't a high-paying corporate job" and is open to part-time or flexible arrangements, which signals a cash-constrained early stage rather than a funded exec search.

**Switzerland CTO / co-founder market rates (2026):**
- Series A+ startup CTO: CHF 160k-230k base
- Seed-stage startup CTO (hired, not founding): CHF 100k-150k + equity
- Late-joining co-founder CTO, cash-constrained: CHF 60k-110k base + 8-20% equity, depending on how "founding" the role really is
- Part-time / advisory-track CTO (as the posting also floats): CHF 0-40k retainer + smaller equity, usually 2-6%

**UpGrid context:** 3,600+ customers already on the platform, funded through Tenity plus BlueLion, Climate Founders and other early investors, three co-founders already in place (Rafa, Hagen, Nick). This reads as a team that has traction and some capital but is still building out the technical leadership layer, likely working with external dev teams rather than a large in-house one. Expect below-market cash, real equity, and some ambiguity about whether this is "join as CTO employee" or "join as fourth co-founder."

**Geneva cost of living anchor:** Magino is based in Geneva, more expensive than Zug on rent and daily costs. Use this to set a cash floor even in a co-founder-equity conversation: CHF 90k-100k minimum base, not CHF 60-70k, regardless of how the equity math looks on paper.

---

## Opening Position

**Target package:** CHF 110k-140k base, 10-15% equity if this is genuinely a fourth co-founder slot (versus 3-8% if it's closer to "first CTO hire" reporting to the existing founders).

**Opening line, when asked for expectations:**

> "Before I put a number on it, I'd like to understand what 'co-founder' means here concretely: cap table position, decision rights, and whether I'd be building the eng org from scratch or slotting under an existing structure. That changes the math a lot. As a starting point though, I'm thinking somewhere around CHF 110-140k base with equity in the low double digits if this is a true founding role, and I'm open to shifting the cash-equity split depending on where you are on runway."

---

## If They Come In Low on Cash

**Scenario:** They offer CHF 40-60k, or float an equity-only / heavily part-time arrangement first.

**Response:**

> "I get that cash is tight at this stage, and I don't need this to be a fully-loaded package on day one. What I'd want instead is a clear ramp: something like CHF 60k now, stepping up to CHF 100-110k once you close the next round or hit a revenue milestone we agree on in writing. If the cash stays low longer term, the equity needs to reflect that, closer to 15-18% rather than 8-10%."

**Absolute floor:** CHF 60k base with 15%+ equity and a written step-up trigger, or CHF 90k base with 8-10% equity and no ramp needed. Below CHF 60k with no equity upside isn't worth the Geneva cost delta, no matter how good the mission is.

---

## Equity Negotiation

**Target equity:** 10-15% if joining as a real fourth co-founder pre-Series A; 4-8% if this is functionally a senior hire with a co-founder title attached for optics.

**Key terms to push for:**

1. **Vesting:** 4-year vest, 1-year cliff is standard. If they propose something shorter or a cliff-free structure, ask why, since it can signal instability.
2. **Founder vs. option pool:** Clarify whether the equity is issued as founder shares or comes out of the employee option pool. Founder shares carry different tax and governance treatment in Switzerland and matter for how "co-founder" is actually defined here.
3. **Pre- or post-pool percentage:** Ask whether the quoted percentage is calculated before or after any option pool top-up planned for future hires. A 12% offer can shrink meaningfully once a 15-20% pool gets carved out later.
4. **Anti-dilution:** Weighted-average protection is reasonable to ask for; full-ratchet is not something investors will accept, so don't push for it.
5. **Acceleration:** Push for double-trigger acceleration on acquisition (unvested shares vest if the company is acquired and you're let go within 12 months).

**Equity script:**

> "When we say 10%, I want to make sure we're talking about the same thing: is that founder equity or does it come from the option pool, and is it calculated before or after any pool expansion for future hires? I don't need the full cap table, just a sense of where things stand and whether there's already been a priced round."

---

## Geographic Discount Pushback

**If they anchor to Zug or Baar salary norms:**

> "I know Zug and Baar run lower than Geneva on comp expectations, but my cost base is Geneva, and that's where I'd be commuting or working remote from. I'd ask that we anchor the cash number closer to Geneva rates even if the equity conversation stays local to the Zug ecosystem."

---

## Part-Time / Flexible Arrangement Clause

Since the posting explicitly floats part-time and flexible work as an option, be ready to negotiate that dimension too rather than assuming full-time is the only path:

> "If it makes sense to start part-time while I wrap up other commitments, I'm open to that, but I'd want the equity and title to reflect a clear path to full-time within a defined window, say three to six months, rather than an open-ended part-time arrangement."

---

## Competing Offer Template

*Use only if there's a genuinely active alternative process, even an early one.*

> "I want to be straightforward: I'm further along in another process with a stronger cash number. What's pulling me toward UpGrid is the mission and the stage, so if we can land the equity and the ramp structure where I described, I'd rather move forward here than keep the other process open."

---

## Questions to Ask Before Committing

1. What's the current runway, and when is the next funding round targeted?
2. Is the CTO role a fourth co-founder position on the cap table, or a senior hire with a founder-style title?
3. What does the existing dev setup look like: is UpGrid working with external contractors or an in-house team today, and would I be building the in-house team from scratch?
4. What's on the 12-month roadmap for smart meter, inverter, and EV charging integrations specifically?
5. What happened with technical leadership before this search started?
6. Is there a shareholders' agreement in place among the three existing co-founders, and how would a fourth co-founder slot into that structure?

---

## Red Flags to Watch For

- Equity framed as "profit share" rather than actual shares in UpGrid AG
- No shareholders' agreement or unclear governance among the existing three co-founders
- Reluctance to share even high-level cap table or runway information
- Pressure to start before anything is in writing
- A co-founder title offered alongside terms that are really just a standard employee option grant
