# Negotiation Script: SMG Swiss Marketplace Group, Staff Frontend Engineer

## Context
- SMG is a joint venture (Ringier, Mobiliar, TX Group, General Atlantic) running ImmoScout24, Homgate, Flatfox, and other marketplace brands across real estate, automotive, general classifieds, and finance. It's a mature, profitable Swiss company, not a startup burning runway, which shapes the equity conversation more than the base conversation.
- The role is posted as Zurich, hybrid, 80-100%. I'm based in Geneva. That's the first thing to resolve, not the number, since regular travel to Zurich changes the real cost of taking the role even before comp is on the table.
- No public salary band was listed on jobs.ch or the mirrored posting, so the opening anchor has to come from market data, not their number.
- Leverage: 17+ years of experience, an actual acquisition exit (Alaya to Benevity), hands-on architecture ownership at platform scale, and a track record of driving cross-team adoption of shared tooling without formal authority, which maps closely onto what this posting is actually asking for.
- Counter-leverage they may raise: my recent hands-on frontend work has been in React within a larger org, not Vue.js at SMG's scale, and the posting leans on 8+ years specifically with Vue. Worth having a direct answer ready: strong transferable React/TypeScript depth, prior multi-framework experience going back to Sagemcom and Alaya, and design-system thinking that isn't framework-specific.

## Switzerland market anchor
Staff-level frontend engineers in Zurich typically land in the CHF 150,000-190,000 base range, with well-funded marketplace and product companies at the top of that band or slightly above for engineers carrying cross-team technical direction rather than pure IC scope. Geneva-based comparable roles run close to the same range, sometimes a touch higher given local cost of living, so there's no real justification for a discount tied to being Geneva-based rather than Zurich-based, especially with hybrid travel already a cost I'm absorbing. Total comp usually includes a bonus in the 8-15% range at this level; equity or profit-share is less standard at an established joint venture than at a venture-backed startup, so that part of the conversation should focus on bonus structure rather than options.

## Opening move: settle the location question first
"Before we talk numbers, I want to flag that I'm in Geneva, not Zurich. I can make hybrid work, but I'd want to understand what that looks like in practice, how many days a week, and whether relocation support or a travel allowance is part of the package, since that's a real cost either way."

## Opening comp anchor
"Given the scope here, setting technical direction across multiple teams plus roughly half the time still hands-on, I'd anchor around CHF 175,000-185,000 base, with a bonus structure on top rather than equity, since that seems to be how compensation is typically structured at SMG's stage. I'd also want clarity on how the staff-level band here compares to senior, since the scope described sounds meaningfully broader."

## If they come in below CHF 160,000 base
"That number reads closer to a senior engineer band than what's described in the posting, cross-team architecture ownership, shared libraries adopted org-wide, mentoring other engineers. I'd rather understand how the band is structured for this level before assuming the number is fixed. If there's room to revisit once the scope is confirmed in the loop, I'd like to do that before we finalize anything."

## Framework depth question, if raised as a gap
"Most of my recent hands-on work has been React and TypeScript at scale, but the architecture problems, shared component libraries, design systems, performance and SSR patterns, don't change much moving to Vue. I've worked across frameworks before, GWT at Sagemcom, Node and React at Alaya, and I'd rather be evaluated on whether I can pick up Vue's specifics quickly than on the label."

## Geographic and travel pushback
"Geneva to Zurich is a real commute, not a rounding error, so if hybrid is 3+ days a week I'd want either a travel allowance or a lighter in-office cadence built into the offer rather than treated as a personal cost I'm expected to absorb quietly."

## Bonus and equity questions
- Ask what the bonus target is at staff level and whether it's tied to individual, team, or company-wide performance.
- Ask whether SMG offers any long-term incentive plan given the joint-venture ownership structure, since traditional startup equity isn't likely to apply here.
- Ask how frequently comp is reviewed and whether there's a defined path from staff to principal, since that affects how much weight to put on the base number today versus the trajectory.

## Competing offer leverage template
Use only if genuinely in play: "I want to be upfront that I have another process at a comparable stage, and the compensation there is close to what I'm proposing here. The Real Estate frontend problem at SMG is the more interesting one to me, but I do need to close this out within a couple of weeks."

## Close
"I'd like to get the hybrid arrangement and the base number settled this week so I can give you a real yes instead of a maybe. If the Zurich requirement is firm and daily, I'd rather know that now too."
