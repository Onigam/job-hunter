# Negotiation Script — Better Stack / Founding Product Engineer

---

## Role Compensation Context

Better Stack lists two tracks:
- **Standard track:** $150K–$400K USD/year base
- **Hardcore mode:** $300K–$800K USD/year (self-described as "1.5x hours for 2x comp")
- Plus equity (founding engineer level — meaningful dilution expected)
- Remote (Europe-friendly) or Prague-based

At a founding product engineer level with Magino's profile (co-founded and grew a platform to acquisition, 17 years experience, staff-level engineering), the realistic target is the **upper half of the standard track** or a conversation about hardcore mode.

---

| Role Level | Geneva Market (CHF/year gross) | USD Equivalent (approx.) |
|---|---|---|
| Senior Engineer (5-8y) | CHF 130K–160K | $145K–$180K |

Better Stack pays in USD and operates globally, so the Geneva cost-of-living argument is less direct than for a local Swiss company. The stronger anchor is Magino's market value as a founding engineer with a successful exit, not geography.

**Target range:** $280K–$350K USD base, plus equity negotiation.

---

## Opening Anchor

If asked for a number first:

> "Given my background as a founding engineer who's taken a platform from zero to acquisition, I'm looking at the $300K–$350K range as a base. I'm also genuinely interested in how equity is structured for a role at this level, since the founding engineer framing implies we're both making a long-term bet."

If they give a number first (e.g., $200K):

> "I appreciate the transparency on the number. I have to be honest with you: given the scope of what you're describing, running a product line with full ownership from product to distribution, I was thinking in the $280K–$320K range. The gap isn't insurmountable, but I'd want to make sure we're accounting for what 'founding engineer' actually means in terms of equity. Can you walk me through how the cap table looks for a role like this?"

---

## Counter-Offer Script (if they come in below $250K)

> "I want to make this work, and I think the fit is real. But I need to be transparent: I'm fielding other conversations at the $270K–$300K range for similar-level roles. If the base is constrained, I'm open to structuring this differently. Could we look at a higher equity grant, an accelerated vesting trigger on a funding event, or a review clause tied to shipping the first product milestone? I'd rather have the conversation now than have it resurface in six months."

---

## Equity Framework (Founding Engineer)

At this stage (early-stage, $28M raised, 300K users), a founding product engineer should expect:

- **Realistic range:** 0.5% to 1.5% in options, depending on dilution and strike price
- **Minimum acceptable:** 0.3% with strong vesting terms
- **Ask for:** 0.75%–1.0% with a 1-year cliff, 4-year vest, and double-trigger acceleration on acquisition

Questions to ask:
- "What's the current fully diluted share count?"
- "What's the most recent 409A valuation, and how long ago was it set?"
- "Does the company have a right of first refusal on secondary sales?"
- "What's the post-termination exercise window? 90 days is a common problem at startups."

If the post-termination window is 90 days (standard but unfavorable):

> "I want to flag that a 90-day exercise window is a real financial risk for someone at this level, especially if the next round is 12–18 months away. Is there any flexibility to extend that to 5 years, or at least for founding engineer-level grants? Some companies are moving this direction and it meaningfully changes how valuable the equity actually is."

---

## Hardcore Mode Conversation

Better Stack's "hardcore mode" is unusual ($300K–$800K for 1.5x hours). Worth clarifying what this means in practice before agreeing or declining:

> "I saw the hardcore mode option in the listing. Can you help me understand what that looks like day-to-day? I'm not opposed to high intensity, especially in a founding role, but I want to make sure we're aligned on what 1.5x hours actually means and how sustainable it is over a 2–3 year horizon. I've been at startups long enough to know the difference between intense-and-purposeful and intense-and-chaotic."

If hardcore mode is genuinely structured and compensated well, it may be worth considering at the $400K–$600K range with the right equity.

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

If you have another offer (or want to use one hypothetically):

> "I'm in final conversations with one other company for a similar founding engineer role, and they're at [$X]. I prefer the Better Stack opportunity for reasons I can explain, but I need to make sure the compensation is close enough that I'm not leaving a significant gap. I'm not trying to start a bidding war, I'm trying to make the decision honestly."

---

## Geographic Discount Pushback

If Better Stack tries to apply a geographic discount because Magino is in Geneva:

> "I understand some companies apply location-based adjustments, but I'd push back on that framing here. The role is remote, the deliverables are global, and my experience building and scaling a platform to enterprise clients in Switzerland, France, and eventually North America is part of what you're hiring. Geneva being expensive is a data point, but it's not the argument for the comp level: my track record is."

---

## Red Lines

- Base below $220K USD: walk away unless equity is extraordinary (1%+ with strong terms)
- 90-day exercise window with no flexibility: flag it explicitly and negotiate or walk
- No equity at a "founding engineer" title: this is a mismatch, ask for clarification
- Vesting cliff longer than 12 months: push back
