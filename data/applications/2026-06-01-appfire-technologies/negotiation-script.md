# Negotiation Script — Appfire Technologies, Principal Software Engineer (Remote from Switzerland)

---

## Market Context

**Appfire profile:** PE-backed (Vista Equity Partners, 2024), 100+ Atlassian marketplace apps, ~600-800 employees globally, bootstrapped-to-acquisition origin. Not a startup, but not a FAANG. Mid-market SaaS with a consolidation strategy.

**Equity situation:** Vista-backed means equity is not pre-IPO stock options. Expect RSUs or an employee equity plan tied to Vista's exit timeline (typically 3-7 years to secondary sale or IPO). The upside is real but less binary than a Series B startup. Ask directly: "What does the equity component look like, and what's the vesting schedule?"

**Switzerland-remote dynamic:** Appfire posts specifically for Switzerland-based remote candidates, which suggests they have either a local entity or an EOR (Employer of Record) arrangement. This matters for your employment contract, pension contributions (BVG/LPP), and health insurance. Confirm before signing.

---

## Swiss Market Benchmarks (2026)

Principal / Staff Software Engineer in Switzerland:
- Geneva / Zurich major tech company: CHF 190,000 - 240,000 base
- Remote from Switzerland for US company: CHF 160,000 - 200,000 (they often apply a discount vs. on-site US rates)
- Total comp target (base + equity + benefits): CHF 190,000 - 220,000 equivalent

Note: Switzerland has mandatory pension contributions (BVG second pillar), no employer-provided health insurance (individual KVG/LaMal), and a minimum of 4 weeks vacation by law. These affect how to value the offer relative to US or UK packages.

---

## Opening Anchor

**Never disclose first.** If asked for your expectation early, deflect:

> "I'd rather hear the range you have budgeted for this level. Compensation at this level varies a lot depending on total package structure, equity, and how you handle Swiss statutory benefits."

If they push harder:

> "Factoring in base, equity, and the Geneva cost of living, I'm thinking in the CHF 200,000 - 215,000 range for base. That's roughly in line with what Principal-level roles pay in the Swiss market right now."

Opening anchor: **CHF 210,000 base** (or USD equivalent at current rates, approximately $240k-250k).

---

## Counter-Offer Scripts

**If the first offer comes in at CHF 160,000 - 175,000:**

> "I appreciate the offer and I'm genuinely interested in the role. The CHF 160k range is a bit below where I'd need to be. My current situation and the market benchmarks I've looked at point toward CHF 190,000 - 200,000 for this level. Is there room to move on the base, or alternatively is there flexibility on the equity side?"

**If they say the band is fixed at CHF 170,000:**

> "I understand there are band constraints. Let me think about what would make that work. A sign-on to bridge the first year, a six-month salary review milestone, or additional equity could all get us to something that makes sense. What flexibility exists there?"

**Floor:** CHF 175,000 base is the minimum worth signing for a Principal-level role in Geneva, and only if equity and total comp make up the gap.

---

## Geographic Discount Pushback

US companies sometimes try to pay Swiss employees less than equivalent US rates, citing "local market" rates. Push back on this directly if it comes up:

> "I'd rather not anchor this to a geographic discount. The work is the same work, and Geneva is one of the more expensive cities in the world to live in. I'd expect to be paid for the Principal Engineer scope, not a Switzerland-adjusted version of it."

If they cite their internal bands:

> "I understand you have global bands, and I'm not asking you to break them. I'm asking whether the band for this role reflects what Principal Engineers in this market actually earn, because there's a real gap between what I'm seeing and what you've described."

---

## Equity Negotiation

**Key questions to ask:**

1. "What is the structure of the equity component? RSUs, options, profit-sharing?"
2. "What's the current valuation or last secondary price?"
3. "What's the vesting schedule? Is there a cliff?"
4. "Vista has been the majority owner since 2024. What's the expected exit timeline, and how does that affect vesting?"
5. "If the company is acquired before my shares vest fully, is there acceleration?"

**What to push for:**
- 4-year vest with 1-year cliff is standard; push for double-trigger acceleration on acquisition
- If they offer RSUs, ask about the grant cadence (annual refresh grants are standard at good companies)
- If the equity feels thin, name it: "The base and equity together need to get me to CHF 210k-220k equivalent. Right now I'm seeing a gap. Can you increase the equity grant to compensate?"

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

Even if you don't have a competing offer in hand, you can reference the market:

> "I'm actively talking to a few other companies at this level. Nothing signed, but I'm not starting from zero. I'd rather close with Appfire because the role is genuinely interesting, but the numbers need to make sense."

If you do have a competing offer:

> "I have an offer at [X] from [company type, e.g. a Series B SaaS company]. The total comp is higher, but I'm not going to pretend the work at Appfire isn't more interesting. Can you get close to that number?"

---

## Benefits to Confirm

Before signing, verify:

- **Employment type:** Employee via Swiss entity or EOR? Who handles payroll taxes and BVG contributions?
- **Health insurance:** Not covered by employer in Switzerland. Confirm there's no expectation that it is.
- **Pension (BVG second pillar):** Mandatory. Confirm employer contribution rate (standard is 50/50 split, some employers contribute more).
- **Vacation:** Minimum 25 days (5 weeks) for anyone 20+ in Switzerland. Some companies offer 30.
- **Equipment budget:** Remote setup, especially home office, is worth CHF 2,000 - 4,000 first year.
- **Annual review cycle:** When does compensation get reviewed? Confirm it's in writing.

---

## Closing

Don't leave the negotiation open-ended. When you're close:

> "I think we can get there. If you can confirm [X] on base and [Y] on equity, I'm ready to move forward this week."

If they need time:

> "Take the time you need. I'd want to wrap this up by [date] if possible, given the other conversations I'm having."
