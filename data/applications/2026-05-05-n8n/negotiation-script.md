# Negotiation Script — Engineering Manager, Core Platform at n8n

## Market Context

**Role level:** Engineering Manager, first-line (leads one Core Platform team directly; not manager-of-managers)

**n8n context:** European scale-up, remote-first, Berlin HQ. Raised $60M Series B in 2023; likely approaching Series C territory now and targeting $1B ARR. EM-level equity at this stage is meaningful but more diluted than early-stage; real upside if they hit targets, not lottery-ticket territory.

**Switzerland market rates (2026):**
- Engineering Manager (first-line), Geneva local: CHF 170,000-210,000 base
- Remote from Geneva for a non-Swiss company: EUR 130,000-160,000 base (Berlin rates anchor them down; push back on this)

**n8n public data:** They advertise "competitive compensation with equity" but no published band. Berlin first-line EM market is EUR 110,000-145,000 base. That's the floor to push off, not the target.

---

## Opening Anchor

When they ask for expectations, give a range with a floor above their likely budget:

> "For an Engineering Manager scope on a platform team, I'm thinking in the range of CHF 175,000-200,000 base, or EUR 145,000-170,000 if we're working in euros. That reflects the Swiss market for this level and the platform complexity involved. I'd also want to understand the equity structure before we anchor too hard on base, because at n8n's stage the two need to be evaluated together."

This anchors high without sounding disconnected, and signals you're sophisticated enough to think in total comp.

---

## If They Come In Low (EUR 100,000-125,000 range)

Stay calm, don't accept or reject on the spot:

> "I appreciate you sharing that. It's below what I was expecting given the scope and honestly below where I'd need to land given Geneva's cost of living. I want to make this work, so let me ask: is there flexibility on base, or is equity the lever you'd use to bridge the gap? And can you walk me through the equity package so I can evaluate the full picture?"

Then:
- Get the equity details: number of options, strike price, current 409A or preferred price, cliff, vesting schedule
- Ask about last preferred round valuation to estimate dilution and upside
- Ask about refresh grants after year 2-3

**Counter-offer if they can't move base:**

> "If base is fixed at that level, I'd need to see meaningfully more equity to make the total picture work. What would it take to get to 0.08-0.12% on a fully diluted basis with a standard 4-year vest and 1-year cliff? At your current trajectory that's the kind of upside that would offset the base delta."

---

## Geographic / Cost of Living Pushback

n8n may apply a "Geneva premium doesn't apply because you're remote" argument. Push back:

> "Remote doesn't change my cost of living. Geneva is one of the most expensive cities in Europe, which is why Swiss-market salaries are calibrated the way they are. I'm not asking for a Geneva local premium; I'm asking for a rate that reflects the EM scope and the market reality for someone with this background. The geography is a wash for you operationally since I'm already in your timezone and working independently."

---

## Equity Negotiation

Key questions to ask regardless of outcome:

1. How many options, and what's the strike price?
2. What was the most recent preferred share price (or 409A valuation)?
3. What's the vesting schedule, cliff, and acceleration on change of control?
4. Has there been any secondary market activity or planned liquidity before a full exit?
5. What's the dilution expectation over the next funding round?

**Target for EM level at n8n's stage:** 0.05-0.15% fully diluted, 4-year vest, 1-year cliff, double-trigger acceleration on acquisition.

If they offer below 0.04%, counter with:

> "For an Engineering Manager on a platform team at this stage, the equity band I'd typically expect is 0.05-0.10%. Happy to be flexible on structure, but I want to make sure the total comp tells a coherent story for the seniority involved."

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

If you have another offer or want to reference market alternatives:

> "I'm in late stages with one other company, also in the developer tools space, at a comparable level. Their comp structure is in the EUR 145,000-155,000 range on base. I'd prefer n8n for reasons I've been clear about, but I do need the numbers to be reasonably close. Is there room to move?"

Without a hard competing offer, reference market data:

> "The Swiss and senior European market data I'm looking at puts this scope in the EUR 145,000+ range. I'd rather not drag this into a negotiation game, so if you can get within 10-15% of that I'm confident we can close quickly."

---

## Benefits to Confirm

Per the job posting:
- 30 vacation days (Europe) + public holidays — standard, don't negotiate this
- EUR 1,000 annual learning budget — ask if this can be increased for an EM (EUR 2,000-3,000 is reasonable given coaching and leadership development costs)
- Unlimited AI tool access — confirm this covers API-level access, not just hosted tools
- Health/wellness benefits — clarify coverage for a Geneva-based remote employee

**Key ask on benefits:**

> "One thing I'd want to clarify: for a Swiss-based employee, does n8n contribute to health insurance, or is that fully on my side? In Switzerland that's CHF 500-800 per month for solid coverage, so it's a meaningful line item in the total comp picture."

---

## Timing and Closing

When you're ready to accept or need to push for time:

> "I'm ready to move forward pending one final look at the equity breakdown. If you can get me the cap table numbers this week, I'd expect to be able to confirm by [date]."

Don't accept verbally without seeing the written offer. Don't let them rush you past the equity review.
