Negotiation Script: Hands-on CTO, AI-First Engineering, Jobgether client (SaaS scaleup)

Context to keep in mind

This posting comes through Jobgether, so the actual company is anonymized until later in the process, a profitable, fast-growing SaaS business running mobile and web products, based in the Netherlands, hiring fully remote. The listing itself carries a range of $10,000 to $15,000 a month, so roughly $120K to $180K a year before equity. That's a modest band for a role titled "CTO" with full ownership of engineering, which usually means one of two things: either the company is smaller than the title suggests, or the equity component is meant to carry a lot of the real value. Either way, that gap between title and cash comp is the first thing worth understanding, not negotiating around blind.

Switzerland market anchors (keep in your pocket, don't lead with them)

A CTO or Head of Engineering role in Geneva or the wider Swiss market for someone with founding and platform-scale experience typically anchors in the CHF 180K to 230K range for cash alone, often higher with a real leadership scope attached. The posted band sits well under that, so if this becomes a serious conversation, the case has to be made on equity, autonomy, and remote flexibility rather than on cash parity with a Swiss in-house role. Naming the Swiss number too early just invites a mismatch; better to let the scope conversation happen first.

Opening move: clarify scope and structure before anchoring on a number

"Before we get into comp, I want to understand the actual scope, is this a solo engineering leader role, or is there a team already in place? And is the $10K to $15K range fixed, or does it flex with equity and level of ownership?"

Opening anchor, once scope is clear

If the role really is full ownership with meaningful equity: "Given the scope of owning engineering end to end across a mobile and web product, I'd want to be at the top of your range on cash, call it $14K to $15K a month, with an equity grant that reflects a CTO-level stake rather than an early-employee one."

If cash has to stay near the bottom of the band

"If the cash number has to sit closer to $10K to $12K, I'd want that made up on the equity side, a defined percentage rather than a share count with no context, plus a comp review at six months tied to concrete milestones, not a vague promise to revisit it."

Equity questions

Ask for the actual percentage or share count, not just "equity included." Ask about the current valuation or last round, if any, so the number means something. Ask about vesting, a one-year cliff with four-year vest is standard, and whether there's any acceleration if the company gets acquired, which matters given how the Alaya story actually ended. Ask whether this is a straight employee grant or something closer to a co-founder stake, since a "hands-on CTO" hire this early sometimes comes with founder-adjacent terms if the company is small enough.

Geographic and remote leverage

The role reads as fully remote, so Geneva shouldn't be a blocker, but it's worth confirming directly: "I want to flag upfront that I'm Geneva-based and not relocating. If the role assumes significant in-person time in the Netherlands, better to know that now than after we're deep into this."

Competing offer template

Use only if a real competing process exists. Never invent a number or company name.

"I'm also in later-stage conversations for a comparable technical leadership role. This one is genuinely more interesting given the scope and the AI-first mandate, so if there's room on cash or equity to close the gap, I'd rather make this work than default to the other option."
