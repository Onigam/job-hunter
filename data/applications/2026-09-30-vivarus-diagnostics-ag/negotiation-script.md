# Negotiation Script: Vivarus Diagnostics AG, Co-Founder / CTO

## Context that shapes everything

This isn't a salary negotiation in the normal sense. Vivarus is pre-seed and pre-revenue, and says plainly that it can't pay salaries before the seed round closes. The posting offers two structures:

1. **Contract now, employment starts after the seed round.** Low commitment, but you do pre-seed work (architecture, roadmap, investor diligence) on the side, presumably unpaid.
2. **Full-time now, salary deferred until the seed closes.** Higher commitment and higher risk, but it's the stronger founder signal and puts you in a stronger position on equity.

Other facts to keep in mind:

- The company already exists (formerly RareSight AG, renamed Vivarus Diagnostics AG in August 2026). The founders are in place, so you're joining as a **late co-founder**, not an original one. That affects the equity range.
- The job is listed in Zürich. The registered address is in Bettwil (Aargau). You live in Geneva.
- The product is heading for MDR Class IIa. The path to revenue is long, with clinical validation, a notified body and hospital sales cycles. Expect it to take years, and price your equity and deferred pay with that in mind.

So the real negotiation is about **equity, vesting, how deferred pay works, a post-seed salary floor, and what happens if the seed never closes.**

## Market reference points (estimates, verify before the call)

These are working ranges from general Swiss startup practice and public salary data. They're not quotes. Check them with a Swiss startup lawyer or a founder network (Venturelab, Swiss Startup Association, ETH/UZH founder circles) before any number comes up.

| Reference | Range | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| CTO salary after seed, Swiss seed-stage medtech | CHF 140k to 180k base | Seed investors usually cap founder pay below market |
| CTO salary after Series A, Zürich | CHF 180k to 230k base | Where the salary should head once there's real funding |
| Staff+ engineer, Geneva/Zürich scale-up | CHF 190k to 240k base | Your opportunity cost, what you'd earn elsewhere |
| Late co-founder CTO equity, pre-seed, existing founding team | ~8% to 20% | Wide range; depends on how much is already built and how many founders there are |
| Original co-founder CTO equity | ~20% to 35% | Only relevant if they treat you as an equal founder |

**Your opportunity cost is the real anchor.** Each year of deferred or below-market salary is roughly CHF 60k to 200k you're putting into the company. Say that out loud; it's not aggressive, it's the maths.

## Your numbers (decide before the first call)

- **Equity target:** ~15% of fully diluted shares, pre-seed, vesting.
- **Equity floor:** decide this yourself. Somewhere around 10% is a reasonable line for a true co-founder seat that owns all technology, but it's your call.
- **Post-seed salary floor:** CHF 150k base, with a written step-up after Series A.
- **Deferred salary:** accrues at the agreed post-seed rate from your start date, and is paid in cash at the seed close or converts into equity on the same terms as the investors, at your choice.
- **Runway without pay:** how many months can you personally carry with no salary? Write it down. That number decides between option 1 and option 2.

## Opening the conversation

Start with what structure they have in mind, not with numbers.

> "Before we get into numbers, I'd like to understand the current cap table. Who holds what today, is there an option pool, and have you already had term sheet conversations for the seed? That tells me what a founding seat actually means here."

Then anchor:

> "For a co-founder who owns all of the technology, leads the handover from your external partner and runs technical diligence for the round, I'd expect something around 15% fully diluted, on standard four-year vesting. I'm giving up a Staff-level salary for however long the seed takes, so I'd want the deferred salary to accrue from day one and either be paid at the close or convert alongside the seed investors."

## If they come in low

**If they offer around 3% to 5% ("key early employee" territory):**

> "That's an early-employee number, and the role you've described is a founder role. You're asking me to take no salary, own architecture, regulatory software practices and the investor diligence, and build the team. If that's worth 4%, then it's probably a VP Engineering hire after the seed rather than a co-founder now, and I'd rather be honest about which one we're doing."

**If they won't move on equity but show some flexibility elsewhere:**

> "I can be flexible on the headline percentage if the structure's right. Specifically, the deferred salary converts into equity at the seed price, there's double-trigger acceleration on a change of control, and there's a written post-seed salary floor."

**If they say "all the founders are unpaid, why should you get deferred salary?":**

> "Fair point. The difference is that the original founders' equity reflects the risk they took early on. If you'd rather not accrue deferred pay, I'm fine with that, but then it has to show up in the equity instead."

## Equity terms to get in writing

Percentages matter less than terms. Ask for these:

- **Vesting:** four years with a one-year cliff is standard. Ask for credit for any pre-seed months worked before signing, so the clock starts on your first real day of work.
- **Acceleration:** double-trigger (acquisition *and* termination without cause) with 50% to 100% acceleration. For a medtech that could plausibly be acquired by a diagnostics or pharma player, this matters.
- **Good leaver / bad leaver:** Swiss shareholder agreements almost always have these. Make sure "bad leaver" is narrowly defined (fraud, gross breach) and that a good leaver keeps vested shares at fair market value, not nominal value.
- **Anti-dilution:** founders rarely get anti-dilution protection, and asking for it can look naive. Ask instead for **pro-rata rights** in the seed, if you want to invest, and for the option pool to be created *before* your equity is set, so it doesn't dilute only you.
- **Shares vs options:** in a Swiss AG, founders usually hold real shares bought at nominal value. Buying shares early, while the valuation is low, typically keeps the later gain in tax-free private capital gains territory rather than employment income. Swiss tax treatment of employee equity is tricky, so **get a Swiss tax advisor to confirm this before you sign**.
- **IP assignment:** fine to assign work IP to the company, but carve out prior work and your own open-source projects explicitly.
- **Board / decision rights:** the posting says the CTO takes part in company decisions. Ask whether that means a board seat, observer status or just the executive committee.

## If the seed never closes

This is the most important section and the one most people skip.

> "What happens if the seed doesn't close in, say, 12 months? I'd like a sunset clause: if there's no close by a set date, either party can walk away, I keep whatever equity has vested including time credit, and the accrued deferred salary becomes a claim on future financing or converts to equity at a pre-agreed valuation cap."

Also ask what their current runway is, who's paying the external development partner today and how much cash is actually in the bank.

## Location

The role is listed in Zürich and you're in Geneva. Pre-seed, it barely matters. After the seed, it will.

> "I'm based in Geneva. Before the seed, I'd work mostly remotely and come to Zürich for founder meetings and investor sessions. After the seed, I'd plan on two days a week in Zürich. If you need more than that, it's a relocation discussion and the post-seed package should reflect it."

Don't accept a lower salary for living in Geneva. Geneva is at least as expensive as Zürich, and the Zürich-Geneva train costs you time and money.

## Competing-offer template

Use this only if it's true. Even without a firm offer, your Staff-level market rate is a real alternative.

> "I'll be transparent. I'm also in conversations for Staff and Principal roles in Switzerland in the CHF 200k+ range. I'm talking to you because I want to build something with this mission, not because I need a job, but it does mean the founding terms have to make up for walking away from that."

## Questions to ask before you commit

- What exactly has the external development partner built, who owns the IP, and can you see the code and documentation before signing?
- What's already in place for ISO 13485 and IEC 62304, and who owns the QMS?
- Who's the Board Technology & AI Advisor, and how are decisions split between that role and the CTO?
- How many founders are there, and how is the current equity split?
- What seed amount, at what valuation, and which investors are already interested?
- What does technical due diligence look like so far, and has any investor raised concerns about the technology?

## Walk-away signals

- They won't share the cap table.
- Equity is offered as options with no vesting credit and no acceleration.
- There's no written answer to "what if the seed doesn't close."
- The external partner owns the core IP or the code can't be reviewed.
- "Co-founder" in the title but no real say in hiring, architecture or financing.
