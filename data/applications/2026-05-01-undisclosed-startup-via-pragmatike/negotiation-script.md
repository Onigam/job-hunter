# Negotiation Script — Principal Fullstack Engineer (Undisclosed Startup via Pragmatike)

## Role Context
- Title: Principal Fullstack Engineer
- Company: Early-stage startup (stealth / undisclosed — stage unknown, likely seed to Series A)
- Stack: Full-stack, likely TypeScript/Node.js/React + cloud infra
- Location: EMEA Remote
- Via: Pragmatike (recruitment intermediary)

---

## Market Anchors — Switzerland / EMEA Principal Engineer Level

**Principal / Senior Staff Full-Stack Engineer (IC), Switzerland:**
- Geneva market: CHF 145,000–180,000 base
- Zurich market: CHF 150,000–190,000 base
- Remote (EMEA, non-CH company paying market rates): EUR/CHF 120,000–155,000 base

**Principal Engineer at startup (Europe), by stage:**
- Seed: EUR 110,000–145,000 base + 0.3–0.8% equity (4-year vest, 1-year cliff)
- Series A: EUR 130,000–170,000 base + 0.1–0.4% equity
- Series B+: EUR 150,000–200,000 base + 0.05–0.15% equity

**AI/fullstack experience premium:**
Production LLM and multi-agent experience commands a 10–15% premium over standard fullstack rates in 2025-2026. Use this, especially for roles where AI integration is in scope.

**Magino's current comp baseline:**
- Senior Staff Engineer at Benevity (Geneva): CHF 140,000–160,000 total cash
- Any offer below CHF 130,000 in cash needs compelling equity or a clear path to correction.

---

## Opening Anchor

When asked about compensation expectations, use this framing:

> "Based on my current role and Geneva market rates for principal-level fullstack engineers with production AI experience, I'm looking at a base in the CHF 140,000 to 160,000 range. For a startup where equity is part of the picture, I'm open to discussing balance between cash and equity, but I'd need to understand the stage and option pool before committing to specifics. What does the comp structure look like?"

Key moves:
- Anchor with your number first
- Leave room for equity flexibility without pre-conceding on base
- Ask them to reveal their structure immediately

---

## If They Come In Below Expectations

**Scenario A: Low base, low equity (e.g., EUR 95k base, 0.15% equity)**

> "I appreciate you sharing that. The base is below where I'd need to land given my current comp and Geneva costs, and the equity doesn't reflect principal-level contribution at this stage. Can you walk me through the current option pool and the last valuation? If there's flexibility to get the equity to 0.4% or above with clean vesting terms, I could consider more flexibility on the cash side. But I'd need to see the full structure before I could say yes to anything."

Do not accept without seeing: cap table, total shares outstanding, last valuation or price per share, option pool size and remaining allocation.

**Scenario B: Acceptable base, minimal equity (e.g., CHF 135k base, 0.1% equity)**

> "The base is workable. Where I'd push back is on the equity. A principal engineer at this stage is setting technical direction that compounds for years. 0.1% doesn't reflect that risk or that contribution. I'd want to be closer to 0.35–0.5% to feel like the upside is properly accounted for. Is there room in the option pool to get there?"

---

## Equity Negotiation Specifics

**Minimum acceptable for principal engineer (non-founding):**
- Seed: 0.3–0.8%
- Series A: 0.15–0.4%
- Series B+: 0.05–0.2%

**Questions to ask before signing:**
- What is the current fully diluted share count?
- What was the last round's price per share or valuation?
- What percentage of the option pool remains after this grant?
- Will there be a refresh mechanism after 2 years or at the next funding round?
- What happens to unvested options if the company is acquired? (ask specifically about acceleration)
- Is there a double-trigger or single-trigger acceleration on change of control?
- What's the exercise window post-departure? (standard is 90 days; push for 2–5 years)

**On vesting terms:**
- Standard: 4-year vest, 1-year cliff. Accept nothing shorter on the cliff.
- Push for: monthly vesting after the cliff rather than annual tranches.
- Nice to have: acceleration on termination without cause (6–12 months).

## Geographic Discount Pushback

If they cite "remote market rates" to offer below Swiss-equivalent:

> "I understand how remote rate bands work, but my taxes, rent, and cost of living are anchored in Geneva. The roles I'm comparing this to are all benchmarked here. I'm not asking for a premium above what the role is worth — just making sure we're starting from the right reference point."

---

## Counter-Offer Leverage

If you have a competing offer or active process:

> "I want to be straightforward: I have another process at a similar stage, and the numbers there are in the CHF 145k–155k range with a meaningful equity stake. I'm more interested in this role based on what I've heard about the problem and the team, but I need the comp to be in a comparable range to make this a clear decision. What's the most you can move?"

Only use this if a competing process genuinely exists. If not, frame it as market rate instead: "the range I'm seeing in comparable Geneva-benchmarked roles."

---

## Pragmatike-Specific Notes

Pragmatike is the intermediary; their placement fee means they're motivated to close. Use that.

- Ask directly: "Can you share the salary range the client has budgeted for this role?" Recruiters often know and will share if you ask plainly.
- If they push you to name a number first: "I'd rather not box in the conversation before understanding the full package. What range did the client give you?"
- Don't negotiate hard with Pragmatike directly. Extract the information from them, then save the real negotiation for the hiring team.
- Confirm before any call with the company: is this a screening call or a technical interview? Knowing the format lets you calibrate when to bring up comp.

---

## Walk-Away Conditions

Do not accept an offer that includes:
- Base below CHF 120,000 without equity above 0.5% at seed stage
- Equity below 0.15% at any stage for a principal-level role
- Vesting cliff longer than 12 months
- No cap table or valuation information provided before signing
- No acceleration clause on change of control
- Exercise window shorter than 12 months post-departure
