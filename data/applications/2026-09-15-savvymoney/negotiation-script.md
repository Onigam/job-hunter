# Negotiation Script: SavvyMoney, Lead ML/AI Platform Engineer (EU/EMEA Remote, Contract)

---

## Market Context

**Role type:** Contract engagement, independent contractor through own entity, not a Swiss-payroll employee position. SavvyMoney is a US fintech (51-200 employees, 1,600+ bank and credit union partners) hiring EU/EMEA-based contractors who overlap US Pacific business hours. This changes the negotiation shape from a classic base-plus-equity conversation to a day-rate or monthly-retainer conversation, even though the listing mentions an equity package as a benefit, worth clarifying early since equity for a 1099-style contractor is unusual and may mean phantom equity or RSU-like instruments rather than real shares.

**Switzerland contractor market rates for Lead/Staff ML platform engineers (2026):**
- Senior/Staff platform engineering contractor, Geneva/Zurich: CHF 1,000-1,300/day
- Lead-level with specialized ML/AI platform scope (SageMaker, Bedrock, GenAI/RAG architecture): CHF 1,200-1,600/day
- Annualized equivalent at ~210-220 billable days/year: roughly CHF 250k-350k gross contractor revenue before Magino's own entity costs (AVS, pension, insurance, admin)

**Why the range skews high on the ask:** the role explicitly asks for someone to set technical direction alongside a Data Platform Architect, not just execute tickets, and it sits inside a regulated financial environment, which raises the bar on judgment and communication, not just tooling familiarity. Lead-level, direction-setting scope justifies anchoring near the top of the range rather than the middle.

**Currency note:** US company, likely USD-denominated contract. Convert using a conservative CHF/USD rate and build in a buffer for exchange rate movement over the contract term, since a fixed USD day rate quietly erodes in CHF terms if the franc strengthens.

---

## Opening Position

**Target package:** CHF 1,300-1,450/day (roughly USD 1,450-1,650/day at a conservative rate), invoiced monthly through Magino's own entity, with a defined minimum committed days per month.

**Opening line, when asked for rate expectations:**

> "Before I give a number, I'd like to understand the engagement shape: is this a defined number of days per month, a full-time-equivalent contract, or a project-scoped engagement with a fixed end date? That changes how I'd structure the rate. As a starting point, I'm thinking somewhere in the CHF 1,300-1,450 per day range, or the USD equivalent, for a lead-level scope that includes setting technical direction alongside your Data Platform Architect, not just implementation work."

---

## If They Come In Low

**Scenario:** They anchor on a generic "senior contractor" rate, USD 700-900/day, without accounting for the direction-setting and GenAI strategy scope.

**Response:**

> "I want to flag that the rate you're describing tracks closer to an implementation-focused senior engineer than to a role setting AI/ML technical direction for the company. Given the scope in the posting, architecture ownership, GenAI/agent strategy, mentoring data scientists through the production handoff, I'd need something closer to USD 1,300-1,500/day to make sense of the engagement relative to what I'd be turning down elsewhere. If the budget is fixed lower, I'd want the scope narrowed to match, rather than absorbing lead-level responsibility at a mid-level rate."

**Absolute floor:** CHF 1,050/day (roughly USD 1,150-1,200) with clearly bounded scope, or a lower rate only if paired with a defined path to a higher rate after a 90-day review once the engagement's real shape is clear.

---

## Equity / Long-Term Incentive Notes

Equity for a foreign independent contractor is the exception, not the rule, so treat any offer here as something to interrogate rather than assume is standard RSU-equivalent value.

**Questions to ask if equity comes up:**

> "When the posting mentions an equity package, how does that actually work for a contractor paid through their own entity rather than a W-2 or Swiss payroll employee? Is it phantom equity, a stock option grant that requires a later conversion to employee status, or something else? I want to understand the real mechanism before I factor it into the rate discussion."

If equity turns out to be effectively inaccessible to a contractor (common, for tax and securities-law reasons), don't discount the day rate in anticipation of upside that likely will not materialize. Price the contract as if equity is zero, and treat any real grant as a pleasant surprise, not a bargaining chip they get to use against the rate.

---

## Geographic and Currency Pushback

**If they anchor to a lower-cost-of-living region within "EU/EMEA":**

> "I know EU/EMEA covers a wide range of cost bases, but I'm operating out of Geneva, which runs well above most of the region on both living costs and the local contractor market rate. I'd ask that we anchor the rate closer to Swiss market norms rather than an EU-wide average."

**If the contract is USD-denominated with no FX protection:**

> "Since my costs are in Swiss francs and the contract would be in US dollars, I'd like to either peg the rate to CHF for invoicing or build in an annual rate review that accounts for exchange rate movement, so a weaker dollar over the contract term doesn't quietly cut my effective rate."

---

## Competing Offer Template

*Use only if there is a genuinely active alternative process.*

> "I want to be straightforward that I'm further along in another process with a comparable or stronger rate on the table. What's pulling me toward SavvyMoney is the scope, setting real technical direction on the GenAI and agent strategy rather than executing someone else's roadmap, so if we can land the rate where I've described, I'd rather move forward here."

---

## Questions to Ask Before Committing

1. Is this a fixed-term contract with a defined end date, an ongoing retainer, or a contract-to-perm path?
2. What does "own entity" mean in practice here, does SavvyMoney require a specific corporate structure, or is a Swiss sole proprietorship or GmbH acceptable?
3. Who is the Data Platform Architect, and how is technical direction split between the two roles day to day?
4. What's the current state of the GenAI/agent strategy, is this greenfield or already underway with decisions I'd be inheriting?
5. How is the equity package actually structured for a non-employee contractor?
6. What does the Java microservices integration surface look like today, is there existing tooling for the ML/Java boundary or would that need to be built?
7. What's the invoicing and payment cadence, and is there flexibility on CHF versus USD billing?

---

## Red Flags to Watch For

- Contractor classification that functions like full-time employment in practice (fixed hours, no ability to take other clients, tight day-to-day management) without the pay or protections of actual employment, a misclassification risk on both sides
- Equity offered verbally but with no clear mechanism for a foreign contractor to actually hold it
- Vague ownership boundary between this role and the Data Platform Architect, a setup for conflicting technical direction
- Reluctance to commit to a minimum number of days or a defined contract term
- No clarity on who owns IP for tooling or code built during the engagement, especially relevant given the regulated financial context
