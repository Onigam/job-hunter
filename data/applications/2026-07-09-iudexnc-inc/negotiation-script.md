# Negotiation Script: iudexnc, Co-Founder & CTO

## Context

This is a pre-seed, three-way equal co-founder deal, not an employee offer. There's already a paid pilot with a Swiss insurer and a working prototype, so the company has more traction than a typical pre-seed, which is real leverage. The comp conversation here is really two conversations: equity structure (the main lever) and cash salary (secondary, usually below-market at this stage). Both need to be pinned down before signing anything.

## Switzerland market anchors

- CTO / co-founder cash comp at pre-seed in Zurich typically runs CHF 90,000 to 140,000, well below the CHF 180,000 to 250,000 a Staff/Principal Engineer commands at an established Swiss tech company or bank. Pre-seed founders often take less, sometimes CHF 60,000 to 80,000, in exchange for real equity and control.
- Reference point for a senior staff engineer in Geneva or Zurich at a scale-up: roughly CHF 160,000 to 200,000 base, plus bonus and equity. Use this as the anchor for what's being given up to join at pre-seed cash levels.
- Geneva and Zurich cost of living is near the top of any European ranking, so a below-market salary needs to be offset either by cash runway commitments or accelerated milestones tied to the next raise.
- A third seat, not a fourth, means the equity math should land close to the stated 33/33/33, not a diluted "CTO gets 15% because they joined a few months later" structure.

## Opening anchor

Frame it around contribution, not need: "Given I'm building and owning the entire technical architecture, the AI agent systems, and the compliance layer as a third equal founder, I'd want the equity split to reflect true parity from day one, 33/33/33, with cash comp in the CHF 100,000 to 120,000 range until the next round closes. That's already a discount against what I could get as a senior IC in Zurich or Geneva, so I see it as fair given the ownership stake."

## If they come in low on equity

If the offer starts at something like 20% instead of a third: "I get that the first two of you have been building this for a while and there's sweat equity baked in already. I'm not asking to erase that history. But if I'm signing on as CTO with full ownership of architecture, compliance, and technical hiring, not as employee number one, the split needs to reflect that I'm carrying a third of the company's technical and regulatory risk going forward. Let's talk about what got the first two founders to their current split, and build from there rather than starting from a number that assumes I'm joining later than I actually am."

## If they come in low on cash

"I understand cash is tight at pre-seed, especially pre-Series A. What I'd want instead is either a floor that steps up automatically at the next raise, written into the founder agreement now, or a slightly higher equity allocation to offset the below-market cash. I'd rather take the hit on salary than on ownership, but I want that trade documented, not just implied."

## Equity and vesting terms to lock down

- Four-year vesting with a one-year cliff is standard and reasonable as proposed; confirm it applies identically across all three founders, no exceptions.
- Ask for single-trigger acceleration on a subsequent acquisition (some or all unvested equity accelerates if the company is acquired), and double-trigger acceleration if terminated without cause after a future financing event.
- Confirm founder vesting is tied to a reverse vesting schedule on shares already issued, not options that expire if the company runs out of cash before a liquidity event.
- Get pro-rata rights to maintain the equity percentage through future rounds, at least through Series A.
- Clarify IP assignment terms up front: anything built pre-signing (personal projects, prior employer IP) should be explicitly carved out.
- Ask who holds the deciding vote on technical direction if the three founders disagree, since a clean 33/33/33 split can deadlock without a tiebreaker mechanism written down somewhere.

## Geographic relocation ask

Since the role requires on-site collaboration in Zurich and Magino is currently in Geneva, negotiate a relocation stipend (CHF 5,000 to 10,000 is reasonable for a founder-level move within Switzerland) and a flexible start date that allows for lease transitions. This is a minor ask relative to the equity conversation but worth putting on the table rather than assuming it out of pocket.

## Competing offer leverage

If there's a competing offer or a strong counterfactual (staying at a senior staff role with a known compensation band): "I want to be upfront that walking away from a stable senior staff position has a real opportunity cost, roughly CHF 180,000 in current comp. I'm doing that because the equity stake and the technical ownership here are worth more to me long-term than the cash gap short-term. I just need the founder agreement to reflect that this is a genuine equal partnership, not a title with a smaller slice attached."

## Close

Don't over-negotiate a pre-seed deal with people who are about to be daily collaborators for years. The goal is a fair, clearly documented structure, not squeezing every last basis point. Get the split, vesting, and decision rights in writing before doing the working trial, not after.
