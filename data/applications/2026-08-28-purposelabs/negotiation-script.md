# Negotiation Script, Purpose:Labs (Co-Founder / CTO)

Context: pre-seed or seed-stage cofounder role, Zurich-based, "Life & Career Navigation Engine" (world models, individual state models, proactive AI agents). No salary was listed in the posting, this reads as an equity-heavy, low-or-deferred-cash cofounder slot rather than a hired-CTO role. Treat this as a cofounder negotiation, not a comp negotiation, the two need different scripts.

## Market anchors (Switzerland, cofounder / early CTO context)

- A hired (non-founder) CTO at a Swiss seed-stage startup typically lands CHF 160k-220k base, often with 1-4% equity if brought in after formation.
- A true cofounder joining pre-product or pre-seed usually takes little to no cash initially (CHF 0-80k, sometimes structured as a deferred/convertible salary once funding lands) in exchange for meaningful equity, commonly 15-35% for a technical cofounder joining at the idea stage with an existing non-technical founder.
- Zurich cost of living is comparable to Geneva; do not accept a discount against Zurich or Geneva rates just because the company frames itself as "early stage." Early stage affects equity split and vesting, not the legitimacy of a living wage once funded.
- If Purpose:Labs has already raised (friends & family, pre-seed), ask directly what the current cash runway is and what fraction can go to a technical cofounder's salary versus what's deferred.

## Opening position

Before discussing numbers, get clarity on two things: how much has already been built (is there a solo non-technical founder, a prototype, any funding), and what decision-making authority "Co-Founder CTO" actually carries (cap table say, hiring say, technical veto). Equity percentage is meaningless without knowing what's already been given away and what board/investor structure exists.

Opening anchor, once those are clear: "Given I'd be the technical cofounder building the entire product and engineering org from zero, similar to what I did at Alaya through to its acquisition, I'd expect founder-level equity in the 20-30% range with a standard four-year vesting and one-year cliff, plus a cash component that covers Zurich or Geneva living costs even if modest pre-funding, something in the CHF 60k-100k range or a deferred salary that activates at the first funding close."

## If they come in low on equity

Script: "I understand pre-seed equity conversations are tight, but a single technical cofounder building the world model, the individual state model and the agent layer from scratch is taking on founder-level risk and founder-level scope. I'd want to see the cap table before agreeing to a number below 15%, and I'd want anti-dilution protection through at least the seed round so an early low offer doesn't get diluted twice."

## If they offer no cash at all pre-funding

Script: "I can work at reduced or deferred cash for a defined period, three to six months is reasonable while we get to a fundable prototype, but I'd want that in writing as a deferred salary that gets paid out or converted once we close funding, not just goodwill. Open-ended zero cash isn't something I can sustain in Switzerland."

## Equity mechanics to nail down

- Vesting: 4 years, 1-year cliff, standard even between cofounders, protects both sides if it doesn't work out.
- Founder vesting acceleration: ask for single-trigger or double-trigger acceleration on acquisition, given the Alaya precedent where the outcome was exactly a strategic acquisition.
- Anti-dilution: at minimum, standard weighted-average protection through seed; don't accept full-ratchet only for investors while cofounders get nothing.
- IP assignment: confirm all code and models built are assigned to the company, not left ambiguous, this protects the eventual cap table story for both of us.

## Geographic discount pushback

If there's any framing that a Zurich or remote-from-Geneva arrangement should pay less than a São Paulo or Lisbon rate: "Geneva and Zurich are both high cost-of-living markets, on par with each other. If the equity and cash package assumes a lower cost-of-living location, that math doesn't hold for me relocating within Switzerland."

## Competing offer as counterweight (use only if real)

"I'm also evaluating a Staff/Principal platform role with a cash-comp package in the CHF 180k-220k range. I'm genuinely more interested in building something from zero again given the Alaya outcome, but the equity and cash structure here needs to reflect that I'm giving up a known, well-paying track to take this on."

## Close

End the negotiation conversation by asking for the term sheet or founder agreement in writing before any public announcement or commitment, and confirm a mutual 30-day trial period is not standard for a true cofounder role, if they propose one, that's a signal they're hiring a CTO, not bringing on a cofounder, and the equity number should shift accordingly.
