# Negotiation Script — Logenta Founder CTO

## Market Context

**Role:** Founder/CTO at early-stage startup (pre-Series A, likely)
**Location:** Hamburg, Germany (preferred) — with potential for hybrid or partial remote

### Hamburg / Germany Market Rates for Founder CTO (2025-2026)

Early-stage Founder CTO compensation in Germany varies widely by funding stage:

- Pre-seed / bootstrapped: 60-90k EUR base + meaningful equity (1.5-4%)
- Seed-funded (500k-2M): 80-110k EUR base + 1-2.5% equity
- Series A: 110-150k EUR base + 0.5-1.5% equity

**Important geographic note:** Magino is based in Geneva, Switzerland, where senior engineering salaries run 140-180k CHF (~155-200k EUR equivalent for comparable roles). This gap needs to be addressed in negotiation: the offset is equity, ownership, and the founding CTO title itself.

---

## Opening Anchor

If asked for your range, anchor high and explain the Geneva-to-Hamburg adjustment honestly:

> "My current frame of reference is the Geneva market, where Senior Staff Engineering roles sit between 150k and 180k CHF. I understand Hamburg is a different market. For a Founder CTO role at this stage, I'd want to find something in the 110-130k EUR range on base, with equity that reflects the founding-level exposure I'm taking on. The base number is negotiable if the equity structure is right."

This does three things: establishes your market baseline, signals you understand the geography, and pivots to equity where you have more upside.

---

## Equity Negotiation

For a Founder CTO joining very early (pre-product or early-product), target range is **1.5-3%**.

Key questions to ask before accepting any equity offer:

1. "What's the fully diluted cap table look like, and where does this grant sit on it?"
2. "What's the vesting schedule? I'd expect 4 years with a 1-year cliff."
3. "Is there any acceleration on a change of control? Single-trigger or double-trigger?"
4. "What's the current valuation and the last round's price per share?"
5. "What's the option pool size, and is there a plan to expand it before the next round?"

**If they offer less than 1%:** Push back clearly.
> "At founding stage, with the scope of what a CTO role involves here, I'd need to be closer to 1.5-2% to make the risk-reward work. Below 1% starts to feel more like a senior hire than a founding team member, and I think what you're describing is closer to the latter."

**If they offer 2%+ but with a low strike price:** Good sign. Confirm the 409A/valuation is recent and not inflated.

---

## Counter-Offer Script (if they come in low on base)

If the first offer is below 90k EUR:

> "I appreciate the offer. I want to make this work, and I'm not anchoring hard to a number. But I'm relocating from a Geneva market to Hamburg, which has real costs attached to it, and the base needs to at least bridge that gap. Can we get to 100-105k on base? If that's not possible right now, I'd rather talk about an equity acceleration or a 6-month review milestone tied to a bump."

If they can't move on base at all:

> "I understand the cash constraints at this stage. If the base is fixed, I'd want to revisit the equity pool: either a larger initial grant or a clear path to additional options tied to specific milestones. What's flexible on that side?"

---

## Geographic Discount Pushback

If they use Hamburg vs. Geneva to argue for a lower comp:

> "That's true, Hamburg is a lower cost-of-living market than Geneva. But I'd push back gently on the idea that the output of a Founder CTO scales with geography. The risk, the scope, and what you need from this role are the same whether I'm in Hamburg or Geneva. The market rate comparison is useful for benchmarking, but it shouldn't be the ceiling."

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

If you have other offers in play (or want to create urgency):

> "I have a couple of other processes moving forward, one of which is further along. I want to be direct with you: Logenta is the one that interests me most at this stage, which is why I'm having this conversation. But I need to make a decision in the next [10-14 days]. If there's a way to move faster on the offer structure, that would help me a lot."

If no competing offer yet but you want to set a timeline:

> "I want to be honest: I don't have a signed offer on the table today, but I have processes that are at offer stage and I'm expecting decisions in the next two weeks. I'd rather make a choice in your favor than default to something else because timing didn't work."

---

## Non-Negotiables to Establish Early

- Vesting starts from day of verbal offer or first day of work (not from contract signing date)
- Equipment budget if based remotely (at minimum laptop + monitors)
- Clear agreement on remote/Hamburg split expectations (days per month, expenses covered)
- Role title: Founder CTO, not "Head of Engineering" or "CTO" without the founding equity structure to match

---

## Red Flags to Watch For

- Equity offered as "phantom stock" rather than real options or shares
- No cap table transparency before signing
- Vesting cliff longer than 1 year
- Base significantly below 80k EUR with vague promises about future adjustment
- Pressure to start before terms are formalized in writing
