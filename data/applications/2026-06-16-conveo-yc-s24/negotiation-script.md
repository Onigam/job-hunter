## Conversation Scripts

### Opening (after offer lands)

"Thanks for the offer. I've been thinking seriously about the role and I want to make this work. Before I respond formally, can you walk me through the full package breakdown: base, equity grant, cliff, vesting schedule, and any refresh policy?"

Wait for full picture before countering.

### If base comes in at £100-120k

"I appreciate the offer. The role is clearly broader than a typical Senior Platform Engineer: you're asking for infra ownership, AI toolchain design, and direct product contribution in parallel. That scope maps to Staff level in most comp frameworks, and based on the London market for this profile, I was expecting something closer to £155-160k. Is there room to move on base, or is equity where you have more flexibility?"

### If base comes in at £120-140k

"I'm close to where I need to be. I'd want to land at £155k to account for the scope and my background. If that's not movable, I'd want to revisit the equity grant — can you walk me through the option pool percentage and how you think about that for a hire at this level?"

### If they say the range is firm

"Understood. Then let's talk equity. At seed stage with this kind of enterprise traction, the equity is the real upside, and I want to make sure we're aligned on what a meaningful grant looks like for a hire in this role. What's the current option pool and what range are you working with for this position?"

## Equity Notes (YC S24, Seed Stage)

**What to ask for:**
- Target: 0.25-0.50% fully diluted (FD) for a Senior/Staff hire at seed
- Cliff: standard 1-year is fine; push back on anything longer
- Vesting: 4-year with monthly vesting post-cliff is standard
- Acceleration: ask for single-trigger acceleration on change of control (50% of unvested)

**Key questions to ask:**
- "What is the current fully diluted share count?"
- "What strike price are options issued at today?"
- "Is this an EMI scheme?" (UK tax-advantaged option scheme — important for London)
- "What's the refresh policy after year one?"

**If they give a share count instead of a percentage:**
Calculate: (your grant / total FD shares) x 100 = your percentage. Know this number before signing.

**Red flags:**
- No disclosed share count
- Options at market rate with no discount
- No acceleration clause at all
- Vesting cliff longer than 12 months

## Remote / Geneva Adjustment Script

If the role is officially London but you want to negotiate remote:

"I'm based in Geneva and work effectively in the European timezone. Given that you're working with distributed teams and interviewing across 50+ languages, remote-first coordination is already part of how you operate. I'm open to regular London travel for key team moments, but I'd want to discuss whether this role could be structured as remote-first rather than 3 days/week in-office."

If they push on in-person:
"I understand. To make that work from Geneva, the compensation would need to account for the travel and the cost-of-living differential. Geneva is roughly 30-40% more expensive than London on a day-to-day basis. That's part of why I was anchoring at £160k rather than the market midpoint."

## Competing Offer Leverage

If you have another offer (or want to create urgency):

"I want to be transparent: I'm at final stages with one other company and expect an offer this week. Conveo is my preference based on the scope and stage, but I need to move by [date]. If we can align on terms, I'm ready to commit."

If no competing offer: do not fabricate one. Use timeline pressure instead: "I've been in active processes with a few companies and I'd like to wrap things up in the next two weeks."

## Non-Negotiables to Protect

- Fully remote or London travel max 2 days/week (Geneva base is non-negotiable)
- EMI options or equivalent (do not accept phantom equity or profit participation certificates without legal review)
- Written confirmation of FD share count before signing
