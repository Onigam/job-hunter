# Negotiation Script — Berry (Founding Full Stack Engineer)

---

## Situation Snapshot

Berry listed a salary range of $50K-$85K with 0.1%-1.0% equity. For a founding engineer role, the equity range is the real lever here. The cash range is well below Switzerland market rates and even below US market rates for someone with 17+ years and founding-engineer-level experience. This needs to be addressed directly but without derailing the conversation.

---

## Market Anchors (Switzerland / Remote Senior Engineering)

- **Geneva Senior/Staff Engineer**: CHF 160K-200K base, depending on company stage and scope.
- **YC-backed founding engineer, US remote market**: $140K-$180K base for equivalent seniority.
- **Berry's listed range ($50K-$85K)** reflects either a tight early-stage budget or a global remote pay scale, not Swiss market rates. Know this going in.
- **Equity at this stage**: For a genuine founding engineer (first or second engineer, pre-product-market-fit), 0.5%-1.0% is reasonable and defensible. 0.1% is advisory-level, not founding-level.

---

## Opening Positioning (before any numbers are exchanged)

When asked about compensation expectations early in the process, deflect to scope:

> "I'd like to understand the scope a bit better first. Founding engineer at a company of eight, where this role owns a significant chunk of the technical roadmap, is a different conversation than a senior hire at a larger org. Once we've aligned on what the role actually is, I can give you a number that makes sense for both sides."

This reframes the conversation around what you're contributing, not what they posted.

---

## Anchoring the Number

If pressed for a number, anchor above your target:

> "For a founding engineer role at this stage, Geneva market rate for my experience level starts at CHF 170K-190K. If we're talking global remote and adjusting for timezone, the equivalent in USD lands around $150K-$165K. I'm flexible on the shape, but that's the range I need to make this work."

Do not apologize for the number. State it cleanly and let the silence land.

---

## If They Come Back Below the Floor

If they counter with something in the $80K-$100K range:

> "I appreciate you being transparent. That's a meaningful gap from what I need for this to work as a long-term commitment. I want to make this work, so let me ask: is there flexibility on the equity side? If we're talking 0.75%-1%, the cash calculation looks different, and I'm willing to have that conversation."

This turns a hard no into a structured negotiation. You're not rejecting them, you're reframing.

If equity is also stuck at the low end:

> "I'm going to be straight with you: a founding engineer compensation package in that range is going to be hard for me to accept given Geneva's cost of living and my current situation. I've had conversations with other companies at closer to CHF 160K base. If the overall package including equity isn't able to get into that neighborhood, I'd need to see something concrete that changes the math."

---

## Equity Negotiation Specifics

Target: 0.5%-1.0%
Floor: 0.3% (below this, it's not a founding engineer package)

Useful framing:

> "The founding engineer designation means the architectural decisions I make in the first six months will still be running in year three. That's a different risk profile than joining at Series B. I'd expect the equity to reflect that."

Key terms to negotiate beyond the percentage:
- **Cliff**: Standard 1-year cliff is fine.
- **Vesting period**: 4 years is standard; 3 years is worth asking for given the early-stage risk.
- **Acceleration clause**: Push for double-trigger acceleration (involuntary termination + acquisition). Single-trigger is worth asking for but unlikely.
- **Anti-dilution protection**: Not standard at this stage, but worth raising if they're planning a near-term raise.
- **Exercise window post-departure**: Ask for 5-7 years. The standard 90-day window is punishing.

> "On the equity, I want to make sure I understand the terms. What's the exercise window if I leave before an exit? And is there any acceleration in the event of acquisition?"

---

## Geographic Premium Pushback

If they use geography as a reason to pay below market:

> "I understand the logic, but Geneva's cost of living is in the top five globally. I'm not asking for a San Francisco premium; I'm asking for a number that's defensible against the actual cost of operating here. My current baseline for 'this makes sense' is CHF 160K, which is already on the lower end for a Staff-level engineer in this market."

---

## Competing Offer Template

If you have other processes running (even if at an early stage):

> "I do have another process moving forward with a company in Switzerland at a higher base. I'd prefer to close with Berry, but I need to be able to make a rational comparison. Is there anything you can do to sharpen the package before I have to give them an answer?"

This creates urgency without fabricating details. Only use if you actually have another process.

---

## Things to Confirm Before Signing

- Total shares outstanding (to calculate real dilution, not just percentage)
- Most recent 409A valuation or equivalent
- Preferred vs. common stock (founders hold preferred; make sure you understand the liquidation preference stack)
- Current cap table composition (how many other employees have equity, what's been committed to advisors)
- Planned fundraise timeline (if they're raising a Seed/Series A in 6 months, your dilution math changes)
