# Negotiation Script, Nexthink Staff Software Engineer (AI-native automation)

## Context
This is a Lausanne-based, hybrid role at a mature Swiss scale-up (1,000+ employees, dual HQ Lausanne/Boston, past unicorn-range valuation), not an early seed company, so the comp conversation should center on cash and RSUs rather than speculative seed equity. Being Geneva-based already removes relocation cost from the equation entirely, and that's a real point in my favor: Nexthink doesn't need to fund a move, cover a relocation package, or absorb the ramp-up risk of an international hire. That should show up in the number, not just in goodwill.

## Market anchors
- Staff Software Engineer, Lausanne/Geneva lake basin, established Swiss tech company: CHF 150,000-185,000 base is the realistic band for 10+ years of experience with 3+ years at Staff or Principal level.
- Companies at Nexthink's scale (1,000+ employees, enterprise B2B, prior unicorn valuation) typically pay toward the upper half of that band for Staff-level hires with production AI/ML integration experience, since that combination is still scarce in the Swiss market.
- Total comp including bonus (commonly 10-15% target at this company size) and RSU or option grants should land CHF 175,000-215,000 all-in for a strong Staff-level offer.
- A founder-track record (built and scaled a platform to acquisition) is above what a typical Staff IC hire brings and is worth naming explicitly, even outside a formal leadership track.

## Opening anchor
> "For a Staff-level role driving the AI-native rebuild of Flow, I'd anchor around CHF 180,000 base plus the standard bonus target and an RSU or option grant reflecting Staff scope. I'm already Geneva-based, so there's no relocation cost or ramp-up risk on your side, and I'd expect that to factor into where we land."

## If they come back low
> "I appreciate the offer and I'm not surprised there's a gap to close, that's normal at this stage. The scope here, greenfield architecture work on a product that doesn't exist yet, plus production experience actually shipping generative AI features, is closer to what Staff or Principal hires command at comparable Swiss companies than what's on the table. Is there room to move the base, or to weight more into the bonus or equity if the base band is fixed?"

## Counter-offer script
> "Thanks for walking me through the structure, I can see how it's put together. Given the scope, distributed systems ownership on Flow's AI-agent orchestration layer, plus the fact that I'm local and ready to start without a relocation timeline, I'd like to counter at CHF 180,000 base with the RSU grant sized for Staff level rather than a senior IC hire. I'm flexible on how the difference splits between base, bonus, and equity, as long as the total reflects that scope."

## Equity and RSU questions
Nexthink isn't disclosing a public valuation, so these need to be asked directly rather than assumed:
- Is the equity component RSUs, options, or phantom stock, and what's the vesting schedule (standard ask: 4 years, 1-year cliff)?
- What was the most recent internal or funding-round valuation used to price grants?
- For options: strike price, exercise window post-termination, and whether early exercise is allowed.
- How does the grant get refreshed at future review cycles, and is there a stated policy or is it purely discretionary?

## Geographic and cost-of-living pushback, if needed
> "I know Lausanne and Geneva get compared to Zurich sometimes as a lower-cost alternative, but the lake basin isn't actually cheap, and I'm not asking for a Zurich premium, just a number that reflects Staff scope in this specific market rather than a discounted regional band."

## Competing offer script
> "I'm currently in later-stage conversations with another company at a comparable level, so I'd like to move relatively quickly here if the scope and comp line up. I'd rather make this work than run a bidding process, but I do need the offer to be competitive with what I'm seeing elsewhere in the Swiss market for Staff-level AI infrastructure work."

## What to hold firm on regardless of base outcome
- Written scope confirming this is architecture-and-build ownership on Flow's agentic layer, not a general backend maintenance role, reflected in the offer letter or a documented first-90-days plan.
- RSU or option terms in writing: grant size, vesting schedule, cliff, and refresh policy, not just a headline number.
- Hybrid expectations in writing (days on-site per week), since "hybrid" ranges widely across Swiss companies.
- Bonus target and whether it's discretionary or tied to explicit metrics, documented rather than described verbally.
