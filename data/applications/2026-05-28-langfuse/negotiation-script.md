# Negotiation Script — Senior Forward Deployed Engineer, Langfuse (ClickHouse)

---

## Market Context

**Role type:** Senior Forward Deployed Engineer — hybrid IC/customer-facing, senior level.

**Company context:** Langfuse is now part of ClickHouse (acquired January 2026). Compensation is likely structured as a ClickHouse employee rather than a Langfuse standalone package. ClickHouse is a private company (no IPO date announced as of early 2026), so equity is in the form of options or RSUs that require a liquidity event to realize.

**Geographic complexity:** The role is listed as hybrid in San Francisco. Magino is in Geneva. This is a meaningful lever — either Langfuse/ClickHouse accommodates remote-from-Europe (with possible travel expectations), or there's a relocation ask. Both scenarios affect the anchor.

**Switzerland benchmark (equivalent seniority, comparable roles):**
- Senior Solutions Engineer / Technical Account Manager, Geneva: CHF 140,000 - 175,000 base
- Staff-level IC equivalent with customer scope: CHF 160,000 - 190,000 base
- These are gross figures; Swiss social contributions (AHV, etc.) add ~12-13% on top from the employer side

**US benchmark (SF-based, equivalent seniority):**
- Senior Solutions Engineer / FDE at Series B-D or well-funded company: USD 180,000 - 230,000 base
- Total comp (base + bonus + equity annual value): USD 250,000 - 320,000

---

## Opening Anchor

**If the role stays remote from Geneva (Europe-based):**

> "For a senior IC role at this scope, with the customer portfolio and production ownership you've described, I'd expect to be somewhere in the CHF 165,000 - 185,000 base range. That's aligned with Geneva market rates for senior technical roles at well-funded product companies. I'm open to discussing equity separately."

**If relocation to SF is on the table:**

> "If this is a genuine SF-based role with relocation support, the market anchor shifts. I'd be looking at a USD 200,000 - 220,000 base, plus meaningful equity in the form of ClickHouse options with standard 4-year vesting and a 1-year cliff. SF cost of living and the cross-timezone setup for my personal situation mean the total package needs to reflect that."

---

## If They Come In Low

**Scenario: They offer CHF 130,000 or USD 160,000 base.**

> "I appreciate the offer. I want to work through this rather than just counter a number. The role asks for ownership of 10-20 strategic accounts, production architecture guidance, and a tight feedback loop to the product team. That's a Staff-level scope, not a mid-level SE role. The market rates I've benchmarked for this scope, in Geneva and in SF, both land above what you've proposed. Can we talk about what's creating the gap? Is there flexibility on base, or is there more headroom on equity or a signing component?"

**If they say the band is fixed:**

> "I understand bands have constraints. If the base ceiling is CHF 140,000, then I'd need to see the equity component be meaningfully larger to bridge the gap. Can you walk me through the current ClickHouse option pool, the most recent 409A or fair market value, and the vesting terms? That gives me what I need to evaluate total comp properly."

---

## Equity Questions to Ask

Since ClickHouse is private:

1. What is the current 409A / fair market value per share?
2. What is the strike price on the options being offered?
3. What is the total option pool size and what percentage of the company do the offered options represent?
4. What is the vesting schedule? (Standard: 4-year, 1-year cliff, monthly vesting after.)
5. What anti-dilution protections exist? (Pro-rata rights in future rounds?)
6. What happens to vested options if ClickHouse is acquired before IPO? (Acceleration clauses?)
7. What is the exercise window after leaving the company? (90 days is punishing; 5-10 years is fair.)

**If options have a short exercise window (90 days), push back:**

> "A 90-day exercise window is a real financial risk for employees, especially at a pre-IPO company. I'd want to either negotiate an extended window or see the strike price adjusted to reflect that risk. Many later-stage companies have moved to 5-year windows — is there flexibility here?"

---

## Geographic Premium Pushback

If they try to apply a "Geneva discount" or argue for European market rates on a SF-listed role:

> "The role was posted as hybrid in San Francisco, which sets the comp expectation. I'm open to being flexible on the geography, but that flexibility should be mutual. If the expectation is that I'm operating in European hours, traveling to SF for key customer engagements, and effectively covering both time zones, the compensation should reflect the scope, not just the location of my apartment."

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

If you have another offer in process (or want to create urgency):

> "I want to be transparent: I have another process at a similar stage that will likely conclude within the next week or two. I'd prefer to make a decision based on fit, not just timing, but I do need to move. If Langfuse is the right place, I want to find a package that reflects that so we can close this."

**If the other offer is real and higher:**

> "The other offer is at [X]. I'd rather be here — the product is something I actually use and want to build on — but I can't leave [X] on the table without something comparable. Is there a path to matching that, either on base or through the equity component?"

---

## Benefits and Non-Cash Items Worth Negotiating

- Remote work flexibility (Europe-based rather than required SF presence)
- Travel budget if regular SF visits are expected
- Home office stipend (CHF 2,000 - 5,000 one-time or annual)
- Professional development / conference budget
- Signing bonus if they can't move on base (often more flexible than salary bands)
- Start date flexibility (notice period in Switzerland is typically 1-3 months)
