# Negotiation Script — Airweave, Founding Software Engineer, Data Infrastructure

---

## Context

**Posted range:** €80,000–€100,000 EUR + 0.25%–0.75% equity
**Location:** Amsterdam, Netherlands (in-person at Herengracht)
**Stage:** YC P25, ~7 people, already revenue-generating with a top-tier AI lab as customer
**Your profile:** 17 years, founding engineer who scaled Alaya to acquisition by a $1B+ unicorn, Staff-level at Benevity since 2021

**Key dynamic:** The posted salary range is appropriate for Amsterdam mid-to-senior levels but underprices a founding-engineer hire at your experience level. You are not a hire who needs onboarding; you are a hire who will define the infrastructure layer and set standards for the engineers who come after you. That scope justifies a premium on both salary and equity.

**Geneva vs. Amsterdam:** Your baseline in Geneva (CHF 150k–180k for Staff+) is not directly comparable due to currency and cost-of-living differences. Amsterdam senior engineers typically earn €85k–€115k; founding engineers at YC-stage companies with your profile sit at €100k–€120k. Use that Amsterdam range, not Geneva, as your reference when talking to the founders.

---

## Salary Anchor

**Your opening ask: €115,000**

Justify it simply. You're not asking as a negotiating tactic; you're describing what the market pays for someone who will own the data infrastructure layer at a founding level, not manage it.

Script:
> "Based on the scope of the role and my experience doing this kind of founding infrastructure work before, I'd be looking at €115,000. I know your posted range is €80k–€100k, but I think there's a difference between a hire who grows into this role and one who hits the ground running and defines what the infrastructure should look like for the next three years. I'm the second kind."

**Minimum acceptable:** €100,000
**Walk away below:** €95,000 (top of posted range is the floor, not the target)

---

## Equity Negotiation

**Your ask: 0.75% (top of posted range)**

At 7 people and pre-Series A, the founding engineer for infrastructure should be at the top of the equity band, not the bottom. The 0.25% floor is for a mid-level hire; 0.75% reflects founding-team-level ownership.

Script:
> "On equity, I'd want to be at the top of the range you posted, so 0.75%. The infrastructure layer is foundational — whoever owns it early will have an outsized impact on how the product scales. I'd rather that alignment be reflected in the equity, not just the title."

**Standard vesting:** 4-year vest, 1-year cliff. If they offer anything shorter or without a cliff, push back.

**If they resist the 0.75%:**
> "I understand there are constraints on equity at this stage. If 0.5% is the ceiling, I can work with that, but I'd want to pair it with a clear path to refresh grants after year two, tied to impact milestones we define together."

**Ask about:**
- Option type (ISOs for US-taxpayer-friendly treatment, though less relevant in NL — ask about their Dutch equivalent)
- Strike price and last 409A valuation date
- What happens to options on acquisition (accelerated vesting? full or partial?)
- Pro-rata rights on future rounds

---

## If They Come in Low on Salary

If the initial offer is at or below €90,000:

> "I appreciate the offer. I want to be direct: the €90k number is below where I need to be for this to work financially, given the relocation from Geneva and the scope of what you're asking me to own. I'd need €105k to make this realistic. Is there room to move on salary, or should we look at making the equity side more compelling?"

If salary is truly capped:
> "If the salary ceiling is firm, I'd want to revisit the equity. Founding-level infrastructure ownership at 0.5% feels more aligned than what the current salary reflects. Can we get to 0.5% with a clear accelerator if the infrastructure layer hits certain scale milestones in year one?"

---

## Relocation

You're moving from Geneva to Amsterdam. That has a real cost: shipping, housing deposit, setup month. Ask for a relocation allowance.

Script:
> "One thing I'd want to factor in is relocation. I'm coming from Geneva, so there's a practical cost to getting set up in Amsterdam: housing deposit, shipping, a transition month. A one-time relocation allowance of €5,000–€8,000 would cover that. Is that something you can include?"

This is a reasonable ask for a startup that wants a serious founding hire. If they decline, it's a small point; don't lose a good offer over it. But ask.

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

If you have another offer in play (or expect one shortly):

> "I want to be transparent: I'm in late-stage conversations with one other company, and I expect to have a decision to make by [date]. Airweave is my first choice for the type of work and the problem you're solving, but I want to make sure the numbers are in the same neighborhood before I commit. Can we move the offer discussion forward this week?"

If you don't have another offer: don't fabricate one. Instead, use timeline leverage:
> "I'm actively interviewing, so my timeline for a decision is compressed. If the offer is strong, I can move quickly."
