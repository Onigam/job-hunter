# Negotiation Script — n8n Senior AI Engineer

---

## Market Context

**Company stage:** Series B, $60M raised (Highland Capital led). ~100 employees. Profitable enough to have grown 6x YoY. Pre-IPO, meaningful equity window.

**Role:** Senior AI Engineer, Europe Remote (n8n benchmarks pay against European markets, likely Berlin as primary anchor).

**Relevant market rates (2026):**
- Berlin Senior AI Engineer: EUR 110,000-145,000 base
- London Senior AI Engineer: GBP 120,000-160,000 base
- Switzerland (Geneva) Senior AI Engineer: CHF 185,000-240,000 base
- "Europe remote" (n8n's frame): likely EUR 120,000-150,000 base range

**Magino's position:** 17 years of experience including founding engineer track record, platform at acquisition scale, Series B-relevant AI engineering work. This is senior-of-senior, not just "5+ years."

---

## Opening Anchor

If they ask for a number first, redirect:

> "I'd rather hear where you're benchmarking the role before anchoring on a number. The comp gap between Berlin-benchmarked remote and what makes sense for Geneva can be significant, and I want to understand how you're thinking about it."

If they push for a number:

> "Given my background and the Geneva cost base, I'd be looking at something in the CHF 200,000 to 220,000 range for base, plus equity. That's in line with what similar roles at Series B companies pay engineers in Switzerland."

Justify if challenged:
- 17 years of engineering, 5 as founding engineer, platform acquired for unicorn valuation
- AI product engineering is a premium skill set right now, not general backend
- Geneva is one of the highest-cost cities in Europe; the CHF/EUR spread is real (~15-20% cost premium over Berlin)

---

## If They Come In Low (EUR 120,000-135,000 range)

> "I appreciate the offer. The number is lower than where I was expecting to land, and I want to be transparent about that so we can figure out whether there's a path. My base target is CHF 195,000. What flexibility exists on equity if base is constrained by your European band?"

Then probe:
- What is the current option pool percentage?
- What was the 409A / last board-approved strike price?
- What's the cliff and vesting schedule?
- Is there acceleration on acquisition or change of control?

If base is hard-capped at their band:

> "If base is at the top of what the band allows, I'd want to see the equity component reflect the delta. For a senior AI role at Series B stage, I'd expect 0.15-0.25% fully diluted as a realistic anchor."

---

## Equity Negotiation

n8n context: Fair-code license, strong open-source moat (100k+ GitHub stars), Series B trajectory. Equity is meaningful but not a lottery ticket.

**What to ask for:** 0.1-0.3% fully diluted options (standard for senior engineer at this stage and company size).

**Key questions to ask:**
1. What percentage of the company does the current option pool represent?
2. How many shares outstanding (fully diluted)?
3. What was the strike price set at the last 409A valuation?
4. Four-year vest, one-year cliff — is there any acceleration clause?
5. What's the exercise window if you leave (important: standard 90 days is punitive, ask for 5-10 years)?

Script for equity conversation:

> "The equity matters to me, not just as upside but as alignment. Can you walk me through the grant size you're thinking, the strike price, and the current fully-diluted share count? I want to understand what the ownership stake actually represents."

If they offer fewer than 0.1% fully diluted:

> "At Series B with the growth trajectory you've described, I'd expect a senior AI engineer grant to be in the 0.1-0.2% range. Is there room to move there?"

---

## Geographic Premium Pushback

n8n may push back with "we pay the same rate regardless of location" or "our band is based on European norms, not Swiss norms."

Response:

> "I understand the philosophy behind location-agnostic pay, and I respect it in principle. The practical reality is that Geneva is 40-50% more expensive than Berlin on housing alone, and my current compensation reflects that. I'm not asking you to pay me more because I happen to live somewhere expensive, I'm asking you to ensure the total comp is competitive enough that this is a viable move for me financially. If the base can't flex, equity and any signing component would help close that gap."

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

If you have another offer (or want a template):

> "I want to be upfront: I'm at a point where I need to make a decision in the next [10 days]. I have another offer at [X], and n8n is genuinely my first choice because of [specific reason: the text-to-workflow problem, the AI team's roadmap, open-source culture]. But I need the numbers to be in the same range to make it work. Is there room to move on [base / equity / signing]?"

If you don't have a competing offer, don't fabricate one. Use timeline pressure instead:

> "I'm at the point in my search where I need to make a decision. I want to be clear that n8n is where I'd rather be — but I need to know whether the total comp can get to a place that makes this a clear yes."

---

## Benefits to Negotiate Beyond Base

- Signing bonus: CHF 20,000-30,000 one-time is reasonable at Series B, especially if you're leaving unvested equity elsewhere
- €1,000 L&D budget is standard; ask if it can be increased for a senior/specialist role
- Home office stipend (hardware, connectivity): ask if not mentioned
- Conference / speaking budget if relevant
- Ask explicitly about extended exercise window on options (default 90 days is punitive — ask for post-termination window of at least 2-3 years)

---

## Red Lines

- Base below CHF 170,000 / EUR 155,000 is difficult to justify for Geneva market
- Equity grant below 0.1% fully diluted is below market for Series B senior engineer
- No acceleration clause on acquisition is a yellow flag given n8n's likely exit trajectory
