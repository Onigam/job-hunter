# Negotiation Script: Nexthink SA, Chief Architect

Context going in: this is a Director/VP-equivalent role, not a Staff IC role, owning platform architecture direction and leading six senior architects across Europe. Nexthink is PE-backed (Permira took a majority stake in 2021), an established company with 1,300+ customers rather than an early-stage startup, so the comp structure leans base plus bonus plus a long-term incentive component rather than founder-style equity. The role sits in Lausanne; I'm in Geneva, so relocation and cost-of-living discounts don't apply.

## Market context

- Director-level architecture leads in the Geneva-Lausanne corridor, owning platform direction for a team of this size, typically land in the CHF 220,000-280,000 base range. The top of that band goes to candidates who are still hands-on, not purely a manager of managers, which is explicitly what this posting asks for.
- Nexthink's scale and the fact that AI integration is a named strategic priority, not a maintenance line item, both argue for the upper half of that range rather than the middle.
- Bonus for a role at this level usually runs 15-20% of base. Worth asking for target versus actual historical payout, since a 20% target that pays out at 65% most years changes the real number.
- As a PE-backed private company, equity is more likely a long-term incentive plan or phantom equity tied to a future liquidity event than a straightforward option grant. That needs to be priced and timed explicitly, not assumed to be worth a fixed percentage.

## Opening position

"I don't see a range listed, so I'd like to start there. Given the scope, owning architectural direction for the platform and leading six senior architects, plus the fact that AI integration is a stated priority rather than a side project, I'd be looking at a base around CHF 250,000, with bonus and long-term incentive structured on top of that. I'd rather anchor on a real number and adjust from data than negotiate blind."

## If they come in low

"I'd want to understand how this number was built, since Director-level architecture roles with team leadership and this much platform scope typically land higher in this market. If there's a banding constraint internally, I'd rather hear that directly so we can figure out where the actual flexibility sits, whether that's base, bonus target, or the long-term incentive piece."

Key move: don't treat a lowball as the market number without asking what it's benchmarked against, and don't let "Director" title inflation substitute for Director-level pay.

## Equity and long-term incentive questions to ask before agreeing to anything

- What form the long-term incentive takes: phantom equity, RSU-equivalent units, or a cash-settled plan tied to a future exit, since Nexthink isn't publicly listed and Permira's ownership structure changes what "equity" actually means here.
- Vesting schedule, cliff, and whether the plan pays out on partial or only full liquidity events.
- Whether there's an annual refresh or if the initial grant is a one-time number.
- Bonus target versus what the leadership team has actually been paid out the last two years, not just the headline percentage.
- What happens to unvested long-term incentive in a future acquisition or secondary sale, worth asking directly given I've been on the acquired side of exactly that scenario once already at Alaya.

## Geographic and hybrid framing

"I'm based in Geneva, close enough to Lausanne that the hybrid schedule isn't a friction point, so there's no relocation cost and no argument for a location-based discount. I'd expect the offer to reflect the Geneva-Lausanne market rate for a role at this level directly."

## Team scope and hands-on framing

"The posting is clear that this role stays hands-on rather than becoming pure people management. I'd want that reflected in how the role is leveled internally, since a hands-on architecture lead over six seniors sits differently on the comp band than a manager who's stepped fully back from the technical work."

## Competing offer template (use only if real)

"I'm further along in a process for a comparable Director-level architecture role elsewhere, with total comp tracking toward roughly CHF [X]. I'd rather make this work since the AI platform direction here is the more interesting problem, but the numbers need to be in the same range for that to be a genuine choice."

Only use this if there's an actual competing process in flight.

## Closing line for any round

"Send me the full package in writing, base, bonus target, and the long-term incentive structure together, and I'll turn around a decision fast. I'd rather negotiate once with complete numbers than go back and forth piece by piece."
