# Negotiation Script — Bluefish AI, Staff Fullstack Engineer

## Market Context

Bluefish AI is a Series B company (raised $43M in April 2026, total $68M). The role is posted for London/remote-Europe. At Staff level, they need someone with 8+ years and genuine architectural ownership. That's not a common profile.

Switzerland market rates for Staff Software Engineer (2026):
- Geneva/Zurich base: CHF 185,000 - 230,000
- Staff with AI specialization: CHF 200,000 - 250,000
- Total comp target (base + bonus): CHF 210,000 - 240,000

London equivalent for reference: GBP 130,000 - 165,000 base. If they quote London rates, push back with Geneva cost-of-living differential.

---

## Opening Anchor

If they ask for your number first, give a range:

"For a Staff role with this scope, I'm looking at CHF 210,000 to 220,000 base, plus equity that reflects the Series B stage. What does your band look like for this level?"

If they share a number first and it's below CHF 185,000, pause before responding. Don't counter immediately. Say: "Thanks for sharing that. I'd like to take a day to think about the full picture before responding." Then come back with a structured counter.

---

## Counter-Offer Script

If they open at CHF 160,000 - 175,000 (common for UK-based orgs underestimating Geneva market):

"I appreciate the offer. The role itself is exactly what I'm looking for, but I want to be honest that CHF [X] is below where I need to be for Geneva. My current comp is in the CHF 190,000+ range, and given the architectural ownership and cross-team scope of this Staff role, I was expecting something closer to CHF 205,000 - 215,000 base. If the base is constrained, I'd want to revisit the equity to close that gap."

If they push back on the Geneva differential:

"I understand you have a London benchmark, and I'm not trying to be difficult. The cost of living difference between London and Geneva is real, roughly 20-25% on housing alone. I'd be giving up that protection by joining. I need the compensation to reflect that, or I'd be effectively taking a pay cut to join."

---

## Equity Negotiation (Series B)

Bluefish AI has raised $68M total at Series B. Likely valuation: $200M - $400M range (estimate).

Realistic Staff grant at this stage: 0.05% - 0.15% (fully diluted).

Questions to ask:
- "What's the current fully-diluted share count, and what's the most recent 409A valuation?"
- "What was the Series B price per share, and what's my strike price?"
- "Are there refresh grants after year 2?"

Standard vesting terms: 4-year vest, 1-year cliff. Push for accelerated vesting on a change of control (double-trigger is standard, push for single-trigger if you can).

If the equity offer feels thin:

"I'm comfortable with the base, but the equity feels light for a Staff hire at this stage. Typically I'd expect [X%] for someone coming in at this level. Can you go back and see if there's flexibility there? Even a 20-30% increase in the option pool would make the overall package significantly more competitive."

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

If you have other offers in progress (or even exploratory conversations):

"I want to be transparent: I'm in late-stage conversations with another company. I'd prefer to join Bluefish given the technical problem and the team, but I need to make the numbers work. If you can get to CHF 210,000 and firm up the equity grant, I'll close this week."

If you don't have a competing offer, don't fabricate one. Instead, use timeline pressure:

"I'm moving on other opportunities in parallel and would like to close this by end of [week]. What's the fastest you can get back to me with a revised offer?"

---

## Geographic Discount Pushback

Some UK/EU companies apply a "Geneva discount" expecting you'll accept lower because you're remote. Don't accept this framing.

Talking point: "Being remote doesn't change the cost of living I'm optimizing against. My rent, taxes, and cost structure are Swiss. The benchmark I'm using is what a comparable Staff Engineer earns in Geneva, not in London."

---

## Signing Bonus

If base negotiation stalls, ask for a signing bonus:

"If you can't move the base, could we look at a CHF 15,000 - 20,000 signing bonus? It bridges the gap without affecting your salary bands long-term."

---

## What to Accept / Walk Away From

Accept if: base CHF 190,000+, equity 0.08%+ at current valuation, standard vesting, possibility of refresh grants.

Walk away if: base below CHF 175,000 with no equity flexibility, no visibility into cap table, or cliff longer than 12 months.
