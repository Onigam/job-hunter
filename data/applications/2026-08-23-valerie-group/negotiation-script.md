## Negotiation Script: Founding Full-Stack Engineer, Valerie Group

Context that changes the math here: this is an IC role, not a management title, so the "founding" label is about equity and architectural ownership rather than headcount. Valerie blends a capital fund, a 25-plus person growth team and an AI product (Neuro-Intelligence) around one multi-tenant platform, which reads as a funded, revenue-generating operation rather than a pre-seed bet, even though no round size is public. The listing itself doesn't state a location, but aggregator sites mirror it as an India-based remote posting, which is worth clarifying early, since a Switzerland-anchored comp conversation only makes sense if the role is genuinely open to a Geneva-based remote hire rather than benchmarked against a different region by default.

## Market anchor

- Senior/staff-level full-stack engineer, TypeScript/React/NestJS/Postgres, founding scope at a funded growth-stage company: base typically lands EUR 90,000-130,000 in a European hub, with meaningful equity given the founding label.
- Geneva-based remote senior engineer, benchmarked against Swiss cost of living rather than a lower-cost regional rate: closer to CHF 140,000-170,000 base equivalent. Geneva runs well above most European tech hubs on cost of living, and that's the number to anchor on if the role turns out to be genuinely remote-flexible rather than tied to a specific lower-cost region.
- Given 17-plus years of experience, a founding-engineer track record with an exit to a unicorn, and direct relevance, owning multi-tenant architecture from zero before, the target sits at the upper end: CHF 155,000-165,000 equivalent, plus an equity grant sized for a true founding technical hire rather than a standard senior IC package.

## Opening anchor script

"Given the scope here, owning features from architecture through production on a multi-tenant system, and coming from a founding-engineer background with an exit through acquisition, I'd expect a base in the range of CHF 160,000 equivalent, benchmarked against where I'm actually based, plus an equity grant sized for a founding technical hire. Before we get further into numbers, can you confirm whether the role is open to a Geneva-based remote setup, since some postings list this as India-specific?"

## If they come in low

"I hear the number. Is that benchmarked against a specific regional market, or a blended global range? I'm Geneva-based, and if the role is genuinely remote-first rather than tied to one region, I'd want the offer to reflect that rather than default to a lower regional band."

If they hold the line on base: "I can flex on base if the equity grant reflects the founding label and the scope, owning a domain end to end at this stage is a different bet than a standard senior IC hire. What I wouldn't want to do is settle below CHF 135,000 equivalent, that undervalues both the track record and the ownership being asked for."

## Equity notes

- Ask what the equity grant is priced against, the most recent valuation or an earlier mark, since a fund-plus-platform structure like Valerie's may value the technology arm differently than the investment side.
- Confirm standard 4-year vesting with a 1-year cliff, and ask specifically about acceleration on a change of control or restructuring, given the company's own business model involves taking stakes in other companies.
- Get written detail on what "founding engineer" equity means in practice here: percentage range, strike price basis, and whether it's meaningfully larger than the grant for a standard senior hire, since the title should carry real weight if it's not honorary.
- Ask whether there's a refresh cadence tied to milestones, since founding hires at fast-growing companies are the ones most exposed to dilution if refresh conversations aren't built in early.

## Geographic and remote pushback

"A few job boards list this role as India-based remote, but the original posting doesn't specify a region. I want to understand that before we go further: if the role is open globally or to Europe, I'd expect comp benchmarked against Geneva, where I'm actually based, rather than a flat rate set for a different market. If it's specifically India-based, that changes the conversation entirely, and I'd rather know that now than after several rounds."

## Competing offer, if genuinely in play

"I'm in later-stage conversations with another company at a similar senior or staff level, in a comparable comp range to what I've outlined. Valerie's model, the mix of capital and platform for founders who usually get passed over, is the one I find most interesting technically, but I want to be upfront about timing so we can move at a pace that works for both sides."

## Close

Get three things confirmed in writing before signing anything: the actual region the role is benchmarked against, since the listings disagree with each other, the equity basis and vesting terms for a founding-labeled grant, and how much of the multi-tenant architecture is still open versus already decided. A funded platform with a fund attached to it isn't a place to accept a first number without at least one round of pushback, especially when the location ambiguity alone could shift the base by a meaningful margin.
