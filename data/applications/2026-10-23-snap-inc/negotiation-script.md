# Negotiation Script: Snap Inc., Cloud Software Engineer (Specs, Zürich)

## The shape of this offer

The LinkedIn listing says Zürich, full-time, on the Specs team (Snap's AR glasses). The page header also showed "Boydton, VA" on one fetch, so **confirm with the recruiter that the role sits in Zürich**. No salary is posted, and **the level isn't stated**, which is the most important thing to pin down.

Snap is a public company (NYSE: SNAP), so there's no startup equity to argue about: no option strike, cliff negotiation or anti-dilution. The package is:

1. **Level.** Snap uses L3 to L7 for engineers (L5 Senior, L6 Staff, L7 Senior Staff/Principal). The title "Cloud Software Engineer" with no prefix suggests an L4/L5 opening. With 17+ years and a cofounder exit, the realistic ask is **L6**. Level matters more than anything else in this negotiation.
2. **Base salary** within the Zürich band for that level.
3. **RSU grant**, set in USD. Check the current vesting schedule (Snap has historically used schedules that aren't front-loaded the same way everywhere, so ask for it in writing) and whether refreshers are annual.
4. **Sign-on bonus**, cash, usually with a one-year clawback.
5. **Annual bonus.** Ask whether there is one at all at this level; at Snap a large part of compensation is in stock.

Two things specific to Snap:

- **Stock volatility.** SNAP has been very volatile. A grant fixed in USD at offer time converts to a number of shares at a reference price, so ask how that price is set (e.g. 30-day average) and model the package at a 30% lower share price too.
- **"Default together".** The posting says four or more office days a week. Geneva to Zürich is roughly 2h45 each way by train, which doesn't work four days a week. You'd be moving to Zürich or close by, so relocation support is a real line item, not a bonus.

Rough estimates. **Check every figure on levels.fyi (Snap, Zürich and London, L5/L6), Glassdoor CH, and with someone at Snap Zürich before any comp call.** Snap's Zürich office is smaller than Google's or Microsoft's, and data points will be thin.

Takeaway: at L6, aim for a base around CHF 225k to 245k and year-one total compensation around CHF 320k to 400k depending on the grant and sign-on. At L5 the total will likely be closer to CHF 230k to 290k, which is below the Google and Microsoft references.

Swiss points to raise:

- **RSU taxation.** RSUs are generally taxed as income at vest in Switzerland, at Zürich rates once you move. Ask how withholding works and whether Snap runs Swiss payroll directly.
- **Pension (BVG/LPP).** Ask about the employer contribution and whether it goes above the legal minimum.
- **Relocation.** Temporary housing, moving costs and a relocation allowance. Given four office days, this is non-negotiable in practice.
- **Notice period.** Three months is common in Switzerland. Make sure the start date and any sign-on payout account for it.

## Scripts

### Clarifying level early (recruiter screen)

> "The posting doesn't mention a level. With my background, seventeen years, a cofounded company that was acquired, and five years as a Senior Staff engineer, I'm looking at Staff-level roles. Is this one scoped at L6, or could it be?"

### When they ask for expectations

> "I'd rather settle the level first. I'm comparing against Staff and Principal roles in Zürich, including Google and Microsoft, so I'd expect an offer that's competitive at that level."

If pushed:

> "For a Staff role in Zürich, I'm thinking around CHF 255k base plus a meaningful RSU grant. I'm flexible on how base, stock and sign-on split, as long as the total reflects the level."

### When the offer comes in low

> "Thanks, I appreciate it. I like the Specs problem a lot; the developer platform for a closed Lens ecosystem is exactly the kind of thing I want to build. The total is below what I'm seeing for Staff in Zürich, though, mostly on the stock side. If the grant moved to around USD 450k and there was a sign-on near CHF 50k, I'd be ready to sign."

Then stop and let them answer.

### When base is fixed

> "Understood. Can we look at the RSU grant and the sign-on instead? And I'd like the relocation package confirmed in writing."

### On stock volatility

> "Since the grant is in USD, could you tell me how it converts to shares, and what the reference price is? Given how SNAP has moved, I'd like to understand the downside before I compare offers."

### Geographic discount pushback

If someone suggests a lower number because you're coming from Geneva or because Zürich isn't a US hub:

> "I'll be in the Zürich office four days a week like the rest of the team, and Zürich's cost of living is among the highest anywhere. I'd expect the Zürich band, not an adjusted one."

### Competing offer (real or in progress)

Google Zürich (Staff, Shopping Graph) and Microsoft Zürich (Principal) were both prepared the same day. Only mention what's actually live.

> "To be transparent, I'm in process with two other Zürich teams at Staff and Principal level. Specs is the most interesting problem of the three, but I need the package to be in the same range. Is there room to close that gap?"

If they ask for the written offer, share it only if you're comfortable, with redactions.

### Closing

> "If we can land at [level], [base], [RSU grant] and [sign-on], with relocation covered, I'm ready to accept this week."

Get everything in writing: level, base, RSU grant with share conversion and vesting schedule, sign-on and clawback, bonus (if any), relocation, start date and office expectations.
