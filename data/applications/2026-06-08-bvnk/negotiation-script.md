# Negotiation Script — BVNK Staff Engineer (Backend)

---

## Market Context

**Switzerland Staff Engineer market (Geneva, 2026):**

- Scale-up / Series B companies: CHF 170,000–210,000 base
- Total comp with bonus and equity: CHF 195,000–250,000
- Top tech benchmarks (Google, Meta): CHF 250,000–350,000 TC
- BVNK's Eastern Europe Tech Lead band (€95-120k) is roughly 50-55% of Geneva base rates — not a valid comparison point

**Cost of living context:**

- Geneva is consistently ranked top 3 most expensive cities globally
- Monthly rent (2BR): CHF 3,000–4,500
- Swiss income tax (Geneva canton, ~CHF 190k income): ~25-30% effective rate
- Employee social contributions (AVS/AI/APG): ~10% of gross salary
- A CHF 190,000 salary in Geneva has roughly equivalent purchasing power to €125,000-135,000 in London or Berlin

**BVNK context:**

- $50M Series B + Visa strategic investment (2025) — meaningful equity potential, credible liquidity path
- Processing $25B+ annually — revenue-generating, not pre-revenue burn
- Global hiring across EMEA suggests precedent for location-based comp adjustment (upward for Geneva)
- They offer "meaningful ownership in the business through an employee option scheme" — get specifics before signing

---

## Opening Anchor

Let them go first if at all possible. If pushed on expectations:

> "Based on my research on Geneva market rates for Staff Engineers at this level of experience, I'm looking at CHF 200,000–210,000 in base, plus a meaningful equity package that reflects the Staff contribution level. I'm flexible on the exact structure if the total comp makes sense."

Hold that anchor. If they push back immediately:

> "That's where the Geneva market sits for this role level. I want to make sure we're calibrated on the same frame before we get into specifics."

## Counter-Offer Scripts by Scenario

**Scenario A — They offer CHF 155,000–175,000 (London-equivalent or location-adjusted down)**

> "Thanks for putting a number on the table. I want to be direct: CHF 160k doesn't work in Geneva when you factor in the actual cost of living here. This isn't a complaint about the company, it's a math problem. The purchasing power is lower than London at this salary, and I'd be taking a real step back from my current situation. Can we talk about what flexibility there is on the base? I think CHF 190,000 is a fair number that reflects both the role level and the market."

**Scenario B — They offer CHF 175,000–185,000 (reasonable but below target)**

> "That's getting closer to where I'd expect. The gap between CHF 180k and CHF 200k on the base is real in Geneva terms. Is there room to close it, either on base or by front-loading equity to offset it? I'd like to make this work and I think we're close."

**Scenario C — They say "salary bands are fixed"**

> "Understood. If the base is capped at that level, the equity becomes the main lever. Can you walk me through the option grant size, the current cap table structure, and the vesting schedule? That would help me understand total comp more clearly before I make a decision."

## Equity Negotiation

### Questions to ask before agreeing to anything:

1. What's the current fully diluted share count, and what percentage of equity does the proposed grant represent?
2. What's the current 409A or fair market value per share?
3. What's the vesting schedule? (Standard: 4 years, 1-year cliff)
4. Are there acceleration provisions on change of control?
5. What's the exercise window after departure? (Standard is 90 days; push for 5 years or lifetime if possible)
6. Is there a right of first refusal on secondary sales?
7. What's the path to liquidity? (IPO timeline, acquisition interest, secondary market access?)

### Target grant for Staff Engineer at Series B:

- 0.1%–0.3% of fully diluted equity
- Or: 4,000–8,000 options at current FMV depending on grant structure
- At a $500M valuation, 0.1% FD = CHF 500k in strike value; at $1B = CHF 1M

### Framing the equity ask:

> "Given the Visa investment and the trajectory, I think there's real upside here. I want the equity component to reflect a Staff-level contribution, not a senior IC contribution. What's the typical range for Staff Engineers on option grants?"

## Geographic Discount Pushback

If BVNK applies a location adjustment that reduces your comp below London rates:

> "I understand the logic of location-adjusted compensation, but Geneva is actually more expensive than London on most cost-of-living indices — sometimes by 20-30%. I'd push back on any Geneva discount. If anything, the comp should be at or above a London equivalent, not below. I can send you a cost-of-living comparison if it would help ground the conversation."

Reference: Mercer Cost of Living Survey, Numbeo city comparisons.

## Competing Offer Leverage

If you have another offer at CHF 190k+:

> "I do have another offer on the table at CHF 195,000 base. I'm more interested in BVNK because of [stablecoin infrastructure specifics / the technical challenge], but I can't take a significant step down on comp to make that happen. Can you match or come close?"

If you don't have a competing offer but are in conversations:

> "I'm in active conversations with a couple of other companies at a similar stage. I'd prefer to close this one because [specific reason about BVNK], but I want the terms to be competitive enough that I can make that case to myself. What can you do to make this easy?"

## Timing and Urgency Management

If they push for a same-day or next-day answer:

> "I want to give this the consideration it deserves. I can give you a clear answer by [5-7 days from now]. Can you confirm that timeline works for you?"

Do not accept artificial urgency. A company that pulls an offer because you asked for a week to decide is not a company you want to join.
