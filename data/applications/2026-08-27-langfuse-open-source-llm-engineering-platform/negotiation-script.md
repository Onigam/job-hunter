# Negotiation Script, Langfuse (Backend / Product Engineer), Berlin / EU-remote

## Context to keep in mind going in

The posted range for these roles is 70,000 to 130,000 EUR plus ClickHouse equity, with roughly one week onsite in Berlin every two months, travel covered. That's a wide band, which usually means Langfuse is open to hiring across levels and pricing the offer to whoever shows up, not that the top number is reserved for a specific title. This profile is Senior Staff level with 17+ years, a founding engineer track record, and a company built to acquisition, so the target here is the top of that posted band or above it, not the middle.

Also worth naming honestly: the title on offer is Backend Engineer or Product Engineer, not Staff or Principal, and Langfuse is a small team (under 30 people pre-acquisition) that just joined a much larger one. Titles at that stage tend to be flat by design. The negotiation lever isn't the title, it's scope: whether this seat owns backend architecture decisions and mentors, or is a line IC role. Get clarity on scope before anchoring on comp, since a Staff-equivalent scope justifies the top of the band and a mid-level IC scope doesn't.

## Opening anchor

Base target: 115,000 to 130,000 EUR, or the CHF-equivalent uplift if the contract can run through a Swiss entity, framed around founding-engineer experience and the direct relevance of the observability gap this profile learned the hard way. Something like:

"The posting lists 70K to 130K. Given founding-engineer experience taking a platform to acquisition, and hands-on scars around exactly the observability problem Langfuse solves, I'd expect to land at the top of that range, around 125K to 130K, assuming the scope includes real ownership over backend architecture decisions rather than a pure execution role."

## If they come in low (below 90,000 EUR)

Ask what's driving the number before pushing back on it directly:

"Is that number reflecting a founding-engineer-adjacent scope, or is it priced closer to an early-career backend role? I ask because the responsibilities described, owning trace ingestion and developer-facing reliability, read as more senior than that number suggests."

If they hold the low number because of budget bands post-acquisition (a real possibility given ClickHouse just absorbed Langfuse's comp structure into its own), ask for the difference in equity or a signing bonus rather than walking away from the base number outright.

## Equity notes

Equity here is ClickHouse stock, not Langfuse stock, since the acquisition closed in January 2026 and ClickHouse just raised a 400M USD Series D at a 15B valuation. That changes the calculus from a typical early-stage grant:

- This is likely RSU-style or options in a company with a recent, well-documented valuation, not a speculative pre-seed grant, so ask directly for the strike price or grant value and the vesting schedule rather than accepting a headline share count.
- Standard 4-year vest with a 1-year cliff is the baseline to expect; push back on anything longer without a stated reason.
- Ask whether the Langfuse team has a separate vesting continuation from their original grants or whether everyone was re-issued under ClickHouse's plan, since founders and early employees sometimes get different treatment than new hires in a post-acquisition structure.
- Given the size of the round, this is a real asset, not a lottery ticket, so it's fair to weigh it meaningfully against base rather than treating it as a bonus.

## Geographic and remote arrangement notes

This is an EU-remote role with periodic Berlin travel, not a Geneva-based hire, so there isn't a geographic discount to fight the way there would be for a Switzerland-only listing. The relevant point instead is that remote-with-travel roles sometimes get quietly priced lower than in-office roles on the assumption of lower overhead for the company. If that comes up:

"I'd expect the remote arrangement to be comp-neutral, since the travel commitment and the onsite weeks mean this isn't a fully detached remote role, it's a hybrid one with extra logistics on my side."

## Competing offer leverage (template, use only if genuinely in play)

"I'm further along in a couple of other conversations at a comparable level, and I'd like to make a decision with full information. Could we land on a number this week?"

Don't use this without an actual competing process behind it. It's easy to spot a bluff, and at this experience level it costs more trust than it buys leverage.

## Bottom line walk-away

If the final number lands meaningfully below 90,000 EUR with thin, unclear equity terms, that's a signal the scope is priced as a junior-to-mid IC role regardless of what the title says. Given the founding and Staff-level background here, that's a reasonable point to say so directly and walk, rather than take a title mismatch and hope the scope grows into it later.
