# Negotiation Script — Zencoder Lead/Staff Platform Engineer

---

## Market Context

Zencoder is a remote-first AI startup (Europe). Compensation not listed in the posting. "Equity participation" explicitly mentioned — treat equity as a real component, not a footnote.

Switzerland-equivalent market anchors for Staff-level Platform Engineer:
- FAANG/large tech (Zurich): CHF 200,000-270,000 total comp
- Late-stage startup with strong equity: CHF 170,000-200,000 base + meaningful options
- Early/mid-stage startup (Series A/B): CHF 150,000-185,000 base + 0.25-0.6% options
- Walk-away floor: CHF 140,000 base (below this, equity must compensate materially)

Target opening anchor: CHF 185,000 base + equity, remote from Geneva.

---

## Opening Anchor Script

When they ask for expectations:

> "I've been benchmarking against Staff-level platform roles in the Swiss and European markets. For a hands-on Staff role at a company building at this layer — developer tooling, AI agent infrastructure — the range I've seen is CHF 170,000 to 190,000 in base. Given that this is fully remote and equity is part of the package, I'd anchor around CHF 185,000 base and then want to understand the equity component before thinking about total comp. Does that work as a starting point?"

If they say the range is lower:

> "That's below where I've been anchoring. I'm genuinely interested in the role — the problem space is the right one for me right now. To close the gap, let's look at the equity. If the base is [their number], what does the options package look like in terms of percentage and the current 409A or equivalent? That's where I can flex."

---

## Counter-Offer Script (if first offer comes in low)

If they offer CHF 145,000-155,000 base:

> "I appreciate the offer. The role is clearly the right fit — the platform scope, the AI agent angle, the IC-first culture. I need to be straight with you: that base is about 20% below where I've been targeting based on comparable Staff-level roles I've seen in this market. I'm not walking away from the conversation, but I'd need to either get the base closer to CHF 170,000-175,000, or have the equity story be compelling enough to bridge the gap. What flexibility do you have on either?"

If they push back on the market claim:

> "I understand the startup constraint. The comparison set I'm using is remote Staff Platform roles at Series A-C companies in Europe — not FAANG. If you can share the equity details, I can put together a more complete picture of total comp and see if there's a path."

---

## Equity Negotiation

Key questions to ask before evaluating:

1. What is the current number of shares outstanding (fully diluted cap table)?
2. What is the current 409A / fair market value per share?
3. What is the most recent preferred share price (if applicable)?
4. What stage is the company at? Has there been a priced round?
5. What is the vesting schedule and cliff?

Standard to accept:
- 4-year vest, 1-year cliff
- Standard option pool (ISOs for US-based companies, similar for Swiss entities)

Push for if possible:
- Double-trigger acceleration on acquisition (protects against acqui-hires where your vest resets)
- 90-day exercise window post-departure instead of 30-day (time to decide without a tax gun to your head)
- Early exercise option (Section 83(b) if US-structured, lets you start the capital gains clock early)

What good equity looks like at this stage:
- Pre-Series A or early Series A: 0.3-0.6% is reasonable for Staff-level
- Series B+: 0.15-0.3%
- Below 0.1% at any early stage: not worth accepting a base discount for

Script for equity conversation:

> "Before I can evaluate the full package, I need to understand the equity. Specifically: what percentage of the fully-diluted cap table does the grant represent, and what's the current fair market value per share? I'd also want to understand whether there's any acceleration provision in the event of an acquisition. Those three things tell me what the equity is actually worth vs. what it looks like on paper."

---

## Geographic Discount Pushback

If they try to apply a "remote discount" for being Geneva-based vs. SF/London:

> "I'm based in Geneva, which is one of the more expensive cities in Europe. The cost of living here is comparable to or above London. I wouldn't expect a geographic discount relative to a London-based engineer, and I'd push back on one relative to a US-based engineer unless the salary floor is meaningfully above the European equivalent. The market data I'm working from is Switzerland and European remote-first companies, not US onshore."

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

If you have a competing offer (or want to create urgency):

> "I want to be transparent: I'm in late-stage conversations with one other company and expect to have a decision by [date]. I'd rather it be Zencoder — the problem space here is more interesting to me — but I can't hold the process open indefinitely. If there's a way to accelerate on your end, that would help."

If you don't have a competing offer but want to convey urgency:

> "I've been deliberate about the roles I'm pursuing — I'm not in 20 processes. This is one of three I'm actively moving forward with, and I'd like to resolve it within the next two weeks if possible. Is that feasible on your side?"

---

## Final Decision Framework

Accept if:
- Base >= CHF 160,000 + equity >= 0.25% at early stage (or >= 0.15% post-Series B)
- Or base >= CHF 175,000 with standard equity

Negotiate further if:
- Base CHF 145,000-160,000 but equity story is compelling (>0.4% pre-Series B)

Decline if:
- Base < CHF 140,000 with weak or unspecified equity
- No acceleration clause and below-market base
