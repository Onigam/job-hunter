# Negotiation Script — Co-Founder, CTO at Marble (Industrial Intelligence Platform)

This is a co-founder negotiation, not a salary negotiation. The leverage points are different and the stakes are higher on both sides. The goal is to structure something that reflects genuine co-founder status while ensuring the runway to build is real.

---

## Understanding Marble's Standard Structure

Based on public information from comparable Marble CTO roles:

- **Incubation allowance**: ~€3,000/month during the residency/studio phase
- **Pre-seed investment at incorporation**: ~€250,000 when the company is officially formed
- **Founders' total equity**: ~80% of the new company at incorporation (Marble retains ~20%)
- **Marble's ask**: typically a meaningful equity stake (exact % not public) in exchange for the studio support, mentorship network, and seed fundraising help

The incubation period typically runs 6-12 months. During this time, you're building with the CEO co-founder, validating the market, and preparing for a seed raise.

---

## Geneva Cost of Living Context

This matters because it frames why the allowance floor is important.

- Average rent for a 1-2 BR in Geneva: CHF 2,200-3,500/month
- CHF/EUR rate is roughly 1.03-1.05, so €3,000 ≈ CHF 3,090-3,150
- That covers rent and almost nothing else in Geneva
- Zurich is comparable or slightly more expensive than Geneva
- Geneva COLI is among the top 3 most expensive cities in the world

**Bottom line**: €3,000/month in Geneva means drawing down savings or having a partner who earns. It's not sustainable for 12 months as the sole income. The ask needs to reflect this.

---

## What to Anchor On

### Incubation allowance
**Their likely opening**: €3,000/month  
**Your opening ask**: €5,500-6,000/month  
**Justification script**:

> "I want to make sure I can be 100% committed to this without a financial distraction running in parallel. Geneva is a CHF market, not EUR, and at €3,000/month I'd either need to maintain part-time income elsewhere or draw down savings aggressively. Neither of those serves the company well. I'm willing to take well below my current market rate, which for a Senior Staff or CTO-level role in Switzerland is CHF 180,000-220,000 per year. But €5,500-6,000/month would let me focus completely."

**Acceptable floor**: €4,500/month if they push back hard. Below that, the math doesn't work.

---

### Pre-seed investment at incorporation
**Their likely opening**: €250,000  
**Your opening ask**: €350,000-400,000  
**Justification script**:

> "The €250k figure I've seen for comparable Marble roles was designed around a different market and cost base. For an industrial intelligence platform targeting EU process industries, the first six months of real product development will require more runway than that before a seed round closes. If we're building data pipelines, physics-ML hybrid models, and even a pilot deployment, €250k goes fast. A €350-400k pre-seed would give us the room to run a proper pilot and show up to seed investors with real customer data, not a prototype."

**Acceptable floor**: €275,000 with specific milestones tied to the additional tranches.

---

### Equity split between co-founders
**What to expect**: Marble's 80% goes to the founders collectively. The split between CTO and CEO is a separate negotiation between you and the CEO co-founder.

**Target**: 50/50 between co-founders for a true co-founder relationship.  
**Potential pushback**: CEO may have been in residency longer and argue for a larger slice.

**Counter**:

> "If you've been in residency for three months before I joined, that's a meaningful contribution. I'd suggest we split 50/50 with your three-month head start recognized as a separate founding advisor stake or a slightly faster vesting start for your portion. But the base co-founder equity should be equal. CTOs who take a secondary equity position relative to CEOs in early-stage companies consistently underperform because the incentive structure signals that the technical role is subordinate. That's not the relationship either of us wants."

---

### Vesting schedule
**Standard structure to push for**:
- 4-year vesting, 1-year cliff
- Monthly vesting after cliff
- Full acceleration on sale/acquisition (double-trigger preferred: acquisition + role termination)
- Right to repurchase at FMV if you leave before cliff (not at grant price)

**Marble-specific consideration**: Ask explicitly what happens to your equity if Marble decides not to proceed with the company at the end of the residency. You want clarity that any IP you built during the residency either stays with the company or reverts cleanly. Get this in writing before signing.

---

### Competing offer leverage

You may not have a directly competing offer for a co-founder role, but you do have a clear opportunity cost.

**Script**:

> "To be transparent: I'm a Senior Staff Engineer at the CTO/Staff+ level in the Swiss market. The roles I'm looking at in employment terms are in the CHF 180,000-220,000 range. Taking this path is a significant financial bet on both the company and on Marble's support model. I've done the research and I'm comfortable with that bet. But I need the economics of the residency period to make sense on a cash-flow basis, which is why the allowance matters more to me than it might to someone earlier in their career."

---

## Things to Get in Writing Before Signing

1. **Equity agreement**: What percentage does Marble retain at incorporation? What are their pro-rata rights in future rounds?
2. **Residency exit clause**: If Marble doesn't proceed, what happens to the work, the IP, and the relationship with the CEO co-founder?
3. **Anti-dilution provisions**: Does Marble's stake dilute normally in future rounds, or do they have any protection clauses?
4. **Seed round commitments**: Does Marble commit to leading or participating in the seed round, or only to introductions?
5. **Monthly allowance start date**: Is it from day one of residency or after some probationary period?
6. **Tax treatment**: Is the allowance treated as salary (taxed) or as a stipend? In Switzerland this matters significantly.

---

## What Not to Trade Away

- Co-founder title and equity parity with the CEO
- Vesting cliff protection (1 year minimum)
- Full acceleration on acquisition
- Board seat or observer rights at incorporation
- Clarity on Marble's exit/follow-on process if they don't invest

---

## If They Say the Terms Are Fixed

> "I understand Marble has a standard structure that's worked well. I'm not trying to negotiate for the sake of it. The two things I genuinely can't make work at current levels are the monthly allowance in a CHF market and the pre-seed amount given the scope of what we'd need to build. If those are truly fixed, I'd want to understand what the flexibility looks like on timeline, or whether there's a path to bridge income for the first three months while I'm still transitioning. I want to make this work."
