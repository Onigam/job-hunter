# Negotiation Script: Ideawise, Group CTO

## Context that shapes the numbers

Ideawise is a venture builder of about 150 people with hubs in Berlin, Barcelona and Shanghai (plus Hong Kong and Düsseldorf entities). The role is remote-first on CET, reports to the COO and sits above every venture CTO. The posting says compensation is "discussed openly in the first conversation, including participation", so expect them to raise numbers early. Be ready with a range rather than waiting for theirs.

Two risks to manage. First, a remote-first European employer may benchmark against Berlin or Barcelona pay rather than Geneva. Second, "participation" across ventures can be worth a lot or nothing, depending on how it's structured.

## Market anchors (estimates, verify before the call)

These are approximate ranges from public salary surveys and recruiter data, not hard figures. Re-check on levels.fyi, Glassdoor CH, Michael Page and Robert Walters Switzerland salary guides before the first conversation.

| Benchmark | Base salary (approx.) |
|---|---|
| CTO / VP Eng, Swiss scale-up, 100-300 people, Geneva or Zurich | CHF 240k to 320k |
| Group CTO / CTO, holding or multi-company group in Switzerland | CHF 280k to 360k + bonus |
| CTO, Berlin or Barcelona scale-up of similar size | EUR 170k to 240k |
| Senior Staff Engineer, Geneva (current reference point) | CHF 200k to 250k |

**My target:** CHF 290k to 320k base, plus a performance bonus of 15 to 25%, plus meaningful participation.
**Opening anchor:** CHF 320k base.
**Walk-away floor:** CHF 250k base, and only if the participation is concrete and well structured.

## Opening anchor script

"Since you've said you prefer to talk numbers early, I'll be direct. For a group-level CTO role with authority over every venture CTO, I'm looking at a base around CHF 320k, with a bonus and real participation in the ventures I help build. That's in line with what group CTO roles pay in Switzerland, where I live and work, and it reflects scope across several companies rather than one. How does that compare with what you had in mind?"

Then stop talking.

## If they come in low

**Script A, base is below range:**
"I appreciate the transparency. That number is closer to what a single-company CTO earns in Berlin, and this role is broader than that: you're asking one person to own the shared platform, set standards across ventures and hire the venture CTOs. I'd need the base closer to CHF 290k to make it work. If there's a hard ceiling on cash, I'm open to shifting some of the gap into participation, but I'd want to see the structure in writing first."

**Script B, they say the band is fixed:**
"Understood. Then let's look at the other parts of the package. Could we revisit the base after six months against agreed milestones, for example the first two ventures launched on the shared platform? And I'd want the participation terms to carry more of the weight."

## Geographic discount pushback

They may say the role is remote, so Geneva pay doesn't apply.

"I get the logic for remote roles in general. For this one, the cost of living is real, but it's not my main argument. The pool of people who've built a platform from zero to acquisition and then driven adoption across teams they didn't manage is small anywhere in Europe, and you're pricing the role, not my postcode. I'm happy to be paid in CHF through an employer of record or a Swiss contract, whichever is simpler for you."

**Practical point:** as a Geneva resident, employment by a German, Spanish or Hong Kong entity raises Swiss social insurance (AHV/AVS) and payroll questions. Ask who carries employer contributions. If they propose a contractor setup, the day rate needs to cover social charges, pension (LPP/BVG), holidays and no paid sick leave, so add roughly 25 to 35% on top of the salary equivalent.

## Participation and equity

"Participation" in a venture builder can mean several things. Get clarity on each point before accepting.

1. **What instrument?** Group-level shares, virtual shares (VSOP/phantom), a carry pool across ventures, or per-venture founder equity. Ask which, and whether it's the same for every venture.
2. **Group or venture level?** Group-level participation diversifies risk across the portfolio. Per-venture equity rewards the ventures I personally help build. Ideally both: a group grant plus a small founder-style allocation in ventures launched on the platform during my tenure.
3. **Size.** Ask for the percentage and the fully diluted share count, not just a number of units. For a first Group CTO, a meaningful group grant is reasonable to ask for. Get their number first, then counter.
4. **Vesting and cliff.** Standard is four years with a one-year cliff. Ask for monthly vesting after the cliff.
5. **Acceleration.** Double-trigger acceleration (change of control plus termination without cause) is a fair ask at this level.
6. **Leaver terms.** Good leaver versus bad leaver definitions matter a lot in VSOP plans. Make sure a good leaver keeps vested units.
7. **Liquidity.** How does anyone get paid? Exits of individual ventures, dividends from the group, buyback programmes? A venture builder with profitable legacy companies (NetCircle, Compay) may have more realistic liquidity paths than a single startup, so ask about past payouts.
8. **Dilution.** Ask how new funding rounds or new ventures affect the group pool, and whether there's any top-up mechanism.

**Script:** "Participation is the part I care most about, since this role exists to create new companies. Can you walk me through the instrument, the size on a fully diluted basis, and how it's paid out? I'd also like to understand whether I'd get anything at venture level for the companies launched on the platform I build."

## Competing offer template (use only if true)

Never invent an offer. If there's a real one in play:

"I want to be straightforward with you. I'm in late-stage conversations elsewhere, with an offer at around CHF [X] base plus [equity]. Ideawise is the role I'd rather take, because group-level platform ownership is rarer. If we can get closer on [base / participation], I'd be ready to commit quickly."

If there isn't one: "I'm talking to a few companies, and I'd like to make a decision within the next few weeks." True, and enough.

## Other things to put on the table

- Title stays "Group CTO" with explicit authority over venture CTOs written into the role description.
- Budget for the shared platform team and the right to hire into it.
- Travel budget for regular time in Berlin, Barcelona and Shanghai, since remote-first doesn't mean never in the room.
- Notice period and start date that work with my current situation.
- Learning or conference budget, and hardware or tooling budget for AI work.

## Before the first call

- Re-check the salary bands above with fresh data.
- Decide the walk-away number and don't move it during the call.
- Prepare one question about how the COO currently makes technical decisions without a CTO, because that tells you how much authority the role really has.
