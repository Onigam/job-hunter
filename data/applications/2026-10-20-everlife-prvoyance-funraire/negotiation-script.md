# Negotiation Script: Everlife, Chief Product & Technology Officer

**Company:** Everlife Prévoyance SA, Villars-sur-Glâne (FR). Founded 2020, ~25 employees, growing. Pre-need funeral planning, claims 6,000+ families served (company figure).
**Role:** CPTO, executive committee member, hands-on at first, team of 2 developers to grow.
**Work model:** Fribourg office, up to 2 remote days per week. Commute from Geneva is roughly 1h15 to 1h30 each way by train.
**Salary in posting:** none listed.

---

## 1. Market reference (estimates, verify before the call)

These are working estimates built from Swiss salary surveys and comparable postings, not quoted data. Check them against Salarium (Swiss Federal Statistical Office), Michael Page / Robert Walters Switzerland salary guides, and any CPTO/CTO postings in Romandie before the first comp conversation.

| Reference | Base salary (CHF/yr) |
|---|---|
| CTO / Head of Tech, Swiss SME 20-50 people, Romandie outside Geneva | ~170k to 220k |
| CTO / CPTO, venture-backed Swiss scale-up | ~200k to 260k |
| Senior Staff / Principal Engineer, Geneva / Zurich | ~180k to 230k |
| Executive committee member at a regulated financial or insurance firm, Romandie | ~220k to 300k+ |

Fribourg pays below Geneva and Zurich, typically by around 10% to 15% for comparable roles. Everlife will know this and is likely to budget somewhere in the 170k to 210k band. The case for pushing above it: the role combines two executive functions (product and technology), sits on the ExCo, carries nLPD accountability for sensitive client data, and touches insurance products.

**Targets**
- Opening anchor: **CHF 240k base** + performance bonus of 15% to 20%
- Target landing: **CHF 215k to 230k base** + bonus + equity or profit participation
- Walk-away: **CHF ~190k base** with no equity, or ~180k if there's meaningful equity and a strong bonus

---

## 2. Opening anchor script

Let them name a number first if at all possible. If they press:

> "For a CPTO seat on the executive committee, covering both product and engineering and owning security and nLPD compliance for client data, I'm looking at a base around 240,000 francs, plus a variable component tied to the roadmap and platform goals we agree on. I know Fribourg isn't Geneva, but the role is effectively two executive jobs, and the people I'd be compared against for it are in Lausanne, Geneva and Zurich. I'm open on the structure, especially if there's equity or a profit share involved."

If asked about current salary, don't give a figure:

> "I'd rather focus on what this role is worth to Everlife. The scope here is broader than my last position, so my current package isn't a useful reference point."

---

## 3. Counter-offer scripts

**If they come in at 180k to 195k:**

> "Thanks, I appreciate the offer. I'm keen on the role, and I'd like to make this work. The gap for me is that this number reflects a head of engineering, while the role is CPTO with product ownership and a seat on the ExCo. Could we get the base to 225k? If the budget is tight this year, I'd consider 215k with a written review at 12 months tied to the six-month goals in the posting: shared risk assessment, delivery organization, agreed roadmap, regular releases."

**If they say the base is fixed:**

> "Understood. Then let's look at the rest of the package. I'd want the bonus at 20% with clear criteria, an equity or profit participation, and the commute covered with a GA travelcard. That gets us to a total package I can say yes to."

**If they offer 210k or more:**

> "That's close. If we can settle on 220k, plus the GA and the equity piece we discussed, I'm ready to move forward."

---

## 4. Equity and profit participation

Everlife is a private SA with ~25 people. There's no public information on outside funding, so ask first:

> "How is the company capitalized today, and is there an existing employee participation plan, or would this be the first?"

What to ask for:
- **Size:** 0.5% to 1.5% for a C-level hire at this stage, depending on valuation and how much is left in the pool. If they offer phantom shares or a profit-sharing plan instead, that's normal for Swiss SMEs; ask for the formula in writing.
- **Vesting:** 4 years, 1-year cliff, monthly or quarterly after that. Push for the cliff at 6 months given the six-month mandate, or at least partial acceleration.
- **Change of control:** double-trigger acceleration (sale plus loss of role) for at least 50% of unvested shares. Pre-need funeral planning is a consolidating sector and insurers are natural acquirers, so this matters.
- **Dilution:** ask for the fully diluted share count and for pro-rata rights in future rounds if any are planned.
- **Tax:** in Switzerland, employee shares can be taxed at grant or at vesting depending on the plan and canton (Fribourg for the company, Geneva for residence). Ask for a tax ruling or at least clarity on the plan structure before signing.

---

## 5. Geographic discount pushback

Here the pressure runs the opposite way to usual: they may argue Fribourg salaries are lower, and that's true for local roles. Responses:

> "I'm based in Geneva and I'd be commuting to Fribourg three days a week, which is around two and a half to three hours a day of travel. I'm happy to do that for the right role, but I'd ask that it be reflected either in the base or by covering the GA travelcard, around 4,000 francs a year."

> "The pool you'd hire a CPTO from isn't really the Fribourg market. Someone with platform modernization, AI-assisted development and executive experience is going to be comparing your offer with Lausanne, Geneva and Zurich."

Also negotiate:
- GA 2nd class (~CHF 4,000/yr) or equivalent travel allowance
- Flexibility on the 2 remote days (which days, and whether more is possible during deep-focus phases)
- Budget for conferences and training (~CHF 5k/yr)
- LPP pension plan: ask whether the employer contribution goes beyond the legal minimum, since at executive salaries this can be worth several thousand francs a year
- 25 vacation days minimum

---

## 6. Competing offer template (use only if true)

> "I want to be transparent: I'm in late-stage conversations with another company, a staff-level platform role in Geneva, in the 200k to 220k range. Everlife is my first choice because of the scope and because the work helps families at a hard moment, which matters to me. I'd need us to be in a comparable range to make the decision easy. Can we find a way to get there?"

Never invent a competing offer. If there isn't one, use the market framing in sections 1 and 5 instead.

---

## 7. Questions to ask before negotiating

1. What budget has the board approved for the CPTO role and for growing the team in year one?
2. How many hires do you expect in the first 12 months, and does the CPTO decide the profiles?
3. How is the rewrite resourced today? Internal only, or with an external agency?
4. How is the bonus measured for ExCo members?
5. Who are the insurance and financing partners, and does the CPTO own those technical relationships?
6. Is there a timeline or a target for a funding round or a strategic partner?

---

## 8. Final checklist before signing

- [ ] Base, bonus % and bonus criteria in writing
- [ ] Equity or profit participation terms in writing, with vesting and change-of-control clauses
- [ ] Title is "Chief Product & Technology Officer" and ExCo membership is stated in the contract
- [ ] Remote days and travel allowance in writing
- [ ] Notice period (usually 3 months for executives in Switzerland) and probation period (max 3 months under CO)
- [ ] Non-compete scope reasonable (limited to pre-need funeral planning, max 12 months, Switzerland)
- [ ] LPP plan details reviewed
