# Negotiation Script: VP of Engineering, Neural Concept (Lausanne, CH)

## Market context

Neural Concept is a Series B-ish deep tech scale-up (AI-assisted engineering simulation for automotive/aerospace/energy), based in Lausanne. VP Engineering at this stage, reporting into the CTO, with a mandate to scale past 50 engineers and manage managers, typically benchmarks as follows in the Swiss market:

- **Base salary**: CHF 190,000 to CHF 240,000 for a VP Engineering at a Series B deep tech company in the Lausanne/Geneva corridor. Zurich-based fintech or big tech VP roles run higher (CHF 230,000 to CHF 280,000+), but a well-funded EPFL-spinout-adjacent company like Neural Concept should be within striking distance of the lower-to-mid end of that band, especially given the executive scope (managing managers, GTM-facing).
- **Bonus**: 10 to 20% target bonus is standard for a VP-level role at this stage, sometimes tied to hiring milestones and delivery predictability rather than pure revenue.
- **Equity**: meaningful at Series B, expect 0.3 to 0.8% for a VP Engineering hire, depending on how early the round is and whether this is a founding-adjacent leadership hire or a backfill.

## Opening anchor

Anchor near the top of the range, justified by the specific overlap between the role and my background, not just "market rate":

"Based on what I'm seeing for VP Engineering roles at Series B deep tech companies in French-speaking Switzerland, and given the scope here includes managing managers through a 50-plus-engineer scale-up, I'd expect a base in the CHF 220,000 to CHF 235,000 range, plus a meaningful equity grant given the stage. I built and scaled Alaya from zero to acquisition, so I've sat on both sides of that growth curve already, which is a big part of why I think this number is fair rather than aspirational."

## If they come in low

If the offer lands meaningfully under CHF 200,000 base:

"I appreciate the offer, and I'm genuinely interested in the role. The number is a bit below where I'd expected to land given the scope, managing managers and driving the org past its current headcount is closer to what I'd call a VP-plus mandate. Would there be room to move the base closer to CHF 215,000, or make up the difference through equity or a signing bonus tied to the first year's hiring targets?"

If they cite budget constraints tied to their funding stage:

"That makes sense given where you are. In that case I'd want the equity grant and vesting terms to reflect the risk I'm taking on, plus a defined comp review at 12 months tied to the growth milestones we're setting together. Can we put that in writing as part of the offer?"

## Equity negotiation

- Ask for the fully diluted percentage, not just a share count, since raw numbers are meaningless without knowing the cap table.
- Standard vesting is four years with a one-year cliff; push back on anything longer than that at this stage.
- Ask directly whether there's an anti-dilution provision or pro-rata right built into VP-level grants, since Series B to C rounds can dilute meaningfully before an exit.
- If cash is capped, equity is the lever: "Given the base is fixed for now, I'd want to look at the equity grant closing that gap, along with a refresh mechanism tied to a future round rather than a one-time grant that just sits there."

## Geographic discount pushback

If they try to anchor lower citing Lausanne versus Zurich or a remote-friendly comp band:

"Lausanne and Geneva run close to each other in cost of living, and I'm not looking at this as a remote-discounted role since I'd be in the office on your hybrid schedule. I'd rather anchor on the scope of the role and the market for VP Engineering at your stage than a regional discount that doesn't really apply an hour outside Geneva."

## Competing offer leverage

Even without a live competing process, frame the negotiation around opportunity cost rather than a bluff:

"I'm currently the person cross-team architecture decisions get routed to at Benevity, so leaving that isn't a small step for me. I'd want the offer to reflect that this is a deliberate move into a bigger mandate, not a lateral one."

## Closing the conversation

"I want to be at Neural Concept, and I think the fit on both sides is strong. I'd like to find a number that works before we lose momentum, so let's find the version of this that gets both of us comfortable this week."
