Negotiation Script: EWOR, AI Co-Founder / CTO Fellowship

## Context worth naming up front

This isn't a normal salary negotiation, EWOR isn't hiring an employee for a fixed role, it's selecting a founder into one of two fellowship tracks and either paying a salary while the company gets built or offering a funding alternative instead. That means the real negotiation has three layers: which track fits (Ideation vs Traction), how the capital-for-equity math works, and what happens to Magino's own equity and control once EWOR is a shareholder. Public reporting (TechCrunch, LionHerald) puts EWOR's fund at roughly 60 to 68 million euros, backed by Paul Müller (Adjust, 1.2B exit) and Petter Made (SumUp), so the fund has real capital behind it, not just mentorship. Given Magino already has a proven track record (Alaya to Benevity acquisition), the Traction Fellowship is the better-fit conversation to steer toward rather than Ideation, even without a live company yet, since the founder history substitutes for early traction in a way a first-time founder couldn't argue.

## The two tracks and what they actually cost in equity

Ideation Fellowship: for founders without traction yet. Up to 300k euros in funding, and if EWOR invests, they take a flat 3% as a tuition fee regardless of the round's actual valuation, on top of which EWOR can invest up to 65k euros at a 1 million euro post-money cap plus another 235k on an uncapped SAFE with an MFN clause. The uncapped piece is the one to watch, an MFN clause protects EWOR if a later round prices better terms, but "uncapped" on the larger tranche means dilution isn't bounded the way founders often assume a SAFE implies.

Traction Fellowship: for founders with early revenue, users, pilots or IP already in hand. 500k euros for a flat 7% stake, no tuition fee layered on top. Simpler cap table, and worth pushing for directly given Magino's history is itself a form of traction even pre-launch.

## Opening position

"Before we talk numbers, I want to understand two things: whether my track record from Alaya qualifies this as a Traction Fellowship conversation rather than Ideation, and whether the salary-while-building option or the funding alternative makes more sense given I'd be starting from an idea stage technically, even with a founder history behind me. That'll tell me which structure to actually negotiate within."

If pushed to pick a lane: "Given I've taken a company from zero to a nine-figure acquisition once already, I'd rather be evaluated as a Traction-track founder on the strength of that history than sit in the Ideation tier's dilution structure by default."

## If offered the Ideation terms despite the track record

"The 3% tuition fee plus the uncapped SAFE tranche is a lot of dilution to take on before there's even a product, especially set against someone who's already proven they can execute from nothing. I'd want either the Traction terms applied given the prior outcome, or the uncapped 235k tranche converted to a capped instrument at a valuation we agree on now, not deferred to whatever the next round prices."

## If offered the Traction terms

"7% for 500k is a real conversation, not a fee dressed up as one. I'd want to know how much follow-on the fund typically puts in past that first check, what the pro-rata rights look like for EWOR in a future round, and whether the 500k is a lump sum or tranched against milestones, since tranching changes the effective runway math a lot."

## Salary vs funding alternative

"If I take the salary-while-building option instead of the funding alternative, I want clarity on what that salary is benchmarked against, Swiss cost of living or a flatter European number, and whether choosing salary now forecloses the funding path later or the two can be sequenced. Geneva isn't a discount market, salary benchmarked to a lower-cost hub doesn't reflect what it costs to build here."

## Equity and control on the founder side

Confirm standard four-year vesting with a one-year cliff on the founder's own shares, not just on EWOR's stake. Ask directly whether EWOR takes a board seat or just an information right at this check size, since board control at the seed stage is disproportionate to a 3 to 7% stake if it comes with more than standard investor protections. Ask what happens to the EWOR stake if the fellowship ends early or the founder exits the program, since unwind terms on an accelerator-style stake are often vaguer than a normal seed round's.

## Competing offer

Use only if real. "I'm also in conversations with traditional seed investors at a comparable check size with less structure attached. I'd rather build inside EWOR's network given the mentor bench, but the equity terms need to be at least as clean as what I'd get writing a standard seed SAFE elsewhere."
