# Negotiation Script, Converge (Founding Platform Engineer, NYC)

Context: seed-stage, raised $5.7M, roughly $2M ARR, four engineers today, NYC onsite only. Posted range is public: $180k-$240k base, 0.4%-0.7% equity, 4+ years experience expected. This isn't a blind negotiation, the range is known, so the job is to anchor toward the top of it and treat relocation from Geneva as a separate line item rather than something absorbed into base.

## Market anchors

- NYC market for a founding or first infra/platform hire at a funded seed-to-Series-A startup: base commonly $170k-$230k, equity 0.5%-1.5% depending on how early and how concentrated the ownership is. Converge's 0.4%-0.7% sits at the low end for a "founding" title, worth noting since the company already has 4 engineers and $2M ARR, this isn't pre-product.
- Geneva and Zurich comparison, used as a sanity check rather than a target: a Senior Staff platform engineer in Switzerland runs CHF 160k-220k (roughly $180k-$250k) with strong pension and healthcare built in. NYC comp needs to clear that bar once relocation costs, US healthcare, and the loss of Swiss pension parity are priced in, otherwise the move is a pay cut dressed up as a raise.
- $4B in annual revenue processed by 4 engineers is an unusually high ownership-to-headcount ratio. That ratio is the argument for equity at the top of the range, not just base at the top of the range.

## Opening position

"Given the scope, I'd be the first dedicated owner of a platform processing $4B in revenue and 20TB a month, that's closer to staff-level ownership than a standard IC infra hire. I'd want to open at $230k base with equity at 0.7%, plus a relocation package covering the move from Geneva, itemized separately from the offer."

## If they come in low on base

Script: "I understand the range is public, but a founding platform hire with sole ownership of the OLTP and OLAP boundary at this data volume is scoped closer to the top of the range, not the middle. I'd rather land at $225k-$230k with the standard equity than $200k with a nicer title, since day-to-day scope is what actually matters here."

## If they come in low on equity

Script: "0.4% at your current ARR and headcount undervalues what founding ownership of the platform layer means once the company is worth more. I'd ask for 0.65%-0.7% with the standard four-year vesting and one-year cliff, and I'd want the fully diluted cap table before agreeing to a final number."

## Equity mechanics to nail down

- Vesting: 4 years, 1-year cliff, standard; confirm nothing beyond time is tied to release.
- Exercise window: push for an extended post-departure exercise window, ideally 10 years rather than the standard 90 days, if the option pool allows it. I've been through one acquisition and know how fast a 90-day window forces a bad decision.
- Strike price and 409A: ask for the most recent 409A valuation before agreeing to a percentage, a smaller number on paper can still be the bigger dollar bet.
- Anti-dilution: standard weighted-average through the next priced round; confirm it isn't full-ratchet against employees while investors get better terms.

## Relocation and geographic discount pushback

Converge is NYC onsite only, so relocation isn't optional, it needs its own line item. Script: "I'm relocating from Geneva, one of the more expensive cities in Europe, so this isn't a discount move on my end, it's a lateral cost-of-living shift at best. I'd want moving costs, 60 days of temporary housing, and visa or immigration sponsorship handled directly, none of that folded into the base number."

## Competing offer leverage (use only if real)

"I'm also in later-stage conversations for a Staff or Principal platform role in the $190k-$220k range with no required relocation. Converge's scope is more compelling to me, but the number and relocation support both need to reflect that I'm giving up a no-move option to make this happen."

## Close

Ask for the offer in writing, base, equity percentage, strike price, vesting schedule, and relocation terms all itemized, before responding to a verbal number. Confirm the paid superday counts as compensated work, not just an interview stage, and ask directly whether the four-step process has a target close date so this can be timed against anything else in flight.
