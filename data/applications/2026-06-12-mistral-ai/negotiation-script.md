# Negotiation Script — Mistral AI, Applied AI Technical Lead (EMEA)

---

## Market Context

**Company profile:** Mistral AI raised ~€600M in mid-2024 at a valuation around $6B. As of 2026 the trajectory has continued; Mistral is the primary European frontier model company with serious enterprise traction. The forward-deployed team is a revenue-critical hire.

**Role level:** Applied AI Technical Lead is effectively a Staff/Principal IC with team lead responsibilities. This is not an entry-level ML role.

**Paris market rate (2026 estimates, Senior Staff/TL in AI):**
- Base: €160,000 to €200,000
- Equity: 0.05% to 0.25% at a company of Mistral's size, on a 4-year vest with a 1-year cliff
- Target total cash: €160-200k base is the floor for this level

**Geneva vs Paris cost-of-living delta:**
Geneva is ~40-50% more expensive than Paris on most cost-of-living indices (housing, taxes, daily expenses). If you're keeping Geneva as your base and commuting or relocating partially, that differential needs to be priced in. If Mistral wants Paris-onsite, relocation costs and the compensation gap are both on the table.

---

## Opening Anchor

When they ask for your number first (common in French startups), give a range with a floor they can't underpay:

> "Based on the scope of this role and EMEA Senior Staff equivalents in the Paris market, I'm thinking somewhere in the €185,000 to €210,000 range for base, plus equity that reflects the Technical Lead responsibility. I'm also factoring in the Geneva-Paris differential if we're talking about a relocation or commuting arrangement."

If they push back on the range:

> "The forward-deployed team is a revenue-critical function, and this role leads a team of AIs engineers as well as being individually hands-on. That combination typically commands Staff+ compensation even in a market that anchors on Paris. I want to make this work, so I'm open to a conversation about how equity and base stack together."

---

## If They Come In Low (under €150k base)

Don't reject immediately. Ask a clarifying question first:

> "Can you walk me through how the equity component is structured? I want to understand total comp before I react to any individual number."

Once you have equity details, if total comp is still below market:

> "I appreciate the transparency. The equity is interesting given where Mistral is headed, but at €150k base I'd be taking a meaningful step down from current market rates for this level. I need to be at least €175k base to make the numbers work, especially with the Paris cost-of-living adjustment if relocation is part of the picture. Is there room to move there?"

---

## Equity Negotiation

Mistral is pre-IPO and growing fast. Equity is genuinely meaningful here. Key things to clarify before accepting any equity offer:

1. **Strike price vs current 409A valuation** — at a $6B+ company, the spread between strike and current fair market value matters. Ask.
2. **Cliff and vest schedule** — standard is 1-year cliff, 4-year vest. If they offer 6-month cliff, that's a positive signal.
3. **Anti-dilution / refresh grants** — ask whether the company has a policy for refresh grants at tenure milestones (common at Staff level).
4. **Liquidity preference** — ask whether recent preferred investors have 1x or 2x liquidation preferences. At $6B valuation this matters for what common stockholders (you) actually see at exit.
5. **Secondary market** — at Mistral's stage, there may be limited secondary liquidity. Ask directly whether employees have been able to sell.

Equity counter if their initial offer feels thin:

> "For a Technical Lead role at this stage of the company, I'd expect to be in the 0.1% to 0.2% range on a 4-year standard vest. If the base is on the lower end, I'd want the equity to compensate for that. Can we discuss where you are on that range?"

---

## Geneva Relocation / Commute Pushback

The job posting says Paris onsite. If you want to negotiate a hybrid arrangement from Geneva:

> "I'm based in Geneva, which is about three and a half hours by train. I've operated effectively in cross-timezone distributed teams for the last five years, including with the North American org at Benevity. Would you be open to a hybrid arrangement where I'm in Paris two or three days a week? I'd be flexible on the cadence depending on what customer-facing work requires."

If they insist on full relocation:

> "I'm open to relocating to Paris for the right opportunity, but I'd want to factor in the relocation support and the cost-of-living adjustment into the comp package. Geneva costs run about 40% higher than Paris, so I'd need the base to account for that transition. Would you be able to include a relocation allowance in the offer?"

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

If you have another offer or strong interest from another company (ElevenLabs, Cohere, a well-funded Swiss AI company, etc.):

> "I want to be direct with you: I have another process at a late-stage company that's moving quickly, and I'd like to resolve this one way or another in the next week. I'd prefer Mistral for the technical mission and the team, but I need to see the offer to make that case to myself. Can we move the timeline up on your end?"

If you don't have a competing offer but want urgency:

> "I've been selective in this search and Mistral is one of two companies I'm genuinely considering. I'd rather work this out with you than let the process drag on. What would it take to close this week?"

---

## Quick Checklist Before Signing

- [ ] Base salary confirmed in writing (not verbal)
- [ ] Equity grant confirmed: number of shares, strike price, cliff, vest schedule
- [ ] Relocation package (if applicable): amount, timeline, whether it claws back if you leave within 12 months
- [ ] Role title confirmed: "Technical Lead" or "Staff Engineer" — this matters for future positioning
- [ ] Start date flexibility: if you need 30-45 days notice at current employer, push back on any unrealistic start date
- [ ] 6-month review clause: for a senior hire, a 6-month performance conversation with a comp review opportunity is reasonable to ask for
