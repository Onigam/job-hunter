# Negotiation Script — Ventura, Founding Engineer

## Context

Ventura published its range on YC's job board: $140K-$220K base + 0.5%-2.0% equity (San Francisco, in-person). This is a YC W26 company, extremely early stage. The range is anchored in the SF engineering market. Magino is relocating from Geneva and would need visa sponsorship.

---

## Market Framing

SF founding engineer rates for candidates with 15+ years experience and a prior acquisition exit typically land $200K-$240K base. At this stage, equity is the real lever: 1.5%-2% is the standard range for a founding engineer who joins pre-product-market-fit. The 0.5% floor is for more junior hires.

Given Magino's profile (co-founded and sold a company, 17 years experience, hands-on AI agent work), the credible anchor is the top of their stated range plus full equity on the upper end.

---

## Opening Position

**Base:** $220K  
**Equity:** 2.0%  
**Vesting:** 4 years, 1-year cliff  
**Relocation:** $15,000-$20,000 lump sum (SF rents run $3,000-$4,500/month for a 1BR; first and last months plus deposit is the immediate hit)  
**Visa:** H-1B sponsorship with timeline commitment

Script for opening:
> "I've looked at the market for founding engineers at this stage in SF, and I'm targeting $220K base with 2% equity. Given the relocation from Geneva and the visa process, I'd also want to discuss a relocation package. I know you listed the range publicly, and I'm at the top of it, which I think reflects what I bring from the co-founding and acquisition side."

---

## If They Come In Low (below $185K)

Script:
> "I appreciate the offer. The equity potential is real, and I want to make this work. That said, $[X] puts me below the range you posted publicly, and it doesn't account for the relocation and visa costs. Can we get to $200K base and 1.5% equity? That's the minimum that makes sense given the move."

Don't go below $180K base + 1.25% equity without a very clear equity story that compensates.

---

## Equity Deep Dive

Questions to ask before signing:

- What is the current 409A valuation and strike price for new option grants?
- What is the total option pool as a percentage of fully diluted shares? Is a refresh pool already authorized?
- Are these ISOs or NSOs? (ISOs are more tax-advantaged for US employees but may not apply cleanly on certain visa categories — clarify with a tax advisor.)
- Is there double-trigger acceleration on change of control? (Protects you if the company is acquired before your 4-year vest completes.)
- What was the last priced round valuation? What are the liquidation preferences?

Script for equity discussion:
> "The equity range you've listed is 0.5-2%. Given my background — I've been through a founding build and a sale — I'm expecting to be at the 2% end. Can you confirm what that number looks like on a fully diluted basis, and what the 409A strike is right now?"

---

## Relocation Negotiation

Ventura is in-person in SF. Magino is in Geneva. This is a real cost.

Reasonable ask: $15,000-$20,000 relocation allowance (covers flight, short-term housing for the first 4-6 weeks, deposit on a lease). Frame it as a one-time cost that removes a barrier to starting quickly.

Script:
> "I'm committed to making the move. To do that cleanly and fast, a relocation package of $15-18K would cover the immediate costs — deposit, first month, temporary housing while I find a place. I want to be on the ground quickly, not spending the first two months stressed about logistics."

---

## Visa Timeline

They listed visa sponsorship as available. Before accepting, confirm:

- Will they sponsor H-1B immediately or wait until the next lottery (April)?
- Can you start on O-1 or other categories while H-1B processes?
- Who covers legal fees for the sponsorship?

Script:
> "I noticed you list visa sponsorship. I'd want to understand the timeline — whether you'd start the H-1B process immediately and whether I can begin on an alternative status while it processes. Who typically covers the legal fees on the sponsorship side?"

---

## Competing Offer Leverage (if applicable)

If you have another offer, use it:
> "I have another offer at $[X] with [Y]% equity. I'd prefer Ventura for the founding role and the team, but I need to get to a comparable number to make it work."

If you don't have another offer, don't fabricate one. Instead, use market data:
> "Based on SF founding engineer comps I've looked at, $200K+ and 1.5-2% is the market for someone with a prior exit. I want this to be a yes, but I need to get there."

---

## Floor (Walk-Away Point)

Do not accept below:
- $175K base + 1.25% equity + relocation support + visa sponsorship with clear timeline

Below that threshold, the relocation risk from Geneva to SF isn't justified unless the equity story changes materially (e.g., they already have strong Series A traction with a credible $100M+ exit path visible).
