# Negotiation Script: Zalando, Principal Software Engineer (Zalando Payments)

## Market Context

**Role level:** Principal Software Engineer (hands-on IC with department-wide technical scope, reports to Director)
**Location:** Berlin, Germany, relocation assistance offered (currently based Geneva, Switzerland)
**Company type:** Public (Zalando SE, listed on Frankfurt Stock Exchange), Zalando Payments is an internal regulated fintech unit

**Berlin market ranges for this level (2025-2026):**
- Principal Software Engineer, general: EUR 110,000-140,000 base
- Principal Engineer, fintech/payments premium: EUR 120,000-150,000 base
- Zalando internal bands for Principal Engineer: roughly EUR 105,000-125,000 base per public comp data, likely understating actual offers for scarce agentic/payments skill sets
- Total comp target (base + bonus + RSUs): EUR 150,000-175,000

**Geneva/Switzerland comparison, use as a relocation anchor, not the primary ask:**
- Equivalent Staff/Principal roles in Geneva run CHF 175,000-210,000 base, which is roughly 60-70% higher in nominal terms than Berlin
- Swiss net take-home at that level, after lower income tax and no mandatory long-term care contribution, is meaningfully higher than the same nominal euro figure in Berlin
- This gap is a legitimate talking point when relocating from a high cost, high pay market into a lower nominal one, not a reason to expect Swiss numbers in Berlin, but a reason to ask for the top of the Berlin band plus relocation coverage that actually closes the gap

**Zalando-specific context:**
- Public company, so equity is RSUs with quarterly or annual vesting, not options with strike price risk
- Payments unit sits inside a regulated perimeter (PCI, fraud, reconciliation), which tends to command a premium over general platform roles at the same title
- The posting explicitly mentions "agent-augmented delivery" as a strategic priority; this is a specialization worth pricing separately from a generic principal engineer band

---

## Opening Anchor

**Use when they make a first offer or ask for your expectations:**

"My target is EUR 140,000-150,000 base, plus the standard Principal-level RSU grant. That's based on Berlin market data for Principal Engineer roles with a payments or regulated-fintech component, and it reflects the agent-augmented delivery scope described in the posting, which is a narrower skill set than a generic platform role. I'd also want to understand the RSU vesting schedule and any signing bonus before I land on a final number."

**Why this anchor works:**
- It sits at the upper end of the credible Berlin range rather than above it
- It separates base from RSUs, creating two levers instead of one
- It ties the ask to a specific, defensible reason (payments premium plus the agentic specialization they called out themselves) rather than a round number

---

## If They Come In Low (EUR 105,000-115,000)

"I appreciate the offer, and the role itself is genuinely interesting. That said, the base is below what I've been seeing for Principal-level roles with payments exposure in Berlin, which I've benchmarked at EUR 125,000-145,000. Is there room to move closer to that, or would the RSU grant and any signing bonus be structured in a way that closes the gap?"

**Follow-up if they push back:**
"I'm not fixed on an exact number. What matters to me is whether total comp, base plus RSUs plus bonus, can land around EUR 155,000-165,000 in year one. If we can get there, I'm ready to move forward."

---

## Relocation and Geographic Framing

If they anchor low citing Berlin cost of living relative to other German tech hubs:

"I understand Berlin's cost base is lower than Munich or Frankfurt, and I'm not asking you to match Geneva numbers, that wouldn't be a fair ask. But I am relocating from a market where equivalent roles pay meaningfully more, so the relocation package and the base both need to reflect that I'm taking a real step down in nominal comp to make this move. Concretely, that means I'd want the relocation assistance to cover a full move including temporary housing, and I'd want base at the top of your internal band rather than the middle."

---

## RSU and Total Comp Questions

Zalando is public, so ask these before weighing the offer:

1. "What's the standard RSU grant size and vesting schedule at Principal level, and is it front-loaded or even over four years?"
2. "Is there an annual refresh grant, and what's it typically been sized at for people at this level?"
3. "How is the annual bonus calculated, company performance, team performance, or individual, and what's been the actual payout percentage the last two cycles?"
4. "Does the offer include a signing bonus to offset relocation costs not covered by the standard package?"

**Framing for the RSU ask:**
"Since this is public equity rather than early-stage options, I'd treat the RSU grant as close to cash-equivalent in my total comp math. Given that, I'd want the grant sized at the top of what you offer Principal-level hires, especially given the specialized scope here."

---

## Competing Offer

If you have or can develop a competing offer:

"To be transparent, I'm in later-stage conversations with one other company at a comparable level, also in the payments and AI-tooling space. Zalando Payments is genuinely my preference given the scope of the agent-augmented delivery mandate, but I'd need the offer to be close to competitive to make this the clear choice. Can you get to EUR 140,000 on base with the RSU grant we discussed?"

If you don't have a competing offer, don't invent one. Instead:

"I've been deliberate about this search, and I'm not moving forward on offers below EUR 135,000 base at this stage, especially with a relocation involved. I'd rather wait for the right number than rush a move that doesn't make financial sense."

---

## On Benefits (fashion discount, pension, volunteering days)

Don't let these substitute for base or RSUs. If they lean on the benefits package to justify a lower number:

"The pension contribution and the extra PTO are real value, and I'd factor that in as maybe EUR 5,000-8,000 a year. The fashion discount is nice but it's not something I'd weigh into a base salary conversation. I'd rather keep the discussion focused on base and RSUs and treat the rest as a given at this level."

---

## Closing Move

Once you're close:

"I think we're at a place where I'd like to move forward. Could you send the offer in writing, base, RSU grant with vesting schedule, and the relocation package, so I can review the full picture? I'll come back to you within 48 hours."

Never accept verbally on the spot. Get the RSU vesting schedule and relocation terms in writing before committing to a start date.
