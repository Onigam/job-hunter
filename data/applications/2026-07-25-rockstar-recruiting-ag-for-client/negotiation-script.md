# Negotiation Script: Lonio (client of Rockstar Recruiting AG), Fullstack / Product AI Founding Engineer

## Context to keep in mind
This is a recruiting-agency placement, not a direct hire, so the first real negotiation likely happens through Rockstar before Lonio's founders get involved directly. The posted band on Adzuna and swissdevjobs is CHF 140,000 to 150,000, based in Zurich (Seidengasse 6), with B permit sponsorship for EU/EFTA candidates and a Claude Max subscription listed as a perk. That band reads like it is calibrated for a strong mid-level engineer stepping into their first founding role, not for 17+ years of experience with a company built from zero through to a nine-figure-plus acquisition. Treat CHF 150,000 as a floor, not a target, and expect the real conversation to move mostly on scope, title, and equity rather than base alone, since this is an early-stage company where cash is tight by design.

## Swiss market anchor
Founding and early engineers at funded Zurich AI startups typically land base salary in the CHF 150,000 to CHF 190,000 range once the candidate has 8+ years of experience and prior ownership at founder or staff level, with the spread driven heavily by how much cash the round actually has versus how much is pushed into equity. Given the founder background at Alaya, the acquisition outcome, and the fact this role is explicitly "own technical decisions end to end," the opening ask should sit at CHF 175,000 to 190,000 base, plus a founding-engineer equity grant sized well above a standard early-hire grant.

## Opening anchor script
"I looked at the range posted for this role, and I want to be upfront that it sits below where a founding engineer with 17 years of experience and a company built from zero through acquisition usually lands in Zurich right now. Given the scope, owning technical decisions end to end rather than executing against someone else's roadmap, I'd be looking at a base in the CHF 175,000 to 190,000 range, plus an equity grant that reflects genuine founding-team risk. Where does that leave us relative to what Lonio has budgeted?"

## If they come in low
If the offer lands at or near the posted CHF 140,000 to 150,000 band: "I get that early-stage cash is limited, and I'm not trying to price myself out of a role I'm genuinely interested in. What I'd propose is a base closer to CHF 160,000, with the difference made up through a larger equity grant and a defined six-month compensation review tied to whatever milestones the company is targeting. That keeps cash burn manageable now while making sure the upside actually reflects founding-level risk." If base truly cannot move, push the equity percentage and ask for a shorter cliff or an accelerated vesting trigger on a future fundraise or acquisition, rather than continuing to push a cash number a seed-stage company may not be able to offer.

## Equity notes
At this stage, ask directly what the last priced round was and what the fully diluted share count looks like, since a percentage number is meaningless without that context. For a true founding or first-ten engineering hire, a reasonable range is 0.5% to 1.5% depending on how early the company actually is versus how the job posting frames it. Standard terms to confirm in writing: four-year vesting with a one-year cliff, strike price and its relationship to the last 409A or Swiss equivalent valuation, whether there is any anti-dilution protection for early option holders, and what happens to unvested equity on an acquisition, since a single-trigger acceleration clause matters a lot more at a company this early than it did at Benevity.

## Geographic and relocation notes
Zurich is materially more expensive than Geneva on housing, so there is no discount to concede on cost of living, if anything the opposite. If the arrangement is Geneva-based with regular Zurich travel, ask for travel costs covered outright rather than folded into base. If full relocation to Zurich is expected, ask for a relocation allowance and a short transition period of remote or hybrid work while housing gets sorted, since Lonio is already sponsoring B permits for EU/EFTA hires and should have that process reasonably well worn.

## Competing offer leverage template
"I'm also further along in conversations with another company for a similar founding-level scope, and comp and equity structure are part of what I'm weighing between them. I'd rather land on the right package with Lonio directly than let this drag into a bidding situation, so if there's room on base, the equity grant, or the vesting terms, this is the point to use it."
