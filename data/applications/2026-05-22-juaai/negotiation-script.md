# Negotiation Script — Jua.ai, Member of Technical Staff (Backend & Infrastructure)

---

## Market Context

**Role level:** "Member of Technical Staff" is the Stripe/Anthropic framing for senior individual contributor. At a well-funded AI startup in Zürich, this maps to Senior/Staff Engineer.

**Zürich market rates (2026, Staff-level backend/infra):**
- Median Senior Software Engineer, Zürich: CHF 140–160k base
- Staff-level / ML infrastructure, Zürich: CHF 165–195k base
- Top-of-band at well-funded AI startups (Series A/B+): CHF 195–220k + equity
- Jua.ai is backed by institutional VCs (Promus Ventures); likely Series A/B stage

**Geneva penalty:** The role is Zürich-based hybrid. Zürich salaries typically run 5–10% higher than Geneva for comparable roles, partly because of the financial/tech premium and partly because of cost of living. Do not accept a "Geneva rate" for a Zürich role.

**Commute factor:** Geneva to Zürich is ~2.5h one way. If hybrid means 2–3 days/week in Zürich, that's a real cost (time and possibly accommodation). This is a legitimate negotiating point.

---

## Opening Anchor

If asked for expectations before they give a number:

> "Based on what I'm seeing for Staff-level infra engineering at well-funded AI companies in Zürich, I'm targeting CHF 185–200k base. That's before equity, which I'd want to discuss separately. Does that range work for what you have budgeted?"

If they give a number first:
- Below CHF 155k: too far off, ask if there's flexibility before countering
- CHF 155–170k: counter to 185k and explain the anchor
- CHF 170–185k: counter to 190k with equity ask
- CHF 185k+: negotiate equity and ask about comp review timeline

---

## Counter-Offer Script

**If their first offer comes in low (e.g., CHF 155k):**

> "I appreciate the offer. I want to make this work, but there's a gap I need to flag. The roles I'm comparing this to in Zürich for Staff-level infra are coming in at CHF 175–195k. Part of that delta is the commute from Geneva, which is a real operational cost for me on a hybrid schedule. Is there room to move on base, or alternatively, could we look at a more generous equity grant to bridge the gap?"

**If they say the budget is fixed:**

> "Understood. If the base is capped at [X], let's make sure the equity component reflects that. What does the option pool look like, and at what strike price were the last grants issued? I'd also want to understand the vesting schedule and whether there's an acceleration clause on acquisition, given your growth trajectory."

---

## Equity Negotiation

Jua.ai is a startup with institutional backing. Equity matters here.

**Questions to ask:**
- What percentage of the company do the options represent? (Always ask for %, not just number of shares)
- What was the last 409A / fair market value for strike price?
- What's the vesting schedule? (Standard is 4 years / 1-year cliff; push for monthly vesting after cliff)
- Is there single-trigger or double-trigger acceleration on acquisition? (Double-trigger is standard; push for single-trigger or at least partial single-trigger)
- What's the post-termination exercise window? (90 days is hostile to employees; push for 5–10 years)

**Equity ask:**
> "Given that I'm joining at a stage where the foundational infrastructure decisions will compound for years, I'd want the equity grant to reflect that leverage. What's a typical grant for this level, and is there room to go higher given the gap we were discussing on base?"

**Minimum acceptable equity target (rough benchmarks for Series A/B in Switzerland):**
- 0.10–0.25% for a Staff-level early hire at a well-funded startup
- If they offer below 0.05%, it's either early-stage restricted or the package is top-heavy on cash

## Commute / Relocation Leverage

> "One thing I want to be transparent about: I'm based in Geneva, so on hybrid days I'm looking at a 2.5-hour commute each way. That's manageable with the right rhythm, but I'd want to understand the expected in-office cadence before we finalize. If it's 3+ days per week, a travel stipend or accommodation allowance is something I'd want to put on the table."

---

## Competing Offer Template

If you have or want to create leverage from another offer (or have one pending):

> "I want to be straightforward with you: I do have another process at a later stage that I expect to land an offer from shortly. I'm more interested in Jua given the technical scope, but I don't want to be in a position where I have to decide without comparable terms in front of me. Is there a way to accelerate the process or get to a firm offer this week?"

If they ask what the competing offer is:

> "I'd rather not name the company at this stage, but it's a backend/infra role at a Series B AI company also based in Switzerland, and the range I'm expecting is CHF 180k+. I'm raising it because I'd like to give Jua a real chance to compete."

---

## Start Date Leverage

Standard notice period in Switzerland is 1–3 months. Use it:

> "My notice period is [X months]. I can start [date]. If that creates a gap problem for you, I'm open to discussing whether there's a signing bonus that could offset any overlap cost on my end."

---

## Summary Checklist

- [ ] Ask for total comp breakdown (base + equity + bonus target + benefits)
- [ ] Confirm in-office cadence before accepting hybrid label
- [ ] Request equity % of company, not just share count
- [ ] Ask about post-termination exercise window
- [ ] Confirm whether there is an annual comp review cycle and when the next one falls
- [ ] If base is below CHF 170k, do not accept without significant equity uplift or written comp review date
