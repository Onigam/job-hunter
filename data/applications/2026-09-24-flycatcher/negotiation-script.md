# Negotiation Script: Founding CTO, Flycatcher SA (Lugano)

## Context
- Flycatcher SA was founded in 2024 in Lugano by Dario Mantegazza, Lorenzo Galli and Elia Cereda, on top of 6+ years of IDSIA (USI-SUPSI) research into unsupervised visual anomaly detection.
- Stage: very early. Venture Kick Stage 1 and Stage 2 (CHF 10k, then CHF 40k as a convertible loan in April 2025), NVIDIA Inception, MassChallenge finalist, a pilot in transportation. No priced round that's publicly known. Assume pre-seed, with a seed round likely being prepared.
- Posted band: **CHF 80k to 130k base + equity**, full-time, CET +/- 2, Lugano office, customer site travel expected.
- The role is "first senior technical hire" with the CTO title, joining three technical founders. That's the key point of the negotiation: are you a hire with a title, or a cofounder who joined late? The equity has to match whichever answer they give.
- Cash is going to be tight whatever you say. The real levers are equity, vesting terms, a salary step-up tied to the next round, and travel costs between Geneva and Lugano.

## Market anchors (2026, gross annual, estimates)

| Profile | Base | Equity |
|---|---|---|
| Senior/Staff engineer, Geneva or Zurich scale-up | CHF 150k to 200k | small |
| Senior engineer, Ticino (Lugano market) | CHF 100k to 140k | small |
| Founding engineer, funded Swiss startup | CHF 130k to 180k | 0.5% to 2% |
| CTO / first senior tech hire, Swiss pre-seed | CHF 100k to 140k | 3% to 8% |
| CTO / first senior tech hire, Swiss post-seed | CHF 150k to 200k | 2% to 5% |
| Late-joining technical cofounder, pre-seed | often below market cash | 5% to 15% |

Ticino salaries run noticeably below Geneva and Zurich, and they'll know it. Your pushback isn't "Lugano should pay Geneva rates", it's that you're keeping a Geneva cost base while commuting, and that the job is CTO, not senior engineer.

Sources to cite if challenged: Levels.fyi (Zurich), Swissdevjobs salary data, Glassdoor Lugano and Geneva, Carta and Index Ventures (OptionPlan) data on early employee and CTO equity in Europe. These are estimates; check the latest numbers right before the call.

## Your target

- **Walk-away:** CHF 120k base with at least 4% equity, or CHF 130k with at least 3%, plus a written step-up at the next round. Below that, the risk isn't paid for.
- **Target:** CHF 130k base (top of band), 5% to 7% equity, step-up to CHF 170k+ when a seed round of CHF 2M+ closes.
- **Opening anchor:** CHF 150k base and 8% equity, framed around 17 years of experience, having taken a platform from zero to acquisition, and the CTO scope.
- If they genuinely can't go above CHF 110k now, trade cash for equity openly: every CHF 10k below target should buy roughly an extra percentage point, and the step-up has to be in the contract.

## Script: when they ask for your expectations

"I've seen the 80 to 130k range, and I understand where a pre-seed company is on cash. Here's how I'm thinking about it. I've done the zero-to-acquisition journey once as a cofounder, and what you're describing, owning the whole system and hiring the team, is a cofounder-level job. For the cash side I'm at about CHF 150k, and for equity I'm thinking in the range of a late-joining technical cofounder, around 8%. I'm flexible on the split between the two. Can you tell me how you're thinking about equity for this role, and what the cap table looks like today?"

Then stop talking. Let them answer first.

## Counter-offer script: if they come in low

"Thanks, I appreciate you being upfront. Where you've landed on cash is below what I'd take for a senior engineer role in Geneva, and this is a CTO role with site travel on top. I can move toward you on base if the equity reflects the risk. At CHF [their number], I'd need [X]% on a standard four-year vest, and a written step-up to CHF 170k once you close a seed round. Would that work for the three of you?"

If they come in at CHF 80k to 100k:

"I don't think I can make that work for a full-time CTO role, even with equity. Is there room to revisit this after the round you're raising? I'd rather find a structure that works now than start with something that doesn't."

## Equity: what to ask for

- **Instrument.** Ask whether it's real shares in the SA, options (ESOP) or phantom shares. Phantom shares are common in Swiss startups and pay out only on an exit, with no voting rights; they're worth less than real shares. At this stage, buying real shares at nominal value is usually the cleanest option, since capital gains on private assets are generally tax-free for individuals in Switzerland, while options can be taxed as income at exercise. Get a Swiss tax adviser to confirm before signing.
- **Percentage of what.** Always "% of fully diluted capital", including the ESOP pool and convertible loans. The Venture Kick loan will convert at the next round, so ask what your stake looks like after that.
- **Vesting.** 4 years with a 1-year cliff is standard. Ask for the cliff to be cut to 6 months, or for some shares to vest at signing, given the CTO title.
- **Acceleration.** Double-trigger acceleration: if the company is acquired and you're let go or your role is reduced, unvested shares vest. You've been through an acquisition, so this is a reasonable ask and they'll understand why.
- **Anti-dilution.** Full ratchet won't happen for an employee. What's realistic is a commitment that the ESOP top-up at the next round doesn't come only out of your stake, and pro-rata rights to invest in future rounds if you want to.
- **Leaver clauses.** Read good leaver / bad leaver terms carefully. A good leaver should keep vested shares at fair value, not at nominal value.
- **Governance.** Ask for a board seat or board observer status. As founding CTO you'll be making calls that shape the company; being in the room is fair.

## Geography: Geneva vs Lugano

"I'm based in Geneva and staying there for now. I'm happy to be in Lugano a good part of each week and on customer sites when needed. That means travel costs the company should cover, either through an expense policy or a fixed monthly allowance of about CHF 1,000 to 1,500 for trains and a place to stay."

If they push for relocation to Ticino: don't reject it outright. Ask them to put a relocation package on the table and push back on any Ticino salary discount, since the role and scope are the same wherever you sleep.

Also worth saying: Italian and German are listed as a plus. Don't claim either unless it's true. French and English are fine for most of what's needed in the first year.

## Competing offer template (use only if real)

"I want to be transparent. I'm also in conversation with [company] for a [Staff/Principal/Founding] role at around CHF [X]. Flycatcher is my first choice because of the technology and because I'd get to build the engineering side from the start. I'm not trying to start a bidding war, I just want a package that makes the choice possible. Can we find something closer to [target]?"

If there's no real competing offer, don't invent one. With a three-founder team this small, they'll find out, and it's the kind of thing that ends a founding relationship before it starts.

## Checklist before signing

- Base, equity %, instrument and vesting written in the offer letter
- Seed-round step-up in writing, with the trigger defined (amount raised, not "when things improve")
- Acceleration and leaver clauses read, ideally by a lawyer
- Current cap table and convertible loan terms seen
- Travel policy between Geneva and Lugano agreed
- Title "CTO" (or "Co-founder & CTO") and reporting line confirmed
- Notice period and non-compete checked against Swiss law (non-competes need a limited scope to be enforceable)
