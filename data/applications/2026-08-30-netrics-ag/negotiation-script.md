## Negotiation Script: Chief Technology Officer, Netrics AG

Context that changes the math here: this isn't a single-company CTO role, it's a group-level executive seat covering Netrics, GARAIO, and Solify, reporting directly to the CEO with a seat on executive management, plus technology due diligence for future acquisitions. That's closer to a Chief Technology Officer of a multi-entity group than a startup CTO title, and comp should be anchored there, not against a single 20-person engineering team. The posting doesn't disclose salary, ownership structure, or whether there's private equity behind the group's acquisition strategy, all three need to be surfaced early since they change the shape of the offer. Location is Biel, which is a lower cost-of-living market than Geneva or Zurich, but I'm Geneva-based, so relocation or a hybrid arrangement is the first thing to align on before numbers.

## Market anchor

- Swiss CTO / VP Engineering roles at mid-size firms (100-400 employees), single entity, individual contributor scope: base typically CHF 180,000-220,000.
- Group-level CTO with executive management membership, multi-entity architecture governance, and M&A due diligence responsibility: base typically CHF 220,000-280,000, plus a bonus or long-term incentive tied to group performance given the acquisition strategy.
- Biel/Bern labor market runs below Zurich or Geneva on local benchmarks, but the scope here, executive team membership, group-wide strategy, and due diligence across multiple companies, argues for pricing against the role's actual scope rather than the regional office location.
- Given 17-plus years of experience, a founding-CTO track record with an exit to a unicorn, and direct experience integrating an acquired company's platform into a much larger one, the target is the upper half of that band: CHF 240,000-260,000 base, plus a bonus structure of 15-25% tied to group technology milestones.

## Opening anchor script

"Given the scope here, group-wide technology strategy across Netrics, GARAIO, and Solify, a seat on executive management, and due diligence responsibility for future acquisitions, I'd expect a base around CHF 250,000, plus a bonus structure tied to group milestones rather than a single entity's numbers. Before we get further into specifics, can you walk me through how comp and any long-term incentive plan are structured for this seat, and whether there's investor or PE involvement in the acquisition strategy that shapes how that's built?"

## If they come in low

"I want to understand where that number is anchored, is it benchmarked against a single-entity CTO role, or against the group-executive scope described in the posting? The due diligence and multi-entity governance responsibilities are usually priced closer to a VP-of-Engineering-plus role at a larger organization, and I'd want the offer to reflect that rather than default to a regional or single-company band."

If they hold the line on base: "I can be flexible on base if the bonus or long-term incentive plan reflects the group's growth trajectory, taking on architecture governance and technology due diligence across several companies is a different risk profile than running one team's roadmap. What I wouldn't want to do is settle meaningfully below CHF 220,000 base, that undervalues both the track record and the executive-level scope of the seat."

## Equity or long-term incentive notes

- Ask directly whether Netrics AG has outside investors or private equity backing the acquisition strategy, since that determines whether there's an equity pool, phantom shares, or a cash-only bonus structure, the posting gives no signal either way.
- If there's an equity or phantom-share component, get the valuation basis in writing, and ask whether it's tied to the group's consolidated value or to individual entity performance given the multi-company structure.
- Confirm vesting terms if equity is offered: standard four-year vesting with a one-year cliff is the Swiss norm, and ask about acceleration if the group is itself acquired or restructured, which is plausible for a company that's actively rolling up smaller firms.
- If it's a bonus rather than equity, get the milestones defined precisely, revenue, integration completion, or specific platform metrics, so it isn't a discretionary number decided after the fact.

## Geographic and relocation pushback

"I'm Geneva-based today. Given the executive scope of this role, I'd expect either a relocation package toward Biel or a hybrid structure with a defined on-site cadence, similar to how other group-level executives who don't live near headquarters are typically supported. I want to make sure we're aligned on which of those two paths this is before we finalize numbers, since it affects both the offer structure and how I'd plan the transition."

## Competing offer, if genuinely in play

"I'm further along in conversations with another company at a comparable executive or Staff-plus level, in a similar comp range to what I've outlined. The scope here, building a shared platform across multiple companies while the group actively acquires more, is the part that stands out to me, but I want to be upfront about timing so we can move at a pace that works for both sides."

## Close

A group-executive CTO seat with M&A due diligence responsibility and a reporting line straight to the CEO is not a role to price against a single-company benchmark. Get the base, the bonus or long-term incentive structure and what it's tied to, whether there's equity or phantom shares given the acquisition strategy, and the relocation or hybrid arrangement, all confirmed in writing before signing. The ownership-structure question, whether there's PE or outside capital behind the roll-up, is the one most likely to reshape the whole conversation if raised early rather than assumed.
