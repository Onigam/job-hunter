# Negotiation Script — Jua / Member of the Technical Staff

## Market Context

**Jua's stated range:** CHF 125,000 – 160,000/year
**Role level:** Member of Technical Staff (early senior, architecture influence)
**Location premium:** Zürich hybrid; Magino is Geneva-based

Switzerland market data for Staff-level backend engineers (2025-2026):
- Geneva/Zürich, 10+ years, Staff/Principal: CHF 155,000 – 210,000
- Early-stage AI startup with funding: typically 10–20% below mature-company rates, offset by equity
- Geneva to Zürich hybrid: adds real commute overhead (2.5h round trip, CHF 80–120/day in travel costs)

At 17 years of experience and a founding engineer credential validated by a unicorn acquisition, Magino sits at the top of, or above, their stated band. The range ceiling of CHF 160k is the floor to negotiate from, not the target.

---

## Opening Anchor

When they ask about expectations, anchor above the range:

> "Based on my experience level and comparable roles in the Zürich market, I'm thinking around CHF 175,000 to 185,000 base. I'm aware your posted range tops out at CHF 160k, and I'm open to discussing equity that bridges that gap if base is constrained at an early stage."

Why this works: it anchors high, signals flexibility toward equity, and doesn't blow up the conversation. It also shows you've read the posting.

---

## If They Come Back at CHF 160k (Their Ceiling)

Don't accept immediately. Take a beat, then:

> "I appreciate the offer. CHF 160k is within the range you posted, though at the lower end of what I see for this experience level in the Zürich market. I want to make this work: can we look at the equity component? If the base is constrained, I'd want to make sure the equity reflects the level I'm coming in at."

Then ask for:
- Grant size as a percentage of fully diluted shares (target: 0.15–0.35% for this level at early stage)
- Current valuation and last funding round (to sanity-check what the equity is actually worth)
- Vesting schedule confirmation

---

## Equity Negotiation

**Standard ask for this role at an early-stage AI company:**
- 0.15% – 0.35% of fully diluted shares (push toward 0.3% given experience level)
- 4-year vesting, 1-year cliff: standard, don't accept anything shorter on the cliff
- Acceleration clause: ask for single-trigger acceleration on acquisition (at least 12 months vested immediately on change of control)

**Questions to ask before accepting any equity:**
- "What was the company's last valuation?"
- "How much of the option pool is remaining, and what percentage am I looking at on a fully diluted basis?"
- "What's the current preferred share liquidation preference?"
- "Is there a right of first refusal on secondary sales?"

**If they can't answer these clearly:** that itself is information. A company that can't explain its cap table clearly is one where equity is likely worth less than it appears.

---

## Geneva to Zürich Commute Pushback

The role is hybrid. Geneva to Zürich is roughly 2.5h round trip by train. At two or three days per week, that's 5–7 hours of travel and CHF 80–120 per trip in rail costs.

If they won't move on base or equity, this is a legitimate line:

> "Given I'd be commuting from Geneva for hybrid days, the travel overhead is real: around CHF 6,000–8,000 per year in rail passes and 6–8 hours per week. I'd want either a remote-first structure, a commute allowance, or for that to factor into the base discussion."

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

If you have another offer in hand (or close to it), use it:

> "I want to be transparent: I'm in final conversations with [Company], and their package is in the CHF [X] range. Jua is my first choice given the mission and the stage, but I need the numbers to be close enough to justify the choice."

If you don't have another offer, don't fabricate one. Instead:

> "I've been speaking with a few companies, and the range I'm seeing for this experience level in Switzerland is consistently above CHF 160k. I want to be at Jua, but I also can't take a significant step back on comp without a clear reason to."

---

## Sign-On Bonus as Bridge

If base is stuck and equity takes time to negotiate:

> "Would you consider a sign-on to bridge the gap in year one? Even CHF 15,000–20,000 helps close the difference between your base ceiling and market rate without changing your recurring cost structure."

---

## Red Lines

Don't accept:
- Base below CHF 155,000 without meaningful equity offset
- Equity with no cliff (cliff protects both sides; a company that won't offer it is a flag)
- A role without clear answers on the cap table
- Hybrid without a commute allowance if they're requiring 3+ days in Zürich

---

## Tone Notes

Jua's culture is described as "radically transparent and truth-seeking." Lean into directness: state your number, explain the reasoning, and don't hedge excessively. They will likely respect clarity over softening language.
