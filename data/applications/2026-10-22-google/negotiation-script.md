# Negotiation Script: Google, Principal Engineer, Hybrid Cloud Environment (Zürich)

## The shape of this offer

The LinkedIn listing puts the role in Zürich, with San Jose, Munich, Kirkland and New York as alternatives, and marks it "Director/VP or equivalent". At Google, "Principal Engineer" is usually **L8**, and some reqs are open at **L7 (Senior Staff)**. The posted ranges are for Germany (base €232k to €237k) and the US (base $307k to $427k), each with a 30% bonus target plus equity. No Zürich range is listed. Pay is base, an annual bonus, and RSUs (GOOG) with yearly refreshers. There's no startup equity, so cliffs, VSOPs and anti-dilution don't apply. The levers are level, the initial RSU grant and the sign-on.

Three things to settle early:

1. **Be realistic about level.** The minimum bar is about 15 years in large-scale distributed cloud infrastructure, policy engines or control planes, plus a track record of setting multi-year architecture for senior engineers. Magino has 17 years of platform work and five as Senior Staff, but no hyperscaler control plane experience. Google's committee may well land on L7 rather than L8. Ask the recruiter early whether the req supports L7 and L8, and decide whether L7 is acceptable before investing in a long loop.
2. **Office days.** Magino lives in Geneva, and Zürich is about 2h45 by train. Ask how many on-site days a week are expected.
3. **Fit check.** The profile prefers startups and scale-ups and avoids large legacy enterprises. Google isn't legacy, but it's very large. The mandate (policy compiler, agent hosting, zero-trust control plane) fits Magino's interests well. Decide before the loop whether that outweighs the size.

These are approximations for Google Zürich and comparable Swiss roles. **Check every figure on levels.fyi (Google, Zürich, L7/L8), Glassdoor CH and with the recruiter before any call.** Swiss bands are usually above German ones, so the Germany range in the posting is a floor, not a ceiling.

The takeaway: L7 and L8 at Google Zürich differ by roughly CHF 150k to 250k a year, which is far more than anything negotiable inside one band. Levelling matters most. Base bands are tight, so most of the room is in RSUs and sign-on.

Swiss specifics: ask about pension (BVG/LPP) contributions above the legal minimum. RSUs are taxed as income at vest at the rate of the canton of residence, so Geneva rates apply if Magino stays put.

## Vesting and refreshers

Recent Google grants have vested monthly or quarterly with no one-year cliff, and the schedule has changed over time (both front-loaded and flat versions have existed). **Confirm the current schedule.** If year one is light, size the sign-on to fill the gap. Ask what a typical refresher is at the offered level, because refreshers carry total comp in years three and four.

## Scripts

### When the recruiter asks for expectations

> "I'd like to agree on level before we talk numbers. The role is posted as Principal, and the mandate is a multi-year direction for HCE. Can you confirm whether the req is open at L8, and share the Zürich band? The Germany range in the posting is a useful reference, but I'd expect Zürich to sit above it."

If they push for a number:

> "For L8 in Zürich, I'm thinking around CHF 750k in total compensation a year. I'm flexible on the split between base, stock and sign-on."

### If they come in low (for example L8 at CHF 290k base, CHF 1M RSUs, no sign-on)

> "Thanks, I want to make this work. The base is close. The gap is the equity. What I see for L8 in Zürich is closer to CHF 1.4M over four years. If you can move the grant there and add a sign-on for the first year, I'm ready to say yes."

### If they offer L7

> "I appreciate it, and to be honest my concern is the level more than the money. The role is scoped as Principal: owning the HCE roadmap and resolving disputes between Core and Product Areas. What would the committee need to see to reconsider L8? If L7 is final, I'd want the top of the band and a defined promotion review at twelve to eighteen months, in writing."

### Geneva versus Zürich

There's no geographic discount to resist. Zürich bands are among the highest in Europe, and a Geneva address doesn't lower them. Use the commute as a lever instead:

> "I live in Geneva, so office days have a real cost for me. I'm not asking for a different band, but I'd like travel support or a relocation allowance included."

If they suggest Munich instead, don't accept the German band. Zürich is the base location and Magino is Swiss-resident.

### Competing offer template

Only use this if there's a real process behind it. Google recruiters verify, and a bluff can backfire in a committee-based process.

> "To be transparent, I'm at offer stage with [company] for a [Principal / VP Engineering] role at about CHF [X] total. Google is my first choice because of the HCE mandate, especially hosting agents under bounded credentials. If the equity can get closer to CHF [Y], I can sign this week."

Credible comparisons: other big tech offices in Zürich, AI labs with a Zürich presence, and well-funded Swiss scale-ups hiring principal engineers or VPs of Engineering.

## Before signing

Get the following in writing: confirmed level, base, bonus target, RSU grant in CHF and in shares, vesting schedule, sign-on amount and clawback terms, expected office days, and the promotion review commitment if the offer is L7.
