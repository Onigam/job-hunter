# Negotiation Script: Taurus SA, Platform Engineer / DevOps Engineer

---

## Market Context

**Role type:** The posting is scoped as a 2-3 year, hands-on ops role (SRE fundamentals, Kubernetes/OpenShift, Ansible/Terraform, Python, Go/Java exposure) at a regulated digital asset infrastructure firm. Taurus has raised roughly USD 65M from investors including Credit Suisse and Deutsche Bank, holds a FINMA license, and just secured a MiFID license for its Cyprus entity in May 2026 to expand EU-regulated custody services. This is a funded, revenue-generating scale-up, not an early seed startup, so there's real budget room even if the posted level is junior.

**Switzerland Platform/DevOps/SRE market rates (2026):**
- DevOps Engineer, national average: CHF 106k-111k, typical range CHF 95k-130k
- DevOps Engineer, Geneva: CHF 114k average, senior (8+ yrs) CHF 141k
- Platform Engineer, Geneva: CHF 128k average, senior (8+ yrs) CHF 145k
- Senior DevOps Engineer, Zurich: CHF 137k average, range CHF 127k-160k
- Cloud/Kubernetes/SRE specialization premium: +CHF 10k-25k on top of base bands

**Level mismatch to address directly:** The posting is written for a 2-3 year profile, and Magino has 17+ years including a founding-engineer track record and a Staff-level role at Benevity post-acquisition. Going in at the posted band would be underselling the actual work he'd do. The negotiation goal is to reframe the conversation toward a senior or staff-adjacent platform/SRE scope, not just accept the junior title and band.

**Geneva/Zurich/Lausanne:** Job is open across all three. Geneva is Magino's base and the most expensive of the three cities; use that as the cash floor regardless of which office ends up being the hub.

---

## Opening Position

**Target package:** CHF 145k-165k base, reflecting senior platform/SRE scope rather than the posted junior band, plus standard Swiss benefits (pension fund above LPP minimum, meal allowance if on-site).

**Opening line, when asked for salary expectations:**

> "Before we talk numbers, I want to flag that the posting reads as a 2-3 year profile, and I'm coming at this with 17 years including building and operating infrastructure as a founding engineer through an acquisition. If the scope ends up matching that level of ownership, I'd be looking at something in the CHF 145-165k range. If the role really is scoped closer to the junior band, I'd rather know that upfront so we're both working from the same expectation."

---

## If They Come In Low

**Scenario:** They anchor to the posted junior band, CHF 95-115k.

**Response:**

> "I get why that's the anchor if the req was written for a 2-3 year hire. Here's my concern: at that level I'd be doing senior-scope work, on-call ownership, architecture input, cross-team infra decisions, for a junior number. I'd rather we either adjust the title and band to match what I'd actually be doing, or talk about a structured review at 6 months tied to specific ownership milestones, with the comp gap closed at that point in writing."

**Absolute floor:** CHF 130k base in Geneva, CHF 138k if the role is Zurich-based, given the higher local comp bar there. Below that, the level mismatch isn't worth it even with a strong mission fit.

---

## Equity / Options Notes

Taurus is private and VC-backed rather than pre-seed, so equity, if offered, is more likely structured as standard employee stock options than founder-style grants.

**Key terms to check:**
1. **Strike price and 409A-equivalent valuation basis** given the last raise (~USD 65M round), so the options aren't priced against an inflated internal valuation.
2. **Vesting:** 4-year vest, 1-year cliff is standard in Switzerland; push back if they propose anything longer on the cliff.
3. **Exercise window post-departure:** Swiss practice varies more than the US; ask for at least 90 days, ideally longer, to exercise vested options after leaving.
4. **Liquidity path:** given the regulated custody business model, ask directly whether there's a realistic exit timeline (IPO, acquisition) or whether options are effectively illiquid for the foreseeable future, since that changes how much weight to put on the equity number versus cash.

**Equity script:**

> "If equity's part of this, I'd want to understand the strike price relative to the last round, the vesting and cliff, and roughly how you're thinking about liquidity, IPO, acquisition, or neither anytime soon. That helps me weigh it properly against straight cash."

---

## Geographic Anchor Pushback

**If they anchor to a lower regional average across Geneva/Zurich/Lausanne:**

> "I'm Geneva-based, and that's the cost base I'd be working from day to day even if some travel to Zurich or Lausanne is part of the role. I'd ask that we anchor the cash number to Geneva rates rather than a blended average across the three cities."

---

## Competing Offer Template

*Use only if there's a genuinely active alternative process.*

> "I want to be upfront that I'm further along in another process with a number closer to CHF 160k. Taurus is the more interesting problem to me technically, regulated custody infrastructure at real scale, so if we can land somewhere close to that, I'd rather move forward here."

---

## Questions to Ask Before Committing

1. Is the title and internal level negotiable given the seniority mismatch with the posted 2-3 year scope, or is this a fixed band regardless of background?
2. What does on-call actually look like: rotation size, frequency, and whether it spans the custody-critical Taurus-PROTECT systems specifically?
3. How is infra split today between Kubernetes/OpenShift-managed workloads and anything still running on more manual Ansible/Terraform processes?
4. What's the reporting line, is this into a platform/infra team lead, and how big is that team today?
5. Given the recent MiFID license and EU expansion, is headcount growing to support new regulated volume, or is this a backfill?
6. What's the actual office split in practice between Geneva, Zurich and Lausanne, and is remote/hybrid formalized or informal?

---

## Red Flags to Watch For

- Unwillingness to discuss title/level adjustment despite the experience gap, which can signal a rigid banding system with little room to grow into
- Vague answers on on-call scope for custody-critical systems, since that's where the actual operational risk sits
- No clear answer on whether this is growth headcount tied to the EU expansion or a backfill for someone who left
- Equity offered without any willingness to discuss strike price or valuation basis
