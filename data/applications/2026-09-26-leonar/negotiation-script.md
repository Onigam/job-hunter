# Negotiation Script: Leonar, CTO SaaS (Full Remote)

## Context before talking numbers

The listing is for a CTO / VP Engineering at a Paris-based SaaS studio running a portfolio of products, leading ~25 remote engineers. It gives no salary and says nothing about equity. The employer is French, so their reference point will be Paris pay, not Geneva pay. That gap drives the whole negotiation.

A second thing to clear up early: I'd be working from Switzerland for a French company. Ask how they'd hire me (Swiss entity, employer-of-record, or a contractor agreement). It changes social charges, who pays them, and whether French BSPCE options are even possible (they're generally reserved for employees of the issuing French company).

Heads-up: Leonar is also the name of a Paris AI recruiting platform (leonar.app). Confirm in the first call whether the studio is Leonar itself or a client hiring through it, since that changes who owns the equity and what "portfolio" means.

## Market reference (estimates, check before the call)

These are rough ranges from general market knowledge for 2025-2026. Recheck on levels.fyi, Glassdoor, Welcome to the Jungle and Swiss salary surveys before any call. Exchange rate assumed around 1 EUR ≈ 0.93-0.95 CHF; check it on the day.

| Reference | Base salary (approx.) |
|---|---|
| CTO / VP Eng, Paris scale-up, team of 20-30 | €140k-190k |
| CTO / VP Eng, Swiss scale-up (Geneva/Zurich) | CHF 220k-280k |
| Senior Staff / Principal, Geneva | CHF 190k-240k |
| Senior freelance CTO / fractional, Switzerland | CHF 1,100-1,500 per day |

**My numbers**

- **Opening anchor:** €210k base (~CHF 197k) plus a performance bonus and meaningful equity.
- **Target:** €185k-200k base plus equity.
- **Walk-away:** below ~€165k base (~CHF 155k) with no real equity. Under that, it's a pay cut against Geneva Staff-level roles, and I'd still be taking on a 25-person team.

## Opening anchor script

> "Before I give a number, it would help to understand the full package: bonus, equity, and how you'd employ someone based in Switzerland. [Listen.] For a CTO role with a team of ~25 across several products, I'm looking at around €210k base. That reflects where Staff and principal roles pay in Geneva, and the fact that I'd own architecture for the whole portfolio, not one product. I'm flexible on the split between cash and equity if the equity is real."

Say the number, then stop talking.

## If they come in low (for example €150k-160k)

> "I appreciate that, and I understand it's in line with Paris. The difficulty is that I'm not in Paris; my alternatives here are Geneva Staff and principal roles, which start well above that. I'd need to get to at least €185k base to make this work. If the cash budget is really fixed, let's look at equity and a guaranteed review after six months tied to specific goals we agree on now."

If they still can't move on base, trade in this order:
1. More equity, or equity at portfolio level instead of a single product (see below).
2. A signing bonus to cover the first-year gap.
3. A written six-month review with a pre-agreed raise if the goals are hit.
4. Title and scope in writing (CTO, reporting to the founders, member of the leadership team).

## Pushing back on the geographic discount

> "I get why a Paris company benchmarks on Paris salaries. But the role is fully remote, so you're hiring on skills, not location, and the Geneva cost of living is real. I'm not asking for a Zurich banking salary. I'm asking to meet in the middle, somewhere between Paris and Geneva rates for this level."

If they propose paying on a local-to-employer basis: point out that an employer-of-record or Swiss payroll arrangement adds cost on their side too, and a contractor setup might be cheaper for them overall. Run the numbers both ways before accepting either.

## Equity at a venture builder

This is the part most likely to be vague. Questions to ask:

- **Which entity?** The studio holding company, or individual products? At a studio, CTO equity in the holding company is usually worth far more than in any single product, because it captures the whole portfolio.
- **What instrument?** BSPCE, BSA, free shares (AGA), or phantom equity? As a Swiss resident, BSPCE may not be available; ask for BSA or a phantom plan with the same economics, and get Swiss tax advice on it.
- **How much?** For a CTO joining a studio of this size, ask for the percentage, the fully diluted share count, and the last valuation. A percentage without a denominator means nothing.
- **Vesting:** 4 years with a 1-year cliff is standard. Ask for monthly vesting after the cliff.
- **Acceleration:** double-trigger (change of control plus termination) at minimum, ideally 50-100% acceleration.
- **Anti-dilution and future products:** ask whether new products spun out of the studio come with a grant for the CTO, or whether the holding stake is expected to cover them.
- **Exit window:** push for a long exercise period after leaving (5-10 years instead of 90 days), or a phantom plan where it doesn't apply.

Script:

> "Equity matters to me here, maybe more than a few extra thousand in base, because the value of a studio CTO is in the portfolio, not in one product. Can you walk me through which entity the grant would be in, the instrument, the fully diluted percentage and the vesting terms? I've been through an acquisition as a cofounder, so I'd rather get the structure right now than discover it at exit."

## Competing offer template

Use only if it's true, or say it as a range of alternatives without inventing an offer.

> "I'll be transparent: I'm in conversations for Staff and principal roles in Switzerland in the CHF 200k+ range. Leonar is the one I find most interesting because of the scope, and I'd like to make it work. Is there room to close the gap on base or equity so the decision is about the role, not the money?"

If there's a real written offer:

> "I've received an offer at [amount] with [equity terms], and I have to answer by [date]. I'd prefer Leonar. Can we get to [target] so I can say yes this week?"

## Other terms to settle before signing

- Hardware and home office budget.
- Travel costs for the two yearly seminars and any Paris visits, paid by the company.
- Notice period and non-compete scope (make sure a non-compete doesn't block Swiss roles for long, and that it's paid if enforced).
- Clear written scope: product portfolio ownership, hiring authority, budget for the engineering team.
- Paid learning and conference budget.

## Rules for the call

- Let them say a number first if possible.
- Always talk total package, never base alone.
- Pause after giving a number. Silence is fine.
- Get everything in writing before resigning from anything.
