# Negotiation Script, House of Charts Founding Engineer

## Context
This is an ETH Zurich spinoff, early-stage MedTech, small founding team, no salary posted. "Founding Engineer" here almost certainly means pre-seed or seed stage, meaning cash will be tight and the real number worth negotiating hard on is equity, not base. Zurich is Switzerland's most expensive tech market, slightly ahead of Geneva on comparable roles, so the geographic argument cuts in my favor rather than against me for once. The Benevity liquidity event is the single strongest card here: I know what an equity grant is actually worth when it pays out, and I should say that plainly rather than treat equity as a vague sweetener.

## Market anchors

**Cash comp:**
- Founding/Staff-level engineer at a Zurich seed-stage deep-tech or MedTech spinoff: CHF 110,000-140,000 base is typical, with founding-team-adjacent scope pushing toward the top of that band.
- Comparable Staff Engineer roles at established Zurich tech companies run CHF 150,000-180,000, but early-stage cash comp reasonably trades some of that gap for equity and ownership, as long as the trade is explicit rather than assumed.
- 17+ years of experience plus a prior founding-engineer track record through to acquisition is above the typical profile for this kind of hire, and the ask should reflect that rather than defaulting to a generic "senior engineer" band.

**Equity:**
- For a true first non-founder engineering hire at pre-seed or seed, 1-3% of fully diluted shares is a defensible range, weighted toward the higher end given the founding-engineer title and the expectation of near-term architecture ownership.
- If the round has already priced (seed or beyond), ask for the percentage in the context of that valuation, not just an option count, since a headline number means nothing without knowing the denominator.
- Standard 4-year vest, 1-year cliff. Don't accept anything longer than a 1-year cliff at this stage, it's not a leadership-track hire without one.
- Ask about strike price and whether early exercise is available, and push for a post-termination exercise window longer than the default 90 days, ideally 1-2 years, given how early and illiquid this stock will be for a long time.

## Opening move: get the stage and cap table context before anchoring
> "Before we talk numbers, I'd like to understand where you are on funding, pre-seed, seed, or already past that, and roughly how much of the fully diluted cap table is set aside for early engineering hires. That context changes how I think about the split between base and equity, and I'd rather anchor correctly once than go back and forth."

## Opening anchor
> "Given the founding-engineer scope, owning architecture from early on, working across generative and extractive AI models in a domain where correctness and privacy actually matter, I'd anchor around CHF 125,000 base plus an equity grant in the 1.5-2.5% range on a 4-year vest with a 1-year cliff. I've taken a platform from zero to an acquisition before, so I know what this kind of ownership is worth on both sides of the table, and I'd want the offer to reflect founding-level scope rather than a standard senior IC slot."

## If they come back low on cash
> "I get that runway is the real constraint at this stage, and a lower cash number isn't a surprise to me. What I'd want to understand is whether that gap gets made up on the equity side, since that's the piece that actually pays out if this works. If the base has to stay closer to CHF 100,000-110,000, I'd want the equity grant to move up correspondingly, with the specifics in writing rather than described verbally."

## Equity negotiation specifics to push on
Fully diluted percentage and last-round valuation, not just an option count. Vesting schedule and cliff length in writing. Strike price and early-exercise eligibility. Post-termination exercise window. What the next funding round is expected to do to dilution, and whether early hires get any anti-dilution protection. None of this is unusual to ask for, and asking for it signals I've done this before, which is true.

## Counter-offer script
> "Thanks for putting this together, the structure makes sense given the stage. Based on the founding-engineer scope and what that typically commands in Zurich, I'd like to counter at CHF 125,000-130,000 base with the equity grant reflecting founding-team-adjacent ownership rather than a standard early hire, somewhere in the 1.5-2.5% range depending on how the cap table looks. I'm flexible on exactly how base and equity split, as long as the total reflects the level of ownership this role is asking for."

## Geographic and stage pushback, if needed
> "Zurich is genuinely one of the more expensive places to live and hire in Europe, so I wouldn't expect a discount on that basis, and I don't think that's what's happening here, I just want to name it if the number comes in low relative to local rates. On the stage side, I understand equity is doing real work to bridge the cash gap, which is fine, as long as the equity terms are concrete enough that the bridge actually holds."

## What to hold firm on regardless of the base outcome
Written equity terms: fully diluted percentage, vesting, cliff, strike price, exercise window, not a verbal "meaningful equity" promise. Clarity on what "prospects for a leadership role" actually means in the first 12 months, ideally tied to specific milestones rather than left open-ended. A clear first-90-days scope, since founding-engineer roles can otherwise turn into undefined firefighting. Confirmation of how medical data handling and compliance responsibilities are split between engineering and any dedicated security or regulatory function, so the job doesn't quietly become a full-time compliance role without that being priced in.
