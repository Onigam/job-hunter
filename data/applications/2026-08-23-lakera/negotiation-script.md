# Negotiation Script: Lakera, Staff ML Engineer (Zürich)

## Context
- No salary range published on the Ashby posting.
- Lakera was acquired by Check Point (NASDAQ: CHKP) in September 2025. As of this role, it's operating as a distinct product/brand inside a public company rather than a standalone startup, which changes the comp conversation: equity is likely CHKP RSUs or a retention/earnout structure, not fresh private-company options with a strike price.
- Leverage: 17+ years experience, a real acquisition exit (Alaya to Benevity), hands-on LLM integration and embeddings work in production, and direct experience operating inside a larger org post-acquisition, which is exactly Lakera's current situation.
- Counter-leverage they'll have: the role calls for deep production ML shipping (NLP/vision, long-tail data) which isn't the center of my background; expect them to weight that gap in the opening number, so the case has to lean on production reliability and LLM-systems experience rather than claiming core ML research depth I don't have.

## Zurich market anchor
Staff-level ML/software engineers in Zurich, one of the most expensive cities in Switzerland alongside Geneva, typically see CHF 160,000-210,000 base, with senior specialists at AI-security or fintech-adjacent companies often landing at the top of that band or above once bonus is included. Converted loosely to USD at a roughly 1:1 rate, that lines up with what comparable Staff ML roles at well-funded security companies pay in the US. That range is the anchor: it justifies sitting in the upper half even given the experience gap on core ML shipping, because the reliability and production-systems side of the role is where the real leverage is.

## Opening anchor
"Thanks for sharing where things stand. Given the Staff scope and that I've already lived through what you're going through now, operating inside a much larger company after an acquisition, I'd want to start around CHF 190,000-200,000 base, plus whatever the standard RSU or retention grant looks like for this level now that you're part of Check Point. I know my background leans more production-systems and LLM-integration than classic ML research, so I'm glad to talk through how that maps to the actual work on detectors and data generation."

## If they come in low (say CHF 150,000-160,000)
"I get that the core ML shipping experience they're likely weighting isn't the strongest part of my profile. Given that, a couple of things that would help close the gap: a defined path to review comp again at 6 months once I've shown I can move on the detector and red-teaming work, or more weight on the RSU/equity side now while we figure out the base together. What I'd want to avoid is landing meaningfully under band for Staff scope, since the platform and reliability work is exactly the same shape as what I've been doing at Benevity."

## Equity and RSU questions specific to a Check Point subsidiary
- Ask directly whether comp here is structured as CHKP restricted stock units, a Lakera-specific retention pool left over from the acquisition, or a mix, since this materially changes vesting mechanics.
- If RSUs: confirm the vesting schedule (commonly 4-year with a 1-year cliff at large public companies), grant date fair market value, and refresh cadence, since public-company RSU refreshes are usually annual and tied to performance review rather than negotiated once at signing.
- If there's a legacy Lakera retention pool: ask about the vesting timeline relative to the original acquisition date, since some of it may already be time-vested from the deal itself rather than starting fresh with this offer.
- Either way, ask what the total comp mix looks like (base/bonus/equity split) since public-company packages often shift more weight to equity than early-stage startups do.

## Geographic and cost-of-living note
Zurich is Switzerland's most expensive city on most rankings, ahead of Geneva by a small margin on several indices. If the number comes in lower because a base pay reference elsewhere is used: "The role is based in Zurich, and Zurich compensation should reflect Zurich's cost of living and market rate directly, not get discounted against a lower reference point elsewhere."

## Competing offer leverage template
Use only if genuinely in play: "I want to be transparent that I'm further along in another process at a similar level, and their number is close to what I've proposed here. I'd rather land at Lakera, the detector and red-teaming problems are the more interesting ones to me, but I do need to move on this within the next couple of weeks."

## Close
"I want this to work. Let's get base and the equity structure clear this week so I can give you a real yes instead of a maybe."
