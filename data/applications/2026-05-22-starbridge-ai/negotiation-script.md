# Negotiation Script — Starbridge AI, Senior Product Engineer

---

## Market Context

Starbridge is a US-based AI startup (NYC headquarters) hiring remotely in Europe/LATAM. The role is Senior Product Engineer with TypeScript and React. Given the AI sector and VC-backed startup profile, expect USD-denominated comp.

**Switzerland market anchors (Geneva, 2026):**
- Senior Software Engineer: CHF 130,000-155,000 base
- Senior/Staff at AI or product-led startup: CHF 150,000-175,000 base
- At current USD/CHF (~0.90): equivalent to roughly $145,000-195,000 USD

**Remote Europe premium:** US startups hiring in Europe often pay 10-20% below US rates but above local market. A Geneva-based candidate is expensive by European standards, which is worth surfacing.

**Your target range:** CHF 160,000-175,000 base (or $175,000-190,000 USD equivalent), plus meaningful equity.

---

## Opening Anchor

If they ask for your number first, respond:

> "Based on my seniority and what I see in the Switzerland market for this kind of role at an AI startup, I'm targeting a base in the CHF 165,000-175,000 range. That's assuming a remote setup from Geneva, which I'm sure you're aware comes with a higher cost-of-living base than most European cities. I'm flexible on structure if equity is part of the conversation."

If they come in with a number first, don't respond immediately. Say:
> "Thanks, I appreciate the transparency. Let me think about that and come back to you with a reaction, probably by end of day."

Then use the counter-script below.

---

## Counter-Offer Script

**If their offer is CHF 130,000-145,000 (or equivalent low USD):**

> "I appreciate the offer and I'm genuinely interested in the role. That said, the number is lower than where I need to land. Based on comparable positions in Switzerland for a senior engineer with founding experience and a platform background, I'd expect to be closer to CHF 160,000-165,000. Is there flexibility there? I'm also happy to talk about how equity factors in, if that changes the picture."

**If their offer is CHF 145,000-155,000:**

> "That's closer, and I want to make this work. My honest benchmark for this level in Geneva is CHF 160,000-165,000, so there's still a gap. If hitting that on base is a constraint, I'd be open to discussing a sign-on or an equity adjustment to bridge it. What does your typical options package look like at this level?"

**If they say the range is fixed:**

> "I understand. Before I decide, can you walk me through the equity structure? I want to make sure I'm looking at the full picture."

---

## Equity Negotiation

Starbridge appears to be an early-stage AI startup. Ask specifically:

1. **Grant size:** "What's the typical options grant for this level, in number of shares or percentage?"
2. **409A valuation:** "What's the current 409A valuation, and when was it last set?"
3. **Vesting schedule:** Standard is 4 years with a 1-year cliff. If shorter cliff is possible, ask.
4. **Exercise window:** "If I leave, how long do I have to exercise vested options?" (90 days is standard; longer is better.)
5. **Preferred vs. common:** "What's the current preferred price vs. the 409A strike?" (Gap matters for liquidation.)
6. **Anti-dilution:** "Is there any anti-dilution protection for early employees?"

**Script if equity seems light:**

> "The base is workable, but the equity feels thin for a senior early hire at this stage. At the pre-Series A or early Series A level, senior engineers typically see 0.2-0.5% in a role like this. Is there room to revisit the grant?"

---

## Geneva Cost-of-Living Pushback

If they compare to other European candidates or suggest Geneva is similar to Berlin or Amsterdam:

> "Geneva is consistently one of the most expensive cities in the world, above London and Zurich on some indices. My rent, healthcare, and pension costs are significantly higher than a comparable hire in Berlin or Paris. That's not a complaint, it's just the market. I'd want the comp to reflect that reality rather than blend me into a European average."

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

If you have another offer (or want to create urgency):

> "I want to be upfront: I have another process at a later stage that I'd need to respond to relatively soon. I'm more interested in Starbridge based on the product direction, but I'd want to make sure we can get to a number that lets me make that call confidently."

If you don't have a competing offer but want to accelerate:

> "I'm actively in a few processes right now. If we're aligned directionally, I'd prefer to close this conversation before I have to make a decision on something else. Is it possible to move quickly?"

---

## Key Asks Beyond Base

In rough priority order:
1. Equity grant (shares, percentage, vesting terms, exercise window)
2. Annual leave (minimum 25 days; Swiss norm is 20-25, startup should be at the high end)
3. Home office setup budget (CHF 1,500-2,000 one-time is standard)
4. Learning/conference budget (CHF 2,000-3,000/year)
5. Health insurance contribution if not covered (Switzerland is mandatory and expensive)

---

## What Not To Do

Don't apologize for asking. Don't say "I know this is a lot to ask." Don't accept the first number without a counter. Don't give a range if they push for a single number: give the top of your range as a single figure.
