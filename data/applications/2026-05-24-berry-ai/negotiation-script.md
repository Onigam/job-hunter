# Negotiation Script — Berry AI

## Context

Berry's posted range is $50K-$85K with 0.10%-1.00% equity. For a founding engineer with 17 years of experience, a prior founding/acquisition exit, and staff-level seniority, this range is roughly half of what the Switzerland market pays for comparable profiles. The listed range likely reflects SF startup economics targeting early-career or lower cost-of-living talent, and needs to be addressed directly.

---

## Market Anchors

**Switzerland / Geneva — Founding Engineer / Staff Engineer (2026)**
- Senior/Staff Full Stack Engineer: CHF 140,000-180,000 base
- Founding Engineer at a well-funded startup (Series A+): CHF 160,000-200,000 base
- VP Engineering / Head of Engineering (IC+Lead): CHF 180,000-230,000 base

**Remote from Geneva context:**
- Geneva has one of the highest costs of living in Europe (above Zurich, above London)
- A CHF 160K salary in Geneva is roughly equivalent in purchasing power to $130K in San Francisco, not $85K
- Any employer paying below CHF 140K for a profile like this in Geneva is paying below market

**YC-stage startup equity norms:**
- Founding engineer at a seed/Series A company: 0.5%-1.5% is standard for the first 5 hires
- Below 0.5% is appropriate for later hires or lower seniority; Berry's range tops out at 1.0%, which is right for this role but needs to be anchored at the high end
- Standard vesting: 4 years with a 1-year cliff
- Watch for: single-trigger vs. double-trigger acceleration on acquisition. Double-trigger is standard and protects you in an acqui-hire scenario

---

## Opening Anchor

When they ask about salary expectations, do not validate the posted range. Instead:

> "I've been in the CHF 160,000-175,000 range for total base comp at my current level, which reflects both the Geneva market and the scope of a founding engineer role. On equity, given the stage and the founding-engineer framing, I'd be looking at the 0.75%-1.0% range. Happy to be flexible on how those are weighted depending on your funding stage."

If they push back and reference the posted $50K-$85K range:

> "I saw the posted range. My sense is that's calibrated for a different profile or a different cost-of-living context. Given 17 years of experience, a prior founding exit, and the stack you're building on, I'd be in a different bracket. That said, if the total package including equity is structured thoughtfully, I have room for conversation."

---

## If They Come In Low (under CHF 130K / $100K)

Do not accept or counter immediately. Buy time:

> "That's a bit below what I'd expect for this level, especially from Geneva. Can you give me a sense of how the equity is structured? Vesting schedule, cliff, strike price relative to last round? The full picture matters for a founding engineer role."

Then counter with a package anchor:

> "Here's what would make this work for me: CHF 150,000 base, 0.75% equity on a standard 4-year vest with a 1-year cliff, and double-trigger acceleration on an acquisition. That puts us in a place where the total picture reflects the risk and the seniority of the role."

---

## Equity Negotiation

When they share the actual cap table numbers, ask:
- What is the current strike price? (FMV at last 409A)
- What was the valuation at the last round?
- How many shares outstanding? (to calculate dilution)
- Is there an option pool refresh planned before the next raise?

Push for the top of their range (1.0%) and frame it as founding-engineer standard:

> "For the first few engineers, 0.75%-1.0% is normal at this stage. I've been through one acquisition and I understand how dilution works, so I'm thinking about what this looks like at a $50M exit versus a $200M exit. I'd rather have a smaller piece of a bigger outcome than over-optimize on base."

---

## Geographic Discount Pushback

If they suggest a lower rate because you're not in SF:

> "I understand the impulse, but Geneva is more expensive than San Francisco on most household metrics. The geographic discount logic runs the wrong direction here. I'd want to be paid at market for the role and the output, not discounted for being in a different city."

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

If you have another offer or a live process:

> "I'm in late-stage conversations with one other company at roughly CHF 170K base and 0.6% equity. If you can get me into that range, I'd strongly prefer Berry given the stage and the problem space. I don't need the numbers to match exactly, but I need them to be close enough that I'm not leaving significant comp on the table."

If no competing offer:

> "I don't have another offer in hand right now, but I know the market well enough to know what this profile commands. I'd rather take a little more time to close on the right number than move fast and resent it six months in."

---

## What to Accept vs. Walk Away

**Accept if:**
- Base CHF 140K+ (or equivalent $115K+ USD with explicit Geneva-cost acknowledgment)
- Equity 0.75%+, 4-year vest, 1-year cliff, double-trigger on acquisition
- Clear growth path toward VP Eng or CTO as the team scales

**Walk away if:**
- Base below CHF 120K with no path to renegotiate at Series A
- Equity below 0.5% for a founding engineer title
- Single-trigger acceleration only (leaves you exposed in an acqui-hire)
- No visa/work arrangement clarity for Swiss remote setup
