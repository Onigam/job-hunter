# Negotiation Script: Founding Engineer, Zenline AI

## Read of the situation

The posted range for this role is CHF 96,000 to 120,000 plus stock options. That band sits at a standard Zurich mid-level full-stack or ML engineer level, not a true senior founding-builder profile. The role asks for 2 to 4 years of experience, which is a strong signal Zenline is budgeting for a strong individual contributor, not someone with 17+ years and a founder/acquisition track record. That gap is the whole negotiation.

External references for Zurich tech pay:
- SwissDevJobs lists Zurich ML engineer pay around CHF 117,500 median, CHF 127,500 at the top 25 percent, and CHF 140,000 at the top 10 percent.
- jobs.ch lists Zurich software engineer pay around CHF 105,000 average, rising to roughly CHF 120,000 to 130,000 for candidates with 15+ years of experience.
- SwissDevJobs' market report puts average Zurich tech compensation around CHF 109,000 to 115,000 across all levels.

The published band is a mid-level anchor. Magino's actual comparable is closer to a senior/staff or early technical co-founder hire, where cash softens and equity carries more of the value.

## Opening move: reframe scope before numbers

Lead with scope, not salary, since the posted range assumes a different level of hire.

> "The problem you're solving is exactly what I look for, and the founding-engineer framing is what got my attention. My background is more senior than a typical 2 to 4 year full-stack hire though, close to 17 years including founding and scaling a company to acquisition. Before we talk numbers, I'd like to understand whether you're hiring inside that posted band for a strong IC, or looking for someone to take broader technical ownership across architecture, hiring, and product direction as you grow."

## If they confirm it's a standard IC hire within the posted band

> "That's useful to know. If the scope stays close to what's posted, I'd want to be at the top of that band, around CHF 120,000, with an equity grant that reflects seed-stage risk rather than a token amount, and a written six-month review tied to concrete milestones."

Target for this scenario:
- Base: CHF 118,000 to 128,000
- Equity: 0.25% to 0.75%, four-year vest, one-year cliff
- Review point: six months, in writing, tied to defined scope or a re-level conversation

## If they want senior founding-level ownership beyond the posted band

> "If what you actually need is someone operating as a technical co-founder-level hire, across product, architecture, and eventually hiring, then I'd frame the package differently. In that case I'd be looking at CHF 140,000 to 155,000 base, with equity in the 0.5% to 1.5% range depending on how early this is and how the cap table looks, plus clarity on whether the title is Founding Engineer or something closer to Head of Engineering as the team grows."

## If they come in at or below the low end of the posted range

> "CHF 96,000 to 100,000 is close to a junior-to-mid market rate for Zurich, and it doesn't match what I'd bring on day one; someone who has already built a platform from zero through acquisition, and can own the LLM layer without ramp-up time. If cash is genuinely capped by seed-stage runway, I'd want to make that up through equity size, a faster vesting start, or a documented path to re-level compensation once the product ships its first real customer results."

## Equity checklist

- Get the actual number of options and the percentage on a fully diluted basis, not just a dollar value.
- Ask for the most recent valuation or 409A-equivalent reference so the grant is interpretable.
- Push for a standard four-year vest with a one-year cliff; resist anything longer.
- Ask about acceleration on change of control; double-trigger acceleration on some portion of the grant is a reasonable ask at seed stage.
- Ask about the post-termination exercise window; 90 days is common but worth pushing past if possible.
- If the offer leans hard on "founding engineer" as a selling point but the equity grant is small, treat that mismatch as a real signal, not an oversight.

## Zurich and relocation-specific points

Zurich is genuinely expensive, and the posted band already reflects that market, so there's no separate geographic discount to fight; the real angle here is level, not location. Being Geneva-based and willing to be on site in Zurich is worth stating plainly early, since it removes one variable from their side without costing anything on comp.

## Close

Best realistic outcome:
- Base at or above CHF 130,000, reflecting senior scope rather than the posted band
- Equity grant large enough to matter on a real outcome, with clean four-year vest and one-year cliff
- Written review point at six months tied to specific milestones, not vague "based on performance" language

Acceptable fallback:
- Base at the top of the posted band (CHF 118,000 to 120,000)
- Equity clearly above token size, terms fully disclosed
- Review point documented before signing, even if compensation itself doesn't move at that point

Walk away if:
- They won't disclose real numbers behind the equity grant (share count, fully diluted percentage, strike price)
- They expect senior founding-level ownership and hours while compensating strictly at the entry end of the posted band with no review path
- There's no credible story for how the role or comp grows as the company raises its next round
