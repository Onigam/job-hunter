# Negotiation Script: Siteimprove, Principal Software Engineer

## What the posting tells us

The role is listed in Copenhagen with a published base range of **DKK 742,225 to 927,781 per year**. Benefits on top: Danica pension with employer contributions, health insurance, paid mobile and internet, Danish holiday entitlement plus five extra days off (the "FF days"), and child sick leave. The posting mentions travel, but it doesn't say the role can be done remotely.

At roughly 0.125 CHF per DKK, the range converts to about **CHF 93k to 116k**. That's a Copenhagen number, and it sits well below Geneva rates for this level. Everything below depends on which of the two setups is on the table, so settle that in the first call.

Siteimprove is a mature SaaS company (around $100M ARR and 550+ people according to public sources) and is private equity owned; Nordic Capital took a majority stake in 2020, but check that this is still true. Don't expect startup-style stock options. Equity, if it exists at all, would come through a management incentive program, and those are usually limited to senior leadership.

## Switzerland market reference (Staff / Principal IC, 2026)

| Employer type | Base, Geneva/Zurich |
|---|---|
| Local scale-ups and fintech, Staff/Principal | CHF 160k to 190k |
| Big tech Zurich, Staff (L6 equivalent) | CHF 200k to 240k base, plus RSUs |
| Foreign companies hiring in Switzerland through an EOR | Usually somewhere between home-country bands and the local market; negotiable |

For comparison, Copenhagen principal-level ICs at product companies usually fall between DKK 850k and 1.1M base. The top of the posted range is a reasonable but not generous number for the local market.

## Scenario A: relocating to Copenhagen

This is the setup the posting is built around. The base looks low next to Geneva, but compare take-home pay, not gross.

- **Danish researcher/expat tax scheme (forskerskatteordningen).** If the salary clears the monthly threshold (around DKK 75k a month in 2025, so check the 2026 figure), income is taxed at a flat 27% plus the 8% labour market contribution, roughly 32.8% in total, for up to 7 years. The entire posted range qualifies. Ask HR to confirm they'll structure the contract for it. It's the single biggest lever in this negotiation.
- **Pension.** Employer contributions in Denmark are usually around 8% to 12% of base, on top of salary. Ask for the exact percentage.
- **Relocation.** Ask for a relocation package that covers moving costs, temporary housing for 1 to 3 months and help finding an apartment. Rental housing in Copenhagen is tight.

**Targets (relocation):**
- Walk-away base: DKK 850,000
- Target base: DKK 927,000 (top of the band)
- Opening anchor: DKK 980,000 to 1,000,000, justified by principal scope and cross-team architecture ownership, and by the fact that the role leads AI integration across the product lifecycle, which is more than a single-team remit
- Plus: relocation package, confirmation of the expat tax scheme, a sign-on bonus if base is capped at the band

## Scenario B: staying in Geneva (remote or hybrid with travel)

Only push for this if they open the door. Siteimprove would need a Swiss employer of record (EOR) or a contractor arrangement, so ask how they've handled other non-Danish hires.

**Targets (Geneva-based):**
- Walk-away base: CHF 150,000. Below that, it's a meaningful cut compared with what Geneva pays for this level.
- Target base: CHF 165,000 to 175,000
- Opening anchor: CHF 185,000
- If they offer a contractor setup, add about 20% to 25% to cover social charges, pension (2nd pillar) and unpaid leave that an employer would otherwise pay

## Script: when they ask for expectations early

"It depends on where I'm based, so it'd help to agree on that first. If this is a Copenhagen role with relocation, I'm looking at the top of your posted range or slightly above, around 950 thousand to a million kroner, plus relocation support, and I'd want the contract set up for the expat tax scheme. If there's a path to doing this from Geneva, the local market for principal-level work is around 170 to 185 thousand francs, and I'd want to talk about how you'd structure that."

If pushed for one number: "For Copenhagen, 980 thousand base with relocation. I'm flexible on the mix."

## Script: counter-offer if the number comes in low

"Thanks, I appreciate it. I'm keen on this one; leading how agents fit into the delivery process on a product where data trust matters is exactly the work I want. The base is below where I need to be, though. Given the principal scope, with architecture across teams and ownership of AI integration across the lifecycle, I'd need [target] to make it work. If the band is a hard ceiling, I'd look at a sign-on bonus and a guaranteed compensation review at twelve months."

Then stop talking and let them respond.

## Script: geographic discount pushback (Scenario B)

"I understand the band is built for Copenhagen. Geneva's cost of living is among the highest in Europe, and principal-level roles here pay CHF 160k to 190k base. My salary would also carry the employer's Swiss social charges, so I'm not asking for a premium, just the local rate for the level. If that's outside what you can do from Switzerland, I'm open to discussing a Copenhagen setup instead."

## Equity and incentive questions (PE-owned company)

- Is there a management incentive program or long-term incentive plan, and are principal-level ICs eligible?
- If yes: what's the instrument (sweet equity, phantom shares, cash LTIP), the vesting schedule, and what happens on exit or if I leave before one?
- Is there an annual bonus? What's the target percentage, and how much of it is company performance versus individual?
- If there's no equity at all, use that to justify a higher base or a sign-on bonus: "Since there's no equity component, base carries more of the weight for me."

## Competing offer template

"I want to be transparent: I'm in late-stage conversations with another company for a staff-level role, and their offer is around [X]. Siteimprove is my first choice because of the Search Insights work and how far along your team already is with coding agents. If you can get closer to [target], I'm ready to commit quickly."

Only use this with a real offer in hand, and keep the number honest.

## Other items to raise

- Title and level: confirm that "Principal" is the top IC level, or find out what sits above it and what it takes to get there
- Remote days and travel expectations, written into the offer
- Notice period and start date; account for the Benevity notice period
- Learning and conference budget
- Hardware and AI tooling budget (Claude Code and similar seats), since the role depends on it
