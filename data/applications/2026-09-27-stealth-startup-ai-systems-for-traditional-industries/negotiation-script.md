# Negotiation Script: CTO / Technical Cofounder, Stealth Startup (AI systems for traditional industries)

## Read this first: what kind of deal this is

This isn't a salaried CTO job with a normal package. The posting says the base salary "reflects pre-revenue stage", pay is weighted toward equity and a revenue share on every delivered project, comp gets reviewed at break-even (they expect that within about six months of the first closed project), and "if you need market-rate cash compensation today, this is not the right role."

So most of the value is in three places, and that's where the negotiation should go:

1. **Equity %** and its terms, which matter more than the salary here.
2. **Revenue share** on delivered projects, and exactly how it's defined.
3. **The break-even review**: when it happens, what triggers it, and what it guarantees.

A few facts shape the numbers:

- The company is based in Spain, fully remote, with a team across Europe and the Middle East. Spanish cash benchmarks are well below Swiss ones, and they'll anchor on Spain.
- I'm in Geneva. My cost of living and my opportunity cost are Swiss. Every franc I don't take in salary is a real bet, and I should size it on purpose.
- The posting requires **fluent Spanish** for client work. If that's a gap, it will affect how they value me, so settle how I'll answer it before talking numbers.

## Questions to ask before any number is discussed

- "How is the cap table set up today: founders, any angels, any SAFE or convertible notes?"
- "Is the CTO seat founder equity (common, vesting) or an option pool grant?"
- "How is revenue share calculated: on gross project revenue, on margin, or on net after delivery costs? Paid per milestone or at project close?"
- "Does revenue share continue on resales of a product I built, or only on the first delivery?"
- "What exactly is break-even, and what does the comp review commit to? A number, a range, or just a conversation?"
- "How much runway is there, and who's funding it?"
- "Contracting setup for someone in Switzerland: employer of record, or an independent contractor agreement?"

This role describes itself as the **first engineering hire and technical cofounder**, pre-revenue, with full technical authority. That's the top row, not the second. The single most important framing in this negotiation is that I'm pricing a cofounder seat, not an early employee grant.

## My numbers

The idea is to set a cash floor I can live on in Geneva for 12-18 months and take the upside in equity and revenue share.

- **Cash floor (walk-away):** about CHF 90k-100k per year equivalent. Below that, running Geneva costs on savings for a pre-revenue bet isn't sensible, whatever the equity. Check this against my actual personal runway before the call.
- **Cash target:** CHF 110k-130k equivalent, rising to at least CHF 180k at the break-even review.
- **Equity opening anchor:** 20%, founder common stock, vesting over 4 years with a 1-year cliff.
- **Equity target:** 12-20% depending on what's already allocated and how much cash I'm taking.
- **Equity walk-away:** below about 8%. At that point it's an early-employee package with cofounder-level risk and workload.
- **Revenue share target:** 8-15% of **gross margin** on every delivered project, including resales of products I architected, paid within 30 days of client payment.

## Opening anchor script

Get their structure first:

> "Before I give numbers, can you walk me through how you're thinking about the split between salary, equity and revenue share, and what's already allocated on the cap table?"

When it's time to anchor:

> "I'm looking at this as a technical cofounder seat, since that's how the role is described: first engineer, full technical ownership, building the product from the first commit. For that I'd expect founder equity around 20% on a standard four-year vest with a one-year cliff. On cash, I understand the stage. I can go well under market for the next twelve to eighteen months, and I'd want roughly CHF 120k equivalent, with a written commitment that it moves to market at break-even. For context, market for a CTO at an early-stage Swiss startup is around CHF 180k-230k, so I'm deliberately putting most of my compensation at risk alongside you."

Then stop talking.

## If they come in low

**Low equity (for example 2-5% options):**

> "I appreciate the offer. The gap for me is that the role as written is cofounder scope: I'd be scoping and pricing client work, building the first versions alone, owning the architecture and hiring the team. A 3% option grant is what I'd expect for a first engineer who joins after the company is funded and has a CTO already. If the equity is fixed at that level, the cash would need to be much closer to market, and the posting says that's not what you want. I'd rather close the gap on equity."

**Low cash:**

> "I'm fine taking a below-market salary at this stage, that's part of the deal. What I need is a floor that keeps me able to do this for eighteen months without distraction. Below about CHF 95k equivalent I'd be quietly funding the company out of savings, and that's not a good position for either of us. If cash has to be lower, I'd want that reflected in equity or a higher revenue share."

**Vague revenue share or break-even review:**

> "Revenue share is a big part of how you're pitching the package, so I'd like it written precisely: the base it's calculated on, the percentage, when it's paid, and whether it applies to resales of what I build. Same for the break-even review. I'm happy for it to be tied to a clear milestone, but I'd want the post-break-even salary written down now, not left to a conversation later."

## Equity terms to negotiate (these matter more than the %)

- **Founder stock, not options,** if I'm a cofounder. Common shares bought at nominal value now, so capital gains treatment later rather than option exercise cost. Check the Swiss tax treatment with a Swiss fiduciary; for a Swiss resident, gains on private shares are generally tax-free, but employee equity has its own rules on taxation at grant or vesting.
- **Vesting:** 4 years, 1-year cliff, monthly after. Ask for vesting credit from the date I start real work, not from incorporation or paperwork.
- **Acceleration:** double-trigger acceleration (change of control plus termination) of at least 50-100% of unvested shares. Single-trigger is worth asking for and giving up.
- **Good leaver / bad leaver:** make sure being let go without cause counts as good leaver and I keep vested shares at fair value, not nominal.
- **Anti-dilution:** as a common shareholder I won't get price-based anti-dilution; that's for preferred investors. What I can ask for is **pre-emption rights** (the right to take part in future rounds to keep my %) and a clear statement of the current fully diluted cap table.
- **Board or voting rights:** as technical cofounder, ask for a board seat or at least observer rights and a vote on technical and hiring decisions. "Complete technical authority" in the posting should be written down.
- **IP:** assignment of IP only for work done for the company. Carve out pre-existing personal projects and open-source work (GitHub: Onigam).
- **Revenue share after I leave:** agree whether it continues on products I built after departure, for a tail period of, say, 12-24 months.

## Geographic pushback (Geneva vs Spain)

They'll likely think in Spanish salary terms. My answer:

> "I get that you're benchmarking against Spain, and I'm not asking for Swiss market salary. I'm already taking well below that. But I'm based in Geneva, and the floor I need to stay focused on this for eighteen months is set by where I live. The way to make that work for both of us is for more of my package to sit in equity and revenue share, which only pay if the company works."

Practical points to raise:

- **Contract structure.** A Spanish company paying a Swiss resident usually needs an employer of record or an independent contractor agreement. As a contractor, Swiss AHV/AVS social contributions and professional insurance are on me, which is roughly 10% on top. Factor that into the cash number or ask them to gross it up.
- **Currency.** If I'm paid in EUR, ask for a CHF-denominated salary or an exchange-rate adjustment clause, because EUR/CHF moves have been eating into euro salaries.
- **Travel.** Client meetings and team time will mean regular travel to Spain and possibly the Middle East. Get it covered as a company expense, not assumed.

## Competing offer template

Use only if true, and never invent specifics:

> "I want to be straight with you. I'm also talking with [a Swiss AI company / another early-stage team] about a [Staff/Principal / CTO] role, and on cash that's around CHF [X]. I'd rather be building with you, because a cofounder seat and full technical ownership are what I'm looking for. But to take the cash cut, I need the equity and revenue share to make the risk worth it. Can we get to [target terms]?"

If there's no competing offer, use the opportunity-cost version, which is always true:

> "My current market for Staff and Principal roles in Switzerland is around CHF 190k-240k base. Taking this role means giving up most of that for the next year or more, so I'm looking for the equity side to reflect that."

## Before signing

- Get a Swiss lawyer or fiduciary to read the shareholder agreement, the vesting schedule and the contractor/EoR contract.
- Ask for the full cap table and any outstanding SAFEs or convertibles, and model dilution through a seed and Series A.
- Make sure revenue share, the break-even trigger and the post-break-even salary are written into the contract, not an email.
- Talk to someone who has worked with the founding team on their previous companies. The posting says they've built and scaled several companies on this model, so ask which ones and check how earlier technical partners came out of them.
