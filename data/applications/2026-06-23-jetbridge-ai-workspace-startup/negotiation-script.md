# Negotiation Script — Head of Engineering, JetBridge AI

_Role context: First senior engineering leader at an early-stage VC-backed AI startup. Hands-on, reporting to CEO. Geneva-based candidate._

---

## Market Anchors (Switzerland, 2026)

| Level | Base (CHF) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Engineering Manager / Lead | 160,000 – 185,000 | Team of 3-8 |
| Head of Engineering / VP Eng | 185,000 – 230,000 | First senior hire, startup context |
| CTO (early-stage) | 190,000 – 250,000 | + meaningful equity |

**Geneva premium:** Geneva cost of living runs 15-20% above Zurich and 30%+ above Berlin. If a company is adjusting for remote location, push back on any "European average" framing.

**Target range for this role:** CHF 195,000 – 220,000 base, depending on equity and stage. Do not go below CHF 185,000.

---

## Opening Anchor

When they ask your expectations, give a number first (don't wait for their offer):

> "For a Head of Engineering role at this stage, I'm looking at CHF 210,000 base, plus equity that reflects the founding-level responsibility. That's in line with what this role commands in Geneva for someone with a successful founding and acquisition behind them. I'm flexible on the exact structure if equity is meaningful."

Why anchor high: if they're a well-funded US company, their internal comp bands may be calibrated to SF or NYC rates. CHF 210,000 is defensible and leaves room to land at 195-200.

---

## If They Come In Low (below CHF 185,000)

Don't reject outright. Probe the structure:

> "That's below what I need to move forward, but I want to understand the full picture before we close a door. What does the equity package look like, and is the base band fixed or is there flexibility?"

If equity is meaningful (>0.5% on a credible cap table), you can negotiate toward CHF 185,000 + equity. If equity is thin and base is low, that's a clear no.

Counter script:

> "I appreciate the transparency. Given the scope of the role — first engineering leader, direct architecture ownership, hands-on from day one — and my track record building a platform that went through a $1B+ acquisition, I need to be at CHF 195,000 minimum on base. If that's not possible with your current band, let's talk about what can flex: signing bonus, accelerated vesting, or a review at six months."

---

## Equity Negotiation (Startup-Specific)

At an early-stage VC-backed startup, equity is as important as base. Key questions to ask:

1. "What's the current cap table, and what round are you on?"
2. "What does a typical option grant look like for this role, as a percentage of fully diluted shares?"
3. "What's the vesting schedule and cliff?"
4. "Is there a strike price set, and roughly what's the current 409A valuation?"

**Target range for Head of Engineering, early-stage:** 0.5% to 1.5% fully diluted, 4-year vest with 1-year cliff.

If they offer below 0.5%:

> "For the level of ownership and responsibility in this role, I'd expect to be in the 0.75-1% range. I'm a long-term hire, not a contractor, and I want my incentives to reflect that. What's the flexibility here?"

Anti-dilution note: ask if there's pro-rata rights in future rounds. Not always available, but worth asking for.

---

## Geographic Discount Pushback

If they cite remote flexibility or "European market" benchmarks to justify lower pay:

> "Geneva is consistently ranked among the five most expensive cities in the world. I'm not asking for SF rates, but I am asking for Geneva rates. The cost of living here is higher than London or Amsterdam, and that's before Swiss taxes. Any comp below CHF 185,000 would effectively be a step backward from what I can get in the local market."

Reference if needed: Swiss Federal Statistical Office data shows Geneva median senior tech compensation well above CHF 140,000, with senior staff and leadership roles regularly clearing CHF 180-200,000.

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

If you have or receive another offer, use it directly:

> "I want to be honest with you: I have another process moving forward at CHF 200,000 base. I'm more interested in this role because of [specific reason — founding ownership, AI product direction], but I need the numbers to work. Can you match or come close?"

If no competing offer yet, you can still frame urgency:

> "I'm at a point in my search where I'm moving quickly. I want to give this a real chance, but I need to close within the next two to three weeks. If we're aligned on compensation, I'm ready to move."

---

## Bottom Line

| Scenario | Minimum to accept |
|---|---|
| Base only (no equity) | CHF 210,000+ |
| Base + meaningful equity (0.75%+) | CHF 185,000+ |
| Base + thin equity (<0.3%) | CHF 210,000+ |
| Fully remote, no Geneva anchor | CHF 195,000+ (Geneva costs don't change) |

Walk away if: base < CHF 175,000 with no credible equity, no path to review, and no signing bonus. That's below market for a senior IC role, let alone the first engineering leader.
