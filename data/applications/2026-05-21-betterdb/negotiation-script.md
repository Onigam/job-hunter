# Negotiation Script — BetterDB Founding Engineer

---

## Context

BetterDB is bootstrapped and profitable. Compensation comes from company revenue, not VC. This changes the equity conversation: there's no 409A, no standard options structure, no cap table in the traditional VC-backed sense. What there likely is: an equity stake, a profit-sharing arrangement, or some combination. The role is founding-level, meaning you'd be among the first 3-5 people technically. That warrants compensation at the top of the range, not a "startup discount."

Switzerland rates apply even for a fully remote role, because your tax basis and cost of living are Geneva.

---

## Switzerland Market Anchors (2026)

**Founding Engineer / Staff+ equivalent, Geneva:**
- Large tech (FAANG-adjacent): CHF 200,000-240,000 base
- Series A-B startups with meaningful equity: CHF 150,000-185,000 base + 0.5-2% equity
- Bootstrapped profitable companies: CHF 160,000-210,000 base (no equity discount is justified here; they're not offering dilutable upside in lieu of cash)
- Geneva premium over Zurich: roughly 15-20%

**Your opening number: CHF 200,000 base.** This reflects 17 years of experience, a successful founding engineer exit acquired by a unicorn, and staff-level architecture credentials. It is not an aggressive ask for this market.

---

## Opening Anchor

When they ask for your compensation expectations, give a number, not a range.

> "I'm targeting CHF 200,000 base. That reflects the level of the role and what I've seen in the Geneva market for founding-engineer positions at this experience level. If there's a profit-sharing or equity component on top, I'd want to understand the structure before factoring it in either direction."

State it, then stop talking. Don't soften. Don't add "but I'm flexible."

---

## If They Come In Below CHF 160,000

This means they haven't benchmarked Geneva rates, or they're anchoring to a non-Swiss cost of living.

> "I appreciate the offer. The number is below what I'd expect for this role at this experience level, particularly given the Geneva market. The founding engineer positions I've looked at here don't tend to come in below CHF 180,000 for someone with this profile. Is there room to move closer to CHF 195,000?"

If they push back on the Geneva angle:

> "The role is remote, but my tax basis and cost structure are Geneva. I'd be taking on the same financial commitments regardless of where the company is headquartered. That's part of what I'm factoring in, and I think it's a reasonable basis."

---

## If They Come In Between CHF 160,000-185,000

This is negotiable territory. Don't accept on the first call.

> "That's moving in the right direction. I'd want to land closer to CHF 195,000. If there's a profit-share or equity structure I haven't seen the details on yet, I'm open to discussing how the total package fits together, but I'd want the cash component to reflect the market first."

Ask for 24-48 hours before responding. Use that time to follow up in writing.

---

## Equity and Profit-Sharing

Since BetterDB is bootstrapped with no VC, standard startup options don't apply. Ask directly:

> "What does ownership or profit participation look like for a founding engineer hire? I want to understand the full structure before I can evaluate the package."

What to ask for at this stage:
- A 1-3% equity stake, or a defined profit-sharing arrangement tied to EBITDA growth
- If equity: 4-year vest, 1-year cliff minimum; don't accept less on the cliff
- Anti-dilution protection or a right of first refusal if they raise in the future
- Ask how the equity is valued and what a liquidity event would look like in practice

If they haven't structured this yet:

> "I'd suggest we agree on the cash number now and come back to the ownership piece within 30 days. I'm comfortable with that timeline if the base reflects the market."

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

If you have another process at a comparable stage (or want to create urgency without fabricating one):

> "I have a couple of conversations at a similar stage right now. Nothing signed, but I'd prefer to close this one first if we can get aligned on the number. What's your timeline?"

You don't need to name the other company. This creates urgency without lying.

---

## Geographic Discount Pushback

If they suggest a "remote discount" because you're not co-located:

> "I understand the logic, but a remote discount assumes my cost structure is lower. Geneva is one of the most expensive cities in the world by any measure: healthcare, taxes, housing. If I were based in San Francisco, you'd be paying significantly more for equivalent experience. The Geneva rate is if anything the more reasonable basis."

---

## What Not to Do

- Don't give a range. Ranges anchor to the bottom.
- Don't volunteer flexibility before they've given a number.
- Don't say "I'm open to whatever is fair."
- Don't negotiate on the first call. Get the offer in writing, then respond.
- Don't accept same-day. Even if you're going to say yes, come back the next day with at least one question or counter, so you don't establish a pattern of immediate acceptance.
- Don't explain or justify the number at length. State it and let the silence work.
