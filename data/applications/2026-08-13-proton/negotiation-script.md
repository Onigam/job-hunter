# Negotiation Script - Proton, Site Reliability Engineer (Application Edge)

## Context

- Established, profitable, no-VC-funding company (100M+ users, 500+ employees), Geneva or Paris hybrid, up to 30% remote.
- Posted comp band is for the Paris office: EUR 50,000-77,000 base gross. That band reads low for a role this senior and almost certainly reflects Paris-level cost of living, not Geneva's.
- Magino brings 17+ years, a founding-engineer exit (Alaya to Benevity, acquired by a Canadian unicorn), and Senior Staff-level scope, well above what the posted band's upper end implies for a mid-level SRE hire.
- This is a base-salary-first negotiation with stock options as a secondary lever, not an equity-split conversation the way an early-stage startup would be.

## Market anchor (Geneva, Senior SRE / Platform Engineer, Staff-adjacent scope)

Geneva base salaries for a senior site reliability or platform engineer with production ownership of load balancing, Kubernetes and traffic infrastructure typically land between CHF 130,000 and CHF 165,000, and a candidate with Staff-level architecture scope and a track record like an acquisition exit can reasonably anchor toward the CHF 155,000 to CHF 180,000 range, particularly at a company with no external funding pressure keeping comp artificially compressed. The Paris band on the posting converts to roughly CHF 47,000 to 73,000 at current rates, which is not a Geneva-appropriate number even before adjusting for seniority, so the first move is establishing which band actually applies before any specific figure gets discussed.

## Opening anchor script

"Before we get into a number, I want to confirm the band that applies to Geneva specifically, since the one posted looks scoped to Paris. Given the seniority of what's described here, production ownership across load balancing, traffic management and Kubernetes at scale, I'd expect a Geneva base in the CHF 155,000 to 180,000 range, plus the stock options mentioned in the listing. I'm open to hearing how you've structured comp for Geneva hires at this level before I anchor harder than that."

## Counter-offer script if they come in low

"I hear that the Paris band is the reference point you're working from, but Geneva's cost of living and the local market rate for this scope of work are meaningfully higher, and I'd rather solve that now than renegotiate in six months. If CHF 155,000 isn't reachable immediately, I'd want to understand what would get us there: a faster review cycle, a larger initial stock option grant to offset the base gap, or a signing bonus that bridges the difference for year one."

## Equity / stock options notes

- Proton is not VC-backed, so options here function more like a long-term retention instrument than a pre-exit equity bet; ask directly what the option pool size is, current strike price basis, and whether there's any secondary liquidity mechanism given there's no funding round or IPO on a visible horizon.
- Get vesting terms in writing: standard is 4 years with a 1-year cliff, confirm this isn't shorter or back-loaded.
- Ask how option grants are refreshed after year one, since a single up-front grant with no refresh cycle quietly loses value relative to base salary over a multi-year tenure.
- Given the "less than 1% acceptance rate" framing in their own materials, it's fair to ask how that selectivity is reflected in comp relative to peer infra companies in the region, not just in prestige.

## Geographic discount pushback

"I noticed the listed range is anchored to Paris. Geneva runs meaningfully higher on both cost of living and local market comp for infrastructure roles at this level, so I'd want any offer benchmarked against Geneva specifically rather than adjusted from the Paris figure. Happy to share what I'm seeing in the local market if that's useful on your end."

## Competing option leverage template

"I'm currently the person other teams come to for platform and reliability decisions where I am now, so I'm not in a rush, and I'm also having conversations with a couple of other infra-focused teams in the region. I'd rather join somewhere with a mission I actually believe in, which is a real point in Proton's favor, but the base needs to reflect Geneva market rate for that to be the easy decision it should be."

Use this only if it's genuinely true; don't invent a specific competing offer or number if asked to name one.
