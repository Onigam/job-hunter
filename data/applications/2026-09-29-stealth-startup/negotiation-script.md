# Negotiation Script: Stealth Startup, Founding CTO / Co-Founder (European AI Infrastructure)

Posted location: London Area, UK. Candidate based in Geneva.
Posted comp: **no salary during the pre-funding stage.** Co-founder equity with vesting, salaries once funding or revenue allows.
Stage: pre-funding / early revenue. Solo founder with 10+ years in software and cloud-native infrastructure.

---

## 1. Read the situation first

This isn't a salary negotiation, it's a co-founder agreement. The things that matter are, in order: the equity split, the vesting terms on both sides, the trigger for salaries, decision rights, and the legal structure. Getting a good number on a salary that may not exist for a year is worth much less than getting these right.

The one number worth putting on the table is your opportunity cost, because it's what justifies the equity ask.

## 2. Opportunity cost: Switzerland market anchors (2026, working estimates)

Check these against Levels.fyi, Glassdoor, the Swiss ICT salary survey and recruiter conversations before the first serious call.

| Role, Geneva / Zürich | Base (CHF) | Total with bonus / equity (CHF) |
|---|---|---|
| Senior Staff / Principal Engineer, scale-up | 180k to 220k | 200k to 260k |
| VP Engineering, Series B+ | 200k to 250k | 230k to 300k |
| CTO, funded seed / Series A startup | 160k to 210k | plus 2% to 5% equity (hired CTO) |
| Co-founder CTO, post-seed salary | 120k to 170k | plus founder equity |

For reference in London: Staff/Principal roughly GBP 130k to 170k base, seed-stage CTO roughly GBP 110k to 150k.

**What that means in practice.** Against a Swiss Staff+ package of around CHF 220k, every unpaid month costs you roughly CHF 18k before pension contributions. Twelve months unpaid is a bit over CHF 200k of forgone cash. You're investing that into the company, and the equity should reflect it.

## 3. Equity: what to aim for

Rough ranges for a technical co-founder joining a solo founder before any priced round. Treat these as starting points, not rules.

| Situation at the time you join | Reasonable range for the CTO |
|---|---|
| Idea stage, no product, no revenue | 40% to 50% |
| Prototype and design partners, no revenue | 30% to 45% |
| Early paying customers, pre-funding | 20% to 35% |
| After a priced seed round | Hired-CTO territory, 2% to 7% |

The posting says "early revenue". Find out what that means (a couple of pilots, or real MRR) before naming a number. You're writing the core platform, so the IP you build will be most of the company's value at this stage; that argues for the upper half of whichever band applies. Even splits are increasingly common in two-founder teams, and asking for one is a normal opening position, not an aggressive one.

**Targets (assuming prototype plus a few pilots)**

- Open: 45%, fully vested over four years like the founder's own shares.
- Target: 35% to 40%.
- Walk-away: below 25%, or any split where the founder's shares aren't also subject to vesting.

## 4. Vesting, structure and protections

**Vesting for both founders.** Four years, one-year cliff, monthly after. The founder's own shares should reverse-vest too, with reasonable credit for time already put in (for example six to twelve months). Asymmetric vesting is the most common red flag in these deals.

**Shares, not options.** Ask for founder ordinary shares bought at nominal value, subject to reverse vesting. For a Swiss tax resident this matters: private capital gains on shares are generally tax-free in Switzerland, while employee options are usually taxed as income. Confirm with a Swiss tax adviser before signing, especially given a UK entity and cross-border setup.

**Acceleration.** Double-trigger: full or 50% acceleration if the company is acquired and you're let go or your role is materially reduced.

**Leaver provisions.** UK agreements usually have good leaver / bad leaver clauses. Make sure "bad leaver" is narrow (fraud, gross misconduct), not "left for any reason in year one". A good leaver should keep vested shares at full value.

**Dilution.** Founders rarely get anti-dilution rights, so don't spend capital on that. Ask instead for equal treatment: both founders dilute pro rata in every round, and any option pool is created before or alongside your grant, not carved only out of yours.

**Decision rights.** CTO title and co-founder status in the incorporation documents, final say on technical architecture and engineering hiring, and a board seat for each founder once a board exists. Written, not assumed.

**IP.** Clean IP assignment from both founders into the company, including anything the founder built before you joined.

## 5. The salary trigger

Get this in writing. Something like: salaries start when the company closes a round of at least GBP 1M to 1.5M or reaches an agreed MRR, whichever comes first, at a level agreed now.

Suggested post-funding salary position, Geneva-based:

- Open: CHF 170k.
- Target: CHF 150k to 160k.
- Floor: CHF 130k. Below that, Geneva living costs make full-time commitment hard without significant savings.

**If they propose London bands for a Geneva-based founder.** Seed-stage London founder salaries of GBP 90k to 110k convert to roughly CHF 100k to 125k, which is a meaningful cut in real terms in Geneva. The fair ask is either a Swiss-market figure paid through an employer of record, or a London figure with a higher equity share to compensate.

## 6. Scripts

**Opening the equity conversation**

> "Before we get into numbers, can I understand where things stand today? What does early revenue look like, is there anything on the cap table yet, SAFEs or angel money, and how long can you run before a round? Given I'd be building the core platform and giving up a senior staff salary in Switzerland, which is roughly CHF 220k a year, I'm thinking of something close to an even split, with both of us on the same four-year vesting. I'm happy to hear how you've been thinking about it."

**If they come in low (for example 10% to 15%)**

> "I appreciate the offer, and I get that you've carried this so far. The trouble is that 15% prices the role as an early hire, and what you've described is a co-founder: building the platform, the engineering team, customer technical work and fundraising, all without pay. If the number has to stay at 15%, I'd expect a salary from day one, and that doesn't match where the company is. I'd rather find a split we both feel good about. Could we get to 35% with vesting on both sides and a credit for the time you've already put in?"

**If the founder won't vest their own shares**

> "I'm not asking for this because I doubt your commitment. It protects both of us and it's what any seed investor will ask for anyway. If either of us walks away in year one, the company shouldn't be stuck with a large block of shares held by someone who's gone. Credit for the months you've already put in is completely fair."

**If they want commitment before an agreement exists**

> "I'd like to work together properly before we sign anything, say four to six weeks on a real piece of the platform, so we both know what it's like. During that time, a short written heads-of-terms on the split and vesting would let me put real hours in without either of us guessing."

**Competing offer template (use only if true)**

> "To be open with you, I'm also talking to a couple of companies about paid staff and founding lead roles, and one of them is moving toward an offer. I'd rather do this, because it's the kind of ownership I'm looking for. To make it work, I need the equity and the salary trigger agreed in writing within the next couple of weeks."

## 7. Due diligence before committing

- Cap table today, including SAFEs, convertible notes, advisers and any promised equity.
- Founder's runway and personal financial position; how long can both of you go unpaid?
- What "early revenue" means: customers, contract values, whether anyone renews.
- Who else is being considered for the role, and has anyone held it before?
- Legal entity: UK Ltd, and whether a Swiss or EU presence is planned.
- Competitive position against existing gateways (LiteLLM, Portkey, Kong AI Gateway, Cloudflare AI Gateway, OpenRouter) and why a European angle wins deals.
- The founder's fundraising plan and timeline, and what your role in it would be.

## 8. Walk-away conditions

- Founder's shares not subject to vesting.
- Equity below 25% with no salary.
- No written salary trigger.
- No technical decision rights, or a "CTO" title without co-founder status.
- Unclear IP ownership or an undisclosed cap table.
