# Negotiation Script — VP Engineering and Data @ Fleek

**Role:** VP Engineering and Data
**Company:** Fleek (YC W22, Series B, backed by a16z + HV Capital)
**Location:** London (3-4 days/week in office; full-time first 6 months)
**Candidate base:** Geneva, Switzerland

---

## Market Context

### UK VP Engineering at Series B (London market)
- Base salary range: £190K-£260K
- At Fleek's stage (~$50M raised, post-Series B, real revenue traction): £210K-£250K is realistic for a first VP Engineering hire
- Total cash (base + bonus if applicable): £220K-£270K
- Equity: 0.5%-1.25% fully diluted at grant; expect it to be negotiated down, so anchor higher

### Switzerland reference (Geneva VP Eng equivalent)
- CHF 220K-320K for VP Engineering at a scale-up
- Note: London base salaries are lower than Geneva in CHF terms once taxes and cost of living are factored in — this is leverage if they try to use London benchmarks to anchor low

### Equity expectations at Series B
- 0.75%-1.0% is the target range for a first VP Engineering
- Below 0.5% for a VP hire at this stage is below market and worth pushing back on
- Standard terms: 4-year vest, 1-year cliff, monthly vesting thereafter
- Ask about: option type (ISO vs. NSO), strike price, last 409A valuation, and liquidation preferences on existing preferred stock

---

## Opening Anchor

If they ask for your expectations first:

> "I've been looking at VP Engineering compensation at Series B companies in London. The range I'm seeing for a first VP hire at this stage is £220K-£250K base, plus meaningful equity. I'd want to be in that range. On equity, I'm looking for something in the 0.75%-1.0% range given the scope of the role — owning engineering and data end-to-end, 30+ engineers, and being a key part of the leadership team. What are you working with?"

If they give a number first:
- If it's below £200K: "That's lower than what I expected given the scope and market benchmarks I've seen. What flexibility is there?"
- If it's £200K-£220K: "That's a starting point I can work with. Can we talk about equity to make the total package right?"
- If it's £220K+: "That works as a base. Let's talk about equity."

## Counter-Offer Scripts

### If they come in low on base (under £200K)

> "I want to make this work, and I think there's a real fit here. But £[X] is meaningfully below what I'd need to make the move make sense, especially given I'm currently in Geneva and this involves either relocating or a significant commute. I'm not trying to extract maximum cash — I'd rather have equity that reflects the long-term upside. But base needs to be at least £210K-£220K for me to be serious."

### If they push back on equity

> "I understand the cap table gets tighter post-Series B. But this is a first VP Engineering hire — the person who's going to build the org structure, own delivery of the AI initiatives, and be in the leadership team as you scale to the next round. The work I'm being asked to do is co-founder-adjacent, and the equity should reflect that. I'd like to stay at 0.75%. If the pool isn't there, I'd want to understand what the refresh cadence looks like."

### If they cite "we have a salary band"

> "I understand that. Can you tell me what's flexible? If base has a ceiling, I'd want to talk about signing bonus or accelerated vesting as a way to close the gap."

## Location Negotiation

The role requires full-time presence in London for the first six months. Magino is based in Geneva. This is a real constraint and needs to be addressed directly.

**If they raise it:**
> "I'm aware the first six months are full-time London. I'd like to understand what that looks like in practice before committing, specifically whether the company provides housing support or a relocation allowance, and whether there's flexibility after the six-month period. I've been working distributed across Geneva and remote teams since 2021, so the operational side isn't the concern — it's making sure the logistics are covered."

**Questions to ask:**
- Is there a relocation allowance or temporary housing support?
- After six months, is 3-4 days/week hard or flexible?
- Is there a possibility of the role eventually being structured around monthly London travel rather than weekly commute?

**Anchor for relocation support:**
> "If I'm committing to six months of full-time London presence from Geneva, I'd expect the company to cover temporary accommodation or a relocation allowance. What's the policy there?"

## Equity Deep-Dive Questions

Before signing, get answers to:

1. **Option type:** Are these stock options or RSUs? Options are standard for UK startups; RSUs typically come later.
2. **Strike price:** What is the current exercise price (should reflect 409A / most recent valuation)?
3. **Last round valuation:** What was the post-money valuation at Series B? (This sets expectations for return math)
4. **Liquidation preferences:** How many times preference do existing investors have? 1x non-participating is acceptable; 2x participating is a red flag.
5. **Anti-dilution provisions:** Standard weighted-average is fine; full-ratchet is problematic.
6. **Refresh grants:** What's the cadence for refreshing equity for senior employees?
7. **Secondary liquidity:** Is there any path to partial liquidity before IPO/acquisition?

## Competing Offer Template

If you have another offer (or want to signal urgency):

> "I want to be transparent: I'm in late-stage conversations with one other company for a similar role. I'd prefer Fleek because of the product and the stage, but I need to make a decision this week. Is there anything you can do to move the timeline on your end?"

If the other offer has a higher number:
> "The other offer came in at [£X / equity Y]. It's not the company I'd rather join, but the package is meaningfully different. If you can get to [your target], I'd rather be at Fleek."

---

## Walk-Away Points

- Below £200K base: not worth the London relocation logistics without very strong equity
- Below 0.5% equity: below market for this scope
- No relocation support for the 6-month full-time London requirement
- Cliff longer than 12 months or vesting longer than 4 years

---

## Notes on Fleek Specifics

- Series B implies the early-stage equity upside is partially baked in, but there's still a meaningful path to a significant outcome if the $350B secondhand market thesis plays out
- a16z backing adds credibility and typically a structured board; this is a feature, not a risk
- The Data team of 4 is small; if data is genuinely core to the product (FleekSort), there may be an opportunity to negotiate headcount growth as part of the offer conversation
- The dual scope (Engineering + Data) is an expansion of a typical VP Engineering role; use that as leverage to anchor higher on both base and equity
