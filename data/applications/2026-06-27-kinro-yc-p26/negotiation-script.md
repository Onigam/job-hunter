# Negotiation Script — Kinro, Founding AI Engineer

---

## Market Context

**Posted range:** $120K–$300K base + 0.75%–2.00% equity (YC P26, SF, 3-person team)

The range is unusually wide, which signals they're flexible and don't yet know what level of experience they'll land. A candidate with Magino's profile (17+ years, founding engineer, acquired startup, Staff+ at a unicorn) should be anchoring at the top of the base range and negotiating equity toward the 1.5%–2% ceiling.

**SF market comparables for this role/level (2026):**
- Founding engineer at a YC seed-stage startup: $200K–$280K base + 1%–2% equity is typical
- Staff Engineer at a later-stage company would be $250K–$320K base with less equity
- The "founding" framing here warrants pushing equity harder than base, since equity upside at 3 people is material if the company works

**Geneva context:** Magino is relocating from Geneva (CHF 15K–20K/month gross for equivalent role). SF costs of living are comparable. There's no reason to discount for geography; if anything, SF is higher cost than Geneva.

---

## Opening Anchor

When asked about expectations:

> "Given my background, I'm thinking in the $270K–$290K range for base, and I'd want to be in the 1.5%–2% equity range. I know your band goes up to $300K and 2%, and I think this is where I'd be most excited to move quickly."

Why this works:
- Anchors at the upper end without exceeding their ceiling
- Frames it as alignment ("where I'd be most excited") rather than a demand
- Leaves room for them to move on equity if they compress on base

---

## If They Come In Low on Base

If they offer $180K–$220K:

> "I appreciate the offer. Honestly, the base is lower than what I was expecting given what I'd be bringing in day one: platform architecture, AI production systems, and a track record of building from zero. I don't think this is far off — can you come up to $250K? That's where I'd feel we're aligned on the founding-engineer framing."

If they push back:

> "I understand there are constraints at this stage. If $250K on base isn't possible, I'd want to revisit the equity to compensate. Getting to 1.75%–2% would shift the calculus for me."

---

## Equity Negotiation

**Standard YC terms to verify:**
- 4-year vesting with 1-year cliff: standard, accept
- Double-trigger acceleration on acquisition: push for this explicitly
- Anti-dilution provisions: ask whether there's any weighted-average protection

**Opening ask on equity:**

> "I'd want to land at 1.5%–2%. Given you're a 3-person team, this is the window where the founding engineer grant makes a real difference. Once you've raised a Series A, this number compresses. I'm not trying to over-index on it, but it matters to me that it's commensurate with what you're asking for: someone who can own the technical architecture from day one."

**If they offer 0.75%:**

> "0.75% is on the lower end of what makes sense for a founding-engineer role, especially pre-Series A. I'd want to get to at least 1.25% to feel like the equity reflects the ownership stake you're describing. I'm not looking to nickel-and-dime this — I'd rather get aligned early and move."

---

## Competing Offer Template

If needed (even hypothetically, can be used to frame the timeline):

> "I want to be transparent: I'm in late-stage conversations with another company that's likely to move to an offer this week. The role and scope aren't identical, but the compensation is in a similar range. I'd rather be here than there, but I need to make a decision by [date]. Can we move to final terms by Wednesday?"

---

## Relocation

Kinro is SF-based. Magino is in Geneva. If they raise relocation:

> "I'm fully committed to relocating. I've relocated before for the right opportunity. I'd ask for a relocation package in the $15K–$20K range to cover the practical costs of moving internationally: flights, first month, shipping essentials. That's a one-time ask and it's pretty standard for international relocations."

---

## Paid Trial Note

Kinro's process includes a one-week paid trial. Before starting:

> "I want to make sure we're aligned on the terms before I start the trial. Can you confirm the weekly rate and whether the trial counts toward any equity vesting period if we do proceed?"

Standard is to pay at a prorated salary rate. If they try to pay below $5K for the week, push back:

> "The role would work out to roughly $5K–$6K per week at the base we discussed. Happy to align the trial week to that rate."

---

## Final Close

> "I want to be here. The combination of the distribution bet, the stage, and the technical problems you're solving is genuinely what I'm looking for. Let's close this out."
