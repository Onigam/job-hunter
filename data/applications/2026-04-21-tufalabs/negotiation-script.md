# Negotiation Script — Tufalabs Senior Research Engineer

## Market Context

**Posted range:** 100,000 to 200,000 CHF (HN posting: "100-130k negotiable for senior roles")
**Actual Zurich market rate** for Senior Staff / Principal Engineer level: 160,000 to 210,000 CHF total comp
**Key facts to know going in:**
- Tufalabs is a well-funded independent lab (4+ years runway secured)
- They have a "generous equity structure" per their own posting
- Zurich cost of living is among the highest in Europe; Geneva is comparable
- Relocation from Geneva to Zurich is a real cost: budget 5,000 to 10,000 CHF in moving costs
- They can sponsor EU national visas; Magino is French-Swiss so this is straightforward

---

## Opening Anchor

When asked about salary expectations, anchor at 180,000 CHF base.

Script:
> "Based on my experience level, 17 years with a founding engineer background and the last five at Staff+ level, and what I see in the Zurich market for senior research engineering roles, I'd expect something in the range of 175,000 to 190,000 CHF base. That's the starting point I'd want to work from. Does that fit where you are?"

Why this works:
- It's defensible against public comp data (levels.fyi, Glassdoor for Zurich) and your own track record
- It opens the conversation rather than closing it
- The range signals flexibility without being a soft anchor

---

## If They Come In Low (e.g., 120-130k CHF)

Script:
> "I appreciate you being direct about that. My concern is that 120k represents a real step down from where I am now, both in base and in total comp, and it doesn't quite line up with what I see for this experience level in the Zurich market. I want to make this work, so let me ask: is the base fixed, or is there room to get to something closer to 160-165k with the equity structure filling in the rest? I'd rather talk numbers than talk myself into a gap that becomes a problem six months in."

Key move: separate base from total comp. If equity is genuinely meaningful, you can bridge a lower base, but you need specifics to evaluate it.

---

## Equity Negotiation (Startup / Research Lab)

Tufalabs has not publicly disclosed its equity structure. Before accepting any offer, get clarity on:

1. **What type of equity?** Options, RSUs, or direct shares? At a research lab, this varies.
2. **Vesting schedule:** Standard is 4 years with a 1-year cliff. Ask for monthly vesting post-cliff.
3. **Strike price vs. 409A / FMV:** For options, the strike price matters. Ask for the current 409A valuation.
4. **Anti-dilution protection:** At early-stage labs, future funding rounds dilute early employees. Ask if there are any protective provisions.
5. **Refresh grants:** Will equity refresh annually or based on performance? Labs that retain people well usually have refresh policies.

Script to get these details:
> "Before I can evaluate the total package, I need to understand the equity structure a bit better. Can you walk me through the vesting schedule, what the current valuation looks like for strike price purposes if these are options, and whether you have a refresh grant policy? That'll help me compare apples to apples."

## Relocation Pushback

Tufalabs requires relocation to Zurich within 6 months. Geneva to Zurich is a genuine disruption.

Script:
> "I'm open to the relocation, but it's a real cost on my end, moving costs, potentially breaking a lease, finding housing in Zurich's market. Is there a relocation package or a one-time signing component that helps bridge that? I'm not looking to negotiate it into the ground, but it would help me say yes cleanly."

Typical ask: 5,000 to 10,000 CHF one-time relocation allowance, or an extra month's salary as a signing bonus.

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

If you receive another offer while in process with Tufalabs, use it directly:

Script:
> "I want to be transparent with you: I have another offer I'm working through, and it's in a range that's somewhat higher than what we've discussed. I'm more interested in Tufalabs because of the research focus, but I'd be asking you to close a gap. Is there room to move on the base or the equity to make that easier?"

If you don't have a competing offer, do not fabricate one. Instead, use market data as the leverage:
> "I've done some homework on Zurich comp for this level and the data I'm seeing from a few sources suggests the market is closer to 160-170k for this profile. I want to be fair to both of us, so that's what I'm using as my reference point."

---

## Walk-Away Point

Know your number before the conversation starts.

- Minimum acceptable base: 145,000 CHF (plus meaningful equity)
- If base is below 145k and equity is not disclosed or clearly early-stage with no liquidity path, decline politely
- Time horizon: if equity vesting is 4 years, you need to believe in the lab's trajectory over that window, which the compute roadmap and 4-year runway suggest is reasonable

---

## Final Script: Closing the Negotiation

Once you've landed on a number you can accept:

> "I can make this work at [X CHF] base with [equity terms]. I want to move fast from here. What does your process look like for getting to a signed offer?"

Don't re-open the negotiation after agreeing to a number. Close it and move to logistics.
