# Negotiation Script: JetBrains, Founding Tech Lead (GrowthBricks)

Posted location: Germany / Boydton, VA, with remote or office options and relocation support. Candidate based in Geneva.
Posted comp: "competitive base salary", no figure given. GrowthBricks runs under JetMinds, backed by JetBrains.

---

## 1. Read the situation first

Three things shape this negotiation more than any number.

**Where the contract sits.** JetBrains has offices in Germany (Munich, Berlin) and elsewhere in Europe, but as far as we know no Swiss entity. Working from Geneva means either a German contract with relocation, a Swiss contract through an employer of record, or a contractor setup. Ask this on the first call, because it decides whether the pay conversation happens in EUR against German bands or in CHF against Swiss ones.

**What "founding" means here.** GrowthBricks is an internal venture, not a normal JetBrains team. Ask whether it's a separate legal entity, whether there's any equity or phantom equity in GrowthBricks itself, and what happens to the team if JetBrains decides not to continue it after the six-month milestone.

**Level.** "Founding Tech Lead" with full architectural ownership is a lead/staff scope role. Anchor to that, not to a senior engineer band.

## 2. Market anchors (base, 2026, working estimates)

Re-check against Levels.fyi, Glassdoor and recruiter feedback before the call. JetBrains is known for paying solidly but not at US big-tech levels, and it's privately held, so there are no RSUs to fill a gap.

| Market | Senior engineer | Tech lead / Staff | Founding lead at funded AI startup |
|---|---|---|---|
| Germany (Munich/Berlin), EUR | 85k to 110k | 110k to 140k | 120k to 150k + equity |
| Geneva / Zürich, CHF | 140k to 170k | 170k to 210k | 170k to 220k + equity |

A Swiss contract at German pay would be a cut of roughly a third in real terms for Geneva living costs. That's the core argument if they propose EUR bands for a Swiss-based hire.

**Targets**

- Swiss contract (EOR or entity): walk-away CHF 170k, target CHF 185k to 195k, open at CHF 205k.
- German contract with relocation: walk-away EUR 120k, target EUR 130k to 140k, open at EUR 150k.
- On top of either: a performance bonus tied to the six-month milestone, and a written answer on venture equity.

## 3. Opening the conversation

If the recruiter asks for expectations early:

> "Before a number, can I check where the contract would sit? I'm in Geneva, and a Swiss contract versus a German one changes the answer quite a bit. For a founding tech lead role with full architecture ownership, based in Switzerland, I'm targeting a base around CHF 205k. If it's a German contract with relocation, I'd be around EUR 150k. I'm also interested in how the GrowthBricks venture structure works, since that affects how I weigh base against any upside."

If they insist on one number:

> "For a Swiss-based founding lead, around CHF 205k base. Happy to adjust once I understand the bonus and whether there's any venture equity."

## 4. If they come in low

**Scenario A: EUR German band for a Geneva-based hire (for example EUR 115k).**

> "Thanks, that's helpful. The difficulty is that EUR 115k in Geneva is well below what a senior engineer earns locally, and this is a founding lead role. If I'm employed from Switzerland, the package needs to reflect Swiss costs, which puts me at CHF 185k or above. If the band can't move, I'd be open to relocating to Munich, but then relocation support and a base around EUR 140k would need to be part of it."

**Scenario B: Swiss contract but at the bottom of the range (for example CHF 160k).**

> "I appreciate it. Looking at what I'd own here, the architecture from a blank repo, the agent workflows and proving business value in six months, that's founding-lead scope. I built Alaya's platform from zero to acquisition, and that's exactly the job. I'd need to be at CHF 185k to make this work. Is there room to get there?"

**If base is truly capped:**

> "If the base is fixed, could we look at a sign-on bonus, a bonus tied to the six-month milestone, and a written comp review at twelve months?"

## 5. Equity and upside

JetBrains is privately held and doesn't run a broad employee stock program the way VC-backed startups do. Don't assume there's equity; ask directly.

- **Venture equity.** Is GrowthBricks (or JetMinds) a separate entity with its own cap table? Is there a founder or early-employee pool, phantom shares, or a profit-share tied to the product?
- **If equity exists:** size as a percentage of fully diluted shares, vesting (4 years with a 1-year cliff is standard; ask for monthly after the cliff), and acceleration on change of control, ideally double-trigger.
- **Anti-dilution.** Employee equity almost never gets anti-dilution protection. Ask instead how future rounds or JetBrains buybacks would be priced and what happens to your stake if JetBrains folds the venture back in.
- **Wind-down.** If JetBrains stops the venture, do you get redeployed inside JetBrains, severance, or accelerated vesting? Get this in writing, since internal ventures are closed more often than companies fail.
- **Swiss tax.** If equity is granted and you're on a Swiss contract, it's generally taxed as income at vesting or exercise. Ask whether a Swiss tax ruling exists.

## 6. Geographic discount pushback

If they suggest a "remote" or "location-adjusted" rate:

> "I get why companies adjust by location, but here the adjustment goes the other way. Geneva is one of the most expensive cities in Europe, and Swiss engineering pay reflects that. I'm not asking for a premium, just the local market rate for the role where I actually live."

## 7. Competing offer template

Use only if there's a real process running in parallel. Don't invent one.

> "I want to be transparent: I'm in late stages with another company for a staff-level platform role in Switzerland, in the CHF 190k to 200k range. GrowthBricks is my preference because of the zero-to-one scope and the agent work, so I'd rather make this one work. Is there flexibility to close the gap?"

## 8. Non-salary items worth asking for

- Clarity on remote from Geneva versus expected time in Germany, and who pays for travel.
- Conference and learning budget (listed as a benefit; ask for the amount).
- Hiring authority: headcount for the first engineer or two after the six-month milestone.
- Title: keep "Founding Tech Lead" or "Head of Engineering, GrowthBricks" in the contract, not a generic engineering title.
- Start date that works with any notice period still running.

## 9. Walk-away check

Walk away or pause if: the pay is a German band for a Swiss-based hire with no path to Swiss rates; there's no written answer on what happens if the venture is wound down; or the role turns out to be a feature team inside an existing product rather than a genuine blank-repository build.
