# Negotiation Script: Bjak, Machine Learning Platform Engineer

Context going in: Bjak is headquartered in Malaysia, runs Southeast Asia's largest insurance portal, and is hiring this specific seat as Switzerland-only remote, which suggests a deliberate, small European pocket around the A1 assistant rather than a standard local hire on a Malaysian pay scale. That works in my favor. The right anchor is the Swiss market rate for the role and level, not a Kuala Lumpur band adjusted upward, and that distinction needs to be made explicitly rather than assumed.

## Market context

- Mid-to-senior ML platform or infrastructure engineers in Switzerland, remote or Geneva-Lausanne-Zurich based, typically land in the CHF 140,000-175,000 base range at scale-ups. Companies actively building inference and serving infrastructure (vLLM, TensorRT-LLM, GPU scheduling) tend to sit at the upper half of that band, since the skill set is scarcer than general backend work.
- A company hiring a Switzerland-only remote seat for a product built primarily for ASEAN users is very likely paying Swiss or near-Swiss rates for that seat specifically, otherwise the geographic restriction wouldn't make sense. Worth testing that assumption directly rather than accepting a lower anchor without pushback.
- Because Bjak is privately held and not disclosing funding stage publicly in the posting, equity terms need clarifying before any number gets accepted, phantom equity, stock options, or profit-share structures all mean different things in a Malaysia-incorporated company hiring Swiss remote staff.
- Bonus or variable comp at this level, if offered at all, tends to run 5-10% of base at product companies this size, and should be asked about explicitly rather than assumed to be built into the number quoted.

## Opening position

"I don't see a range in the posting, so I'd like to start there. Given the scope, owning ML infrastructure and inference systems for a production AI product, I'd be looking at a base around CHF 160,000, with equity and any bonus structure discussed on top of that. I'd rather anchor on a real number than negotiate blind."

## If they come in low

"I'd want to understand how this number was built. If it's benchmarked against a Malaysia-based band and adjusted for remote work, that's a different conversation than benchmarking against the Swiss market directly, and since the role requires being based in Switzerland, I'd expect the second approach. If there's an internal constraint, I'd rather hear it plainly so we can figure out where the real flexibility sits."

Key move: don't let a Switzerland-only location requirement get priced like a discount. If anything, it signals the company already decided this seat needs Swiss-market comp to be filled.

## Equity questions to ask before agreeing to anything

- What instrument the equity actually is: options, phantom equity, or a profit-participation structure, since Bjak is Malaysia-incorporated and Swiss tax treatment of foreign equity grants varies a lot depending on the vehicle.
- Vesting schedule and cliff, and whether there's an acceleration clause tied to acquisition, given the fintech insurance space has seen real consolidation.
- Whether the company has had any external funding rounds or is self-funded through its insurance portal revenue, since that changes how to value the grant at all.
- Refresh policy after year one, rather than treating the initial grant as the only conversation that happens.

## Geographic framing

"I'm in Geneva, which meets the Switzerland requirement outright, no relocation, no visa process, no ramp-up delay. I'd expect the offer to reflect the Swiss market rate for this scope rather than a rate set for the wider team in Kuala Lumpur."

## Production ownership and scope framing

"The role covers training and evaluation pipelines, inference optimization, and observability for a live AI product. That's platform ownership across the full model lifecycle, not a narrow MLOps ticket queue, and I'd want leveling and comp to reflect owning that surface rather than a single slice of it."

## Counter-offer script if the number lands mid-band

"That's closer, but the scope here spans deployment, inference cost and latency, and monitoring for a production system, which is broader than a typical mid-level infra role. If base is fixed at this stage, I'd want the difference made up through the equity grant or a defined review point at six months tied to concrete platform milestones."

## Competing offer template (use only if real)

"I'm further along in a process for a comparable ML infrastructure role, with total comp tracking toward roughly CHF [X]. I'd rather build A1's platform since the inference and evaluation problem here is the more interesting one, but the numbers need to be close enough for that to be a real choice rather than a sacrifice."

Only use this if there's an actual competing process in flight.

## Closing line for any round

"Send me the full package in writing, base, equity structure, and any bonus terms together, and I'll turn around a decision quickly. I'd rather negotiate once with the complete picture than go back and forth on pieces of it."
