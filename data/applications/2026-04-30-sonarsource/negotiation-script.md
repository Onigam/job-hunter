# Negotiation Script — SonarSource, Staff Software Engineer (CodeNext)

## Market Context

**Role level:** Staff Software Engineer (individual contributor, technical authority level)
**Location:** Geneva, Switzerland (hybrid, 3 days in-office)
**Company type:** Private, General Catalyst-backed, $7M+ developer user base, mature product revenue

**Geneva market ranges for this level (2025-2026):**
- Staff Software Engineer (generalist): CHF 155,000-180,000 base
- Staff Software Engineer with AI/ML specialization: CHF 170,000-200,000 base
- Principal/Senior Staff level at mature companies: CHF 185,000-215,000 base
- Total comp target (base + variable + equity): CHF 200,000-230,000

**SonarSource-specific context:**
- Benefits package is notably strong: 60% employer pension contribution, 27 PTO days (+seniority bonus), 60% transportation subsidy, discretionary growth bonus
- Private company, so equity is likely RSUs or stock options, not publicly liquid
- General Catalyst backing suggests a secondary or IPO path exists, but timeline unknown
- No listed salary range in the JD, which is common in Switzerland and means anchoring matters a lot

---

## Opening Anchor

**Use when they make a first offer or ask for your expectations:**

"My target for this role is in the CHF 185,000-195,000 base range. That's based on Geneva Staff-level benchmarks for AI/ML-focused roles, and it reflects the seniority and specialization you're hiring for. I'd also want to understand the equity structure before landing on a final number."

**Why this anchor works:**
- It's at the top of the credible range, not above it (avoids negotiating yourself out)
- It separates base from equity, which creates two negotiation levers
- It signals you've done market research without being aggressive

---

## If They Come In Low (CHF 150,000-165,000)

"I appreciate the offer. The role is genuinely interesting to me, but I have to be honest that the base is below what I'd need to make this work given Geneva costs and the seniority level. I've been benchmarking Staff+ AI engineering roles in Geneva at CHF 175,000-190,000. Is there flexibility to get closer to that range, or would equity and the growth bonus be structured in a way that bridges the gap?"

**Follow-up if they push back:**
"I'm not anchored on an exact number. What I'm trying to understand is whether total comp can reach CHF 200,000-210,000 between base, equity, and the performance bonus. If we can get there, I'm ready to move forward."

---

## Equity Negotiation

SonarSource is private. Ask these questions before accepting any offer:

1. "What does the equity structure look like at the Staff level? Are these stock options or RSUs, and what's the vesting schedule?"
2. "What's the current valuation, and has there been a recent 409A or share pricing event?"
3. "Is there a secondary market or any liquidity mechanism for employees?"
4. "What's the anti-dilution protection?"
5. "What happens to unvested options if there's an acquisition before my cliff?"

**Framing for equity ask:**
"Given the private company structure, I'd treat equity more as a long-term upside than current comp. I'd want to understand the strike price relative to FMV and the realistic path to liquidity before factoring it heavily into my decision. That said, I'd expect a competitive grant at this level, which typically means something in the range of [ask them for their standard band before naming a number]."

---

## Geneva Cost of Living Pushback

If they resist on base citing "we pay fairly for Geneva":

"I understand the benefits package is strong, and the pension contribution in particular is above market. I'd factor that in as roughly CHF 10,000-15,000 in additional value annually. But Geneva's housing and general cost of living means base compensation has to carry more weight than it would in, say, Zurich or Berlin. The CHF 185,000 target already accounts for the benefits premium you're offering."

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

If you have or can develop a competing offer:

"I want to be transparent: I'm at a similar stage in conversations with one other company in Geneva at a comparable level. I'm more interested in SonarSource because of the CodeNext mission specifically, but I'd need the offer to be competitive to make this the clear choice. Can you get to CHF 185,000 on base?"

If you don't have a competing offer, don't fabricate one. Instead:

"I've been disciplined about this search and I'm not entertaining offers below CHF 175,000 at this stage. I'd rather take a bit longer and find the right fit at the right comp."

---

## On the 27 PTO Days

Don't give this up without getting something in return. If they ask you to accept below your target and point to PTO:

"The PTO is a real benefit and I appreciate it. But I'm already above Swiss legal minimums at my current company. I'd keep the conversation on base and equity, and treat PTO as a given at this level rather than a trade-off."

---

## Closing Move

Once you're close:

"I think we're at a place where I'd like to move forward. Can you send the offer in writing at CHF [agreed number], with the equity terms we discussed? I'll review it and come back to you within 48 hours."

Never accept verbally on the spot. Always ask for it in writing before committing.
