# Negotiation Script: Granular (Product Builder / CTO, Co-Founder)

Context: pre-seed/seed-stage Swiss fintech (restaurant finance automation), pilot customers live, founded in Zurich, two known cofounders already (Patrick Joye on product/strategy, Christian Sandrini on engineering/architecture). This role is a third cofounder or a CTO hire filling a specific gap, likely more junior equity-wise than a day-one cofounder slot. Read the actual cap table before anchoring hard on numbers.

## Market anchors (Switzerland, Staff/Principal to CTO band, 2026)

- Senior/Staff engineer at a funded Swiss startup: CHF 130k-160k base, Zurich or Geneva.
- Engineering lead / early CTO at seed-stage Swiss startup, cash-constrained: CHF 90k-130k base is common, with the gap made up in equity.
- Geneva cost of living runs 10-15% above Zurich; don't let a Zurich-anchored range get applied to a Geneva-based hire without adjustment.
- At true pre-seed/seed with a handful of pilot customers, full market cash is unrealistic. The real negotiation is equity percentage, vesting terms, and how much salary is deferred versus paid.

## Opening anchor

Don't open on a number. Open by asking about the cap table and how the two existing cofounders are compensated, then request a range from them first. If pushed to name a number:

"For a founding CTO role with real ownership of the stack, I'd expect something in the CHF 110k-140k base range plus a meaningful founder equity grant, weighted more toward equity given the stage. What does that look like against what Patrick and Christian are drawing right now?"

Anchoring against the existing cofounders' comp keeps the ask grounded in this company's actual constraints rather than a generic market number, and it surfaces early whether this is really a cofounder role or a senior-hire role with a cofounder title attached.

## If they come in low on cash

Restaurant fintech at pilot stage means runway is tight, so a low cash offer is expected, not a red flag by itself. Counter on the equity side rather than pushing hard on cash:

"I get that cash is tight at this stage, that's normal for where you are. I can work with a lower base if the equity and vesting reflect founding-level risk and founding-level ownership of the technical decisions, not just a hire slotted under an existing CTO."

If the base offered is under CHF 90k with less than 1% equity, that's a signal the role is a senior engineering hire, not a cofounder role, regardless of the title. Worth naming that directly rather than negotiating around it.

## Equity and vesting

- Ask for the fully diluted percentage, not just a share count. A share count means nothing without knowing the total pool.
- Standard is a 4-year vest with a 1-year cliff. Push back on anything longer than 4 years or a cliff over 1 year.
- Ask whether there's an existing option pool or whether this grant would come from a fresh pool being carved out, which changes the real dilution math.
- If joining as a third cofounder rather than employee number one, ask directly how founder equity is split between Patrick, Christian, and this role, and whether there's a vesting schedule already running for the other two that this role would be joining mid-stream.
- Anti-dilution: at seed stage this is less about formal anti-dilution terms and more about founder-round protections, ask what happens to this equity stake in the next priced round.

## Geographic discount pushback

If the offer assumes remote-from-anywhere-Europe rates rather than Geneva or Zurich rates:

"Geneva's cost of living is close to Zurich's, and I'd be relocating or commuting for a role that wants me in customer calls and shipping the same day, so this isn't a remote-discount situation. I'd want the comp to reflect being based here."

## Competing offer leverage (use only if real)

"I'm mid-conversation with a couple of other platform and founding-engineer roles at a similar stage, so I'd like to move on this within the next week or two either way."

Keep this vague and only use it if there's genuinely something else in motion. A specific fabricated competing offer is easy to expose in a small-team, small-network Swiss startup scene.

## Questions to ask before naming any number

- What's the current runway, and what does the next raise depend on technically?
- What decision rights come with the CTO title: hiring, architecture, pricing/packaging as mentioned in the posting?
- Is there a written founder agreement or vesting schedule for Patrick and Christian already, and would this role be added to it or run on a separate schedule?
- What's the plan if the next round doesn't close on the current timeline?

## Walk-away line

"I think there's a real fit here technically, and I like what you're building. If the equity and role scope don't add up to genuine cofounder ownership, though, I'd rather stay friendly and revisit this if the stage or the terms shift."
