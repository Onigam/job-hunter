# Negotiation Script, Founders Factory / Immoly CTO & Co-Founder

## Context
This isn't a normal salary negotiation, it's a founder-level deal: cash comp, equity, vesting, and decision rights all move together. The posting says "founder-level salary from day one" and "meaningful equity with standard vesting" but gives no numbers, which is typical of venture-studio-backed pre-seed roles. Founders Factory itself usually keeps its own stake small, often under 10%, structured as a convertible loan blending a modest cash check with a services package, so most of the cap table pressure is between the CEO co-founder and the incoming CTO, not from the studio. The honest framing going in: Geneva-based Staff/Principal comp is the opportunity cost being given up, so the equity and title need to actually reflect a co-founder position, not an early-employee one wearing a co-founder label.

## Switzerland market anchors (opportunity cost baseline)
- Staff/Principal Software Engineer, Geneva/Zurich: CHF 150,000-190,000 base, plus bonus and equity at a scale-up.
- Senior Staff with a founder track record and an acquisition exit (Alaya/Benevity-level): CHF 170,000-210,000 base is realistic at a well-funded company.
- This is the number being walked away from to take a pre-seed founder salary, and it's worth naming once, plainly, rather than letting it go unsaid.

## Berlin / German pre-seed reality
- Founder salaries at incorporated, pre-seed-funded German startups typically land EUR 70,000-110,000 base for a CTO co-founder, sometimes with a step-up tied to the next funding round.
- GCP/AI engineering leadership hires in Berlin at Series A run EUR 100,000-140,000, useful as the target to grow into once the seed round closes rather than the opening ask.
- Relocation from Geneva to Berlin is a real cost of living drop on paper but not in practice once Swiss taxes and health insurance are netted out; don't let a headline EUR number get compared to a headline CHF number without that context.

## Opening anchor (cash)
> "I want to be upfront that I'm giving up a Geneva-market Staff-level base to do this, so the cash side matters to me even in a founder role. I'd want to open around EUR 95,000 base, with a written commitment to revisit it at the next priced round rather than leaving it open-ended. I know pre-seed budgets are tight, and I'm not trying to run this like a normal hire, I just don't want the founder framing to end up meaning 'below-market and permanent.'"

## If they come back low (below EUR 75,000, or vague on timeline)
> "I get that runway is the constraint right now, and I'm genuinely fine trading some cash for equity and speed. What I'd want instead is a specific trigger, at the seed close, or in twelve months, whichever comes first, where base moves to market. Open-ended 'we'll revisit it' without a date attached is the part I'd push back on, not the number itself."

## Equity negotiation (pre-seed, co-founder joining an existing venture)
- Ask for the current cap table before evaluating any percentage in isolation, specifically how much Founders Factory holds, how much is already allocated to the CEO co-founder, and what's reserved for the option pool.
- A CTO co-founder joining a studio-backed venture post-formation typically lands somewhere between 15% and 30%, depending on how early this is and how much of the working prototype is already built; below 15% is worth questioning given the role explicitly asks for full technical ownership.
- Push for standard 4-year vesting with a 1-year cliff, nothing longer. Ask whether any of it can be structured with a shorter cliff given the prototype already exists and isn't being built from literally nothing.
- Get single-trigger acceleration on a sale of the company before the first priced round, and double-trigger acceleration (change of control plus termination) after. Founders who join post-formation are more exposed to being squeezed out post-acquisition than the original founder, so this is worth being specific about rather than assuming it's covered by "standard docs."
- Ask directly what Vonovia's relationship is on the cap table, customer, strategic investor, or right of first refusal on future rounds, since that shapes both the ceiling on outcomes and who else has a say in decisions later.
- Confirm IP assignment terms explicitly cover work product from before the formal offer if any prototype contribution happens during a trial period.

## Counter-offer script (verbal or written)
> "Thanks for putting this together, I can tell there's real structure behind it already. Based on what I'd be walking away from and the scope of full technical ownership here, I'd like to counter at EUR 95,000 base with 22% equity on a standard 4-year vest and a 1-year cliff, plus a written comp review tied to the seed close rather than a vague future date. I'm open to moving pieces around, more equity for less cash, a defined step-up instead of a higher opening number, as long as the total reflects a co-founder stake and not an early hire's package."

## Geographic / relocation angle
> "I'm Geneva-based today, and I'd relocate to Berlin for this because a co-founder role only works with real proximity, not because Berlin comp resets what I'd need. I'd ask for a relocation allowance covering the actual move, and I'd rather have that be explicit now than assumed later."

## Competing offer as leverage (use only if real, or omit entirely)
> "I'm also in later-stage conversations for a Staff-level role in Switzerland in the CHF 170,000-190,000 range with no equity uncertainty attached. I'd rather build this if the numbers and the equity story hold together, the problem is more interesting to me than a pure comp bump would be, but I wanted to be transparent about the comparison I'm actually making."

## What to hold firm on regardless of the base outcome
- Actual co-founder title and cap table position, not "Head of Engineering" with a co-founder story attached verbally.
- A specific, dated trigger for comp review, not an open-ended promise.
- Written clarity on Vonovia's role and any rights that come with it before signing.
- Double-trigger acceleration post-first-round, confirmed in the actual founder agreement, not just discussed on a call.
