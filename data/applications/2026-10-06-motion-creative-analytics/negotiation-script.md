# Negotiation Script: Motion (Creative Analytics), Executive IC, Engineering

## The shape of this offer

Motion is a remote-first company with about 75 people, hubs in Toronto, Montreal, Vancouver and Porto, and a LinkedIn posting tagged "United States". Its 2025 Series B ($30M, led by Inovia) took total funding to about $42M, and the posting now says $65M+, so there's probably been a later round. Check this before the call, because the stage decides how equity should be valued. No salary band is published. All the posting says is "competitive salary and equity for all employees".

Two structural questions come before any number:

1. **Who employs a Geneva resident?** The hubs are in Canada and Portugal. Ask early whether they'd hire through an employer of record in Switzerland, sign a contractor agreement with a Swiss entity, or go through the Porto entity. Each option changes the cost to them and the net for Magino. With a contractor setup, Magino pays Swiss social charges (AHV/AVS and pension) directly, so the gross has to be about 15 to 20% higher to break even.
2. **Which band applies?** A Canadian company will often use Canadian or Portuguese bands for non-US hires. Executive IC is a rare level, so push for the band to follow the level and not the payroll country. Geneva's cost of living is above Toronto's and far above Porto's.

## Market anchors

These are rough base-salary ranges for Principal or Distinguished-level ICs and VP-equivalent roles at Series B/C SaaS companies. Check them on levels.fyi, Glassdoor CH and with recruiters before the call.

| Market | Base (approx.) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Geneva / Lausanne | CHF 200k to 260k | Few roles at this level locally; finance and international orgs set the top |
| Zurich | CHF 220k to 280k | Big tech offices sit above this once RSUs are included |
| US remote, Series B/C | USD 240k to 310k | The comparison set for a role posted as "United States" |
| Canada (Toronto), Series B/C | CAD 230k to 300k | The band Motion is most likely to use internally |
| Portugal (Porto) | EUR 90k to 140k | Too low for Geneva and not a band to accept |

At today's rates USD 250k is roughly CHF 200k to 210k, so a US-level offer and a Geneva Principal anchor land in about the same place. That makes it the cleanest anchor to argue from.

## Targets

| | Base | Equity | Other |
|---|---|---|---|
| Opening anchor | CHF 250k (or USD 300k equivalent) | 0.3% to 0.5% in options, 4 years | Swiss pension contribution covered if via EOR |
| Target | CHF 220k to 235k | 0.2% to 0.35% | Signing bonus to offset unvested Benevity equity, if any |
| Walk-away | CHF 190k base, unless equity is clearly outsized | Below 0.15% is weak for this level | Contractor rate below CHF 1,100/day |

Equity percentages at a ~75-person Series B or C are a judgement call. Ask for the fully diluted share count and the latest preferred price so you can turn the grant into a CHF value, not just a percentage.

## Scripts

### When they ask for salary expectations first

"I'd like to understand the level and band you've set for this seat before I give a number. It's a role I don't see often. For context, Principal and VP-equivalent roles in Geneva and in US-remote Series B/C companies tend to sit around CHF 220k to 260k base, plus meaningful equity. I'd expect us to land in that range."

### Opening anchor (once they've confirmed the level)

"Based on the scope, owning a product surface end to end and setting the AI-tooling standard for the engineering org, I'm looking at CHF 250k base, or the equivalent in USD, with an equity grant that matches an executive-level hire. I've built and sold a platform on almost exactly your stack, so I'd expect the ramp-up to be short."

### If they come in low

"Thanks, I appreciate the offer and I'm keen on the role. The base is below what I'm seeing for this level in both Geneva and US remote, by roughly CHF 30k to 40k. Can we get the base to CHF 230k? If the base band is fixed, I'm open to closing the gap through equity or a signing bonus, but I'd want the total to reflect the level you've put on the title."

### Pushing back on a geographic discount

"I understand the bands differ by location. But Geneva is one of the most expensive cities in Europe, more expensive than Toronto and a long way above Porto. If a location adjustment applies, I'd ask that it uses Geneva cost-of-living data and not the payroll country's. The other option is to anchor to the US band the posting is listed under."

### Competing-offer template

"To be transparent, I'm in late-stage conversations for a Staff/Principal role with a base around CHF [X]. Motion is my preference because of the scope and the product, and I'd like to make it work. Can you get closer to [target] so it's an easy decision?"

Only use this if there's a real process running. Never invent a specific offer.

## Equity checklist

Motion grants equity to all employees, which is likely to mean stock options. Questions to ask:

- **Instrument and price:** options or RSUs, the strike price, and the latest preferred price. The gap between them is the paper value today.
- **Vesting:** 4 years with a 1-year cliff is standard. For an exec-level hire, ask for monthly vesting after the cliff, or a shorter cliff (6 months).
- **Exercise window:** standard is 90 days after leaving. Ask for 5 to 10 years. It matters more than almost anything else for an option holder.
- **Acceleration:** double-trigger acceleration (change of control plus termination) is a reasonable ask at this level. Point to Alaya's acquisition as the reason you care about it.
- **Refreshers:** is there an annual refresh policy, and is it based on performance?
- **Dilution:** you can't get anti-dilution protection as an employee. What you can ask for is the fully diluted count, and whether a top-up is possible after the next round.
- **Swiss tax:** in Switzerland options are usually taxed at grant or exercise, depending on the plan and the canton. Get a Geneva tax adviser to read the plan before signing, especially if the grant is meant to come through an EOR.

## Benefits worth negotiating

The posting lists a $1,500 home-office stipend, a monthly health and lifestyle stipend and 20 days of PTO. In Switzerland 20 days is the legal minimum, and 25 is normal at senior levels. Ask for 25. Also ask for travel cost to the bimonthly in-person gatherings and the summer retreat to be covered from Geneva, and for a learning or conference budget.

## Order of operations

1. Settle the employment structure (EOR, contractor or Porto entity) before discussing numbers.
2. Confirm the level and which band applies.
3. Anchor on base, then equity, then benefits.
4. Get the full offer in writing, including the equity plan document, before accepting.
