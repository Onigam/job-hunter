# Negotiation Script, Almedia, Founding Engineer

## Market Context

**Role level:** Founding Engineer, full ownership of scope/architecture/implementation, backend-heavy (TypeScript/Node.js)
**Location:** Berlin, Germany, on-site, no relocation support offered; Magino currently based in Geneva, Switzerland
**Almedia profile:** Bootstrapped, no outside investors, €100M+ annual revenue, first acquisition (zave.it) closed August 2025, 130+ employees, posted range €90,000 to €150,000 base plus meaningful equity

**Swiss market rates, used as the anchor even though the role pays in EUR:**
- Founding/early-stage engineer, Geneva or Zurich, comparable ownership scope: CHF 150,000 to 190,000 base equivalent
- Senior/Staff-level product engineer at a profitable, late-growth bootstrapped company: CHF 160,000 to 200,000
- Converted at roughly 0.96 EUR/CHF: EUR 144,000 to 192,000 equivalent

**Honest read before anchoring:** the posted band (€90K to €150K) sits well under the Swiss equivalent, and Berlin's cost of living is also lower than Geneva's, so a straight geographic-band argument only goes so far here. The stronger case is the ownership scope and the founding-engineer track record, both places Magino's profile is unusually strong for this level, not a pure cost-of-living comparison. The honest gap to name upfront: this requires relocating out of Geneva, with no relocation support offered, which is itself a cost worth pricing into the ask.

---

## Opening Anchor

**Target:** EUR 145,000 base, plus equity at the high end of whatever the standard founding-engineer grant is at Almedia's stage

When asked for a number:

> "Given the scope here, full ownership from architecture through rollout, and a founding-engineer track record that already includes a build-to-acquisition exit, I'd anchor around 145k. That also reflects that there's no relocation package on this one, so the number needs to cover an actual move, not just be competitive against a Berlin baseline."

Let them respond with their internal band before conceding on the relocation point.

---

## Counter-Offer Scripts

**If they come in at or near the bottom of the posted range (around 90 to 100k):**

> "I want to be direct: that's below what I'd need to make a move like this work, especially with no relocation support built in. For the ownership this role carries, ground-up scope and architecture decisions, I'd need to see something closer to 135 to 145k to say yes. Is there room to move on base, or to close the gap through a signing bonus that covers the move itself?"

**If they lean on "we're bootstrapped, we don't pay top-of-market cash":**

> "That's fair, and honestly it's part of why I'm interested, a profitable company not answering to a VC term sheet is rare. But bootstrapped also usually means no dilution stack eating into what equity is actually worth, so I'd want that reflected somewhere, either a stronger base or a more generous equity grant than a VC-backed company at the same stage would offer."

**If they push back on the relocation cost:**

> "I get that relocation support isn't standard here. I'm not asking for a full package, just for the base to account for the fact that this is a one-way move from Geneva with real costs attached, not a local hire."

**If they're firm on base and won't move it:**

Push on other levers, in this order:
1. One-time relocation and moving stipend: EUR 8,000 to 12,000, given none is offered by default
2. Equity increase: ask for the top of whatever band exists for a founding-engineer grant at this stage
3. Signing bonus: EUR 10,000 to 15,000, to bridge the first few months post-move
4. 90-day compensation review tied to shipping the first major owned project, rather than waiting a full year

> "If base is capped, I'd want to close the gap through a relocation stipend and a signing bonus, since those two costs are real and immediate for me in a way they might not be for a Berlin-based hire."

---

## Equity Negotiation

**Ask for:** top of whatever the standard founding-engineer band is at this stage; ask directly what that band is before naming a number, since Almedia hasn't taken outside funding and doesn't have a public round valuation to anchor against
**Structure:** standard 1-year cliff, 4-year vest, monthly vesting after cliff
**Questions to ask before accepting any equity number:**

- What's the current 409A or most recent internal valuation, given there's no external round to reference?
- Is this an options grant or something else, given the company is bootstrapped and majority founder-owned?
- What's the strike price, and what's the post-departure exercise window?
- Since Almedia just made its first acquisition (zave.it), how did that affect the cap table or option pool, if at all?
- Is there a refresher policy for early non-founder engineering hires?

**Framing if the equity number feels low:**

> "For a founding-engineer seat with this much scope, I'd expect equity that reflects real ownership in the business, not a token grant. Since there's no VC preference stack diluting outcomes here, the numbers should actually mean more per point than at a typical Series A company. I'd like to understand the full pool and where this grant sits in it before I evaluate the offer."

---

## Geographic and Relocation Pushback

If Almedia frames the offer purely against a Berlin cost-of-living baseline:

> "I understand benchmarking against Berlin for the team already there, but I'm not a local candidate, I'm relocating from Geneva, and that's a real cost with no support package attached. I'd rather we price this as a relocation hire, not a local one."

**Data point to cite if needed:** Swiss engineering salary benchmarks (salary.ch and similar sources) put senior product/founding-level engineering roles in Geneva or Zurich at CHF 150-200k, which converts to roughly EUR 144-192k, meaningfully above the posted Berlin band.

---

## Using Competing Offers

If other conversations are genuinely active:

> "I've got a couple of other founding and staff-level conversations moving in parallel, in a similar range once you account for equity. What makes Almedia different is that you're already profitable and shipping at scale instead of burning a Series A round, that's a real draw. But the offer needs to be close enough to that range, including something for the move, for it to be the clear choice."

If there's no competing offer in hand, don't invent one. Fall back to market data instead:

> "The Swiss market data I'm working from puts a role with this scope in the 150 to 190k CHF range. I know Berlin pays differently, but that's the frame I'm using to evaluate whether this move makes sense."

---

## Timeline and Process Notes

Before naming a final number, get clarity on how "meaningful equity" is actually defined at this stage, since a bootstrapped company's equity math is different from a VC-backed one and changes how the offer should be read.

> "Before I land on a number, I'd like to understand what 'meaningful equity' looks like in concrete terms here, percentage, vesting, and how the option pool has moved since the zave.it acquisition. That changes how I weigh base against equity."

**Offer deadline:** ask for at least 5 business days, more if a relocation decision needs to be discussed with family or a lease needs unwinding in Geneva.

> "I'd like a week to think this through properly, there's a relocation decision behind it, not just a job change. Let me know if there's a hard deadline I should be aware of."
