# Negotiation Script — n8n / Sr AI Engineer

---

## Market Context

**Role level:** "Sr AI Engineer" sits at the Senior IC band. At n8n's funding stage ($240M raised, $2.5B valuation), Senior AI Engineers with a founding background and production LLM experience command a premium above the generic Senior band. AI specialization is still attracting above-market comp at most funded companies.

**n8n's compensation model:** Berlin-headquartered, pays in EUR for European remote hires. No Geneva office means no local overhead for a Geneva hire.

**Estimated range for Sr AI Engineer at n8n:**
- Market range for this role in Europe: €110K to €145K
- With Magino's profile (founding engineer, 17 years, production AI/LLM work): realistic offer range €125K to €150K
- AI specialization premium at funded scale-ups currently adds €10-20K over generic senior engineering bands

**Geneva vs. Berlin cost delta:** Geneva consistently runs 40-60% more expensive than Berlin on housing, taxes, and living costs. A €125K offer calibrated to Berlin buys materially less in Geneva. Swiss social contributions add further overhead beyond what German employment carries.

**Switzerland market rate for Senior AI Engineer with this profile:**
- Senior AI/LLM Engineer at funded scale-up: CHF 140,000 to 175,000 base + equity
- Founding-engineer background with acquisition validation: adds CHF 10-15K of negotiating leverage
- EUR/CHF is approximately 0.95-1.00; EUR denomination is acceptable but CHF preferred to remove FX drift

**n8n equity:** Company is at late growth stage ($2.5B valuation, $240M raised). Senior-level options/ESOP typically represent 0.02-0.06% fully diluted. Meaningful given the growth trajectory and potential IPO or strategic exit.

## Negotiation Strategy

### Opening Anchor

If n8n asks for salary expectations before making an offer:

"Based on market data for Senior AI engineering in Switzerland and the AI specialization premium right now, I'm targeting €145K to €155K base, or CHF equivalent. Geneva sits in the same cost tier as Zurich and London, not Berlin, so I'd expect the package to reflect that gap. I'd also want to discuss equity given where n8n is in its trajectory."

If they push for a single number: "€150K is where I'd anchor the base."

### When They Make an Offer

**If the offer lands at €135K or above:** A reasonable starting point. Counter for the top of the range:

"I appreciate the offer. Given Geneva's cost structure relative to Berlin, I'd want to close toward €145-150K. Can you move on base, or if base has a ceiling, is there flexibility on the equity grant size or a sign-on component?"

**If the offer lands between €115K and €135K:** This signals geographic compression and is worth pushing back on directly:

"That's below what I'd expect for this level and specialization in Switzerland. The cost of living here is significantly higher than Berlin, and the Swiss tax and social contribution burden at this income is real. I'd need to see base move toward €135-140K minimum to feel the offer is calibrated for Geneva rather than a European average. If base has a hard ceiling, I'm happy to discuss whether equity or a sign-on can bridge the gap."

**If the offer is below €115K:** This is a blanket European discount that doesn't reflect the market or the AI premium. Push back firmly:

"To be direct, that's below what I'd expect for a Senior AI Engineer with production LLM experience and a full founding-to-acquisition background in Switzerland. The market for this profile here runs CHF 140-170K base. I'd need to see the offer move materially before I can consider it seriously."

---

## Equity Negotiation

n8n offers equity to all employees. At a $2.5B valuation with significant growth ambition, these options carry real potential value on a strategic exit or IPO.

**Questions to ask before signing:**
- "What's the current strike price and the most recent independent valuation?"
- "What percentage of fully diluted shares does this grant represent?"
- "What's the vesting schedule? Is there a one-year cliff?"
- "Does the agreement include single or double-trigger acceleration on acquisition?"
- "What was the price per share in the most recent primary round?"

**What to look for:**
- Standard: 4-year vest, 1-year cliff. Acceptable. Push back on anything beyond 4 years total.
- Acquisition acceleration: particularly important at n8n's stage, given its profile as a potential strategic target for a larger automation or AI platform.
- The €1K/year learning budget and $100/month OSS stipend are perks. Don't let them substitute for base or equity in the negotiation.

**If base is firm but equity feels light:**
"I'd consider anchoring slightly lower on base if the equity grant is meaningfully larger. What does a competitive Senior-level equity package look like at n8n? I want to be genuinely invested in the outcome."

---

## Geographic Discount Pushback

n8n defaults to Berlin-based compensation bands. Geneva is not a Berlin-equivalent market.

"I understand the logic of geographic bands. For context, Geneva consistently ranks alongside Zurich and London as one of the most expensive cities in Europe. The Swiss social contribution system and tax rates at this income level are materially different from Germany's. I'd ask you to use Geneva-specific data rather than a European average when calibrating the offer. Happy to share cost-of-living comparisons if that helps your process."

---

## Remote Premium Angle

n8n doesn't have a Geneva office. There are no local office costs, HR overhead, or facilities footprint associated with a Geneva hire.

"You don't carry any local office costs for a Geneva hire. I'd expect that overhead saving to factor into the package calibration rather than being absorbed as margin on a below-market offer."

---

## AI Specialization Premium Angle

AI engineering at the LLM/agent level is a distinct skill set with limited supply and strong demand. It's worth naming this explicitly if the offer feels like a generic senior engineering band:

"The role is specifically for AI engineering: LLM orchestration, RAG pipelines, agent architectures, evaluation systems. That specialization commands a premium over a generic senior backend band right now, and the offer should reflect it. I'm not asking for CTO comp; I'm asking for the market rate for someone who's shipped this work to production."

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

If another offer is in hand:

"I want to be transparent: I have an offer from [Company] at a comparable stage at around CHF 155-165K. n8n's AI engineering scope is more interesting to me because of the workflow infrastructure angle. But I need the package to be close enough that I'm not leaving significant comp on the table."

If no competing offer:

"I've been deliberate in this search, which is why I'm at a late stage with only a small number of companies. n8n is at the top of that list for the technical problem. But I want to make sure the package reflects the level and specialization before I close off other conversations."

---

## Timing and Closing

Once an offer is verbal, ask for it in writing and take 48 hours before responding. Use that window to:

1. Verify equity details: strike price, grant size as percentage of fully diluted shares, vesting schedule, and acceleration clause
2. Confirm currency denomination (EUR or CHF) and check for any FX adjustment mechanism
3. Review any non-compete or IP assignment clauses under Swiss employment law
4. Confirm start date flexibility and that Swiss work authorization is clear (EU passport holders have automatic right to work in Switzerland)

Do not accept verbally before seeing the written offer document.
