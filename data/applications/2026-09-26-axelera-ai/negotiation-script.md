# Negotiation Script: Axelera AI, Staff/Principal Software Engineer (AI Applications)

## Context before talking numbers

Axelera AI is a Dutch deep-tech scale-up (Metis AIPU, Voyager SDK) with about $450M raised and 250+ employees. The role is listed for Zürich, but they'll also take Leuven, Eindhoven/Amsterdam, Florence/Milan, Bristol, or fully remote in Europe, and they say they prioritise candidates in Bavaria, Belgium or Italy. That tells you their pay bands are probably set with lower-cost locations in mind. Expect them to offer a band that works in Leuven or Milan, and be ready to argue that a Swiss hire gets paid Swiss rates.

Two things to clear up early:
1. **Which contract?** Swiss employment through their Zürich office, or remote on another entity? Swiss payroll is what I want: it settles social charges, the pension (BVG/LPP), and which country taxes the stock options.
2. **Staff or Principal?** The title says "Staff/Principal". Get the level pinned down before any number, because it probably decides the band. Given the domain gap (no TensorRT/OpenVINO or quantization track record), they may lean toward Staff. Argue for Principal on scope (SDK architecture, cross-team contracts, mentoring), but don't make it a dealbreaker.

**My numbers**

- **Opening anchor:** CHF 225k base plus share options.
- **Target:** CHF 200k-215k base plus a meaningful option grant.
- **Walk-away:** below ~CHF 180k base on Swiss payroll. Below that, it's a step down from Geneva Staff-level roles, and the commute to Zürich or the remote setup costs something too.
- If they'll only hire on a non-Swiss entity or as a contractor: equivalent daily rate of about CHF 1,000-1,200, after I've checked social charges and tax with an advisor.

## Opening anchor script

> "Before I give a number, can you walk me through the full package: base, bonus if there is one, the option grant, and the pension setup in Switzerland? [Listen.] For a Staff/Principal role owning part of the Voyager application layer, based in Switzerland, I'm looking at around CHF 225k base. That's in line with principal roles at Zürich scale-ups. I'm flexible on how cash and options are split if the options are well structured."

Say the number, then stop talking.

## If they come in low (for example CHF 160k-175k, or a euro figure)

> "Thanks, I understand that's probably consistent with your bands in Leuven or Eindhoven. The thing is, I'm in Switzerland, and my alternatives here are Staff and principal roles that start above that. I'd need to get to at least CHF 200k base. If base is really fixed, let's look at a larger option grant, a signing bonus, and a written review at six months against goals we agree now."

If base doesn't move, trade in this order:
1. A larger option grant (ask for the grant as a percentage of fully diluted shares, not just a count).
2. A signing bonus to cover the first-year gap.
3. A written six-month review with a pre-agreed increase if the goals are met.
4. Level and scope in writing (Principal, named areas of ownership in the SDK).
5. Flexibility: mostly remote from Geneva, with Zürich days as needed, and travel to Eindhoven/Leuven paid by the company.

## Pushing back on the geographic discount

> "I understand you hire across Europe and want consistent bands. But the posting lists Zürich as the primary location, and a Swiss employee comes with Swiss costs: pension, insurance and cost of living. I'm not asking for Google Zürich pay. I'm asking to be paid like a principal engineer at a Swiss scale-up, which is what this role is when it's based here."

If they suggest a Belgian/Dutch contract with me working from Geneva: say no, or at least be very careful. Cross-border social security, Swiss tax residence and permanent-establishment rules make it messy for both sides. Swiss payroll through the Zürich office is the clean option.

## Share options at a heavily funded deep-tech company

With $450M raised, the preference stack matters as much as the percentage. Questions to ask:

- **What instrument, what entity?** Options on the Dutch parent (likely a B.V.), or something else? Ask for the plan document, not a summary.
- **How much, relative to what?** Number of options, fully diluted share count, strike price, and the price per share at the last round. A number of options without a denominator tells you nothing.
- **Liquidation preferences.** After several large rounds, investors are paid first in an exit. Ask whether preferences are 1x non-participating (standard) or anything more aggressive. That decides whether common shares are worth anything in a middling exit.
- **Vesting.** 4 years with a 1-year cliff is standard. Ask for monthly or quarterly vesting after the cliff.
- **Acceleration.** Ask for double-trigger acceleration (change of control plus termination), ideally 50-100%.
- **Exercise window after leaving.** Push for 5-10 years instead of the usual 90 days, or confirm there's a cashless or net exercise option.
- **Refresh grants.** Ask whether there's a refresh policy for senior ICs, since the first grant is diluted by every future round.
- **Swiss tax.** In Switzerland, unlisted options are generally taxed as income at exercise, not at grant, but check with a Swiss tax advisor and ask HR if other Swiss employees have been through it. Ask whether the company handles the Swiss withholding and reporting.

## Competing offer template

Only use it if it's true, or phrase it as an alternative without inventing an offer.

> "I want to be transparent: I'm also in late-stage conversations for a principal-level role in Switzerland, with a base in the CHF 210k-230k range. Axelera is my preference because of the Voyager work and the hardware angle, and I'd like to make it work. Can we close the gap on base, or make it up with the option grant?"

If there's no real competing offer:

> "I'm talking to a few other companies at the Staff and principal level in Switzerland, and the bands I'm seeing start around CHF 200k. I'd rather be here, so I want to get the numbers to a place where it's an easy yes."

## Before signing

- Get the level, base, grant (count, strike, fully diluted percentage), vesting and acceleration terms in writing.
- Confirm pension contributions (BVG/LPP), insurance, and whether the employer pays above the legal minimum.
- Confirm the working location arrangement (Geneva remote plus Zürich office days) in the contract, not just by email.
- Have a Swiss tax advisor look at the option plan before accepting.
