# Negotiation Script - Paradvisor, CTO / Co-Founder

## Context

- Pre-seed, Zurich-based, two-sided marketplace connecting independent investment advisors with retail clients, Vue.js stack front and back.
- No confirmed funding round on the listing, so this is a founder-equity conversation first and a salary conversation second, not the reverse.
- Magino brings 17+ years including a founding-engineer exit (Alaya to Benevity, acquired by a Canadian unicorn), which is well above what a typical pre-seed technical cofounder search turns up.
- Relocation: Geneva to Zurich, both Swiss, so this is a moving-cost conversation, not a visa or cross-border one.

At pre-seed the real negotiation isn't base salary, it's the founder equity split, decision rights, and how much of a stipend the company can actually afford without burning the seed round before it closes.

## Market anchor (Zurich pre-seed, technical cofounder)

For a technical cofounder joining pre-idea-validation at a Swiss pre-seed startup, equity splits between a non-technical founder who originated the idea and a technical cofounder joining slightly later typically land somewhere between 60/40 and 50/50, weighted by who's raised money, who has existing traction, and who's putting in full-time hours first. A founding CTO joining after the idea and possibly a first prototype exists, but before a funding round has closed, should expect to anchor toward 35% to 45% if genuinely joining as an equal partner in decision-making, not lower unless there's already meaningful traction or capital in the business that Magino didn't contribute to. Cash comp at this stage is usually a token monthly stipend, CHF 3,000 to 6,000 if the company has any pre-seed capital at all, or CHF 0 if it's entirely bootstrapped and pre-funding.

## Opening anchor script

"Before we talk about a split, I want to understand where the company actually stands: how much of the product exists, whether there's a first version of the trust and matching problem solved already, and whether any capital has come in yet. If I'm joining as a genuine technical cofounder rather than a very early hire, I'd want to be around 40% of the founding equity, vesting over four years with a one-year cliff, and I'd want CTO title and equal say on product and technical direction reflected in whatever governance document we set up, not just implied."

## Counter-offer script if they come in low

"I get that you've been building this alone and have more invested so far, so I'm not expecting a straight 50/50 split. But if the number you're proposing puts me meaningfully below a third while I'm expected to own the entire technical build and be full-time from day one, that doesn't match the risk I'd be taking on. I'd rather find a split that reflects sweat equity going forward fairly, even if that means a vesting schedule that front-loads more of my allocation into the first eighteen months so it's earned quickly rather than argued about later."

## Equity mechanics to nail down before signing anything

- Get the actual cap table, not just percentages, including any advisor or friends-and-family allocations already granted.
- Standard vesting for a new cofounder: 4-year vesting, 1-year cliff, and confirm whether unvested shares are clawed back on an early exit versus staying with the leaving party under Swiss AG or GmbH structuring, this varies and a lawyer should confirm it, not a handshake.
- Ask about a founder vesting acceleration clause if the company is acquired or if the other cofounder leaves early, this protects against being the only one still vesting after a partner exits.
- If a pre-seed round is imminent, ask how much dilution both founders will take together, and whether there's an option pool being carved out of the founders' shares or set aside separately.
- Confirm IP assignment terms explicitly: everything built for Paradvisor belongs to Paradvisor, and any prior Magino side projects are excluded in writing.

## Cash stipend and runway

- If there's pre-seed capital already in the bank, ask directly how many months of runway a CHF 4,000 to 5,000 monthly stipend for each founder would leave, and treat this as a joint planning conversation rather than a demand.
- If the company is fully bootstrapped, be explicit about how long Magino can go without any stipend, and set a check-in point, three to six months, to revisit once a round closes rather than leaving it open-ended.

## Geographic and relocation notes

- Zurich versus Geneva isn't a cost-of-living discount conversation the way a Geneva-to-abroad move would be, both are expensive Swiss cities, but moving costs and any temporary housing during the transition are still worth raising if the company has any capital to work with.
- If the role is remote-friendly or hybrid, get that in writing early since it affects the actual relocation timeline and whether a move needs to happen before or after the first funding milestone.

## Competing option leverage template

"I want to be upfront that a technical cofounder role is a serious commitment, and I'm also weighing a couple of senior IC and platform-lead opportunities that come with immediate market-rate cash comp and much less risk. I'd rather build something from zero again given the chance, but I need the equity and governance terms here to actually reflect founder-level ownership for that trade-off to make sense."

Use this only if it's genuinely true; don't invent a specific competing offer or number if asked to name one.
