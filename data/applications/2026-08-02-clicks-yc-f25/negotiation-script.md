# Negotiation Script: Clicks (YC F25), Software Engineer, Zurich

Posted range: $120,000 to $160,000 base, equity 0.50% to 2.00%, on-site Zurich. Seed-stage, YC F25, backed by Fly Ventures and 20VC, $4M raised, 8 people on the team as of the posting. Magino brings 17+ years and an actual founding-engineer-to-acquisition outcome against a role that likely targets 2 to 5 years of experience, which is the core leverage point for the whole conversation.

## Market anchors (Zurich, backend/infra engineer, seed-stage AI startup)

Zurich backend engineers at funded seed-stage startups typically land CHF 110,000-140,000 base, roughly $125,000-$160,000 at current rates, so the posted USD range sits at fair market value for a mid-level hire but is light for someone with a proven exit and 17 years of production experience. Zurich cost of living runs close to Geneva, so there's no geographic discount to concede on either side. Seed-stage companies often quote a lower base specifically to protect the equity pool for early hires, so total comp, not base alone, is the number worth anchoring on.

## Opening anchor

Lead with the experience gap and total comp together: "The posted range looks calibrated for someone a few years into their career. I've got 17 years, most of it building and owning backend systems in production, plus a founding-engineer background that includes taking a platform from zero to an acquisition by a company valued north of a billion. I'd like to talk about $155,000-$160,000 base with equity toward the top of your band, rather than the middle of the posted range." Following the number immediately with the Alaya to Benevity proof point keeps it from reading as a generic ask.

## If they come in at or near the posted range

Counter script: "I understand the range was likely set with a more junior profile in mind. Given that I've already run the outcome you're presumably hoping this bet leads to, building something from nothing through to acquisition, I'd like to explore $150,000 base with equity closer to 1.5%, or $140,000 base with equity nearer 2%, whichever gives you more room on your side." Offering two paths keeps the conversation moving instead of stalling on one rejected number.

## Equity negotiation

Ask directly what the fully diluted share count looks like and what round this equity is priced against before agreeing to a percentage; a headline number without the denominator tells you nothing. For a non-founder engineering hire at a seed-stage company with 8 people and a fresh $4M round, 0.75%-2% is a realistic band depending on how senior the mandate is; push toward the top given the experience and the fact this is effectively employee number 9 or 10. Confirm standard 4-year vesting with a 1-year cliff, and ask specifically about acceleration on acquisition, single or double trigger, since Magino has been on the other side of exactly this clause and knows how much it matters when a deal actually happens. Get the strike price and current 409A or Swiss equivalent valuation in writing before signing anything.

## Relocation and Zurich on-site terms

The role is on-site in Zurich; Magino is in Geneva. Script: "I'm ready to relocate to Zurich, that's not a blocker. I'd like to talk through relocation support, and whether a short transition period, splitting time between Geneva and Zurich for the first few weeks while housing gets sorted, is workable given the team's size." This is a one-time ask, not a recurring cost, since Zurich isn't materially cheaper than Geneva to live in.

## Competing offer template

Use only if genuinely in play, kept low-key: "I'm in later-stage conversations with another team for a similar backend and infra role, so I'd like to move at a reasonable pace here if the fit is right on both sides. I'd rather make a decision based on this conversation than have you compete against a number I can't fully disclose."

## Close

End with a concrete next step rather than an open-ended "let me think about it": propose a specific date to reconvene on numbers, and ask what Clicks needs to see next, a technical screen, a pairing session on their agent infrastructure, or references from the Benevity or Alaya years, to get to a final offer.
