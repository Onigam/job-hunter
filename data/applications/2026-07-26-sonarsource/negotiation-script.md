# Negotiation Script: Staff Site Reliability Engineer, SonarSource

## A location flag to resolve before any of this matters

The posting lists the role as Austin, TX, hybrid (three days in office), and states no visa sponsorship is available. Magino is Geneva-based. Before spending time on comp strategy, the first question on any call needs to be whether this is open to remote-from-Switzerland, a relocation package, or genuinely requires existing US work authorization, because if it's strictly the last one, the rest of this script is moot. What follows gives a Swiss-market anchor for a remote-from-Geneva or relocation-with-support conversation, plus the US figures for context if the company can only hire onsite in Austin.

## Market Context

General Site Reliability Engineer pay in Switzerland runs about CHF 139,500 to 190,500 (25th to 75th percentile), averaging around CHF 159,000 nationally and CHF 168,500 in Zurich, per Glassdoor and ERI SalaryExpert data. Staff level, meaning broad ownership of reliability strategy, SLOs, and incident process rather than a single service, typically sits 25-40% above that general band. That puts a realistic Swiss-market anchor for this scope at roughly CHF 190,000 to 225,000 base.

On the US side, Staff SRE pay in Austin averages around $202,600, with the 25th to 75th percentile spanning roughly $167,000 to $250,000, and senior individual reports landing well above $300,000 total comp at the 10-15 year mark. SonarSource is a mature, profitable company (backed by Insight Partners, Advent, Permira, among others), not an early bet, so the negotiation posture should lean toward "pay me what a company at your scale pays for this scope," not "convince me the equity is worth the risk."

**Working anchor if remote/relocation is on the table: CHF 200,000-230,000 base.** If the conversation shifts to a US-payroll Austin hire: $215,000-250,000 base. Either way, this is a starting hypothesis to sharpen once a number is on the table.

## Opening Anchor

If asked for expectations before an offer, and the location question is still open:

"Before we talk numbers, I want to understand the setup, is this open to remote from Geneva or relocation support, or does it need to be someone already authorized to work in Austin? That changes how I'd frame this. If it's a Switzerland-based arrangement, I'd expect a base in the CHF 200,000 to 225,000 range for a Staff-level reliability mandate. If it's a US hire in Austin, that would be closer to $220,000 to 250,000."

If pushed for a single number in the Swiss frame: "Around CHF 215,000 base, with bonus and any equity or RSU component built on top."

## If the Offer Lands Low

**Offer at or above the anchor:** Good starting point. Shift to bonus structure, equity mechanics, and vesting rather than pushing base further.

**Offer 10-15% below anchor (roughly CHF 170,000-190,000, or $190,000-210,000 in the US frame):**

"I appreciate the offer. Given this role covers SLO ownership, security automation, and on-call across five global offices, not a single-team scope, I'd expect this closer to the CHF 210,000 range (or $230,000 if this stays a US-payroll role). Is there room to move there, or flexibility on bonus to close the gap?"

**Offer meaningfully below that:**

"To be direct, that number reads closer to a senior IC scope than a Staff one, particularly for a role that owns error budgets and incident process across the whole SDLC. I'd need to see this closer to my opening range for the level and responsibility in the posting to make sense."

## Equity Notes

SonarSource is past the stage where equity is a lottery ticket, so this is a mechanics conversation, not a conviction sale.

Questions worth asking directly: is the equity component RSUs, options, or something else, and how does that interact with the company's current stage toward any future liquidity event. What's the vesting schedule, standard four years with a one-year cliff, or different. For a Staff-level grant, what's the typical range as a percentage of base for someone at this scope. If there's ever an IPO or acquisition, how does that affect vesting acceleration for individual contributors versus management.

Treat whatever equity is offered as a real but illiquid long-term component. Don't let a rich-sounding grant pull the base number down.

## Geographic Discount Pushback

If the conversation frames a Geneva-based or remote arrangement as a discount relative to onsite Austin pay: Geneva's cost of living is on par with major US tech hubs for housing and daily expenses, and a remote-from-Switzerland arrangement removes the company's office overhead entirely, that's not a reason to pay less, if anything it offsets the lack of an onsite presence. If the pushback instead frames Austin as cheaper than, say, the Bay Area, that's a fair point about the local market but shouldn't be used to discount a Staff-level scope below the Austin band itself.

## Competing Offer Leverage Template

Use only if a real competing process exists.

"I'm in later-stage conversations with another company for comparable reliability scope, and the numbers there are landing above my opening range. I'd rather move forward here if the scope, team, and location logistics line up, so I wanted to be transparent about where the market's pricing this."

## Closing Notes

Confirm the reporting line and whether this role sits within a platform org or a standalone SRE function, since that materially changes scope and banding. Get clarity in writing on the visa sponsorship question early, not after multiple interview rounds. Ask about the on-call rotation structure and compensation for it specifically, since five global offices usually means a follow-the-sun schedule, and that should be priced into the offer, not assumed as part of base.
