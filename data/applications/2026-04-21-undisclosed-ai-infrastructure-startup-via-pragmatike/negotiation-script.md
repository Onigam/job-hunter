# Negotiation Script

## Role framing

This is not a standard senior full stack hire. The role combines product-facing React work, backend ownership, AI-integrated data pipelines, async processing, and founding-level ambiguity. The job description also points to direct work with founders on architecture and technical strategy. For a Switzerland-based candidate with Senior Staff and founder background, that should be priced above a normal senior full stack band.

## Market anchors

SwissDevJobs' 2024 market report shows Geneva among the higher-paying Swiss tech markets, with average software salaries around CHF 103k and top-end city salaries around CHF 135k. That is broad-market data, not founding-engineer data.

Sovereign Salary's Geneva benchmark for Principal Software Engineer shows roughly CHF 251k gross as a local principal-level reference point, with about CHF 164k net. I treat that as an upper local-market marker, not a direct startup benchmark.

Robert Half's Switzerland guide lists CTO salary benchmarks around CHF 166.8k at the 25th percentile, CHF 187.5k at the median, and CHF 210.5k at the 75th percentile. For an early-stage startup, that is useful as an executive-side ceiling check.

My read is that a realistic Switzerland-based target for this role is:

- Cash-heavy package: CHF 185k to CHF 210k base, plus meaningful equity
- If they insist on broader EMEA pricing: do not go below CHF 160k base unless equity is clearly material
- Equity target: ask for a range and push for something that reflects true founding scope, not token options

## Opening anchor

Use this when they ask for expectations:

> Based on the scope here, full stack delivery, AI-integrated infrastructure, and founding-level ownership, I'm targeting a package built around CHF 195k to CHF 210k base, with equity that reflects the fact that this is an early technical hire. How are you thinking about the cash and equity split?

## If they anchor low on salary

Use this if they come in around typical remote EMEA senior-engineer pricing:

> I understand the role may be benchmarked broadly across EMEA, but my reference point is the actual scope of the job and the level of ownership you're asking for. This is closer to a founding platform engineer than a standard senior full stack seat. If the cash band is fixed, then the equity needs to move enough to compensate for that.

## If they say the role is "senior" not "staff"

> Titles matter less to me than scope. What I'm pricing is end-to-end ownership, architecture decisions close to the founders, and the expectation that I can operate independently in an ambiguous environment from day one. That's not ordinary senior scope.

## Equity questions to ask

- What is the exact equity range for this role?
- What fully diluted percentage does that represent today?
- What is the current valuation context for the option strike?
- Is there a standard 4-year vest with 1-year cliff, or any flexibility for an early senior hire?
- Is there accelerated vesting on change of control or termination without cause after an acquisition?
- Is there a refresh policy after the initial grant?

## Equity push points

If base is below target:

> If we need to stay lower on salary, I can work with that only if the equity reflects the early-stage risk and the actual scope of the role. I'd want to understand the percentage, dilution assumptions, and whether there's any acceleration protection.

If the grant sounds symbolic:

> I want to avoid a situation where the equity is presented as upside but is too small to matter in any realistic outcome. If this is a founding engineer role in practice, the grant should look like one.

## Switzerland-specific pushback

If they apply a geographic discount:

> I'm based in Geneva, which is one of the most expensive labor markets in Europe. I'm not asking to be matched to a big-tech package, but the compensation still needs to reflect Switzerland-level cost and the seniority of the role.

If they push remote-market logic:

> Remote cuts both ways. It expands your hiring pool, but it also means you're asking for someone who can self-direct, make architectural calls, and ship without a lot of management overhead. That usually deserves a premium, not a discount.

## Counter-offer script

> I like the role and the problem space. To get this over the line, I'd need one of two things: either bring the base closer to CHF 190k, or keep the current base and improve the equity package enough that the tradeoff is real. If you can move on one of those two dimensions, I think we're close.

## Close

Do not accept on the call. Ask for the full package in writing, including equity terms. The minimum useful details are base salary, bonus if any, number of options or percentage, vesting schedule, strike price or valuation context, and any acceleration terms.
