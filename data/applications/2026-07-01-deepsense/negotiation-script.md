# Negotiation Script: DeepSense, Founding DevOps / Platform Engineer

## Context to anchor on

The posted band for this role is roughly $60K to $90K, remote, which reads like a global rate built around a lower cost-of-living hire rather than a Senior Staff profile with 17+ years and a founding-engineer track record. DeepSense is a small AI-first online slots studio, 11 to 50 people, remote-first, building the automation that generates, validates, and scales the games themselves. That's a real founding-level scope even if the posted number doesn't reflect it yet, so the first job in this conversation is closing the gap between the title and the pay band before getting into specifics.

## Opening anchor

Don't reject the posted range outright, since it may just be a placeholder aimed at a broader applicant pool. Instead, anchor on scope and seniority before a number gets locked in.

"Before we talk numbers, I want to understand what 'founding' means here in terms of ownership, whether this is one person defining the whole automation and infrastructure layer or a role inside a bigger existing team. Given 17 years of doing exactly that kind of build, most recently taking Alaya from zero to an acquisition by a billion-dollar company, the posted range reads more like an early hire than a founding one. For Switzerland-based Staff-level infrastructure work, even remote, I'd expect a base closer to CHF 140K to 170K, or the USD equivalent, plus equity that reflects the founding label."

## If they come in low on salary

Early-stage companies often protect runway by leading low. Redirect toward total package rather than arguing the number in isolation.

"I get that a slots studio at this size is watching burn closely, so if the base has to sit near the posted range, I'd want the difference made up in equity and title, something that actually reflects sole ownership of the automation stack rather than a support role. I'd also want a written comp review at 6 or 12 months tied to concrete milestones, not a verbal promise, since that's usually how the gap between an early-stage number and a fair one gets closed later."

## Equity negotiation

A founding title with a sub-market salary should come with real equity, not a token grant.

"If the base stays close to what's posted, I'd want equity in the range that matches a founding hire rather than an early employee, with standard 4 year vesting and a 1 year cliff. I'd want to see the cap table shape first, how much is left in the option pool, and whether there's any anti-dilution protection for early hires going into a priced round. A small percentage on a studio with real product-market fit in slots can still be meaningful, but I need the actual numbers before agreeing to a figure."

## Geographic angle

Remote-first should mean the pay isn't quietly discounted for being Geneva-based, but it's worth confirming rather than assuming.

"Since the role is remote-first, I want to check whether comp is set globally or adjusted by location. Geneva is expensive, and if there's a location-based adjustment, I'd want the conversation to be explicit about it rather than something that shows up as a lower number without explanation. I'm not asking for a Swiss market rate to be paid regardless of where the company is based, just for the logic behind the number to be visible."

## Competing offer template

Use only if there's an actual live process; a vague bluff is easy to spot and costs credibility.

"I'm further along with a couple of other companies at a similar stage, and the comp conversations there have been closer to what I mentioned. I'd rather decide this on fit and what DeepSense is actually building, but if there's room to move once we're both serious, I'd want to know before those other conversations close."

## Closing move

End on scope and ownership, since that's what makes the number defensible either way.

"None of this is a blocker if the role really is what 'founding' implies. I just want the comp and the scope to match, because if I'm the one building and owning this from the ground up, I'd rather that be reflected upfront than negotiated for the next two years."
