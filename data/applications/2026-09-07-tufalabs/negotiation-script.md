# Negotiation Script: Tufa Labs Research Engineer (Zurich)

## Market Context

Tufa Labs' own posting has referenced a 100,000 to 200,000 CHF band, with senior roles negotiable toward the top. For a Senior Staff-equivalent profile (17 years, founding-engineer background, last five years at Staff+ level), the real Zurich market for research-adjacent systems engineering runs closer to 160,000 to 210,000 CHF total comp, base plus equity plus any signing component.

Relevant facts going in:
- Tufa Labs is independent and reportedly well capitalized, with multi-year compute runway (the DGX B200 / NVL72 GB300 hardware mentioned in the posting isn't cheap to run casually)
- The role requires relocation to Zurich within six months; Geneva to Zurich is not a small move
- Zurich and Geneva cost of living are broadly comparable, so this isn't a discount conversation, but relocation costs are real
- As an EU/EFTA-adjacent profile, visa sponsorship shouldn't be a blocker

---

## Opening Anchor

When asked for salary expectations, anchor at 180,000 CHF base.

Script:
> "Given 17 years of experience, a founding-engineer track record, and five years at Staff-plus level, plus what I'm seeing for senior systems roles in Zurich right now, I'd want to start somewhere around 175,000 to 190,000 CHF base. Where does that land relative to what you've got budgeted?"

This works because it's grounded in your own history and public comp data, it opens a conversation instead of closing one, and the range signals you're willing to talk rather than digging in.

---

## If They Come In Low (100-130k CHF)

Script:
> "Thanks for being upfront about the number. My concern is that this would be a real step down from where I am now, and it's a bit below what I'm seeing for this level in the Zurich market. I'd like to make it work, so, is the base fixed, or is there room to get closer to 160-165k with equity filling in the rest? I'd rather sort the numbers out now than run into a mismatch six months in."

The move here is separating base from total comp. If the equity story is genuinely strong, a lower base can make sense, but you need the actual terms to evaluate that, not a hand-wave.

---

## Equity Questions to Ask Before Accepting Anything

1. Options, RSUs, or direct shares, and how does that interact with being an independent lab rather than a traditional VC-backed startup?
2. Vesting schedule: is it the standard four years with a one-year cliff, and does it vest monthly after the cliff?
3. If options, what's the strike price and what valuation is it based on?
4. Any anti-dilution protection for early hires if there's a future funding or restructuring event?
5. Is there a refresh grant policy, or is this a one-time award?

Script:
> "Before I can weigh the full package, can you walk me through the equity structure, vesting schedule, and whether there's a refresh policy down the line? I want to compare this properly against the base."

---

## Relocation Pushback

The posting asks for relocation within six months. That's a genuine disruption from Geneva.

Script:
> "I'm fine with the relocation piece, but it's a real cost: moving, potentially breaking a lease, finding housing in a tight Zurich market. Is there a relocation allowance or a signing component that helps bridge that gap? I'm not trying to pad the offer, just cover what it actually costs to move."

Reasonable ask: 5,000 to 10,000 CHF one-time relocation support, or an extra month's pay as a signing bonus.

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

If there's a real competing offer, use it plainly:
> "I want to be straightforward: I've got another offer in progress that's meaningfully higher. I'm more drawn to Tufa Labs because of the research focus and the compute, but I'd need you to close some of that gap."

Without a real competing offer, don't invent one. Lean on market data instead:
> "I've looked into Zurich comp for this profile across a few sources, and it clusters around 160-170k base for someone at this level. That's the reference point I'm working from, and I want it to be fair to both of us."

---

## Walk-Away Point

- Minimum acceptable base: 145,000 CHF, with equity that has actual disclosed terms
- If base lands under 145k and equity terms are vague or undisclosed, decline politely rather than accept on faith
- Given a four-year vesting horizon, weigh it against the lab's compute roadmap and stated runway before treating the equity number as real

---

## Closing Script

Once a number is agreed:
> "I can make [X CHF] base with [equity terms] work. Let's move. What's the next step to get to a signed offer?"

Don't reopen a number once it's agreed. Close it and move to logistics.
