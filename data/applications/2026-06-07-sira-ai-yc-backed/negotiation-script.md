# Negotiation Script — Sira AI Contractor Role

## Context

Sira is a YC S25 company, founded 2025, based in San Francisco. This is a remote contractor role. Very early stage (pre-Series A or at seed). Founding engineer contractor positions at YC companies typically have the following dynamics:
- Rate negotiation happens before or at offer, not during
- Equity for contractors is rare but not impossible at founding-team level; worth asking
- SF-based companies often default to USD rates; push for CHF if that's cleaner for you
- Early contractors at YC companies sometimes convert to full-time equity roles — keep that door open

---

## Market Rate Anchor

Senior full-stack founding engineer contractor (remote, Europe-based):

- **Switzerland contractor market:** CHF 160-240/hour for Senior Staff-level full-stack
- **USD equivalent (SF-based YC company):** $150-200/hour
- **Monthly retainer equivalent:** CHF 18,000-24,000/month (assuming ~120h/month)
- **Comparable reference:** Toptal/Arc senior contractors at this level range $130-180/hour; founding-engineer premium adds 15-25%

Your anchor, given 17 years of experience and a co-founder track record: **CHF 200/hour** or **CHF 22,000/month retainer**.

---

## Opening Script

If asked "what are your rate expectations?":

> "For founding engineer contractor work, I typically work on a monthly retainer. Given the scope and level of involvement you're describing, I'd want to be in the CHF 20,000-22,000/month range, or the USD equivalent if that's simpler for your payroll. That's in line with what senior founding engineers are billing in this market, and it reflects the expectation that I'm involved in architecture decisions, not just feature delivery."

If they ask for hourly:

> "If hourly works better for you, I'd say CHF 195-210/hour. At full-time equivalent that maps to roughly the same number."

---

## Counter-Offer Script (If They Come in Low)

If they offer CHF 140/hour or CHF 14,000-15,000/month:

> "I appreciate the offer. The rate is a bit below where I'd need to be. To put it in context: at Alaya I was the technical co-founder, so I've done exactly this kind of work, and the expectation I have for myself in a founding engineer role is full ownership, not scoped delivery. If we can get to CHF 180-200/hour, I can commit properly. Is there flexibility there?"

If they cite budget constraints:

> "I understand early-stage constraints are real. One option worth discussing: a lower base rate with a small equity grant. If I'm contributing to the technical foundation of the company, some alignment on upside makes sense for both sides. Even a modest advisory-level grant would be meaningful."

---

## Equity Conversation (If Applicable)

Contractors at YC companies at founding stage can sometimes negotiate advisory equity (0.1-0.5%) or a conversion path to full-time with standard founder/early employee equity.

**How to raise it:**

> "Given that this is founding engineer work and I'd be shaping the technical architecture from day one, I'd want to understand if there's any equity component available for contractors. I'm not expecting standard employee terms, but some alignment with the company's outcome seems worth discussing."

**If they say contractors can't get equity:**

> "Understood. In that case, the rate needs to reflect the full value of the contribution, including the founding-level ownership I'd be taking on. That puts me at CHF 200+/hour."

---

## Geographic Pushback (If They Discount for Europe/Remote)

If they try to apply a geographic discount versus SF market rates:

> "The work is remote and the output is the same regardless of where I'm based. Geneva is one of the more expensive cities in Europe, comparable to San Francisco on cost of living. I'd rather keep the conversation on the work and the value rather than on geography."

---

## Competing Offer Template

If you have or want to signal competing interest:

> "I'm in conversations with another YC company for a similar founding engineer contract. I'd prefer to work with Sira given the problem space, but I'll need to make a decision in the next week or so. Is there a timeline on your end that works with that?"

## Additional Notes

- Confirm payment terms upfront: net-15 or net-30, invoicing cadence, currency
- Ask about scope: hours per week expected, whether there's a minimum commitment
- If they mention converting to full-time later, ask what that equity package looks like before committing to a low rate now
- Swiss work permit / tax: contractor invoices from Switzerland to a US company are straightforward; no withholding on either side, but confirm they're OK with a Swiss invoice (most YC companies are)
