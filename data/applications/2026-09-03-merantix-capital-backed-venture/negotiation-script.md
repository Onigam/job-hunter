# Negotiation Script: Co-founder & CTO, Stealth AI Infrastructure for Physical Commodity Trading

Context: pre-seed, venture-studio-backed (Merantix Capital), single trading-founder, working capital and initial commercial flows already live. This is a true cofounder slot, not an employee CTO hire, so the negotiation is mostly about equity split and vesting terms, with cash comp being secondary and likely below a normal Staff/Principal market rate.

## Switzerland market anchors

- Senior Staff / Principal Engineer, Zurich or Geneva, established company: CHF 170,000-220,000 base, plus bonus.
- CTO, funded startup (seed to Series A), Switzerland: CHF 150,000-200,000 base, often with equity on top.
- CTO / technical cofounder, pre-seed or idea-stage venture: CHF 100,000-150,000 base is typical, sometimes below-market cash by design, offset by meaningful equity. Some idea-stage studio deals pay closer to CHF 80,000-120,000 until the seed round closes.
- Zurich cost of living is comparable to Geneva, so there's no real geographic discount to concede on either side.

My anchor: aim for CHF 140,000-160,000 base once the venture has its pre-seed round closed, with a lower interim figure (CHF 90,000-110,000) acceptable only if it's explicitly tied to a defined runway and a near-term raise, not indefinite.

## Opening anchor

"Given the studio backing and that there's already working capital and live commercial flows, I'd expect a base in the CHF 140,000-160,000 range once we're past the interim, pre-raise period, plus a cofounder-level equity stake with standard four-year vesting. I know idea-stage cash comp is usually lower than that, so let's talk about how the interim period is structured and how equity offsets it."

## If they come in low on cash

"I get that pre-seed cash comp is compressed, and I'm not looking to make this a pure salary negotiation. What I need is clarity: what's the interim base, how long does that period realistically last before the seed round, and does the base step up automatically once funding lands, or is that a separate conversation each time? If the interim number is going to sit meaningfully below CHF 100,000, I'd want the equity and the timeline to reflect that I'm carrying more of the founding risk, not less."

## Equity negotiation

- Ask directly what percentage is being offered and what the fully diluted cap table looks like today, including the studio's stake, any advisor pool, and the option pool being carved out.
- For a genuine technical cofounder joining pre-product, 15-25% is a reasonable range before the studio's own carry is netted out; below 10% starts to look like an early-employee package rather than a cofounder package, even with the "cofounder" title.
- Push for standard four-year vesting with a one-year cliff, not anything longer, and confirm there's no unusual clawback if the founder relationship doesn't work out within the first year.
- Ask for double-trigger acceleration on a change of control, and pro-rata rights to maintain my percentage through the next one or two priced rounds.
- Anti-dilution: confirm my shares carry the same protections as the founder's in future down-round scenarios, not a weaker class.

Script: "Fifteen to twenty-five percent is where I'd expect to land for a founding CTO role at this stage, with a four-year vest and a one-year cliff, and I'd want pro-rata rights through the seed and Series A so the stake doesn't get diluted away before we've proven anything out. Can you walk me through the current cap table, including the studio's position, so we're negotiating against real numbers?"

## Geographic and studio-structure pushback

"I know venture studio deals sometimes assume the cofounder takes on more of the early risk in exchange for the studio's platform and network. I'm fine with that trade in principle, but I'd want it reflected concretely, either in the equity number or in explicit milestones that trigger a base increase, rather than staying implicit. And since Zurich and Geneva are priced similarly, I don't think there's a discount to apply either way on the cash side."

## Competing offer, if there is one

"I'm in later-stage conversations with another early-stage venture at a similar equity range, so I'm not shopping this offer around, I just want to move quickly if the terms line up. If we can agree on the interim base and the equity split this week, I'd rather commit here than keep two processes running in parallel."

## Closing

Get the interim base, the post-raise base trigger, the equity percentage, the vesting schedule, and the cap table in writing before agreeing verbally to anything. A studio-backed idea-stage deal moves fast; the terms discussed informally should show up unchanged in the actual founder agreement.
