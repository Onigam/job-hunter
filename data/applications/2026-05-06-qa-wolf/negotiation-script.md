# Negotiation Script — QA Wolf / Staff AI Engineer

---

## Context

QA Wolf is a US-headquartered company, $57.5M raised, fully remote with hiring across Europe. They likely benchmark compensation against US market rates for remote Staff-level roles. Magino is based in Geneva, one of the highest-cost cities in Europe (CoL comparable to San Francisco or NYC). This gives legitimate grounding for anchoring at the higher end of their band.

---

## Market Anchors

**Switzerland Staff AI Engineer (Geneva), 2026:**
- Base: CHF 185,000 - 230,000
- Total comp target with equity: CHF 200,000 - 260,000+

**US remote Staff AI Engineer (comparable role), 2026:**
- Base: $195,000 - 250,000
- Equity: 0.10% - 0.25% at Series B stage

**QA Wolf likely band for this role:**
- Base: $180,000 - $220,000 USD (remote Staff)
- Equity: 0.08% - 0.20% depending on their current valuation and dilution

**Opening anchor:** $225,000 base USD (or CHF 210,000 if they offer local currency), with 0.20% equity over 4 years.

---

## Opening Anchor Script

When they make an offer or ask for your number first:

> "Based on what I'm seeing for Staff AI Engineer roles at this stage (Series B, US-headquartered companies hiring remote in Europe), the range I'm working with is around $215,000 to $230,000 base. I'm also factoring in Geneva's cost of living, which is on par with San Francisco - the market rate for this level here sits at the upper end of whatever band you're working with. What does your comp structure look like for this role?"

If pressed for a single number before seeing their range:

> "$220,000 base is where I'd start the conversation, with the understanding that equity is part of the total picture. I'm flexible on the mix if the equity is meaningful and the vesting is standard."

---

## If They Come In Low (under $180,000 / CHF 170,000)

> "I appreciate the offer, and I want to make this work. That number is meaningfully below what I've been benchmarking for Staff-level AI roles at Series B companies, and it's also below what the Geneva market pays for this profile. Can you tell me whether there's flexibility in the band, or whether equity can compensate for the base? I'm not trying to squeeze - I just need the total picture to make a fair assessment."

If they hold firm on base:
> "If the base is fixed at that level, I'd want to revisit the equity component. What would it take to get to 0.20% or above? At your current valuation, that's where I'd need to be to make the numbers work."

---

## Equity Negotiation

**Questions to ask:**
- What is the current valuation / last 409A?
- What percentage of the company does my offer represent?
- What is the current capitalization table (number of diluted shares)?
- Are there anti-dilution provisions or refresh grants?
- What is the standard vesting: 4 years with 1-year cliff?
- Are options ISOs or NSOs (matters for US tax treatment; as an EU employee, ask what the tax treatment is in Switzerland)?

**Target:** 0.15% - 0.25% for Staff+ at this funding stage, 4-year vest, 1-year cliff.

**If equity feels thin:**
> "The option grant is a bit below what I'd expect for a Staff-level role at this stage. Can you tell me more about your refresh grant policy? If I'm performing well after year 1, what would a standard refresh look like?"

---

## Geneva / Geographic Discount Pushback

Some US companies apply a geographic discount to EU-based remote employees ("local benchmarking"). Push back directly:

> "I want to flag something before we get too deep into numbers. I've seen some companies discount for European remote employees using local benchmarks. I'd push back on that framing for this role. The skill set you're hiring for (Staff AI engineering, full-stack, multi-agent systems) is genuinely scarce regardless of timezone. I'm comfortable benchmarking against US Staff AI market rates, not Swiss software engineer median."

---

## Competing Offer Leverage Template

If you have (or can reference) another offer:

> "I do have another process at [Company] at a similar stage. Their comp is in the $200,000-$215,000 range with comparable equity. I'd prefer to work at QA Wolf based on what I know about the product and the team, but I need the numbers to be in the same territory. Is there room to move to $215,000 base with the equity grant we discussed?"

---

## What to Accept / Walk Away From

**Accept if:**
- Base >= $195,000 USD (or CHF 185,000) AND equity >= 0.12%
- Or base >= $210,000 with equity >= 0.08%
- Clear refresh grant policy at year 1+

**Walk away if:**
- Total comp below CHF 180,000 equivalent with no equity path upward
- Equity vesting longer than 4 years with no acceleration clause
- Geographic discount applied that brings base below $180,000

---

## Closing Move

Once you have an offer in range:

> "I think we can make this work. I'd want to confirm the equity details in writing (current share count, 409A, exercise price), and then I'm ready to move forward. Can you send the full offer letter by [date]?"
