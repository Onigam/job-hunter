# Negotiation Script: newinsights.ai, Founding AI Engineer (co-founding partner)

## What's unusual about this one

This isn't a normal job offer, it's closer to joining as a cofounder. That changes what you're negotiating.

- **Stage.** Closed beta with "a small group of regulated banks and insurers". The founder, Ryan Grenier, appears to be the only visible team member. Funding status is unknown, so ask before anything else.
- **Location is unclear.** LinkedIn lists the role under Germany, while the description itself says Des Moines, IA. The customers are DACH banks and insurers, and the founder's background is Frankfurt. Find out which legal entity would employ you (German GmbH, US Inc. or something else) because that drives tax, social security and how equity is issued.
- **"Entry-level" on LinkedIn** is almost certainly a mis-tag. The scope (owning the full stack, model choice and enterprise platform engineering) is founding-engineer or CTO-track. Don't let that label anchor anything.
- **"Co-founding partner"** is the phrase that matters. It points to founder equity, not a small option pool grant. Negotiate on that basis.

## Market anchors (estimates, verify before the call)

Rough figures from public surveys, recruiter data and startup comp reports. Re-check on levels.fyi, Glassdoor, swissdevjobs.ch, the Michael Page and Hays Switzerland guides, and Index Ventures' OptionPlan for European equity norms.

| Benchmark | Base salary (approx.) | Equity (approx.) |
|---|---|---|
| Senior/Staff AI Engineer, Zürich or Geneva, scale-up | CHF 170k to 220k | Small, 0.05% to 0.3% |
| Founding engineer, Swiss seed-stage startup | CHF 130k to 170k | 0.5% to 2% |
| Founding AI engineer, German seed-stage (Berlin, Munich, Frankfurt) | EUR 90k to 130k | 0.5% to 2% (often VSOP) |
| First technical hire as cofounder / CTO, pre-seed | EUR/CHF 0 to 100k, often deferred | 5% to 20%+ |

EUR and CHF are close to parity (around 0.93 to 0.95 CHF per EUR lately, check on the day).

The last row is the one this posting points to. If they're pre-funding, expect low or no cash and real founder equity. If they've already raised a seed round, you should get both a proper salary and a large stake.

## First call: questions before any number

1. "Where are you with funding? Bootstrapped, pre-seed, or already raised, and how much runway is there?"
2. "Which entity would I join, and where is it incorporated?"
3. "When you say co-founding partner, what does that mean in practice? Founder shares, a title such as CTO, a board seat?"
4. "How are the beta customers paying today, if at all?"
5. "What's the current cap table? Is it only you, or are there angels or an accelerator already in?"

You can't value an equity offer without those answers, so don't name a number until you have them.

## Opening anchor

Pick the scenario that matches what they tell you.

**Scenario A, pre-funding (no salary or a token one):**

"If we're doing this before a round, I'd be taking the same risk as you, so I'd expect to be treated as a true cofounder: 15% to 20% in founder shares with standard reverse vesting, and a CTO title. Once the seed closes, salary would move to a market founding level, around CHF 140k or the EUR equivalent."

**Scenario B, seed funded:**

"For a founding role where I own every technical decision, I'm looking at CHF 160k base, or EUR 150k if the contract is German, along with 3% to 5% equity. I'm flexible on the split. Less cash in exchange for more equity is fine with me if the vesting terms are clean."

Why that's defensible: 17 years of engineering, a startup built from zero to a $1B+ acquirer, and five years at Senior Staff. In a startup selling to banks, an engineer who's already been through enterprise due diligence on the selling side lowers the risk.

**Walk-away floor (decide before the call):** for example, 8% founder equity if unpaid, or CHF 120k plus 2% if funded. Below that, a Staff role in Geneva pays more with far less risk.

## If they come in low

On cash:

"I understand runway is tight at this stage. I can take a lower salary for now, but I'd want that reflected in the equity, and a written commitment that salary moves to market once you close the next round. Could we put a number and a trigger on that?"

On equity (for example, they offer 1% "as an early employee"):

"The posting describes this as a co-founding partnership. One percent is an early-employee grant, which is a different deal. If the role really is to own the tech stack, model choice and enterprise architecture from scratch, that's a cofounder's job, and I'd want the equity to match. What would it take to get to [anchor]?"

## Equity terms to negotiate

Founder shares and options are different instruments. Make sure you know which one is on the table.

- **Founder shares (best).** You buy real shares at nominal value now, subject to reverse vesting. In Switzerland this is usually taxed lightly if bought at fair value early, and later gains on private wealth are generally tax-free capital gains. Germany is less generous, so check with an adviser.
- **VSOP / virtual shares (common in German startups).** These are a cash payout at exit, taxed as employment income, with no voting rights. Worth noticeably less than real shares. Push for real shares if you're a "co-founding partner".
- **Options (US entity).** Ask about ISO vs NSO status, strike price, and an extended post-departure exercise window of 5 to 10 years instead of 90 days.

Terms to get in writing:

- Vesting: 4 years with a 1-year cliff is standard. Ask for credit if you start pre-incorporation or pre-funding.
- Acceleration: double-trigger acceleration on change of control (sale plus termination) is reasonable for a founding role. Single trigger is worth asking for.
- Good leaver / bad leaver: make sure being let go without cause counts as good leaver, so you keep vested shares.
- Anti-dilution: founders rarely get it, but ask for pro-rata rights in future rounds so you can maintain your stake.
- Decision rights: a board seat or observer seat, and a clear say on technical hiring and architecture.
- IP: get a clean IP assignment that excludes your prior personal projects (Givernance, open-source work).

Script:

"Before I sign up, I'd like to see a term sheet covering the share class, total shares outstanding, vesting with cliff and acceleration, and leaver terms. For a cofounder role I'd also expect real shares rather than virtual ones. Is that how you're set up?"

## Geographic discount pushback

If the company is US- or Germany-based and tries to pay a lower rate:

"I'm based in Geneva and would stay here. It's in the same timezone as your DACH customers and a short flight from Frankfurt, which helps with bank meetings in person. I'm not asking for a Zürich big-tech salary. But a Swiss resident needs Swiss-compliant employment (Swiss payroll through an EOR, or a contractor setup) and the employer costs differ, so let's make sure the structure works and we agree the gross on that basis."

Structure options:

| Option | What it means |
|---|---|
| EOR with Swiss payroll (Deel, Remote, Oyster) | Cleanest. Swiss AVS, LPP and accident insurance all handled. Employer pays about 12% to 15% on top of gross |
| Contractor (Swiss sole trader) | You pay your own AVS (about 10%), pension and insurance, with no paid holidays. Only worth it at a premium of 25% or more |
| German or US payroll while living in Geneva | Avoid. Swiss social security normally applies when you work from Switzerland |

## Competing offer template (use only if true)

"To be open with you, I'm also in late-stage talks for a Staff platform role at a Swiss-based company in the CHF 180k range. newinsights.ai is the more interesting problem and I'd rather build something from the ground up, but I need the equity to make up for the difference in cash and risk. If we can agree on [anchor], I'm ready to commit."

If nothing concrete is on the table, use the honest version: "I'm talking to a few companies and expect offers in the next few weeks, so I'd like to understand your terms by [date]."

## Other levers

- A deferred salary that converts to cash or equity when the next round closes.
- A hardware, compute and API budget for model experiments. Running on-prem LLMs isn't cheap.
- A title: CTO or Co-founder & CTO, not "Founding Engineer", if equity is in the cofounder range.
- A say in the first two or three engineering hires.
- Conference and customer travel paid for, especially to Frankfurt and Zürich.

## Before signing

- Have a Geneva startup lawyer review the shareholder agreement (budget CHF 1.5k to 3k). Worth it at this stake size.
- Check the Swiss tax treatment of the share grant with a fiduciary before you accept.
- Get everything that matters into the term sheet: equity percentage, vesting, title, salary trigger, leaver terms.
