# Negotiation Script: Founding AI Platform Engineer, via Atarus (client: AI data-for-agents startup, renewable energy)

## Context before talking numbers

- **Atarus is the recruiter, not the employer.** The client isn't named in the posting. What's public is an early-stage London company connecting trusted industry data with AI agents, starting with renewable energy, with paying customers and meaningful revenue. It's backed by Visionaries Club and by Google DeepMind leaders (probably angels).
- **Posted band:** £100k-£140k base, plus "meaningful equity with founding-level ownership".
- **Location:** on-site in London, full-time. That's the real negotiation, more than the number.
- **Level:** LinkedIn lists it as mid-senior. The scope (owning agent architecture, backend, evals, observability, CI/CD and developer tooling) is Staff-level work in a founding seat, and that's the argument for the top of the band.

Questions for the recruiter, first call:
- "Who's the client, and how much have they raised? When was the last round, and how many months of runway do they have?"
- "How many engineers are there today, and am I the first platform hire?"
- "Is on-site five days a week, or could it be hybrid with regular London weeks and the rest remote from Geneva?"
- "Will they sponsor a UK Skilled Worker visa if needed, and do they hold a sponsor licence?" (Check first whether Magino needs one. An EU passport alone doesn't give a right to work in the UK after Brexit.)
- "What does founding-level ownership mean in numbers: what percentage, on what fully diluted count, and is it EMI options?"
- "Is there a relocation package?"

## Market reference (estimates, verify before the call)

These are rough 2025-2026 ranges from general market knowledge. Check levels.fyi, Glassdoor, the Swissdevjobs salary report, Otta/Welcome to the Jungle UK and Pave-style startup benchmarks before any call. GBP/CHF has been around 1.05-1.12 recently, so check the current rate.

| Reference | Base salary (approx.) |
|---|---|
| Founding / senior AI engineer, London seed-Series A | £110k-150k + 0.5-1.5% |
| Staff engineer, London scale-up | £130k-170k |
| Staff/Senior Staff, Geneva or Zürich scale-up | CHF 180k-230k |
| Senior AI engineer, Swiss scale-up (Lausanne/Zürich) | CHF 150k-190k |
| Founding engineer, Swiss seed startup | CHF 140k-180k + equity |

At about 1.07, the posted £140k top works out to roughly CHF 150k. That's below Geneva Staff market before London's cost of living comes in. So the cash gap has to be made up in equity, relocation support, or a hybrid setup that doesn't need a full move.

## My numbers

**Scenario A: relocate to London, on-site**
- **Opening anchor:** £150k base, plus 1.25% equity with founding-level terms, plus a relocation package.
- **Target:** £140k base (top of their band) plus 0.75-1.25% equity plus relocation and visa costs paid.
- **Walk-away:** below £130k base or below ~0.5% equity. At that point the move costs more than it's worth compared with Swiss Staff offers.

**Scenario B: hybrid, based in Geneva with regular London weeks**
- Needs a Swiss employment setup (EOR such as Deel or Remote) or a contractor agreement.
- **Opening anchor:** CHF 185k base on Swiss payroll or EOR, plus the same equity. Travel is paid by the company.
- **Target:** CHF 165k-175k. It's more than their band in pounds, but they save on relocation and the employer social charges are lower than UK NI.
- **If contractor only:** about CHF 900-1,050 per day. Check AVS/AHV self-employed contributions, LPP and tax with an advisor first. Keep in mind that options usually can't be EMI for a non-employee.

## Opening anchor script

> "Before I give a number, can you walk me through the whole package? Base, equity and on what fully diluted count, vesting, relocation, and how flexible on-site is? [Listen.] The role owns the agent architecture, the backend, evals and dev tooling as the first platform engineer, so it's Staff scope in a founding seat. For that, and a move from Geneva, I'm looking at £150k base with around 1.25% in options. If cash is tight at this stage, I'm open to talking about more equity for less base."

Say the number, then stop talking.

## If they come in low (for example £110k-125k)

> "Thanks, that helps. I know it's within your band, but it's below what I'd earn staying in Geneva at Staff level, and I'd be moving country to take it. To make it work, I'd need at least £140k, or equity that clearly makes up for the difference. If base is fixed, can we look at relocation, a signing bonus to cover the move, and a written salary review after the next round?"

If base doesn't move, trade in this order:
1. More equity, with the percentage and fully diluted share count in writing.
2. A salary review tied to the next funding round, with the number agreed now.
3. Relocation: flights, temporary housing for about two months, visa and legal costs, and a repayment clause no longer than 12 months.
4. Hybrid terms: for example two weeks a month in London for the first quarter, then agreed in-office days.
5. Title and scope in writing: Founding Platform Engineer (or Founding Staff Engineer), owning AI platform architecture, with a path to lead the platform team once they hire.

## Equity: what to pin down

- **Percentage, not a number of options.** Ask for the fully diluted share count and the strike price. "20,000 options" means nothing without them.
- **Scheme:** in the UK, EMI options are the tax-efficient default for employees. Check the company qualifies. If Magino stays Swiss-resident, EMI treatment may not apply, and Swiss taxation of options (usually taxed at exercise) needs checking with an advisor.
- **Vesting:** 4 years with a 1-year cliff is standard. Ask for monthly vesting after the cliff.
- **Acceleration:** double-trigger acceleration on acquisition (change of control plus termination). It's a reasonable ask at founding level, and I've been through an acquisition before, so it's a credible one.
- **Exercise window:** push for 5-10 years after leaving instead of the default 90 days. Early employees often leave before an exit and shouldn't lose options because they can't pay to exercise within three months.
- **Refresh:** ask whether there's a refresh policy after the next round, since founding grants get diluted quickly.
- **Anti-dilution:** employees almost never get it, so don't spend capital on it. Ask for a top-up grant at the Series A instead.

## Pushing back on "the band is the band"

> "I get that the band is set for London. But this is a founding seat with Staff scope, and I'd be leaving a Swiss market where Staff roles pay CHF 180k and more. I'm not asking you to match Zürich big tech. I'm asking for the top of your band plus equity that reflects being the first platform hire, because that's the bet I'd be making alongside the founders."

If hybrid from Geneva comes up:

> "If it's hybrid, I'd rather be paid on Swiss terms through an EOR than on a London salary converted to francs. Geneva is one of the most expensive cities in Europe, and a London salary in pounds doesn't cover Swiss social charges and LPP."

## Competing offer template (use only if true)

> "I want to be upfront: I'm in late-stage conversations with a couple of Swiss AI teams, including one Staff-level platform role. This one is my first choice because of the problem and the founding scope. If you can get to £140k with around 1% in equity and a relocation package, I'm ready to commit this week."

Never invent an offer. If there's no competing offer, say "I'm in conversations with other teams" and leave it there.

## Red flags to check before signing

- The client still hasn't been named after the first round of interviews.
- Equity is promised verbally but won't be written into the offer letter.
- "On-site" turns out to mean six days, or there's no plan for visa sponsorship.
- Runway is under 12 months and no round is in progress.
- The founders can't explain how evals and data quality are measured today. It's the core of the product, and it's the job I'd be inheriting.
