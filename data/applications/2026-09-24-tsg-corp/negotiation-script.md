# Negotiation Script: AI Engineering & Adoption Lead @ TSG Corp (Lausanne area)

## Context
- **TSG Corp**: Geneva-based advisory and delivery firm (HQ Rue de Lausanne 64), founded in 2018 by Kinan Khadam, 60+ consultants, offices in Barcelona and Bucharest. Model is "Shape. Build. Operate." for regulated industries: banking, insurance, commodity trading, manufacturing. They hold an AVG/LSE licence (staff leasing and placement) and swissstaffing SQS certification, and Yariv Levy leads their AI practice.
- **The role**: full-time, Lausanne area, insurance sector. Almost certainly a placement with a client, probably tied to the "Swiss insurance group" digital transformation and QA program on their case list. Requirements: Java, Python, CI/CD, AI-assisted development in production, fluent French.
- **Posted range: CHF 140k to 160k/year.** 94 applicants within 9 hours, so the process will move fast and the range is mostly fixed.
- **Things to keep in mind:**
  1. A range is published, so the ceiling is visible. Aim for the top and move the talk to everything that isn't base salary.
  2. Consulting firms bill the client a daily rate (for this profile in Romandie, probably CHF 1,300 to 1,700/day). Your salary is their margin. That's why they have less room on base and more room on bonus, title and terms.
  3. There's **no equity** here. Anything you'd have asked for in stock has to come back as cash, bonus or protection.
  4. Your profile says to avoid pure consulting. Before you negotiate at all, get clarity on the contract type (see below). That matters more than CHF 10k.

## Your target

- **Walk-away:** CHF 150k base on a permanent (CDI) contract, 13th salary included or on top, and a written guarantee that you stay employed between client missions. Below that, or on a mission-bound contract, it doesn't make sense compared with your current Senior Staff level.
- **Target:** CHF 160k base plus a 10% performance bonus, title of Lead or Principal, training/certification budget, and 2 remote days a week.
- **Opening anchor:** CHF 165k to 170k. Yes, that's above the posted range. Justify it with 17 years of experience, Senior Staff level, an adoption track record that matches the brief almost word for word, and the fact that you'd be a credible reference for TSG's AI practice beyond this one client.

## Questions to ask before any number comes up

1. "Is this a permanent contract with TSG, or is it tied to this client engagement?" (Fixed-term or mission-bound = very different negotiation.)
2. "How long is the engagement planned for, and what happens when it ends?" (Bench time policy, next mission, notice.)
3. "Is the range with or without 13th salary? Is there a bonus, and is it linked to utilisation?"
4. "Where would I actually be working, and how many days on site at the client?"
5. "Would I also contribute to TSG's own AI practice, with Yariv Levy's team?" (If yes, that's a senior scope and justifies the top of the range.)

## Counter-offer script: if they come in low (e.g. CHF 140k to 145k)

"Thanks, I appreciate it. That number sits at the bottom of the range, and what we've talked about is a lead scope: defining the practice, running the assessment and bringing teams along, in a regulated environment where mistakes are expensive. I'd need the base closer to CHF 160k. If there's a hard ceiling on base, I'm open to closing the gap with a guaranteed first-year bonus and a salary review at six months, once you've seen the client feedback."

If they won't move at all: "Understood. Then let's look at what else can move: the title, the bonus, the training budget, remote days and the guarantee between missions. I'd rather find a package that works than argue about one number."

## Levers when equity isn't on the table

In order of value to you:
1. **Permanent contract with a guarantee between missions.** Non-negotiable, really.
2. **Signing bonus** (CHF 10k to 15k), to compensate for leaving your current role and any unvested Benevity stock or bonus. Ask for it explicitly if you're leaving money behind.
3. **Guaranteed first-year bonus** of 10%, not tied to utilisation.
4. **Title**: "Lead" or "Principal Consultant, AI Engineering". It helps your next move and costs them nothing.
5. **Remote days and travel**: Geneva to Lausanne is ~40 minutes by train. Ask for a half-fare or GA travelcard (about CHF 3,900/year for a GA 2nd class) or 2 remote days a week.
6. **Training / certification budget**: CHF 3k to 5k a year plus conference time.
7. **LPP (pension)**: ask whether TSG pays more than the legal minimum share. On a CHF 150k+ salary the difference adds up.
8. **Notice and non-compete**: check that there's no clause stopping you from joining the client directly later, or that it's short and paid.

## Geographic pushback

The usual "Geneva is expensive" argument works less well here, because Lausanne is almost as expensive and the range already assumes Romandie. Use it only on commuting: "I'm Geneva-based, so the daily commute is a real cost. A travelcard or two remote days would handle it."

## Competing offer template (use only if true)

"I want to be transparent: I'm in late-stage talks with another company for a Staff-level role in Switzerland, in the CHF 170k to 190k range. TSG is interesting to me because of the AI adoption scope and the insurance context, and I'd like to make it work. If you can get to CHF 160k with a guaranteed bonus, I'm ready to decide this week."

Don't bluff. Swiss consulting and staffing circles are small, especially in Romandie.

## Red flags to watch

- Contract that ends when the client engagement ends.
- Bonus entirely tied to billable utilisation.
- Pressure to accept within 24 to 48 hours because "the client wants someone fast".
- A job title that turns out to be "consultant" with no lead scope.
- A non-compete that blocks you from joining the client.
