# Negotiation Script — Inkeep Principal TypeScript Engineer

---

## Market Context

**Role level:** Principal / MTS (above Senior, equivalent to Staff+)
**Company stage:** YC + Khosla Ventures backed, Series A/B territory, growing quickly
**Location note:** Role was listed SF/NY on-site for the Senior variant. The Principal role via Ashby may be remote-eligible. Confirm during first call.

**Senior variant comp (posted publicly):** $180k-$250k base + 0.1%-0.4% equity
**Principal uplift estimate:** Base $220k-$300k USD, equity 0.3%-0.6%

**Switzerland / Geneva anchor:**
Geneva is consistently in the top 3 most expensive cities globally. CHF and USD are near parity. Remote roles at US companies paying European candidates often try to apply a geographic discount; push back on this directly. Your cost of structure (healthcare, pension contributions, taxes) in Switzerland is not lower than SF.

**Magino's target range:**
- Base: CHF 200k-240k (or USD equivalent, ~$215k-$260k)
- Equity: 0.3%-0.5% on a 4-year vest, 1-year cliff
- Total target package: CHF 260k-320k equivalent (base + equity fair value at current valuation)

---

## Opening Anchor

When they ask for your number first, use this:

> "Based on the Principal level and the stage of Inkeep, I'm looking at a base in the CHF 220k-240k range, with meaningful equity on top of that. I'm flexible on the structure if the total package makes sense, but that's my starting point."

If they ask you to convert to USD: "The CHF/USD rate is essentially at parity right now, so that's roughly $220k-$240k."

Do not open lower. Do not say "I'm flexible" before giving a number.

---

## If They Come in Low (below CHF 180k base)

> "I appreciate the offer. The base is below where I'd need to land given my level and Geneva cost of living, which tracks closely to SF on most metrics. Can we explore whether there's flexibility on the equity component to close that gap, or is the base range fixed?"

If they say the base is fixed:

> "Understood. If the base is at [X], I'd need the equity to be in the [Y]% range to make the total package work. That's not a hard no from me, it's just the math I need to get to yes."

---

## Equity Script

For a YC/Khosla startup at this stage, ask these questions before accepting any equity number:

1. "What's the current valuation cap or last round valuation?"
2. "What's the total option pool size, and how much has been granted?"
3. "Is this ISO or NSO?" (ISOs have better tax treatment; NSOs may require immediate Swiss tax on paper gains)
4. "Is there acceleration on change of control? Single or double trigger?"
5. "What's the strike price on the options?"

Counter-equity template if they offer below 0.3%:

> "At Principal level I'd expect to be in the 0.3%-0.5% range. Can you share where that lands relative to your option pool and band for this level? I want to make sure I'm not asking for something outside the structure you've built."

---

## Geographic Discount Pushback

If they try to apply a discount for working from Switzerland:

> "I understand the instinct, but Geneva and SF have effectively the same cost structure at the moment. My social security and health contributions are mandatory and non-trivial here, and CHF is at USD parity. I'd rather not build the conversation on a geographic discount that doesn't reflect the actual cost reality."

If they insist:

> "If you're set on a location-adjusted number, I'd need to see the full comp band for this role at SF-based candidates and understand the specific discount methodology. That's a reasonable ask before accepting a discount."

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

If you have or generate another offer, use this structure:

> "I want to be transparent: I have an offer from [company] at [base + equity]. Inkeep is my preference for the work itself, but I can't take a meaningful step backward financially. If you can get close to [target number], I'd prefer to close this with you."

If no competing offer: do not fabricate one. Instead:

> "I'm in active conversations with a couple of companies at similar stage, and I want to close something in the next few weeks. Getting the compensation right now saves us both from a longer process."

---

## What to Negotiate Beyond Base

In order of importance for this role/stage:

1. **Equity percentage and strike price** — most value creation at startup stage
2. **Acceleration clause** — especially relevant post-acquisition (single trigger preferred)
3. **Start date flexibility** — gives you time to set up contractor or employment structure in Switzerland
4. **Hardware/home office allowance** — CHF 2k-5k one-time is reasonable to ask
5. **Conference/learning budget** — CHF 3k-5k/year standard at engineering-forward companies
6. **Cliff modification** — if you negotiate hard on comp, offer to move from 1-year to 6-month cliff as a goodwill gesture (rarely costs them anything, signals commitment)

---

## Hard Stop

Do not accept below:
- CHF 180k base (absolute floor for Principal level in Geneva market)
- Less than 0.2% equity with no meaningful acceleration
- No cliff or vesting acceleration provision if the company is likely to be acquired
