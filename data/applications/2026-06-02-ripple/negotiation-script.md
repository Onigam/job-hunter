# Negotiation Script — Ripple Staff Software Engineer (Geneva)

---

## Market Context

**Role level:** Staff Software Engineer (Ripple uses this for senior IC contributors; post-Metaco acquisition, Geneva team is working on core custody infrastructure).

**Ripple specifics:**
- Private company (pre-IPO as of 2026); compensation likely includes base + cash bonus + equity grants
- Equity may be in RSUs (if preparing for IPO) or XRP-based grants (historically Ripple has used XRP as part of comp)
- In-office requirement (10+ days/month in Geneva) adds geographic premium justification
- The Metaco acquisition ($250M in 2023) signals serious investment in this product line

**Switzerland/Geneva market — Staff Software Engineer in fintech/crypto (2025-2026 data):**
- Base salary range: CHF 170,000-220,000
- Annual bonus: 10-20% of base (standard for fintech at this level)
- Total cash: CHF 190,000-260,000
- Equity at Staff level in funded company: CHF 30,000-80,000/year in vesting value
- Total compensation target: CHF 220,000-300,000 (all-in)

**Comparable roles Geneva market:** Credit Suisse/UBS Staff/Principal Engineers land CHF 180,000-210,000 base. Crypto/fintech companies with VC/strategic backing typically pay 15-25% above traditional finance.

---

## Opening Anchor

**When they ask your expectations, anchor first:**

> "Based on my research into the Geneva market for Staff engineers in fintech and custody infrastructure, and given my background, I'm targeting a total compensation around CHF 250,000-270,000. That would include a base in the CHF 210,000-220,000 range, with the remainder in bonus and equity. I'm flexible on the mix depending on how you structure equity."

**Why this anchor works:**
- It's high enough to leave room for their counter without falling below target
- Phrasing it as "total compensation" opens the equity conversation naturally
- "Flexible on the mix" signals you're reasonable, not a one-number wall

---

## Counter-Offer Scripts

### If they come in at CHF 170,000-180,000 base:

> "I appreciate the offer. The base is below what I was targeting. To be direct: based on the market data I've looked at and what's being offered for this level in Geneva's fintech space, I'd need to see something in the CHF 200,000 range to move forward. Is there flexibility there, or would it help to discuss the equity component alongside it?"

### If they come in at CHF 185,000-195,000 base:

> "That's closer. I think there's a gap of around CHF 15,000-20,000 to get me to where I'd feel comfortable. Would it be possible to get to CHF 205,000-210,000? If base has a hard ceiling, I'd want to understand the equity better before deciding."

### If they say the range is fixed:

> "I understand there are internal bands. Can you help me understand how equity and bonus factor in at this level? I want to make sure I'm evaluating the total picture correctly before giving you a definitive answer."

---

## Equity Negotiation

**Ripple-specific: XRP compensation is unusual and volatile.**

If equity is offered in XRP grants (not RSUs):
- Ask for the dollar-equivalent value at grant date
- Ask about the vesting schedule and any lockup periods
- Ask whether grants are denominated in USD or XRP units (price exposure difference)
- Ask for a floor: "Is there any protection if XRP price drops significantly before vest?"

**Questions to ask:**
- "Are the equity grants in RSUs, XRP units, or something else?"
- "What does a typical annual equity grant look like at this level?"
- "Is there a refresh grant after the cliff, and on what schedule?"
- "Are grants denominated in CHF/USD or XRP units?"
- "Is there any price protection or floor on XRP grants?"

**Negotiating equity amount:**

> "The base works if we can get the equity grant up. For a Staff role in this market, I'd expect to see vesting value in the CHF 40,000-60,000/year range. Where does this role land relative to that?"

---

## Geographic Premium Talking Points

Geneva is one of the most expensive cities in Europe. Use this if they reference "remote-friendly" pay scales or European averages:

> "I'm fully in-person in Geneva as requested, which means I'm benchmarking against Geneva's cost of living and local market rates, not a European average. Geneva consistently ranks in the top 5 most expensive cities globally for cost of living. I wouldn't expect to be benchmarked against a remote rate for an in-office role."

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

If you have another offer (real or forthcoming):

> "I want to be transparent: I'm in late-stage conversations with one other company at a higher total comp number. I'm genuinely more interested in the Ripple role because of the technical depth and the custody infrastructure problem space. But I need to be fair to myself on the numbers. Is there room to move?"

If no competing offer (use as hypothetical framing):

> "At my level, I'm getting inbound from a few companies in the Geneva market. I haven't committed to a process elsewhere, but I want to make sure we're aligned before I do. The number I mentioned is what I need to not feel like I left something on the table."

---

## Minimum Acceptable Package

Walk away if the all-in total compensation is below CHF 190,000 (base CHF 175,000 + bonus + minimal equity), unless the equity upside is clearly exceptional and verifiable (e.g., pre-IPO RSUs with a credible valuation path).

**Key factors that could justify flexibility:**
- Strong equity with imminent liquidity event (IPO filing, XRP at high valuation)
- Unusually fast title progression path documented in writing
- Exceptional team or product opportunity not available elsewhere

---

## Tone Notes

Stay warm, concrete, and specific. Ripple's Geneva team is technical, not a traditional finance negotiation. Cite market data, not feelings. The strongest position is "I've done the research and here's what the market says," not "I'm worth more."
