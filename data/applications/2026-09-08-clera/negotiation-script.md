# Negotiation Script: Founding Engineer (Potential CTO), Clera

Context going in: this is described as "well-funded, early-stage," second technical hire, with an explicit path to CTO. That combination changes the negotiation on two fronts at once, the immediate comp for a founding engineer role and the equity premium that should come with being told you're the CTO candidate, not just an early IC. The role is on-site in San Francisco, New York, or Montreal, and there's no visa sponsorship, so work authorization is a real, first-conversation topic here, not a footnote.

## Market context

Founding engineers with genuine CTO trajectory at well-funded US pre-seed to seed-stage startups typically land in the 1.5%-4% equity range, meaningfully above a standard early engineer grant of 0.5%-1.5%, precisely because the role is being sold as the technical co-leadership seat, not just headcount. Base for a second technical hire at this stage in SF or NYC commonly runs $170,000-230,000 once cash compensation is part of the picture, though pre-seed healthtech sometimes still runs partly on deferred or below-market cash with equity making up the difference. A Geneva-based Senior Staff engineer would command roughly CHF 150,000-190,000 locally, which is a useful anchor for what guaranteed income looks like even though this role pays in USD and requires relocation. The relocation itself has a real cost, visa or immigration process, moving, giving up a stable base in Switzerland, and that cost belongs in the equity or signing conversation rather than being absorbed silently.

## The visa and relocation question, addressed first

"Before we get into comp, I want to flag the work authorization piece directly since the posting says no sponsorship. I'd need to understand what path exists for someone relocating from Switzerland, whether that's an existing visa category you've used before, a green card process already underway for other hires, or something I'd need to arrange independently. If there's no clear path, I need to know that now rather than after we've gone deep into everything else."

## Opening position

"Given this is framed as a CTO-track hire and not just an early engineer, I'd want the equity grant closer to 3% than to 1%, reflecting both the technical ownership and the relocation risk I'm taking on. On base, I'd anchor around $210,000 to $220,000 if cash compensation is part of the offer, with a written understanding of what triggers the actual CTO title and any additional grant that comes with it, rather than leaving 'potential CTO' undefined indefinitely."

## If they come in low on equity

"I'd want to understand what that number assumes, whether it's benchmarked against a standard early engineer or against someone stepping into technical co-leadership from day one. If the CTO path is real, the grant should reflect that even before the title formally changes, since the accountability starts immediately either way. I'd also want a defined milestone, whether that's a funding event or a time-based review, for when the CTO conversation actually happens, rather than it staying an open-ended maybe."

## If cash compensation is below market, deferred, or partly contingent on funding

"I can work with a lower cash number for a founder-stage role, but I'd want that gap made up on the equity side in a way that's explicit, not implied. If there's a funding milestone that unlocks a market-rate base, I'd want that written down with a number and a rough timeline attached, the same way I'd want clarity on what happens if that milestone slips by six months or more."

## Counter-offer script if the offer lands mid-band

"That's a reasonable starting point. Given the relocation and the ambiguity around exactly when 'potential CTO' becomes actual CTO, I'd want the equity grant pushed toward the higher end of what you mentioned, plus a clear, written trigger for the title change and any associated re-grant. I'd also want to understand the vesting schedule, standard four-year with a one-year cliff, and whether the clock starts from my actual start date."

## Geographic and relocation position

"I'm currently in Geneva, and moving to San Francisco, New York, or Montreal is something I'm genuinely open to for the right founding role, but it's a bigger life change than a remote hire relocating within the US. I'd want either relocation support or a signing component that reflects that, separate from the equity conversation, since the two are addressing different kinds of risk."

## Competing offer template

"I'm in conversations with other early-stage teams where the technical leadership path is already defined rather than conditional. I'd rather build something in a domain I find genuinely interesting, and the biosignal work here is exactly that, but for me to choose a role with an undefined visa path and a conditional title over one that's clearer on both fronts, the equity and the CTO trigger both need to be concrete before I'd sign."

## Equity and structure questions to ask before agreeing to anything

Current fully diluted share count and what the offered percentage represents in real terms against your last round's valuation, not just the headline number. Strike price and how it's set at this stage, given there may not be a recent 409A to reference. Whether there's an existing legal process for sponsoring a visa or supporting a green card application, and who has gone through it before, if anyone. What specifically triggers the shift from "potential CTO" to CTO, in writing, whether that's a funding round, a team size, or a fixed date.
