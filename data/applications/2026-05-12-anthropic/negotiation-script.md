# Negotiation Script — Research Engineer, Pre-training (Anthropic Zürich)

---

## Market Context

**Posted range:** CHF 280,000 – 680,000 annually (total compensation, likely base + equity)

This is an unusually wide range, which typically means Anthropic is open to candidates at multiple levels — from strong individual contributors up to very senior researchers. The lower end (~CHF 280K-360K) likely covers Research Engineers without PhD backgrounds; the upper end is for senior researchers or people with strong publication records in pre-training/LLM research.

**Switzerland market anchors for comparable roles:**

- Staff Engineer at Google/Meta Zürich: CHF 260K-380K total comp (base ~CHF 180K-230K + bonus + RSUs)
- ML Engineer (senior) at European AI labs: CHF 220K-320K base
- Staff Engineer at a well-funded AI startup (Zürich): CHF 200K-280K base + meaningful equity
- Benevity Senior Staff IC (Geneva, current): ~CHF 180K-220K base range (public sector-adjacent comp)

**Realistic target for Magino's profile:** CHF 320K-420K total compensation, weighted toward equity given Anthropic's stage.

---

## Opening Anchor

**If asked "What are you looking for in terms of compensation?"**

"Based on my research into Anthropic's posted range and comparable roles at top-tier AI labs in Switzerland, I'm targeting total compensation in the CHF 380,000-420,000 range. I'm open to how that's structured between base and equity, particularly given Anthropic's growth trajectory."

Why this anchor:
- It's within the posted range but above the midpoint, signaling confidence without being unrealistic.
- It leaves room to negotiate down while staying above CHF 320K.
- It opens the equity conversation naturally.

---

## If They Come In Low (CHF 280K-320K range)

**Script:**

"I appreciate the offer. I want to be transparent: CHF [X] is below what I was targeting based on my research into the role and the Zürich market. I'm genuinely interested in the work and the team, so I don't want compensation to be the reason this doesn't happen. Is there flexibility to get closer to CHF [target + 20%]? If the base is fixed, I'm also open to discussing equity or a sign-on component."

**Fallback if base is firm:**

"If the base is fixed at that level, what does the equity component look like? I'd want to understand the vesting schedule, the current strike price if it's options, and how Anthropic thinks about refreshes for strong performers."

---

## Equity Negotiation

Anthropic is privately held at an estimated valuation of $18B+ (as of 2025). Equity will likely be in the form of RSUs or options.

**Key questions to ask:**

1. "What's the current preferred share price, and when was the last 409A valuation?" (Gives you a sense of strike price risk for options.)
2. "What's the standard vesting schedule? 4 years / 1 year cliff?"
3. "Is there an acceleration clause in case of acquisition?"
4. "How does Anthropic handle equity refreshes for continuing employees?"
5. "Is secondary liquidity available? Can employees sell on secondary markets?"

**Equity anchor:**

If offered standard 4-year RSU vesting with a 1-year cliff, ask for:
- An accelerated first-year cliff (6 months) given that you're joining from a stable position.
- A sign-on grant to offset unvested equity left at Benevity.

**Script for equity negotiation:**

"I have unvested equity at Benevity that I'd be walking away from. A sign-on grant that partially offsets that would make the transition significantly easier to justify. What's the typical sign-on package for this level?"

---

## Geographic Angle (Geneva vs. Zürich)

The role is based in Zürich. Magino is in Geneva. This creates a commute or relocation conversation.

**If the role requires on-site presence:**

"I'm in Geneva, which is about 90 minutes by train. I'm open to a hybrid arrangement, spending 2-3 days in Zürich and working remotely the other days. Given the commute, I'd also factor travel costs into the overall comp conversation. Is there a relocation allowance or travel stipend for employees in that situation?"

**If they require relocation:**

"Relocating to Zürich is something I'd consider, but it's a meaningful life change. A relocation package that covers moving costs and a few months of temporary housing would make that decision much more straightforward."

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

If you have (or can create) a competing offer:

"I want to be transparent with you: I'm in final stages with [Company X], and their offer is in the CHF [range] range. Anthropic is my first choice based on the mission and the technical problems you're working on, but I need the compensation to be in the same range to make the decision easy."

Even without a formal competing offer, you can reference market data:

"I've been speaking with a few companies in this space, and the range I'm seeing in the Zürich/Geneva AI engineering market for senior roles is CHF [range]. I want to join Anthropic, but I want to make sure we're aligned on market comp."

---

## What Not to Do

- Don't accept the first offer in the room. "Let me think about this and get back to you tomorrow" is always legitimate.
- Don't reveal your current salary if you can avoid it. "I'm looking at market rates for this role and level rather than anchoring to my current comp."
- Don't negotiate equity without understanding the liquidation preferences and cap table structure. An 18B valuation means different things depending on how much preferred stock is senior to common.
- Don't treat the CHF 280K floor as the anchor. The floor is where they'd hire a recent PhD grad. Start the conversation at the top of your realistic range.
