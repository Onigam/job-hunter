## Phase 1: Before You Have an Offer

Ask early, conversationally, ideally with the CTO or founder directly:

> "Before we go deeper, I'd like to understand two things: where a founding engineer sits on the cap table here, and how you're currently splitting frontend versus backend and AI pipeline work day to day. That tells me a lot about whether 'frontend focus' means I own the whole surface or I'm one of several hands on it."

Also worth asking directly, since the role spans frontend and AI pipeline work:

> "How much of the day-to-day is genuinely frontend versus being pulled into the LLM pipeline and backend? I want to size the role accurately before we talk numbers."

## Phase 3: Equity Deep-Dive

Swiss pre-seed equity is usually structured as an ESOP or a direct participation agreement. Get specifics, not headline percentages:

1. **ESOP pool size.** If the pool is 10% and the offer is 1%, that 1% is of the pool, not of the company. Ask which.
2. **Current pre-money valuation.** A 1% stake is worth very different things at CHF 2M versus CHF 8M.
3. **Vesting schedule.** Confirm four-year vest, one-year cliff explicitly; don't assume.
4. **Options versus phantom equity.** Phantom equity pays out on exit but never confers real ownership. Fine to accept, but know which one is on the table.
5. **Acceleration on acquisition.** Single-trigger (immediate vest on acquisition) versus double-trigger (vest only if also terminated). Double-trigger is standard; ask anyway.
6. **Strike price.** Should be very low at pre-seed. Get it in writing.
7. **Liquidation preference stack.** 1x non-participating preferred is standard and founder/employee-friendly; anything stacked higher erodes a modest exit fast.

Script:

> "I want to make sure we're looking at the same thing when we talk equity. Can you walk me through the ESOP pool size, how my grant is sized against it, the vesting terms, and whether these are options or phantom equity? The percentage alone doesn't tell me much without that context."

## Phase 4: Geography and Hybrid Setup

Rotkreuz sits in Zug canton, one of the more expensive parts of Switzerland, though the posting mentions a hybrid setup rather than five days on site.

> "Commuting from Geneva daily isn't realistic given the distance, so I'd want to lock in the specifics of the hybrid arrangement, how many days on site, and whether that flexes during onboarding versus steady state, before we finalize terms."

If base gets pushed back on and relocation comes up:

> "If this moves toward relocation rather than hybrid, the Zug/Rotkreuz cost of living is comparable to or above Geneva for most of the rental market, which supports a base nearer CHF 140,000 than CHF 120,000."

---

## Phase 5: Using a Competing Offer

With a live competing process:

> "I want to be straightforward: I'm in late stages elsewhere and expect to hear back this week. Scait is the more interesting problem for me technically, so I'd like to close here first if the terms line up. If you can get to CHF 135,000 base and 1% equity, I'm ready to move."

Without one, lean on timeline instead:

> "I'm trying to make a decision within the next week or two. Is there room to move quickly on final terms?"

---

## Phase 6: Accept / Walk Away

**Accept at:**
- CHF 130,000+ base with 0.8%+ equity, standard 4/1 vest
- CHF 118,000-130,000 base with 1.0%+ equity and a written 12-month compensation review
- Any base with real equity if pre-money valuation is above CHF 5M (1% worth CHF 50,000+ pre-seed dilution)

**Walk away if:**
- Base under CHF 110,000 with no movement and equity under 0.5%
- Phantom-only equity with no path to real ownership and no compensating cash
- No acceleration clause and a vesting cliff longer than 12 months
- The team can't clearly explain the cap table or ESOP mechanics when asked directly
