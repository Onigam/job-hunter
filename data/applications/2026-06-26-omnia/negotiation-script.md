# Negotiation Script — Co-Founder & CTO at Omnia

## Context

This is a co-founder role, not a standard employment offer. The negotiation has two layers: equity structure (primary) and cash compensation (secondary). Treat them separately — anchoring only on cash is a mistake here.

Omnia is early-stage, likely pre-seed or seed. The offering is "significant founding equity" with unspecified cash. Go in assuming minimal cash and negotiate both.

---

## Equity: What to Aim For

**Market reference for co-founder CTO at pre-seed/seed in Switzerland/Europe:**
- Solo technical co-founder from day one: 15-30%
- Joining pre-seed with one or two non-technical founders already in place: 10-20%
- Joining seed-stage with some product already built: 5-12%
- Joining post-seed as "founding CTO" (not true co-founder): 2-5%

**What to ask:**
- Request the current cap table before any number conversation: "Before we talk percentages, can you walk me through the current ownership structure and what's been allocated so far?"
- Understand the ESOP pool: "Is there an option pool set up, and if so, what percentage does it represent post-round?"
- Understand the round timeline: "What's your fundraising plan for the next 12 months, and what does dilution look like from that?"

**Opening anchor (if they ask first):**
"Given that this is a founding-level commitment with full technical ownership from day one, I'd expect to land somewhere in the 10-15% range. I'd want to understand the full cap table before getting specific."

**If they come in below 8%:**
"I'd want to understand the reasoning. If we're talking about a true co-founder role, meaning I'm architecting this from nothing, setting technical culture, and tied to this for the next 4-7 years, the equity should reflect that level of commitment and risk. Is there room to move?"

---

## Cash: What to Ask

**Market reference for CTO cash in Switzerland (2026):**
- Early-stage startup (pre-seed/seed): CHF 80,000-140,000 (below market, offset by equity)
- Seed to Series A: CHF 140,000-180,000
- Series A and beyond: CHF 180,000-260,000
- Geneva premium: add 10-15% over Zurich equivalents given cost of living

**Geneva minimum floor for this role:**
CHF 120,000. Below that and equity needs to be meaningfully higher to compensate for opportunity cost.

**Opening line on cash:**
"On the cash side, I'm anchoring to Geneva market for a senior technical leadership role, which puts me in the CHF 140,000-160,000 range. I understand early-stage means compression, and I'm open to discussion if the equity is structured well."

**If they push back on cash with equity:**
"I understand the trade-off, and I'm willing to take below-market cash if the equity structure reflects the risk. But below CHF 120,000 doesn't work for Geneva living costs, and that's a hard constraint rather than a negotiating position."

---

## Vesting: Non-Negotiables

**Standard for a co-founder:**
- 4-year vest, 1-year cliff (industry standard)
- Monthly vesting after cliff (not annual)
- Acceleration on acquisition: push for double-trigger (termination + acquisition), not single-trigger
- Reverse vesting clause: fair if limited to 12-18 months, resist anything longer

**Questions to ask before signing:**
- "Does the vesting apply retroactively from a start date, or from the date of the formal agreement?"
- "Is there a good leaver / bad leaver distinction in the shareholder agreement? What does 'bad leaver' cover?"
- "If the company is acquired before my cliff, what happens to unvested shares?"

**Negotiating double-trigger acceleration:**
"I'd want to include a double-trigger acceleration clause. Single-trigger means I'm incentivized to leave before an acquisition to cash out, which is the opposite of what you want. Double-trigger aligns my incentives with the company's."

---

## Anti-Dilution and Pro-Rata Rights

**Ask for:**
- Broad-based weighted average anti-dilution (standard, not full-ratchet)
- Pro-rata rights on future rounds up to Series B: lets you maintain your percentage by participating in subsequent raises
- Information rights: board-level financial reporting

**How to raise it:**
"On the shareholder agreement side, I'd want to confirm we're using broad-based weighted average anti-dilution, pro-rata rights through Series B, and standard information rights. These are pretty typical at founding-level, but worth confirming before we finalize."

---

## Non-Cash Terms Worth Negotiating

- Board seat or observer seat: "As co-founder, I'd expect a board seat or at minimum observer rights."
- Technical hiring authority: "I want clarity that technical hiring decisions sit with me as CTO, with the CEO's input but not a veto."
- IP assignment scope: Review carefully. Make sure personal side projects that predate the company are explicitly excluded.
- Notice period: Negotiate down from whatever the contract says. 3 months is common in Switzerland; push for 2.

---

## Competing Offer Script

If you have another offer or want to create urgency without lying:

"I want to be transparent: I'm in late stages with one other company, and I expect to have something concrete from them within two weeks. I'd prefer to close with Omnia, but I need to be able to make a clear comparison. Can we get the term sheet down to specifics this week?"

---

## If the Offer Is Good

Don't negotiate for the sake of it. If the equity percentage is in the right range, the cash is acceptable, and the vesting is standard, the main ask is:
1. Double-trigger acceleration
2. Board seat
3. Pro-rata rights through Series B

Those three are low-cost for them and meaningful for you.
