# Negotiation Script: Deel, Head of Engineering (FinTech)

Role is listed as fully remote from EMEA. Candidate is based in Geneva, Switzerland. Deel is a well-funded, high-growth remote-first company (last private valuation in the low double-digit billions USD), which usually means salary bands are set against a US or UK anchor with a geographic adjustment rather than a flat local-market number, so the geographic discount conversation matters here even without a Swiss office.

## Market anchors (Switzerland, Head of Engineering / Director-level, fintech or regulated platform)

- Geneva/Zurich base salary for Head of Engineering or Director of Engineering in fintech: roughly CHF 210k to CHF 260k, depending on team size and P&L scope.
- VP Engineering equivalent scope (this role's remit spans payments, ledger, treasury and compliance, closer to VP than a typical "Head of"): CHF 240k to CHF 300k base.
- Total comp for this level at a well-capitalized, late-stage fintech (base plus bonus plus equity) commonly lands 25 to 40% above base alone.
- Swiss benchmarks (Geneva especially) run 15 to 25% above equivalent London or Berlin roles for cost-of-living reasons, and that gap is the first thing to put on the table if the initial number looks US-remote-adjusted downward rather than Swiss-adjusted.

## Opening anchor

Lead with scope, not just title: "Given this role owns technical strategy across payments, ledger, treasury and compliance, plus the IPO-readiness work, I'd expect base compensation in the CHF 250k to CHF 280k range, with equity and bonus structured on top. That's consistent with Director/VP-level fintech comp in the Swiss market, and with the audit and reconciliation stakes this role carries from day one."

Anchor high and let them negotiate down, not the other way around. Cite scope (payments plus ledger plus treasury plus compliance is a wide remit) rather than just years of experience, since Deel's own posting frames this as touching IPO-grade standards.

## If they come in low

Script: "I hear the number. Given the scope here spans four financial systems domains and this role sits at the intersection of Engineering, Finance and Compliance, I was anchored closer to [X]. Can you walk me through how the band was set, US-remote-adjusted or Swiss market rate? If it's the former, I'd want to talk about how we close that gap, through base, a sign-on, or accelerated equity vesting."

Keep the tone curious, not confrontational. Asking how the band was built usually surfaces whether there's real room or a hard floor.

## Geographic discount pushback

If the offer reflects an EMEA-wide average rather than Geneva specifically: "Geneva's cost of living is meaningfully higher than most EMEA hubs this band probably averages against, closer to London or above it on housing. I'd rather solve that with base than with a relocation stipend I don't need, since I'm staying in Geneva either way."

## Equity negotiation

Deel is private and late-stage, so equity here is more retention tool than lottery ticket, treat it accordingly.

- Ask for the current 409A or last-round valuation reference point, the strike price, and whether shares are RSUs or options, since late-stage private companies increasingly issue RSUs.
- Standard vesting is four years with a one-year cliff. Push back on anything longer than that at this seniority.
- Ask directly about secondary sale or tender offer history and cadence, since liquidity timing matters more than headline share count for a company that hasn't IPO'd yet, and this role is explicitly framed around IPO-readiness work.
- If equity is RSU-based, ask how refresh grants are handled after year one, since the first grant is often front-loaded and refresh policy is where real long-term comp lives.

## Competing offer template (use only if genuinely in play)

"I'm currently further along in conversations with another team at a similar comp band, and I like what I've seen here more so far, the scope and the technical bar in particular. If we can land close on comp, I'd rather move forward with Deel." Only deploy this if there's an actual competing process; an empty bluff is easy to spot and costs credibility for the rest of the negotiation.

## Close

Confirm the full package in writing before verbally accepting: base, bonus target and structure, equity type and vesting start date, sign-on if any, and remote-EMEA employment terms (contract type, benefits, any Swiss-specific tax or social security handling since Deel's own product is payroll and compliance infrastructure, worth asking how they handle their own EMEA remote hires).
