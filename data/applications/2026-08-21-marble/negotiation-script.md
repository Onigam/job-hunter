# Negotiation Script: Marble, Co-Founder / CTO, Physical AI for Process Industries

This isn't a normal salary negotiation. The posting sets fixed program terms: a €3,000 monthly allowance during the studio program, €250,000 inception funding at incorporation, and 80% founder ownership split between the cofounders (the studio keeps the rest). The real negotiation is how that 80% splits between CEO and CTO, the vesting terms attached to it, and whether the €3k stipend is workable given I'd be commuting to or partly based in Zurich while living in Geneva.

## Market context

- €3,000/month is roughly in line with what European venture studios and accelerator-style cofounder programs pay (Antler and Entrepreneur First run similar ranges), so it's not an outlier to push back on hard. It's tight against Zurich costs specifically though: a one-bedroom there commonly runs CHF 2,000-2,500, well above what the stipend covers on its own.
- A Senior Staff Engineer in Geneva or Zurich at a funded startup would typically see CHF 150,000-190,000 base. The gap between that and the stipend is the real cost of the founder bet, and it's worth naming once rather than pretending it doesn't matter.
- Marble's own numbers, fourteen companies incubated and all fourteen reaching follow-on funding, are the strongest argument for taking the equity bet seriously despite the low cash comp during the program.

## Opening position on equity split

"Before we go further, I want to understand how the 80% founder pool splits between the two of us. Since neither of us is bringing a pre-existing product or IP, and I'd be owning the technical platform end to end the same way I did at Alaya, I'd want to start the conversation close to an even split rather than assume the CEO seat carries a structural majority. I'm open to a modest tilt toward the CEO role given the domain expertise and industry relationships being brought in, but I want that to be a deliberate choice, not a default."

## If the split is offered heavily CEO-weighted (below 35% for CTO)

"I get why domain expertise and industry access carry weight here, especially with an ETH process engineering background behind it. But building and owning the entire technical platform, hiring the engineering team, and being the one accountable when the pilot has to actually work in a live plant is a full cofounder role, not a hire. I'd want to see that reflected closer to parity, or understand specifically what's driving the gap so we can address it directly rather than leave it as a given."

## Vesting and reverse vesting

- Standard structure to expect: 4-year vest, 1-year cliff, applied to both cofounders' shares, not just mine. Push back if only the CTO stake is subject to reverse vesting while the CEO's isn't.
- Ask what happens to unvested equity if the studio itself decides to shut the company down before the cliff, since that's a real outcome given not every incubated company survives even with a 100% follow-on-funding track record across the portfolio.
- Ask about acceleration if the CEO seat changes or if there's a founder split before Series A. Worth having this in writing given how much of the early technical direction would sit with me specifically.

## Questions on the €250,000 inception funding and runway

- Who controls the spend decisions on the €250k: joint founder sign-off, or does the studio retain approval rights.
- What's included in that figure: does it cover early hires and infrastructure, or is it meant to stretch to Series A on founder time alone.
- What's the typical timeline from incorporation to follow-on funding across the studio's portfolio, since that number tells me how long the €3k stipend period realistically lasts.

## On the stipend and location

"I'm Geneva-based and the role is Zurich-based, with visa sponsorship apparently tied to a France relocation, which reads a little inconsistent. I'd want to clarify early whether this is meant to be Zurich in-person, hybrid, or whether a Geneva-based arrangement with regular Zurich time is workable, since that changes what the €3k a month actually needs to cover."

## Competing offer leverage template (use only if real)

"I'm also in conversation with an infrastructure-focused role that comes with a market-rate salary rather than a founder stipend. I'd rather build something from the ground up again if the equity terms make sense, but I want to be upfront that the cash gap is a real factor in how I'm weighing this."

Only use if there's an actual competing process in flight. An invented one is easy to expose and costs more credibility than it buys.

## Closing line for any round

"I want the split and the vesting to be something both of us are genuinely comfortable defending to investors in six months, not just something we agreed to get the conversation moving. Let's get it in writing once we land on numbers."
