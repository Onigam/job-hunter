# Negotiation Script — Memora ApS, Founding Engineer

## Context

Memora ApS is a Danish company (ApS = private limited, Danish equivalent of a GmbH/SARL). Five-person team, likely pre-seed or seed stage. Remote role with CET overlap requirement. As a founding engineer, you are joining before the product is established, which means you carry disproportionate risk and should price accordingly.

Key lever: this is a **founding** role. That word carries equity expectations distinct from a standard senior engineering hire.

---

## Market Anchors

**Danish market (Copenhagen, senior/staff engineer):**
- DKK 750,000–950,000 (~€100–127k) for senior roles
- Founding engineer premium typically adds 15–25% to cash, plus meaningful equity

**Remote European market (senior founding engineer):**
- €110–140k is a reasonable range for a founding hire at this level
- Candidates with Magino's profile (prior founding engineer + unicorn acquisition) sit at the upper end

**Switzerland context (why it matters even for a remote role):**
- Geneva cost of living is among the highest in Europe
- CHF 180,000+ is the local equivalent for Staff-level engineering
- You can use this to justify your number without asking them to pay Swiss rates: "I'm not asking you to match a local Geneva salary, but my cost of living is relevant to what makes this work for me."

---

## Opening Anchor

If they ask your expectations first:

> "For a founding engineer role at this stage, I'm thinking in the range of €120–130k, plus equity that reflects the founding-level risk. I'm flexible on the exact split depending on how the equity is structured."

If they make the first offer:

Wait for their number. Don't fill the silence. Then respond:

> "Thanks for putting that on the table. Can you walk me through the equity piece as well before I respond? I want to think about the full package together."

---

## Counter-Offer Scripts

**Scenario A: Offer comes in at €85–95k cash + small equity**

> "I appreciate the offer. The equity component is a bit below what I'd expect for a founding role at this stage, and the cash is lower than the range I'd been calibrating to for this level of responsibility. Can we look at either bringing the base to €110k, or if that's constrained, increasing the option pool to compensate? I'm not trying to optimize one number in isolation."

**Scenario B: Offer comes in at €95–110k cash**

> "That's closer to where I'd been thinking. My main question is the equity. For a founding engineer joining a five-person team, I'd expect something in the 0.5–1.5% range, vesting over four years with a one-year cliff. Where are you on that?"

**Scenario C: They say the budget is fixed**

> "I understand there are real constraints at this stage. Let's talk about what else is on the table: cliff length, accelerated vesting on exit, title clarity, and the roadmap for the next raise. I can be flexible if the equity terms are solid."

---

## Equity Negotiation

**What to ask for:**
- Founding engineer at pre-seed/seed: 0.5–2% is the realistic range
- Push for the top of that range given Magino's profile (prior exit, staff-level experience)
- Request: 4-year vest, 1-year cliff, standard for European startups

**Key questions to ask before signing anything:**
1. What is the current fully diluted cap table? (You need to know what 1% actually means)
2. What round of funding are you on, and what's the anticipated next round dilution?
3. Are the options ISO or NSO? (Or European equivalent: EMI/warrants if UK-adjacent, phantom stock in some Danish structures)
4. Is there an acceleration clause on exit? Single trigger or double trigger?
5. What is the strike price, and is it tied to a recent 409A or fair market value assessment?

**If they offer phantom stock or a profit-sharing scheme instead of options:**
> "I'd prefer real equity in the company. Phantom stock doesn't transfer in an acquisition the same way, and for a founding hire I want to be aligned with the cap table from day one. Is real equity something we can explore?"

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

If you have other active processes (use only if true):

> "I have another process in late stages that I expect to hear back from by [date]. I'd prefer this role given the problem and the stage, but I want to be transparent about my timeline so we can move efficiently if there's mutual interest."

If you don't have a competing offer but want to signal you're not desperate:

> "I'm being selective about where I apply at this stage, specifically looking for a founding role where I can set the technical direction from the ground up. That's why this one is on my shortlist. But I do want to land somewhere in the next 4–6 weeks."

---

## Geographic Discount Pushback

If they try to lower the number because you're not in Denmark:

> "The role is remote by design, which is part of why I'm applying. My cost of living in Geneva is higher than Copenhagen, not lower, so a geographic discount in that direction doesn't quite work. I'd anchor to European market rates for this level, not a specific city."

---

## Red Flags to Watch For

- Equity below 0.5% for a founding engineer with this level of responsibility
- No cliff (unusual and unfavorable to you if things don't work out)
- Vesting without an acceleration clause on acquisition (you could vest for four years and see nothing in a quick exit)
- "We'll sort out the equity later" as a response to direct questions now
- Option pool that has already been heavily diluted before you join
