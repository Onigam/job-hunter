# Negotiation Script: n8n, Senior/Staff Engineer (n8n Labs)

## Market anchor

The posting uses a dual Senior/Staff title, same pattern as most companies that have not decided where a candidate lands until after the interview loop. Do not self-select into the Senior band before they tell you where they have landed. This role also is not a normal IC engineering seat: it is a founding member of a skunkworks team reporting close to the CEO, expected to behave like a founder rather than an employee executing a spec. That framing matters for the number, because n8n Labs headcount is scarce and each hire is meant to shape a disproportionate share of the company's next act.

n8n is based in Berlin with a remote-first team across Europe, so Berlin senior/staff engineering bands (roughly EUR 95,000-130,000 base for a well-funded, late-stage company) are the closest public reference point, but they undersell this specific seat. A Staff-level 0-to-1 builder with founder experience and an acquisition on record sits above a typical Staff IC doing roadmap work, and Geneva cost of living is real even on a remote contract.

- **Target range:** CHF 175,000-195,000 base equivalent (or EUR equivalent at Berlin rates plus a premium for scope)
- **Floor (walk-away consideration point):** CHF 155,000 equivalent
- **Stretch anchor (opening ask):** CHF 200,000 equivalent

Push hard on equity here specifically, more than on a typical Staff role, since this is functionally an internal founder seat.

## Opening anchor

If asked for a number first, tie it to the scope, not just the title:

"Given that n8n Labs is asking someone to operate like a founder, identify and validate new product bets with minimal support, I'd put the base around CHF 195,000-200,000 equivalent, plus a meaningful equity grant given the founder-level scope. That reflects 17-plus years of experience, having actually built and sold a company, and the kind of ambiguity this role is explicitly designed around."

If pushed for one number, give CHF 195,000, near the top of the range. It moves down more easily than it climbs back up from a low opening figure.

## Counter-offer script if they come in low

If the offer lands below CHF 160,000 equivalent:

"This reads closer to a standard Staff IC number than a founding role on a new product line. Building something 0-to-1 with direct exposure to leadership usually prices higher than maintaining an existing roadmap, not the same or lower. Based on what comparable founding or Staff-plus roles in this space pay, I was anchored closer to CHF 185,000-195,000 equivalent. Is there room to move there, either on base or through the equity grant?"

If base is genuinely fixed:

"If base is capped right now, I'd want to close the gap through a larger initial equity grant or a one-time signing bonus in the range of CHF 10,000-15,000."

## Equity notes

The posting itself states new hires get "a slice of n8n with equity," and n8n just raised at a $5.2bn valuation with SAP as a strategic investor, so this is not a seed-stage speculative grant, it is meaningful paper with real signal behind the mark. Treat it as a genuine second lever, not a footnote:

- Ask for the actual grant size in shares or a percentage, not just "equity is included." A vague promise is not a number you can compare against anything.
- Ask whether it is options or RSU-equivalents, and get the strike price or reference valuation if it is options, since Swiss tax treatment on each differs and changes what the grant is actually worth on paper versus in hand.
- Ask about vesting and cliff, standard is four years with a one-year cliff, and whether founding-team members on n8n Labs get any different terms given the higher risk profile of a skunkworks bet.
- Ask directly whether there is a refresh cycle tied to the Labs team's milestones, since a 0-to-1 team hitting a real launch is a natural moment for a follow-on grant.

## Geographic and remote discount pushback

Some remote-first companies quietly apply a lower band to candidates outside the head office country. Push back if this comes up:

"I understand the team is centered in Berlin, but I'm coming from Geneva, which runs at or above Berlin cost of living, so there isn't a case for a discount based on location here. I'd expect the band to reflect the scope of the role, not where I happen to be sitting."

## Competing offer template

Only use if there is an actual second process running:

"I'm further along in another process at a similar level, so I have a timeline I'm working against. I'd like to keep this moving, and if n8n can land closer to the range we discussed, I'd rather prioritize this one, the Labs mandate is the more interesting problem on the table right now."

## Practical notes

- Negotiate base, equity, and signing bonus as one package rather than line by line, it leaves more room to trade pieces against each other.
- Confirm whether the role settles at Senior or Staff before the offer stage, since that decides which end of the range to push.
- Ask directly how n8n structures Swiss remote employment, whether through a local entity or an employer-of-record, since that affects pension and social contributions and is worth knowing before comparing net numbers.
- Get level, equity grant size, and start date confirmed in writing before signing anything.
