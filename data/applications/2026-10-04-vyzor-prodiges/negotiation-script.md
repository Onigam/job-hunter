# Negotiation Script: Vyzor (Prodiges), Co-Founder & CTO

## What we actually know

- **Company:** Vyzor, an AI financial copilot for SME directors, sold through their accounting firms. Incubated by Prodiges, a French and US AI-native incubator partnered with Google Cloud.
- **Stage:** Working MVP, entering beta with its first accounting firms. No funding round is mentioned, so assume pre-seed.
- **Location:** Paris. Magino is in Geneva.
- **Commitment:** Full-time or part-time, with "equity adjusted accordingly".
- **Stack:** Next.js, TypeScript, Firebase.
- **Cash:** Nothing stated. At this stage, assume little or no salary until a round closes.

So this isn't a salary negotiation in the usual sense. It's a co-founder deal, and the things that matter most are the equity percentage, the share type, vesting, leaver terms, and what happens to cash once money comes in. Get the cap table before you discuss any number.

## Market anchors

### Switzerland (what the opportunity cost looks like)

| Role, Geneva / Zurich, 2026 | Base (CHF) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Senior Staff / Principal IC, scale-up | 170k to 210k | Magino's current level |
| CTO, funded Swiss seed / Series A | 160k to 200k | Plus roughly 1% to 5% equity if hired, not founding |
| Co-founding CTO, pre-seed | 0 to 90k | Equity is the compensation |

### France (what Vyzor will be benchmarking against)

| Role, Paris, 2026 | Base (EUR) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| CTO, seed-funded startup | 80k to 110k | Usually paid only once the seed closes |
| CTO, Series A | 110k to 150k | |
| Co-founding CTO, pre-seed | 0 to 50k | Often unpaid or a small stipend |

EUR is roughly at parity with CHF (about 0.93 to 0.95 CHF per EUR, check on the day). A Paris salary paid to someone living in Geneva is a big real pay cut. Use that when the cash discussion comes up after a round.

### Equity benchmarks for a co-founding CTO joining after the MVP

| Situation | Typical range |
|---|---|
| Full-time, joining pre-seed with an MVP already built by others | 15% to 30% |
| Full-time, technical cofounder of an equal team (no MVP yet) | 30% to 50% |
| Part-time co-founder (2 to 3 days a week) | 5% to 12% |
| "Advisor CTO" (a few hours a week) | 0.5% to 2% |

Vyzor already has an MVP, a business founder (or founders) and an incubator, so the full-time realistic band is the first row. Prodiges will probably hold its own stake, so ask what it is: the percentage that matters is your share of the fully diluted cap table, not your share of the founders' allocation.

## Targets

**Full-time scenario**
- **Opening anchor:** 25% to 30%, fully diluted, as ordinary shares.
- **Target:** 20%.
- **Walk-away:** 12%. Below that, it's an employee CTO deal dressed as a cofounder title.
- **Cash:** a stipend from day one if possible (even EUR 3k to 4k a month), and a written commitment to a market salary (EUR 100k+ or the CHF equivalent) when the first round closes, with the amount tied to the size of the round.

**Part-time scenario (recommended way to start)**
- **Opening anchor:** 10%.
- **Target:** 7% to 8%.
- **Walk-away:** 4%.
- **Plus:** a pre-agreed conversion. If Magino goes full-time within 6 to 12 months, the stake steps up to the full-time number, at a ratio written down now.

## Equity terms to insist on

1. **Share type.** Ordinary shares held directly as a founder (actions ordinaires, via the holding or directly), not BSPCE. BSPCE are employee options; they're reasonable for hires but wrong for a cofounder, and they're awkward for a Swiss tax resident anyway.
2. **Vesting.** 4 years with a 1-year cliff is standard. Ask for credit for time already spent if there's a part-time trial period. Push for monthly vesting after the cliff.
3. **Acceleration.** Double-trigger: 100% acceleration if the company is acquired and Magino is let go or the role is materially reduced within 12 months.
4. **Leaver clauses.** Good leaver keeps vested shares at fair market value. Bad leaver should be narrowly defined (gross misconduct, breaching non-compete), never just "left before year X". This is the clause French pactes d'associés most often get wrong.
5. **Anti-dilution and pro-rata.** Founders rarely get anti-dilution protection, so don't spend capital on it. Ask instead for pro-rata rights in future rounds and for founders to dilute equally.
6. **Governance.** A board seat (or seat on the strategic committee) while Magino holds more than 10%. Visibility on the term sheet and on Prodiges' rights (preference, veto, information rights).
7. **IP.** Anything built before signing stays Magino's unless explicitly assigned. Check if Prodiges takes a share of IP or revenue.

## Scripts

### When they ask what you're looking for

"Before we get to numbers, could you share the current cap table, including what Prodiges holds, and what you're planning for the first round? I'd rather we agree on a structure than trade percentages in the dark. For context, if I came in full-time as the technical cofounder, taking the MVP to production and building the team, I'd be thinking of something around 25 to 30% fully diluted, as ordinary shares with standard four-year vesting."

### If they come in low (say 5% to 10% for full-time)

"I understand you've already built the MVP, and that counts. But the role you've written is the whole technical side of the company: production, the AI layer, hiring, and talking to investors. A seed investor will look at the cap table and ask whether the CTO is properly committed. At 8%, the honest answer is that I'm a senior hire with a title. I could get to 20% if the vesting and leaver terms are clean, and that's the structure that makes me fully committed to this."

### If they push back on part-time

"Starting part-time protects both of us. You get someone who has taken a product from MVP to an acquisition, without committing a quarter of the company on a few conversations. I get to see how the beta firms respond before I give up a Staff salary. If we agree now on how the stake steps up when I go full-time, nobody has to renegotiate in six months."

### On cash, once a round closes

"I'm comfortable with little or no salary until we raise. After the round, I'd want something in line with a CTO salary for a funded startup. I'm based in Geneva, where a senior engineer's cost of living is well above Paris, so I'd want us to agree on a number in CHF, or on a EUR figure that reflects that, rather than a standard Paris grid."

### Geographic discount pushback

"Being in Geneva isn't a cost to Vyzor; I'm three hours from Paris by train and I'd be in the office regularly, at least a couple of days every couple of weeks. It does mean a Paris salary is effectively a 30 to 40% cut in purchasing power for me. I'm not asking for a Swiss salary at seed. I'm asking that it's taken into account when we set the post-round number, or offset with equity."

### Competing opportunity (use only if true at the time)

"I'll be transparent, I'm also talking to [a funded Swiss startup / a Staff role at a scale-up] where the offer is around CHF [X] base plus equity. Vyzor is the one I'm most drawn to because I'd own the product end to end. But I need the equity to reflect that I'm walking away from that certainty."

## Cross-border practicalities (Geneva resident, Paris company)

- **Tax.** Under the France-Switzerland treaty, salary for work physically done in Geneva is generally taxed in Geneva. Days worked in Paris can change that. Have a Geneva fiduciary check before signing.
- **Social security.** If more than about 25% of the work is done in Switzerland, Swiss social security usually applies, and the French company would need to register and pay Swiss contributions. Many early-stage French companies don't know how to do this. Alternatives: invoice through a Swiss entity (self-employed or a small GmbH/Sàrl) during the part-time phase, or use an employer-of-record once salaried.
- **Founder shares.** Gains on privately held shares are generally tax-free in Switzerland for private investors, but the rules on employee/founder shares are subtle (acquisition at below-market value can be taxed as income). Buying the shares at nominal value on day one, before any valuation is set, is the cleanest setup. Get advice before signing the pacte d'associés.
