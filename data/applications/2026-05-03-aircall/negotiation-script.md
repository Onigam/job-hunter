# Negotiation Script — Aircall Staff AI Software Engineer

---

## Market Context

**Aircall profile:** Series D unicorn (raised $225M+, $120M Series D in 2021 led by Goldman Sachs and HubSpot Ventures). ~600-900 employees as of 2026. European HQ in Paris, commercial HQ in New York. 22,000+ enterprise customers. AI push is active (AI Voice Agent, AI Assist Pro, dedicated LLM governance page).

**Role level:** Staff AI Software Engineer. This is a senior IC role in a high-demand specialty. AI engineers with production LLM experience command a consistent premium over general Staff eng in 2026.

**Market salary bands (2026 data):**

| Location / Arrangement | Base Range |
|---|---|
| Paris (on-site) | €125k – €165k |
| Paris Staff AI premium (2026) | €140k – €180k |
| Geneva remote (Swissco entity or payroll) | CHF 180k – CHF 230k |
| European remote via French entity | €145k – €175k + remote premium |

Geneva cost-of-living is ~25-30% above Paris. If Aircall hires you as a French-entity employee working remotely from Geneva, you'll be paid in euros at Paris market rates but living on Swiss costs. That gap is the main negotiation lever.

---

## Opening Anchor

If asked for a number first, deflect once:

> "I'd like to understand the full package structure before giving a range, including how you handle equity, remote work, and any geographic adjustments. Can you walk me through what the band looks like for this level?"

If they push for a number:

> "Based on the scope of a Staff AI role and my profile, I'm targeting around €165,000 base. I'm flexible depending on how equity and the remote arrangement are structured."

Why €165k: it's at the high end of Paris Staff AI, which gives you room to land at €145k-€155k as a realistic close while signaling you've done your research.

---

## If They Come In Low (€110k-€130k)

This would be below market for a Staff-level role. Don't dismiss; redirect to scope and data:

> "I appreciate the offer. To be transparent, the Staff AI compensation band I've been seeing in market research for this level is closer to €140k-€165k, particularly with the LLM systems experience in scope. Is there flexibility on the base, or would it make more sense to look at how equity could bridge the gap?"

If they won't move on base, push hard on equity and sign-on:

> "If the base ceiling is firm at €125k, I'd want to revisit the equity grant and explore whether there's a sign-on component. A one-time sign-on of €20k-€30k would make the first-year package competitive while staying within your ongoing comp structure."

---

## Geographic Premium — Geneva Remote

If the role is officially Paris-based but you're working remotely from Geneva:

> "I want to flag something practical. I'm based in Geneva, which runs ~25-30% above Paris on cost of living. If I'm hired on a French entity contract working remote from Switzerland, I'm essentially taking a geographic discount. Companies that want to retain Geneva-based talent usually either provide a remote premium in the range of 15-20% on top of Paris base, or they use a Swiss employer-of-record. Is that something Aircall has handled for remote hires before?"

If they offer to use a Swiss EOR (employer of record), verify the base is re-benchmarked to Swiss market rates (CHF 185k-CHF 215k range), not just the Paris rate converted at spot.

---

## Equity

Aircall is post-Series D, so stock options (BSPCEs if French entity, ISOs/NSOs if US entity) are likely rather than RSUs.

**Key questions to ask:**

- "What's the current strike price and the most recent 409A or preferred share valuation?"
- "What's the vesting schedule? Is there a 1-year cliff?"
- "What's the exercise window after departure? Is it the standard 90 days or extended?"
- "What's the total shares outstanding, and what does this grant represent as a percentage?"

**Negotiation levers:**

A Staff AI grant at this stage typically represents 0.05% to 0.15% of the company. Don't accept without knowing what that's worth at the last preferred valuation.

If the exercise window is 90 days post-departure (standard): push for 5 years or the full term. Many companies in 2026 accept this for Staff+ hires.

> "One thing that matters a lot to me on equity is the post-departure exercise window. The standard 90 days is a structure that financially disadvantages employees, especially at a private company where liquidity events are unpredictable. I'd like to negotiate a 5-year window. Is that something Aircall can accommodate?"

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

If you have another offer (or want to signal you're active):

> "I want to be transparent. I'm at an advanced stage with another company for a similar Staff AI role. Their offer is in the €155k-€165k range. Aircall is my first choice because of the voice AI infrastructure problem and the team I've spoken with, but I'd need the packages to be comparable to make that call."

Even if the competing offer is hypothetical, this framing is accurate if you're actively interviewing. Don't fabricate specific numbers if you don't have a real offer.

---

## Total Package Checklist

Before signing, verify:

- [ ] Base salary (in EUR or CHF depending on entity)
- [ ] Equity: grant size, strike price, vesting schedule, cliff, exercise window
- [ ] Sign-on bonus (one-time, usually not recoverable if you leave after 12+ months)
- [ ] Remote policy: fully remote or expected Paris office days per month
- [ ] Equipment budget
- [ ] Professional development / conference budget
- [ ] Parental leave
- [ ] Notice period (French CDI standard is 3 months for cadres; negotiate down to 1 month if possible, or reciprocal early-exit clauses)

---

## Closing Lines

When you're ready to close but haven't fully aligned:

> "I think we're close. If we can get the base to €150k and confirm the equity grant details in writing, I'm ready to move forward."

When you want to keep the process moving without committing:

> "This is helpful context. I'd like to see the offer in writing with the equity details before I can give you a firm answer. Can you send that over by end of week?"
