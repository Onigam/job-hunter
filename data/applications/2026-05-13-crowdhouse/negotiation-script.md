# Negotiation Script — crowdhouse Senior Fullstack Engineer

## Market Context

**Posted range:** 120,000-140,000 CHF/year (swissdevjobs.ch listing)

**Market benchmarks (Zurich, 2026):**
- Senior Fullstack Engineer (8-12 years): 120,000-145,000 CHF
- Staff/Lead Fullstack Engineer (12+ years, architecture scope): 140,000-165,000 CHF
- Magino's profile sits at the top of the senior band and into staff territory: 17 years, founding engineer track record, platform scale, acquisition validation

**Note on geography:** This role is in Zurich. Magino is based in Geneva. Commute or relocation costs are a legitimate negotiation variable: either a location allowance, remote flexibility, or a salary that accounts for the gap.

---

## Opening Anchor

If asked for a number first, give a range anchored above the posted maximum:

> "Based on my experience and the scope of the role, I'm targeting 145,000-155,000 CHF. That said, total comp matters more than base alone. If there's equity or meaningful variable comp in the package, I'm open to a conversation on how the numbers fit together."

**Why this works:**
- 145k is only 5k above the posted ceiling, so it's not aggressive enough to kill the conversation
- 155k leaves room to come down
- Framing total comp signals you're not fixated on base alone

---

## If They Come In at the Bottom (120,000-125,000 CHF)

> "I appreciate the offer. I want to make this work, so I'd like to share where I'm coming from. The posted range goes to 140k, and with 17 years of experience including founding and scaling a platform that was acquired by a unicorn, I'd expect to sit at the top of that band or slightly above it. Is there flexibility to move the base to 135,000-140,000? If the base is truly fixed, I'd like to understand what the equity or bonus structure looks like."

**Then stop talking. Wait for a response.**

---

## If They Match the Top of the Posted Range (140,000 CHF)

> "That's a better starting point. Two questions: is that the ceiling, or is there flexibility if I can show you the scope of what I'm bringing? And what does the package look like beyond base, specifically equity, bonus, and any flexibility on remote days given I'm in Geneva?"

---

## Commute / Remote Flexibility Push

crowdhouse is Zurich-based. Geneva-Zurich is a 2.5-hour train. Raise this proactively:

> "I want to flag logistics early so we're aligned. I'm in Geneva. If this is an in-office role five days a week, that's a real constraint. If there's flexibility for two or three days remote per week, that changes the equation significantly for me. What does the team's typical working arrangement look like?"

**If they want mostly in-office:** factor in travel costs (around 600-800 CHF/month for a GA rail pass) and ask for either a commute allowance or a corresponding base increase.

---

## Equity (if applicable)

crowdhouse is not an early-stage startup, but they may have a long-term incentive plan or profit-sharing:

> "Is there any equity or long-term incentive component? I ask because I came out of a startup environment where equity was part of the comp conversation, and I want to understand the full picture."

If they offer equity:
- Ask for vesting schedule (standard is 4 years with 1-year cliff)
- Ask for strike price vs. last valuation (to assess dilution risk)
- If options only: ask how liquidity events are structured in a proptech company of their size

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

If you have or receive another offer (real or in progress):

> "I want to be direct with you: I'm in conversations with one other company and expect to have a concrete number from them within the next week or two. I'd like to move forward with crowdhouse if we can get to a number that makes this the obvious choice. What would it take to close this in the next few days?"

---

## Total Comp Summary Targets

| Scenario | Base Target | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Best case | 150,000 CHF | Remote 3d/week, staff scope acknowledged |
| Realistic close | 140,000-145,000 CHF | Top of posted range + small premium |
| Floor (walk away below) | 130,000 CHF | Below this, Zurich commute cost erodes net value |

---

## Timing

Don't accept verbally in the meeting. Standard script:

> "Thank you, this is encouraging. I'd like to take a day to review the full package in writing before I respond. Can you send over the offer letter and any equity documentation by tomorrow?"
