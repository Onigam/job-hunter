# Negotiation Script — Neuroscale AI / Founding Software Engineer (TypeScript)

## Market Context

Neuroscale AI is a US-based AI startup (signals: DoD SBIR Phase II, NVIDIA Inception Program, HPE Unleash AI Program, enterprise clients including NVIDIA, Deloitte, Palantir, U.S. Air Force). The "Founding Software Engineer" title signals early-stage, high-leverage, equity-significant compensation. The Senior Backend TypeScript role posts at $120K-$150K USD; a founding engineer at this stage typically anchors higher given the ownership scope.

**Switzerland/Geneva market anchors for senior TypeScript/Node.js engineers (2026):**
- Senior Backend Engineer at a scale-up (Geneva): CHF 150,000-175,000 base
- Staff/Founding Engineer at a funded US AI startup (remote): $155,000-$200,000 USD base + meaningful equity
- Comparable TypeScript Staff roles in Zurich: CHF 165,000-195,000 at fintech/AI companies

**Relevant comp data points:**
- Benevity Senior Staff Engineer (Geneva, post-acquisition): ~CHF 165,000-175,000 base
- LinkedIn Staff Engineer in Switzerland: CHF 175,000-195,000
- AI startup founding engineers (US, Series A): $165,000-$210,000 + 0.3-0.8% equity

---

## Opening Anchor

**If asked for expectations first:**

> "I'm at the Senior Staff level with a founding engineer background and 17 years of production TypeScript and Node.js experience. For a founding engineer role at this stage, I'm targeting CHF 175,000-190,000 base, or the USD equivalent if the comp is structured in dollars. On top of that, equity needs to be meaningful given the founding scope. I'm flexible on the mix, but those are the numbers I need to start from."

Do not anchor lower. A founding engineer hire is being asked to take on early-stage risk and shape the architecture. Price that in from the start.

---

## If Their First Offer Is Below Range

**If they come in at CHF 150,000 or below:**

> "I appreciate the offer, and the product and problem space are genuinely interesting, which is why I want to make this work. But CHF 150,000 is below what I need given the seniority and founding scope of the role. Based on comparable Staff+ TypeScript positions at AI startups in Switzerland and internationally, I was expecting CHF 175,000-185,000. Can you move closer to that range?"

**If they push back with "we're early-stage, budget is tight":**

> "I understand. If the base is harder to move, I'm open to discussing equity as a partial offset. But I'd need to understand the cap table before I can evaluate that trade properly. What's the current option pool, the vesting schedule, and the last valuation?"

---

## Equity Negotiation (US Startup Structure)

**Questions to ask before accepting any equity offer:**
1. What is the current fully diluted share count?
2. What was the last valuation (409A strike price for options, preferred price for context)?
3. What is the vesting schedule? (Standard: 4-year / 1-year cliff)
4. Is there acceleration on change of control? (Single-trigger or double-trigger)
5. What is the exercise window after departure? (Standard is 90 days; push for 5-10 years if possible)
6. What is the option pool size and how much has been allocated?

**Equity target for a Founding Engineer hire at pre/early Series A:**
- Below 0.2%: low for this title at this stage, worth negotiating up
- 0.2-0.5%: reasonable range; verify vesting terms
- 0.5-0.8%+: strong; still confirm the strike price vs. last 409A

**Script if equity is presented as offsetting lower base:**

> "I'm open to that trade, but I want to understand what the percentage actually represents in realistic exit scenarios. Can you share the current cap table structure and the 409A strike price? I want to make sure the options have real value at the grant price before I build my compensation picture around them."

## Geographic Discount Pushback

Some US startups apply a geographic discount for engineers outside San Francisco or New York. Neuroscale may have a remote pay policy that tiers by location.

**If they cite a geographic discount:**

> "I'm in Geneva, which is one of the most expensive cities in the world to live and work in. The cost-of-living adjustment here runs the other direction compared to most US cities outside of NYC or SF. I don't think a geographic discount applies in this context, and I'd want the comp to reflect the market for the talent you're actually hiring."

You can back this up: a software engineer in Geneva pays Swiss federal and cantonal income taxes plus mandatory social security contributions, carries Geneva rent costs (~CHF 2,500-3,500/month for a standard apartment), and faces a cost of living index that consistently ranks above New York and comparable to San Francisco in most surveys.

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

> "I'm in conversations with two other companies at similar stages, both at the founding or Staff level, and the comp discussions are in the CHF 175,000+ range. I'm prioritizing Neuroscale because the problem is more interesting, but I need the economics to not be significantly below what else is on the table."

Only use this if you have at least one other active process. Do not fabricate specifics.

---

## Timing and Process

- Ask about the offer timeline before starting negotiations: "When are you aiming to close this hire?"
- Request the written offer before agreeing verbally to any specific number
- Give yourself 48-72 hours to review any written offer before responding
- If they compress the timeline ("we need to know by tomorrow"), push back: "I want to say yes, but I need to make sure the terms are right. Can I have until Thursday?"

---

## Walk-Away Point

For this role, the minimum acceptable package (Geneva, full-time):

- Base: CHF 160,000 / $160,000 USD minimum
- Equity: at least 0.2% with standard 4-year / 1-year cliff vesting
- Benefits: health insurance contribution or Swiss-equivalent coverage at minimum

Below this threshold, the financial risk of joining an early-stage startup without the upside to match doesn't make sense.
