# Negotiation Script — Adaptyv Bio, Forward Deployed Engineer

## Context

- Company: Early-stage startup (~22 employees, Biopole, Lausanne)
- Likely Series A or early Series B (biotech + AI intersection, automated lab infrastructure)
- Role: FDE — customer-facing, production code, occasional travel to client sites
- Location: Lausanne or hybrid/remote from Geneva

---

## Market Anchors (Switzerland, 2026)

| Reference point | CHF / year |
|---|---|
| Senior Software Engineer, Lausanne/Geneva | 140,000 – 165,000 |
| Staff Engineer, product company | 165,000 – 190,000 |
| Customer-facing technical role (SA/FDE) premium | +10–15% |
| Biotech sector premium (competing with pharma) | +5–10% |

**Realistic target base:** CHF 170,000 – 190,000  
**Opening anchor:** CHF 190,000 – 195,000 base

At 22 employees you're taking startup risk, and that should price in.

---

## Opening Anchor Script

When they ask about salary expectations, lead with the number, not a range.

> "Based on the scope of the role, the seniority level, and Swiss market rates for technical customer-facing positions at this stage of company, I'm thinking around CHF 190,000 base. That's before equity, which for a company at this stage is a meaningful part of the package. What does the comp structure look like on your side?"

If they push back immediately:

> "I'm open to the full picture. If the base is lower, I'd want to understand what the equity stake looks like and what the vesting terms are, because that changes the calculus significantly."

---

## If They Come In Below CHF 155,000

Don't decline immediately. Extract information first.

> "I appreciate you being direct. CHF [X] is below where I'd need to land to make this work on base alone. A couple of questions before I respond: what's the equity grant size you're thinking, and what's the current cap table context — pre-money, recent round size, approximate outstanding shares? That's what I'd need to model this properly."

Then, once you have the equity numbers:

> "Here's my read: if the equity is [0.3–0.5%] on a 4-year vest at your current valuation, that closes a meaningful part of the gap for me. But I'd want to get to CHF 165,000 on base to feel comfortable with the cash flow in Geneva. Can you get there?"

---

## Equity Negotiation

For a ~22-person company, these are reasonable ranges depending on the stage:

| Stage | Target equity |
|---|---|
| Pre-Seed / Seed | 0.3% – 0.7% |
| Series A | 0.15% – 0.4% |
| Series B | 0.05% – 0.15% |

**Cliff and vesting:** 4-year vest, 1-year cliff is standard. Don't accept anything longer.

**Acceleration:** Push for double-trigger acceleration (full vest on acquisition if role is terminated or substantially changed). Adaptyv, given the protein engineering + AI intersection, is an acquisition target. This clause is worth negotiating for.

> "One thing I'd want in the offer is a double-trigger acceleration clause. If the company is acquired and my role is either eliminated or significantly changed, I'd want the unvested equity to accelerate. That's standard at this stage and it protects both of us from an awkward outcome."

**Valuation context:** Ask for the current valuation cap and the most recent round's price per share. Without this, the percentage is abstract.

> "Can you share the current 409A valuation or the most recent round's post-money? I want to understand what the equity actually represents in dollar terms before I respond to the offer."

---

## Geographic Pushback (if they try to discount for remote)

If they argue Geneva is "outside the target area" or propose a remote discount:

> "Geneva and Lausanne are 45 minutes apart, and I'm fully available for on-site work and customer travel. The cost of living in the Geneva area is comparable to Lausanne — COLA data puts them within 5% of each other. A discount based on geography doesn't make sense here."

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

If you have another offer (or want to create time pressure):

> "I want to be transparent with you: I'm in late-stage conversations with one other company in Switzerland that's moving quickly. I'd prefer this role given the problem you're solving, but I need to give them an answer within [10 days]. If you're able to move the offer to [CHF 175,000 base + equity], I'm ready to sign and move things forward on your timeline."

If no competing offer:

> "I'm focused on a small number of roles right now and this one is at the top of my list. That said, I want to make sure the numbers work before I commit. If we can get to CHF [X] and confirm the equity terms, I'm ready to move quickly."

---

## Non-Salary Terms Worth Discussing

- **Remote flexibility:** Confirm how many days/week are expected in Lausanne
- **Travel policy:** If customer travel is required, confirm company covers all costs including per diem
- **Equipment:** Laptop spec and who owns it
- **Conference/learning budget:** Ask for CHF 3,000 – 5,000 / year for tech conferences, training
- **Notice period:** Aim for 2 months (standard Swiss), push back if they want less

---

## Offer Evaluation Checklist

- [ ] Base salary at or above CHF 165,000
- [ ] Equity grant with clear % and current valuation reference
- [ ] 4-year vest, 1-year cliff
- [ ] Double-trigger acceleration clause
- [ ] Travel policy documented
- [ ] Remote/hybrid terms in writing
- [ ] Start date agrees with your availability (July or later)
