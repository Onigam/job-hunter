# Negotiation Script — Inkeep, Staff Engineer AI Agents

## Context

**Published range:** $180,000-$250,000 base + 0.1%-0.4% equity  
**Location:** On-site, San Francisco or New York (relevant if relocation is required)  
**Stage:** Khosla Ventures + Y Combinator backed, early-stage  
**Candidate location:** Geneva, Switzerland (CHF market, high cost of living)

---

## Compensation Benchmarks

**US market, Staff/Principal TypeScript engineer at a Khosla-backed AI startup (2025-2026):**
- Base range for Staff+: $220k-$270k in SF/NYC
- Inkeep's top of published range ($250k) is slightly below market for SF
- Equity for Staff+ at seed/early Series A: 0.3%-0.6% is competitive; 0.1% is low

**Switzerland reference (if you have competing offers):**
- Geneva Staff Engineer at a tech scale-up: CHF 160,000-200,000 base
- CHF converts to roughly $175k-$220k at current rates, but Swiss benefits (pension, healthcare) add ~25-30% effective compensation
- Total CHF package of CHF 180k base + employer pension contributions effectively competes with a $230k US base with no benefits

---

## Opening Position

**Target base:** $245,000-$250,000 (top of their range, justified by Staff+ experience and the founding-engineer background)  
**Target equity:** 0.35%-0.4% (middle of their published range, negotiable up)  
**Relocation:** If on-site is required, ask for a relocation package ($15,000-$25,000 is standard)

**Do not anchor below $230,000.** The published range goes to $250k, so starting anywhere below $230k gives up room unnecessarily.

---

## Script: When They Make an Offer

If they open at $200k or below:

> "I appreciate the offer. Based on the scope of the role and what I've seen of similar Staff-level positions at AI infrastructure companies in San Francisco, I was expecting something closer to $245,000. Is there room to move in that direction?"

If they open at $215k-$225k:

> "That's a reasonable starting point. I'd want to get to $245,000 on the base. Given the on-site requirement and the cost of living in SF, that's where I'd need to be to make the math work. On the equity side, can we discuss moving toward 0.35%?"

If they open at $240k or above:

> "That works well on the base. I'd like to spend a moment on equity. At 0.1%, the upside is limited relative to the stage and the risk involved in joining early. Can we get to 0.35%?"

---

## Equity Negotiation

Inkeep is early-stage (Khosla + YC). Equity matters here more than at a late-stage company.

**Key asks:**
- Vesting: 4-year with 1-year cliff (standard, confirm they're not doing anything unusual)
- Exercise window: 90 days post-termination is the default, but push for 5 years or "early exercise" rights if you can get it
- Anti-dilution: Ask if the options are ISO or NSO; ISOs have a tax advantage up to $100k/year of exercise
- Current 409A valuation: Ask what the current 409A is vs. the last preferred round price. The spread tells you how underwater options might go in a down round.

**Script on equity:**

> "I want to make sure I understand the equity package fully before we finalize. What's the current 409A valuation, and what was the price per share in the last preferred round? And can you confirm whether these are ISOs or NSOs?"

If they're offering 0.1% and won't move:

> "I understand there's a range constraint. If we can't move on percentage, is there any flexibility to accelerate the cliff from 12 months to 6 months, or to extend the post-termination exercise window? That would meaningfully change the risk profile for me."

---

## Relocation Leverage

If the role is truly on-site (SF or NYC):

> "I'm based in Geneva right now, so relocation is part of the equation. Can you include a relocation package in the offer? Standard for this kind of move is $15,000-$25,000. That covers the practical costs and makes the timing workable."

If they say remote is possible (it was listed as on-site, so confirm):

> "If there's flexibility on location, I'd want to understand what that looks like in practice. Full remote, or expected to be on-site a certain number of weeks per month?"

---

## Competing Offer Template

If you have another offer (or want to create urgency without lying):

> "I want to be transparent: I'm also in conversations with [company/type of company], and they're moving quickly. I'd like to resolve this with Inkeep by [date] if possible. Can we get the final offer to me by then?"

If no competing offer but you want to signal you're not desperate:

> "I'm running a focused search right now, and Inkeep is genuinely one of my top priorities. That said, I want to make sure the economics make sense before I wind down other conversations. Can we move quickly on the final terms?"

---

## Bottom Line / Walk-Away

**Minimum acceptable package to accept without a competing offer:**
- Base: $225,000+
- Equity: 0.2%+
- If on-site: relocation package included

**Walk away if:** Base under $210k and equity under 0.2%, no movement on either. At that level, the Geneva market with Swiss benefits is financially equivalent and carries significantly less lifestyle disruption.
