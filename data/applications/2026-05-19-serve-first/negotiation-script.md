# Negotiation Script — Serve First / Staff AI Backend Engineer

---

## Market Context

**Company profile:** Seed-stage UK AI startup, £4.5M raised, distributed team (UK/US/India/Philippines). Budget exists but runway discipline is real at this stage.

**Role level:** Staff (8+ years required; Magino brings 17+). Their stack is Node.js, TypeScript, MongoDB, AWS — a direct match, not a ramp-up hire.

**Compensation listed:** "Competitive salary, UK pension, 20 days holiday." No numbers disclosed, which signals room to anchor.

**UK Staff Backend market (London):** £120,000-£160,000 for Staff-level AI engineering. At seed stage, skews toward the lower end unless they're competing for scarce AI talent.

**Geneva equivalent:** CHF 190,000-230,000 is the realistic Staff+ range for this role level in Geneva's market. Even at a geographic discount for remote, CHF 175,000-195,000 is defensible.

**Key tension:** A UK company paying UK rates to a Geneva-based remote hire will create a ~30-40% compensation gap vs. local market. Push for CHF-denominated salary or a rate that reflects Geneva CoL, not London CoL.

---

## Opening Anchor

When they ask for your salary expectation, respond:

> "Based on Staff-level AI backend roles in the Swiss market and the scope of what you're describing (architecture ownership, AI integration, mentoring), I'm targeting CHF 195,000-215,000 base. I'm flexible on how that's structured, and I'd want to understand the equity component alongside the cash."

**Why this anchor works:**
- It's grounded in a real market (Geneva, not London), which is legitimate
- It opens above your floor (CHF 175,000-180,000) without being unrealistic
- It frames equity as a separate conversation, which preserves two levers

---

## If They Come in Low (£100,000-£115,000 / ~CHF 110,000-125,000)

> "I appreciate the offer. The role and the problem are genuinely interesting, which is why I want to make this work. That said, the number is materially below what Staff-level AI engineering commands in the Swiss market, where my cost of living is benchmarked. I'd need to get to at least CHF 175,000 to take this seriously, and ideally closer to CHF 190,000 to align with the scope you've described. Is there flexibility on the base, or would equity be part of making up the gap?"

**Hold the line on CHF framing.** If they push back that they're a UK company: "I understand, but I'm being paid in Swiss francs and taxed in Switzerland — Geneva CoL is simply higher than London. That's not a negotiating position, it's the economic reality."

---

## If They Offer a Reasonable Base (£120,000-£140,000 / ~CHF 130,000-150,000)

This is in range but below target. Counter:

> "That's closer to where I'd expect for this level, though still a bit light relative to the Swiss market. I'd want to get to CHF 175,000-180,000 on the base. If that's a stretch, I'd like to understand what the equity package looks like, because at seed stage with a well-defined AI platform mandate, that's a real part of the conversation."

---

## Equity Negotiation (Seed Stage)

At a £4.5M seed round with a distributed team, expect options in the 0.05-0.25% range for Staff-level hires. Push for the top of that range given the scope.

**Ask:**
> "What does the equity package look like in terms of percentage of fully diluted cap, vesting schedule, and cliff?"

**Target:** 0.15-0.25% fully diluted, 4-year vest, 1-year cliff. Standard.

**If they offer below 0.1% for a Staff+ role:**
> "For someone taking on architecture ownership at this stage of the company, I'd expect to see something closer to 0.15-0.2%. The risk profile of joining at seed deserves to be reflected in the option grant, especially if the base is also at the lower end."

**Anti-dilution:** At seed, you can ask whether the plan includes standard anti-dilution provisions (weighted average), but don't make this a dealbreaker at this stage.

**Exercise window:** Ask about the post-termination exercise window. Many startups still default to 90 days. Push for at least 2 years or 10 years. This is a real gotcha for early employees.

---

## Geographic Discount Pushback

If they argue Magino should accept a discount for being remote vs. being in London:

> "I'd flip that framing: Geneva has a higher cost of living than London, not lower. Swiss income tax, Swiss housing, Swiss healthcare — the baseline costs are higher, not lower. Being remote from Geneva is a benefit for you (no relocation, no London office overhead) not a discount event for me."

---

## Competing Offer Leverage Template

If you have or acquire other offers during the process, use this framing:

> "I want to be transparent with you: I have another process that's moving forward at [CHF X / £X]. I'd rather work on the Serve First problem, but I need the numbers to be within reach. If you can get to CHF [X], I'll close the other process and commit here."

Keep this honest. If the competing offer is real, name it. If it's hypothetical, don't fabricate it — the loss of credibility if caught is not worth it.

---

## Benefits to Negotiate Beyond Base

- **Hardware budget:** They mention one — ask for the amount (£2,000-£3,000 is reasonable to request)
- **Home office stipend:** If not listed, ask for CHF 100-150/month toward internet and workspace
- **Learning budget:** They mention "professional development funding" — anchor on CHF 3,000/year
- **Holiday:** 20 days is below Swiss norms (25 days is standard). Ask for 25 or a flexible arrangement
- **Contract type:** Clarify whether they hire you as a contractor (likely, for CH remote) or as an employee via an EOR. Tax and benefits implications differ significantly

---

## Walk-Away Points

- Below CHF 160,000 base with no meaningful equity: walk
- Below CHF 175,000 with standard equity (0.15%+): negotiable, but thin
- Below CHF 190,000 with excellent equity (0.25%+): borderline acceptable at seed with strong upside
- Any role where you're not owning architecture decisions: not the right fit regardless of comp
