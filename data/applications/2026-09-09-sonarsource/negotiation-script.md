Negotiation Script, SonarSource, Staff Software Engineer (Java), Identity Squad, Geneva

Context: SonarSource is an established, profitable Geneva-based company, not a cash-strapped startup, so this is a standard senior comp negotiation, base plus bonus plus whatever long-term incentive plan they run for staff-level ICs, rather than a cofounder-style equity conversation. The role is hybrid, Monday, Tuesday, and Thursday in the Geneva office, which removes any relocation discount argument since Magino already lives in Geneva full time.

## Market anchors, Geneva, Staff Software Engineer, 8+ years including tech lead scope

Staff-level Java engineers at established, profitable Geneva or Zurich tech companies typically land in the CHF 145k to 185k base range, with the top of that band reserved for candidates bringing specialized domain expertise such as identity and access management or security-adjacent systems, or who own cross-team architecture decisions. Total comp including bonus, often a 10 to 15% target at this level, commonly lands around CHF 165k to 210k. Geneva's cost of living sits among the highest in Europe, on par with Zurich, so there's no basis for anchoring below a standard Geneva rate. SonarSource is privately held with a strong valuation history; the posting doesn't mention equity for staff-level ICs, so that question needs a direct answer early in the process. If there's no equity or RSU plan at this level, that gap should show up as a higher base or bonus target instead.

## Opening position

Before naming a number, get clarity on where the offered range actually sits and whether the role carries any equity or long-term incentive component.

Opening anchor: "Given the scope here, owning identity architecture across both cloud and self-managed products at a Staff level, with mentoring and cross-team ownership built in, I'd expect a base in the CHF 170k to 185k range plus your standard bonus structure. I led comparable cross-team architecture work at Benevity after the Alaya acquisition, so this isn't a stretch relative to the responsibility."

## If they come in low on base

"I hear the range you're working with is lower than that. Given the Staff scope, the identity domain complexity, and that I'm already Geneva-based so there's no relocation cost to offset, I'd want to understand what's driving the gap. If the base has a hard ceiling, let's talk about bonus target or a signing bonus that closes some of the difference in year one."

## If there's no equity or long-term incentive plan at this level

"If Staff engineers don't participate in an equity or RSU plan here, I'd want that reflected somewhere else, either a higher base or a larger bonus target. Most comparable Staff roles I'm looking at include some long-term component, and I'm not asking for founder-level equity, just parity with what a role at this scope usually carries elsewhere."

## Geographic discount pushback

If there's any suggestion Geneva pay should track a lower Swiss city or a remote-from-elsewhere rate: "Geneva is one of the more expensive cities in Switzerland, comparable to Zurich, and I'm already based here full time, so there's no relocation saving to pass through. The offer should reflect standard Geneva market rate."

## Competing offer as counterweight, use only if real

"I'm also in later-stage conversations for a Staff or Principal platform role in the CHF 175k to 195k base range. I'm more drawn to the identity and access problem space here since it's closer to the architecture work I want to keep doing, but the base needs to be in a comparable range for that preference to win."

## Equity and incentive plan questions to ask directly

Ask whether Staff-level ICs participate in any equity, RSU, or phantom share plan, and if so, the vesting schedule and cliff. Given SonarSource's size and valuation history, a vague answer here is worth pushing on rather than letting it slide to "we'll cover that after you start."

## Notice period and start date

Standard Swiss notice periods run one to three months depending on tenure and contract. Confirm this on the first call rather than letting it surface as a surprise at the written offer stage.

## Close

Ask for the offer in writing with base, bonus target, and any equity or incentive plan terms and vacation and benefits spelled out before accepting anything verbally. If the equity question comes back vague a second time, push for a specific number or a specific date when one will exist.
