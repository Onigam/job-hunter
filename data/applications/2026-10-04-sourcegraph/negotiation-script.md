# Negotiation Script: Sourcegraph, Tech Lead - Code Plane [IC5]

## What the posting tells us

The role is remote, with a preference for Europe or EST and at least 20 hours a week of overlap with CEST. Sourcegraph publishes base pay by geographic zone:

| Zone | Base (USD) |
|---|---|
| Zone 2 | $192,000 |
| Zone 3 | $144,000 |

Equity and benefits come on top. Sourcegraph is private (a16z, Sequoia, Redpoint), so equity will almost certainly be stock options, not RSUs.

The single biggest variable is which zone Geneva lands in. At roughly 0.80 CHF per USD, Zone 2 is about CHF 154k and Zone 3 about CHF 115k. CHF 115k is below what a senior engineer earns in Geneva, never mind a Staff-level tech lead. Getting placed in Zone 2 (or above) matters more than any other line item.

## Switzerland market reference (Staff / Tech Lead IC, 2026)

| Employer type | Base, Geneva/Zurich |
|---|---|
| Local scale-ups and fintech, Staff/Lead | CHF 160k to 190k |
| Big tech Zurich, Staff (L6 equivalent) | CHF 200k to 240k base, plus RSUs |
| US-remote startups paying into Switzerland | USD 170k to 220k base is common for Staff |

Geneva is one of the most expensive cities in the world. Mandatory health insurance, rent, and the employee share of pension and social contributions all eat into a USD number faster than people in the US expect.

## Targets

- **Walk-away base:** USD 180,000 (about CHF 145k). Below this, it's a pay cut relative to the local market for this level.
- **Target base:** USD 192,000, the Zone 2 figure, which is the realistic win.
- **Opening anchor:** USD 205,000 to 215,000, justified by Geneva cost of living and the Staff-level scope (tech lead plus roadmap ownership).
- **Equity:** ask for the number of options, strike price, latest 409A and preferred share price, and total fully diluted share count, so the grant can be valued as a percentage, not just a count.

## Script: when they ask for expectations early (recruiter screen)

"For a tech lead role at this scope, and given I'm in Geneva, I'm looking at a base around 205 to 215 thousand USD, plus equity. I saw the zone-based ranges in the posting, so I'd like to understand early which zone Switzerland falls into. Geneva's cost of living is closer to San Francisco than to most of Europe, and I'd want the offer to reflect that."

If pushed for a single number: "205 base. I'm flexible on the mix if the equity is meaningful."

## Script: if they place Geneva in Zone 3

"I understand the zone model, and I'm not asking you to make an exception for no reason. The cost of living in Geneva is among the highest anywhere, and local Staff-level roles here pay CHF 160k to 190k base before equity. At the Zone 3 number, this role would be a meaningful step down from what the local market pays. Is there a path to Zone 2 for Switzerland, or flexibility on level or equity that would close the gap?"

If they hold firm on zone: "Then let's look at the equity grant and a sign-on bonus to bridge the first year, and agree on a compensation review at six months tied to clear expectations for the role."

## Script: counter-offer if the number comes in low

"Thanks for this, and I'll be honest, I'm keen on Code Plane; the agentic Batch Changes work is exactly where I want to spend the next few years. The base is below where I need to be, though. Given the scope, which is tech lead and roadmap ownership across Batch Changes, Code Monitors, Insights and src CLI, and the Geneva market, I'd need the base at [target] to make this work. If base is truly capped, I'd look at a larger option grant or a sign-on bonus to get there."

Then stop talking and let them respond.

## Equity questions to ask (private company options)

- Number of options, strike price, and the latest 409A valuation
- Last preferred share price, so the paper value is grounded
- Total fully diluted shares outstanding, to calculate ownership percentage
- Vesting schedule and cliff (standard is 4 years with a 1-year cliff; ask whether there's any acceleration on change of control)
- Post-termination exercise window. A 90-day window is a real cost for an option holder; ask if they offer an extended window (some companies give 5 to 10 years)
- Refresh grant policy for tech leads
- Liquidation preference stack, at least at a high level, since it determines what common shareholders actually see in an exit

Anti-dilution protection is for investors, not employees, so don't spend negotiating capital there. Exercise window and grant size are where the value is.

## Swiss employment specifics to clarify

- **Employment vehicle:** direct Swiss entity, Employer of Record (Deel, Remote, etc.), or contractor? Under an EOR, confirm the employer covers its share of AHV/AVS, LPP pension and accident insurance on top of base, not carved out of it.
- **Currency:** paid in USD or CHF? If USD, ask whether there's any FX adjustment policy. A 10% swing in USD/CHF is a 10% pay cut.
- **Pension (LPP/BVG):** what plan the EOR uses and the employer contribution rate.
- **Holidays:** Swiss legal minimum is 20 days; ask for 25, which is normal for senior roles here.
- **Equity tax:** in Switzerland, options are generally taxed at exercise. Worth knowing the exercise mechanics before signing.

## Competing offer template (use only if true)

"I want to be transparent: I'm in late-stage conversations with another company, and they've come in at [X base] plus [equity]. Sourcegraph is my first choice because of the Code Plane work itself, so I'd rather find a way to make this work. Is there room to get closer to that number?"

Never invent a competing offer. If there isn't one, use market data instead: "Staff-level roles in Geneva and Zurich are paying CHF 160k to 240k base, and that's what I'm benchmarking against."

## Things to trade if base is fixed

1. Zone 2 placement (worth roughly USD 48k a year, the highest-value ask)
2. Larger option grant or extended exercise window
3. Sign-on bonus to bridge year one
4. Six-month compensation review with written criteria
5. Home office and co-working budget (Geneva co-working runs CHF 300 to 500 a month)
6. Conference and learning budget
