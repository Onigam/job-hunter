# Negotiation Script: Senior Staff MLOps & Cloud Platform Engineer, Robotics @ Hexagon Robotics (Zürich)

## Context
- Hexagon Robotics is a division of Hexagon AB, the Swedish-listed precision measurement group. It builds AEON, a humanoid robot for industrial use that launched in June 2025 and is piloting with five customers. This is a big, listed company with a startup-style unit inside it, not a VC-backed startup.
- Role: Senior Staff IC who owns cloud, MLOps and data infrastructure architecture for robotics ML. The posting says 100% on-site in Zürich but the benefits list a "hybrid model", so **clarify the on-site expectation early**. It matters a lot when you're commuting from Geneva.
- No salary range is published. The benefits listed are a bonus system, strong pension contributions, 25 to 30 days of vacation, a CHF 500 mobility credit and relocation support.
- Be realistic about your position: you're strong on platform architecture, founding-engineer ownership and Kubernetes/CI/CD, and lighter on GPU and MLOps depth, Azure and robotics. Anchor on scope and seniority, and don't pretend the gaps aren't there. They'll probably level you partly on those gaps, so push back on level before you push on salary.

## Your target

- **Walk-away:** CHF 185k base plus a target bonus of at least 10%. Anything less is a mid-level Zürich salary for a Senior Staff title, before counting the commute or a move.
- **Target:** CHF 205k base, 15% target bonus, eligibility for Hexagon's long-term incentive plan, plus a relocation package or a commuting arrangement.
- **Opening anchor:** CHF 225k base plus 15 to 20% bonus. Justify it with the Senior Staff scope (owning architecture across R&D teams), 17+ years of experience, founder-level ownership through an acquisition, and the fact that Zürich big tech sets the ceiling for ML infrastructure talent.

## Script: when they ask for your expectations

"For a Senior Staff role that owns the cloud and MLOps architecture across the robotics R&D teams, and based on Zürich market data for Staff+ platform and ML infrastructure roles, I'm looking at around CHF 225k base plus a meaningful bonus. I'm flexible on the mix. Could you tell me how the band for this level is structured, and whether the role is eligible for the group's long-term incentive plan?"

Then stop talking and let them answer.

## Counter-offer script: if they come in low (e.g. CHF 170k)

"Thanks, I appreciate the offer and I'm keen on the role. The number is below what I see for Staff+ infrastructure roles in Zürich, and it's a Senior Staff scope, with architecture ownership across teams and the vendor decisions that come with it. If you can move the base to CHF 205k, I can commit quickly. If the base band is fixed, let's look at the bonus target, a sign-on payment or the level itself."

If they point to your limited MLOps or robotics background:

"That's fair, and I raised it myself. What you're getting is someone who has already built a production platform from zero and taken it through an acquisition, which is the harder part to find. I'd be glad to agree on concrete 6-month milestones, with a compensation review tied to them."

## Levers other than base (industrial corporates often have more room here)
- **Level/title:** make sure the offer really is Senior Staff (or Principal) in Hexagon's grading. The level sets the band, the bonus percentage and LTIP eligibility for years afterwards.
- **Sign-on bonus:** CHF 15k to 30k to cover any forfeited Benevity bonus or unvested equity.
- **Long-term incentive:** ask whether senior ICs in Hexagon Robotics take part in Hexagon's share-based LTIP or a division-specific plan. If the robotics unit is ever spun out or separately funded, ask how employees would participate.
- **Pension:** "strong pension contributions" can be worth a lot. Ask for the employer's share of the LPP/BVG contribution and whether there's a supplementary (1e) plan. An employer share above the legal minimum can be worth 5 to 10% of base.
- **Relocation or commute:** Geneva to Zürich is about 2h45 each way by train. Ask for either full relocation support, or an arrangement with a set number of on-site days, a GA travelcard paid by them (roughly CHF 4k/year in 2nd class; check current SBB prices) and a remote-work allowance for the other days.
- **Vacation:** the posting says 25 to 30 days depending on age. Ask for the upper end given your seniority.
- **Development:** a conference and training budget (NVIDIA GTC, ROSCon, KubeCon), and time to get cloud or MLOps certifications that close the Azure gap.

## Geographic discount pushback
Zürich pay is the benchmark here and it's already high, so the usual risk is the opposite one: being treated as a "remote Geneva" hire and offered less. If they suggest that:

"The role is based in Zürich and I'll be there as the role requires, so I'd expect the Zürich band. The commute or the move is on my side to manage, and I'd like the package to reflect that it's real."

## Competing offer template (use only if true)
"I want to be transparent. I'm at a late stage with another company for a Staff platform role, and their offer is around CHF [X] base plus [equity/bonus]. Hexagon Robotics is my first choice because of AEON and the chance to build the data platform while it's still being shaped. If we can get closer to CHF [target], I'll withdraw from the other process."

## Questions to ask before signing
1. What does on-site actually mean week to week, and is there a formal hybrid policy?
2. Which level in Hexagon's grading is this, and what's the band?
3. Is the bonus paid on individual goals, division results or group results, and what has it paid out historically?
4. Is the role eligible for any long-term incentive plan?
5. How much of the GPU budget and vendor selection will this role own?
6. Where does the robotics data platform sit today: cloud-first, on-prem GPUs, or hybrid?
