# Negotiation Script: Wolters Kluwer, Principal Full Stack Engineer, AI Platform & Agents

Posted location: Hürth, Germany, remote-first in the US or EU with hybrid options near major offices. Needs a 9 to 11 AM US Central overlap (4 to 6 PM in Geneva).
Posted comp: not disclosed. Level: Principal, reporting to the Director of Engineering, AI Platform.
Company: Wolters Kluwer is a large, listed Dutch information services group. Expect structured salary bands, an annual bonus and possibly a long-term incentive plan, but no startup equity.

---

## 1. Read the situation first

**Where the contract sits.** The posting anchors on Germany. Ask on the first call whether a Geneva-based hire would be on a German contract (with or without relocation), a Swiss contract through any local Wolters Kluwer entity, or an employer of record. That decides whether the conversation is in EUR against German bands or CHF against Swiss ones. Don't assume a Swiss entity exists; check.

**Bands are real here.** A company this size runs formal grading, and the recruiter will usually know the range for the grade. Ask for it. The biggest lever is getting mapped to the right grade in the first place, since moving within a band is much harder than moving between them.

**Level.** Principal with architecture ownership across a 100-engineer platform org sits above staff. Anchor to principal, not senior or staff bands.

## 2. Market anchors (base, 2026, working estimates)

Re-check against Levels.fyi, Glassdoor, Kununu and recruiter feedback before the call. These are estimates, not data from Wolters Kluwer.

| Market | Staff engineer | Principal engineer | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Germany (large corporate), EUR | 95k to 120k | 115k to 145k | Bonus typically 10% to 20% of base at this level |
| Geneva / Zürich, CHF | 170k to 205k | 195k to 240k | Swiss corporates often add 10% to 20% bonus |
| US remote (for reference), USD | 190k to 230k | 220k to 280k | Not directly relevant, useful if they cite US peers |

A German package paid to someone living in Geneva is a real cut of roughly a third in purchasing power. That's the core argument if they propose EUR bands for a Swiss-based hire.

**Targets**

- Swiss contract (entity or EOR): walk-away CHF 195k base, target CHF 210k to 225k, open at CHF 235k. Plus bonus.
- German contract with relocation: walk-away EUR 120k, target EUR 130k to 140k, open at EUR 150k. Plus bonus and relocation support.
- On top of either: bonus target in writing, LTIP eligibility confirmed, and conference/training budget.

## 3. Opening the conversation

If the recruiter asks for expectations early:

> "Before a number, can I check how the contract would work from Geneva? A Swiss contract and a German one lead to quite different figures. For a principal role owning architecture on the GenAI platform, based in Switzerland, I'm looking at a base around CHF 235k plus bonus. On a German contract with relocation to Hürth, around EUR 150k plus bonus. Could you share the band for this grade? That'd help me be precise."

If they push for a single number:

> "For a Switzerland-based principal, around CHF 235k base. I'm flexible on the split once I understand the bonus and any long-term incentive."

## 4. If they come in low

**Scenario A: German EUR band for a Geneva-based hire (for example EUR 120k).**

> "Thanks, that's useful. The problem is that EUR 120k in Geneva sits below what a senior engineer earns locally, and this is a principal role. If I'm working from Switzerland, the package has to reflect Swiss costs, which puts me at CHF 210k or above. If the band truly can't move for a Swiss hire, I'd consider relocating, but then I'd need relocation support and a base around EUR 140k."

**Scenario B: Swiss contract at the low end (for example CHF 185k).**

> "I appreciate it. Looking at the scope, architecture across a shared agent platform, mentoring, and owning projects that 20+ agents depend on, that's principal work. I built Alaya's platform from zero through to an acquisition and I've spent the last years on LLM and multi-agent work at Benevity. I'd need to be at CHF 210k to make it work. Is there room within the grade, or should we look at whether this maps to the right grade?"

**If base is truly capped:**

> "If base is fixed at that grade, could we look at a sign-on bonus, a higher bonus target, and a written compensation review at twelve months?"

## 5. Equity and long-term incentives

No startup equity here, so the questions are different:

- Is the principal grade eligible for the long-term incentive plan (performance shares or similar)? If so, what's the typical annual grant as a percentage of base, and what's the vesting schedule and performance conditions?
- Is there an employee share purchase plan, and at what discount?
- If LTIP isn't available at this grade, push for either a higher bonus target or a sign-on bonus to compensate.

> "Since there's no startup equity, I'm weighing base plus bonus plus any long-term plan together. Is this grade eligible for the LTIP, and roughly what's a typical grant?"

## 6. Geographic discount pushback

If they suggest a "remote" or "location-adjusted" rate:

> "I understand the logic, but I'm not asking for a Zürich-HQ premium. Geneva is simply where I live, and it's one of the most expensive cities in Europe. My afternoons line up with your Central-time overlap window, and I'm a short flight from Hürth for anything in person. I'd rather be paid for the scope of the role than discounted for where I sit."

## 7. Competing offer template

Use only if true at the time, or leave out entirely.

> "I want to be transparent: I'm in late stages with another company for a staff-level platform role, with a base in the CHF 2XXk range. Wolters Kluwer is my preference because of the UpToDate work and the platform scope, so I'd like to see if we can close the gap."

## 8. Non-salary items worth asking for

- Written confirmation of remote-first from Geneva and expected travel frequency
- Home office and equipment budget
- Conference and training budget (useful for LangGraph, MCP and Rust ramp-up)
- Pension contribution level (German bAV or Swiss 2nd pillar above minimum)
- Holiday entitlement (German contracts often start at 30 days)
- Notice period and non-compete terms

## 9. Walk-away check

Walk away or pause if: the grade maps below principal, a Geneva-based contract comes in under CHF 195k (or the German contract under EUR 120k) with no bonus path, or the remote arrangement from Switzerland isn't confirmed in writing.
