# Negotiation Script: Adaptyv, Head of Software Engineering (Lausanne)

## The shape of this offer

The LinkedIn listing puts the role in Lausanne (Vaud), marks it "Director", full-time, and doesn't list a salary. It's a player-coach role: own the engineering roadmap, set architecture across frontend, backend, data pipelines and lab-hardware integration, ship code, and hire a small team. Adaptyv is a venture-backed techbio startup, so the package will be base plus equity (likely options or a VSOP), with little or no bonus. The levers that matter are base, equity percentage, vesting terms, and title and reporting line.

Three things to settle early:

1. **Funding stage and runway.** The equity number means nothing until you know the last round, the post-money valuation and the runway. **Check Crunchbase, Dealroom, Startupticker.ch and the recruiter before the first comp conversation.** Don't quote a percentage before you know the stage.
2. **Scope of "Head of".** Ask who the role reports to (CEO or CTO), how many engineers there are today, and how many hires are budgeted. A Head of Engineering reporting to the CEO with a hiring budget is a different job, and a different price, from a team lead under a technical cofounder.
3. **Location.** Lausanne is in Vaud, which is top priority in the profile, and about 40 minutes by train from Geneva. Ask how many on-site days a week they expect; a lab company will probably want real presence.

## Market anchors

These are approximations for the Lake Geneva region. **Check every figure against levels.fyi, Glassdoor CH, the swissdevjobs.ch salary data and, ideally, one or two founders or VCs in the Vaud ecosystem (EPFL Innovation Park, Biopôle) before any call.** Lausanne startups tend to pay a little below Zürich and somewhat below Geneva big employers, but the cost of living is close to Geneva's.

| Role / market | Base (CHF) | Bonus | Equity (fully diluted) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Head of Engineering, Seed / Series A startup, Vaud | 170k to 210k | rare | 0.5% to 1.5% | Higher % the earlier the hire |
| Head of Engineering / VP Eng, Series B+, Vaud or Geneva | 200k to 250k | 0 to 15% | 0.2% to 0.8% | |
| Staff / Principal IC, Swiss scale-up | 170k to 220k | 0 to 15% | 0.05% to 0.3% | Reference point for "IC price" |
| Geneva corporate / big employer, Director Eng | 220k to 280k | 15 to 25% | little or none | Useful as the opportunity cost |

The takeaway: for a startup Head of Engineering, base will probably land between CHF 180k and 230k. Most of the upside and most of the room to negotiate sits in equity and its terms, so that's where to spend negotiating capital.

Swiss specifics worth raising: pension (BVG/LPP) contributions above the legal minimum, and whether the equity plan is real shares, options or a VSOP. Tax treatment differs a lot. Real options with a low strike can be taxed at grant or at exercise depending on the plan and the cantonal ruling, while VSOP payouts are taxed as income. Ask whether they have a tax ruling from the canton of Vaud. Since Magino lives in Geneva, Geneva income tax rates apply unless Magino moves.

## Equity terms to check

- **Vesting and cliff.** Four years with a one-year cliff is standard. Ask for monthly vesting after the cliff.
- **Acceleration.** Ask for double-trigger acceleration (change of control plus termination or a material role change). Alaya's exit is the argument here: you've lived through an acquisition and know how roles get reshuffled afterwards.
- **Exercise window.** Standard is 90 days after leaving. Ask for an extended window (five to ten years), which costs the company little.
- **Strike price and valuation.** Get the strike price, the last post-money valuation and the fully diluted share count, so the percentage can be turned into a CHF figure.
- **Dilution and refreshers.** Anti-dilution protection is for investors and won't be granted to an employee. Ask instead about a refresher policy after the next round, and about the size of the option pool and whether it gets topped up before or after your grant.
- **Leaver terms.** Read the good leaver and bad leaver clauses carefully. Some Swiss VSOPs forfeit even vested units if you leave before an exit.

## Scripts

### When they ask for expectations

> "Before I give a number, can you tell me where you are in funding, and how the equity plan works? For a Head of Engineering role in a startup, the split between cash and equity matters more to me than either figure on its own."

If they push:

> "For this scope, with ownership of the roadmap, the hiring and the architecture, I'm thinking around CHF 235k base with equity in the 1.5% range. I'm flexible on how that splits if the equity terms are good."

### If they come in low (for example CHF 180k base and 0.4%)

> "Thanks, I'd like to make this work. The base is at the bottom of what I see for this role on the Arc lémanique, and the equity feels like a senior IC grant rather than a head of engineering one. Could we get the base to around CHF 210k and the grant closer to 1%? If cash is tight, I'm open to taking more of it in equity."

### If cash really is fixed

> "Understood. In that case could we look at the equity terms instead? A larger grant, double-trigger acceleration and a longer exercise window would close most of the gap for me."

### Geographic discount pushback

If they suggest Lausanne pay should be lower because it's not Zürich, or that living in Geneva changes nothing:

> "Lausanne and Geneva rents and costs are within a few percent of each other, and both sit near Zürich on the Swiss cost scale. I'm also comparing this with Geneva offers, so I'd rather we price the role on scope than on postcode."

### Competing offer template (use only if something real exists)

> "I want to be transparent. I have another offer at around CHF [X] base with [Y]. Adaptyv is my first choice because of the problem and the team, and I'd rather not decide on money alone. Is there room to close part of that gap?"

Never invent a competing offer. If nothing concrete exists, talk about the market range instead: "What I'm seeing for comparable roles in Vaud and Geneva is…"

## Things not to give away

- Don't accept a title change (for example "Engineering Lead") without renegotiating equity, since it signals a smaller scope.
- Don't agree to a number before seeing the equity plan documents.
- Ask for the full offer in writing, plan rules included, and take at least two or three days before signing.
