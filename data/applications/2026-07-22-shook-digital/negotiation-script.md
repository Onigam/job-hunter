# Negotiation Script: Shook Digital, Founding Engineer, ShookAI Platform

## Context

Posted range: EUR 70,000 to EUR 82,000 base, plus up to 1.5% equity (stated as roughly EUR 165,000 at current valuation, five-year vest). Role is based in Helsinki or remote within EU timezones, so there's no Geneva cost-of-living adjustment on the table. The Switzerland figures below are a personal anchor for evaluating the offer against staying in the Swiss market, not something to raise with Shook Digital as if they owe a Geneva rate for a Helsinki-based role.

## Switzerland market anchor (for personal reference)

Staff-level or founding engineers in Geneva or Zurich at funded startups typically land around CHF 150,000 to CHF 190,000 base, and that's before Switzerland's comparatively low income tax is factored in. Converted roughly, EUR 82,000 sits well under half of that band. This isn't a case where the base salary alone closes the gap: the offer only becomes competitive with a Swiss baseline if the equity is real, meaningfully sized, and priced at a valuation that has a credible path to liquidity. That's the frame for the whole conversation, not the base number by itself.

## Opening anchor

Open above the posted range and tie the ask to the specific weight of being employee number one on the technical side, not to relocation cost.

Script: "I've seen the range posted, EUR 70K to 82K plus equity. Given this is a founding engineer role where I'd be making architecture calls without legacy constraints and potentially hiring the next few engineers, I'd like to open the conversation at EUR 92,000 base, with the equity structure adjusted to reflect that scope."

## If they come in low or hold at the posted ceiling

Don't get defensive about the number. Ask what it's benchmarked against, since founding engineer compensation varies a lot by how much ownership and hiring authority comes with the title.

Script: "Can I ask what the EUR 82K figure is benchmarked against, market data for Helsinki generally or specifically for a first technical hire with founder-level scope? Those two things usually price pretty differently, and I want to make sure the offer reflects the second one."

If they hold firm on base: ask for a defined comp review at six months tied to specific milestones, for example shipping the first self-serve campaign workflow to production, or ask whether a signing bonus can bridge the gap while the equity vests toward something meaningful.

## Equity negotiation

The posted 1.5% figure needs real numbers behind it before it means anything.

- Ask for the current valuation used to calculate that EUR 165,000 figure, and whether it's post-money or pre-money.
- Confirm the vesting schedule in writing. The listing mentions five years, which is longer than the standard four-year vest with a one-year cliff, worth asking why and whether it can be shortened.
- Ask about acceleration on acquisition, since a founding engineer role at a company already expecting fast growth should have single or double trigger acceleration written into the grant.
- Ask what percentage of the fully diluted cap table 1.5% actually represents today versus after the next funding round, since dilution matters more at this stage than the headline number.

Script: "Before I can weigh the equity properly, could you share the valuation it's calculated against, whether that's pre or post-money, and whether there's any acceleration on a future acquisition? I'd also like to understand the vesting timeline, since five years is longer than what's typical."

## Geographic and remote discount pushback

If there's any suggestion that remote-from-Switzerland pricing should be lower than Helsinki-based pricing, push back once rather than conceding by default.

Script: "I understand the role is anchored to Helsinki, but the scope and the hours I'd be working in EU timezones are the same either way. I'd want to understand the reasoning if the location changes the number, since the work doesn't."

## Competing offer angle

Script, if there's an actual competing process: "I'm further along in a parallel conversation for a similar founding-level scope. I'd rather move forward here if we can land close to what I've outlined, since the product direction is the one I find more interesting."

Script, if no competing offer exists yet: don't invent one. Lean on comparable founding engineer data instead. "Founding and first engineer roles with real hiring scope, not just an IC title, are commanding higher bases than the general market rate for the seniority level, so I'd like the offer to reflect that."

## Close

Once base and equity are aligned, get the vesting schedule, valuation basis, and acceleration terms confirmed in writing before signing, and ask for the six-month comp review to be documented rather than left as a verbal commitment.
