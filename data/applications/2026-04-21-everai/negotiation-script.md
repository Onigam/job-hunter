# Negotiation Script — EverAI, Tech Lead LLM & Generative AI

---

## Context & Market Position

**Contract type:** EverAI prefers B2B. This changes the math significantly: as a B2B contractor in Switzerland, you bear your own health insurance (CHF 400-600+/month), pension, and professional liability. Their stated $100/month health allowance covers roughly 20% of actual Swiss health insurance cost.

**Company stage:** ~$4.4M USD revenue (2025), ~75 people. Well-funded for growth but not a late-stage company with generous comp packages. The role is in the AI companionship space, which has high revenue potential but also regulatory risk.

**Role level:** Tech Lead of a 3-person LLM team, hands-on. This is equivalent to a Staff Engineer / IC lead, not a pure manager. That framing matters for anchoring.

---

## Market Rate Anchors (Switzerland / Geneva)

| Reference Point | Range |
|---|---|
| Staff Engineer, Geneva, large tech (employment) | CHF 160k-200k/year |
| Staff Engineer, Geneva, startup Series A-B (employment) | CHF 130k-170k/year |
| B2B equivalent (add ~25-30% for overhead) | CHF 170k-260k/year |
| LLM/ML specialist premium | +10-15% |
| Tech Lead / IC lead premium | +5-10% |

**Target anchor: CHF 190,000-210,000/year B2B** (roughly €185k-205k or $200k-225k USD).
**Walk-away floor: CHF 150,000/year B2B** (below this, the math doesn't work with Geneva costs).

Note: EverAI posts USD amounts. Ask if the rate is USD or EUR. If USD, factor in CHF/USD exchange rate (currently ~0.90, so $200k USD = ~CHF 180k).

---

## Opening Anchor Script

When they ask about your rate expectations:

> "I'm thinking in the range of CHF 190k-210k per year on a B2B basis. That's calibrated to Geneva market rates for a Staff/Tech Lead position in AI, and it accounts for the full overhead I carry as a contractor here: health insurance alone is CHF 5k-7k per year, no employer pension contribution, and no employment protections. If the number works better in USD, I'm happy to align on that as well."

Do not apologize for the number. State it once, let silence do the work.

---

## If They Come In Low (Below CHF 150k)

> "I appreciate the offer. I want to make this work, but I need to be honest: below CHF 150k on a B2B basis in Geneva, I'm not covering my fixed costs without stress. The health insurance gap alone is material. What I'd ask is: is there room to revisit the base, or is there a way to bridge through equity or an accelerated review at 6 months?"

Then stop talking.

---

## Counter-Offer Script (If Initial Offer is CHF 120k-149k)

**Step 1:** Acknowledge, don't accept.
> "Thank you for putting a number out there. I want to think through it carefully. Can I come back to you tomorrow?"

**Step 2:** Come back with a specific counter.
> "I've given it serious thought. My counter is CHF 165k. Here's my reasoning: Geneva health insurance is CHF 6k/year unsubsidized, I carry all professional liability and no employer pension. The net take-home on CHF 120k B2B is closer to CHF 95k after those deductions, which is well below where I'd set the floor for this scope of work. CHF 165k gets us to something workable."

**Step 3:** If they hold firm, shift to non-cash.
> "If the base is a hard ceiling, can we talk about equity, an accelerated 6-month review, or improving the health allowance? The $100/month health contribution covers about a fifth of actual costs here."

## Equity Negotiation

EverAI does not mention equity in the job posting, which is unusual for a startup at their stage. This is worth surfacing explicitly.

**How to ask:**
> "The posting doesn't mention equity or share options. For a Tech Lead role at a company at this stage, I'd typically expect some form of participation in upside. Is that something EverAI offers, and if so, what does the structure look like?"

**What to look for:**
- BSPCE (France-based company) or stock options (Malta/international)
- Vesting schedule: standard is 4 years with 1-year cliff
- Strike price relative to current 409A/valuation
- Anti-dilution provisions in future rounds

**If they offer options:**
> "Can you share the current strike price and the most recent 409A or preferred share price? I want to understand the spread and what dilution looks like in a future round."

**If they offer nothing:**
> "I understand that's not in the current structure. I'd ask that we revisit it at the 6-month mark if the role goes well. I'm not walking away from the conversation over it, but it's a factor in how I weigh total comp."

## Health Insurance Gap

EverAI offers $100/month health allowance. Swiss mandatory health insurance (LAMal) costs CHF 400-600+/month depending on plan and deductible. The gap is CHF 300-500/month or CHF 3,600-6,000/year.

**How to raise it:**
> "One thing I want to flag: the health allowance in the offer is $100/month. In Switzerland, mandatory health insurance (it's not optional, it's required by law) runs CHF 400-600/month. I'd want to either adjust the base to reflect that gap, or increase the health allowance to CHF 350-400/month."

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

If you're in conversations elsewhere (or even if you're not, this framing is honest):

> "I want to be transparent: I'm in late-stage conversations with two other companies, both closer to my location in Geneva. The EverAI role is genuinely the most interesting of the three technically, which is why I'm investing time here. If you can get to CHF 185k, I can close this quickly and stop the process elsewhere."

Use this only if you're willing to follow through. It's most credible when true.

---

## Timing

- Don't accept on the call. Always ask for 24 hours.
- If they pressure you for an immediate answer, that's a red flag about how decisions get made internally.
- Best time to negotiate is after they've made a verbal offer, before you sign anything.
