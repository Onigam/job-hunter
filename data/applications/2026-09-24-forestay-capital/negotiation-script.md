# Negotiation Script: Lead AI Engineer, Fund & Portfolio, Forestay Capital (Geneva)

## Context
- Forestay is the enterprise AI early-growth fund founded by B-FLEXION, the Bertarelli family's investment firm, and led by Frederic Wohlwend. Offices in Geneva and London.
- Forestay II closed at USD 220M, writing lead tickets of about USD 10-15M in Vertical AI, Cybersecurity and AI Infrastructure across Europe, Israel and the US. Fund I (13 companies) included Nexthink, Scandit, Wasabi and K2View, with three unicorns and two exits.
- The posting asks for 2 to 5 years of experience. That's the main risk: their budget was probably set for a mid-level engineer, while the title says "Lead" and the scope covers a roadmap, production systems, diligence and portfolio advisory. The negotiation is really about which of those two jobs they're paying for.
- This isn't a startup, so there's no equity in the usual sense. The long-term levers at a fund are **carried interest**, **co-investment rights** and **bonus**. Your base will be in CHF on a Swiss contract if you're in Geneva.
- No salary is posted. Get their range before giving yours if you can.

## Your target

- **Walk-away:** CHF 160k base including 13th month, plus a bonus target of at least 10%.
- **Target:** CHF 185k to 200k base, 15% to 20% bonus target, a carry allocation in Forestay II (or the next fund), and co-investment rights.
- **Opening anchor:** CHF 210k base plus carry, justified by the lead scope, 17 years of engineering, and having been through an acquisition from the founder's side.
- If they can't move base, move the conversation to carry and bonus; those cost them less today.

## Script: when they ask for your expectations

"Before I give a number, can you tell me the range you've budgeted? The posting says 2 to 5 years but the role reads as a lead position, so I want to make sure we're pricing the same job."

If they insist:

"For a lead role that owns the roadmap, runs production systems and supports diligence, I'm at around CHF 210k base, plus a bonus and participation in carry. That's in line with lead AI roles in Zurich and senior tech roles at Geneva funds. I'm flexible on how it's split between base and carry, since I'd rather be aligned with how the fund does."

Then stop talking.

## Counter-offer script: if they come in low

"Thanks. I understand how you got there, since the posting was written around 2 to 5 years. What I'd bring is closer to the lead end: I've built a platform from nothing to acquisition, and I've been examined in diligence myself, which is directly useful for the technical side of your deals. At CHF [their number] base I'd need the gap made up elsewhere, a carry allocation and a bonus target of 20%. Is that something you can do?"

If they're at CHF 120k to 140k with no carry:

"That's below what I'd take for a senior IC role in Geneva, and this is a lead role. Could we look at the scope again? If the budget is really set for a mid-level engineer, I'd rather talk about whether a Head of AI title and budget is the better fit, because that's closer to what the posting describes."

## Carry and co-investment: what to ask for

- **Carry points.** Ask directly whether non-partner staff get carry in Forestay II. Tech roles at funds increasingly do. A reasonable ask is a small share of the carry pool (often expressed in points out of 100 of the total carry). Ask how many points the pool has and how many you'd get.
- **Vesting.** Carry usually vests over the investment period (often 4 to 5 years) with a cliff. Ask about acceleration if you're let go without cause.
- **Leaver terms.** Good leaver should keep vested carry. Bad leaver clauses are sometimes written broadly; read them carefully.
- **Which fund.** If Forestay II is already mostly deployed, carry in it is worth less. Ask where they are in the investment period, and whether you'd be in Fund III.
- **Co-investment.** Ask for the right to co-invest alongside the fund on the same terms, often with no fees or carry. It's cheap for them and valuable to you.
- **Tax.** In Switzerland, carry can be taxed as income or as capital gain depending on structure. Get a Swiss tax adviser to check before signing.

## Geography

"I'm in Geneva and would work from the Geneva office. I'm happy to be in London regularly for the team and for portfolio work, and I'd expect travel costs to be covered."

If they suggest a London contract: London pay is usually lower after converting from GBP, and a UK visa or right to work needs to be checked. Stay on a Swiss contract.

## Competing offer template (use only if true)

"I want to be open with you. I have another offer at CHF [X] base with [equity]. Forestay is my first choice because of the mix of building and diligence work, and I'd rather make it work here. Could you get closer to [target]?"

## Other items worth asking for
- Training and conference budget (e.g. NeurIPS or AI Engineer Summit), CHF 5k to 10k a year.
- A defined compute and API budget for the AI stack, so the roadmap isn't blocked by approvals.
- Title: "Lead" at minimum; ask whether "Head of AI" is possible if the scope is confirmed.
- Pension (LPP) contribution above the legal minimum, which Geneva funds often offer.
