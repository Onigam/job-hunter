# Negotiation Script — EverAI, Tech Lead LLM & Generative AI

---

## Context

**Contract type:** EverAI prefers B2B. This changes the math materially: as a B2B contractor in Switzerland, you carry your own health insurance (CHF 400-600+/month under LAMal, mandatory by law), your own pension contributions, and professional liability. Their stated $100/month health allowance covers roughly 20% of actual Swiss health insurance costs. Factor this into every number.

**Company stage:** ~$4.4M USD revenue (2025 data), approximately 40-75 people, $8M seed round. Growing fast but not a late-stage company with deep comp packages. Revenue-to-team-size ratio is healthy; they're not burning cash recklessly.

**Role level:** Tech Lead of a 3-person LLM team, hands-on. This is equivalent to a Staff Engineer / IC lead, not a pure manager. Anchor accordingly.

---

## Market Rate Reference (Switzerland / Geneva)

| Benchmark | Range |
|---|---|
| Staff Engineer, Geneva, large tech (salaried employment) | CHF 160k-200k/year |
| Staff Engineer, Geneva, Series A-B startup (salaried employment) | CHF 130k-170k/year |
| B2B equivalent (add ~25-30% for contractor overhead) | CHF 170k-260k/year |
| LLM/ML specialist premium above general software | +10-15% |
| Tech Lead / IC lead with direct team responsibility | +5-10% |

**Target anchor: CHF 190,000-210,000/year B2B.**
**Walk-away floor: CHF 150,000/year B2B.** Below this, the numbers don't work once you account for fixed costs in Geneva.

Note: EverAI quotes in USD. Always clarify whether the offered rate is USD or EUR, then convert to CHF at current rates (USD/CHF roughly 0.90, so $200k USD ~ CHF 180k).

---

## Opening Anchor

When they ask about rate expectations, lead with a specific number:

> "I'm thinking in the range of CHF 190k-210k per year on a B2B basis. That's calibrated to Geneva market rates for a Staff/Tech Lead position in AI, and it accounts for the overhead I carry as a contractor: health insurance alone runs CHF 5k-7k per year, there's no employer pension contribution, and no employment protections. If it's easier to work in USD, I'm happy to align on that."

State the number once. Don't soften it or pre-apologize. Then stop talking.

---

## If They Come In Low (Below CHF 150k / ~$165k USD)

> "I appreciate the offer. I want to make this work, but I should be honest: below CHF 150k B2B in Geneva, I'm not covering my fixed costs without real stress. The health insurance gap alone is material. Is there room to revisit the base, or could we bridge through an accelerated review at six months or equity participation?"

Then stop talking. Let them respond.

---

## Counter-Offer Script (If Initial Offer Is CHF 120k-149k)

**Step 1:** Don't react immediately.
> "Thank you for putting a number out there. I want to think through it carefully. Can I come back to you tomorrow?"

**Step 2:** Return with a specific counter and the reasoning.
> "I've given it serious thought. My counter is CHF 165k. The reasoning: LAMal health insurance is CHF 6k/year unsubsidized in Geneva, I carry full professional liability with no employer pension. The net take-home on CHF 120k B2B is closer to CHF 95k after those deductions, which is below where I'd set the floor for this scope. CHF 165k makes the numbers work."

**Step 3:** If they hold firm on the base, shift to non-cash terms.
> "If the base is a hard ceiling, can we talk about equity, a structured 6-month review with a defined raise, or increasing the health allowance? The $100/month contribution covers about a fifth of actual costs here."

---

## Equity Conversation

The job posting doesn't mention equity, which is worth raising directly. For a Tech Lead at a company this stage, some form of upside participation is reasonable to ask about.

**How to raise it:**
> "The posting doesn't mention equity or options. For a Tech Lead role at a company at this growth stage, I'd typically expect some form of upside. Is that something EverAI offers, and if so, what does the structure look like?"

**What to evaluate if they offer options:**
- BSPCE (if France-registered) or standard stock options (if Malta/international)
- Vesting: 4 years with 1-year cliff is standard
- Strike price relative to most recent 409A or preferred share valuation
- Anti-dilution protection in future rounds

**If they offer options, ask:**
> "Can you share the current strike price and the most recent preferred share price? I want to understand the spread and what dilution looks like in a future funding round."

**If they offer nothing:**
> "I understand that's not the current structure. I'd ask that we revisit it at the 6-month mark if the role goes well. It's a factor in how I weigh total comp, but it's not a dealbreaker for the right base."

---

## Health Insurance Gap

Swiss LAMal health insurance is mandatory and non-negotiable. EverAI's $100/month allowance covers roughly one-fifth of actual cost.

**How to raise it:**
> "One thing worth flagging: the health allowance is $100/month. In Switzerland, mandatory health insurance runs CHF 400-600/month depending on plan and deductible. I'd want to either adjust the base rate to reflect that gap, or increase the health allowance to CHF 350-400/month. It's not a small number on an annual basis."

---

## Competing Offer Framing

If you're in conversations elsewhere (use only if true):

> "I want to be transparent: I'm in late conversations with two other companies, both closer to Geneva. The EverAI role is the most technically interesting of the three, which is why I'm investing time here. If you can get to CHF 185k, I can move quickly and close the other processes."

---

## Process Notes

- Never accept on the call. Always ask for 24 hours to review.
- If they pressure for an immediate answer, that's a signal about how decisions get made internally.
- Negotiate before signing, not after.
- Get any verbal offer confirmed in writing before stopping other processes.
