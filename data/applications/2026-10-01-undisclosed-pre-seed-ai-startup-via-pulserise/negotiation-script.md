# Negotiation Script: Founding Engineer (AI Agents), pre-seed startup via PulseRise

## Context that shapes the numbers

The role is in San Francisco, on site six days a week, at a pre-seed company led by a second-time founder with a prior exit. They've closed an angel round from European and US investors, they're hiring three engineers in total, and the posting says equity is 1 to 3%, with "co-founder-level equity possible for exceptional candidates". Base salary isn't disclosed. They offer relocation support, including O-1 sponsorship.

So the comparison isn't Geneva against Geneva. You'd be swapping a stable Swiss Senior Staff package for US pre-seed cash plus a real equity stake, and you'd be moving countries to do it. The Swiss numbers are your opportunity cost and your walk-away reference; the SF numbers are what the founder will benchmark against.

## Market anchors (estimates, verify before the call)

These are rough ranges from public salary data and recruiter reports, not hard figures. Check levels.fyi, Carta's startup compensation reports, Pave and Glassdoor CH before the first conversation.

| Benchmark | Base salary (approx.) | Equity (approx.) |
|---|---|---|
| Senior Staff Engineer, Geneva (your current reference point) | CHF 200k to 250k | small or none |
| Staff / Principal, Swiss scale-up, Zurich or Geneva | CHF 180k to 240k | 0.05 to 0.3% |
| Founding engineer (first 1-3), SF pre-seed, senior profile | USD 160k to 210k | 1 to 3% |
| Founding engineer with CTO-level scope, SF pre-seed | USD 180k to 230k | 2 to 5%+ |
| Staff engineer, established SF company (for comparison) | USD 230k to 300k | liquid RSUs |

**Target:** USD 200k to 220k base, 2.5 to 3% equity, plus full relocation and O-1 costs covered.
**Opening anchor:** USD 220k base and 3% equity, framed as a CTO-calibre seat (their wording).
**Walk-away floor:** about USD 175k base with at least 2% equity, or a lower base only if equity moves clearly toward co-founder level. Below that, staying in Geneva is the better deal.

## Opening anchor script

"You've described this as a CTO-calibre seat, and that's how I'm thinking about it too. I'm giving up a Senior Staff role in Geneva and moving my life to San Francisco, so the package needs to work on both sides. I'm looking at around 220k base and 3% equity, plus relocation and the O-1 costs. I've taken a company from a blank page to an acquisition as a cofounder, and that's the experience I'd be bringing to the first year here. How does that sit with what you had in mind?"

Then stop talking.

## If they come in low

**Script A, base below range:**
"I understand cash is tight at pre-seed, and I'm not trying to drain the round. But San Francisco on 160k, after leaving a Swiss salary, is a real step down for a six-day week. I'd need the base closer to 200k. If that's a hard ceiling, I'm open to moving some of the gap into equity, but then I'd want to talk about the 3% end of the range, or above it."

**Script B, equity at the low end (around 1%):**
"One percent is what I'd expect for engineer number five or six. I'd be one of the first three, shaping architecture and hiring, and you've said yourself the role is CTO-calibre. For that scope I'm looking at 2.5 to 3%. If the base were to move up, I could be flexible on where in that range we land."

**Script C, they say the numbers are fixed:**
"Fair enough. Then let's look at the terms rather than the headline. Could we agree a base review once the next round closes, and talk about how the vesting and exercise terms are set up? Those matter to me nearly as much as the percentage."

## Equity terms to ask about

At pre-seed, the terms can matter as much as the percentage. Ask about each of these, and get the answers in writing before signing.

**Vesting and cliff.** Four years with a one-year cliff is standard. Ask whether the cliff could be shorter (six months) given the relocation risk you're taking, or whether some vesting could credit the start date if you begin working before the move.

**Instrument and exercise.** Ask whether it's options (ISO or NSO) or restricted stock. At this stage, restricted stock or early-exercisable options with an 83(b) election can save a lot of tax later. Get US tax advice on how this interacts with your Swiss tax position in the year you move.

**Post-termination exercise window.** The default 90 days is harsh. Ask for five to ten years; many early-stage companies now agree to it.

**Acceleration.** Ask for double-trigger acceleration (change of control plus termination). A founder with a prior exit will understand why you'd ask.

**Dilution.** Classic anti-dilution protection belongs to preferred investors, and you won't get it on common stock. What you can ask is the current fully diluted share count, the size of the option pool, and the founder's expectation for dilution at seed and Series A. That way 3% is a number you actually understand.

**Co-founder-level equity.** The posting leaves this door open. If the conversations go well and your scope is effectively CTO, raise it directly: "If the role ends up being CTO in all but name, I'd want the equity and the title to reflect that, at least by the seed round."

## Geography, relocation and the Geneva question

This turns the usual geographic discount argument around. They may assume a European candidate will accept less. Your answer is that you'd be paying SF costs and giving up a Swiss package to do it.

"I'm not asking to be paid Geneva rates in San Francisco. But the move has a real cost for me: Swiss pension contributions I'd stop building, a higher-cost city, and the risk of moving on a visa tied to one employer. I'd like the relocation package to reflect that."

What to ask for in the relocation package:
- All O-1 legal and filing fees paid by the company, including premium processing.
- A clear plan if the O-1 is delayed, for example a remote start from Geneva on a contractor or EOR arrangement, with vesting starting from that date.
- A lump-sum relocation allowance (roughly USD 15k to 30k is typical for international moves) or covered temporary housing for the first one to two months.
- One trip home a year in the first two years, if family stays in Europe for a period.

## Competing offer template

Use only if it's true, or adapt to describe the alternative honestly (staying in your current role is a competing offer too).

"I want to be transparent with you. I'm also talking to [a Swiss company / a remote AI team] about a [Staff / Principal] role at around [CHF X] base. I'd rather build something from zero with you, which is why I'm still here, but the gap is real. If we can get to [USD target] and [X]% equity, I'm ready to commit and start the visa process."

If staying put is the real alternative:

"My honest alternative is staying in Geneva, which is a comfortable position. For me to uproot that and commit to six days a week in SF, the equity has to be meaningful enough to make the risk worth it."

## Final checks before signing

- Base, equity percentage and fully diluted share count, all in writing.
- Vesting schedule, cliff, exercise window and acceleration terms.
- O-1 sponsorship commitment and what happens if it's denied or delayed.
- Relocation amount, and any clawback if you leave within the first year.
- Title, and whether CTO is on the table later.
- Health insurance and benefits from day one, since you'll arrive without US coverage.
