# Negotiation Script: Glass Health, Founding Software Engineer (Backend, Remote)

## Context
- Posted range: $120K-$210K/year; hybrid SF band specifically $180K-$210K, remote likely location-adjusted downward from that
- Equity: 0.5%-1.0% stated in the posting
- Stage: seed, $6.5M raised (incl. $5M seed) from YC and Initialized Capital, Kaiser Permanente Mid Atlantic as a partner, targeting Series A
- Leverage: 17+ years experience, founding engineer with an actual exit (Alaya to Benevity, $1B+ acquirer), backend/platform ownership including on-call at a much larger scale than Glass Health currently runs at, Python already in the stack
- Counter-leverage they'll have: seed-stage cash discipline, small team, likely limited room on cash comp until Series A closes, backend role priced slightly below the full-stack/product postings on the same board

## Switzerland market anchor
Geneva-based Staff/Principal-level backend engineers with founding or exit experience typically see CHF 150,000-190,000+ base in the local market, and Geneva consistently ranks among the most expensive cities globally for cost of living. Converted at a rough 0.90-1.00 USD/CHF rate, that's roughly $165,000-$210,000, which sits at or above the top of Glass Health's posted band. That number is the anchor, not the ask: it justifies sitting at the top of their range even though the role would be paid on a US-calibrated remote band.

## Opening anchor
"Thanks for sending the range. Given the backend ownership here, on-call included, and that I've carried that kind of responsibility at meaningfully larger scale at Benevity, plus the founding-engineer exit at Alaya, I'd want to talk about the top of your posted band, around $200K-$210K base, with equity toward the top of your 0.5%-1.0% range. I know seed-stage cash is tight, so I'm open to structuring the gap between cash and equity if that helps on your end."

## If they come in low (say $150K-$160K)
"I hear you on where the budget sits post-seed. A couple of options that might work: we true up base as part of the Series A comp review with a written commitment to revisit, or we shift more of the difference into equity now, while the strike price is still low. What I'd rather not do is land meaningfully under band for a role that owns production reliability and security for a live healthcare platform, that's closer to a technical co-founder scope than a typical senior backend hire."

## Equity negotiation
- Confirm where in the stated 0.5%-1.0% range the offer actually sits, and ask what the fully diluted share count is so the percentage means something concrete.
- Confirm standard 4-year vesting with a 1-year cliff, and ask whether there's a double-trigger acceleration clause given the stated Series A trajectory.
- Push for a 10-year post-termination exercise window instead of the standard 90 days. This is increasingly common and costs the company nothing at signing.
- Ask whether there's a refresh grant tied to the Series A close, since the option pool will get diluted at that raise and early hires often get left out of the conversation unless they ask upfront.

## Geographic discount pushback
If they try to peg comp lower because Geneva "isn't a US tech hub": "Geneva is consistently one of the most expensive cities in the world to live in, ahead of most US metros including the Bay Area on several cost-of-living indices. I'm not asking for a US-hub premium, I'm asking that the offer reflect where I actually live and what a comparable local role pays, which is the same top-of-band number we already discussed."

## Competing offer leverage template
Use only if genuinely in play with another process: "I want to be transparent that I'm further along with another company at a similar stage, and their initial number is close to what I've proposed here. I'd rather join Glass Health if we can get aligned, the backend problem here is the one I'd actually enjoy owning, but I do need to move on this within the next week or two."

## Close
"I want this to work, on-call and all. Let's get the base and equity numbers aligned this week so I can give you a real answer instead of a maybe."
