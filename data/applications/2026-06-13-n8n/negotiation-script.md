# Negotiation Script — n8n Senior AI Engineer

## Market Context

n8n is Berlin-headquartered, Series B ($60M raised, ~$2.5B valuation post-2025), remote-first across Europe. Compensation is likely EUR-denominated, potentially with the option to receive in CHF if you negotiate for it.

**Market data for Senior AI Engineer, Remote Europe, 5+ years:**
- Median Berlin/Germany rate: €90,000-110,000 base
- Senior AI specialist premium (LLM production experience): +15-25%
- Geneva cost of living index vs. Berlin: ~1.35x
- Target range for this profile: €120,000-145,000 base + equity

For a Geneva-based remote hire, the geographic parity argument is strong: Swiss income taxes, Swiss healthcare costs, Swiss rent, all on a EUR salary. Make this explicit.

---

## Opening Anchor

When they ask for your salary expectation, do not give a range. Give a number at the high end and justify it:

> "Based on my level and the market for engineers with production LLM integration experience in Switzerland, I'm looking at €140,000 base. I'm aware you're EUR-denominated and I'm in Geneva, so I factor in the cost of living differential. I'm also interested in understanding the equity component before settling on an exact number."

If they push for a range: "I'd consider anywhere from €130k to €145k depending on the equity structure, but I'd rather see the full picture first."

---

## Counter-Offer Script (if they come in below €120k)

> "I appreciate the offer and I want to find a way to make this work. The role is genuinely interesting to me. That said, €[their number] is below what I need to make the move work practically from Geneva, where my cost base is significantly higher than in most European cities. If we can get to €130k, I'm ready to sign. Alternatively, if the base is fixed, what does the equity package look like, and is there flexibility there?"

If they say the band is fixed:
> "Understood. What's the review cadence, and is there a structured merit increase path? If the base starts lower, I want to know how quickly engineers at my level have moved within the band."

---

## Equity Notes

n8n offers equity to all employees (confirmed in job postings). As a Series B company, the equity package matters.

**What to ask:**
- Number of options and the current strike price
- Total shares outstanding (to calculate dilution)
- Vesting schedule: standard is 4 years with a 1-year cliff
- Whether there's acceleration on change of control (single-trigger vs. double-trigger)
- The most recent 409A or preferred share price (to estimate current value)

**Script for equity conversation:**
> "I want to understand the equity piece properly. Can you share the number of options, the strike price, and the last 409A valuation? I also want to know the vesting schedule and whether there's any acceleration clause."

If the equity feels thin relative to the base:
> "The options are interesting but hard to value without knowing the share count. If the equity is in the lower range, that puts more weight on the base. If the equity is meaningful, I have more flexibility on base."

---

## Geographic Premium Pushback

If they try to apply a geographic discount (i.e., pay you less because you're not in Berlin):

> "Remote-first means I'm contributing the same output regardless of where I sit. My cost base in Geneva is higher than in Berlin, not lower. I'd actually expect a parity argument to go the other way. I'd rather we anchor to output and level rather than my zip code."

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

If you have other active conversations (or want to create urgency):

> "I have a couple of other conversations moving forward, one of which is also a Senior AI Engineer role at a European scale-up. I have a real preference for n8n based on the product and the AI architecture work specifically, but I need the numbers to be in range. If you can move to €135k, I can give you a decision by end of week."

If you don't have a competing offer, don't manufacture one. Instead use time pressure:
> "I'm trying to make a decision in the next 10-14 days. If we can align on the number this week, that helps me close out my other considerations."

---

## Benefits to Confirm

- €1,000/year learning budget (confirmed in job postings)
- 30 days PTO (Europe)
- Unlimited AI tool budget (confirm whether this includes API credits or just subscriptions)
- Health insurance contribution (especially relevant for Swiss residents)
- Equipment stipend

---

## Summary Target

| Component | Target | Floor |
|-----------|--------|-------|
| Base salary | €140,000 | €125,000 |
| Equity | Meaningful options with 4yr/1yr vesting | Anything with a reasonable path to value |
| PTO | 30 days | 25 days |
| Learning budget | €1,000+ | Confirmed |

Walk away if base is below €115,000 with no equity flexibility and no structured merit review path.
