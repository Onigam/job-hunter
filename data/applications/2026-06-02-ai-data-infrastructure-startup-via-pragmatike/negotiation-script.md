# Negotiation Script — AI Data Infrastructure Startup (via Pragmatike)

Founding Full Stack AI Engineer

---

## Market Context

**Switzerland (Geneva) — Senior Staff / Founding Engineer, 2026:**
- Base salary range for Senior Staff / founding IC: CHF 130,000-160,000
- Geneva premium: ~15-20% above Zurich or Basel for equivalent roles, driven by cost of living
- Remote international startup: may anchor to EU/US rates (EUR 90,000-120,000 / USD 130,000-160,000) — counter with Geneva reality
- Magino's current level at Benevity (Senior Staff, post-acquisition) is the floor, not the target

**Founding engineer premium:**
- First or one-of-three engineers is a founding team position, not a mid-level hire
- Warrants a premium over standard IC comp: 15-20% above equivalent non-founding role
- Equity should compensate for any base discount vs. a scaled company

---

## Opening Anchor

If asked salary expectations before an offer:

> "Given the founding engineer scope and Geneva-based cost structure, I'm looking at CHF 145,000-155,000 base. On the equity side, for a founding position at this stage, I'd expect something in the 0.5-0.75% range with a standard 4-year vest and 1-year cliff. Happy to look at total package flexibility — if one side moves, I can work with that."

If they're paying in EUR or USD (remote international):

> "I'm based in Geneva, so I'm anchored to Swiss cost of living — that translates to roughly CHF 145,000. In EUR terms, that's around EUR 150,000-160,000 at current rates. If that's out of your range, let's talk equity and see if total comp can work."

---

## When They Come In Low

If the offer is CHF 115,000-125,000 (or equivalent):

> "Thanks for putting the offer together. The role itself is genuinely compelling, and I've been taking it seriously. That said, CHF [X] is below what I was expecting given the founding engineer scope and the Geneva market for senior engineers with this background. I was looking at something closer to CHF 145,000. Can we get there on base, or is there flexibility on equity to bridge the gap?"

If they say base is fixed:

> "Got it. If base is capped at CHF [X], I'd want to make sure the equity reflects the founding engineer contribution. At this stage, 0.75% on a 4-year vest with acceleration on acquisition would get me comfortable. Is that workable?"

---

## Equity Negotiation

**Before accepting any number, ask:**

1. What's the current valuation and when was the last round?
2. How many fully diluted shares are outstanding?
3. What's the exercise price on the options?
4. What vesting schedule and cliff are you proposing?
5. Is there acceleration on change of control (single-trigger or double-trigger)?

**What to ask for:**
- 0.5-0.75% for founding engineer, first or near-first hire
- 4-year vest, 1-year cliff, monthly vesting after
- Double-trigger acceleration on acquisition (avoids the "acquihire and your options get reset" trap)
- If pre-Series A: ask for early exercise rights (83b election, reduces tax exposure on appreciation)

**If the equity number seems low:**

> "The percentage you're offering is below what I'd expect for a founding engineer position. Early employees at this stage typically see 0.5% and above, precisely because the role carries the technical risk of building the foundation the rest of the team inherits. I'd like to get to 0.75% — is that something we can discuss?"

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

If you have another offer (or want to frame urgency without fabricating):

> "I'm in late-stage conversations with one other company at a higher base. I'm genuinely more interested in this role because of the founding engineer ownership, but I need the package to be in the right range to make the decision easy. Can you move on [base/equity]?"

If no competing offer but you want to set urgency:

> "I've been actively interviewing and I'm getting to decision points on a couple of processes in the next 2-3 weeks. I'd like to get to yes on this one if we can align on comp — what's the fastest path to getting the paperwork moving if we agree?"

---

## Geneva Cost of Living Pushback (if they say "you're remote, we use EU rates")

> "Understood, but my fixed costs are Geneva costs: housing, taxes, healthcare all run at Swiss rates. A EUR 100,000 package that works fine for someone in Berlin or Lisbon doesn't have the same purchasing power here. I'd need the package to reflect that, either in base or with a meaningful equity position that compensates over time."

---

## Anti-Patterns to Avoid

- Don't name a single number first. Give a range with a floor.
- Don't accept verbally without seeing it in writing.
- Don't let them anchor on your current salary; anchor on market rate for the new role.
- Don't agree to a vesting schedule without asking about acceleration on exit.
- Don't negotiate equity percentage without knowing the fully diluted share count.
