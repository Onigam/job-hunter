# Negotiation Script: Speechify, Tech Lead (Web Core Product & Chrome Extension)

## Context

Posted range across job boards: $140,000 to $200,000 USD base, plus bonus and stock options. Speechify is a ~200-person fully distributed company, no office, and the range is posted against several US city listings (Milwaukee, Aurora, Carrollton), which suggests the number may be calibrated to a US cost-of-living band rather than a single fixed figure. That's worth confirming directly rather than assuming either way. There's no Switzerland presence, so the Swiss comparison below is a personal anchor for evaluating the offer, not something to raise with Speechify as if they owe a Geneva rate.

## Switzerland market anchor (for personal reference)

Tech lead and staff-level frontend or product engineers in Geneva and Zurich typically land in the CHF 160,000 to CHF 200,000 total comp range at established tech companies, with lower income tax than most US states. A $200,000 USD base with no state withholding, converted at typical rates, sits close to or above the top of that local band before bonus or equity are added. That means the top of Speechify's posted range is a reasonable outcome relative to staying local, and the case for pushing toward that top end rests on scope and market comparison, not on need.

## Opening anchor

Don't open in the middle of the range. Open near the top and justify it with the scope of the role, not personal circumstances.

Script: "I've seen the range posted for this role, $140K to $200K plus bonus and stock. Given the scope here, tech lead on a product surface serving over 50 million users, with a Chrome extension that's currently the industry benchmark, that reads like the top of the band to me rather than the middle. I'd like to talk about landing at $200K base, plus the standard bonus and equity structure for this level."

## If they come in low (below $170K)

Don't get defensive. Ask what the number is anchored to, then redirect to scope and comparable market data.

Script: "Thanks for sending that over. Can I ask what level or geography that number is benchmarked against? Looking at the responsibilities, owning a widely used web surface and a Chrome extension with real accessibility stakes, that reads closer to the top of your posted band. I'd like to understand if there's room to move before we get into equity."

If they hold firm on base: shift the conversation to a defined comp review at six months tied to concrete deliverables, for example shipping a specific extension milestone, or ask about a sign-on component to bridge the gap.

## Equity negotiation

Speechify is a private, venture-backed company, so the standard checklist applies:

- Ask for the strike price and the most recent 409A valuation date, not just a share count.
- Confirm the vesting schedule. Standard is four years with a one-year cliff; push back if theirs is longer.
- Ask about early exercise eligibility and whether the post-termination exercise window extends past the standard 90 days.
- Ask how refresh grants work after year one, since a tech lead offer should include a refresh conversation, not just an initial grant.
- If the equity number feels light against base, ask what percentage of the company the grant represents rather than accepting a raw share count, since share counts alone don't mean anything without knowing the cap table.

Script: "Could you share the strike price, the vesting schedule, and whether there's an extended post-termination exercise window? I'd also like to know what percentage of the company the grant represents, so I can evaluate it properly."

## Geographic discount pushback

If a recruiter suggests a lower number specifically because the role would be based in Switzerland rather than the US, don't concede without pushing back once.

Script: "I understand there may be a geographic component here, but the responsibilities and impact are the same regardless of where I'm sitting. I'd want to understand the reasoning behind any adjustment before assuming it applies to this role."

## Competing offer angle (use only if real, or reference market data if not)

Script, if there's an actual competing process: "I'm further along in a parallel process for a comparable scope with a similar base. I'd rather move forward here if we can land close to what I've outlined, since the product and the team are the better fit."

Script, if no competing offer exists yet: don't invent one. Lean on the posted range and comparable roles instead. "Tech lead roles with this kind of user-facing scope and platform ownership are commanding the top of posted ranges right now, so I'd like this to be priced at that level."

## Close

Once base and equity are aligned, confirm start date flexibility and ask for the six-month comp review to be documented in writing, tied to specific deliverables rather than left as an informal promise.
