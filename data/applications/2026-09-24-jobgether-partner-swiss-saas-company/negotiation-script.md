# Negotiation Script: Chief Technology Officer, Swiss SaaS (via Jobgether)

## Context
- Hands-on CTO for a Swiss SaaS company, reporting to the CEO and visible to the board. You'd own the R&D budget, infrastructure (multi-region Kubernetes), security and compliance, and the shift to a product-focused engineering team. The team is small and senior.
- 100% remote within supported European locations. The posting says **"location-adjusted compensation"**, so expect them to apply a location formula. Geneva is one of the most expensive cities in Europe, and the company is Swiss, so there's no case for a discount.
- The posting gives **no salary and no equity**, and doesn't say what stage the company is at. Before you name any number, find out: funding stage, ARR range, headcount, whether the CTO seat is new or a replacement, and whether there's an equity plan.
- Jobgether is a matching platform. Once you're through to the company, negotiate directly with the CEO.

## Your target
- **Walk-away:** CHF 210k base plus a real bonus or equity. Below that, the Staff-level market in Geneva pays about as much, with less risk.
- **Target:** CHF 240k to 260k base, 15 to 20% target bonus, 0.75% to 1.5% equity (more if early-stage).
- **Opening anchor:** CHF 275k base, 20% bonus, 1.5% equity.

## Script: when they ask for your expectations

"Before giving you a number, it'd help to understand the stage and the package structure. Is there an equity plan, and how does the bonus work for the leadership team?"

Once you have that:
"For a hands-on CTO seat at a Swiss SaaS company, with the budget, the infrastructure and compliance on my plate, I'm looking at around CHF 275k base with a 20% bonus, plus equity that's meaningful for the executive team. I've built a company's platform from zero through to an acquisition, so I'd rather be paid mostly for results. If you'd like more of the package in bonus or equity, I'm open to that."

If they push you to go first before explaining the package:
"I'd rather not negotiate against myself. What range did you budget for this role?"

## Script: pushing back on location adjustment

"I understand why you adjust for location. But I'm in Geneva, which is at the top of any European cost index, and you're a Swiss company, so there's nothing to adjust down. If your formula benchmarks against Zurich, fine; Geneva is close enough that I'd use the same number."

If the formula is built on a European average:
"That formula works for a senior engineer in Lisbon. For a CTO who's also in Switzerland, it prices me below local Staff-level engineers. Can we anchor on the Swiss market for this role instead?"

## Script: counter-offer if they come in low

If they offer, for example, CHF 210k, 10% bonus and 0.3%:

"Thanks, and I'm serious about wanting this to work. Right now it's priced closer to a Head of Engineering in Geneva, but the role includes the R&D budget, the infrastructure bill, compliance and time with the board. I'd suggest we meet in the middle on base at CHF 245k, move the bonus to 20% with objectives we write together (delivery speed, cloud cost per customer, certification milestone), and bring equity closer to 1%. If cash is the constraint, I'm happy to put more weight on the bonus and equity."

If they say base is fixed:
"Understood. Then let's close the gap elsewhere: a sign-on bonus, more equity, and a written salary review after 12 months or at the next funding round, whichever comes first."

## Equity (if there's a plan)
Ask for the numbers, not just a percentage:
- **Fully diluted share count and last valuation**, so you can work out what the grant is worth. The strike price matters for options.
- **Instrument:** options, restricted shares, or a phantom/virtual plan (common in Switzerland). Phantom plans pay out in cash on an exit only, so check the payout rules and taxation.
- **Vesting:** 4 years with a 1-year cliff is standard. Ask for monthly vesting after the cliff.
- **Acceleration:** you've been through an acquisition, so you know what happens to leadership on a change of control. Ask for **double-trigger acceleration** (at least 50 to 100% of unvested shares vest if you're let go or your role is reduced within 12 months of an acquisition).
- **Exercise window:** ask for 5 to 10 years after leaving instead of 90 days.
- **Liquidation preferences:** ask what preference stack sits above common shares. Beyond 1x non-participating, your equity is worth less than it looks.
- **Swiss tax:** get a tax ruling or at least advice on when the grant becomes taxable. Swiss rules on employee equity can tax you at grant or at vesting, depending on the structure.

If the company is PE-backed or later-stage, ask about the **LTIP or management equity plan** and whether there's a sweet equity pool for the leadership team.

## Executive terms to negotiate (often worth more than the last 10k in base)
- **Notice period:** 6 months both ways (standard is 3 for executives in Switzerland).
- **Severance:** 6 months base if terminated without cause, beyond the notice period.
- **D&O insurance:** confirm you're covered as an officer.
- **Employment entity:** a Swiss employment contract (Swiss social security and pension) rather than a contractor setup or foreign payroll. Ask what the employer contributes to the **2nd pillar (LPP)**; above-minimum plans for executives are worth a lot.
- **Title and reporting line in writing:** CTO, member of the executive team, reporting to the CEO, with a regular slot at board meetings.
- **Hands-on time protected:** agree how much time goes to hands-on work versus management, so the role doesn't turn into pure admin after six months.
- **Budget:** learning budget, travel for in-person team meetings, conferences.

## Template: competing offer

Only use this with a real process, and don't invent numbers.

"I'll be transparent: I'm also in late-stage conversations for a senior role at another company, with a package around [X]. This role is my first choice because of [specific reason: the product/infrastructure challenge, the scope, the team]. I'm not trying to start a bidding war, but I'd like to be able to say yes without regret. Is there room to move on [base / equity / bonus]?"

If you have no competing offer, fall back on market data instead:
"I've been looking at the market for CTO roles at Swiss SaaS companies, and the range I'm seeing is CHF 230k to 300k base. I'd like us to land in the middle of that."

## Closing the deal
- Get everything in writing: base, bonus mechanics and targets, equity grant with all its terms, notice, severance, start date.
- Ask for the equity plan documents before signing, not after.
- Final line when you're happy: "That works for me. Send the written offer and I'll review it within 48 hours."
