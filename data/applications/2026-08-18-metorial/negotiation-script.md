# Negotiation Script: Founding Engineer, Metorial

Context that shapes the math: Metorial is a YC F25 company, four people, posting a wide $100K-$180K salary band with 1.00%-2.00% equity for a remote founding engineer. A band that wide almost always means they haven't settled on a number and will follow the candidate's anchor more than a typical Series A would. The team is Vienna-anchored but the role is remote with no visa requirement, so there's no hard geographic tie to negotiate around, only the general question of whether they're benchmarking against US or European pay.

## Market anchor

- Founding engineer at a four-person YC seed-stage company, remote, EU-based: realistic range is CHF 130,000-165,000 base equivalent, with the top of that band justified by 17+ years of experience and a founder track record through to acquisition rather than a first-time founding engineer.
- The posted $100K-$180K range converts to roughly CHF 88,000-160,000 at current rates, so anchoring near the top of their stated band, or slightly above it, is not an aggressive ask, it's asking to be priced at the level the range was clearly built to accommodate.
- Seed-stage cash is usually the constrained side of the equation, so equity and vesting terms matter more here than at a later-stage company, worth spending real negotiation time on the cap table math rather than just the base number.
- Opening anchor: $170,000 base, or CHF equivalent if paid in francs, plus equity toward the top of the posted 2% band, framed around distributed-systems depth and a founder history rather than a generic senior engineer rate.

## Opening anchor script

"Given the scope here, distributed systems, DevOps, and being one of the first five engineers, I'd expect a base around $165,000 to $180,000, plus equity toward the higher end of the range you've posted. I know early-stage comp is a negotiation between cash and ownership, so I'm flexible on the split as long as the total reflects that scope. Where does this role currently land within your posted band, and how much of the option pool is still unallocated?"

## If they come in low

"I hear the number. Is that priced against a general backend engineer, or does it account for the founding scope, owning infrastructure decisions with real equity stakes attached? That's closer to what I did at Alaya from the first year than a typical IC hire, and I'd want the offer to reflect that."

If they hold the line on cash: "I can work with a lower base if the equity grant moves toward 2% and vesting starts from an early date rather than from acceptance. What I wouldn't want is to land below $140,000 base with equity also at the bottom of the range, that combination undervalues the risk on both sides of the ledger."

## Equity notes (seed-stage specifics)

- Ask for the current fully diluted share count and where the option pool sits post the F25 round, a 1-2% grant means very different things depending on how diluted the cap table already is.
- Confirm standard 4-year vesting with a 1-year cliff, and ask specifically whether early exercise and an 83(b)-equivalent or its Austrian/EU analogue is available, this matters more at a four-person company than at a Series A.
- Ask about the strike price basis and how recently the 409A or local equivalent valuation was set, since a seed company's valuation can move fast between fundraise events.
- Ask what happens to the grant on a future round, whether there's a refresh policy or whether early joiners are expected to just accept dilution as the price of being first.
- Get clarity on double-trigger acceleration in an acquisition scenario, worth asking for explicitly given the acquisition precedent at Alaya made this a real, not hypothetical, question for me.

## Remote and geographic framing

"I'm Geneva-based and the role is listed as remote with no visa requirement, so I'd assume the comp isn't discounted for location the way an on-site Vienna hire might be. If there's a geographic band I should know about, I'd rather hear it now than after we've agreed on scope."

## Competing offer, if genuinely in play

"I'm in later-stage conversations at comparable scope on the platform and infrastructure side. What makes this one stand out is the MCP infrastructure problem specifically, but I want to be upfront about timeline so we can move at a pace that works for both of us."

## Close

A posted range this wide is a signal that they're still calibrating, not a ceiling, use the top of it as the floor of the conversation rather than the target. Get base, the equity percentage and vesting start date, the strike price basis, and acceleration terms in writing before accepting, seed-stage equity grants are the part most likely to be handled informally and the part most likely to matter five years from now if this goes the way Alaya did.
