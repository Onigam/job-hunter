# Negotiation Script, Zauber Technology GmbH, Founding Engineer

## Context to anchor on

Zauber is a Berlin GmbH, seed/early Series A stage, backed by P9 (top-tier European early-stage fund). The founder previously scaled Forto to $600M+ raised, which is a strong signal on fundraising ability but not on this round's actual size, so don't assume Zauber itself is well-capitalized just because the founder's last company was. Confirm round size and runway before anchoring hard on cash comp.

My home reference point is the Swiss market. A Senior Staff / founding-level engineer in Geneva or Zurich commands roughly CHF 160,000 to 220,000 base at a scale-up, more at a late-stage or public company. Berlin cash comp runs 25 to 40% below that for equivalent seniority, so any Berlin offer needs to be read against that gap, not against Berlin market medians alone. The ask isn't "match Geneva exactly," it's "the discount has to be justified by something real, mainly equity and role scope."

## Opening anchor

Lead with base, not total comp, and make them name a number first if possible. If asked for expectations directly:

"For a founding engineer role with this scope, I'd expect a base in the range of EUR 100,000 to 120,000, plus a meaningful early-employee equity grant. I'm coming from Swiss market rates, so I know Berlin cash comp runs lower, but I'd want the equity and ownership to reflect that I'm one of the first engineering hires, not a mid-stage hire."

This gives room to land at 90 to 100k base if the equity and role are strong, without starting there.

## If they come in low

Don't reject on the spot, ask what's driving the number:

"Can you walk me through how you landed on that? I want to understand if it's a budget constraint at this stage or a leveling question, because the answer changes how I'd want to structure the rest of the offer."

Then counter on structure, not just amount:

"If cash is tight at this stage, I'd rather see that gap closed on the equity side, or with a review tied to a funding milestone, than just accept a lower number outright."

## Equity notes, GmbH specifics

Zauber is a German GmbH, which matters mechanically. German startups mostly grant VSOP (virtual stock option plans, phantom equity) rather than real shares, because direct share transfers under German law are notarization-heavy and expensive to administer for every employee. VSOP is economically similar at exit but has different tax timing and no voting rights, worth confirming explicitly rather than assuming "equity" means the same thing it did at a US or UK company.

Questions to ask directly:

- Is this VSOP or real shares (Geschäftsanteile)? What's the strike/exercise price basis?
- What's the fully diluted percentage, not just the number of options, and what's the current fully diluted cap table size?
- Vesting: confirm it's 4 years with a 1-year cliff, standard for early hires. Push back if it's longer.
- What happens to vested VSOP on an exit or acquisition, is there accelerated vesting on a change of control?
- German VSOP historically has had less favorable tax treatment than real equity, ask if they're using the newer preferential regime for qualifying startups (Zukunftsfinanzierungsgesetz-related changes), since that materially affects what the grant is worth after tax.

As a very early non-founding engineer at a seed-stage company backed by a fund like P9, 0.3 to 1.0% fully diluted is a reasonable range to ask for, more toward the top of that if joining before a Series A closes.

## Geographic and relocation leverage

Since this is Berlin on-site (in-office days expected) and I'm Geneva-based, relocation or a hybrid arrangement is itself a point to negotiate, not just cash:

"Relocating from Geneva means giving up Swiss market comp and cost-of-living, so I'd want a relocation package covering the move itself, and I'd want to understand if there's flexibility on how many days a month need to be in Berlin versus remote."

If they push back on remote flexibility, use it as a comp lever instead: ask for a relocation stipend or a temporary housing allowance rather than dropping the point entirely.

## Competing offer leverage template

Use only if genuinely in parallel process, don't fabricate:

"I'm further along in a couple of other conversations at a similar stage, and comp there is landing closer to the range I mentioned. I'd rather join Zauber if the role and equity line up, so I wanted to be upfront about where things stand instead of letting this drag."

## Close

Always confirm the full package in writing before verbally accepting: base, equity type and percentage, vesting start date, relocation terms, and start date. Verbal numbers in a call have a way of shifting once someone writes them down.
