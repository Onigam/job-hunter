# Negotiation Script: Senior AI Engineer @ Rivia (Zurich, on-site)

## Context
- Rivia AG, Zurich. Founded 2022 by Erik Scalfaro (CEO) and Tiago Kieliger (CTO). Agentic data engine for clinical trial intelligence; products include Rivia Detect (data quality) and Rivia Spark (natural language analytics).
- Funding: €3M seed led by Speedinvest (2024), then a $15M (€13M) Series A led by Earlybird in March 2026, with Defiant, Speedinvest, Amino Collective and Nina Capital. ARR grew about 4x in 2025 and they support around 40 trials globally. **Freshly funded, growing revenue, and hiring to scale agentic product**, so there's real cash for senior hires, though Series A equity budgets stay tight.
- Role: Senior AI Engineer, full-time, **on-site in Zurich**. LinkedIn tags the seniority as "Management", which is worth clarifying. Salary isn't disclosed. Recruiter: Elena Rudakova, Sr Talent Partner.
- **Two issues to settle before any number:**
  1. **Level.** You're coming from Senior Staff. Ask where this role sits in their ladder and whether there's a Staff or Lead AI Engineer band. A title mismatch now becomes a salary ceiling later.
  2. **On-site vs. Geneva.** Geneva to Zurich is about 2h45 by train. Daily commuting isn't realistic, so find out what "on-site" means in practice: 3 days a week? 4? Is a relocation package available? This drives the cost side of your number.

## Your target

- **Walk-away:** CHF 170k base, at least 0.15% of fully diluted in options (or an equivalent phantom plan), 4-year vest with a 1-year cliff, and a written hybrid arrangement you can live with from Geneva (or relocation support if they insist on full-time on-site).
- **Target:** CHF 185k to 195k base, 0.25% to 0.4% options, a travel or accommodation allowance for Zurich days, and a formal review toward a Staff title within 12 months.
- **Opening anchor:** CHF 205k base and 0.5% options. Justify it with 17 years of experience, a platform built from zero to a unicorn acquisition, Senior Staff scope at Benevity, and hands-on LLM, multi-agent and embeddings work, which is exactly the profile Zurich big tech is also bidding for.

## Script: when they ask for your expectations

"I'm coming from a Senior Staff role, so my reference point is a Staff-level package in Switzerland. For this scope, owning agentic systems end to end, plus evals and LLMOps, I'm thinking around CHF 205k base with meaningful equity, roughly half a percent. I'm flexible on how cash and equity balance out, and on-site logistics matter too since I'm based in Geneva. What band have you set for the role, and where does it sit on your engineering ladder?"

Then stop and let them answer.

## Counter-offer script: if they come in low

"Thanks, I appreciate the offer. That number fits a senior band, and what we've discussed looks closer to Staff: architecting the agent layer, owning evaluation and working directly with product and customers. I also have real costs attached to being in Zurich several days a week. If base stays at CHF [their number], I'd need the gap covered elsewhere: more equity, something like [X]%, a Zurich travel allowance, and a written review toward Staff at 12 months. Can we make that work?"

If base won't move: "Understood. Then let's look at the other levers: option percentage, the exercise window after leaving, the on-site cadence, and the review date. I'd rather build a package that works than argue over one line."

## Pushing back on "you don't have clinical experience"

"That's fair, and I won't pretend otherwise. Domain knowledge is something your team has in depth and I'd learn from them in the first months. What's harder to find is someone who's owned a production platform end to end, through a unicorn's due diligence, and who treats evals and tracing as engineering rather than an afterthought. That's what I'm pricing."

## Pushing back on a Geneva discount

If they suggest a lower rate because you'd be partly remote: "Geneva is at least as expensive as Zurich, and being on-site means travel and probably a second base on my side. If anything, the arrangement costs me more, not less. I'm happy to discuss the cadence, but I wouldn't price it lower."

## Equity notes (Swiss Series A)

1. **Plan type.** Swiss startups use real options (ESOP), phantom shares or, sometimes, direct share grants. Ask which one and get the plan document. Phantom shares pay out in cash on an exit and are taxed as income.
2. **Percentage of fully diluted capital** and the strike price. A number of options means nothing without the total share count and the post-money valuation of the Series A.
3. **Vesting:** 4 years with a 1-year cliff is standard. Ask for double-trigger acceleration on acquisition; healthtech data platforms are regular acquisition targets for CROs and pharma software vendors.
4. **Exercise window after leaving:** 90 days is common and often makes equity worthless. Push for 5 to 10 years, or at least 2.
5. **Leaver clauses:** Swiss plans often have strict bad-leaver terms. Make sure vested equity stays yours as a good leaver, and that "good leaver" isn't narrowly defined.
6. **Dilution:** ask about the current pool and whether a top-up is expected at Series B (likely given US expansion plans).
7. **Swiss tax:** employee options are generally taxed as income, at grant or at exercise depending on the plan design. Rivia is in Zurich but you'd be taxed in Geneva as your residence; get a tax ruling or at least a Swiss tax adviser's review before signing.

## Other levers

- On-site cadence in writing (for example, 3 days in Zurich, 2 remote from Geneva).
- GA travelcard (about CHF 3.9k/year, 2nd class) or a monthly travel allowance.
- Accommodation support for Zurich nights, or a relocation package if full-time on-site is non-negotiable.
- Title review toward Staff or Lead AI Engineer at 12 months, with defined criteria.
- Conference and learning budget (NeurIPS, the AI Engineer Summit, clinical data conferences like DIA).
- Pension (LPP/BVG): ask whether the employer contributes above the legal minimum.

## Competing-offer template (use only if true)

"I want to be transparent: I'm in late-stage conversations with another company, a [Staff/Principal] role at around CHF [X] base. Rivia is my first choice because of the problem and the team, and I'd like to make it work. Is there room to close the gap on base or equity?"

## Final check before signing

- Level and title match the scope discussed.
- On-site cadence and travel support are in the contract, not just agreed verbally.
- Equity plan document received, with percentage, strike, vesting, leaver terms and exercise window.
- Notice period (Swiss standard is often 3 months for senior roles) and non-compete scope.
