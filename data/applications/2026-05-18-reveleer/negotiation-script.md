# Negotiation Script — Full Stack AI Engineer @ Reveleer

---

## Market Context

Reveleer is a US-based healthcare AI company. Compensation will be quoted in USD. The posted range is $175,000-$200,000 annually (US market). Key variables:

**US market rates for a Full Stack AI Engineer at staff level (2026):**
- Posted range: $175,000-$200,000 USD base
- Senior/staff AI engineers with 15+ years and founding-engineer background typically sit above mid-range
- No equity mentioned in the listing — worth asking explicitly

**Geneva purchasing power and cost context:**
- Cost of living in Geneva is among the top 3 globally
- CHF/USD rate: approximately 0.91 (strong franc)
- $200,000 USD = roughly CHF 182,000 — competitive but not exceptional for a staff-level engineer in Geneva
- Staff engineers at Swiss tech companies typically earn CHF 180,000-230,000+ base
- If paid in USD with no Swiss-specific benefits, the lack of pension contribution (LPP) and other local benefits is a real gap

**Important flag: the role is listed as "Remote (United States)"**
- A separate LATAM posting for the same role suggests Reveleer has some flexibility on geography
- Before going deep on compensation, confirm explicitly whether they can hire a Geneva-based contractor or employee
- If contractor engagement: no benefits, higher effective tax burden, anchor higher accordingly

---

## Opening Anchor

When asked about compensation expectations:

> "The posted range puts us in roughly the right territory. Given my background, specifically building a platform from zero that went to acquisition at $1B+ valuation, and 17 years of end-to-end engineering work on exactly this stack, I'd expect to be toward the top of that range or just above it. Something in the $200,000-$215,000 range as a base makes sense to me. What flexibility do you have there?"

Key moves:
- Acknowledge the range rather than ignoring it (they've already published it)
- Justify the top-of-range anchor with specific experience, not generic seniority
- Name a number that's at the top and slightly above, not wildly outside
- Turn it back to them quickly

---

## If They Come In Below $185,000

> "I appreciate you putting a number out. It's a bit below what I'd expect for the level of ownership I'd be taking on — full-stack, infrastructure, observability work, the agentic systems layer — particularly given the founding-engineer and post-acquisition experience I'm bringing. The comp should reflect that I'm not stepping into a narrow IC slot. Can we look at moving the base to $200,000, or is there an equity component we haven't talked about yet that changes the picture?"

If they cite budget constraints:

> "I understand there are constraints. One thing that can help bridge the gap is a six-month review with a committed step-up if I hit agreed milestones, or front-loading some equity value if that's on the table. Would either of those structures be possible?"

---

## Equity Discussion

Reveleer has not disclosed equity in the listing, so push for clarity early:

**Questions to ask:**
- "Is there an equity component to this role? I don't see it mentioned in the posting."
- "What's the current cap table stage — have you raised a Series B or beyond?"
- "If there are options, what's the vesting schedule and cliff?"
- "Is there acceleration on acquisition or change of control?"

**If they offer equity:**
- Target: 0.1%-0.3% for a staff-level hire at a growth-stage company
- 4-year vest, 1-year cliff is standard and reasonable to ask for
- Push for double-trigger acceleration on acquisition (standard, rarely resisted)

**If they offer no equity at this stage:**

> "For a role where I'd be owning infrastructure and AI architecture decisions that affect the product's core value proposition, I'd want to understand why equity isn't part of the package. If the answer is that the company is already at a stage where options don't make sense, I'd want the base to compensate for that — something at the higher end of the range becomes more important."

---

## Geographic Pushback Script

If they apply a geographic discount because you're based in Geneva:

> "I understand some companies tier by location. The practical issue is that Geneva is consistently in the top three most expensive cities globally, and the Swiss franc is strong. Staff engineers at companies based here earn CHF 180,000-230,000 equivalent. A geographic discount from the US rate would actually put you below the local market, not above it. I'd rather land on a number that works for both of us long-term and doesn't create retention pressure down the road."

Do not apologize for your location. This is a factual, business-case argument.

---

## Non-US Benefits Gap Script

If they offer US benefits (401k, US health insurance) that don't apply to a Geneva-based hire:

> "The 401k matching and health coverage are part of the US compensation package, but they won't apply to me in Switzerland — I'd be carrying those costs separately. The effective compensation difference is real: Swiss mandatory pension contributions and health coverage cost CHF 10,000-20,000 per year that a US-based employee gets covered. I'd need the base to reflect that gap."

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

If you're in conversations with other companies or want to create urgency:

> "I want to be transparent with you: I'm in active conversations with a couple of other companies in the AI/healthcare or developer infrastructure space. Reveleer is the one I'm most interested in given the agentic architecture work you're doing, but I need the offer to be clean for this to be the clear choice. What can you do on the base?"

---

## What to Get in Writing Before Signing

- Confirmation that the role is permanently remote from Geneva (not a temporary arrangement)
- Total equity grant in shares or percentage, not just "competitive equity"
- 409A valuation at grant date if options
- Vesting schedule and cliff in writing
- Acceleration clause language (exact wording matters — single-trigger vs double-trigger)
- Equivalent of US benefits or cash allowance for Swiss-based employees
- Hardware budget and home office stipend
- Any performance review timelines with salary step-ups
- Governing law and employment structure (employee vs contractor)

---

## Red Flags to Watch For

- "Remote (US only)" enforced strictly: verify before investing further in the process
- No equity whatsoever at what appears to be a growth-stage company: ask why
- Offer below $175,000 base with no equity offset: the risk-reward doesn't work for a staff role
- Benefits package that assumes US residency with no equivalent for international hires: a real cost gap
- Vague equity ("we'll sort it out after you start"): get specifics before signing
