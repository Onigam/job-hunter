# Negotiation Script — Lobby / Founding Engineer (Applied AI & Systems)

## Context
Pre-seed startup, Zürich, AI-native orchestration for travel. Founding Engineer role means you're likely engineer #1 or #2. That position commands a premium on equity but often comes with a below-market base. The leverage here is the scarcity of someone with a proven founding + exit track record at this seniority.

---

## Switzerland Market Anchors

**Comparable base salaries (Zürich/Geneva, 2025-2026):**
- Senior/Staff Engineer at established tech company: CHF 170k-200k
- Staff Engineer at Series A startup: CHF 140k-165k
- Founding Engineer at pre-seed (equity-heavy comp): CHF 110k-145k base + meaningful equity
- Your current comp at Benevity (Senior Staff, Geneva): likely CHF 150k-175k range

**Equity benchmarks for Founding Engineer (pre-seed, Switzerland):**
- Engineer #1 with cofounder-adjacent scope: 1.5%-3.0%
- Engineer #2-3 at founding stage: 0.75%-1.5%
- Standard vesting: 4 years, 1-year cliff
- Anti-dilution: ask for standard weighted average broad-based; avoid full ratchet requests (they won't accept)
- Acceleration: request single-trigger acceleration on involuntary termination (acqui-hire protection)

---

## Opening Anchor

When they ask for your number, lead with this:

> "Given my background, an exit on my last company, 17 years of experience, and the founding-engineer scope of this role, I'm anchoring at CHF 150k base with 1.5% equity on a standard 4-year, 1-year cliff schedule. I'm flexible on the mix depending on where you are in your funding, but that's the total comp I'm working from."

**Why this works:** It's high but not unrealistic. It signals seniority and signals you've thought about the equity side, which most candidates don't. It opens room to negotiate down on base in exchange for equity upside.

---

## If They Come In Below CHF 120k Base

Don't counter immediately. First:

> "Can you walk me through the reasoning? Is this where you're anchored for all engineering roles, or is this specific to where you are in the pre-seed?"

Then, if the base stays low:

> "I understand the cash constraint at this stage. I've been there. What I'd need to make that work is equity closer to 2%, with single-trigger acceleration on involuntary termination, and a commitment to revisit the base at Series A. Can you work with that?"

**Floor:** Don't accept below CHF 110k base + 1.0% equity unless there's a very strong reason (e.g., you're taking a cofounder-adjacent title with formal cofounder terms).

---

## If They Offer CHF 130k-140k Base

This is a reasonable pre-seed offer for Zürich. The lever here is equity:

> "CHF 130k base is workable for the right equity package. I'd want to land at 1.25-1.5% to make the total comp make sense given what I'd be leaving. Is there room to move there?"

If equity is capped:

> "If equity is fixed, I'd need the base at CHF 145k to make the numbers work. I'm not trying to be difficult, it's just Geneva rent."

---

## Equity Deep-Dive Questions to Ask

Ask these before accepting any offer:
- What's the current cap table and post-pre-seed dilution?
- What's the total option pool and how much is allocated?
- What's the current valuation (post-money pre-seed)?
- Is the equity in stock options (ISOs/NSOs) or warrants? What's the strike price?
- What's the exercise window on termination? (Push for 10-year post-termination window if possible)
- Do you have a standard 409A valuation or a Swiss equivalent?

---

## Geographic Context / Geneva Pushback

If they use Zürich vs. Geneva as a reason to discount:

> "Geneva and Zürich have comparable costs of living. If anything, Geneva housing is slightly more expensive right now. I'm not asking for a Zürich premium; I'm asking for a Switzerland-appropriate rate."

If they want you to relocate to Zürich:

> "I'm open to relocation. That changes the equation, though, because I'd need relocation support and a few months of transition time. What does that look like on your end?"

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

If you have another offer (or even an active process), use it:

> "I want to be transparent: I'm in late stages with another company. The offer there is at CHF [X] base with [Y]% equity. I'd prefer Lobby for the stage and the problem, but I need the comp to be in the same range to make the decision easy. Can you get there?"

If you don't have a competing offer, don't fabricate one. Instead:

> "I want this to work, but I'm also in conversations elsewhere and the market for someone with my background is active. I'd rather not let this drag out and risk losing the opportunity on both sides. Can we get to a number by end of week?"

---

## Red Flags to Watch

- Equity in a pool that's already heavily diluted (ask for the cap table)
- No formal option plan in place yet ("we'll sort it out later")
- Vesting with no cliff (unusual, often a sign of disorganized equity structure)
- Base significantly below CHF 100k with vague equity promises (walk away)
- No mention of Series A timeline or fundraising plan

---

## Final Framing If Negotiations Stall

> "I'm genuinely interested in what you're building. The problem is real, the market is large, and the timing is right. I just need the comp package to reflect the risk I'm taking by joining at this stage. Help me get there and we can move fast."
