# Negotiation Script: Director of Engineering, n8n (Core Experience)

## Context to keep in mind

n8n is growth-stage, not early startup: $5.2B valuation, backed by Sequoia and SAP, 260-plus employees, and this specific role is remote-first across Europe with a Berlin ±1 hour timezone requirement rather than a fixed office. That changes the negotiation shape versus a seed-stage founding role. Cash comp is likely benchmarked against a German or broader EU remote band rather than San Francisco or even Zurich rates, and equity at this stage means a smaller percentage than a pre-seed grant but with real, less speculative value given the SAP and Sequoia backing. The role also covers four teams, AI, Nodes, Community, and Adoption & Retention, which is a manager-of-managers scope, so comp should reflect leading multiple Engineering Managers, not a single-team EM band.

## Switzerland market anchors (context, not the primary lever)

Geneva isn't the natural salary anchor for a remote-Europe role benchmarked to Berlin, but it's worth having in reserve. A Director of Engineering with manager-of-managers scope based in Geneva typically sits in the CHF 220k to 260k base range, sometimes higher with strong equity upside at a company this size. German market rates for an equivalent Director role in Berlin tend to run lower in base, roughly EUR 140k to 180k, with the gap made up in equity or total comp framing. The honest move is not to lead with the Swiss number as if it's what Berlin pays, but to flag that working from Geneva doesn't reduce the value of the work, and ask directly whether comp is banded by role and level or by employee location.

## Opening anchor

Wait for their number first if possible. If asked to name one:

"For a Director role owning four teams and multiple Engineering Managers at this stage of company, I'd expect a base around EUR 175k to 190k, plus an equity grant sized for a manager-of-managers hire rather than a senior IC one. I'm flexible on the exact split between cash and equity, but that's roughly where I'd start the conversation."

## If they come in low on cash

"I understand growth-stage companies often weight comp toward equity, and I'm genuinely fine with that trade if the equity grant reflects it. If base has to land lower than that, I'd want to see it made up in the equity number, in a defined refresh at the twelve-month mark, or in the professional development and tooling budget being scoped up front rather than left as a standard line item."

Fallback if base truly can't move: ask for a larger initial equity grant, a shorter path to a comp review tied to the first two quarters of results across the four teams, and clarity on whether there's a bonus or variable component tied to org-level outcomes.

## Equity negotiation notes

Ask for the strike price, the current 409A or equivalent valuation, and whether the grant is options or RSUs, since at $5.2B valuation the mechanics matter more than the raw share count. Confirm the standard structure, most likely a one-year cliff with four-year vesting, and ask whether there's any acceleration on acquisition or change of control. Given this is a Director-level hire managing other managers, it's reasonable to ask how the grant compares to what existing Directors received, and whether refresh grants are standard practice at n8n or something to negotiate for explicitly at the two-year mark.

## Geographic and remote framing

Address this directly rather than letting it surface as an assumption: "I'm based in Geneva, which sits in the same timezone band as Berlin, so the ±1 hour requirement isn't an issue day to day. I want to make sure comp is scoped to the role and its scope rather than adjusted downward for not being physically in Berlin, especially since the team itself is distributed across Europe already." If they push a location-based discount, ask what the actual policy is and whether it applies uniformly or is negotiable for a role this senior.

## Competing offer template

Use only if a real competing process exists. Never invent a number or company name.

"I'm also further along in conversations for a comparable engineering leadership role elsewhere. I'd rather join n8n given how the Core Experience org is scoped and the AI-native product direction, so if there's room to move on base or the equity grant, this is the point where that would help me decide."
