# Negotiation Script — DomaFlow, Technical Co-Founder

---

## Context

DomaFlow is offering 18-20% equity with 4-year vesting and a 1-year cliff, plus deferred salary tied to investment. This is an early co-founder deal, not a standard employment offer. The negotiation is mostly about equity structure, deferred salary triggers, and governance rights — not about a fixed salary number.

---

## Switzerland Market Anchors

**Technical co-founder / CTO market rates:**
- CTO at Series A (Switzerland): CHF 180,000-250,000/year total comp
- Technical co-founder at seed stage, with significant equity: CHF 80,000-120,000/year (below-market, equity offset)
- Senior Staff / Principal Engineer (Geneva): CHF 150,000-200,000/year
- Current role (Benevity Senior Staff Engineer, Geneva): approximately CHF 160,000-180,000/year

Any deferred salary agreement should be anchored to these numbers so that when it kicks in, it reflects real market value and not what felt convenient in the early days.

---

## Equity: Negotiate for the Top of the Range

They offered 18-20%. Push for 20% as the floor, not the target.

**Framing:**
"You've set the range at 18-20%. Given that I'm bringing 17 years of engineering experience, a direct founding track record, and a platform already acquired by a unicorn, I'd expect to land at the top of what you've defined as the market for this role. I'm comfortable with 20%."

**If they push back on percentage:**
"I'm not looking to renegotiate the entire structure. 20% instead of 18% is a 2-point difference that matters a lot at exit. I want us to build something worth arguing about, and I want to be motivated to do that."

---

## Deferred Salary: Define the Trigger Precisely

"Deferred salary tied to investment" is a vague clause that needs to be pinned down before signing.

**Questions to ask:**
- What constitutes "investment" — is it the first external raise, or a specific amount (e.g., CHF 500k seed)?
- Is there a revenue trigger as an alternative? E.g., if revenue reaches CHF 50k/month before an institutional raise, does salary kick in?
- What's the actual number when salary activates? CHF 80k/year? CHF 100k?

**Counter-script if they're vague:**
"I want to make sure we're aligned on what 'investment' means before we sign anything. If it means a formal seed round, that could be 12-24 months away. I need a revenue-based fallback: something like, if monthly recurring revenue crosses CHF 30k, a base salary of CHF 100k/year activates automatically. That protects both of us and keeps incentives clean."

**Minimum acceptable:**
- CHF 80,000-100,000/year base when triggered
- Trigger should fire on EITHER institutional raise OR CHF X MRR, whichever comes first
- No cliff on the salary trigger beyond 6 months from signing

---

## Vesting Terms: Standard is Fine, with One Addition

4-year vesting and 1-year cliff is industry standard. Accept this. But add:

**Double-trigger acceleration clause:**
"If the company is acquired and I'm let go within 12 months of closing, I'd like my remaining unvested shares to accelerate. Single-trigger (acquisition alone) is aggressive; double-trigger (acquisition + termination) is reasonable."

**Script:**
"The 4-year / 1-cliff structure is fine. The one thing I'd add is a standard double-trigger acceleration clause on acquisition. I want to build this with you for the long term, but if there's an exit and I'm not part of the resulting entity, I shouldn't lose vesting credit for the equity I've earned through the work that created the exit."

---

## Pro-Rata Rights and Anti-Dilution

**Questions to ask before signing:**
- Do co-founders have pro-rata rights on future rounds? Can you maintain your percentage by investing in future raises?
- What's the cap table structure? Are there any existing options pools, advisor shares, or prior equity grants?
- What anti-dilution protection exists, if any?

**Script:**
"I'd like to understand the full cap table before we finalize percentages. If there are advisory shares or options pools I'm not seeing, my 20% might already be on a diluted basis. What does the cap table look like today, and what do you expect to reserve for future hires?"

---

## If They Come In Low (Below 18%)

If they revise the offer down or reframe the equity as options rather than shares:

"I'm going to be direct: below 18% for a technical co-founder role at this stage doesn't reflect what's being asked of me. I'm not taking a market salary, I'm taking the risk of a deferred income, and the equity is the reason this conversation makes sense. If the range has changed, I'd need to understand why before we continue."

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

If you have or can surface another co-founder opportunity:

"I'm having conversations with one other early-stage opportunity in Switzerland, also in the B2B SaaS space. I'm more drawn to DomaFlow because of the traction you've already demonstrated, but I want to make sure we close this quickly if we're aligned. Can we set a timeline for finalizing terms?"

Even without a competing offer, establish urgency:

"I have a few things I'm evaluating in parallel. My preference is DomaFlow, but I need to move to a decision within the next two weeks. What's the process on your end?"

---

## Geographic and Lifestyle Terms

- Ask explicitly about remote setup: if you're based in Geneva, what does "occasional meetings in Ticino" mean in practice? Monthly? Weekly?
- Confirm: is this a contractor relationship or employment (employed by a Swiss entity)? This matters for AVS, health insurance, and tax.
- If employed: what canton? Vaud, Ticino, or Zürich each has different implications.
- If contractor: make sure the equity vesting and deferred salary are in a signed shareholder agreement, not just an email.

---

## Red Flags to Watch For

- Deferred salary with no specific trigger or timeline: walk away or push hard
- Equity structured as options with a strike price above current valuation: means nothing at exit
- No shareholder agreement, only a verbal commitment: not acceptable
- Founder who controls 100% of decisions post-signing with no governance rights for co-founders: understand the power structure before signing
