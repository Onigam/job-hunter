# Negotiation Script - Grafana Labs, Staff Software Engineer, Grafana Cloud k6

## Market Context

**Posted ranges (from live listings across regions):**
- USA, remote: $174,986 - $209,983 base
- Ireland, remote: EUR 117,600 - 141,120 base
- UK, remote: GBP 103,958 - 124,750 base
- All roles include RSUs on top of base; Grafana Labs is late-stage private (Series D territory, prior valuation in the $6B range), so RSUs likely carry a double-trigger vesting condition tied to a liquidity event, not immediate public-market liquidity.

**Location factor:** These ranges are anchored to US, Dublin, and UK cost of living respectively. Geneva runs materially higher than all three. Rough premium over London: 30-35%. Over Dublin: similar or slightly higher.

**CHF conversion of the EMEA ranges:**
- Ireland: EUR 117,600 - 141,120 → roughly CHF 112,000 - 134,000 at current rates
- UK: GBP 103,958 - 124,750 → roughly CHF 122,000 - 146,000 at current rates
- Applying the Geneva cost-of-living premium (~1.32x) to the UK-converted figure: roughly CHF 161,000 - 193,000

**USD range sanity check:** $174,986 - $209,983 at ~0.90 CHF/USD is roughly CHF 157,000 - 189,000, which lands in the same band as the Geneva-adjusted EMEA figure. That convergence is useful: it means the Geneva ask isn't an inflated number, it's roughly what the role pays in a higher cost-of-living US market already.

**Geneva market anchor for Staff Software Engineer (SRE/platform-leaning):**
- Comparable remote-friendly companies (Grafana itself, Sentry, Stripe, PlanetScale): CHF 175,000 - 210,000 base
- Total comp target (base + RSU annual equivalent): CHF 220,000 - 260,000
- Floor: CHF 160,000 base

---

## Opening Anchor

Use this when asked for expectations, or once a first number is on the table.

> "I looked at Staff-level pay in Geneva for remote roles at companies around Grafana's stage, and I landed on CHF 185,000 to 195,000 for base. I know the posted ranges are anchored to the US, UK, or Ireland, and Geneva sits above all three. I'd want the base to reflect that before we get into equity."

**Why 185-195k:**
- Sits at or above the Geneva-adjusted top of the EMEA ranges
- Leaves room to settle around 175-180k without it reading as a concession
- The USD range cross-check backs this up if they push back on the number

---

## If They Come In Below CHF 160,000

> "I appreciate the offer. To be straightforward, it's hard to make Geneva work at that level. I'm not trying to max out base at the expense of equity or scope, but CHF 160,000 is close to the floor given living costs here. If base is constrained, I'd want to look at the RSU grant and refresh schedule, since that's where the gap can close."

**If they hold firm on base:**
> "If CHF [their number] is the ceiling on base, let's talk equity. For a Staff role at this stage, I'd expect an initial RSU grant in the $150,000 - $200,000 range over four years with a standard one-year cliff. Is that realistic for this level, and is there an annual refresh?"

---

## RSU / Equity Script

Given Grafana's likely still-private status, treat these RSUs as double-trigger unless told otherwise. Questions to ask directly:

1. "Are these RSUs single-trigger or double-trigger? If double-trigger, what's the current thinking on timeline to a liquidity event?"
2. "What's the latest valuation these grants are priced against?"
3. "Is there an annual refresh grant at Staff level, and roughly what percentage of the initial grant does that tend to be?"
4. "If there's no liquidity event within the vesting window, what happens to unvested RSUs if I leave or the company gets acquired?"

**Equity counter-offer language:**
> "Base at CHF [X] works. To get total comp where I need it, I'd want an initial RSU grant of at least $180,000 over four years, plus an annual refresh that keeps pace with vesting. Given the double-trigger structure, I'd also want clarity in writing on what happens to that grant if there's no liquidity event in the next few years."

---

## Geographic Discount Pushback

> "I'd push back on applying a UK or Ireland-level discount here. The work is the same regardless of whether I'm in Geneva or Dublin, and Geneva has a higher cost of living than either, not a lower one. If anything the base should be adjusted upward from the posted EMEA range, not held to it."

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

> "I want to be upfront: I'm in later-stage conversations with another company for a comparable Staff role, and I need to make a call in the next week or two. I'd rather close quickly here if the fit is right than stretch this out."

If pressed for details:
> "I'd rather not name it while it's still live, but the comp range is comparable and the scope is similar. What's pulling me toward Grafana specifically is the chance to help set the reliability standards for k6 while the framework is still being built, not just operate inside one that's already fixed."

---

## Closing Framework

**Ideal outcome:** CHF 185,000 base + RSU grant of $180,000+ over 4 years (annual equivalent ~$45,000 / ~CHF 40,000) = total comp ~CHF 225,000

**Acceptable outcome:** CHF 175,000 base + RSU grant of $200,000 over 4 years = total comp ~CHF 220,000

**Walk-away line:** Total comp below CHF 200,000 (base plus annual RSU equivalent) doesn't clear the bar against Geneva-based alternatives.

## Notes

- Confirm how Grafana handles Switzerland-based employment: local entity vs. employer of record (EOR). This affects take-home pay and benefits structure. Ask directly: "How do you currently employ people based in Switzerland?"
- If EOR, expect the RSU and benefits administration to be slightly more friction-heavy; factor that into the base ask rather than trying to negotiate it away separately.
- 30 days annual leave is already standard per the posting; don't spend negotiation capital re-confirming it, just get it in writing.
- Ask early whether this specific k6 role is being leveled and paid against the US, UK, or Ireland band internally, since that determines which of the ranges above is the real starting point for their side.
