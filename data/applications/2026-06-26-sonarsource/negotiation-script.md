# Negotiation Script: VP of Engineering, SonarSource
**Geneva, Switzerland · June 2026**

---

## Market Context

SonarSource is a mature, well-funded B2B SaaS company (backed by Insight Partners and General Catalyst) with 400,000+ organizations as customers and over 7 million active developers. At that scale, a VP of Engineering overseeing multiple Directors across Code Orchestration, Code Quality, and Onboarding is a senior organizational role with significant business impact.

Switzerland VP of Engineering compensation benchmarks (2025-2026):
- **CHF 220,000 - 260,000** — VP Eng at mid-size Swiss tech (series A-B equivalent)
- **CHF 260,000 - 320,000** — VP Eng at established SaaS, product-led growth companies, Geneva market
- **CHF 300,000+** — VP Eng at unicorn-adjacent or post-IPO scale, or dual-market companies

SonarSource sits in the upper-middle band. Geneva cost of living (one of the highest in the world), the B2B enterprise customer base, and the cross-Director scope all support pushing toward CHF 280,000-300,000 base.

Benefits package already disclosed by the company:
- 27 days PTO + seniority additions
- 60% pension contribution (13.5-15.5% savings + 2% risk)
- Annual discretionary company growth bonus
- 60% transportation reimbursement

---

## Opening Anchor

**Target opening ask: CHF 285,000 - 300,000 base**

If asked for a number first, say:

> "Based on what I know about Geneva-market VP Engineering compensation at companies at this scale, and the scope of what this role covers, I'm thinking in the range of CHF 285,000 to 300,000 for base. Is that in the right territory for what you've budgeted?"

This anchors high but with a justified range, and the closing question puts the ball back in their court without confrontation.

---

## If They Come In Below Your Range

If the offer comes in at CHF 240,000-260,000:

> "I appreciate the offer. I want to be straightforward with you: the range I'd been targeting was CHF 280,000-295,000, based on Geneva market rates for this scope and seniority level. Is there flexibility to move closer to that? I'm not trying to play games here, I just want us to start from a number that reflects the market."

If they push back on the market data:

> "I'm happy to walk you through the benchmarks I've been working from. The gap might be narrower than it looks depending on how the bonus and pension land. Can we model the total comp together?"

Never accept the first number without at least one counter. The ask itself is expected at this level.

---

## Equity / Bonus Leverage

SonarSource is not a typical early-stage startup; equity is unlikely to be the main lever, but if there is a company growth bonus or RSU component:

- Ask for the vesting schedule explicitly: "What's the structure on the growth bonus, and is there a threshold before it pays out?"
- If there are RSUs or warrants, ask: "What was the last 409A or valuation, and what's the liquidation preference structure above that?" (Insight Partners will have specific terms.)
- If bonus is discretionary and uncapped, ask what it has paid out historically as a percentage of base for the VP level.

---

## Geographic / Cost-of-Living Pushback

If they reference a "standard Swiss package" or compare to a Paris or London rate:

> "I've been in Geneva for years, so I know what the cost of living actually is here. A VP Eng role at a company of this caliber, in this city, is a CHF 280,000+ conversation. I don't think that's out of line with what you're offering other senior leaders here."

This is factual and confident without being aggressive. It grounds the ask in local reality rather than abstract market data.

---

## Competing Offer Leverage (If Applicable)

If you have or expect another offer:

> "I do have another process that's moving toward the offer stage. I want to be honest about that, because SonarSource is where I'd prefer to land, and I don't want to create pressure artificially. But I also need to make sure the numbers work. If you can get to CHF 285,000, I can close this without waiting to see the other number."

Only use this if true or actively in motion. If hypothetical, don't invent it.

---

## Acceptable Package Floor

Walk away if:
- Base below CHF 250,000 and no meaningful bonus
- Total cash (base + expected bonus) below CHF 280,000
- No clarity on bonus mechanics before signing

A VP role at SonarSource with Geneva tenure is worth holding on. Don't accept a "we'll discuss compensation growth after your first year" framing as a substitute for a market-rate offer at signing.

---

## Timing Notes

- Do not name a number before they do if at all avoidable. Let them anchor first.
- If they ask "what are you currently making?" redirect: "I'm more focused on what the role is worth at market than on what I'm making now. What's the range you've built for this position?"
- Give yourself 24-48 hours to respond to any written offer. Ask for it in writing before responding verbally.
