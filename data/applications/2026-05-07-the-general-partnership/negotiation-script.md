# Negotiation Script — The General Partnership (Staff Software Engineer, Dev Experience / FOSS)

---

## Context

The General Partnership is a VC firm that embeds engineers in portfolio companies rather than deploying capital from a distance. This is an unusual structure. The comp model may differ from a standard product company: it could be salary + carry, salary + bonus, or salary + equity in a fund vehicle. Worth clarifying the structure early, before anchoring on numbers.

The role is based in New York City but the posting does not say remote-only or remote-friendly explicitly. Magino is in Geneva. This creates two scenarios with different comp logic:

- **Scenario A: Remote (Geneva-based).** Swiss market rates apply. CHF is the anchor. Geneva senior staff engineering rates for this profile are CHF 180,000-230,000 base.
- **Scenario B: NYC-based or hybrid.** NYC Staff Engineer market: USD 220,000-280,000 base + equity at product companies. At a VC/studio structure, comp may be lower on base with carry upside. Negotiate accordingly.

---

## Market Anchors

### Switzerland / Geneva (Staff Engineer, 17+ years, platform/DX focus)
- Market range: CHF 170,000-240,000 base
- Target anchor: CHF 210,000-220,000 base
- Walk-away floor: CHF 175,000 base (below this, Geneva cost of living makes it untenable)

### New York City (Staff Software Engineer, comparable profile)
- Market range: USD 200,000-280,000 base (product companies)
- VC/studio structures often run lower base with fund carry or bonus: USD 170,000-220,000 base
- If NYC is required and fully on-site, factor in relocation and cost-of-living differential

### Equity / Carry (VC context)
- If the comp includes carry (fund economics), ask for the fund size, vintage, and carry percentage
- Carry is typically 20% of profits split across the investment team; a staff engineer share might be 0.5-2.0% of the carry pool
- Vesting for carry is typically 4 years with a 1-year cliff, aligned to fund investment period
- If offered equity in a portfolio company instead of the fund, treat it as a startup equity negotiation (see below)

---

## Opening Conversation

When they ask about salary expectations, respond with a question first:

> "Before I give you a number, I want to make sure I understand the structure. Is this compensation primarily salary plus carry in the fund, or is it more of a traditional base plus bonus? And is the expectation that I'd be working from Geneva or would there be a NYC requirement?"

Once the structure is clear, anchor:

> "Given my level and the Geneva market, I'd be targeting around CHF 210,000 base for a remote arrangement. If there's carry or a meaningful bonus structure on top, I'm happy to think about how those balance against base. What's the range you're working with?"

---

## If They Come In Low

If the initial offer is CHF 165,000-180,000 (or equivalent):

> "I appreciate the offer. The base is lower than I was expecting given what I see in the Geneva market for this profile. Based on market data and my experience level, I was expecting something closer to CHF 210,000. Is there flexibility there, either on base or by adjusting the bonus or equity component to close the gap?"

If they say the range is fixed:

> "I understand there are constraints. Let me think about what else might close the gap. A signing bonus, additional PTO, or a faster review cycle at six months would help make the total package work. Are any of those available?"

---

## Equity / Startup Equity (if applicable to portfolio placement)

If the role involves equity in a specific portfolio company:

- Ask: percentage of fully diluted shares, not number of shares
- Ask: last round valuation and cap table structure (is there significant liquidation preference stack above common?)
- Vesting: standard is 4 years / 1-year cliff; push back on anything longer
- Acceleration: ask for double-trigger acceleration on acquisition (this is standard for staff+ engineers)
- Anti-dilution: not typically negotiable for employees, but worth understanding the preference stack

Standard ask:

> "I'd want to understand the cap table before putting a value on the equity. What was the last round valuation, and is there significant liquidation preference sitting above common stock? That context helps me understand what the equity is actually worth at exit."

---

## Geneva Cost-of-Living Pushback

If they push for a lower number citing a "fully remote" discount:

> "Geneva is consistently ranked among the top five most expensive cities globally. The cost-of-living delta between Geneva and a lower-cost European city is significant. The Swiss market rate for this profile at this experience level is CHF 190,000-230,000. I'm not pricing in a Geneva premium so much as pricing at market."

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

If you have another offer in process (or want to create urgency):

> "I want to be transparent: I'm in late stages with a couple of other companies. I'm more interested in this role because of the embedded model and the FOSS dimension, but I do have a decision to make in the next two to three weeks. If the comp can get close to what I'm looking at elsewhere, this is my preference."

If asked to specify:

> "The other offers are in the CHF 200,000-220,000 range base. I'm not trying to use them as a hammer, I'd genuinely rather be here, but I need the numbers to be in that territory to make it work."

---

## Timing

- Don't accept on the spot. Always ask for 48-72 hours minimum.
- If they pressure for immediate acceptance: "I want to give this a fair read before committing. I can get back to you by [specific date, 3 days out]."
- Counter within that window, don't let it lapse.
