# Negotiation Script - Zenline AI

## Read of the situation

Zenline's published range for this role is **CHF 100,000 to 125,000 plus stock options**. That range matches the upper end of a regular Zurich ML/full-stack market band, not a true senior founding-engineer profile.

External market references:
- SwissDevJobs lists Zurich ML engineer pay around **CHF 117,500 median**, **CHF 127,500 top 25%**, **CHF 140,000 top 10%**: https://swissdevjobs.ch/salaries/Machine-Learning/Zurich/all
- Jobs.ch lists Zurich software engineer pay around **CHF 105,000 average** and **CHF 120,000** for 21+ years experience across Switzerland: https://www.jobs.ch/en/salary/?canton=ch&term=software%20engineer
- SwissDevJobs' 2024 market report lists Zurich average tech pay around **CHF 109,400**: https://static.swissdevjobs.ch/market-reports/IT-Market-Report-2024-SwissDevJobs.pdf

For Magino's background, the core question is not only salary. It is whether Zenline wants a regular full-stack hire inside the posted band, or a more senior founding builder with broader ownership.

## Recommended position

Lead with scope first, then comp.

Use this:

> "The role is interesting to me because it sits at the intersection of product, platform, and AI systems. My background is more senior than a standard 2 to 4 year full-stack profile, so before we talk numbers I'd want to understand whether you're hiring for a strong execution engineer inside the posted band, or for someone to take broader founding ownership across architecture, product modules, and engineering quality."

If they confirm the role is truly the published level, the cleanest target is:
- **Base:** CHF 125,000 to 135,000
- **Equity:** meaningful option package, ideally **0.25% to 0.75%**
- **Review:** written 6-month scope and compensation review

If they want senior founding leverage beyond the JD, target:
- **Base:** CHF 135,000 to 155,000
- **Equity:** **0.5% to 1.5%**, depending on actual founding status, dilution, and hiring sequence
- **Title:** clarify whether this is effectively Founding Engineer, Lead Engineer, or early technical lead

## Opening anchor

If the conversation looks like a standard hire:

> "Given the published range and the Zurich market, I'd want to be at the very top of the cash band, and I would expect the equity piece to reflect the founding-stage risk and ownership. For me that means CHF 130,000 plus a meaningful option grant, with a clear review point once I've had time to prove impact."

If the conversation looks broader than the posted level:

> "If you're looking for someone to operate as a true founding builder across full-stack product, AI workflows, and technical foundations, I'd frame this differently from a regular engineer hire. In that case I'd be targeting CHF 145,000 base with equity that reflects early ownership."

## If they come in low

If they offer cash below CHF 125,000:

> "That number makes sense for the market median, but it doesn't match the scope I'd be taking on here. If cash is capped, then I'd want to rebalance through equity, title, and a short review cycle tied to concrete milestones."

If they say the published band is fixed:

> "Understood. Then I'd want to make sure we're honest about role design. If the cash band is fixed, the package has to work through ownership: option size, strike price transparency, vesting terms, and a written path to re-level the role once value is visible."

## Equity points to negotiate

- Ask for the **number of options and percentage on a fully diluted basis**. Do not negotiate on option count alone.
- Ask for the **latest valuation or 409A-equivalent exercise reference** so the grant is interpretable.
- Push for a **4-year vest with a 1-year cliff** at most. If they want a longer cliff, push back.
- Ask whether there is **acceleration on change of control**. A fair ask is double-trigger acceleration for some portion of the grant.
- Ask whether there is a **post-termination exercise window** longer than 90 days.
- If they use "founding engineer" language heavily, push for a grant that reflects it. If the equity is token, treat the title as marketing.

## Switzerland-specific pushback

Use this if needed:

> "Zurich cash compensation at this stage needs to reflect both market reality and startup risk. If the role is on site and high-intensity, the package has to be stronger than a normal software engineering benchmark, not equal to it."

If they try to offset with startup upside only:

> "I'm open to startup risk when the upside is real and the terms are clear. What I don't want is startup risk priced like a normal salary band with unclear equity economics."

## Close

Best outcome for this process:
- Cash at the top of or above band
- Equity with real percentage ownership
- Clear title and scope
- 6-month written review tied to concrete outcomes

Worst acceptable outcome:
- Top of posted cash band
- Equity terms fully transparent
- Review point documented before signing

Walk away if:
- Equity is vague or token
- They want senior founding scope inside a regular-engineer package
- They cannot explain how this role grows as the company scales
