# Negotiation Script — AssetOS First Engineer

## Market Context

**Role level:** First engineer at seed-stage Swiss proptech startup (incorporated Oct 2024)
**Location:** Winterthur / remote from Geneva
**Frame:** This is not a "senior engineer IC" hire. First engineer means founding-level responsibility: architecture ownership, early hiring, shaping the engineering culture. That commands a different range than a typical senior hire.

**Switzerland market rates (2026, CHF gross):**
- Senior fullstack engineer (5-8 years): CHF 120,000-145,000
- Principal/Staff level (10+ years): CHF 145,000-175,000
- First/founding engineer premium: +10-15% over equivalent IC title
- Magino's justified range: CHF 155,000-175,000 base

**Comparable data points:**
- Geneva market is one of the most expensive in Europe; even Winterthur/Zurich-area roles benchmark at CHF 130,000+ for strong seniors
- Benevity (post-Series C, Canadian unicorn) pays Staff engineers in this range
- SaaS startups in Switzerland at seed stage typically offer CHF 120,000-150,000 with meaningful equity to compensate

---

## Opening Anchor

When they ask about compensation expectations, respond:

> "I'm looking at CHF 165,000 as a base for this kind of role. That's grounded in what Staff and founding-engineer roles go for in the Swiss market, and the first engineer scope justifies the positioning. I'm also expecting meaningful equity given the stage and the risk premium."

**Why CHF 165,000:** Sets the anchor above market midpoint. Leaves room to land at CHF 150,000-155,000 and feel like a reasonable compromise without leaving money on the table.

---

## If They Come In Low (CHF 120,000-135,000)

> "I appreciate the transparency. I want to make this work, but I have to be honest: that range doesn't reflect the scope of what a first engineer role actually means at this stage. Architecture ownership, early hiring decisions, setting the technical culture — those are founding responsibilities, not senior IC responsibilities. The Swiss market prices that differently. Can we talk about what flexibility looks like, either on base or equity?"

**Fallback position:** CHF 145,000 base minimum + equity increase. Below CHF 140,000 base, the equity package needs to be substantial (0.75%+) to justify the risk premium vs. a larger company.

---

## Equity Negotiation

**Target:** 0.75-1.0% fully diluted, 4-year vest, 1-year cliff

**Opening ask:**
> "For a first engineer at seed stage, I'd expect something in the 0.75-1.0% range on a fully diluted basis. What does the current option pool look like, and what's the vesting structure?"

**If they offer less (e.g., 0.25-0.4%):**
> "That's on the lower end for a first engineer at this stage. I've looked at comparable Swiss proptech and SaaS startups and the range for this kind of role is typically 0.5-1.0%. Is there room to revisit that, or is there flexibility on the base to compensate?"

**Key questions to ask about equity:**
- Is this real stock options or phantom stock? (Swiss startups often use phantom stock)
- What is the current valuation cap or latest round price?
- Is there an acceleration clause on acquisition? (Especially relevant given AssetOS's likely M&A trajectory)
- What happens to unvested options if the company is acquired before the cliff?

**Phantom stock note:** If it's phantom stock (Genussscheine or similar), push for a lower strike price and ask for transparency on the liquidation waterfall. Real equity is preferable; phantom stock should come with a higher cash premium.

---

## Geographic / Remote Negotiation

If they try to apply a "remote discount" because Magino is in Geneva rather than Winterthur:

> "I'd push back on a geography discount here. The Swiss labor market doesn't segment by city the way some countries do. Geneva and Zurich benchmark similarly for tech roles, and the cost of living difference doesn't run in the direction you'd expect. I'm available to travel to Winterthur regularly, so the remote arrangement isn't a limitation on output."

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

If needed (use only if you have real discussions ongoing):

> "I have a few other conversations in progress, including one with a funded startup in the Geneva area. I'd prefer to close with AssetOS because the problem space is genuinely interesting and the technical stack is one I'd build with today. But I need the numbers to make sense."

---

## Counter-Offer Response Template

If they come back with a revised offer:

> "That's closer. Here's where I'm at: [X] on base works if we can get to [Y] on equity, or I'm comfortable at [X+10k] with the current equity package. Which direction is easier for you?"

---

## Red Lines

- Base below CHF 140,000: walk unless equity is 0.75%+ real stock with clean terms
- No equity at all: walk
- No acceleration clause in an acquisition scenario: negotiate hard or walk
- Probation period > 3 months: flag and push back (3 months is Swiss standard)

---

## Timeline / Close

When ready to close:

> "If we can align on the package, I can be available from [date]. I'd need the written offer by [date] to give you a decision by end of the following week."
