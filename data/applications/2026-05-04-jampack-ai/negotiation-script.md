# Negotiation Script — Jampack AI, Founding Engineer (Fullstack)

---

## Context

Jampack AI is a seed-stage startup (Brooklyn, NY) with $3.2M raised from Maveron in February 2026. The company is ~5 months post-public-launch and processing $500M+ in annualized wholesale volume. This is a founding engineer role: early equity, high ownership, pre-Series A risk profile.

This is a US-based company. Compensation will likely be in USD. Role may be remote-friendly given they listed it publicly without a relocation requirement, but confirm early.

---

## Market Anchors

**Founding Engineer, AI Agents — US Seed Stage (2026)**
- Base: $170k–$220k USD depending on remote vs. on-site and team seniority context
- Equity: 0.5%–2.0% for founding engineer #1–3 at pre-Series A; below 0.3% is below market for this title

**Switzerland-adjusted context:**
- If Jampack offers "local rate" for a Geneva-based remote engineer, the CHF equivalent is CHF 155k–185k (roughly $175k–$210k at current rates)
- Geneva CoL is among the top 3 most expensive cities in the world; a "local rate discount" relative to NYC is not justified — Geneva is more expensive than New York for housing and taxes
- Push back firmly if they cite geographic adjustment: Geneva's cost of living exceeds NYC's by most indices

**Reference point:** Senior Staff / Principal Engineer roles at Series B–C companies in Switzerland pay CHF 180k–230k all-in. A founding engineer title at a high-growth seed company with significant equity should clear that bar on base alone, or compensate with meaningful upside.

---

## Opening Ask

After receiving an offer, respond with:

> "Thanks for the offer. I've been looking at what founding engineer compensation looks like at seed-stage AI companies right now, and the range I'm seeing is $195k–$210k base with 1.0–1.5% equity. That's what I'd need to make this work comfortably. What flexibility do you have on those numbers?"

Don't negotiate base and equity separately in the first exchange. Anchor the full package together.

---

## If They Come in Below $170k Base

> "I appreciate the offer. The base is below what I was expecting for a founding engineer role with this scope of ownership. I'm seeing $185k–$200k as the relevant range for this type of position, and I'd need to get closer to that to make the move from a Senior Staff role at Benevity. Is there room to revisit the base, or would it help to talk about how equity could offset some of the difference?"

If they push back on budget constraints (common at seed stage):

> "I understand seed budgets are tight. If the base ceiling is firm, let's talk about equity. At 0.3% or below I'd need a significantly higher base to compensate for the risk. At 1.0%+ I could be more flexible on salary. What's the equity range you're working with for this role?"

---

## Equity Negotiation

**What to ask for:**
- Minimum acceptable: 0.5% fully vested over 4 years, 1-year cliff
- Target: 1.0–1.5% for founding engineer #1–3
- Ask about: total shares outstanding, current valuation cap or post-money, and the option pool size

**Questions to ask before signing:**
1. What is the current fully diluted share count?
2. What was the post-money valuation on the seed round? (Maveron-led $3.2M round — ask for the cap table context)
3. Is there a standard 409A valuation in place? What is the current strike price?
4. What happens to unvested shares if the company is acquired before the cliff? (Push for single-trigger acceleration or at minimum double-trigger acceleration on acquisition)
5. Is there a right of first refusal on secondary sales?

**On acceleration:**
> "Given the founding engineer risk profile, I'd want to understand the acceleration provisions. If the company is acquired before my cliff, I'd like to see at least partial acceleration — ideally single-trigger on acquisition events. Is that something you've built into the option agreements?"

---

## Geographic Pushback

If they cite a "Geneva discount" on salary:

> "I'd push back on a geographic adjustment here. Geneva's cost of living consistently ranks above New York in housing, healthcare, and taxes. A Geneva-based engineer working UTC+2 hours is also covering the European market timezone, which is an operational advantage for the company, not a discount. I'd want to stay at the US market rate."

---

## Competing Offer Template

If you have (or can credibly reference) another offer:

> "I want to be transparent: I'm in late stages with another company in the developer tools space that's come in at [X base / Y equity]. I'd prefer Jampack because of the market timing and the agent architecture problem specifically, but I need the offers to be in the same territory. Is there any room to move on [base / equity] to close that gap?"

Use only if truthful or if you can back it up in follow-up conversation.

---

## Timing Leverage

Jampack is hiring fast post-seed. They need to move quickly to build out the engineering team before Series A. Use this:

> "I can move quickly if the terms work. I'm available to start within [X weeks] of signing, which I know matters at this stage."

But don't give away urgency too early. Let them set the pace on closing.

---

## Red Lines

- Base below $165k USD: decline unless equity is exceptional (1.5%+) and vesting terms are favorable
- Equity below 0.3% with standard vesting: not worth the seed-stage risk
- No acceleration clause at all: flag as a deal risk, push for at least double-trigger
- "We'll revisit comp after Series A": non-answer, push for written commitment or adjust ask now
