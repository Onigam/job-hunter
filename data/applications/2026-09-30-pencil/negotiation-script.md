# Negotiation Script: Pencil, Head of Agent Architecture (EMEA, Remote)

## Context that shapes everything

Pencil isn't a standalone startup anymore. The Brandtech Group acquired it in 2023, and Brandtech is a private group with more than $1BN in annual revenue, headquartered in London. That changes the negotiation in three ways.

First, **the salary band is probably set in GBP, against the London market**, because the head office and the Shard office are there. You're in Geneva, so expect them to price you in pounds or euros, not CHF.

Second, **Pencil equity in the startup sense probably doesn't exist**. Any long-term incentive would sit at Brandtech level, most likely a group LTIP, growth shares or phantom equity. Ask what the instrument is before you put any value on it.

Third, **eligibility comes before money**. The LinkedIn listing says remote within the European Union, and Switzerland isn't in the EU. Settle how they'd employ you (EOR, contractor, a UK or EU entity) in the first recruiter call. The answer affects your net pay a lot, because as a contractor you'd pay both sides of Swiss social charges (AVS/AHV and the rest) and fund your own pension.

The posting lists no salary. The benefits it does mention (25 days PTO, private medical, wellness and learning stipend, enhanced parental leave) are UK-style and may not carry over to someone on an EOR or contractor setup. Ask.

## Market reference points (estimates, verify before the call)

These are working estimates. Check them on Levels.fyi, Glassdoor (Brandtech Group, Pencil) and Swiss sources such as Swissdevjobs before a number leaves your mouth. Assume roughly 1 GBP ≈ 1.1 CHF and 1 EUR ≈ 0.94 CHF, and check the rate on the day.

| Reference | Base | Approx. CHF | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| London, Head of AI/ML or Principal AI architect at a scale-up | ~£130k to £175k | ~CHF 145k to 195k | Plus a bonus of 10 to 20% in some groups |
| EU remote (Amsterdam, Berlin, Paris), same scope | ~€120k to €160k | ~CHF 115k to 150k | This is the band you'll be pulled toward if they pay "EMEA remote" rates |
| Geneva, Senior Staff or Head of Engineering at a scale-up | ~CHF 190k to 240k | same | Local market reference |
| Zurich, Head of AI or Principal AI Engineer | ~CHF 200k to 260k | same | Upper Swiss reference |

Takeaway: London pay for this role roughly reaches the bottom of the Geneva range. An EU-remote band would land well below it. Most of the negotiation is about which of those two bands you're paid on.

## Your numbers

**Walk-away floor:** decide this before the first call and write it down. If you're on a contractor or EOR setup, work out the gross you'd need to match a Geneva salaried role after employer-side social charges and pension. As a rough guide, a contractor rate should be about 20 to 25% above the salaried number it's replacing.

**Target (salaried equivalent):** base around £165k to £175k (about CHF 180k to 195k), plus a bonus target and a Brandtech-level LTIP.

**Target (contractor):** around CHF 1,100 to 1,300 a day, or an annual contract of about CHF 230k to 250k.

**Opening anchor:** top of the London band, framed as the London rate for a Head-of role, not a Swiss premium. That's easier for them to approve than a Geneva salary.

## Scripts

### When the recruiter asks for your expectations early

"Before numbers, can we sort out how you'd employ someone in Switzerland? The listing says EU, and that decides whether we're talking salary or a contract rate. Once that's clear, I'm assuming this is scoped as a Head-of role at London rates, since that's where Brandtech's senior bands are set."

If they insist on a number:

"For a Head of Agent Architecture with a team to build, I'd expect a base in the range of £165k to £175k, plus bonus and whatever long-term incentive Brandtech offers at this level. If it's a contract setup, I'd convert that to a day rate that covers the social charges I'd be paying myself."

### If they come in with an EU-remote band

"I understand you have an EMEA remote band. The thing is, this role owns the agent architecture that serves 1,000+ brands, it's the final escalation for your hardest client deployments, and it builds a team. That's a London head-of role in scope, wherever the person sits. I'm also based in Geneva, one of the most expensive cities in Europe, so a Berlin-style number doesn't work for me. Could we look at the London band instead?"

### If the offer comes in low

"Thanks, I appreciate it and I want to make this work. The base is below what I'm seeing for this scope in London, and London is already under the local market where I live. I'm thinking closer to £170k. If that's hard on base, I'm open to closing part of the gap with a sign-on payment or a bigger LTIP grant."

Then stop talking and let them come back.

### Addressing the "8+ years of GenAI" requirement if they use it to push the level down

"Fair point, and I said so in my letter. What I bring is 17 years of architecture, a company built from zero to acquisition, and the last few years on LLM systems and multi-agent workflows. The success metrics in your posting, failure rates, token efficiency, adoption and team retention, are architecture and leadership outcomes. I'd rather be measured on those than on years since a date."

## Equity and long-term incentives

Questions to ask, since it'll be Brandtech-level and not Pencil options:

1. What is the instrument? Options, growth shares, phantom equity or a cash LTIP?
2. What's the vesting schedule and is there a cliff? Four years with a one-year cliff is typical. Push for monthly vesting after the cliff.
3. What happens on a change of control or an IPO of the group? Ask about acceleration: single-trigger is rare, double-trigger (a sale plus losing your role) is a reasonable ask for a Head-of role.
4. How is it valued, and when did the last valuation happen? Ask for the current value per unit and the number of units outstanding, or at least the percentage of the pool.
5. Does it work for someone employed through an EOR or as a contractor in Switzerland? Some UK schemes (EMI options, for example) are only open to UK employees, so this can quietly cut you out.
6. Swiss tax: employee equity is usually taxed as income at grant or vesting, depending on the instrument. Get a quick check from a Geneva tax adviser before you accept.

If there's no LTIP at your level, ask for a larger bonus target or a sign-on payment instead.

## Geographic discount pushback

"I understand remote roles often get priced by location. For Geneva, a discount goes the wrong way. Cost of living here is higher than London, and the local market for this level is CHF 190k to 240k. I'm not asking for a Geneva salary, I'm asking for the London band that this scope would get anyway."

## Competing offer template (use only if true)

"I want to be open with you. I'm in late-stage conversations for another role, [title] at [company], with an offer around [amount]. Pencil is my first choice because [specific reason, e.g. the multi-tenant agent problem and building the team]. If we can get closer to [number], I'm ready to commit this week."

Never invent a competing offer. If you don't have one, say you're "in conversations with other companies at a similar level", which is true while you're actively searching.

## Things to get in writing

- Employment structure (EOR, contract, entity) and who carries the social charges
- Base or day rate, currency, and whether FX risk sits with you
- Bonus target and what it depends on
- LTIP details: instrument, number of units, vesting, acceleration, eligibility from Switzerland
- Team size and hiring budget for Agent Architects, since hiring is one of your success metrics
- Travel expectations for client deployments and the London office, and who pays
- Notice period and any non-compete (check it against Swiss law)
