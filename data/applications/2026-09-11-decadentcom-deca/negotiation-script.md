# Negotiation Prep, Decadent.com / DECA (Founding AI-Native Platform Engineer)

## Context worth naming up front

The posted range, $180K-260K base with 1.0%-4.0% equity and milestone-based bonuses, is already a real, structured founding-engineer offer rather than one hiding behind vague equity promises. The open question is where in that band a senior, Geneva-based hire lands, especially since the listing is written around US locations, Austin, SF, NYC, and this application would mean joining remotely from Switzerland. That's worth surfacing early: is DECA set up to employ someone based in Switzerland, through an EOR, a foreign contractor arrangement, or a Swiss entity, or does the posted structure assume US payroll and taxation? That mechanical question shapes the rest of the conversation before a single number gets discussed.

## Market anchors

- US anchor, their stated range: $180K-260K base, roughly CHF 160,000-230,000 at typical exchange rates, for a senior founding backend/platform engineer.
- Switzerland anchor: Senior Staff or Principal engineers at funded startups in Geneva or Zurich land around CHF 150,000-190,000 base; a founding hire owning a compliance-heavy, multi-domain scope sits at the top of that band or above it.
- Founding equity for a company with real revenue and live clinics, not pre-product, typically runs 1-3% for hire #1-5, which lines up with DECA's posted 1-4%. The equity band itself isn't really the fight, the position within it is.
- Scope check: agent orchestration with LangGraph, Temporal workflow infrastructure, a pgvector/RAG data layer, and HIPAA/SOC2 ownership from day one is closer to three senior specialities bundled into one founding seat. That's the strongest argument for anchoring high in the posted range rather than settling for a mid-band number by default.

## Opening anchor

"The range you've posted, $180K to $260K plus 1 to 4% equity, tells me you've thought about this seriously, so I want to be direct about where I'd start: given the scope, agent orchestration, workflow infrastructure, the data layer, and compliance ownership, that's really three senior roles bundled into one founding seat. I'd anchor at $240K base, 2.5% equity, with the milestone bonuses tied to concrete platform milestones we agree on together, not ones left to discretion later."

## Counter-offer script if they come in low

"I hear where you're starting. I've taken a platform from zero to a unicorn acquisition before, and this role covers three specialities most companies would hire three separate senior engineers for, so I'd want the number to reflect that combined scope, either in base or in equity if cash is tighter at this stage. If cash is genuinely constrained, I'd rather see that show up as a higher equity percentage or a shorter cliff than as a lower headline number. Where's the actual flexibility, base, equity, or the bonus structure?"

## Equity negotiation notes

- Get the fully diluted share count and current valuation before agreeing to any percentage; 2.5% of a company that just closed a strong round is a very different number from 2.5% of a seed-stage cap table.
- A standard four-year vest with a one-year cliff is fine as a baseline, but for a founding-level hire it's worth asking for early exercise rights and a longer post-termination exercise window than the default 90 days, since that default can force someone to either cover a large tax bill fast or walk away from equity they earned.
- Ask whether the milestone-based bonuses in the posting are cash, equity, or accelerated vesting, and get the specific milestones written down rather than left to founder discretion after the fact.
- Clarify current ESOP pool size and whether it gets topped up post-hire in a way that dilutes this grant before it's even fully vested.

## Geographic and remote-structure pushback

Nothing about healthcare interoperability or agent orchestration work requires sitting in Austin, SF, or NYC, and Geneva overlaps enough of a US workday to make async collaboration workable with some schedule shifting. If there's any instinct to discount the offer for being Switzerland-based rather than US-based, the response is straightforward: Swiss senior engineering comp isn't cheap labor, it's a different high-cost market, and the posted range was set for senior US talent at this scope, which this background matches or exceeds.

## Competing offer template

"I'm also in later-stage conversations with a couple of other founding and staff-level roles at a similar stage, one of which is already in the range I mentioned. DECA's problem, real clinics, real interoperability constraints instead of a sandbox, is the more interesting one technically, which is why I'm prioritizing this conversation, but I need the offer to be competitive for that to keep being true."

Use only if genuinely true; the real strength here is the Alaya-to-acquisition story and the AI-tooling-adoption story at Benevity, not a bluffed offer.
