# Negotiation Script — Clera Founding Engineer

## Context
- Stage: Pre-seed, $3M from 1984 Ventures (2025)
- Team size: 2-10 employees; this is engineer #4
- Location: SF onsite preferred; Magino is in Geneva
- Product: AI talent agent via iMessage/WhatsApp, $1M+ ARR, 80k+ users
- Comp structure: "competitive salary + meaningful equity"
- Equity pool at pre-seed is typically 15-20% total; founding engineer #4 should target 0.5-1.5%

---

## Scenario A: Remote from Geneva (preferred)

### Market Anchor
Senior founding engineer with 17 years of experience, co-founder background, and AI delivery track record. For a comparable profile at a US pre-seed startup operating remotely from Europe:
- Base: $140k-$175k USD (Geneva senior staff equivalent: CHF 160k-200k)
- Equity: 0.75-1.25% with 4-year vest, 1-year cliff
- Total comp at current valuation: meaningful, but equity upside is the thesis

### Opening Ask
"Based on what I've seen at this stage with similar profiles, I'd expect a base in the $160k-$175k range and equity in the 0.75%-1% band given I'm joining at founding team stage. I'm flexible on the exact split if the equity story is stronger than that."

### If They Come In Low (under $130k base or under 0.5% equity)
"I want to make this work, and I'm not anchored to a specific number. But I've benchmarked what senior founding engineers have received at pre-seed rounds similar to yours, and what you're describing is below that range. What's the flexibility on the equity side? If the base is constrained by burn rate, I'd rather have more shares and less cash."

### Counter-offer Script
If they offer $120k + 0.3%:
"I appreciate the offer. The equity is on the lower end of what founding engineers typically receive at this stage given the risk premium, and the base is below what I'd accept for a senior staff role even accounting for the startup discount. Could we get to $140k and 0.6%? That feels like a fair compromise on both sides."

---

## Scenario B: Relocation to San Francisco

### Cost-of-Living Anchor
SF cost of living is roughly 1.5-1.8x Geneva. Magino's current Geneva market value: CHF 170k-200k base. Equivalent SF anchor:
- Base: $185k-$210k USD
- Equity: 0.75-1.25% (same logic as above; location doesn't reduce founding contribution)
- Relocation: at minimum 3 months temporary housing covered, ideally a $10k-$15k relocation stipend

### Opening Ask
"For a full relocation to SF, I'd anchor around $195k base. Geneva senior staff engineering roles in my bracket run CHF 170k-200k, and SF is meaningfully more expensive. On top of that, equity at 0.75-1% given I'm joining as engineer #4 at pre-seed feels right. And I'd want relocation covered."

### If They Push Back on Location Premium
"The cost-of-living gap is real. Rentals in SF for something comparable to what I have in Geneva are $4k-$5k/month. I'm not trying to extract rent money, but the base needs to reflect that reality or the equity has to compensate. Which lever has more flexibility?"

---

## Equity Notes

### What to Ask For
- Grant size: 0.75-1.25% for founding engineer #4 at pre-seed (lower than #2 or #3 but materially higher than a post-Series-A hire)
- Vesting: 4 years, 1-year cliff. Standard. Don't accept less than 4 years; it signals they want you for the long haul.
- Acceleration: ask for double-trigger acceleration on acquisition (both a change of control AND termination without cause). This is common and reasonable.
- Cliff: confirm the cliff date starts from your start date, not from signing

### Red Flags to Watch
- Options rather than RSUs at pre-seed is normal; don't worry about this
- A strike price significantly above the 409A valuation suggests a company that hasn't kept the 409A current; ask for the most recent one
- Less than 0.5% for a true founding engineer #4 means either the option pool is already depleted or they don't see the role as founding; clarify which

### Dilution Math
Current implied valuation with $3M pre-seed: likely $8-15M post-money. If you receive 1%:
- At a $100M exit: ~$1M (after dilution from future rounds, probably 50-60% remaining = $600k-$800k net)
- At a $500M exit: ~$5M pre-dilution
- Ask what the current cap table structure looks like and what the next round is expected to be

## Competing Offer Leverage

If you have another offer (or are in process elsewhere):
"I'm currently in final stages with another company at a senior level. I want to be at Clera, but I need to make sure the terms are competitive. Their package is around $X base and Y%. Is there room to match or come close?"

If you don't have a competing offer:
Don't manufacture one. Instead: "I've benchmarked this against a few comparable offers I've researched and the range I mentioned reflects that. I'm not playing games with a competing offer I don't have."

---

## Final Notes
- Clera is pre-seed and burn matters; they may be genuinely constrained on cash. Lean into equity in that case.
- The company has strong early traction ($1M ARR, 80k users before Series A) — that's a credible risk/reward narrative.
- If they won't negotiate meaningfully on either salary or equity, that's a signal about how they treat early contributors. Note it.
