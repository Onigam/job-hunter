# Negotiation Script — Synthesia Senior ML Platform Engineer

## Market Context

Synthesia is a well-funded UK company (Series C, $90M+, raised additional rounds post-2023). The role is advertised as UK-based remote. As a Geneva-based candidate, you're applying across a UK/Swiss pay differential, which cuts both ways: UK senior IC salaries in ML infrastructure are typically £120k-£155k; Geneva equivalents for the same profile run CHF 165k-210k given the cost of living.

**Your anchor: CHF 185,000 base.** This is justifiable with 17 years of experience, a founding-engineer track record with a unicorn acquisition, and explicit AI platform expertise. It's not the top of the Geneva market but it's solidly in the upper half for Staff-level roles at a growth-stage company.

---

## Opening Anchor Script

When they ask about compensation expectations, respond before they make an offer:

> "I'm thinking around CHF 185k base for this level. That's consistent with Staff-level platform engineering roles in Switzerland, and it reflects the fact that I'm bringing both the founding-engineer breadth and the AI tooling depth you're specifically looking for. Happy to discuss the full package, including equity."

Do not apologize for the number. Do not say "it's flexible" upfront.

---

## If They Come In Low (below CHF 155k base or GBP equivalent)

> "I appreciate the offer. The role and the team are genuinely interesting, and I want to find a number that works. The gap from my target is meaningful enough that I'd need you to come up on base before I could seriously consider it. What's the realistic ceiling on base for this position?"

This puts the ceiling question back on them without asking for a specific counter. If they give you a ceiling:

> "If you can get to CHF 170k, I think we can make this work, and I'm open to a structure where part of the delta is addressed through equity or a performance review at 12 months."

---

## Equity Negotiation (UK-Based Startup)

Synthesia is UK-incorporated. Options are likely structured as EMI options (Enterprise Management Incentive) for UK employees, or unapproved share options for non-UK employees. As a Swiss resident you'd fall into the unapproved category, which has different tax treatment.

**Target range:** 0.15-0.30% fully diluted equity over 4-year vesting with 1-year cliff.

Questions to ask:
- "What's the current strike price on the options, and when was it last set by 409A/409A-equivalent valuation?"
- "What's the fully diluted share count? I want to understand what this percentage represents in absolute terms."
- "Is there acceleration on acquisition?" (double-trigger is standard; single-trigger is better)
- "Are these EMI or unapproved options for a Swiss resident?"

If the equity grant feels small:
> "Given that the role is at the Senior/Staff level and involves significant platform ownership, I'd expect the equity to reflect that. Can we revisit the grant size? I'm thinking [X shares or Y%] given the scope of the role."

---

## Geographic Positioning

If they raise the UK vs. Switzerland location as a reason to pay less:

> "Geneva is one of the most expensive cities in the world. The cost of living here is higher than London on most measures, so a geographic discount in my direction would actually be a pay cut in real terms. I'd expect the Geneva market rate to be the floor, not a ceiling."

If they insist on UK pay scale and won't budge:
> "If we can't align on base, I'd want to see that reflected in additional equity. The gap between a UK base and a Geneva-market base over a four-year vest compounds meaningfully."

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

If you have other offers in play (or want to create urgency without one):

> "I'm at final stages with a couple of other companies and expect to be making a decision in the next two to three weeks. I want to be straightforward with you: Synthesia is genuinely my first preference for this type of role, but I need the compensation to be in the right range before I can get there. If you can come to CHF [X], I'd be ready to move quickly."

Do not name the competing companies unless pressed. If pressed: "I'd rather keep that confidential for now, but I'm happy to share timelines."

---

## Benefits to Probe

- Remote equipment budget (CHF 1,500-2,500 is standard at growth-stage)
- Learning and conference budget (CHF 2,000-3,000/year)
- Pension/retirement: UK companies rarely contribute to Swiss pension plans; ask how they handle retirement contributions for remote Swiss employees
- Health insurance: Swiss mandatory health insurance is private and expensive (CHF 500-800/month); ask if they subsidize
- Signing bonus if they can't move on base

---

## Red Lines

Walk if:
- Base below CHF 145k with no meaningful equity offset
- No acceleration clause on acquisition (Synthesia is a clear acquisition target)
- They refuse to discuss Swiss-specific benefits (pension, health) at all
