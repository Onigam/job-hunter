# Negotiation Script: Staff Engineer (Core & MLOps), undisclosed company via Jobgether

## What we know from the posting

- Title: Staff Engineer (Core & MLOps). Jobgether is posting for a partner company; the employer isn't named.
- Location: Switzerland, remote-first, flexible hours, freedom to work from a preferred location.
- The company runs "large-scale web data products" with distributed squads. Stack: Java (Vert.x/Netty), Python, Kubernetes, Terraform, Kafka, gRPC, Valkey. Architecture is run through RFDs.
- Salary: not disclosed. Equity: not mentioned.

What that means:

- A fully remote company with a global engineering team often has one compensation band and pays through a local entity or an employer of record. Ask early whether Swiss employees are paid on a Swiss band.
- No equity in the posting doesn't mean there isn't any. Ask directly.
- The role is a strong platform match but asks for reactive Java, Kafka and gRPC depth that isn't core to Magino's background. Expect them to test level. Be ready to defend Staff on scope and architecture track record, not on stack years.

## Step 0: questions for the Jobgether recruiter

1. "Can you share the company name, or at least its size, stage and where it's headquartered?"
2. "What's the budgeted range for this role in Switzerland? Is it a Swiss band or a global one?"
3. "Is the employment contract with a Swiss entity, or through an employer of record?"
4. "Is there equity, and if so, options or RSUs?"
5. "How is on-call compensated?"

Don't give a number first. If pushed: "I'd like to know the range you've budgeted for the role. If it's set for a Staff engineer in Switzerland, I'm confident we'll find something that works."

## Market anchors (estimates, verify before the offer stage)

These are approximate, based on public salary surveys and recruiter data. Re-check on levels.fyi (Zurich/Geneva), Glassdoor, the Michael Page and Hays Switzerland salary guides and Salarium before the first salary conversation.

| Benchmark | Base salary (approx.) |
|---|---|
| Senior Platform / Backend Engineer, Geneva / Lausanne | CHF 140k to 170k |
| Staff Platform / Infrastructure Engineer, Geneva | CHF 180k to 230k |
| Staff / Principal Engineer, Zurich (scale-ups, big tech) | CHF 200k to 260k |
| Remote-first European company, global band, Staff | EUR 120k to 170k (often below Swiss levels) |

Platform and SRE-heavy Staff roles with on-call tend to sit at the upper half of the Geneva range.

**Target:** CHF 195k to 210k base, plus equity if offered and on-call pay.
**Opening anchor:** CHF 220k base.
**Walk-away floor:** CHF 175k base with a Swiss contract. Lower only with meaningful, liquid equity.

## Opening anchor script

"For a Staff role owning core infrastructure, with on-call and cross-squad architecture, Staff engineers in Switzerland typically land between CHF 180k and 230k, and Zurich runs higher. I've owned a platform end to end through an acquisition and spent the last few years as Senior Staff at Benevity setting cross-team direction. I'm looking at a base around CHF 220k, plus equity if it's part of the package. Does that fit your range?"

Then stop talking.

## If they come in low

**Script A: offer in the CHF 140k to 165k range.**
"Thanks, I'm keen on the role. That number reads like a Senior band, though, and the scope you've described, owning the control plane, the golden path and reliability practice across squads, is Staff work. To make this work I'd need the base at CHF 190k or above. If base is truly capped, I'm open to closing part of the gap with equity or a signing bonus."

**Script B: the band is global and isn't adjusted for Switzerland.**
"I get why a remote-first company keeps one band. The trouble is that the same number buys very different things in Geneva and in most of Europe, and as a Swiss resident I pay Swiss rent and Swiss health insurance whatever the company's location. Is there a location adjustment for Switzerland? If not, I'd want the gap closed through equity, a signing bonus and a written compensation review at six months."

**Script C: they push back on level because of stack depth.**
"Fair question. I haven't run Vert.x or Kafka in production, and I won't pretend otherwise. What I've done at Staff level is own platforms and architecture decisions end to end, from Alaya's platform to cross-team design work at Benevity, and that's most of what this role asks for. I'm happy to do a level calibration on system design and on how I'd write the first RFDs for your control plane."

## Geographic pushback (Geneva is expensive)

- Geneva is among the most expensive cities in Europe for housing, and Swiss health insurance is paid by the employee.
- If the contract is Swiss, ask what the employer contributes to the second pillar (LPP). Some companies go well above the legal minimum.
- Ask whether there's a 13th month salary; it changes the comparison. 13 x CHF 15k is CHF 195k, not CHF 180k.
- If it's an employer of record, confirm the contract is still Swiss-law, with LPP and accident insurance included, and that the base isn't reduced to cover EoR fees.

## On-call

The posting includes on-call rotations and leading post-mortems. Ask:

- Is on-call paid as a flat stipend per week, per incident, or both?
- How often does the rotation come round, and how many squads share it?
- Is there time off in lieu after a night incident?

Script: "On-call is fine with me, I've carried production for a platform I built. I'd just like to know how it's compensated and how often the rotation comes round, since that's part of the real package."

## Equity (if offered)

1. **Type:** options or RSUs? For options, ask the strike price, the latest preferred share price and the total fully diluted share count so the percentage can be worked out.
2. **Vesting:** four years with a one-year cliff is standard. Ask whether there's any acceleration on change of control (double trigger is a reasonable ask; Magino has been through an acquisition and knows how this plays out).
3. **Exercise window:** for options, ask for an extended post-termination exercise window (several years rather than 90 days).
4. **Refreshers:** "Are there annual refresh grants at Staff level?"
5. **Anti-dilution:** standard protection usually applies to preferred shareholders, not employees. Ask instead how grants have held up through past rounds and whether top-ups happen after big raises.
6. **Swiss tax:** options in Switzerland are usually taxed at exercise (or at grant in some cases), RSUs at vesting. Check the specifics with a Geneva tax advisor before accepting.

Equity ask: "On equity, I care less about the number of units than what they're likely to be worth. Can you share the latest valuation, the vesting schedule and how refreshers work? For a Staff hire I'd expect a grant worth at least CHF 30k to 50k a year at today's valuation."

## Other levers if base is stuck

- Signing bonus (CHF 20k to 40k) to bridge year one.
- Written six-month review tied to agreed platform milestones.
- Conference and learning budget (the posting mentions conference attendance, so get a number).
- Home office and remote stipend.
- Extra holidays: 30 days is a reasonable ask at Staff level.

## Competing offer template (use only if true)

"To be upfront, I'm in late stages with another company for a Staff platform role in Switzerland, with a base in the CHF 195k to 215k range. This one is my preference because of the scope on the control plane and the RFD culture. If you can get closer to that number, I'm ready to decide quickly."

Never invent a competing offer. Without one, lean on market data: "Based on what I'm seeing for Staff platform roles in Switzerland..."

## Before signing, check

- Employing entity and contract law (Swiss entity or EoR), with LPP and accident insurance.
- Remote from Geneva written into the contract.
- Staff level stated in the offer letter.
- On-call compensation in writing.
- Equity terms, vesting and leaver provisions in writing.
- Notice period and any non-compete (Swiss non-competes must be limited in scope, duration and geography).
