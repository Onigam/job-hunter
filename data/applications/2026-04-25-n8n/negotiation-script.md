# Negotiation Script — n8n / Staff Software Engineer, Core Workflow Engine

---

## Market Context

**Role level:** This is explicitly titled "Staff Software Engineer," which is a distinct band above Senior in most engineering ladders. At n8n's funding stage ($240M raised, $2.5B valuation), the Staff band should command a meaningfully higher base than a standard senior IC.

**n8n's compensation model:** Berlin-based company, pays in EUR for European remote hires. Remote-first across Europe; no local office costs for a Geneva hire.

**Indicated range from job postings:** European engineering ranges at n8n suggest up to approximately €192K at the top of the Staff band. Realistic offer range for a Staff engineer with a founding-to-acquisition background: €140K to €175K base, depending on how aggressively they apply geographic adjustment.

**Geneva vs. Berlin cost-of-living delta:** Geneva consistently ranks 40-60% more expensive than Berlin on housing, taxes, and general living costs. Swiss social contributions add further overhead. A €140K offer calibrated for Berlin is a materially different real-world outcome than €140K in Geneva.

**Switzerland market rate for Staff+ IC with this profile:**
- Staff Platform/Engine Engineer at funded scale-up: CHF 155,000 to 195,000 base + equity
- Founding-engineer background with acquisition validation: adds CHF 10-20K of negotiating leverage
- EUR/CHF rate is approximately 0.95-1.00, so EUR denomination is acceptable; CHF denomination removes any FX drift risk

**n8n equity:** Company is at a late growth stage ($2.5B valuation on $240M raised). Options/ESOP at Staff level typically represent 0.05-0.12% fully diluted at this company size. Given the growth trajectory and potential IPO or strategic exit, this slice is worth negotiating carefully.

## Negotiation Strategy

### Opening Anchor

If n8n asks for salary expectations before making an offer:

"Based on market data for this level in Switzerland, I'm targeting €165K to €175K base, or CHF equivalent. Geneva sits in the same cost-of-living tier as Zurich or London — it's meaningfully different from Berlin — so I'd expect the package to reflect that. I'd also want to discuss a meaningful equity stake given where n8n is in its growth trajectory."

If they push for a single number: "€170K base is where I'd anchor the conversation."

### When They Make an Offer

**If the offer lands at €155K or above:** A reasonable starting point for the Staff band. Counter for the top of the range:

"I appreciate the offer. Given Geneva's cost structure relative to Berlin, I'd want to close the gap to the €165-170K range. Can you move on base, or alternatively, is there flexibility on the equity grant size or vesting terms?"

**If the offer lands between €130K and €155K:** This signals geographic compression and is worth pushing back on directly:

"That's below what I'd expect for Staff level in Switzerland. The cost of living here is significantly higher than Berlin, and the Swiss tax burden at this income is real. I'd need to see base move toward €155-160K minimum to feel the offer is calibrated for Geneva rather than a European average. If there's a ceiling on base, I'm happy to discuss whether equity can bridge the gap."

**If the offer is below €130K:** This is a blanket European discount that doesn't account for the market or the level. Push back firmly:

"To be direct, that's below the range I'd expect for a Staff IC with a full founding-to-acquisition background in Switzerland. The market for this profile here runs CHF 155-195K base. I'd need to see the offer move materially before I could consider it seriously."

## Equity Negotiation

n8n offers equity to all employees. At a $2.5B valuation with significant growth ambition, these options have real potential value on a strategic exit or IPO.

**Questions to ask before signing:**
- "What's the current strike price and the most recent 409A or independent valuation?"
- "What percentage of fully diluted shares does this grant represent?"
- "What's the vesting schedule? Is there a cliff?"
- "Does the agreement include single or double-trigger acceleration on acquisition?"
- "What was the price per share in the most recent primary round, and what was the dilutive effect?"

**What to look for:**
- Standard: 4-year vest, 1-year cliff. Acceptable. Push back on anything beyond 4 years total.
- Acceleration on acquisition: important at n8n's stage given its profile as a potential strategic target.
- The €1K/year learning budget and $100/month OSS stipend are perks. Don't let them substitute for base or equity in the negotiation.

**If base is firm but equity feels light:**
"I'd consider anchoring lower on base if the equity grant is meaningfully larger. What does a competitive Staff-level equity package look like here? I want to be genuinely invested in the outcome, not just the salary."

---

## Geographic Discount Pushback

n8n defaults to Berlin-based compensation bands. Geneva is not a Berlin-equivalent market.

"I understand the rationale for geographic bands. For context, Geneva ranks alongside Zurich and London as one of the most expensive cities in Europe. The Swiss social contribution system and tax rates at this income level are materially different from Germany's. I'd ask you to use Geneva-specific data rather than a European average when calibrating the offer. I'm happy to share cost-of-living comparisons if that's helpful in your process."

---

## Remote Premium Angle

n8n doesn't have a Geneva office. There are no office costs, no local HR overhead, no facilities footprint associated with a Geneva hire. That's a net cost saving relative to a Berlin-based employee.

"You don't carry any local office costs for a Geneva hire. I'd expect that overhead saving to factor into the package calibration rather than being absorbed as margin on a below-market offer."

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

If another offer is in hand:

"I want to be transparent: I have an offer from [Company] at a comparable stage with a base around CHF 160-170K. n8n is the more interesting technical challenge for me, specifically the Core Workflow Engine scope. But I need the package to be close enough that I'm not leaving meaningful comp on the table."

If no competing offer:

"I've been deliberate in this search, which is why I'm at a late stage with only a small number of companies. n8n is at the top of my list for the technical problem. But I want to make sure the package reflects the level before I close off other conversations."

---

## Timing and Closing

Once an offer is verbal, ask for it in writing and take 48 hours before responding. Use that window to:

1. Verify equity details: strike price, grant size as percentage of fully diluted shares, vesting schedule, and acceleration clause
2. Confirm currency denomination (EUR or CHF) and check for any FX adjustment mechanism
3. Review any non-compete or IP assignment clauses under Swiss employment law
4. Confirm start date flexibility and that Swiss work authorization is straightforward (EU passport holders have automatic right to work in Switzerland)

Do not accept verbally before seeing the written offer document.
