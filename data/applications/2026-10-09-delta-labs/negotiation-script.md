# Negotiation Script: Delta Labs, Senior Full Stack Developer (Zurich)

## Context first

Delta Labs AG is a 2021 University of Zurich spin-off. It raised a €4.4M seed round in March 2026, led by Cusp Capital with Auxxo Female Catalyst Fund. The posting lists no salary, only "competitive salary" and "potential equity participation". LinkedIn tags the role Mid-Senior, and it's an IC role with 4+ years required.

Magino's level sits above the posting (17 years, Staff level, co-founder with an exit). At a seed company that usually means one of two outcomes. Either they stretch the band, or the title and scope grow to something like Lead or Founding Engineer. Push for both, and use the title as a lever if the cash won't move.

Seed-stage cash is constrained. With €4.4M in the bank and a team in Zurich, they're unlikely to go far past CHF 150k base for an IC role, so the real negotiation is about base plus equity plus scope.

## Market anchors (Zurich, 2026, CHF gross per year)

| Profile | Typical base range |
|---|---|
| Senior full stack, Zurich scale-up or established company | 120k to 150k |
| Senior full stack, seed-stage startup | 110k to 140k + equity |
| Staff / Lead engineer, Zurich startup | 140k to 170k |
| Staff+ at big tech in Zurich (reference only) | 200k+ total comp |

These are approximate bands built from Swiss salary surveys and recent postings. Check them against current data (levels.fyi Zurich, Glassdoor, swissdevjobs.ch salary pages) before the first call.

**Target:** CHF 150k to 160k base, plus meaningful equity.
**Walk-away floor:** around CHF 135k base, and only if the equity is strong and the title reflects the scope. Below that, the Geneva to Zurich cost outweighs the role.

## Opening anchor

If they ask for expectations before making an offer:

"For a senior role where I'd own features end to end and help shape the platform as it grows, I'm looking at around CHF 155k to 165k base, plus equity. That reflects 17 years of experience, including building a platform from zero through an acquisition, and it's in line with lead-level engineering in Zurich. I'm flexible on how base and equity are split if the equity is real."

Say the number, then stop talking.

## If they come in low (for example CHF 120k to 130k)

"Thanks, I appreciate it, and I'm keen on the role. The base is below what I'd expect for the scope we discussed, especially since I've already built and scaled the kind of multi-tenant, enterprise-facing platform you'll need as Elaiia grows. Could we get the base to CHF 150k? If cash is the constraint at this stage, I'm open to closing part of the gap with more equity, but I'd want that reflected clearly in the grant."

If they say the band is fixed:

"Understood. Then let's look at the other levers: a larger option grant, a Lead or Founding Engineer title that matches the scope, a salary review written into the contract at your next funding round, and travel support for the Geneva to Zurich days."

## Equity

Ask these before agreeing to anything:

1. **Grant size as a percentage of fully diluted shares**, not only a number of options. For an early senior hire at seed, roughly 0.25% to 1% is common; a founding or lead engineer title can justify the upper end.
2. **Instrument:** options, or shares at nominal value under an ESOP. In Switzerland the tax treatment differs a lot depending on whether the benefit is taxed at grant, vesting or exercise. Ask whether they have a tax ruling with the Zurich cantonal authority, since a ruling makes the outcome predictable.
3. **Vesting and cliff:** 4 years with a 1-year cliff is standard. Ask for monthly vesting after the cliff.
4. **Leaver terms:** good leaver and bad leaver definitions, and how long you have to exercise after leaving. A short exercise window can make vested options worthless in practice.
5. **Acceleration:** ask for double-trigger acceleration (on acquisition plus termination). Having been through the Alaya acquisition, Magino can speak to this credibly.
6. **Dilution:** the latest valuation or strike price, and whether there's a refresh policy after future rounds. Full anti-dilution protection is rarely granted to employees, so don't spend capital on it; ask about refresh grants instead.

Script:

"Could you share the grant as a percentage of fully diluted capital, the strike price, and the vesting and leaver terms? I've been on the founder side of an acquisition, so I'd like to understand how the plan behaves in one, especially on acceleration and the exercise window."

## Location: Geneva to Zurich

The team is in Zurich and meets in person regularly. Magino lives in Geneva, and the train takes about 2 hours 45 minutes each way.

Don't accept a discount for not living in Zurich. Zurich and Geneva are both among the most expensive cities in Europe, so neither side has a cost-of-living argument for a lower number.

Script:

"I'm happy to be in Zurich for the in-person days. Since that's a long commute, could we agree on a set rhythm, say one or two days a week, and cover travel with an SBB GA travelcard or a travel budget? That makes the setup sustainable for the long run."

Agree the in-person cadence in writing before signing.

## Competing offer template

Use only if it's true. Never invent an offer.

"I want to be transparent. I'm in late-stage conversations with another company and expect an offer around CHF [X] base. Delta Labs is my first choice because of [specific reason, for example the simulation and evals problem, or the stage]. If we can get closer to [target], I'm ready to commit quickly."

If there's no competing offer, use market data instead:

"Based on what I'm seeing for lead-level roles in Zurich, the range is around CHF 140k to 170k. I'd like us to land in that range."

## Other things to put on the table

The contract should cover a salary review at the next funding round or after 12 months, a learning and conference budget, hardware of your choice, and a pension (LPP/BVG) contribution above the legal minimum. Also confirm the notice period, typically 3 months, and line up the start date with any notice you owe.

## Before signing

Get the following in writing: base, bonus if any, grant percentage with strike price and vesting, title, in-person cadence, travel support, review date, and start date.
