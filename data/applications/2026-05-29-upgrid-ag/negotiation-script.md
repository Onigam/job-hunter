# Negotiation Script — UpGrid AG CTO / Co-Founder

---

## Market Context

**Role type:** Late co-founder CTO at Tenity-backed seed-stage startup, Baar (Zug canton).

**Switzerland CTO market rates (2025-2026):**
- Series A+ startup CTO: CHF 150k-220k base
- Seed-stage startup CTO: CHF 90k-140k + meaningful equity
- Co-founder CTO (very early, cash-constrained): CHF 60k-100k + 8-20% equity

**UpGrid context:** 3,600+ customers, Tenity-backed, founded team of 3 already in place. This is likely post-seed or early seed with limited runway. Expect below-market cash in exchange for meaningful co-founder equity.

**Geneva cost of living note:** Magino is based in Geneva, which has higher living costs than Zug. Use this as a floor anchor: CHF 100k minimum base even at this stage.

---

## Opening Position

**Target package:** CHF 130k-150k base + 8-12% equity (co-founder terms)

**Opening line (first call, when they ask about expectations):**

> "I'm focused on the right fit first, but to give you a calibration point: for a co-founding CTO role I'm thinking CHF 130-150k base, and equity in the 8-12% range given that I'd be joining post-validation but pre-scale. I'm flexible on the cash-equity split depending on where you are with runway."

---

## If They Come In Low on Cash

**Scenario:** They offer CHF 70-80k base.

**Response:**

> "I appreciate the transparency. I want to make this work, so let me think about it as a full package. At CHF 70k I'd need the equity to reflect the difference, so we'd be talking 12-15% rather than 8. Alternatively, if the initial runway is tight, I'm open to a ramp structure: CHF 80k now with a pre-agreed step-up to CHF 120k at the next funding close. That lets you preserve cash while giving me a clear path to market rate."

**Absolute floor:** CHF 80k base with equity above 12%, or CHF 100k base with equity at 8%. Below that the risk-adjusted value doesn't work for Geneva cost of living.

---

## Equity Negotiation

**Target equity:** 8-12% for CTO co-founder joining post-validation, pre-Series A.

**Key terms to push for:**

1. **Vesting:** 4-year vest, 1-year cliff. Standard. If they offer shorter, ask why.
2. **Anti-dilution:** Request weighted-average anti-dilution protection (not full-ratchet, which investors won't accept anyway). This protects your stake if they raise at a lower valuation.
3. **Acceleration on acquisition:** Push for double-trigger acceleration (unvested shares vest if acquired AND you're let go within 12 months). Single-trigger is less common but worth asking.
4. **Option pool:** Confirm whether your percentage is calculated pre or post the option pool reserved for future hires. Post-money percentages after pool creation look smaller. Clarify now.

**Equity script:**

> "On equity, I want to make sure we're aligned on what 10% means in practice: is that 10% of fully diluted shares today, before or after the employee option pool? And what's the current cap table look like at a high level? I don't need exact numbers, just a sense of whether there's been a previous funding round with dilution already baked in."

---

## Geographic Discount Pushback

**If they reference Zug salaries as the benchmark:**

> "I'm comparing this to Geneva market rates rather than Zug, because that's where my cost base is. Geneva and Zug are both expensive, but Geneva runs slightly higher on rent and local taxes. I'd ask that we anchor to a Geneva-competitive number."

---

## Competing Offer Template

*Use if you have another active process or want to create urgency.*

> "I want to be upfront: I'm at a late stage with one other offer at a higher cash number. I'm more interested in what UpGrid is building, but I'd need the total package to be comparable to make the decision easy. If we can get the equity and salary to where I described, I'm ready to move forward without waiting for the other process to close."

---

## Questions to Ask Before Committing

1. What's the current runway, and when is the next funding round targeted?
2. What does the cap table look like today (founders, investors, option pool)?
3. What's the product roadmap for the next 12 months, and what does "done" look like for the platform?
4. How are co-founder decisions made? Is there a shareholders' agreement or term sheet already in place?
5. What happened with the previous technical lead? (Establish why this role is open now.)
6. Is there any existing codebase, or is this a greenfield build?

---

## Red Flags to Watch For

- Equity offered as "percentage of profits" rather than actual shares in the company
- No formal shareholders' agreement in place
- Vague answers about the cap table or funding history
- Pressure to start immediately without a signed term sheet
- Equity percentage calculated after a very large option pool reservation (e.g., 20%+ for future hires, which effectively halves your real stake)
