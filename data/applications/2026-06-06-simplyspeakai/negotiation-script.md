# Negotiation Script — SimplySpeak.ai (Co-Founder / LLM ML Engineer)

## Context

SimplySpeak is pre-seed and explicitly equity-only until a funding round closes. This is a co-founder conversation, not a standard salary negotiation. The variables are: equity percentage, vesting terms, when salary starts, and how the co-founder relationship is structured legally.

---

## Market Context (Zurich / Switzerland)

**Once funded, benchmark salaries for a technical co-founder / CTO in Zurich:**
- Pre-Series A CTO: CHF 120,000-160,000 base
- Series A CTO: CHF 160,000-200,000 base
- Senior Staff / Principal Engineer (IC): CHF 140,000-180,000 base
- These figures reflect Zurich cost of living, which is among the highest in Europe

**Co-founder equity norms at pre-seed:**
- Technical co-founder (sole or primary): 20-40% depending on how many founders and how much was built before you joined
- If another founder is already in place and has done significant pre-work: 10-25% is more realistic
- Standard vesting: 4 years total, 1 year cliff

---

## Opening Anchor (Equity)

Use this when they ask what you're looking for in terms of equity:

> "For a technical co-founder role where I'm owning the entire ML and platform architecture, I'd expect something in the 20-30% range at this stage. That's consistent with what a founding engineer taking this level of risk and responsibility would typically see at pre-seed in the Swiss/European market. I'm flexible on the exact number if the structure is fair, but that's my starting point."

If they push back because another co-founder is already holding a large stake:

> "I understand. If the cap table is already constrained, I'd want to understand the full picture before adjusting my number. What matters to me is that the equity reflects the actual risk and contribution. If I'm building the entire technical foundation from zero, the number needs to reflect that."

---

## Salary Trigger Conversation

This is critical. Equity-only is a significant risk. Pin down exactly when salary starts:

> "I want to make sure we're aligned on the salary timeline. What's the target funding milestone that triggers a salary for founders, and what's the rough expected timeline to get there? I'm fine with equity-only for a defined period, but I'd want that period and the trigger to be written into the agreement."

If they can't give a clear answer:

> "I've been through a pre-seed period before. The most important thing isn't the length, it's having a clear trigger. If the round closes at X, I start drawing Y. If we can put that in writing, I'm comfortable with the equity-only phase."

---

## Equity Negotiation Details

**Vesting schedule (what to ask for):**
- Standard: 4 years, 1 year cliff
- Push for: acceleration on acquisition (double-trigger or single-trigger depending on your risk appetite)
- Double-trigger is the more founder-friendly option for acquisition scenarios

Script if they propose shorter vesting or no cliff:

> "The 4-year / 1-year cliff structure is pretty standard for a reason. It aligns incentives for both sides. A shorter vesting without a cliff exposes both of us to someone leaving early with a large stake, which doesn't serve either party well. I'd prefer to stick with the standard structure."

**Anti-dilution:**
- At pre-seed, most equity agreements include standard dilution (you get diluted as new rounds close, which is normal)
- Ask for: pro-rata rights in future rounds (the right to invest to maintain your percentage)

> "I'd want pro-rata participation rights in future rounds, so I have the option to maintain my ownership percentage if I want to."

---

## Counter-Offer Script (If Equity Offer Comes In Low)

If they offer 8-12% for a co-founder role:

> "I appreciate the offer. I want to be straightforward: for a role where I'm architecting and building the core technical product from zero, I'd expect something closer to 20-25%. The 8-12% range feels more like a very early employee than a technical co-founder. Can we talk about what the full cap table looks like and how you arrived at that number?"

If they push back on the percentage:

> "I'm not trying to be difficult here, I'm trying to make sure the structure reflects the actual contribution and risk. What would 20% look like for you, and what would need to be different about the arrangement for that to be possible?"

---

## Geographic and Risk Conversation

Zurich is expensive. Even equity-only for 6-12 months is a meaningful financial commitment:

> "I want to be transparent: taking equity-only is a real financial commitment from my side, especially in Zurich. I'm willing to do it if the equity stake is meaningful and the path to salary is clear. If the equity number is on the lower end, that changes my calculus on the risk I'm taking."

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

If you have or expect other offers:

> "I'm in conversations with a few other opportunities, some of which involve a salary from day one. I'm genuinely interested in what you're building, which is why I'm here. But I'd need the equity and structure to reflect that I'm choosing to take the early-stage risk here rather than a more certain path."

---

## Red Flags to Watch For

- No clear funding timeline or milestone for salary trigger
- Equity offer below 10% for a "co-founder" title
- No standard vesting schedule proposed
- Reluctance to formalize agreements in writing
- Vague answers about the existing cap table

If any of these come up, slow down before committing.
