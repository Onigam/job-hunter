# Negotiation Script: Productboard, Staff Backend Product Engineer

## The shape of this offer

The posting covers Prague, Brno or remote within the EU, and the salary is published in CZK: 2,295,000 to 3,305,000 a year. Productboard also published how current employees are spread across the band, which helps a lot:

| Quartile | Range (CZK) | Share of people |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | 2.30M to 2.55M | 33% |
| Q2 | 2.55M to 2.80M | 27% |
| Q3 | 2.80M to 3.05M | 33% |
| Q4 | 3.05M to 3.30M | 7% |

Only about 7% of people sit in the top quartile. Asking for Q4 is credible for someone with founder plus Staff experience, but expect real pushback, so anchor at the top and plan to land somewhere in Q3 to low Q4.

Two structural issues come before any number:

1. **Switzerland isn't in the EU.** "Remote EU" may not cover a Geneva resident. Ask early whether they can employ through an employer of record in Switzerland, or contract with a Swiss entity. If the answer is no, the only path is relocating to Prague or Brno, and the relocation bonus (USD 6k to 13k for EU-authorised candidates) comes into play. Confirm that Magino's nationality or residence permit counts as EU-authorised.
2. **The band is built for the Czech market.** At current rates (check them on the day), 3.3M CZK is roughly €130k to €135k, or around CHF 125k. The bottom of the band is about CHF 85k to 90k. That's well below a Geneva Staff base, so this offer only works financially with relocation to Czechia, a meaningful equity grant, or a remote arrangement that gets them to stretch the top of the band.

## Market anchors

Rough base-salary ranges for Staff-level backend/platform engineers. Verify on levels.fyi, Glassdoor CH/CZ and with recruiters before the call.

| Market | Staff base (approx.) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Geneva / Lausanne | CHF 170k to 220k | Few true Staff roles; fintech and international orgs push the top end |
| Zurich | CHF 180k to 240k | Big tech offices sit above this with RSUs |
| Prague / Brno, scale-ups | CZK 2.2M to 3.3M | Productboard's band sits at the top of the local market |
| EU remote, US-headquartered | €120k to 170k | The comparison set for "remote EU" roles at US-funded companies |

Cost of living is the honest point to make. 3.3M CZK in Prague goes a long way. The same figure paid to someone living in Geneva doesn't.

## Equity

Productboard is a late-stage, VC-backed company (Index, Kleiner Perkins, Sequoia) whose last priced round valued it above $1B. Verify the latest round and any down-round or secondary before the call. Employees in Europe normally get options on the US parent company. Questions to ask, in this order:

1. How many options, and what percentage of fully diluted shares does that represent?
2. What's the strike price, and how does it compare with the preferred price from the last round? A strike set at a 2021 peak valuation can mean the options are underwater.
3. What's the vesting schedule? Expect 4 years with a 1-year cliff. Ask whether monthly vesting starts after the cliff.
4. What's the post-termination exercise window? The default is often 90 days, which forces a large cash outlay on leaving. Ask for an extended window (5 to 10 years). Many late-stage companies have already moved to that.
5. Is there a refresh grant policy, and have there been tender offers or secondaries for employees?
6. How does tax work for a Swiss (or Czech) resident on exercise and sale? Get the answer in writing, or ask a Swiss tax adviser.

Anti-dilution protection is for preferred investors, not common-stock option holders, so don't spend capital asking for it. Push on grant size, strike price and exercise window instead.

## Scripts

### When they ask for expectations first

> "I've seen the published band, and thanks for sharing the quartile spread, it's useful. Given founder experience taking a platform to acquisition plus Staff scope at Benevity, I'd expect to be at the top of it, 3.3M CZK. I'm in Geneva, so the number has to work against a Swiss cost base, and I'm flexible on how equity fits around it."

### If they come in low (Q1 or Q2, say 2.5M to 2.8M)

> "Thanks, I appreciate the offer. It's below where I need to be. The posting describes this role as setting agent-native standards across the backend and owning the incident and code-review agent work, which is Staff scope, and Q2 reads more like senior. Can we look at 3.2M to 3.3M? If base really is capped, I'd want to make up the gap in the option grant."

### Pushing back on a location discount

> "I understand the band is set for the Czech market. My point is the opposite one: if I'm remote from Geneva, my costs are Swiss, and I'm already accepting a large discount against the local Staff market, which starts around CHF 170k. Paying from the top of the band is what makes that discount workable for me."

### If base is fixed, trade on other levers

> "If base can't move beyond 3.1M, could we look at a larger initial grant, an extended exercise window, and a six-month comp review written into the offer?"

### Competing-offer template (only use with a real process in play)

> "To be transparent, I'm in late stages with another company for a Staff role at around CHF [X] base. Productboard is my preference because of the agent architecture work and the Kotlin migration. I'd rather make this work, and getting closer to [number] would make it an easy decision."

Never invent a competing offer. If there isn't one, use the Geneva market range instead, which is real and easy to check.

## Before signing

Get in writing: the employment entity and contract type (Swiss EOR, Czech contract, or contractor), base and currency, the full equity terms (grant, strike, vesting, exercise window), relocation amount and clawback conditions, and the remote-work policy, including any travel expected to Prague or Brno.
