# Negotiation Script - Multiverse, Staff AI Engineer (AI Transformation)

## Context

- Growth-stage, VC-backed EdTech company, London HQ, just closed $70M at a $2.1B valuation (Schroders Capital, StepStone, Lightspeed, General Catalyst). Not an early-stage cash-strapped startup, so there's real room in the comp structure even if the base band looks conservative on paper.
- Role is a permanent contract with remote allowed; Magino would work remote from Geneva rather than relocate to London.
- Public salary estimates for this listing (Glassdoor, ~£56K-70K) clearly track a mid-level engineer band, not a Staff role leading an integration and standards squad. That number should be treated as noise, not an anchor, and it's worth saying so directly if it comes up.
- This is a base-plus-equity negotiation, not a pure equity bet: the company is well past seed stage, so options are a real instrument, but not a founder-level dilution play.

## Market anchor (Geneva-based remote, Staff AI/Platform Engineer, UK-headquartered company)

UK Staff-level AI or platform engineers in London typically land between GBP 110,000 and 160,000 base at a company this size, and Geneva-based candidates working remote for a UK or international employer at Staff scope usually see CHF 160,000 to CHF 195,000 when the employer benchmarks to the market the person actually lives in rather than discounting to the hiring market. Given Magino's founding-engineer exit and the fact this role is effectively building a new function from scratch, the reasonable opening anchor sits at CHF 175,000 to CHF 195,000 base, plus equity, with the explicit ask that comp be benchmarked to Geneva rather than converted from a London figure.

## Opening anchor script

"Before we talk numbers, I want to understand how you benchmark comp for a remote hire based in Geneva. Some companies convert from the London band, others benchmark to where the person actually lives, and those land in very different places. Given the scope here, building the integration layer and setting standards the rest of the org will eventually follow, I'd expect a Geneva-benchmarked base in the CHF 175,000 to 195,000 range, plus equity. I'm happy to hear how you've structured this for other remote Staff hires before I anchor any harder."

## Counter-offer script if they come in low

"I get that the reference point you're working from is probably the London band, but Geneva runs meaningfully higher on both cost of living and market rate for this level of scope, and I'd rather get that right now than revisit it in six months. If CHF 175,000 isn't reachable at the outset, I'd want to know what closes the gap: a larger initial equity grant, a faster comp review at six months instead of twelve, or a signing bonus that covers the difference through year one while the band gets confirmed."

## Equity notes

- At a $2.1B valuation with institutional investors like Schroders and General Catalyst on the cap table, this is very likely RSUs or options priced closer to a later-stage strike than an early-stage one; ask directly for the current 409A or UK equivalent valuation basis and how many shares outstanding it's calculated against, since the headline grant number means little without that context.
- Confirm vesting: standard is four years with a one-year cliff. At this stage, ask whether there's a refresh cycle after year one, since a single up-front grant quietly loses relative value over a multi-year tenure at a company still growing into its valuation.
- Ask what liquidity actually looks like given the company is private and well past the point where an exit timeline is purely theoretical: is there a tender offer history, secondary market access, or stated IPO horizon.

## Geographic and remote-arrangement pushback

"I want to flag early that I'm not looking for a discount because I'm remote. The scope of this role doesn't change based on where I'm sitting, and Geneva's market rate for Staff-level engineering is on par with or above London's. If there's a standard remote-adjustment policy, I'd want to understand it now rather than find out it's baked into the offer."

## Competing options template

"I'm not actively interviewing elsewhere right now, but I get inbound fairly regularly for Staff and Principal infrastructure roles, and I'm being selective on purpose. What makes this one worth prioritizing is the actual mandate, not just the comp, so I'd rather get the number right upfront than have it become a reason to keep looking."

Use only if genuinely true; don't invent a specific competing offer or figure if asked to name one.
