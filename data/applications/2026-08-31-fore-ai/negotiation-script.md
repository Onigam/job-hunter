# Negotiation Script, Fore ai (Software Engineer, Infrastructure & Scaling, Zurich)

Context: early-stage, VC-backed startup founded by ex-Google engineers, serving enterprise clients in banking, travel, software, and media. The posting is scoped mid-level to senior, which sits below Magino's actual level (17+ years, Senior Staff / cofounder background), so the first job in this negotiation is getting the scope and title reset before talking numbers, not just negotiating within their posted band. Role is based in Zurich; Magino is Geneva-based, so relocation or a remote/hybrid arrangement needs to be resolved early, since it affects both the base anchor and the geographic discount conversation.

## Market anchors (Zurich, senior infra/platform engineer, early-stage startup)

- Senior software/infra engineers at Zurich startups with real institutional clients (as opposed to pre-revenue) typically land CHF 130k-165k base, with the top of that range going to candidates who've owned platform or scaling decisions end to end, not just executed on them.
- Given Magino's cofounder and Senior Staff background, the right anchor is the top of that band or above it, CHF 155k-175k, framed explicitly as a senior/staff-scope hire rather than the mid-level range the posting implies.
- Total comp at this stage of company is usually base plus meaningful equity rather than a large cash bonus. Expect equity to matter more here than at an established company like Benevity or SonarSource.
- Zurich cost of living is on par with Geneva, so there's no legitimate geographic discount argument in either direction, only the relocation or remote-work question, which is separate from pay level.

## Opening position

Before naming a number, get clarity on two things: whether the role is actually scoped at the level the posting suggests or whether there's room to reset it given the experience, and what the equity band looks like for someone coming in above the posted level.

Opening anchor: "Given the scope of owning core infrastructure for an agentic AI platform serving banking and enterprise clients, and that my background is closer to Staff-level platform ownership than the mid-level range in the posting, I'd expect a base in the CHF 155k-170k range, plus an equity grant that reflects a senior hire rather than an early-career one. I built and scaled Alaya's platform from zero through an acquisition, so this isn't a stretch relative to what the role needs."

## If they push back on level or come in low on base

Script: "I hear that the posted band is lower than that. Given the infra and scaling ownership this role actually requires, plus the fact that I've already done this exact kind of work, first at Alaya and then across a much larger microservices org at Benevity, I'd want to understand what's driving the gap. If the base has a hard ceiling at this stage, I'd want to talk about a larger equity grant or a signing bonus to close the difference."

## Equity negotiation (startup-specific)

Ask for the specifics before evaluating any number: strike price, vesting schedule (standard is four years with a one-year cliff), total shares outstanding or at least the percentage the grant represents, and whether there's any anti-dilution protection for early hires. Script: "Before I can weigh the equity piece, I'd need the percentage or share count relative to fully diluted shares, the vesting and cliff terms, and where the company is on runway and next raise. A number without that context doesn't mean much."

## Geographic and remote-work discussion

Since Zurich and Geneva are comparably expensive, there's no discount to push back on here, but the commute or relocation question needs a direct answer early: "I'm Geneva-based currently. Before we get further into comp, I want to understand whether this role expects full relocation to Zurich, a hybrid arrangement, or something else, since that shapes how I think about the offer as a whole."

## Competing offer as counterweight (use only if real)

"I'm also in conversation for a Staff-level platform role in the CHF 160k-185k base range at a more established company. I'm genuinely drawn to the infra problem here, agentic AI test execution at real enterprise scale is a harder and more interesting problem than most, but the base and equity together need to be competitive with that for the trade-off to make sense."

## Notice period and start date

Standard Swiss notice periods run one to three months depending on tenure and contract. Raise this in the opening call so it doesn't surface as a surprise once an offer is written up.

## Close

Ask for the offer in writing with base, equity terms (strike price, vesting, cliff, share count), and any benefits spelled out before accepting anything verbally. If the level question stays unresolved, push for a clear answer on title and scope before signing, since a mid-level title attached to senior-scope work tends to cap future comp conversations at this company.
