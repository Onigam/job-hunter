# Negotiation Script: Alohi SA, Head of Engineering

## Context

Alohi is bootstrapped and financially independent, incubated early by Fongit, roughly 29 employees, four million customers across Sign.Plus, Fax.Plus, Dial.Plus and Scan.Plus. No VC funding means no comp compression from an investor-driven burn rate, but it also means there's likely no traditional equity pool the way a venture-backed startup would run one. This is an in-office role in Plan-les-Ouates. No salary band was published on the listing, so the first job in this conversation is getting a number on the table before reacting to one.

Magino brings 17+ years, a founding-engineer exit (Alaya, acquired by Benevity, a Canadian unicorn), and direct experience hiring, coaching and scaling an engineering team from zero, which maps closely to what a 29-person company needs from its first real Head of Engineering.

## Market anchor (Geneva, Head of Engineering / Engineering Director, small-to-mid company)

Geneva engineering leadership comp for a role owning a 15-25 person engineering org across multiple products typically lands between CHF 170,000 and CHF 210,000 base, with larger corporate benchmarks (Engineering Director averages around CHF 213,000, VP Engineering higher still in Zurich) running above what a bootstrapped 29-person company can realistically offer. A fair target here sits around CHF 175,000 to CHF 200,000 base, reflecting both Geneva cost of living and the fact that this is a self-funded company without a large payroll budget behind it. Given no equity pool is guaranteed, base salary should carry most of the negotiation weight, with any profit-share or bonus structure treated as a genuine bonus, not a substitute for market-rate base pay.

## Opening anchor script

"Before we talk numbers, can you walk me through how comp is structured here, since the listing doesn't include a band? Given the scope, leading engineering across backend, frontend, mobile and AI for four live products, plus hiring and coaching a team through its next stage, I'd expect a Geneva base in the CHF 175,000 to 200,000 range. I'm also curious how you think about bonus or profit-share given the company is self-funded, since that usually replaces the equity conversation at a bootstrapped company."

## Counter-offer script if they come in low

"I understand a company that's never raised outside money runs a tighter comp model than a funded startup, and I respect that discipline, it's part of what makes this role interesting. That said, CHF 175,000 is close to the floor for a Head of Engineering role with this scope in Geneva specifically. If the base can't move much right now, I'd want to talk about a defined review point at six months tied to concrete milestones, plus whatever profit-share or bonus mechanism exists, so the gap closes on a timeline rather than staying open indefinitely."

## Equity and bonus notes

Since Alohi has stayed bootstrapped, there is likely no standard stock option plan. Ask directly whether any equity, phantom equity or profit-share scheme exists for leadership hires, and if so, get the mechanics in writing: vesting or accrual period, what triggers a payout (acquisition, annual profit distribution, both), and whether it's been offered to any current leadership hire as a precedent. If nothing exists yet, that's not necessarily a dealbreaker, but it means the base salary conversation needs to fully account for the absence of upside, and a signing or retention bonus becomes a reasonable ask in its place.

## Geographic and market pushback

"Geneva is one of the more expensive cities in Europe for engineering talent, and this role is in-office at Plan-les-Ouates, so there's no remote-from-a-cheaper-city offset to factor in. I'd want any offer benchmarked against what Geneva-based leadership hires actually cost, not a national or European average."

## Competing offer template

"I'm currently in a Senior Staff role at Benevity with cross-team architecture scope, so I'm not under pressure to move quickly, and I'm having a couple of other conversations in parallel. What makes this one worth prioritizing is the chance to run engineering end to end again, the way I did at Alaya, rather than the comp alone."

Use only if genuinely true at the time of the conversation; don't reference a specific competing offer or figure that doesn't exist.
