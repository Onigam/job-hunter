# Negotiation Script: Staff Fullstack Engineer, Applied AI, Aleph

## Context to keep in mind

Aleph is an AI-native FP&A platform backed by Khosla Ventures and Y Combinator, with real enterprise logos (Webflow, Turo, Notion), so this isn't pre-seed scrappiness, it's a funded company with published bands: $185K to $240K in the US, CAD $150K to $190K in Canada, $109K to $155K in LATAM, plus equity, unlimited PTO, health insurance and a 401(k) for US-based hires. The posting is explicitly remote-first across the Americas with a preference for time zone overlap, and I'm in Geneva, so the first real negotiation isn't comp, it's whether they'll even consider a Europe-based hire and how that maps onto a band built for US, Canada and LATAM. That has to get resolved before a number means anything.

## Switzerland market anchors (keep in your pocket, don't lead with them)

Geneva isn't the natural anchor for a US-banded remote role, but it matters as a floor if they're open to a Europe-based arrangement. A Staff-level fullstack engineer in Geneva with founding and platform-scale experience typically sits in the CHF 160K to 190K range, and the Alaya-to-acquisition track record pushes toward the top of that or above it. If Aleph wants Americas-hours overlap from someone living in a high cost-of-living market, that's worth naming directly, since paying a discounted "remote-anywhere" rate for someone doing US-hours work from Switzerland ignores both the cost of living and the overlap they're asking for.

## Opening move: clarify scope before anchoring on a number

"Before we get to comp, I want to understand how a Geneva-based hire fits the Americas-remote structure. Is the expectation US business hours overlap, and does the posted band apply, or is there a separate structure for hires outside the Americas?"

## Opening anchor, once scope is clear

If they confirm the US band applies and overlap is workable: "Given the scope, 8-plus years of building and owning systems end to end, and the platform and applied-AI experience, I'd be looking at the upper half of your posted range, call it $220K to $240K base, plus a standard equity grant for the level."

## If they come in low on cash

"I understand the band is fixed and I don't want to force a number that doesn't fit your structure. If base has to land lower in the range, I'd want that reflected in the equity grant or in a defined comp review tied to a milestone, say six months in, rather than treating the opening number as final."

## Equity questions

Ask for the strike price, the last preferred round valuation, and the standard vesting schedule (one-year cliff, four-year vest is typical) rather than accepting a bare percentage or share count with no context. Ask whether there's double-trigger acceleration on acquisition, which is a reasonable thing to want given how the Alaya story actually ended. Ask how the option pool was sized relative to the last round, since that tells you whether the grant on offer is generous or already diluted before it's even issued.

## Geographic and remote leverage

If Aleph pushes back on hiring outside the Americas at all, that's useful information early rather than late: "I want to be upfront that relocating isn't something I'm planning on, and I understand that might not fit how you've structured the team. If there's flexibility on that, I'm glad to talk specifics; if not, better to know now."

## Competing offer template

Use only if a real competing process exists. Never invent a number or company name.

"I'm also further along in conversations for a comparable staff-level role with better geographic fit for me. Aleph is genuinely the more interesting problem, so if there's room on base or equity to close that gap, this is where I'd want to know before deciding."
