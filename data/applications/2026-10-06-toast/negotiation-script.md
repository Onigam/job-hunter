# Negotiation Script: Toast, Principal Software Engineer, AI Foundations

## The shape of this offer

- **Location:** Dublin, Ireland, hybrid, at least 2 days a week in the office. This isn't a remote-from-Geneva role, so the real question is relocation.
- **Salary:** not published on the posting. Ask the recruiter for the band on the first call. Toast is US-listed and generally shares bands when asked.
- **Company:** Toast (NYSE: TOST) is public, so equity means RSUs at market value, not startup options. That makes it far easier to value than a private-company grant.
- **Level:** Principal is above Staff at Toast. Confirm the internal level name and where it sits on their ladder, since that decides the band.

Two things to settle before talking numbers:

1. **Relocation and work authorisation.** With an EU passport, Magino can work in Ireland with no permit. If not, the role would need a Critical Skills Employment Permit, which Toast would have to sponsor. Ask what relocation package Toast offers for Dublin hires (lump sum, temporary housing, flights, tax gross-up).
2. **Remote flexibility.** Ask once, directly, whether the role could be based from Geneva with regular travel to Dublin. If the answer is no, drop it and negotiate the relocation package instead.

## Market anchors

Approximate figures. Verify on levels.fyi (Toast, Dublin, and Principal at comparable US companies in Dublin), Glassdoor and with recruiters before the call.

| Market | Principal / Senior Staff base (approx.) | Total comp notes |
|---|---|---|
| Geneva / Lausanne | CHF 180k to 230k | Few true Principal roles; equity usually small |
| Zurich, big tech | CHF 200k to 260k | RSUs often add CHF 80k to 200k+ a year |
| Dublin, US tech companies | €150k to 200k | RSUs often add €50k to 150k a year at Principal |
| Dublin, local and smaller firms | €110k to 150k | Little or no equity |

What this means in practice: a Dublin base will look lower than Geneva on paper. Dublin rents are high, but overall living costs are still below Geneva, and Irish tax is heavy above about €44k (40% marginal rate plus USC and PRSI). Compare net take-home and total comp, not headline base.

Make the case for the top of the band by tying it to the posting, not to Geneva prices. The role asks for full-stack architecture, platform leadership, mentoring and hands-on agent and MCP work. Magino covers all of it, with a founder exit and AI adoption results on top.

## Scripts

### When asked for salary expectations (before they share a band)

> "I'd rather understand the band for this level first, since Principal means different things at different companies. Could you share the range you've got for the role in Dublin? I'm sure we can find something that works once I know where it sits."

If they push:

> "For a Principal role at a US-listed company in Dublin, with relocation from Geneva, I'm looking at total comp around €300k a year, with base around €200k. I'm flexible on how that splits between base and equity."

### If the offer comes in low

> "Thanks, I appreciate it, and I'm keen on the role. The base is below what I'm seeing for Principal at comparable US companies in Dublin, and it's a significant step down from Geneva. This role covers architecture for the proxy, key management, observability and the plugin marketplace, plus mentoring across the team. I've built a platform from zero to acquisition and led AI tooling adoption across two teams at Benevity. Could you get the base to €195k? If base is fixed, I'd want to see that gap made up in the RSU grant."

### Pushing back on a location discount

> "I understand the band is set for Dublin, and I'm not asking for Geneva pay. What I'm asking for is the top of the Dublin band, because the posting asks for the full scope of a Principal. The relocation is a real cost on my side, so I'd want the package to cover it properly."

### Using a competing offer (real or in process)

> "Toast is my first choice, so I want to be upfront. I'm in late stages with another company at around [X] total comp. I'd much rather make this work with you. Is there room to close the gap, either in base or in the RSU grant?"

Only use this if the other process is real. Don't invent numbers; recruiters at public companies often ask for the written offer.

## Equity (RSUs at a public company)

Toast is public, so the questions are different from a startup's:

1. **Grant value and how it's converted to shares.** Is the grant set in USD and converted at a 30-day average price, or at the price on the grant date? It makes a real difference when the stock is volatile.
2. **Vesting schedule.** Ask for the exact schedule: is there a 1-year cliff, and does it vest quarterly or annually after that? Some companies front-load or back-load vesting.
3. **Refresh grants.** Ask how annual refreshers work at Principal level and what a typical refresh looks like. Without refreshers, total comp drops sharply in year 5.
4. **Sign-on to bridge the cliff.** If there's a 1-year cliff, ask for a cash sign-on bonus to cover the first year, especially with relocation costs.
5. **Irish tax on RSUs.** RSUs are taxed as income at vest in Ireland (income tax, USC and PRSI), and the employee generally has to pay within 30 days. Ask whether Toast uses sell-to-cover.
6. **ESPP.** Ask whether Toast's employee stock purchase plan is open to Irish employees and on what terms.

## Relocation checklist

- Lump sum or managed relocation, and is it grossed up for tax?
- Temporary housing in Dublin (aim for 4 to 8 weeks; the rental market is tight)
- Flights and shipping of belongings
- Clawback terms if leaving within 12 to 24 months. Try to get this prorated.
- Support with Irish tax registration (PPS number, Revenue) and any permit paperwork
- A tax advisor session, since moving from Switzerland to Ireland mid-year affects both countries

## Order of the conversation

1. Confirm the level and the band.
2. Confirm location rules and the relocation package.
3. Negotiate base.
4. Negotiate the RSU grant and refresh policy.
5. Use sign-on to close any remaining gap.
6. Get everything in writing before you resign or commit to moving.
