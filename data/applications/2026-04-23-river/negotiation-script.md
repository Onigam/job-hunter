# Negotiation Script — River Staff Software Engineer (ML/AI)

---

## Market Context

**Posted range:** $200K–$250K USD + equity (remote, US, Europe, South America listed)

**Geneva market anchor (Staff+ / Principal Engineer):**
- Geneva Staff Engineer market: CHF 180K–230K base (cash)
- At current USD/CHF rate (~0.90), $250K ≈ CHF 225K — the top of River's range aligns with Geneva senior rates
- But Magino's profile (17+ years, cofounder, built-and-sold platform at $1B+ valuation) justifies pushing above posted range

**Target outcome:** $245K–$265K USD base, meaningful equity grant, full remote confirmed

---

## Opening Anchor

If asked for salary expectations before an offer:

> "Based on my research and what I've seen at staff-level roles in fintech and ML, I'd be looking at something in the $250K–$270K range for base, depending on how equity is structured. I'm flexible on the exact split if the total comp picture makes sense."

Frame it around total comp, not just base. River is a Bitcoin company — their equity may be worth more than it looks on paper if they're profitable and growing.

---

## Offer Scenarios and Counter-Scripts

### Scenario A: They offer $200K (bottom of range)

> "I appreciate the offer and I'm genuinely interested in the role. Given the scope of what I'd be stepping into and the experience I'm bringing — built and exited a platform at $1B+ valuation, 17 years in production systems, applied ML work in production — I was expecting something closer to $240K–$250K. Is there flexibility in the base, or could we make up some of the difference in equity?"

Hold on $240K as the floor. Don't accept $200K without significant equity compensation.

### Scenario B: They offer $225K (mid-range)

> "That's closer to where I was thinking. I'd want to get to $240K to feel fully aligned. If base is constrained, I'm open to a larger initial equity grant or an earlier performance review at 6 months. What's possible on your end?"

This is a reasonable negotiation — the gap is small. Push for the equity lever if they can't move base.

### Scenario C: They offer $250K (top of range)

Accept or use as a launching pad for equity:

> "That works for me on base. I'd like to make sure we land the equity piece well, too — what does the vesting structure look like, and is there flexibility on the initial grant size?"

---

## Equity Negotiation (Bitcoin Startup Specifics)

River is a profitable, bootstrapped-feeling company ($52.7M raised, custody of 25,000+ BTC). Their equity may be in stock options or RSUs.

**Questions to ask:**

- "What's the current 409A valuation, and when was the last mark?"
- "What's the vesting schedule? 4-year with 1-year cliff is standard — any accelerated vesting on acquisition?"
- "Are these ISOs or NSOs? For a Geneva-based employee, the tax treatment differs significantly."
- "Is there a secondary market or buyback program for early exercise?"

**Typical ask for Staff level:** 0.05%–0.15% of the company in options, depending on stage. At $52.7M raised, push toward the higher end.

**Cliff negotiation:** If they offer a standard 4-year/1-year cliff, you can ask for a 6-month cliff given seniority:

> "Given the level we're talking about, would you be open to a 6-month cliff rather than 12? That signals mutual commitment without a full year of exposure on either side."

## Geographic Considerations (Geneva)

If they try to apply a geographic discount:

> "I'm seeing that the listed range includes remote from Europe. Geneva is one of the more expensive cities to live in globally — cost of living here is comparable to San Francisco or New York, not a lower-cost market. I'd want to stay within the posted USD range regardless of location."

Don't accept a geographic discount on the USD rate. The role is posted as remote including Europe, so the full range should apply.

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

If you have another offer in process (or want to create urgency):

> "I'm in late stages with one other company. I'd prefer to go with River if we can align on comp — the problem space here is more compelling. Is there a timeline on your side for closing this?"

Don't fabricate specifics. Use this framing only if you have real alternatives in process.

## Benefits to Confirm

- Full remote confirmed (Europe timezone respected — no expectation of US overlap past 6pm Geneva)
- Health coverage (confirm if it applies for Geneva-based employees or if it's US-only)
- Equity refresh policy at annual reviews
- Conference / learning budget
- Equipment stipend (remote setup)

## Walk-Away Line

Below $215K USD base with no meaningful equity, or with a geographic discount applied to the posted range, is not worth accepting. The role is interesting but the market supports better.
