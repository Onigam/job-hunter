# Negotiation Script: Qonto, Staff Applied AI Engineer (AI Compliance Tooling)

## Read this first: the location changes the whole negotiation

The posting is fully remote **within France, Germany, Spain or Italy**. Switzerland isn't on the list, so the contract will almost certainly be a French (or other EU) contract paid in EUR, on Qonto's European bands. You live in Geneva. Settle these before any number comes up:

1. **Can Qonto employ you at all?** Ask the recruiter directly in the first call: "I live in Geneva. Is a Swiss-resident hire possible, or does this need French residency?" If French residency is required, decide whether moving across the border (Pays de Gex, Annemasse area) is realistic for you before going further.
2. **Tax and social security.** A French contract while living in Switzerland, or a Swiss-resident cross-border setup, has real tax and social-security consequences, and the favourable BSPCE tax regime assumes French tax residency. Get one hour with a cross-border tax advisor before accepting anything.
3. **Comparison basis.** Swiss anchors are below for reference, but Qonto won't pay Geneva rates for a role it has scoped to EU countries. The real comparison is net take-home and cost of living wherever you'd actually live.

**Targets (EUR, French contract)**
- Opening anchor: **€145k base** + meaningful BSPCE grant
- Realistic landing: **€125k-140k base**
- Walk-away: decide this only after the tax/residency check. On a French contract a number like €120k may look fine on paper but be well below what you'd net in Geneva at CHF 170k+; work out your own after-tax floor first.

Why you can push toward the top of the band: the posting combines Staff backend system design with production agentic AI, and there are only two more of these hires planned. That profile is scarce, and the team has no PM, so the role also absorbs product scoping.

## Opening anchor script

> "From what I've seen for Staff engineers working on production AI systems at European scale-ups, I'm looking at around €145k base, plus an equity grant that reflects a Staff-level hire. I'm happy to talk about how the BSPCE side is structured before we settle on the split."

If they ask for your current salary:

> "I'd rather anchor on the role. It's Staff scope, backend design plus production agents, owned end to end without a PM, so I'm benchmarking against Staff AI roles rather than my current package, which is in Swiss francs and doesn't translate cleanly anyway."

## If they come in low

> "Thanks, I appreciate you sharing it. It's below what I've seen for comparable scope. This role asks for someone who has shipped agents in production and can also own the backend foundations and the stakeholder work, since there's no PM. Could we get the base to €140k? If the band for this level is fixed, I'd want to understand the band itself, or look at a larger BSPCE grant to close the gap."

If they say the band is fixed:

> "Understood. Then let's look at the level. If the expectations are Staff, with technical direction for the team, the offer should sit at the top of that band, not the middle. What would it take to get there?"

## Pushing back on a location or currency discount

This is unusual: you're the one coming from a higher-cost market, and the role isn't open to Switzerland, so don't argue Geneva rates directly. Argue scope.

> "I'm not asking for Swiss pay. I'm asking to be paid at the top of your Staff band, because the scope is Staff plus product ownership. Where I live shouldn't push that number down, since the posting already treats the role as fully remote."

If they suggest a lower band for a cross-border or non-Paris setup:

> "The role is fully remote across four countries, so I'd expect one band for the level. Is there a reason a remote Staff hire would be paid differently from someone in Paris?"

## Equity (BSPCE): what to ask

Qonto is a late-stage, profitable company (since 2023), so the equity is a long-term bet, not a lottery ticket. French employees often undervalue BSPCE, but a grant that's just "X BSPCE" with no context tells you nothing. Ask for:

- Number of BSPCE and the **strike price**
- **Total fully diluted share count**, so you can work out your percentage
- The latest valuation basis used for the strike price, and how it compares to the last priced round
- **Vesting schedule and cliff** (commonly 4 years with a 1-year cliff; confirm)
- **Exercise window after leaving.** A short window can force you to pay cash or lose vested options
- Any **secondary or liquidity programme** for employees, past or planned
- **Acceleration** on change of control (single or double trigger)
- Whether a refresh grant policy exists for Staff-level engineers
- How BSPCE is taxed if you're **not a French tax resident** (check with your advisor; the favourable regime depends on conditions including the three-year holding period)

Script:

> "Before I value the equity, could you share the number of BSPCE, the strike price, the fully diluted share count and the post-departure exercise window? Without those I can't compare it to cash, and I'd rather not trade base for something I can't price."

## Competing offer template (use only if it's true)

> "I want to be upfront: I'm in late stages with another company, and their offer is at [amount] base with [equity]. Qonto is my first choice, because the AFC agent work is exactly the kind of production AI problem I want to own. If you can get to [target], I'm ready to accept this week."

Never invent an offer. If you don't have one, use the timeline instead:

> "I'm in conversations elsewhere and expect to make a decision in the next two to three weeks. I'd like Qonto to be part of that decision."

## Other levers if base is stuck

- Signing bonus to cover the gap in year one
- Equipment and home-office budget, and coworking costs if needed
- Travel budget to Paris for team time
- Start date and notice period flexibility
- Title confirmed as Staff in writing, with a clear path to Principal
- Learning budget, including AFC or compliance training to shorten your ramp-up

## Closing

> "Thanks, this is close. If we can get to [final number] and confirm the BSPCE details in writing, I'm ready to sign."
