# Negotiation Script — Lakera, Senior Product Engineer (Zürich)

---

## Market Anchoring

**Zürich senior engineering salaries (2026 reference):**
- Senior Software Engineer, 5-8 years: CHF 140k-170k
- Senior Software Engineer, 8+ years: CHF 160k-190k
- Staff / Principal Engineer: CHF 180k-220k

**Magino's justified anchor:** CHF 185k-200k base, supported by:
- 17+ years of hands-on engineering experience
- Co-founding track record (platform built from zero, acquired by a $1B+ company)
- Senior Staff Engineer at Benevity post-acquisition (large-scale, cross-team scope)
- Switzerland-based, no relocation needed, familiar with Swiss employment norms
- AI tooling expertise directly relevant to the role

Lakera is a funded AI security startup (Series A/B stage). Equity is meaningful; treat total compensation as base + equity + benefits.

---

## Opening Anchor Script

If asked "What are your salary expectations?":

> "Based on my background and the scope of this role, I'm looking at CHF 185-200k base. I know the Zürich market for senior engineers fairly well, and given the end-to-end product ownership the role involves and what I'd bring on day one, that range feels right to me. Happy to talk about how the equity component factors in too."

If they push for a single number: land on **CHF 190k** as the opening anchor.

---

## If They Come In Low (CHF 150k-165k range)

> "I appreciate the offer and I'm genuinely interested in making this work. That said, CHF 155k is below the Zürich market rate for someone at this level, particularly with a founding background and 17 years of hands-on experience. I'd need to get to at least CHF 175k to move forward seriously. Is there flexibility there, either on base or on the equity side?"

If they won't move on base:
> "I understand the constraints. If the base is fixed, I'd want to look more closely at the equity. Can we talk about what the current package looks like in terms of options, vesting, and the last-round valuation?"

---

## Equity Negotiation (Startup-Specific)

For a Series A/B stage company, target: **0.15%-0.35% fully diluted**, depending on stage and total raised.

**Questions to ask:**
- What's the vesting schedule? (Standard: 4 years, 1-year cliff)
- How many options would be in the grant, and what's the current strike price?
- What was the most recent 409A valuation (or fair market value per share)?
- What's the approximate total shares outstanding / option pool?
- Is there a pro-rata right on future rounds for employees?
- What happens to vested options if I leave before an exit?

**Script:**
> "I'd like to understand the equity component properly before comparing packages. Can you share the current vesting schedule, the strike price, and a rough sense of the option pool size and total shares outstanding? I'm not expecting guarantees, I just want to model it realistically."

**Red flags to watch for:**
- No vesting cliff (unusual, makes grants feel cheap)
- Strike price very close to current FMV (limited upside)
- Option pool hasn't been refreshed after recent rounds (dilution already happened)

---

## Geographic Premium — Geneva vs. Zürich

If they attempt a discount because you're based in Geneva rather than in the Zürich office full-time:

> "Geneva's cost of living is comparable to Zürich, sometimes higher for housing in certain neighborhoods. If the role were fully remote with no office expectation, I could see an argument for a market adjustment. But I'm open to the Zürich office, which means real commuting costs on top. I'd expect the compensation to reflect the Swiss market regardless."

---

## Competing Offer Leverage Template

> "I'm also in late-stage conversations with another company in Switzerland. I'm not using that as pressure, I'm genuinely more interested in this role. But it does mean I'm making a decision in the next two to three weeks. If we can align on a package in that window, this is where I'd rather land."

Use only if true. If hypothetical, frame it as a timeline constraint rather than a competing offer.

---

## Benefits to Confirm

- **Swiss pension (2nd pillar / LPP):** Confirm employer contribution rate; aim for 50%+ employer share of BVG contributions
- **Health insurance:** Not mandatory, but many Zürich employers contribute CHF 200-400/month
- **Remote flexibility:** How many days per week in the Zürich office?
- **Hardware budget:** Full setup (laptop, monitor, peripherals)
- **Learning budget:** CHF 2,000-5,000/year is reasonable at this level; conferences, courses
- **Notice period:** What's theirs if the company needs to let you go? (reciprocal expectation)

---

## Walk-Away Thresholds

| Scenario | Floor |
|---|---|
| Base only, no equity | CHF 185k minimum |
| Base + meaningful equity (0.15%+) | CHF 170k base acceptable |
| Base + weak equity (< 0.1%) | CHF 185k+ required |
| Below CHF 165k in any scenario | Decline |

---

## Offer Acceptance Script

> "I'd like to take 48 hours to review everything in writing before confirming. I appreciate the offer and I'm planning to say yes, I just want to make sure I've read everything carefully."

Always get the offer in writing before accepting verbally. Check: base, equity grant size and terms, start date, notice period, benefits summary.
