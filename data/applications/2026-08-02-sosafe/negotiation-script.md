# Negotiation Script: SoSafe, Staff IT Engineer (Automation & AI Transformation)

Context: SoSafe is a Cologne-based, VC-backed (Highland Europe, Global Founders Capital) cybersecurity awareness / human risk management scale-up, hiring this role as EU remote (postings span UK, Ireland, Portugal, Germany). No Geneva office, so this negotiation runs as a remote-from-Switzerland case, not a local-market one.

## Market anchors

Base salary bands to have in your head before any number gets said out loud:

- German/DACH SaaS scale-ups, Staff/Principal IC level: roughly EUR 95,000 to 130,000 base, occasionally higher at hypergrowth companies with strong funding.
- UK remote Staff Engineer at a similar-stage SaaS company: roughly GBP 90,000 to 120,000 base.
- Swiss-resident remote-for-a-foreign-company arrangements typically get negotiated as a premium over the local band, not the Swiss band itself, since the employer isn't pricing against Zurich or Geneva cost of living. Expect resistance if you anchor purely on Swiss numbers; the stronger anchor is your track record plus the fact that remote-from-Switzerland means a stable, English-fluent, CET-adjacent hire with founder-level experience they'd otherwise have to source in London or Berlin at a premium.
- Total comp for this level usually includes equity, since SoSafe is still VC-backed and not yet public. Treat base and equity as two separate negotiation tracks.

## Opening anchor

Anchor near the top of the band you can defend, and justify it with the acquisition outcome and the specific overlap with the role, not with a generic "market rate" claim.

"Based on what I've seen for Staff-level automation and platform roles at scale-ups this size, and given that I've already taken a company through the full arc from zero to acquisition by a unicorn, I'd expect a base in the range of EUR 115,000 to 130,000, plus a meaningful equity grant given SoSafe's stage. I'm flexible on structure if the total package reflects that level."

## If they come in low

Don't reject a number outright. Ask what it's built from, then counter with specifics tied to scope.

"That's below where I expected for this scope, especially given the mandate includes mentoring the existing IT engineering team and driving the *-as-code transformation org-wide, not just an individual contributor track. Can you walk me through how that number was built? I'd want to get closer to [target], and I'm open to discussing how equity or a faster review cycle could bridge the gap if base is capped."

If they cite Swiss cost of living as a reason to discount, redirect to the value of a remote hire who's already fluent in the exact hybrid-automation problem they're solving, rather than arguing cost of living in the abstract.

## Geographic discount pushback

"I'd push back a little on pricing this against Geneva living costs. I'm not asking you to match a Zurich office role, I'm asking for what this role is worth against the UK and DACH bands you're already recruiting into. If anything, hiring remote from Switzerland gets you someone in a CET-adjacent, low-risk jurisdiction without adding a new legal entity, which is usually a plus for a company at your stage, not a discount."

## Equity negotiation

Since SoSafe is VC-backed and not public, treat the equity grant as seriously as base:

- Ask for the current fully diluted share count and the strike price basis so the percentage means something concrete.
- Standard structure to expect: 4-year vesting, 1-year cliff. Push back if the cliff is longer than a year or vesting extends past four.
- Ask directly whether there's an early exercise option and how long the post-termination exercise window is; a 90-day window after a 4-year vest can be a real financial trap if the company is doing well and the strike price has moved.
- If the round terms include anti-dilution provisions for investors, ask how those interact with employee equity in a future down round, particularly whether option holders get any protection or notice.

## Competing offer template

Use this even if the other conversation is early, framed honestly rather than as a bluff:

"I'm also mid-process on a couple of other Staff-level conversations, one of which is further along on comp. I'd rather move forward with SoSafe if the package lines up, since the automation and AI-native mandate here is the more interesting problem, but I do need to be able to compare the two on paper before I can commit."

## Closing move

Once a number is close, ask for the offer in writing before verbally accepting, and confirm the equity documentation (grant date, vesting start, strike price) arrives alongside the base and bonus terms, not weeks later.
