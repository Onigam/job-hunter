Negotiation prep: Co-Founder, CTO (Industrial Intelligence Platform) — Marble

## Context that shapes this negotiation
Marble is a venture studio, not a traditional startup with a term sheet already in place. Public program terms put a pre-seed check around EUR 250k on company creation, with the founding team (CEO plus CTO) holding roughly 80% of the company at that stage, the studio taking the remainder for the platform, capital, and operating support it provides. That structure means this isn't a salary negotiation against a VC-backed budget; it's closer to negotiating founder terms with a studio that has run this playbook many times before. Assume they know their numbers cold. The leverage here is technical credibility and the fact that a co-founder with a real acquisition on record is not the median applicant for a CTO seat.

## Switzerland market anchors
- Senior Staff / Principal Engineer, Zurich or Geneva, established company: CHF 170k-220k base, plus bonus and equity.
- VP Engineering / CTO, funded startup (Series A-ish), Switzerland: CHF 160k-210k base, often below Staff IC comp in exchange for meaningful equity (1-4% at this stage for a non-founding CTO, more for a true co-founder).
- Early pre-seed studio ventures generally pay below both of the above in cash, sometimes CHF 90k-140k, because the equity stake is doing most of the work. That's the range to expect Marble to open around, if they lead with cash at all before the venture spins out.
- Geneva/Zurich cost of living is high enough to justify pushing above a "generic Europe pre-seed CTO" number; don't let a EUR-denominated, Paris-anchored offer get applied to a Swiss-based hire without adjustment.

## Opening anchor
Don't anchor on cash first, since the studio's model is equity-led. Anchor on ownership and role clarity instead: ask directly what percentage of the 80% founder pool splits between CEO and CTO, and whether that split is negotiable or fixed by the program. Only after that's answered, anchor cash at CHF 130k-150k base as a floor, framed as "enough to not think about rent while we find product-market fit," not as a market-rate ask. This keeps the conversation on the numbers that actually move (equity split, vesting, cliff) rather than getting stuck on a modest salary figure.

## If they come in low
If the offer comes in with junior-sounding equity (well under 30-35% of the founder pool) or a cash number below CHF 100k with no clear path to adjustment post-fundraise: ask what specific milestones (pilot signed, seed raised, team hired) trigger a re-negotiation, and get that in writing as a term rather than a verbal promise. A reasonable counter: "I'd take the lower cash number if the founder split is closer to even and there's a written comp review at seed close." If they won't move on either equity or a review trigger, that's a signal about how this studio treats its technical co-founders long-term, not just about this one offer.

## Equity specifics to nail down
- Vesting: standard 4-year with a 1-year cliff; push back on anything longer than 4 years or a cliff over 12 months.
- Founder vesting acceleration on a change of control (single or double trigger) since Marble itself may sell or restructure the studio's stake.
- Anti-dilution treatment at the pre-seed to seed conversion, specifically whether the studio's ~20% is diluted alongside the founders or protected ahead of them.
- IP assignment and what happens to the equity and the tech if the venture gets killed by Marble's internal review process before it reaches seed. Studios do sometimes shut down a venture; know the walk-away terms before signing anything.

## Geographic discount pushback
If Marble tries to benchmark comp against a lower-cost-of-living city (their Paris HQ, for instance) rather than Zurich or Geneva: point out that the CEO's own background and the ETH Zurich tie mean the operational center of gravity for this venture is likely Swiss, and Swiss hiring costs, taxes, and rent aren't optional context, they're the baseline the offer needs to clear.

## Competing offer leverage template
"I'm currently weighing this against a Staff-level platform role at an established company that would clear CHF 190k plus standard equity, with none of the pre-seed risk. I'd take less cash here because building something from zero again is what I actually want to do, but I need the equity and the vesting terms to reflect that I'm walking away from a safer, better-paid option to do it."
