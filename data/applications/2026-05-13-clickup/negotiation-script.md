# Negotiation Script — ClickUp Staff AI Engineer (AI Product)

## Context

**Posted range:** $200,000–$242,500 USD annually (US Remote)
**Role level:** Staff Engineer (IC4/IC5 equivalent)
**Company status:** Private, last known valuation ~$4B (Series C, 2021). No public comp benchmarks post-2023.
**Key constraint:** The role is listed as "Remote (United States)." Magino is based in Geneva, Switzerland. This geographic question must be resolved before negotiation over salary makes sense.

---

## Step 0: Resolve the Geographic Question First

Before any comp discussion, confirm whether ClickUp will hire internationally or if they require US residency/entity.

**Script:**
> "Before we talk numbers, I want to flag upfront that I'm based in Geneva, Switzerland. I'm fully remote-compatible and Europe-friendly timezone is a non-issue, but I want to confirm that's workable on your end — either as a direct international hire or through a contractor/EOR arrangement — before we get into compensation."

If they say US-only, politely close the loop:
> "Understood. If the structure changes or you open an international track, I'd be happy to revisit. Thanks for being direct about it."

If they're open to international:
> "Great. Then let's talk about what makes sense for the role and level."

---

## Step 1: Market Anchoring

### US Market (if they're hiring directly in USD)

Staff AI Engineer at a $4B private tech company, fully remote:
- **Market range:** $230,000–$280,000 total cash (base + bonus)
- **Equity:** 0.05%–0.15% over 4-year vest at Staff level, depending on stage and round
- **ClickUp's posted range:** $200,000–$242,500 — the floor is below market for a Staff AI hire in 2026

**Anchor:** Open at $265,000–$275,000 base. This is defensible against current market comps for Staff AI roles (Stripe, Notion, Linear, and equivalents are paying in this band for IC5+).

### Switzerland Market (if they hire via EOR or contractor, paid in CHF)

Staff-level AI/platform engineer in Geneva/Switzerland:
- **Market base:** CHF 200,000–250,000
- **Total comp with equity:** CHF 220,000–280,000+
- Geneva cost-of-living index is among the highest globally (comparable to SF Bay Area, not NYC)

**Anchor in CHF:** CHF 240,000 base equivalent, plus equity on par with a US hire at the same level.

---

## Step 2: Opening Anchor Script

> "Based on my research into Staff AI Engineer comps at companies at ClickUp's stage and scale, and accounting for 17 years of experience including a founding engineer exit, I'd be looking at a base in the $265,000–$270,000 range. I want to be upfront about that rather than waste anyone's time — if that's workable, I'm happy to get into the details of the role and equity."

Why this works:
- It's above their posted max ($242.5K) but not absurdly so
- Frames it as transparency, not aggression
- Leaves room for them to counter at $250K–$255K, which is still a win

---

## Step 3: If They Come In at Offer Level ($230K–$242.5K)

**First response (soft counter):**
> "Thanks for putting that together. I appreciate the transparency on the range. Based on where Staff AI Engineer comps are sitting in 2026 — especially for roles involving LLM orchestration and production AI systems — I was expecting to be closer to $260,000–$265,000. Is there room to move on base, or is the range firm?"

**If they push back on the range being firm:**
> "Understood. If the base is capped at $242,500, I'd want to look at the equity component more closely to see if we can close the gap there. What does the equity package look like at this level, and what's the current strike price?"

**If they offer something around $242.5K and can't move:**
> "If $242,500 is the ceiling on base, I can work with that if the equity is structured well. Two questions: what's the vesting schedule and cliff, and is there a refresh grant cadence?"

---

## Step 4: Equity Negotiation

ClickUp is private with a ~$4B last valuation (2021). No public liquidity event confirmed. Treat equity as speculative.

**Questions to ask:**
1. What's the current 409A / fair market value per share?
2. What's the vesting schedule? (Standard is 4-year, 1-year cliff)
3. Is there a double-trigger acceleration clause in case of acquisition?
4. Are refreshes tied to performance reviews? What's the cadence?
5. What was the last round price, and what's the preference stack?

**Script for equity ask:**
> "For a Staff IC role at this level, I'd expect equity in the 0.05%–0.10% range over four years. What's the package you have in mind, and what's the current 409A?"

**If they offer below 0.05%:**
> "I appreciate the transparency. Given that the base is at the lower end of what I'd be targeting, I'd want to make sure the equity compensates for that. Is there flexibility to add another tranche upfront, or is the initial grant fixed?"

---

## Step 5: Geographic Discount Pushback (if they try to apply one)

If they say "we'd need to apply a geo adjustment for Switzerland":
> "I'd want to push back on that framing a bit. My output and scope are the same regardless of where I sit, and Geneva is one of the most expensive cities in the world — comparable to San Francisco. A geo discount that brings my comp below a US-based peer at the same level wouldn't reflect that. I'd want to be at parity with a US Staff AI Engineer."

---

## Step 6: Competing Offer Script (if applicable)

If you're in late-stage conversations elsewhere and need to accelerate:
> "I want to be straightforward with you: I'm at the offer stage with another company at a comparable level. I'd prefer to join ClickUp if we can get aligned on comp, but I have a decision deadline of [date]. Is there a path to move quickly on your end?"

If no competing offer but timeline pressure exists:
> "I'm actively in conversations with a few companies at a similar stage and want to make a decision by [date]. I don't want to play games about it — is ClickUp in a position to move on an offer in that window?"

Below these numbers, the role does not justify displacement from a Geneva market role at a comparable level.
