# Negotiation Script: Kraken, Senior Software Engineer, Agent Systems

## Context that shapes the numbers

Kraken (Payward Inc.) is one of the largest crypto exchanges, remote-first, and a late-stage private company heading for an IPO. It confidentially filed with the SEC in late 2025, paused in March 2026 because of market conditions, and in May 2026 its co-CEO Arjun Sethi said the company was "80% ready" to list. Reported valuation fell from around $20B (November 2025) to about $13.3B (April 2026). Check the latest news before any call; IPO timing changes the value of the equity a lot.

What that means for the negotiation:

- The US version of this posting lists a base range of **$110.4k to $220.8k**. That's very wide, which usually means several levels or location tiers share one band. It's useful as a ceiling reference, not as the Swiss number.
- Kraken sets pay by location. Expect a Switzerland figure, and expect them to start from their own Swiss benchmark, which may be Zurich or may be a generic "Europe" tier. Push for the former.
- The title is Senior. Magino's last title was Senior Staff. The level the offer is built on matters more than the first base number, so raise leveling before money.
- Equity is pre-IPO stock (options or RSUs, confirm which). It could be worth a lot or very little depending on timing, so negotiate for terms as well as size.
- Confirm how they'd employ someone in Switzerland: a local Kraken entity or an employer of record. That decides whether Swiss social contributions, pension (LPP/BVG) and the 13th month are handled properly.

## Leveling first

Raise this in the recruiter screen, before any number is mentioned:

"Before we talk compensation, I'd like to understand how you're leveling this. My last role was Senior Staff, and the scope here, building a 0→1 team's agent platform and defining how agents execute safely, sounds closer to Staff in a lot of companies. Is there flexibility on level, or is this firmly a Senior opening?"

If they say it's firmly Senior, accept it calmly and use it later: "Fine, then I'd like the comp to sit at the top of the Senior band, and I'd want the criteria for Staff written down so we can revisit it at the first review cycle."

## Opening anchor script

"Based on what I'm seeing for senior engineers with production LLM experience in Switzerland, and given the 0→1 scope, I'm looking at a base around CHF 205k, plus an equity grant that reflects joining before the IPO rather than after it. I know your US band goes up to about $220k, so I don't think that's outside what you'd consider. How does it compare with what you had in mind?"

Then stop talking.

## If they come in low

**Script A, base below range (for example CHF 150k to 165k):**
"Thanks for being direct. That's a fair number for a senior engineer joining an established team, but this is a new team building agents that write into production systems in a regulated company, and that's exactly the work I want to own. I'd need to see the base closer to CHF 190k. If there's real constraint on cash, I'm open to closing part of the gap with additional equity, as long as the terms are clear."

**Script B, they apply a 'Europe' discount:**
"I understand you benchmark by location. Geneva isn't a Europe-average market though; cost of living here is closer to Zurich, and the Swiss engineers you'd compete with for this profile are being paid on Zurich numbers. Could you benchmark the offer against Zurich rather than a general European tier?"

**Script C, they say the band is fixed:**
"Understood. Then let's look at what's flexible: where I land in the band, the size of the equity grant, a sign-on bonus, and the review timeline. A six-month compensation review tied to agreed outcomes would go a long way."

## Equity: what to ask and what to push on

Pre-IPO equity is the piece most likely to be misunderstood. Get answers in writing to each of these:

1. **Instrument.** Options or RSUs? If RSUs, are they double-trigger (vest on time and on a liquidity event)? If options, what's the strike price and the current fair market value?
2. **Grant size.** Ask for the number of units and the price or valuation used to convert dollars into units. A grant "worth $X" at a $20B valuation is worth much less if the IPO prices nearer $13B.
3. **Vesting.** Typical is four years with a one-year cliff. Ask whether vesting is monthly or quarterly after the cliff, and whether there are annual refreshers.
4. **Exercise window.** If options, push for a long post-termination exercise window (several years, not 90 days). This matters a lot if the IPO slips again.
5. **Lockup and liquidity.** After an IPO there's usually a lockup of around six months. Ask whether there's been or will be any tender offer before listing.
6. **Swiss tax.** In Switzerland, employee options and RSUs can be taxed at vesting or exercise rather than at sale, and private-company shares can be hard to value. Get a short opinion from a Geneva tax advisor before signing, and ask Kraken whether they provide tax support or withholding for Swiss employees.

Script if the grant looks small:
"The grant is a big part of why this is interesting to me at this stage. Given that the valuation has moved since your last round and the IPO timing isn't fixed, could we either increase the number of units or use the current valuation for the conversion, so the grant reflects what it's actually worth today?"

## Other things worth negotiating

- **Sign-on bonus** to cover unvested equity or a bonus being left behind. Ask for it in CHF.
- **Swiss benefits.** Confirm LPP/BVG pension contributions above the legal minimum, accident and daily allowance insurance, and whether salary is paid as 12 or 13 months.
- **Home office and coworking budget**, plus travel to any team offsites.
- **Learning and conference budget**, especially for AI and agent-focused events.
- **Working hours.** With a US-heavy team, agree upfront on a reasonable overlap window so evenings don't quietly become the norm.

## Competing offer template (use only if true)

"I want to be transparent: I'm in late stages with another company and have an offer at CHF [X] base with [equity details]. Kraken is my first choice because of the Agent Systems scope, and I'd rather make this work. Is there room to move on base or equity so I can say yes without second-guessing it?"

If there's no competing offer, don't invent one. Use the market data and the scope of the role instead; it's a strong enough case on its own.

## Before the call: checklist

- Re-read the latest Kraken IPO news and any updated valuation.
- Confirm whether Kraken has a Swiss entity or uses an employer of record.
- Pull fresh Switzerland numbers from levels.fyi and one salary guide.
- Decide the real floor in advance and don't move it during the call.
- Have the leveling question ready; it shapes everything else.
