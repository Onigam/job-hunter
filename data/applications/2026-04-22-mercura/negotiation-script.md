# Negotiation Script — Mercura / Founding Staff Engineer

---

## Context

**Role:** Founding Staff Engineer  
**Company:** Mercura (YC W25, Munich, ~14 people, ~$2M ARR)  
**Posted range:** EUR 50K-110K (very wide; the low end is for junior hires, not Staff+)  
**Equity:** Company mentions "favorable tax treatment, no ESOP/VSOP" — this likely means real shares or options structured better than typical German VSOP (which has poor tax treatment). Worth clarifying.  
**Location:** Munich (on-site). You're in Geneva. This is a life relocation, not just a job change.

---

## Market Anchors

### Munich Staff Engineer market (6-15 years experience)
- Senior Engineer (6-8 yrs): EUR 90K-115K base
- Staff Engineer (8-12 yrs): EUR 110K-140K base
- Founding engineer with 17 years + co-founder track record: top of or above published range

### Geneva / Switzerland equivalent
- Senior Staff Engineer in Geneva: CHF 180K-220K total comp
- That's roughly EUR 195K-240K at current rates

### YC Founding Engineer benchmarks (seed stage)
- Base: typically at or near top of published range
- Equity: 0.5-1.5% for truly founding-level engineers at 14-person stage
- Vesting: 4-year vest, 1-year cliff is standard
- Acceleration: ask for single-trigger acceleration on acquisition (relevant given your track record)

---

## Opening Anchor

When they ask about expectations, go first if you have a number in mind. Don't wait for their offer if you can avoid it.

**Script:**
> "Based on the market for Staff+ engineers in Munich with a founding background, and given this is a genuinely founding-level role rather than a hire into an established team, I'm targeting EUR 105-110K base. I'd also want to understand the equity structure before settling on a base number, because at this stage of the company the equity is a meaningful part of the picture."

If they ask you to come down before naming a number, hold:
> "I'd rather hear your thinking on the equity first. The base and the equity are connected for me at this stage."

---

## If They Come In Low (EUR 80K-90K range)

**Script:**
> "I appreciate the offer. EUR 85K doesn't quite work for me given what I'd be leaving behind in terms of compensation in Switzerland and the relocation involved. I've done the math, and for this move to make sense financially, I need to be at EUR 100-105K minimum. I think that's still well within reach for a founding-level role. Is there room to get there?"

If they say the budget is fixed:
> "I understand budget constraints at this stage. If the base can't move much, I'd want to revisit the equity number. What does the founding engineer equity look like currently, and what's the last preferred price?"

---

## Equity Negotiation

### What to ask for
At 14 employees, seed stage (probably valued EUR 5-15M post-money), a Founding Staff Engineer joining now should be in the 0.5-1.0% range. This is meaningful because:
- If they reach EUR 50M valuation next round (which is plausible at EUR 10M ARR), 0.75% = EUR 375K pre-dilution
- If they exit at EUR 200M+, it's life-changing

### Questions to ask
1. "What's the current cap table structure? How much has been granted to employees so far?"
2. "You mentioned favorable tax treatment — can you walk me through the exact instrument? Is it direct shares, options, or warrants?"
3. "What was the last valuation on the preferred shares?"
4. "Is there an option pool refresh planned for the next round?"
5. "Does the equity have single-trigger or double-trigger acceleration on acquisition?"

### Script for asking for more equity
> "Given where the company is (seed stage, founding hire, before the next inflection point), I'd want to make sure the equity reflects the founding contribution. I'm thinking 0.75-1.0% with a standard 4-year vest and 1-year cliff. I'd also like single-trigger acceleration for acquisition, given that I've been through one already. Is that something you can work with?"

---

## Geographic Discount Pushback

Munich is significantly cheaper than Geneva, but it's still one of the most expensive German cities. Don't accept a Munich salary that's treated as a big discount from a German-average role.

**Script:**
> "I want to be transparent: Munich is meaningfully cheaper than Geneva, so I'm not expecting Swiss comp. But Munich rents have risen sharply, and the subsidized housing you offer covers the first 1-2 years, not the long term. I'm anchoring to Munich Staff Engineer market rates, not to a discount from Geneva."

If they ask you to compare Munich directly to Geneva:
> "That comparison doesn't work cleanly because the tax systems are entirely different. I'm using Munich market data for this role, not a Geneva baseline."

---

## Relocation Package

Since this is a genuine relocation from Geneva to Munich (not just a commute), ask for:
- Moving cost reimbursement: EUR 3-5K (reasonable for an international move)
- Extended time in subsidized housing (the EUR 800/month apartment they offer): ask for 12-18 months minimum, not 6
- A signing bonus if the base can't move: EUR 5-10K is reasonable for a founding hire

**Script:**
> "Since this involves relocating from Geneva, I'd want to make sure there's a relocation package that covers the practical costs. I'm thinking moving costs reimbursement and an extended run on the subsidized housing so I'm not in a rush to find something permanent in a city I'm new to. Is that something you've done with other hires?"

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

If you have other offers in progress (even in late stages):
> "I want to be straightforward with you: I have another process moving forward that's closer to my base number. I'd prefer to join Mercura because the problem and stage are more interesting, but I can't leave significant money on the table without a comparable package. Can you match EUR 105K?"

If you don't have a competing offer, don't fabricate one. Instead:
> "I've been selective about which conversations I've taken further, and I'm at a point where I need the economics to work before I can commit. This isn't about squeezing you, it's about making sure I can be fully in without financial stress in the background."

---

## Summary: Walk-Away Scenarios

**Accept without hesitation:** EUR 100K+ base + 0.75%+ equity + single-trigger acceleration + relocation support  
**Accept with mild reservation:** EUR 95K+ base + 0.5%+ equity (negotiate equity harder)  
**Counter strongly:** EUR 85-95K base + less than 0.5% equity (not worth the relocation risk)  
**Walk away:** Under EUR 85K base with no equity flexibility (economic case doesn't hold)

---

## Tone Guidance

Mercura is a founder-led YC company. The people negotiating with you have probably negotiated their own terms before. Be direct, be friendly, and don't apologize for knowing your worth. They respect operators, and operators know what things cost.
