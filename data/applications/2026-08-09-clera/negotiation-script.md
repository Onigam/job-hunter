# Negotiation Script: Founding Engineer, Clera (Munich)

## Context to keep in mind

This is a pre-seed, first-engineering-hire role, on-site in Munich, working directly with the CTO. The posted range (roughly $70k-95k USD, likely quoted in that currency for a US-facing job board rather than reflecting an actual EUR offer) is low for someone with 17+ years of experience and a founding-engineer track record that ended in an acquisition, but pre-seed cash is genuinely constrained, so the real lever here is equity and title, not base salary. There's also an open question the job posts don't answer: the listing says no visa sponsorship and implies German work authorization is required. That has to be resolved before comp talk goes anywhere, since Geneva-based hiring with Swiss/EU freedom of movement may or may not clear that bar depending on nationality and permit status, and it's worth confirming directly and early rather than assuming.

## Switzerland market anchor (context, not the primary lever)

A Staff-level founding or early engineer based in Geneva would typically anchor around CHF 160k-190k base in a comparable Swiss startup, with pre-seed companies usually below that and offset by equity. Munich cash comp for an equivalent founding engineer role tends to run EUR 90k-120k base at pre-seed, well below the Swiss number, which is normal given the cost-of-living and market gap between Geneva and Munich. The point isn't to demand Geneva-level cash for a Munich role, it's to make clear that the posted $70k-95k figure reads more like a placeholder than a considered number for someone at this level, and to anchor the conversation closer to the EUR 100k-120k range plus a real equity stake.

## Opening anchor

Let them name a number first if possible. If asked directly:

"For a first engineering hire owning infrastructure, trading model deployment, and market integrations solo alongside the CTO, I'd expect a base in the EUR 105k-120k range, plus a founding-engineer equity grant, something in the 1-3% range is typical at this stage depending on how much has already been allocated to the founders. I'm open on the exact split if the equity number reflects the risk I'd be taking on."

## If they come in low on cash

"I get that pre-seed cash is tight, and I'm fine weighting more of this toward equity if the grant actually reflects that. If base can't move much past what's posted, I'd want a defined comp review at six months tied to whatever milestones the seed round is built around, not just a vague 'we'll revisit it later.'"

Fallback if base truly can't move: ask for a larger initial equity grant, a shorter cliff or accelerated first-year vesting, and a relocation stipend covering the move from Geneva to Munich, since that's a real, quantifiable cost they're asking the candidate to absorb.

## Equity negotiation notes

Ask what percentage of the company has been allocated to the option pool and where this grant falls within it, since at pre-seed the number sounds bigger than it often is once dilution from the next two rounds is accounted for. Confirm strike price, current valuation from the pre-seed round, and whether it's options or a direct equity grant given German entity structures sometimes differ from the more familiar Delaware C-corp setup. Ask about the standard vesting schedule, most likely a one-year cliff with four-year vesting, and whether there's any acceleration on acquisition. Given this is engineer #1, it's fair to ask how the offer compares to what the CTO or founders themselves are earning in cash, since founding-team parity is a reasonable thing to want visibility into.

## Work authorization and relocation

Raise this before comp gets detailed, not after: "Before we get into numbers, I want to confirm the work authorization path, since the posting mentions no visa sponsorship. I'm Geneva-based, so I'd want to understand whether that's workable given my situation before we spend more time on comp specifics." If it turns out sponsorship truly isn't possible and there's no path to work authorization, that's a hard blocker regardless of how good the offer is, better to know in the first conversation than after negotiating a number.

## Geographic framing

"I'm coming from Geneva, which is a more expensive market than Munich, so I'm not trying to import Swiss comp levels into a pre-seed budget. What I do want is a package, cash plus equity plus relocation support, that reflects building this alone with the CTO rather than joining as employee ten."

## Competing offer template

Use only if a real competing process exists. Never invent a number or company name.

"I'm also in later-stage conversations for another engineering role. I'd rather build this with Clera given the scope and the chance to own infrastructure end to end from day one, so if there's room to move on the equity grant or the relocation piece, this is the point where that would help me decide."
