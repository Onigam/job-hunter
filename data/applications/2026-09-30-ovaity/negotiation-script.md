# Negotiation Script: OVAITY, CTO / Technical Co-Founder, Lab Automation

## Context that shapes everything

This is a co-founder search, not a hire. The posting gives no salary or equity figures and invites a casual message about what you've built and why you'd co-found. So the negotiation is about **equity, vesting, the split of roles, salary once funded, and what happens if it doesn't work out**, not about a base salary band.

What we know:

- OVAITY has a live product in **private beta** with research teams at Balgrist Campus, ETH Zurich and UZH. There's already a platform, branding and a named framework (TRACE). So you'd be joining a company that already has momentum, as a **late co-founder** rather than an original one.
- It's a Swiss-hosted, GDPR-focused platform for life-science R&D. Sales to pharma and biotech are slow; academic labs pay little. The path to meaningful revenue will take time.
- Funding stage and team size aren't public. Find out before any number is discussed.
- Based in Zürich. You live in Geneva.

## Market reference points (estimates, verify before the call)

Working ranges from general Swiss startup practice and public salary data. They aren't quotes. Check them with a Swiss startup lawyer or founder network (Venturelab, Swiss Startup Association, ETH/UZH founder circles) before naming a number.

| Reference | Range | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| CTO salary, pre-seed / bootstrapped | CHF 0 to 90k | Often zero or a living stipend until a round closes |
| CTO salary after seed, Zürich deep-tech/SaaS | CHF 140k to 180k base | Seed investors usually cap founder pay below market |
| CTO salary after Series A, Zürich | CHF 180k to 230k base | Where it should head once there's real funding |
| Staff+ engineer, Geneva/Zürich scale-up | CHF 190k to 240k base | Your opportunity cost |
| Late co-founder CTO equity, product already in beta | ~10% to 25% | Depends heavily on what's built, how much money is in, and founder count |
| Equal co-founder CTO (solo founder, little built) | ~25% to 45% | Only if the founder really wants a partner, not a lead engineer |

**Opportunity cost is the real anchor.** Every year below market is about CHF 60k to 200k you're putting in. Say it plainly; it's arithmetic, not aggression.

## Your numbers (decide before the first call)

- **Equity target:** ~20% fully diluted, four-year vesting, one-year cliff. Move up toward 30%+ if the founder is solo and most of the platform still needs to be built.
- **Equity floor:** your call. Around 10% is a sensible line for a seat that owns all of the technology.
- **Salary until funding:** a number you can live on, or zero with deferred accrual. Know your personal runway in months.
- **Post-seed salary floor:** CHF 150k base, with a written step-up after Series A.
- **Title and scope:** CTO with authority over architecture, hiring for engineering, and a board or founder-level vote on product direction.

## Opening the conversation

Start with facts, not numbers.

> "Before we talk terms, I'd like to understand where things stand. What's the cap table today, is there an option pool, how much have you raised and from whom, and who built what's running in the beta? That tells me whether this is a co-founder seat or a first senior engineering hire, and both are fine, but they're priced differently."

Then anchor:

> "If I'm taking ownership of the whole technical side, instrument integrations, the agent layer, provenance, security, and building the engineering team, I'd expect around 20% fully diluted on standard four-year vesting with a one-year cliff. I'm stepping away from a Staff-level salary to do this, so until we raise I'd want either a modest salary or deferred pay that accrues from day one."

## If they come in low

**If they offer 2% to 5% ("early employee" range):**

> "That's a strong first-employee package, but what you've described is a founder role. You're asking me to own the architecture, the integrations and the hiring, and to carry the technical side in investor conversations. If the equity is 4%, it's probably better to hire a lead engineer after the next round, and I'd rather we're honest about which one this is."

**If the percentage won't move but other things can:**

> "I can be flexible on the headline number if the structure's right. Deferred salary converting to equity at the next round's price, double-trigger acceleration on change of control, and a written post-seed salary floor."

**If they say "I'm not paying myself either":**

> "Fair, and I'm not asking for more cash than you take. I'm asking that the gap is recorded. Deferred pay for both of us, converting at the round, keeps it symmetric and costs the company nothing now."

## Equity terms to get in writing

- **Vesting:** four years, one-year cliff, monthly after. Ask whether the existing founder is also on vesting; if they're fully vested and you aren't, that's worth raising.
- **Vesting start date:** from your first day of real work, including any trial period, not from signing.
- **Acceleration:** double-trigger (change of control plus termination) at 100%, or at least 50%.
- **Good leaver / bad leaver:** clear definitions. A good leaver keeps vested shares at fair value, not nominal.
- **Anti-dilution:** don't expect it as a founder; ask instead for pro-rata rights in future rounds and a clear view of the option pool size the next investor will require (usually 10% to 15%, and it dilutes founders).
- **Decision rights:** CTO seat on the board or a founder agreement with defined vetoes on technical and engineering-hiring matters.
- **IP assignment:** check that prior IP, including your own open-source work on GitHub, is explicitly carved out.
- **Swiss specifics:** confirm whether it's an AG or GmbH, whether shares are real shares or phantom/options, and the tax treatment of shares acquired at nominal value. Get advice before signing; Swiss tax on founder shares can bite if they're priced after a round.

## Geography

Geneva to Zürich is about 2h45 by train. The posting is Zürich-based.

> "I'm happy to be in Zürich two to three days a week, especially early on and when we're with the beta labs. I'd ask that travel is covered by the company once we're funded, and that remote days are the norm otherwise."

If they push back on Geneva: Lausanne's EPFL and Geneva's life-science cluster (Campus Biotech, HUG) are a real sales channel for an academic-first product. Present it as coverage, not a compromise.

## Competing offer template (use honestly)

> "I'm talking with a couple of other early-stage teams about a technical co-founder role, one in medtech. I'd rather decide on fit than terms, so I'm being upfront about the timeline: I'd like to know where we stand within the next three weeks."

Only use this if it's true. You're in active conversations with several co-founder searches right now, so it probably is.

## Walk-away signals

- The founder won't share the cap table or funding details.
- Most of the equity is already gone to advisors or an agency that built the beta.
- No vesting for the existing founder while you're asked to take a cliff.
- The "CTO" role turns out to be lead contractor with a title.
- No clarity on who decides product direction when you disagree.
