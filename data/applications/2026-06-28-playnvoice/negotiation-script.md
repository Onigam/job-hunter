# Negotiation Script — PlaynVoice, Head of Engineering

---

## Market Context (Switzerland, 2026)

PlaynVoice is an early-stage Swiss healthtech startup (~10 people, Zürich, bootstrapped or light-funded). The Head of Engineering role involves both hands-on engineering and team leadership, which commands a premium over pure IC roles.

**Benchmark ranges for this role type in Switzerland:**

| Context | Base (CHF/year) | Equity |
|---|---|---|
| Early-stage startup (pre-Series A, 5-15 people) | 130,000 – 160,000 | 1 – 3% |
| Seed-funded startup (1-3 engineers to manage) | 145,000 – 175,000 | 0.75 – 2% |
| Series A+ startup, Head of Eng | 170,000 – 210,000 | 0.25 – 1% |
| Large tech company, equivalent seniority | 190,000 – 240,000 | RSUs |

PlaynVoice sits in the first bracket. Base will likely be constrained; equity is where the upside lives.

---

## Opening Anchor

**When they ask for your expectations:**

"Based on my experience at this level, and the scope of the role — building the engineering function from where it is now, hiring, architecture, and hands-on delivery — I'd be looking at CHF 165,000 as a base. I know early-stage compensation is a package conversation, so I'm more interested in how the equity is structured than in maximising base."

Why this works: anchors above the likely band, signals flexibility on structure, opens the equity conversation immediately.

---

## If They Come In Low (CHF 120,000 – 140,000)

"I appreciate the transparency. That range is below where I'd need to be to make this work, given my experience level and what the market looks like for this role in Switzerland. That said, if the equity component is meaningful, I'm open to finding a number that works on both sides. Can you walk me through the cap table structure and what my stake would look like at current and a 10x scenario?"

If equity terms are strong, counter with: "If we can land at CHF 145,000 base and something in the 1.5 – 2% equity range on a standard four-year vest, I think we can get to yes."

---

## Equity Negotiation

**What to ask:**
- What percentage of the fully diluted cap table does this grant represent?
- What's the current valuation, and what was the most recent round price?
- Is there a 1-year cliff on the vest?
- What happens to unvested shares on acquisition or if I'm let go without cause?
- Is there acceleration on a double-trigger (termination post-acquisition)?

**Reasonable targets for this stage:**
- 1.5 – 2.5% on a standard 4-year vest, 1-year cliff
- Push for single or double-trigger acceleration if they won't move on base

**If they offer below 1%:**

"At this company stage, I'd expect the equity component to be a meaningful part of the compensation story, not just a symbolic gesture. A grant below 1% at a 10-person company doesn't reflect the risk or the scope of what you're asking the Head of Engineering to do. I'd need to see something closer to 1.5 – 2% to feel aligned with the long-term bet."

---

## Geographic Discount Pushback

PlaynVoice is Zürich-based but remote-first. If they try to adjust salary based on Geneva vs. Zürich location:

"The role is remote-first, which I appreciate. My cost of living in Geneva is comparable to Zürich. I'd expect compensation to reflect the role scope and Swiss market rates rather than commute distance."

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

If you have or can plausibly have another offer (real is always better):

"I have a parallel conversation at another Swiss company at a slightly higher base, but PlaynVoice is more interesting to me for the founding-team dynamic and the product direction. If you can match CHF 160,000 and firm up the equity, I'd close here."

If you don't have a competing offer, don't fabricate one. Instead:

"I've been selective in my search and PlaynVoice is one of three conversations I'm actively pursuing. I want to make a decision this month, so I'd appreciate clarity on the timeline for your side."

---

## Timeline and Process

- Ask about the decision timeline upfront
- Don't accept an offer verbally on the spot; request it in writing first
- Standard Swiss notice periods run 1 – 3 months depending on contract; be transparent about your notice period at Benevity
- If the offer letter arrives, give yourself 48 – 72 hours before signing, even if you intend to accept

---

## Bottom Line

| Scenario | Minimum to accept | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Base only (no meaningful equity) | CHF 165,000 | CHF 175,000 |
| Base + meaningful equity (1.5%+) | CHF 145,000 base | CHF 155,000 + 2% |
| Pure equity play (very early / pre-revenue) | Not recommended at this stage | — |
