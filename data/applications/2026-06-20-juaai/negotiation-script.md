# Negotiation Script — Staff AI Engineer at Jua.ai

## Context

- **Company stage:** Series A equivalent. Raised €10M in June 2025 (EU Startups reported).
- **Location:** Zürich office (Magino based in Geneva: ~3h by train, negotiate remote-first or commute terms).
- **Role level:** Staff AI Engineer. Senior individual contributor with significant scope.
- **Jua's stated offer:** "Fair compensation + generous shares."
- **Leverage points:** 17 years experience, founding engineer pedigree, acquisition exit, Switzerland-based.
- **Risk factor:** Role is ML-research oriented; Magino's AI background is engineering/tooling, not model research. Adjust expectations accordingly.

---

## Switzerland Market Anchors (2025-2026)

| Context | Base Salary (CHF/year) |
|---|---|
| Large tech (Google, Meta, Zurich) — Staff AI | 220,000 – 300,000+ |
| Scale-up (Series B+), Staff AI Engineer | 180,000 – 230,000 |
| Series A startup, Staff AI Engineer | 155,000 – 190,000 |
| **Target for this role** | **170,000 – 190,000** |

Note: If the role is research-heavy and Jua benchmarks against ML researchers rather than engineers, expect them to anchor lower (~140–160k). Come in higher regardless.

---

## Opening Move

**When asked about salary expectations, say:**

> "I'm targeting CHF 185,000 base. That's consistent with what senior AI engineers with a decade-plus of experience are earning at well-funded startups in Switzerland. I'm also thinking about equity seriously, so I'm flexible on the exact base if the equity terms are strong."

Hold that number. Do not offer a range first. Ranges anchor low.

---

## If They Come In Below CHF 160,000

**Script:**

> "I appreciate the offer. The number is below what I was expecting for this level in Zurich. My research puts the market at CHF 170–190k for Staff-level AI engineers at Series A startups here. Is there room to move on the base, or is equity the primary mechanism you're using to compensate at this stage?"

If they confirm equity is the lever:

> "That makes sense for an early-stage company. In that case I'd want to understand the current valuation, the option pool size, and the vesting terms before I can evaluate the full package. Can you share those?"

---

## Equity Negotiation

Jua is pre-Series B. Equity can be meaningful. Target terms:

| Parameter | Target | Floor |
|---|---|---|
| Option grant (% of fully diluted) | 0.30 – 0.50% | 0.15% |
| Vesting schedule | 4 years, 1-year cliff | 4 years, 1-year cliff |
| Exercise window post-departure | 5–10 years | 2 years |
| Anti-dilution | Standard broad-based weighted average | — |

**On option pool size:**

> "Can you tell me the current fully diluted share count and what % of the option pool this grant represents? I want to make sure I understand the effective dilution before any future raises."

**If they push back on the % ask:**

> "I understand the pool is limited. Even a smaller grant is fine if the strike price reflects current fair market value rather than a valuation from a prior round. What is the 409A / fair market value right now?"

---

## Location and Commute Terms

Magino is in Geneva. Zürich office-required roles add real friction and cost.

**Negotiate upfront:**

> "I want to be transparent: I'm based in Geneva, which is roughly three hours from Zürich. I can commit to being in the office X days per month, but a fully on-site requirement wouldn't work for me without relocation or a significant travel allowance. Is the role flexible on remote, or is Zürich presence mandatory?"

**If they require Zürich presence frequently:**

> "If that's the expectation, I'd want to factor in either a travel allowance or a higher base to account for the commute cost and time. For context, a Zürich–Geneva monthly GA pass is around CHF 1,000, plus the time cost. It's a real variable."

---

## Counter-Offer Leverage Template

If you have another offer or process underway:

> "I should mention I'm in late stages with another company. I'm genuinely more interested in Jua's mission, but the other offer is competitive and I'd need to make a decision within [X] days. Is there a way to accelerate the timeline on your end?"

If you don't have a competing offer, don't fabricate one. Use the market data instead:

> "I'm not in a rush, but I want to be transparent that my reference point isn't just this role. The market for senior AI engineers in Switzerland is active right now, and I want to make sure we're both aligned on value before investing more time."

---

## Things to Clarify Before Signing

- Current ARR or revenue (if they're willing to share) — affects how much the equity is worth
- Whether options are ISOs or NSOs (Swiss tax implications differ)
- Cliff date: when does the 1-year cliff hit?
- Acceleration clause: is there any single or double trigger on acquisition? (Relevant given the company's AGI ambitions and acquisition potential)
- Health insurance: Swiss requirement, confirm employer contribution
- Bonus: is there a performance bonus structure? At this stage likely no, but worth confirming
