# Negotiation Script, Neuromorphic Labs, Founding Engineer, Full Stack

## Context
Neuromorphic Labs just closed an oversubscribed $5.1M seed led by Flying Fish, with Toyota Ventures, Amplify Partners, and others in. That's a real but modest cash cushion, so cash comp will be constrained relative to a Series B or later, and equity is where most of the negotiating room actually lives. The founding team includes ex-CoreWeave and ex-AWS AI-infra leadership, which is a good signal on execution but also means they know exactly what founding-engineer comp should look like, so vague numbers won't land well. The role reads as hybrid, likely US Bay Area, which raises the relocation-versus-remote question early rather than late. Open with clarity: "I'm Geneva-based, this is a founding IC role at seed stage, and I want to talk base, equity, and location as three separate variables rather than one bundled number."

## Switzerland market anchors
- Senior/Staff-level founding engineer, Geneva or Swiss remote, 15+ years: CHF 160,000-190,000 base equivalent if the role were priced purely on the local market, before equity.
- Founder-exit track record (Alaya to Benevity) plus platform ownership typically supports the upper half of that band on cash alone, without factoring in the founding-engineer premium at all.
- A US seed-stage startup hiring remote-from-Switzerland will often price closer to a blended band, somewhere between Swiss and US seed-stage cash comp, roughly USD 150,000-180,000 base equivalent, precisely because seed-stage cash is tight and equity is expected to cover the gap.
- If relocation to the Bay Area is on the table, the conversation shifts to US market rate for a founding full-stack engineer at a funded seed company, which typically runs USD 160,000-200,000 base plus a meaningfully larger equity grant than a remote hire would get.

## Opening anchor
> "I'm Geneva-based and open to either a remote arrangement or relocation, so let's treat those as two different offers rather than assume one. For a founding full-stack engineer role at this stage, with the scope of owning a core piece of the trust layer platform, I'd want to anchor around USD 170,000 base if remote, or closer to USD 190,000 if relocating, plus a founding-level equity grant, before we get into the details. I know seed-stage cash is tighter, so I'm just as focused on getting the equity and vesting terms right as I am on the base number."

## If they come back low on cash (meaningfully below the anchor)
> "That makes sense given where you are post-seed, and I'd rather solve this through equity and terms than push hard on cash alone. Is there room to close some of the gap with a larger initial grant, an early refresh tied to the next round, or a sign-on that bridges the first year while the platform gets to its next milestone?"

## Geographic and remote-vs-relocation pushback
> "Geneva isn't a low-cost location, so if the offer is benchmarked against a broad remote-EMEA band, I'd want that adjusted to reflect where I actually am. If relocation to the Bay Area is genuinely preferred for this role, I'm open to it, but that should come with a market-rate US base and relocation support, not the remote number with a location change attached."

## Equity negotiation (seed stage, founding engineer)
Equity does most of the work in a package like this, so the terms matter as much as the headline percentage.
- Ask for the current fully diluted share count and the post-seed valuation before evaluating any percentage or option count in isolation.
- For a founding full-stack engineer joining just after a $5.1M seed, a grant in the rough range of 0.5%-1.5% is a reasonable starting ask depending on scope and how early "founding" actually means; push toward the higher end if the role effectively covers what would otherwise be two hires.
- Standard 4-year vest with a 1-year cliff is fine; resist anything longer than a 1-year cliff at this stage.
- Ask about early exercise and a post-termination exercise window meaningfully longer than the default 90 days, ideally several years, since a short window at a seed-stage company can force a bad decision later.
- Ask whether there's a plan for a refresh grant or anti-dilution protection tied to the next priced round, since seed-stage grants get diluted fast if nothing is negotiated up front.

## Counter-offer script
> "Thanks for putting this together, I can tell there's real thought behind the scope, not just a generic offer. Given the founding nature of the role and where the company is post-seed, I'd like to counter at USD 175,000 base if remote, with the equity grant moved toward 1% and a 1-year cliff, plus an early exercise option. If the base has a hard ceiling right now, I'm open to structuring more of this through equity or a near-term refresh instead."

## Competing offer as a bargaining chip (use only if real, or omit)
> "I'm also in later-stage conversations with another company at a comparable founding scope, where the combined package is tracking somewhat ahead of this one on equity. I'd rather build here if we can get close, the provenance and trust-layer problem is the more interesting one to me, but I wanted to be upfront about where things stand."

## What to hold firm on regardless of base outcome
- Founding-engineer title and scope confirmed in writing, not diluted to a generic senior engineer role once the offer is signed.
- Clear ownership boundary for at least one major platform component from day one, not just "full stack, wherever needed."
- Equity terms (cliff, exercise window, refresh policy) confirmed in writing before accepting, not left as "standard, we'll send docs later."
- If remote from Geneva, written confirmation that the arrangement is stable for at least the first year, with no unstated expectation of relocation tied to a future round or milestone.
