# Negotiation Script: Leapsome, Staff Product Engineer (via Jack & Jill)

## The shape of this offer

The role is based in Berlin, with the option to work from anywhere for two months a year. It isn't a Geneva-remote role. That changes the negotiation: the comparison isn't "Geneva rate vs. German discount", it's "what does it take for me to leave a Geneva market and relocate". Keep that framing throughout.

Published bands don't match. Jack & Jill lists €130k–165k + equity; another aggregator shows €110k–165k. Treat €165k as the real ceiling of the band and anchor there, and treat the €130k floor as the number the recruiter has already committed to in writing.

Leapsome raised a $60M Series A led by Insight Partners and serves 2,000+ organisations. The posting mentions pre-IPO equity. In Germany that's very often a VSOP (virtual shares), not real options, so read the equity section carefully.

## Market anchors

All figures are rough ranges for Staff-level product/platform engineers, base salary only. Verify against current data (levels.fyi, Glassdoor CH/DE, recruiter conversations) before the call.

| Market | Staff base (approx.) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Geneva / Lausanne | CHF 170k–220k | Few true Staff roles; fintech and international orgs push the top end |
| Zurich | CHF 180k–240k | Big tech offices sit above this with RSUs |
| Berlin, scale-ups | €120k–165k | Leapsome's band sits at the top of this market |
| Berlin, top payers | €150k–190k | US-headquartered companies with Berlin hubs |

CHF has traded slightly stronger than the euro, so €165k is still below a mid-range Geneva Staff base before you even count Swiss vs. German tax and social charges. Check the rate on the day and use it.

## Scripts

### When they ask for expectations first

> "I've looked at the band on the posting, and for a Staff role that's carrying the agent architecture and mentoring the team on AI-native development, I'd expect to be at the top of it, so €165k base. That's also the number that makes relocating from Geneva work for me. I'm flexible on how equity and relocation fit around it."

### If they come in low (e.g. €135k–145k)

> "Thanks, I appreciate the offer and I'm keen on the role. The base is below where I need to be. I'm coming from a Geneva market where Staff roles sit well above this, and I'd be moving countries to take it. I founded and built a platform through to acquisition, and the work you're hiring for, agent workflows over sensitive HR data, is the exact kind of ownership I've done before. Can we get to €160k?"

If base is truly capped:

> "If €150k is the hard ceiling on base, then I'd want to close the gap elsewhere: a sign-on bonus to cover the move, a larger equity grant, and a compensation review at six months against agreed goals."

### Geographic framing

The usual pushback is "Berlin is cheaper than Geneva". Don't argue cost of living. Argue replacement cost:

> "I'm not asking you to pay Geneva rates in Berlin. The point is that I'd be leaving a market where I can earn more locally, and the relocation itself has real costs. The top of your band is the fair place to land for that."

### Competing offer template

Only use this if it's true at the time of the conversation.

> "To be transparent, I'm also in late stages with [company] for a [Staff/Principal] role at [CHF/€ X]. Leapsome is my preference because of the agent work and the product area, which I know well from Alaya. If we can get to [number], I'm ready to sign this week."

## Equity: questions to ask in writing

1. Is this a VSOP (virtual shares) or a real ESOP? A VSOP usually only pays out on exit or IPO, and the payout is taxed as income in Germany.
2. Number of units granted, and total fully diluted shares, so you can compute your percentage.
3. Strike price, and the latest preferred share price or 409A-equivalent valuation.
4. Vesting schedule and cliff (typically 4 years with a 1-year cliff). Monthly or quarterly vesting after the cliff?
5. Leaver clauses: what happens to vested units if you leave as a good leaver vs. bad leaver? Some VSOPs forfeit vested units on departure; push back on that hard.
6. Treatment on acquisition: single or double-trigger acceleration? (You've been through an acquisition at Alaya, so ask how unvested units are handled.)
7. Dilution: is there any protection, and how have previous grants been treated in later rounds?
8. Refresh grants: is there a policy for Staff-level refreshers?

Value the equity at zero when deciding whether the base is acceptable. Treat it as upside.

## Other asks

- Relocation package: moving costs, temporary housing for the first month or two, help with the Berlin rental market.
- Visa/registration: confirm work authorisation path and any support with Anmeldung and tax setup.
- Start date that allows a proper notice period and the move.
- Learning budget is €2,000; ask if conference travel for AI/eval work sits outside that.
- Get the work-from-anywhere two months confirmed in the contract, including whether those can be spent in Switzerland.
