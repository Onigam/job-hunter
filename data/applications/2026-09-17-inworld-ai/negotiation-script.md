# Negotiation Script, Inworld AI, Staff/Principal AI Engineer (Switzerland Remote)

## Context to keep in mind going in

The posting is written toward a research-adjacent hire: PhD or equivalent research/engineering experience, deep ML framework fluency, expertise in speech, video, NLP, or action planning. That's not this profile. This profile is a strong systems and platform engineer with real hands-on AI-augmented workflow experience (multi-agent tooling, embeddings, semantic search), but not a published ML researcher and not someone who has trained models from scratch. Naming that honestly, as already done in the cover letter, changes the negotiation: the conversation should be framed around production ownership and the engineering that surrounds the research, not around competing on the research axis itself.

General Swiss market data for senior ML/AI engineering roles clusters around CHF 140,000-170,000 for strong ICs, with Staff/Principal titles at well-funded, VC-backed companies running higher, often CHF 180,000-220,000 base plus equity. Inworld is a Series A company (reported in the $50M-plus range, with some sources citing larger cumulative raises), so there's real funding behind the role but also real dilution risk this early. Anchor on Staff-level scope and scarcity of the platform-engineering skill set, not on the generic "ML engineer" salary bands that describe a narrower, more junior job.

## Opening anchor

Base target: CHF 175,000-200,000, framed as Staff-level compensation for the scope this hire would likely actually own: production reliability, orchestration, and the engineering discipline around research systems, rather than the research itself. Say it like this:

"I want to be upfront that my strength is the systems and platform side of shipping AI infrastructure, not the research side the posting leans toward. Given that scope and the Staff-level responsibilities described, I'd expect a base in the CHF 175K-200K range, with the rest of the package reflecting how central you see this seat as."

## If they come in low (say, below CHF 150,000)

Don't reject on the spot. Ask what's driving the number:

"Is that number reflecting the full Staff scope, or is it priced closer to a mid-level IC band? I ask because owning production ML systems end to end, the way the posting describes it, reads as Staff-level work regardless of geography."

If they hold the line on base, ask for the difference in equity or a signing bonus instead. Early-stage-adjacent companies often have more flexibility there than in fixed cash bands.

## Equity notes

- Vesting: standard 4-year vest with a 1-year cliff; push back on anything longer without a clear reason.
- Ask for the strike price and the most recent valuation in writing before evaluating any percentage they quote; a percentage without a valuation is not a number.
- Ask about double-trigger acceleration on acquisition. This isn't hypothetical caution, Alaya was acquired by Benevity, and that experience is a legitimate reason to ask what happens to unvested equity if Inworld gets acquired too.
- If early exercise is on the table, get clarity on the Swiss tax treatment before deciding; this is not a US-style ISO situation and the tax timing can differ meaningfully.

## Geographic discount pushback

If they try to lower the offer because Geneva "costs less" than a US hub: it doesn't. Housing and mandatory health insurance in Geneva run comparable to or above most US tech hubs. Use this line if needed:

"Geneva isn't a discount market, it's comparable on housing and higher on mandatory health insurance. I'd rather this be priced as a market-adjusted Staff role than a geographic discount hire."

## Competing offer as a counterweight (template, use only if genuinely in play)

"I'm in later-stage conversations with another company at a comparable level, and I'd like to make a decision with full information. Could we get to a final number this week so I can compare properly?"

Only use this if there's an actual competing process. An empty bluff is easy to spot at this level and costs more in trust than it's worth as a bargaining chip.

## Bottom line walk-away

Given the mismatch between the posting's research emphasis and this profile's platform strength, don't anchor ego on the top of the range. If the final offer lands meaningfully below CHF 150,000 base with thin equity, that's a signal the company is pricing this as a mid-level hire under a Staff title. It's fine to say so directly and walk.
