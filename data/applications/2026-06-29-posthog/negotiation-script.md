# Negotiation Script — PostHog Backend Engineer (Ingestion)

---

## Context

PostHog is a remote-first, YC-backed company (Series C/D, ~$225M valuation as of 2023, still private as of 2026). They use a transparent compensation calculator that applies a US/SF benchmark multiplied by a location factor. Their stated philosophy is "pay for impact, not location," but in practice European engineers are adjusted downward from US benchmarks.

Key facts to hold:
- Geneva cost of living is comparable to London or higher.
- PostHog's location factor for London is typically in the 0.80-0.90 range. Push for the same factor for Switzerland, not a European-mainland discount.
- The role is Backend Engineer, not Staff. Positioning as overqualified creates risk. The play here is to negotiate the band ceiling and argue for day-one seniority placement.
- PostHog offers equity (stock options). For a company at this stage, options are speculative. Factor that into base-vs-equity trade-offs.

---

## Market Anchors (Switzerland)

| Benchmark | CHF/year |
|---|---|
| Senior Backend Engineer, Geneva market | 150,000-175,000 |
| Staff/Senior Staff Engineer equivalent, Geneva | 180,000-220,000 |
| Magino's current level expectation | 190,000+ |

**Target opening ask:** CHF 195,000 base, or equivalent in USD at the London location factor applied to Switzerland.

If PostHog quotes in USD: a London factor of 0.85 on a $185,000 SF benchmark = ~$157,000. Convert to CHF at ~0.90: approximately CHF 142,000. That's below market for Geneva. The counter is to argue Switzerland deserves the London factor minimum, not a generic EU factor.

---

## Opening Position

When asked for expectations:

> "Based on the level of work and the Geneva market, I'm targeting around CHF 190,000 to 195,000 as a base. I know PostHog uses a location-adjusted model, so I'm happy to work through how that translates if you use a different denomination, but that's the number I need to make the move make sense."

Don't volunteer a range first. If they push for a range, give a narrow band: CHF 185,000-200,000.

---

## If They Come In Low (under CHF 160,000 equivalent)

> "I appreciate the transparency on the number. I want to make this work, but CHF [X] puts me below what I'm currently earning, and Geneva's cost structure means I can't treat equity as a salary substitute the way someone in a lower-cost market might. Is there flexibility to move the base up, or is there a seniority band I could be placed in that gets us closer to [target]?"

Follow-up if they cite the location factor:

> "I'd push back a bit on applying a mainland-EU factor to Geneva. The cost of living here is closer to London than to Berlin or Amsterdam. If your calculator has a London rate, I'd argue Switzerland deserves at least that factor."

---

## Equity Conversation

PostHog offers stock options. At their current stage, treat options as a bonus rather than comp replacement.

Questions to ask:
1. "What's the current strike price, and what was the last 409A valuation?"
2. "What's the vesting schedule and cliff?" (Standard: 4-year vest, 1-year cliff. Accept this.)
3. "Is there an acceleration clause on acquisition?" (Push for double-trigger if not already offered.)
4. "What's the total option pool size, and what percentage of shares outstanding would my grant represent?"

If they offer a large equity package to offset a lower base:

> "I think the options have real upside potential, but I'm not in a position to price that into my day-to-day needs. I'd rather have a base that works and treat the equity as the upside it is. Can we bring the base closer to [target] and adjust the option grant accordingly?"

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

If you have another offer (or want to create urgency without fabricating):

> "I'm in late-stage conversations with one other company, and I expect to have a decision to make in the next two weeks. PostHog is genuinely my preference for the engineering work, but I need the numbers to be close enough that I'm not leaving significant money on the table."

If pressed for specifics, keep it general: "It's a Series B/C product company, similar backend scope."

---

## If They Ask About Flexibility on Role Level

PostHog may not have a "Staff" title, just Backend Engineer at different seniority levels. If they offer you the role at a junior band:

> "Given that I'm coming in with 17 years of experience and have run architecture decisions at both a startup and a $1B+ company, I'd want to make sure we're aligned on which band this sits in. I'm not chasing a title, but the compensation should reflect the seniority of what I'd actually be doing."

---

## Walk-Away Number

Below CHF 155,000 base, the move doesn't make economic sense relative to current alternatives. Don't negotiate below this point. If they can't get to CHF 165,000+, it's a no.
