# Negotiation Script: PEAX AG, Senior Agentic-AI Engineer (Lucerne, hybrid)

No salary is disclosed in the posting. The ranges below are estimates from public Swiss comp data for senior AI and backend engineers at scale-ups. Central Switzerland usually pays a bit below Zurich and roughly in line with Geneva. Check levels.fyi, Glassdoor and the Swissdevjobs salary pages before the first comp call.

## 1. Market picture (Switzerland, 2026 estimates)

| Reference point | Base (CHF) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Swiss SaaS scale-up, Senior Backend Engineer (Lucerne / Central CH) | 125k to 150k | Bonus small or none |
| Swiss scale-up, Senior / Lead AI Engineer | 145k to 180k | Specialist premium for agentic/LLM experience |
| Zurich tech, Staff-level AI engineer | 170k to 210k | Upper end at well-funded companies |
| Big tech / AI labs, Zurich | 220k+ | Not a realistic comparison for PEAX, but useful context |

The posting lists the role as management level with direct CTO collaboration and a mandate to set the agentic AI strategy. That's closer to Lead/Staff scope than a standard senior IC, and it's the argument for pushing toward the top of the band.

**Your targets**
- Walk-away base: CHF 150k
- Target base: CHF 170k to 178k
- Opening anchor: CHF 185k base

## 2. Opening the conversation

If they ask for expectations first:

> "For a role that owns the agentic AI strategy and works directly with the CTO, I'm looking at a base around CHF 185k. I'm open on how the rest of the package is put together, including bonus and home-office days, and I'd like to see the whole picture before we fix anything."

If they push for a range, don't give one. Repeat the number and bring it back to scope.

Justification to have ready: 17 years in backend and platform systems, five years running engineering as a cofounder through to an acquisition by a $1B+ company, Staff-level cross-team architecture at Benevity, and hands-on LLM, embeddings and multi-agent work. They're hiring one person to define the architecture, build the first agents and coach the team, which is more than one senior engineer's job.

## 3. If the offer comes in low

> "Thanks, I appreciate the offer and I'd like to make this work. The base is below where I'd expected for the scope, since this role sets the direction for agentic AI across the platform. If you can get to CHF 175k, I'm ready to move forward."

If they say the band is fixed:

> "Understood. If base can't move, can we look at a signing bonus to cover the gap in the first year, a performance bonus tied to agents going live, or a formal comp review at twelve months?"

Don't accept on the call. "Let me look at the full package and I'll get back to you by Friday" is always fine.

## 4. Commute and location (Geneva to Lucerne)

This is the one area where you have a real case, because the commute is long: Geneva to Lucerne is roughly 2h45 by train each way.

> "I'm fully committed to being on site when it matters. Given the distance from Geneva, I'd like to agree on one or two office days a week, and for PEAX to cover a GA travelcard. That keeps me in Lucerne regularly without it costing me a day of work in travel."

Asks, in order of priority:
1. Office presence of one to two days a week, written into the contract or an addendum
2. GA travelcard (1st or 2nd class) paid by the employer
3. Flexibility on which days, so you can cluster them around team sessions

Don't accept a lower base in exchange for remote days. The two are separate conversations.

## 5. Equity and bonus

PEAX is an established Swiss scale-up, so a startup-style option grant may not exist. Ask how ownership is structured and whether an employee participation plan exists before assuming anything.

If options or shares are on the table, ask:
- What percentage of the fully diluted share count the grant represents, not just the number of shares
- Vesting schedule and cliff (4 years with a 1-year cliff is standard; push for monthly vesting after the cliff)
- What happens on acquisition: ask for double-trigger acceleration
- Exercise window after leaving and the Swiss tax treatment at grant and exercise

If there's no equity, push the value into cash: a bonus target of 10 to 15% or a higher base.

Also ask about pension (BVG/LPP) contributions. Some employers pay well above the legal minimum, and on a CHF 175k salary that difference adds up to several thousand francs a year.

## 6. Competing-offer template

Only use this if it's true, or phrase it as something that's still in progress:

> "I want to be transparent: I'm in late-stage conversations with another company for a Staff-level AI platform role, with a base above CHF 180k. PEAX is my preference because of the scope and working directly with the CTO, and I'd rather decide on the merits than on money. Is there room to close the gap?"

## 7. Things to clarify before signing

- Actual reporting line and whether "management level" means people management, now or later
- Budget for model APIs, vector databases and observability tooling
- Data residency rules for LLM calls (Swiss-hosted models only, or Azure OpenAI in Switzerland North, etc.)
- German requirement: how much day-to-day work is in German versus English
- Notice period and start-date flexibility
