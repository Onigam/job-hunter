# Negotiation Script — SonarSource Principal Platform Engineer

---

## Market Context (Geneva, 2026)

SonarSource (branded as Sonar) is a profitable, VC-backed developer tools company headquartered in Geneva, backed by General Catalyst. Not a pre-revenue startup. Principal-level roles at this tier in Geneva command:

- Staff / Principal Engineer (10+ years): CHF 185,000 - 220,000 base
- Distinguished / Principal (15+ years, founding background): CHF 210,000 - 240,000 base
- Total target with bonus: CHF 220,000 - 250,000

Geneva-specific note: Geneva runs 12-15% higher than Zurich in cost of living. If SonarSource applies a Swiss flat rate, push back on that specifically.

The role includes a discretionary Company Growth Bonus (typically 10-20% at this level), pension contribution of 60% of total cost (~15% of salary), and 27 PTO days.

---

## Opening Anchor

When asked for your range or current comp, anchor high and give a range rather than a single number.

**Script:**
"For a Principal-level role in Geneva with my background, I'm looking at something in the range of CHF 215,000 to 235,000 in base salary, with bonus and equity/phantom shares on top. That's consistent with what I've seen for this level in the Geneva market. I'm open to the right overall package, so happy to understand the full structure before locking in."

Why this works: The range is credible, not greedy. Starting at 215k leaves room to settle around 200-210k if needed. The phrase "full structure" signals you're not just looking at base.

---

## If They Come In Below CHF 180,000

This is a significant gap. Don't panic, but don't accept.

**Script:**
"I appreciate the offer, and I'm still very interested in the role. That said, CHF [X] is below what I'd expect for a Principal-level position in Geneva with 17 years of experience, including a founding engineer background at a company that was acquired by a $1B+ business. I'm targeting something closer to CHF 200,000 in base. Is there flexibility there, or room to offset the gap through a higher bonus guarantee or additional equity?"

If they push back on the market rate claim:
"I'm happy to share what I've been seeing in conversations. The Geneva market for Staff and Principal engineers has moved up significantly in the last couple of years. CHF 180-200k is the band I've been benchmarking against."

---

## If They Come In at CHF 180,000 - 195,000

Reasonable gap. Counter to CHF 205,000 and be willing to settle at 197-200k.

**Script:**
"That's closer. I was targeting CHF 205,000-210,000 given the level and the Geneva market, but I want to make this work. Could we get to 200k on base, and if not, is there room to offset through the bonus or a one-time signing? I'm not trying to squeeze the last franc, I just want to make sure the comp is aligned with what I'd expect for this level."

---

## If They Come In at CHF 195,000+

This is acceptable territory. Ask about bonus and equity before accepting.

**Script:**
"That's in range for me. Before I say yes, can we talk about the bonus structure and whether there's any equity or phantom shares involved? I want to understand the full picture."

Clarifying questions to ask:
- "What's the typical bonus range at this level? Is it discretionary or target-based?"
- "Does SonarSource offer any equity participation? If so, what does that look like?"
- "Is there a cliff or vesting schedule?"

---

## Equity / Phantom Shares

SonarSource is not public and may offer phantom shares or profit participation rather than traditional equity. This is common in Europe for pre-IPO companies.

**If they offer equity:**
"Can you tell me more about the vesting schedule and the cliff? And what happens to unvested shares if the company is acquired?"

**If they offer nothing:**
"I noticed the role doesn't include equity participation. Given the level and the fact that this is a principal IC role with significant organizational impact, I'd want to explore whether there's room to add equity or a performance-based retention component. If equity isn't possible, I'd offset that with a higher base or guaranteed bonus."

---

## Geneva Geographic Premium

If they reference a Switzerland-wide pay band:

**Script:**
"I want to flag that Geneva has a meaningfully higher cost of living than other Swiss cities, running about 12-15% above Zurich. If the band is set at a Swiss average, I'd want to discuss a Geneva adjustment. This is pretty standard in conversations I've had with other Geneva-based employers."

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

If you have another offer in process (or want to create urgency):

**Script:**
"I'll be transparent: I'm in late stages with one other company, and I'm hoping to wrap up these conversations by end of [week/month]. SonarSource is my preferred outcome because of [specific reason: Geneva base, developer tools domain, the platform mission], but I want to make sure the numbers work so I don't have to make a decision purely on comp."

Even without a hard competing offer, you can reference the market:
"Based on what I've been seeing in conversations more broadly, CHF [X] is where the market is at for this profile, and I want to make sure I'm not leaving significant comp on the table."

---

## Signing Bonus

If they can't move on base:

**Script:**
"If base is hard to move, would you consider a one-time signing bonus? Even CHF 15-20k up front helps bridge the gap without changing your base salary structure."

---

## Things Not to Negotiate Away

- Remote flexibility: The Principal Platform Engineer role should allow at least 2 remote days. Don't give this up for cash.
- Pension contribution: 60% is above average. Don't accept a reduction.
- PTO: 27 days is the Geneva legal minimum for this tier. Don't accept less.
