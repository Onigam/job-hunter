# Negotiation Script — EverAI, Tech Lead LLM & Generative AI

---

## Context

- Contract type: B2B preferred (no employment contract)
- Location: Geneva, Switzerland (Europe remote)
- No salary published
- Company: ~75 people, 50M users, strong consumer revenue, one of fastest-growing AI companies in Europe
- Team: 3 AI engineers reporting to you, you report directly to CTO

---

## Market Anchor (Switzerland / Europe Remote)

**Tech Lead AI/LLM — B2B contractor rates:**

| Market | Annual gross (B2B) | Daily rate |
|--------|-------------------|------------|
| Switzerland (Geneva) | CHF 165k–210k | CHF 850–1,100/day |
| France / Germany (remote) | €110k–150k | €550–750/day |
| Europe average (senior AI) | €120k–160k | €600–800/day |

Because EverAI pays B2B, there are no employer social charges. The gross rate they quote is what Magino nets before personal taxes. At a Geneva cost of living (~CHF 6k–8k/month household baseline), anything below CHF 150k/year gross B2B is a real-purchasing-power cut.

**Anchor to open with: CHF 195k/year (or equivalent EUR 190k).**

This is justifiable: Staff+ AI engineer with founding experience, proven LLM production work, Switzerland-based, leading a team of 3 in a high-autonomy role. The alignment/moderation challenges they're working on aren't entry-level; benchmark accordingly.

---

## Opening Script

When they ask for expectations, lead with range, not a number:

> "I'm looking at CHF 190–210k gross annual, B2B. That's calibrated to Staff-level AI engineering in Geneva and the scope of the role, leading the LLM team with direct CTO access and production ownership at that token scale. I'm open to discussing how equity or performance components would factor in."

If they push for a single number before revealing their range: give the top of your range (CHF 210k). Better to negotiate down than anchor low.

---

## If They Come In Low (below CHF 150k)

Don't accept on the call. Say:

> "That's lower than what I had in mind for this scope. I want to make this work, so let me ask: is there flexibility to move closer to CHF 175–190k, or is there equity upside that changes the picture?"

Then stop talking. Let them respond.

If they say the range is fixed, push for:
- Accelerated review at 6 months (not 12)
- Equity / warrant package
- Annual professional development budget (CHF 3–5k)
- Equipment upgrade or home-office stipend

---

## Equity / Warrants

EverAI is likely a French or European-registered entity. Ask directly:

> "Is there a warrant or equity program for the tech leadership team?"

What to look for:
- BSPCEs (French startup warrants): tax-advantaged if held 3+ years
- Vesting schedule: standard is 4 years with 1-year cliff. Push for 3-year cliff-free if you can.
- Ask about the current cap table stage: how many rounds, what's the last valuation, where would a grant land relative to the fully diluted share count
- Anti-dilution protection is unlikely at this stage, but ask about pro-rata rights if there's a next round

If they offer equity as a substitute for cash, be careful. The cash compensates for Geneva cost of living today; equity is speculative. Don't accept a 20% cash cut for equity unless the company is clearly pre-IPO with real momentum.

---

## Geographic Discount Pushback

If they try to benchmark against French or Eastern European rates:

> "I'm based in Geneva, which is one of the more expensive cities in Europe. The Swiss cost of living is roughly 40–50% higher than Paris and 2x Berlin. I'm open to European market benchmarks for the role scope, but the location has to factor into the base."

---

## Competing Offer Leverage

If you have other offers in play (or want to signal optionality):

> "I'm at final stages with a couple of other companies, both in the AI infrastructure space, with packages in the CHF 180–200k range. EverAI is the role I'm most interested in, so I wanted to be direct about where the numbers need to land before I go further."

Only use this if true or nearly true. Don't manufacture competing offers — they sometimes ask for details.

---

## Closing Move

If they're close but not quite there:

> "Here's what I can agree to: CHF 185k to start, with a written commitment to a compensation review at month 6 tied to defined milestones. That gives both sides a fair landing. Can we agree on that?"

---

## Non-Negotiables to Confirm Before Signing

- Invoicing currency (EUR vs CHF; CHF preferred for Geneva-based)
- Payment terms (30 days net is standard; ask for 15 if possible)
- IP ownership: confirm your open-source work and side projects remain yours
- Non-compete scope: should be narrow (12 months, direct competitors only, not "any AI company")
- Termination notice: push for 3 months minimum given senior role
