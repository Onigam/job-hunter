# Negotiation Script — Bloom / Founding Engineer

---

## Market Context

**Posted range:** $150K-$250K base + 0.50%-2.00% equity
**Location:** Zürich, Switzerland / Remote

**Switzerland market reality (2025-2026):**
- Zürich is one of the most expensive cities globally. Cost of living is roughly 40-50% higher than the European average.
- Founding Engineer / Staff+ in Zürich: CHF 190K-260K base is the realistic band for someone at this experience level.
- The posted range in USD is likely meant to be read as CHF-equivalent, or they're aware they need to price for the Swiss market.
- Do not accept a below-market CHF number because the posting says "USD." Ask early: "Is the range quoted in USD or CHF, and is there a geographic adjustment for Zürich-based work?"

**Total compensation target:**
- Base: CHF 220K-250K (or USD equivalent)
- Equity: 1.5%-2.0% (founding engineer with CTO track justifies pushing toward the top)
- Vesting: 4-year / 1-year cliff, with double-trigger acceleration on acquisition (important for a YC startup that may be acquired)

---

## Opening Anchor

When they ask for your number, don't give a range. Give a number at the top of your target band and explain it briefly:

> "I'm targeting CHF 240K base. Given the Zürich cost of living and the scope of the role (founding engineer with a clear path to CTO), that's the number I'm working from. On equity, I'd want to be at 1.75% or above given what this role means to the company at this stage."

If they push back on the number being high relative to the posting range, respond:

> "The range is wide, which suggests flexibility. I'm at the senior end of the founding-engineer profile you're looking for, and the platform-building experience from Alaya to acquisition is genuinely uncommon. I want to start this partnership at a number that works long-term, not negotiate upward in a year."

---

## Counter-Offer Scripts

**If they offer base at the low end of the range (CHF/USD 150K-170K):**

> "I appreciate the offer. The equity and the role are compelling, but I need the base to get closer to CHF 210K-220K to make the numbers work in Zürich. Is there flexibility there, or would you prefer to adjust the equity side instead? I'm open to either direction."

**If they offer mid-range (CHF 180K-200K):**

> "That's getting closer. I'd want to get to CHF 220K on base to feel fully aligned, or if base is fixed, I'd want to move the equity to 1.75%. I'd rather resolve this now than have it sit in the background."

**If they say the budget is fixed:**

> "Understood. Then let's talk about the equity more carefully. If the base can't move, I want to make sure the equity reflects that. At 1.5% or above with a clean vesting schedule, I'm comfortable."

---

## Equity Negotiation

**Why push hard on equity for this role specifically:**
Bloom is a YC X25 company with a founding-engineer hire who may become CTO. This is not a standard employee hire. The expected dilution path (Series A, Series B, potential acquisition) means 0.5% at hiring could be 0.1%-0.2% by exit. 1.75%-2% gives you meaningful upside even after two rounds of dilution.

**Specific asks:**
- Vesting: 4-year / 1-year cliff (standard, non-negotiable from your side)
- Acceleration: double-trigger acceleration on acquisition (single-trigger is common at YC startups but more founder-friendly; double-trigger is the ask)
- Strike price: ask to see the current 409A valuation and exercise price before signing
- Refresh grants: ask what the policy is for additional equity grants as the company grows

**Script if equity is offered at 0.5%-0.75%:**

> "Given the CTO track you mentioned, I'd expect to land higher on the equity range. The founding-engineer role at this stage means the technical architecture decisions I make in the next 12-18 months are the ones you'll live with. I want to make sure the equity reflects that stake. Can we get to 1.5%?"

---

## Geographic Notes

If the role involves any remote flexibility and they attempt to apply a "home-office discount" because you're in Geneva:

> "Geneva and Zürich have comparable costs of living. I'm not in a lower-cost geography, so I wouldn't expect a geographic discount to apply."

If they offer a Switzerland-adjusted lower number compared to a US hire:

> "I understand US compensation can look different. The comparison that matters to me is the Zürich market rate for this profile, not a cross-currency normalization from US data."

---

## Competing Offer Template

If you're in conversations with other companies at the same time (or want to create urgency without fabricating):

> "I have a few other processes moving. None of them are as well-aligned as Bloom on the founding role, but I want to be transparent that I'm being realistic about timing. If we're close on terms, I'd rather move quickly than let this drag for weeks."

If you have an actual competing offer:

> "I have an offer at [CHF X] base from another company, also in Switzerland. I'd prefer this role, but I need the numbers to be close enough that it's not a difficult decision. Can you get to [CHF Y]?"

---

## What to Protect

In order of importance:
1. Equity percentage at or above 1.5%
2. Base at or above CHF 210K
3. Double-trigger acceleration clause
4. 409A visibility before signing
5. Clear written description of CTO track criteria and timeline
